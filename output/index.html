Alexandra Eala Cruises Past Mika Stojsavljevic in Tokyo Opener at Pan Pacific

While her opponent managed 24 winners compared to Eala's eight, Stojsavljevic struggled with consistency, committing 41 unforced errors that allowed the 21-year-old to control the scoreboard comfortably in straight sets.

Eala expressed respect for Gibson's playing style, acknowledging her as a big hitter and a strong competitor. Outside of singles play, Eala also noted her disappointment regarding a canceled doubles partnership with Desirae Krawczyk, though she emphasized the strong personal connection she felt with Krawczyk due to their shared Filipino roots.
