Two Lucky Winners Split Massive $12.2 Million Toto Jackpot In Singapore

Two winning tickets shared the $12,198,558 Group 1 jackpot following the Toto cascade draw held on Nov 9.

The winning numbers drawn were 5, 27, 29, 38, 40 and 43, with 4 serving as the additional number. Each of the two triumphant ticket holders earned a share worth more than $6 million from the total prize pool, which reached $12,198,558. These winning entries were purchased through the Singapore Pools Account Betting Service, consisting of one QuickPick System 7 entry and one QuickPick Ordinary entry. Beyond the top prize, 12 shares were awarded in Group 2, with each share valued at $115,990. The significant payout followed a series of draws where the jackpot failed to find a winner, causing the funds to snowball from just over $1.1 million on Oct 29. By the time of the third draw on Nov 5, the total had already swelled to more than $5.5 million. The cascade draw process dictates that if no one claims the Group 1 prize after four consecutive draws, the accumulated money is split among winners in the subsequent prize group. This particular event marks the fifth time in 2026 that the jackpot has reached or exceeded $10 million, echoing a similar occurrence in July when two tickets shared a $12.8 million prize.
