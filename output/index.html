Police car mounts Geylang Bahru kerb and crashes into metal road barrier

A 31-year-old police officer is assisting with investigations after his patrol vehicle hit a road divider on October 10.

The front of the police vehicle suffered visible damage after mounting a kerb and colliding with a green barrier on Geylang Bahru Road, sending railings tumbling into the rightmost lane of traffic. The accident occurred at approximately 7:45 am while the vehicle was traveling toward Bendemeer Road, leaving the divider structure damaged at the scene.

This incident is currently under investigation by authorities, who confirmed that the 31-year-old male officer behind the wheel is assisting with those inquiries. While the vehicle collision disrupted the immediate area, local authorities stated that no injuries resulted from the impact. The aftermath of the crash was captured in footage circulating on social media, which shows the damaged cruiser alongside fallen road railings while other police personnel managed the site.

As investigations continue, the exact circumstances leading to the vehicle mounting the kerb remain undetermined. Witnesses who drove past the site noted the presence of multiple police officers securing the area and a secondary police car positioned across the roadway to manage the flow of traffic following the impact. There is no timeline provided for when the official findings of the investigation will be released or when the damaged road infrastructure will be fully repaired.
