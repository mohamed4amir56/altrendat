Elon Musk Wealth Plunges $90 Billion Following Major Midterm Election Losses

Elon Musk's net worth has dropped below $800 billion after shares of Tesla and SpaceX plummeted following the midterm elections.

Elon Musk saw his personal wealth drop by approximately $90 billion over the past week as markets reacted to the outcome of the U.S. midterm elections. According to Forbes, his total net worth fell to below $800 billion, a significant decline from the $1 trillion mark he reached following the SpaceX initial public offering in June. Despite this sharp reduction in valuation, Musk remains the wealthiest individual in the world.

The recent sell-off saw Tesla and SpaceX shares decline by 11% and 9% respectively by Thursday afternoon. Investors are reportedly concerned about the potential for stricter regulatory oversight and new congressional probes following the election results. Deutsche Bank analysts noted that SpaceX, in particular, may face incremental headwinds regarding its Texas operations. The company is currently developing significant AI infrastructure and a semiconductor factory in Grimes County, as well as a supercomputer at its Starbase launch facility.

Musk’s financial involvement in the midterms was substantial, with contributions exceeding $470 million to GOP candidates and causes. Despite this investment, the majority of the candidates he supported were defeated. In Texas, where both Tesla and SpaceX maintain headquarters, political shifts have created new uncertainty for these companies. Gov.-elect Gina Hinojosa has previously identified data centers as a campaign issue, calling for a moratorium on new facility approvals until the legislature secures stronger protections regarding electricity costs and water use.

Analysts suggest that future company expenditures may need to rise to meet the requirements of anticipated regulatory changes. Deutsche Bank specifically warned that SpaceX’s Texas compute capacity could face delays as a result of the changing political landscape in the state.
