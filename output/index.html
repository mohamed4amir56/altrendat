Ghana faces Gambia in Africa Cup of Nations qualifier with fans urged to show belief

Ghana's Sports Fund Administrator calls on supporters to trust the Black Stars ahead of their Africa Cup of Nations qualification match against Gambia today.

Dr. David Kofi Wuaku, Administrator of the Ghana Sports Fund, has urged Ghanaians to give the Black Stars their full support ahead of today's match against Gambia, saying players perform best when they feel the nation is behind them.

Wuaku acknowledged that Ghanaian football fans are passionate about lineups and tactics in the days before matches, but emphasized a shift in responsibility once play begins. "Every Ghanaian is a coach in the days before a big match," he said. "But once the whistle blows, our job changes. Our job is to believe."

The administrator pointed to how players respond to crowd energy, noting that young players feel every pass and missed chance deeply. "A player who hears his name chanted after a mistake plays the next ball without fear. A player who hears silence or insults hesitates," Wuaku explained, underscoring how national support shapes performance. He emphasized that football careers are shaped by moments of confidence and doubt, with a supportive nation able to tip the balance.

Wuaku also called for maturity in how fans respond after the final whistle, whatever the result. "If we win, let us celebrate with humility. If we do not, let us respond with respect and encouragement," he said. He stressed that lasting success requires a nation standing with its team across every season, not only on match day.

    GTA 6 preorders heavily favor PS5 over Xbox despite Microsoft's optimism

    Grand Theft Auto 6 is drawing significantly more preorders on PlayStation 5 than Xbox Series X|S, with Sony's marketing partnership dominating the game's promotion ahead of its November 19 launch.

    GTA 6 preorders are "heavily skewed" toward PlayStation 5, according to reports cited by The Verge, marking a challenge for Xbox as the industry's most anticipated game approaches its November 19 release. Sony's exclusive marketing deal with Rockstar Games has flooded promotional channels with PlayStation-exclusive content: all major trailers and Netflix footage carry the disclaimer that footage was recorded on PS5, custom GTA 6 UI appears on Sony's console, and the PS5 Pro has faced supply shortages due to GTA 6 anticipation.

    Xbox executives acknowledge the disparity but frame it favorably. Chief Strategy Officer Matthew Ball stated that GTA 6 is "breaking records for XBOX" and that the company's "internal analysis shows our share of pre-orders to be matching our share of the console market." However, this claim carries context: PS5 has sold more than 95 million units as of June 30, while analysts suggest Xbox Series X|S combined are outsold by PS5 by at least 2:1, meaning twice as many GTA 6 preorders likely exist for PlayStation.

    Xbox faces additional headaches beyond marketing. Sony's partnership explicitly prevents Xbox from formally mentioning GTA 6's existence on its platforms, and no guarantee exists that GTA 6 Online will support cross-play between systems. The timing also challenges Xbox, which is simultaneously navigating layoffs, studio consolidation, subscription price increases, and hardware stock constraints, leaving few alternative blockbuster titles to drive engagement through the final months of 2026.
