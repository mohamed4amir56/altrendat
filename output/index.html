SpaceX Spectrum Buy Wrecks Telecom Stocks As Starlink Targets Mobile Market Dominance

SpaceX agreed to purchase a nationwide 800 MHz spectrum portfolio from Grain Management to expand Starlink into mobile services, triggering steep declines for major U.S. carriers.

Verizon shares plummeted 8.75% on Friday, marking the company’s worst single-day performance since July 2002. T-Mobile and AT&T shares mirrored this volatility, dropping 13.27% and 9.81% respectively, as investors reacted to the expansion of Elon Musk’s satellite venture into the terrestrial telecommunications industry.

This shift carries significant implications for American consumers and wireless providers alike. By acquiring a nationwide low-band spectrum portfolio, SpaceX aims to ensure Starlink signals can penetrate walls and reach cellular dead zones. Analysts from JPMorgan suggest this acquisition lends credibility to the company’s long-term mobile ambitions, while Evercore ISI noted that SpaceX now possesses the necessary framework for a legitimate network.

SpaceX announced the agreement to purchase the 800 MHz band spectrum from Grain Management on Thursday. The Federal Communications Commission will oversee the deal, with Chair Brendan Carr characterizing the increased competition as positive for the market. Although CEO Elon Musk has previously expressed a personal distaste for manufacturing a smartphone, investor Gene Munster posits that a vertically integrated Starlink handset remains a future possibility to deliver unmatched service features.

While market observers anticipate a new era of competition, some analysts maintain that the immediate risk to established wireless incumbents remains limited. Experts cite the massive capital requirements and extensive infrastructure construction still needed before Starlink can effectively challenge current cellular giants. As the regulatory process unfolds, the industry will monitor how the integration of satellite capabilities and newly acquired terrestrial spectrum alters the competitive landscape.

Cristiano Ronaldo Nets 981st Career Goal as Al-Nassr Routs Neftchi Fergana 3-1

Cristiano Ronaldo scored in Al-Nassr’s 3-1 victory over Uzbekistan’s Neftchi Fergana on Monday after a missile-related security delay forced a one-hour kickoff postponement in Riyadh.

Riyadh residents received emergency phone alerts warning of a hostile aerial threat shortly before the scheduled match start, forcing spectators to remain in their seats as the Saudi-led military coalition intercepted and destroyed an incoming ballistic missile. The kickoff at Alawwal Park, originally set for 9:15pm, was delayed by one hour, with the match finally beginning at 10:15pm Mecca time.

Despite the external security concerns, Al-Nassr secured a victory that keeps their campaign active in the AFC Champions League Elite. The win remains a key result for the Saudi side as they navigate the tournament following recent escalations in regional security.

According to The Guardian, the stadium announcer confirmed the situation was safe before play commenced. Cristiano Ronaldo scored his 981st career goal just before half-time, marking his first strike in this season’s AFC Champions League Elite competition. Goal.com reports that the 41-year-old forward hammered a powerful strike from inside the penalty area to make it 2-0 in the 42nd minute. The intensity spiked shortly after when the visitors had a goal ruled out following a video assistant referee review, followed by an on-pitch altercation between Al-Nassr defender Inigo Martinez and a Neftchi player that resulted in yellow cards for both.

With this performance, Ronaldo has reached five goals across all competitions this season. He now stands 19 goals away from his stated career milestone of 1,000 goals.
