Disruptive passenger removed from Scoot flight TR905, triggers 2-hour delay

A passenger was removed from Scoot flight TR905 in Macau on September 27 for disruptive behaviour, causing the Singapore-bound aircraft to delay departure by two hours.

The woman was escorted off the plane by Macau police officers and Scoot flight attendants as other passengers applauded in the background. A video of the removal posted on Chinese social media Xiaohongshu on September 28 garnered nearly 1,000 likes and sparked widespread comment from users asking what had triggered her removal.

The incident disrupted travel for 173 passengers on board. Scoot said the delay stemmed from the time required to remove the passenger, wait for local authorities to arrive, and offload her checked baggage. All remaining passengers stayed on the aircraft and received refreshments during the wait.

Flight TR905 was scheduled to depart Macau International Airport at 6:45 p.m. local time but left at 8:46 p.m., landing in Singapore around 12:30 a.m. Scoot confirmed on October 1 that the passenger was handed over to Macau authorities but did not disclose specifics of her conduct on board. Investigations into the incident remain ongoing, the airline said in a statement apologizing for the disruption and reaffirming that "the safety and well-being of our customers and crew are our top priority."
