Met Eireann issues Status Yellow fog warning for eight Irish counties tonight

Met Eireann has issued a Status Yellow fog warning for eight counties, covering the period from 10pm Monday until 10am Tuesday.

Temperatures are set to plummet to as low as -1C tonight, creating conditions where dense fog will likely become widespread. This overnight chill brings a risk of frost as skies remain mostly clear, leading to reduced visibility for anyone traveling across the affected regions.

Motorists should prepare for difficult driving conditions as the dense mist and fog are expected to persist through the early morning. With visibility significantly impacted, road safety is a primary concern for local authorities, and commuters are advised to exercise caution throughout the duration of the alert.

According to Met Eireann, the warning applies to counties Cavan, Donegal, and Monaghan, along with all of Connacht. While the fog is forecast to clear gradually throughout Tuesday morning, it may linger in some areas until the afternoon. The cold front is accompanied by near-calm conditions, which have prompted Donegal County Council to schedule gritting for all roads on its winter maintenance list starting at 6am on Tuesday, 3rd November.

Following the clearance of the fog and frost on Tuesday, the forecaster expects the conditions to improve as the day progresses. Residents in eastern and southern coastal areas may experience slightly milder weather compared to the inland regions, but the overall cold trend remains throughout the current 12-hour warning window.
