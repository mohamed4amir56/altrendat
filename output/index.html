Harry Kane exits injured as 400 Köln ultras boycott Bayern away clash

Bayern Munich star Harry Kane was substituted in the 20th minute while 400 visiting fans opted to leave the stadium before kickoff.

The 20th minute marked an early end for Harry Kane, who left the pitch limping after a 14th-minute collision with Köln defender Joel Schmied. According to SPORT1, the striker received treatment on his ankle from medical staff on the field before needing assistance from two caregivers to walk off, with Tom Bischof replacing him in the lineup. This departure leaves Bayern without their forward for the remainder of the league fixture.

Simultaneously, the atmosphere outside the stadium turned turbulent as 400 organized Köln ultras decided to boycott the match and depart before the opening whistle. While roughly 7,200 Köln fans traveled to Munich, this specific group protested against restricted fan items, according to BILD. Specifically, officials limited the number of large flags to five, a restriction the fan scene deemed unacceptable. Despite discussions between the clubs, BILD reports that authorities left no room to negotiate these regulations.

While the ultras began their journey home, the remaining thousands of Köln supporters stayed inside the arena to watch the game. The team is now tasked with navigating the contest against the Bundesliga giants without the vocal backing of their organized fan base. The situation highlights the ongoing friction between traveling supporters and the strict stadium security policies imposed by local authorities. As the match continues, all eyes turn to the severity of Kane’s ankle injury and the potential aftermath of the protest by the visiting fans regarding fan equipment allowances.
