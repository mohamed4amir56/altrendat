Ciaron Maher trainer routs Melbourne Cup as Point of Law wins easily

Point of Law, formerly owned by King Charles III, won the 3200m Melbourne Cup under jockey Ethan Brown at Flemington.

Trainer Ciaron Maher achieved a historic feat by producing the top three finishers in the A$10.2 million Lexus Melbourne Cup. While stablemate Royal Supremacy surged to a massive 12-length lead during the middle stages of the 3200m race, jockey Ethan Brown remained patient in the chasing pack before Point of Law roared through on the inside to secure a victory by nearly four lengths. The runner-up and third-place horses, Zahraan and Juja Kibo, completed a dominant podium sweep for the Maher stable.

This victory holds significant intrigue as Point of Law was previously bred and raced by King Charles III and Queen Camilla. Royal policy barred the King from maintaining ownership alongside the current syndicate, leading to a sale in July estimated between A$2 million and A$3 million. The new owners, including former AFL player Chris Judd and his wife Bec, saw their investment pay off immediately as the three-year-old thoroughbred proved his status as a budding superstar following his previous win in the Geelong Cup.

Jockey Ethan Brown, who returned to the sport after a serious injury left him hospitalized in 2023, described the win as every jockey's dream. The race day at Flemington attracted tens of thousands of spectators under sunny conditions, featuring a pre-race performance by Robbie Williams. The event was not without incident, as the horse Asterix was scratched on race day after a stable worker administered forbidden medication for muscle soreness. Despite these complications, the prestigious event proceeded to crown a clear winner in front of a massive crowd.
