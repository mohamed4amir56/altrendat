Seven South African provinces under thunderstorm warning as heavy downpours hit KwaZulu-Natal

Disaster management teams in KwaZulu-Natal are on high alert Sunday following a yellow level 4 weather warning for damaging winds and heavy rain.

The region is bracing for the continuation of heavy downpours, damaging winds, hail, and severe lightning throughout the day.

This level 4 warning specifically targets the northern and central parts of KwaZulu-Natal. Officials are highlighting the potential for widespread damage as these weather patterns persist.

MEC for COGTA, Thulasizwe Buthelezi, issued a direct call for vigilance among motorists and residents alike, citing that recent rains have already caused significant devastation in various parts of the province. The South African Weather Service reports that these conditions are expected to remain a threat for the duration of Sunday.

The alert remains active as authorities monitor the impact of the storm systems across the province. With disaster management teams deployed and on standby, officials continue to urge the public to monitor official updates and avoid high-risk environments until the weather stabilizes.

Pep Biel seals historic 2-1 Charlotte playoff win over Chicago Fire

Charlotte FC secured its first regulation postseason victory in club history by defeating the Chicago Fire 2-1 on Sunday.

The dramatic finish came just moments after goalkeeper Josh Cohen entered the match specifically to defend a potential penalty shootout, only to concede the deciding goal.

While the fourth-seeded Fire entered the playoffs with a higher conference standing, the loss hands Charlotte the advantage in the opening round. The victory also highlights Charlotte’s persistent offensive threat, having finished the regular season with 66 total goals.

Liel Abada opened the scoring for Charlotte three minutes into the second half, heading in an assist from Henry Kessler. The Fire responded in the 68th minute when Robert Lewandowski leveled the score with a header of his own. Despite the efforts of goalkeeper Chris Brady, who recorded two saves, the Fire defense could not withstand the late pressure. Charlotte goalkeeper Kristijan Kahlina anchored the defensive effort by stopping four shots throughout the match.

The two clubs will face each other again this Saturday for the second match of the series. The Fire are set to travel to Charlotte as they attempt to stay alive in the best-of-three matchup.
