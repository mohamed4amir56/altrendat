Arteta emphasized that he maintained close communication with various national team managers to monitor player health, noting that the return of key defensive personnel will be vital as Arsenal attempts to stifle a much-improved Leeds side that has found early-season form.

Other drivers, including Liam Lawson, Ollie Bearman, and Nico Hulkenberg, moved up the field to take advantage of the late-race incident. As the track dried out, teams faced difficult choices regarding tyre wear, with Charles Leclerc noting his intermediate tyres were heavily degraded before the finish. Qualifying for the Singapore Grand Prix is scheduled to follow these results as the racing weekend at the Marina Bay Street Circuit continues.
