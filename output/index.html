Cowboys send two picks to Steelers for Porter Jr., then watch him roll ankle in practice

The Dallas Cowboys traded a 2027 sixth-round pick and 2028 second-round pick to the Pittsburgh Steelers for cornerback Joey Porter Jr., who debuted October 4 but suffered an ankle injury in practice and is now questionable for Week 6.

The Cowboys made a significant trade earlier this month, sending two draft picks to Pittsburgh to acquire cornerback Joey Porter Jr., a former Penn State star who had refused to play for the Steelers without a new contract. In exchange, Dallas shipped a 2027 sixth-round pick and a 2028 second-round pick to complete the deal.

Porter made his Cowboys debut on October 4 against the Texans but has since faced multiple setbacks. He missed the Week 5 game against the Buccaneers due to illness, and on Saturday, NFL insider Ed Werder reported that Porter rolled his ankle during practice earlier that week. He is currently listed as questionable for Week 6's "Sunday Night Football" matchup against the Packers.

Werder noted that Porter is hoping to play and stated that his game status has nothing to do with his contract situation. However, Cowboys fans have expressed skepticism on social media, with many speculating that Porter's repeated absences are contract-related rather than health-related. Dallas will need Porter available and healthy if the team hopes to contain the Packers' receivers.

GTA 6 hits PC just 4 months after console launch, Rockstar's fastest turnaround yet

GTA 6 will release on PC in the first quarter of 2027, about four months after its November 19, 2026 console debut on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, according to GamesBeat's report citing four industry sources.

Rockstar Games is planning to bring GTA 6 to PC far faster than it did for previous titles. The Q1 2027 window marks a dramatic shift from GTA 5's strategy, which spent 19 months locked to consoles before reaching PC in April 2015. This accelerated timeline suggests Rockstar wants to establish the game on PC before emulation technology advances further, as PS5 emulators have already reached playable performance levels for other titles.

The move matters because GTA has a massive active player base on PC, driven by GTA Online and role-play servers like FiveM. Rockstar acquired FiveM's developer, Cfx.re, in August 2023, signaling commitment to the PC community. For players, faster parity between console and PC versions means less fragmentation in the game's online ecosystem.

GamesBeat's Alexander Lee reported on October 13 that four sources familiar with Rockstar's plans confirmed the Q1 2027 release window. Rockstar and Take-Two have not publicly commented on the report. The PC version is also expected to launch alongside or near GTA 6's multiplayer mode, which industry figures have referred to as GTA Online 2, though Rockstar has yet to officially announce or name the online component. Take-Two boss Strauss Zelnick has consistently stated that Rockstar "always starts on console" to serve core consumers first, but acknowledged the industry is "moving towards PC."

الهلال يستقبل الحزم غدًا بحضور إنزاغي وثقة في الحل الهجومي

فريق الهلال يختتم تحضيراته لمواجهة الحزم غدًا الاثنين الساعة 9 مساءً في ملعب الجزيرة بالرياض ضمن الجولة العاشرة من دوري روشن السعودي.

اختتم فريق الهلال الأول تحضيراته أمس على ملعب المجيدية تحت قيادة المدرب سيمونه إنزاغي، وركزت الجلسة التدريبية الأخيرة على الجوانب التكتيكية وتطبيق تدريبات تقنية متعددة.

أكد إنزاغي خلال مؤتمر صحفي أن الهلال جاهز تمامًا، وقال: "بدأنا التحضير قبل ثلاثة أيام، ننتظر فريقًا منظمًا بقيادة مدرب من الطراز الأول". أشار المدرب إلى أن الفريق يملك حلولًا هجومية متعددة، وأثنى على مستويات سلطان مندش وعودة قائد الفريق سالم الدوسري، كما امتدح أداء ثنائي محمد مايت ونواف الحبشي في التدريبات الجماعية.

بخصوص الدوسري، أوضح إنزاغي أنه كان في حالة جيدة لكن إصابة منعته من المشاركة في المباراة السابقة، وهو الآن يشارك في التدريبات الجماعية، وسيتم تحديد القائمة بعد جلسة اليوم. أكد المدرب أن الفريق يمتلك مجموعة كاملة من اللاعبين القادرين على إضافة قيمة حقيقية.

المواجهة ستكون اختبارًا في الجولة العاشرة من الدوري، حيث يسعى الهلال لمواصلة مسيرته أمام ضيف يتمتع بتنظيم عالي.
