South Africa and Australia meet in the third Test at Newlands starting October 27 with the series level at 1-1.

The Proteas secured that win despite a morning session washout and the loss of frontline bowler Anrich Nortje to a groin injury during his fourth over. Australia, meanwhile, looks to regain momentum after suffering a 214-run defeat in the second Test, having previously dominated the series opener in Durban. With the series tied, the final Test serves as a direct showdown to determine the winner of the contest.

This match carries significant weight for both squads, as the outcome will settle a hard-fought series that has seen both teams secure decisive wins. For the hosts, the challenge lies in replacing the injured Nortje, with Gerald Coetzee, Corbin Bosch, Dane Paterson, and Keshav Maharaj listed as potential options to bolster the attack. Australia faces its own personnel questions, including a late fitness test for Steve Smith regarding a neck issue and a decision on whether to rotate between bowler Nathan Lyon and Scott Boland.

According to Yahoo Sports, play at Newlands Cricket Ground is scheduled to commence at 10:30 a.m. local time. South Africa has confirmed its lineup, featuring captain Temba Bavuma and key contributors from the second Test, including Marco Jansen.
