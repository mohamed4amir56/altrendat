Ontario Minimum Wage Climbs to 17.95 Dollars Amid Rising Cost Concerns

The provincial minimum wage increased by 35 cents to $17.95 per hour starting Thursday to match the Consumer Price Index.

A full-time worker logging 40 hours per week will see their annual earnings grow by roughly $728 following this adjustment. The increase impacts more than 700,000 employees throughout the province as the government attempts to keep pace with global economic uncertainty.

This mandatory annual change, required under the Employment Standards Act, ties pay growth to Ontario’s Consumer Price Index, which currently sits at 1.9 per cent. Labour Minister Trevor Jones stated the move aims to provide a predictable environment for businesses while supporting workers facing higher living costs. The adjustment is part of a broader trend across Canada, where five provinces have recently lifted their hourly rates to combat inflation.

Despite the rise, the Ontario Living Wage Network argues the new rate remains insufficient for basic needs. Executive director Anne Coleman noted that in the Greater Toronto Area, a living wage requires $27.20 an hour to cover essential bills. Her organization calculates these figures based on actual community costs, highlighting a gap of $9.25 between the new legal minimum and what is deemed necessary for workers to make ends meet.

While Ontario updates its figures, other provinces have also enacted similar hikes. Prince Edward Island now sits at $17.30, Nova Scotia at $17, Manitoba at $16.40, and Saskatchewan at $15.70. Alberta remains the only province that has not updated its minimum wage since 2019, maintaining the lowest rate in the country at $15 per hour.

    Kenya Power imposes dumping surcharges on unauthorized residential solar energy grid connections

    The Energy and Petroleum Regulatory Authority has introduced new fines for homes and businesses feeding excess solar electricity into the grid without approval.

    Wind and solar power accounted for 34 per cent of Kenya’s energy mix during daytime peak demand on August 11, 2026. This reliance on renewable sources has driven regulators to impose stricter oversight on how private systems interact with the national electricity network. Homes and businesses that feed excess solar energy into the Kenya Power network without a valid net-metering agreement or prior authorization now face a formal dumping surcharge. Under the updated 2023 tariff schedule, any electricity exported to the grid without approval is treated as dumping and will be billed at the applicable base tariff. Kenya Power stated these measures are necessary to protect its distribution infrastructure, maintain grid stability, and prevent safety hazards for maintenance staff. Fluctuations in renewable energy generation can impact grid frequency and voltage, which forces the utility company to activate other generators to balance the system. The Energy (Net-Metering) Regulations, 2024, provide a formal framework for consumers with systems up to one megawatt to export surplus power, provided they enter into a legal net-metering agreement. The regulator and Kenya Power retain the authority to disconnect any solar system that threatens the safety, reliability, or security of the distribution network. Moving forward, homeowners and business operators must ensure all connections comply with these established technical and regulatory standards to avoid penalties and potential disconnection from the national supply.

    Starting October 1, Australia’s Reserve Bank has banned checkout surcharges on card payments, a move aimed at saving consumers $1.6 billion annually.

This nationwide reform enforced by the Reserve Bank of Australia covers transactions made on Visa, Mastercard, eftpos, and American Express, effectively eliminating surprise fees at the point of sale.

    While the elimination of surcharges provides price transparency for consumers, the change is forcing a widespread reshuffling of credit card economics. Because the Reserve Bank is simultaneously lowering the cap on interchange fees that stores pay to banks, financial institutions are aggressively looking to recoup lost revenue. Financial literacy expert Nicole Pedersen-McKinnon warns that this has triggered a massive clawback of card perks, including reduced earn rates for frequent flyer points and the curbing of complimentary services like travel insurance.

    For businesses, the transition presents a different challenge. Many cafe owners and merchants, faced with the inability to pass on individual processing fees, are opting to increase base prices to cover their overheads. For instance, some businesses report that rising supplier costs and staff wage increases, combined with the new regulations, have prompted immediate price hikes on everyday items. Experts note that consumers must now pay closer attention to notices from their banks regarding changes to annual fees and interest rates.

    The Reserve Bank anticipates that the policy will eventually lead to lower overall costs for shoppers as reduced merchant fees filter through the economy. Canstar data insights director Sally Tindall emphasized that the policy aims to ensure shelf prices match the final amount paid at checkout. Customers are advised to monitor official communications from their banks, as providers are required to disclose any significant adjustments to card products.

          حالة طارئة على متن طائرة طيران الإمارات تجبرها على الهبوط بفرانكفورت

          اضطرت رحلة طيران الإمارات EK31 المتجهة من دبي إلى لندن للهبوط الاضطراري في فرانكفورت بسبب حالة طبية لأحد الركاب.

          بعد مرور ست ساعات من التحليق على ارتفاع 40 ألف قدم، أطلق طاقم قيادة رحلة طيران الإمارات EK31 نداء استغاثة دولي عبر رمز "Squawk 7700"، لتبدأ الطائرة من طراز إيرباص A380-800 رحلة هبوط سريع ومفاجئ نحو مطار فرانكفورت بدلاً من وجهتها الأصلية في مطار هيثرو بلندن. كانت الطائرة قد غادرت دبي في رحلة مقررة تستغرق 7 ساعات وعشرين دقيقة، لتتحول الأجواء الهادئة فوق أوروبا إلى حالة تأهب قصوى استجابةً لظرف طارئ واجه أحد المسافرين على متنها.

          تأتي أهمية هذا الحدث في كونه يمس مباشرة سلامة المسافرين وتغيير مسار الرحلات الدولية، حيث يضع الأولوية القصوى لتقديم الرعاية الطبية الفورية للحالات الحرجة وسط الرحلة. أكد مكتب دبي الإعلامي أن طاقم الضيافة قدم المساعدة الأولية للمسافر المعني طوال فترة التحليق، بينما كان الفريق الطبي في مطار فرانكفورت على أهبة الاستعداد فور هبوط الطائرة بسلام ليتسنى للراكب إكمال تلقي العناية اللازمة.

          وفقاً لبيانات "airlive"، قامت شركة طيران الإمارات بتنسيق رحلة تعويضية لضمان استمرار الرحلة إلى وجهتها النهائية في لندن، مع إعادة حجز رحلات الربط المتأثرة للركاب. وبحسب وكالة الأناضول و"تايمز أوف إنديا"، فقد تم تحديد موعد استئناف الرحلة عند الساعة السادسة مساءً بالتوقيت المحلي، في وقت ساد فيه الترقب بعد يوم واحد من حادثة أخرى شهدت هبوط طائرة تابعة لـ"فلاي دبي" في مطار الأمير سلطان بن عبدالعزيز بتبوك في السعودية.
