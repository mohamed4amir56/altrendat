Dua Lipa, Tame Impala, SZA, Daddy Yankee Clinch 2027 Coachella Festival Headlining Slots

Coachella returns to Indio for two weekends in April 2027 with a stacked lineup featuring four top-billed artists and a new sale of passes.

The Coachella Valley Music and Arts Festival returns to the Empire Polo Club in Indio, California, with a star-studded lineup headlined by Dua Lipa, Tame Impala, SZA, and a special production by Daddy Yankee. Festival organizers officially confirmed the schedule following weeks of speculation, setting the stage for two identical weekends of performances on April 9-11 and April 16-18, 2027. The official social media accounts described the upcoming event as "The return to innocence."

For music fans and those planning to attend the desert gathering, the festival provides a chance to catch major global stars alongside specialized acts. The diverse bill includes legendary pop star Madonna in the dance tent, indie folk artist Phoebe Bridgers, rock legends Weezer, and a performance by the French electronic duo Justice. The announcement confirms that YouTube will continue its long-standing partnership, serving as the exclusive livestream host for the 15th consecutive year, ensuring global access to the performances.

According to the Los Angeles Times, the 2027 festival bill builds on recent years, featuring a mix of established superstars and niche acts like the French Canadian duo Angine de Poitrine and the punk band the Damned. The festival previously sold out its initial advance passes in May, but the official announcement brings a new opportunity for fans to secure tickets. Further details noted in the lineup poster include the debut of the "Bunker" stage experience, which will feature a set from Justice.

Prospective attendees can purchase remaining tickets beginning November 13 at 12 p.m. PT. Available options for the November sale include general admission bundles with shuttle passes for $779, alongside VIP passes priced at $1399 for the first weekend and $1349 for the second.
