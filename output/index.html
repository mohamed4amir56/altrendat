The pressure was not limited to U.S. exchanges, as the S&P/TSX composite index in Canada also slipped into correction territory after a 2.47% single-day decline. With the Nasdaq having erased all of its gains accumulated throughout 2026, market participants are now bracing for continued volatility as the reality of the AI-led rally faces its most severe test to date.
