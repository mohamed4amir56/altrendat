Taylor Swift returns to CMA stage with mashup after 13-year absence

Taylor Swift performed at the 60th CMA Awards on Wednesday, marking her first time singing at the ceremony since 2013.

Taylor Swift returned to the Country Music Association Awards stage on Wednesday night, performing a mashup that bridged her history with the genre. During her set, she sang "I Knew It, I Knew You"—a track featured in Disney and Pixar's "Toy Story 5"—and her first No. 1 country hit, "Our Song." Before beginning her performance, Swift acknowledged the lengthy break from the show, telling the audience, "It's been a while."

For country music fans and long-time followers of the awards, the performance signals a significant return for the artist, who last graced the CMA stage in 2013 to perform an acoustic version of "Red" with Alison Krauss and Vince Gill. Her presence at the 60th annual ceremony highlights her continued influence in the country music landscape, underscored by her nomination for Single of the Year for her contribution to the "Toy Story 5" soundtrack.

According to Good Morning America, Swift joined a lineup of performers for this year's 60th annual celebration, which featured artists including Ella Langley, Miranda Lambert, Kacey Musgraves, Cody Johnson, Tucker Wetmore, Tim McGraw, Zach Top, and The Red Clay Strays. The event was broadcast live on ABC and Disney+, with streaming availability beginning on Hulu the following day.

As Swift moves forward, her career remains a focal point of industry attention, both for her music and her recent life updates. The singer, who married Kansas City Chiefs tight end Travis Kelce in July, continues to navigate her work across various entertainment sectors while retaining her roots in country music.
