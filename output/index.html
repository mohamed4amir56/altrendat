Munster storms to Champions Cup victory in emotional tribute to Anthony Foley

Munster secured a 33-25 win over Racing 92 at Páirc Uí Chaoimh to kick off their Investec Champions Cup campaign.

Youngster Brian Gleeson epitomized the spirit of the evening, wearing the number 24 jersey to score the opening try in memory of the late Anthony Foley, a decade after his passing. Playing with the intensity of an emotional grudge match, Munster maintained their 100% record at the Cork venue as 24,906 spectators watched them clinch a five-try win. While Racing 92 struck first through an early Antoine Gibert penalty, the hosts quickly regained control, with Shay McCarthy adding a spectacular 40-plus metre score to extend the lead.

For the Munster squad, the victory served as a significant opening statement in their European campaign, even if the performance was marked by occasional turnovers and inconsistent kicking. The match carried immense weight, with the home side honoring the legacy of their former coach and number eight by retiring that jersey for the night. The occasion was further defined by communal tributes, including a rendition of the Shannon RFC anthem and a moment of silence for Lions player Hyron Andrews, who recently passed away.

According to RTE, the winning effort featured scores from Gleeson, McCarthy, Tadhg Beirne, Fineen Wycherley, and Player of the Match Craig Casey. The Irish Times noted that while the team played in fits and starts, their set-pieces remained reliable, supported by strong contributions from Marnus van der Merwe. Racing 92 kept the pressure on throughout the encounter, but they were unable to overcome the gap created by Munster's clinical strike plays and late-game surges. This victory ensures Munster begins their season on positive terms, building momentum as they look ahead to the remainder of the tournament.
