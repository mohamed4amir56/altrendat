KFC jumps 30% bigger on chicken tenders after complaints of dry, scrawny rivals

KFC relaunched larger, crispier chicken tenders this week after a surge in complaints and lost sales to competitors, marking the first step in its biggest menu overhaul in decades.

Over 18 months, a team of chefs developed tenders that are 30 percent bigger and crispier than before, responding to what KFC senior innovation development manager Kathryn Lake called "a high number of complaints about them." The relaunch comes as fast-food rivals like El Jannah, Guzman y Gomez, and Wingstop have chipped into KFC's customer base with their own fried and boneless chicken offerings, spurring customer complaints and a drop-off in sales.

Vanessa Rowed, chief marketing officer at Yum! Brands (which operates KFC in Australia), said the old tenders felt too small and dry. "We didn't have the best tenders in the market," she told the Australian Financial Review. The new version achieves crispiness and juiciness through a change in how they are cooked.

The tenders come at a higher price: a three-pack bundle rose from $9.45 to $10.95, and a five-pack from $12.95 to $14.95. Rowed signaled the overhaul extends beyond tenders. "We're going to set the benchmark here and then we're going to do that with every single product in our portfolio," she said, though she stopped short of confirming changes to KFC's bestselling Original Recipe, developed by Colonel Harland Sanders in 1940.

The shift reflects a broader industry pivot toward chicken as a menu centerpiece across Australian fast food, with chains like Hungry Jack's launching Jack's Fried Chicken in 2021 and McDonald's introducing the McCrispy in 2023.

SASSA flags 169,000 grant beneficiaries as investigations surge in 2026

Social Development Minister Dina Pule reports 169,000 beneficiaries face investigation after missing mandatory grant review deadlines during the 2026/27 financial year.

Nearly half of the 352,000 social grant beneficiaries targeted for review in the 2026/27 financial year have been flagged for further investigation. Minister of Social Development Dina Pule revealed that 169,000 individuals failed to respond to official notices, leaving their grant status in jeopardy as they are now considered potential subjects of fraudulent transactions.

These reviews serve as a vital safeguard for the integrity of social assistance, ensuring that public funds are only directed to those who remain eligible. For beneficiaries, the failure to engage with the 90-day review period risks an immediate suspension of their financial support, which impacts their ability to meet daily needs. While 56,807 grants were initially suspended following the first-quarter review of 105,600 beneficiaries, some individuals have successfully restored their access by meeting the required compliance standards.

According to IOL and The Witness, SASSA conducts these reviews to identify cases where circumstances have changed, payments continue after a death, or residents are unreachable due to outdated contact information. IOL reports that 105,508 reviews were completed in the first quarter, while The Witness notes that 48,793 beneficiaries were confirmed as eligible during that same period. Notably, 5,231 suspended grants were reinstated after beneficiaries finally complied with the review process.

SASSA maintains an annual target of 352,000 reviews for the 2026/27 period. Future efforts will include continued risk analysis to address remaining segments of the beneficiary population, as the agency continues its legal obligation to audit the validity of its grant distribution system.

                                    تضرب حالة من التقلبات الجوية العنيفة أجزاء واسعة من المملكة العربية السعودية اليوم الأربعاء 30 سبتمبر 2026، حيث تشهد مناطق جازان وعسير والباحة ومكة المكرمة والمدينة المنورة وتبوك أمطارًا رعدية تراوحت بين المتوسطة والغزيرة، وفقًا لتقارير المركز الوطني للأرصاد. ولا تتوقف التوقعات عند الأمطار فحسب، بل يمتد التأثير ليشمل تساقط زخات من البرد، ونشاطًا ملحوظًا في الرياح السطحية المثيرة للأتربة والغبار، مما يعزز مخاطر حدوث سيول منقولة في بعض المناطق. بالنسبة لقاطني هذه المناطق، تبرز أهمية متابعة التنبيهات الجوية بدقة، خاصة مع تسجيل انخفاض في مدى الرؤية الأفقية بسبب الرياح والغبار، فضلاً عن تحذيرات المركز الوطني للأرصاد من مخاطر السيول. وفي المدينة المنورة تحديدًا، أصدر المركز إنذارًا خاصًا يستمر حتى الساعة 8:00 مساءً، يشمل احتمالية عالية لهطول أمطار غزيرة ورياح سريعة تؤثر على سير الحركة اليومية. وتتزامن هذه الظروف مع تأثر مناطق أخرى كأبها بضباب كثيف، بينما تشهد الدمام فرصًا لتشكل الضباب أيضًا، في حين تظل مدن أخرى مثل الرياض في حالة استقرار نسبي مع طقس صحو. وتأتي هذه التوقعات نتيجة استمرار تأثير السحب الرعدية على مساحات واسعة من المملكة، مما يستوجب الحذر من التقلبات المفاجئة في درجات الحرارة ونشاط الرياح التي قد تساهم في إثارة العواصف الرملية في المناطق المفتوحة. يظل المركز الوطني للأرصاد المصدر الرئيسي لمتابعة تطورات هذه الحالة الجوية ساعة بساعة، حيث يستمر رصد تأثير السحب والرياح النشطة على كافة الأنحاء المتأثرة حتى استقرار الأجواء.
