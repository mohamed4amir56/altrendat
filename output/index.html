Lotto players chase estimated £2million jackpot after Saturday night double round draw

The National Lottery draw on Saturday, October 31, 2026, featured two separate rounds with a combined estimated jackpot of £2 million.

The winning numbers for the first round were 1, 3, 18, 25, 38, and 43, with 45 as the bonus ball. The second round saw 16, 41, 42, 48, 55, and 56 drawn, accompanied by a bonus ball of 12. For the Thunderball draw, the numbers selected were 1, 4, 6, 26, 36, and the Thunderball 13. These results represent the outcome of the modified Lotto format that splits the jackpot across two rounds to increase the total number of winners and millionaires.

This two-round structure is designed to offer ticket-holders two chances to win a prize with every line played for the same original cost. According to the National Lottery, while this format improves the frequency of winning, it also increases the likelihood that a jackpot prize will be shared between multiple winners should more than one person match all six numbers in either of the two rounds. The Thunderball draw remains a separate game offering a top prize of £500,000.

Players must purchase their tickets by 7:30pm on the days the draws occur, which are Wednesdays and Saturdays for the main Lotto game. The Thunderball draw occurs more frequently, taking place on Tuesdays, Wednesdays, Fridays, and Saturdays. Beyond the prize pools, the lottery continues to function as a significant fundraiser for various good causes. Ticket sales for these draws remain open throughout the week, though enthusiasts often track the results immediately following the 8pm drawing time.

Ryan Naderi 83rd-minute strike sends Rangers into Premier Sports Cup final

Rangers secured a 1-0 victory over Aberdeen at Hampden Park on Saturday to advance to the Premier Sports Cup final.

The breakthrough arrived in the 83rd minute when Ryan Naderi redirected a strike from James Penrice into the net. This decisive moment marked Naderi’s eighth goal of the season and finally dismantled a stubborn Aberdeen defence that had held firm for the vast majority of the contest. Following the match, the 23-year-old Czechia international joked about his goal-scoring positioning, noting he opted to stay inside the box after failing with long-range efforts earlier in the game.

For Rangers, the victory maintains their momentum as they remain unbeaten in 10 matches. The win carries significant weight as the club now looks toward a major trophy opportunity, having successfully navigated a semi-final that remained deadlocked for over 80 minutes despite relentless pressure from the favourites.

According to The Guardian, Aberdeen entered the match with a makeshift lineup due to five defenders being sidelined through injury or suspension. Despite the defensive adjustments, Aberdeen’s Nesta Guinness-Walker forced a save from goalkeeper Ivor Pandur in the 23rd minute, and Kevin Nisbet narrowly headed wide. The match also saw an injury setback for Aberdeen when Emmanuel Gyamfi was forced off following a tackle on Kim Min-su, requiring a substitution by Stuart Armstrong.

Rangers will face the winner of Sunday’s other semi-final matchup between Hearts and Hibernian to determine the final trophy destination. The club awaits the conclusion of that fixture to confirm their opponent for the upcoming final.
