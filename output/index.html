Breaking Bad star RJ Mitte dies at age 34 in Texas

The actor who portrayed Walter White Jr. in the hit television series passed away on Monday in Brownsville.

Born in 1992 in Jackson, Mississippi, RJ Mitte gained international fame for his portrayal of Walter White Jr., the son of a chemistry teacher turned drug kingpin, in the television series Breaking Bad. Appearing in 56 episodes between 2008 and 2013, Mitte brought personal authenticity to his performance as he lived with cerebral palsy, the same condition as his iconic character. Beyond his acting career, he was a dedicated philanthropist and activist who campaigned for the rights of people with disabilities, working to ensure that such figures were defined by their personality and strength rather than their limitations.

The loss of the actor is felt deeply by fans and the entertainment industry alike, as confirmed in a statement from his management team to outlets including Variety and Deadline. While the management confirmed the death on Monday, the official circumstances are currently under investigation by authorities. Reports from TMZ indicate that the actor may have died by suicide, though definitive information regarding his passing remains limited as the investigation proceeds.

Mitte's personal life had recently reached a significant milestone when he proposed to his longtime partner, Kennedy Suarez, during a charity gala in San Diego in October 2025. In one of his final public appearances just days before his death, Mitte spoke on a podcast about his early career struggles in Hollywood, noting that his first audition was for an anti-drug commercial, a ironic precursor to his career-defining role in a show focused on the drug trade.

Coventry City Routs Newcastle 4-1 to Clinch First Premier League Home Win

Coventry City climbed to 14th place in the Premier League after a 4-1 victory against Newcastle United at the CBS Arena.

Coventry City ended a 25-year drought with a resounding 4-1 win over Newcastle United, marking their first top-flight home victory since their return to the Premier League. The breakthrough came just six minutes into the match when Jack Rudoni converted a penalty after a foul on Sidiki Cherif, sparking a dominant performance that lifted Frank Lampard’s side out of the relegation zone.

This result provides a critical foundation for Coventry, who now sit level on points with Manchester United after six matches. For a squad that struggled in opening games against top-tier opponents like Arsenal and Manchester City, securing successive wins—following an away victory at Nottingham Forest—establishes necessary momentum. Manager Frank Lampard noted that the performance signals the rapid development of players who previously lacked top-flight experience.

The match saw Cherif net his first goal for the club, with Ellis Simms and Stephen Mfuni adding late strikes to secure the scoreline. According to BBC, this was the first time Coventry scored four goals in a top-flight home match since May 2000. While the home crowd had previously endured losses to Hull City and Brighton at the CBS Arena, the win against Newcastle effectively resets the club's trajectory for the remainder of the year.

Looking ahead, Coventry faces a challenging stretch of seven games as they aim to solidify their place in the division. The team prepares for upcoming fixtures against Tottenham and Fulham before playing Sunderland, Everton, Crystal Palace, Leeds, and Ipswich leading into December. Lampard emphasized a need for balance, urging his players to remain humble as they look to build on the confidence gained from this result.

Cristiano Ronaldo Nets 981st Career Goal as Al-Nassr Routs Neftchi Fergana 3-1

Cristiano Ronaldo scored in Al-Nassr’s 3-1 victory over Uzbekistan’s Neftchi Fergana on Monday after a missile-related security delay forced a one-hour kickoff postponement in Riyadh.

Riyadh residents received emergency phone alerts warning of a hostile aerial threat shortly before the scheduled match start, forcing spectators to remain in their seats as the Saudi-led military coalition intercepted and destroyed an incoming ballistic missile. The kickoff at Alawwal Park, originally set for 9:15pm, was delayed by one hour, with the match finally beginning at 10:15pm Mecca time.

Despite the external security concerns, Al-Nassr secured a victory that keeps their campaign active in the AFC Champions League Elite. The win remains a key result for the Saudi side as they navigate the tournament following recent escalations in regional security.

According to The Guardian, the stadium announcer confirmed the situation was safe before play commenced. Cristiano Ronaldo scored his 981st career goal just before half-time, marking his first strike in this season’s AFC Champions League Elite competition. Goal.com reports that the 41-year-old forward hammered a powerful strike from inside the penalty area to make it 2-0 in the 42nd minute. The intensity spiked shortly after when the visitors had a goal ruled out following a video assistant referee review, followed by an on-pitch altercation between Al-Nassr defender Inigo Martinez and a Neftchi player that resulted in yellow cards for both.

With this performance, Ronaldo has reached five goals across all competitions this season. He now stands 19 goals away from his stated career milestone of 1,000 goals.

ترامب يتصل بولي العهد عقب الهجمات الحوثية الدموية على مطار الرياض

كشف الرئيس الأمريكي دونالد ترامب عن اتصال هاتفي مع الأمير محمد بن سلمان، في أعقاب هجمات حوثية دامية استهدفت مطار الرياض.

أكد الرئيس الأمريكي دونالد ترامب، في تصريحات أوردتها شبكة سي إن إن، أنه أجرى اتصالاً هاتفياً مع ولي العهد السعودي الأمير محمد بن سلمان، وذلك في أعقاب سلسلة من الهجمات التي شنها المتمردون الحوثيون على مطار الرياض. ورداً على سؤال صحفي حول إجراء المكالمة، اكتفى ترامب بالتأكيد بكلمة "نعم" دون الخوض في تفاصيل المباحثات، واصفاً سلوك الحوثيين بـ "غير المقبول"، ومؤكداً أن الولايات المتحدة ستدرس خيارات الانضمام للسعودية في مواجهة التهديد الحوثي المدعوم من إيران.

تأتي هذه التحركات الدبلوماسية في وقت تعاني فيه المملكة من تصعيد عسكري غير مسبوق؛ حيث أعلنت السلطات السعودية عن اعتراض وتدمير ثلاثة صواريخ باليستية يوم الاثنين. وقد شهد مطار الملك خالد الدولي بالرياض اعتداءً دموياً يوم السبت 10 أكتوبر، أسفر وفقاً للهيئة العامة للطيران المدني عن مقتل 12 شخصاً، بينهم 4 مواطنين ومواطن أمريكي، وإصابة 309 آخرين من جنسيات متعددة، في حصيلة تعد الأكثر دموية للمملكة منذ أكثر من عقد.

وفي سياق متصل، شددت الولايات المتحدة في جلسة مجلس الأمن الدولي المنعقدة يوم الجمعة 9 أكتوبر على ضرورة وقف الميليشيا الحوثية لهجماتها، مطالبة إيران بوقف كافة أشكال الدعم المقدم لهم. وأكد السفير الأمريكي لدى الأمم المتحدة جيف بارتوس، في كلمته، وقوف واشنطن بحزم إلى جانب المملكة في مواجهة تهديدات الملاحة والاستقرار الإقليمي. من جانبه، أكد مندوب المملكة لدى الأمم المتحدة، عبدالعزيز الواصل، أن الرياض تحتفظ بحقها الكامل في الدفاع عن النفس.

تأتي هذه الأحداث وسط تحذيرات أممية من عودة البلاد فعلياً إلى حالة "حرب شاملة"، حيث دعا المبعوث الأممي هانس غروندبرغ إلى ضرورة دعم وساطة عاجلة لوقف التصعيد الذي طال المطارات والمنشآت المدنية.
