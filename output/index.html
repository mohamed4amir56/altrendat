Tom Cruise transforms into oil baron in Alejandro González Iñárritu's 'weirdest' satirical comedy

Tom Cruise plays Digger Rockwell, a bizarre Texan oil tycoon in director Alejandro González Iñárritu's divisive eco-satire that marks the biggest creative risk of Cruise's career.

Tom Cruise appears unrecognizable in Digger, hidden beneath prosthetic makeup as Digger Rockwell, a balding, greying, paunchy oil baron—a stark departure from his typical athletic, ageless hero roles. The BBC describes it as the "weirdest film" Cruise has ever made, one where he risks being called pretentious and self-indulgent rather than breaking bones on set.

Directed by Alejandro González Iñárritu, the Mexican filmmaker behind Birdman and The Revenant, Digger functions as an eco-satire that explores self-delusion and the blurred line between art and artist. According to IndieWire, it represents the third chapter in Iñárritu's loose trilogy of postmodern films examining creativity when reality has become stranger than fiction. The film is conceptually challenging and deliberately bizarre, making it unlikely to appeal to mainstream audiences expecting another Mission: Impossible installment.

Rockwell's character lives in a historic mansion filled with stuffed exotic animals—a Victorian natural history museum aesthetic—yet worries obsessively about his scrawny pet cat. He is deeply concerned with money above all else, caring least about the environment. His half-sister, a green-energy entrepreneur played by Sandra Hüller, appears underused in the narrative. The BBC notes that nothing in Digger is subtle; most viewers won't attend, and some who do may leave halfway through. Yet this divisiveness doesn't signal failure—it signals a film content to be exactly what it is: hectoring, challenging, and entirely uninterested in conventional entertainment.

الترند: x2 roller coaster

Six Flags closes X2 roller coaster permanently after years of reported injuries and deaths

Six Flags Magic Mountain has permanently closed the X2 roller coaster following a history of injuries and deaths, with the park announcing the decision on Tuesday after a temporary closure in July.

The X2 at Six Flags Magic Mountain in Valencia, California, will not operate again. The ride's seats spin riders 360 degrees forward and backward while positioned on the side of the track, leaving their feet dangling as they plunge headfirst toward the ground.

The closure follows multiple lawsuits from guests who suffered brain injuries after riding X2. Christopher Hawley, 22, died in 2022 shortly after riding the coaster when he lost consciousness after disembarking. Hilda Farias developed a brain bleed that led to her death after riding in 2010, and at least two other riders filed suits claiming traumatic brain injuries in 2020 and 2021, according to court records.

Brian Oerding, Six Flags Magic Mountain president, said in a statement: "While X2 consistently passed a multitude of safety tests, we have decided to close the ride because we believe it's the right thing to do. Ride safety is a cornerstone of our business, and when we see guest confidence affected, we take it seriously." The park temporarily closed X2 in July following reports of back-to-back brain injuries before announcing the permanent shutdown.

X2 opened as X in 2002 but was closed months later to repair design flaws. The ride was updated and reopened as X2 in 2008 with lighter trains following another closure in 2007. It had become one of Magic Mountain's main attractions and was noted by ride enthusiasts for its distinctive design allowing riders' seats to spin independently from the track.

Social Security COLA for 2027 forecast at 3.5%, largest increase in four years

The Social Security Administration will announce the 2027 cost-of-living adjustment on October 14, with forecasts predicting a 3.5% increase—the largest in four years—that would raise average monthly benefits by about $67.90.

The Senior Citizen League predicts a 3.5% cost-of-living adjustment for 2027, up from 2.8% in 2026 and 2.5% in 2025. For the average Social Security recipient currently receiving $1,940 monthly, this would mean an increase of $67.90, bringing the average benefit to $2,007.

But the larger raise comes with a significant caveat: inflation is driving the increase, and that same inflation erodes much of the gain for seniors. According to TSCL Executive Director Shannon Benton, "The reality is that older Americans allocate their budgets differently than people still in the workforce, so inflation hits them differently." Social Security benefits have lost 13.7% of their buying power over the past 10 years, according to the Senior Citizens League, because the COLA formula is based on the Consumer Price Index for Urban Wage Earners and Clerical Workers, which measures how workers spend, not retirees.

Additionally, seniors enrolled in Medicare Part B will see some of their COLA gains offset by premium increases. In 2026, the standard monthly Part B premium rose by $17.90. The exact 2027 Part B increase is not yet known, but any rise will reduce the net benefit of the COLA.

The Social Security Administration will announce the official 2027 COLA on October 14, following the Bureau of Labor Statistics release of September's Consumer Price Index. If the 3.5% prediction holds, it would rank 19th among COLA increases since 1977, when Social Security began calculating the annual adjustment.

مدينة ألاماتا تبعد نحو 120 كيلومترًا جنوب مكيلي عاصمة إقليم تيغراي، وتتمتع بأهمية استراتيجية كبيرة لأنها تقع بالقرب من الحدود الجنوبية للإقليم وتعمل كمركز لتوزيع الإمدادات والوقود والغذاء. استعادة القوات الفيدرالية لها قد تمهد الطريق لها للتقدم شمالًا نحو العاصمة الإقليمية.

القتال الجديد يعني عودة النزاع بعد اتفاق سلام وقعت عليه الأطراف في نوفمبر 2022، وينهي فترة جديدة من الهدوء النسبي. الحرب السابقة من 2020 إلى 2022 أسفرت عن مئات الآلاف من القتلى وملايين النازحين، لذا فإن انهيار الاتفاق يشكل تهديدًا خطيرًا لاستقرار المنطقة.

أعلنت قوة تيغراي السلام، وهي مجموعة مسلحة من المقاتلين التيغراويين المتحالفين مع الحكومة الفيدرالية، السيطرة على ألاماتا يوم الاثنين بالتعاون مع الجيش الوطني. أظهرت مقاطع فيديو تحقق منها وكالة رويترز جنودًا يسيرون في المدينة حاملين الأعلام الإثيوبية. اندلع القتال الجديد الأسبوع الماضي عندما شنت قوات تيغراي المتمردة هجومًا واسعًا واستولت على ثلاث مطارات في الإقليم وتوغلت في مناطق أفار وأمهرة المجاورة. تنتشر الاشتباكات الآن عبر عدة جبهات في شمال إثيوبيا.

مصدر عسكري قال لـ الجزيرة إن القوات الحكومية تقدمت بالفعل وتتجه نحو العاصمة الإقليمية أو تحاصرها. كما قصفت طائرة حربية مقر قناة تيغراي التلفزيونية يوم الأحد.
