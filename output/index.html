Everton faces Newcastle United tonight at Hill Dickinson Stadium in a Carabao Cup fourth-round tie for a chance to play Fleetwood Town.

The path to the EFL quarter-finals is officially open, with the winner of tonight’s clash set to travel to League Two side Fleetwood Town. Fleetwood secured their spot in the next stage by defeating Premier League champions Arsenal on penalties earlier this week.

This match offers a high-stakes opportunity for both clubs. For Newcastle United, the objective is to secure a second trophy in three years, building on their successful 2025 League Cup campaign that ended a 56-year wait for silverware. For Everton, the stakes are historical. The club, which halted its long streak of trophy-hoovering in 1995, is hunting for its first major cup success in 31 years. Supporters are increasingly hopeful as this campaign marks the first significant cup run for the club in quite some time.

According to BBC Sport, the winner of this evening's match will join the other quarter-finalists, including Manchester City, Crystal Palace, and Liverpool. The Guardian reports that kickoff at Hill Dickinson Stadium is scheduled for 7:45pm GMT. Only one team will remain in contention for the title past the conclusion of the match, expected around 10pm.

The quarter-final matches are currently scheduled to take place during the week commencing 14 December, according to official competition details.
