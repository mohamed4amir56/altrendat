Ancelotti shifts Raphinha to centre-forward role for Brazil against Australia

Brazil head coach Carlo Ancelotti will deploy Raphinha as a centre-forward in Tuesday's friendly against Australia, copying the successful tactics Hansi Flick uses at Barcelona where the 29-year-old has scored 14 goals in eight appearances.

Raphinha has already registered 14 goals in just eight appearances for Barcelona this season, with 12 in La Liga and two in the Champions League. Now Carlo Ancelotti wants to replicate that success at international level.

Brazil struggled in their first friendly against Australia, drawing 1-1 and requiring a late strike from youngster Rayan to avoid defeat. Ancelotti has concluded that moving Raphinha from his previous attacking midfielder role into a more central position offers the solution to Brazil's recent attacking struggles.

Ancelotti confirmed tactical changes were coming, stating: "Yes, the team will change a little. Not much, but a little." The shift mirrors the transformation Raphinha underwent at Barcelona following the departures of Robert Lewandowski and Ferran Torres. According to Globo Esporte, Ancelotti has already been drilling a new-look XI with Raphinha leading the line in a 4-3-3 formation. The team will include Otavio in goal, a defence of Vanderson, Jair Cunha, Gabriel Magalhaes, and Mauro Junior, with Danilo, Bruno Guimaraes, and Gabriel Bontempo in midfield, and Rayan and Vinicius flanking Raphinha in attack.

Brazil face Australia again on Tuesday in Brisbane, four days after their first meeting in Townsville. Australia's head coach Tony Popovic acknowledged the challenge of facing an elite team but said his side wants to prove it can maintain a competitive level.

Henry Winkler's 'Chanukah Song' shoutout from Adam Sandler led to Waterboy role

Actor Henry Winkler said a 1994 Saturday Night Live shoutout from Adam Sandler mentioning his Happy Days character led to him calling Sandler, which eventually resulted in Winkler being cast as Coach Klein in the 1998 comedy film The Waterboy without an audition.

When Adam Sandler performed "The Chanukah Song" on Saturday Night Live in 1994, he included a line about Winkler's iconic character: "Guess who eats together at the Carnegie Deli? Bowzer from Sha Na Na and Arthur Fonzarelli." That shoutout prompted Winkler to call Sandler directly to thank him.

The unexpected connection changed Winkler's career path. Years later, when Sandler was casting The Waterboy, he offered Winkler the role of Coach Klein without requiring him to audition. "All of a sudden, I got picked to be the coach. And I didn't have to audition," Winkler told Forbes. "So the man is brilliant."

In The Waterboy, Winkler's college football coach character recruits Sandler's waterboy to play on his struggling team. The 1998 comedy became one of Sandler's most popular films. Winkler noted that college football players have told him they watch the film on team buses heading to games.

During production, Sandler called Winkler at 11:30 p.m. asking for an emergency favor: an actor hadn't shown up. Winkler drove to the park in Los Angeles, received makeup until 3:30 a.m., and filmed the scene. For his unscheduled night work, Winkler said he received a cookie and didn't even get to keep his wardrobe.

    كاليفورنيا تعترف بعيد الفطر والأضحى كأعياد رسمية للدولة

    وقّع حاكم كاليفورنيا قانونًا يعترف بعيدي الفطر والأضحى كأعياد رسمية للدولة، ما يسمح لموظفي الدولة والطلاب باستخدام إجازاتهم لمراعاة المناسبتين.

    أصبحت كاليفورنيا الولاية الثانية في الولايات المتحدة التي تعترف رسميًا بعيدي الفطر والأضحى كأعياد حكومية، بعد أن وقّع حاكمها جافين نيوسوم القانون الاثنين الماضي. عيد الفطر يحتفل بانتهاء شهر رمضان المبارك، وعيد الأضحى يكرّم استعداد سيدنا إبراهيم للتضحية بابنه تقربًا إلى الله.

    يعكس القانون حاجة ملموسة لأكثر من مليون مسلم يعيشون في كاليفورنيا، كانوا مضطرين سابقًا للاختيار بين ممارسة شعائرهم الدينية والالتزام بالدراسة أو العمل. القانون يسمح لموظفي الدولة باستخدام إجازاتهم المتاحة لمراعاة العيدين، وللمدارس الحكومية والكليات المجتمعية بالإغلاق عند الحاجة بموجب اتفاقيات محلية.

    يأتي الاعتراف في وقت حساس؛ فقد وثّقت منظمة الحقوق المدنية الإسلامية زيادة بنسبة 183 في المئة في الحوادث الموجهة ضد المساجس والمراكز الإسلامية من مايو إلى يوليو مقارنة بالفترة ذاتها من العام السابق. قال الرئيس التنفيذي للمنظمة إن الاعتراف الرسمي "يزيل الحواجز الهيكلية" ويرسل رسالة قوية بأن كاليفورنيا تقف مع مجتمعها المسلم.

    بما أن المسلمين يتبعون التقويم الهجري، فإن تاريخا العيدين يتغيران سنويًا. في السنة المقبلة، يتوقع أن يصادف عيد الفطر 10 مارس وعيد الأضحى 17 مايو. للطلاب الحق في الاستشهاد بالقانون الولائي عند غيابهم عن المدرسة لمراعاة العيد.
