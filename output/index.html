The ball struck a spectator directly in the face, leading to the disqualification of the Russian player just a few minutes later. Under tennis regulations, players are strictly barred from hitting a ball in a dangerous or reckless manner, or with negligent disregard for the consequences regarding the safety of officials, ball kids, and spectators. Despite his tendency for explosive behavior during matches, Medvedev remains a popular figure with the media for his humorous and reflective interviews after play concludes. Budget 2027 Ramps Up Help To Buy To €35k For First-Time Buyers

Government leaders have finalized Budget 2027, featuring a €5,000 tax rebate increase, a €10 weekly pension hike, and adjustments to inheritance thresholds.

First-time buyers will see their Help to Buy tax rebate rise by €5,000 to a total of €35,000 as part of the newly finalized financial package. This specific adjustment comes as government coalition leaders concluded crunch talks on Monday night to shift the focus toward workers, following a previous cycle that officials acknowledged left households worse off.

For most citizens, the budget brings immediate changes to social welfare and income tax thresholds. The State pension is set to rise by €10 per week, a figure that also applies to all other core social welfare payments. Additionally, the entry point for the 40% higher rate of income tax will shift from €44,000 to €46,500, a move that is €500 higher than what was initially expected by market analysts and observers.

Beyond basic income adjustments, the government has introduced a new permanent disability payment of €500. Families will receive an additional €6 per week in child support payments, though the standard Children’s Allowance remains unchanged. Those relying on social welfare will also receive a fuel allowance increase of €5 and the customary Christmas bonus. Regarding capital acquisitions, inheritance tax thresholds are rising across all categories, with the parent-to-child limit climbing by €20,000 to reach €420,000.

While the budget aims to alleviate cost-of-living pressures, it notably excludes energy credits, which will not be part of the 2027 package. Finance ministers Jack Chambers and Simon Harris are scheduled to present the formal details to Dáil Éireann on Tuesday afternoon to confirm the public spending and taxation strategy for the coming year.

British Airways jet plunges 28,000 feet before emergency landing at Heathrow

A Chicago-bound flight returned to London Heathrow on Monday after a technical issue forced a rapid descent over Ireland.

Oxygen masks dangled from the cabin ceiling as a Boeing 777-200ER descended from its cruising altitude to below 10,000 feet in a matter of minutes. The sudden drop occurred roughly an hour into the transatlantic journey, prompting pilots to issue a 7700 emergency squawk and abort the flight to Chicago’s O’Hare airport.

This incident highlights the standard emergency protocols required when an aircraft encounters unexpected technical difficulties at high altitude. The immediate descent ensures that cabin pressure is stabilized, protecting the safety of those on board, while the subsequent return to a major hub like Heathrow allows for a controlled resolution of the mechanical alert.

According to The Guardian and the Daily Star, the flight, identified as BA295, departed London Heathrow on Monday. While passengers reported uncertainty as the masks deployed, they noted that the descent itself did not feel overly rapid and praised the crew for their handling of the situation. After declaring the emergency over Ireland, the pilots spent several hours flying in a holding pattern over the Bristol Channel. This maneuver was necessary to burn off sufficient fuel to meet weight requirements for a safe landing. The aircraft eventually landed back at Heathrow just after 5pm.

British Airways confirmed that the aircraft landed safely and that customers disembarked normally. The airline is now coordinating efforts to assist passengers in rescheduling their travel plans to their intended destinations.
