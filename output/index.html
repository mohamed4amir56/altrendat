While an investigation does not confirm fraud or guarantee enforcement, the scrutiny into accounting practices arrives as federal authorities also examine chip export rule compliance and previous leadership changes. The pressure on the company is mounting from Washington. Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent has publicly requested answers from the board and outside counsel within 10 days regarding the internal situation. Nvidia has also delayed its 10-Q filing, opting to release the detailed quarterly report in the coming weeks while an independent board committee conducts its own ongoing investigation.
