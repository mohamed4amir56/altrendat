The federation expressed deep gratitude for his service, noting that he inspired generations and significantly raised the nation's footballing profile. While his international career is over, Ronaldo intends to continue playing at the club level, where he currently pursues his 1,000th career goal with Al-Nassr, having reached 985 goals to date.

Looking ahead, Portugal prepares to announce the squad for upcoming Nations League fixtures. Despite the recent fallout, the federation remains committed to celebrating the player who competed in six European Championships and six World Cups, marking a definitive end to an era that included a European title in 2016.
