While Lewis Hamilton and George Russell successfully locked out the front row, the session proved difficult for other contenders. Oscar Piastri suffered a surprise exit in Q2, leaving the McLaren driver to start 13th, while Liam Lawson was disqualified from his eighth-place qualifying spot due to a technical breach regarding the MGU-K power requirement.

Lewis Hamilton will lead the field on Sunday as the grid prepares for the race, with Kimi Antonelli's potential title clinching performance serving as the weekend's primary focus.
