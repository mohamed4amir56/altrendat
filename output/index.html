Philadelphia hosts 40,000 runners for the half and full marathon this weekend as road closures and Sunday afternoon rain arrive.

The city faces a logistical challenge this weekend as road closures on the Benjamin Franklin Parkway and Eakins Oval coincide with a high volume of visitors. Beyond the race, the Philadelphia Eagles play at home on Sunday afternoon, further complicating travel for those navigating the city. Local businesses are looking toward the influx of runners to bolster holiday market sales. Vendors at Dilworth Park and the Christmas Village in LOVE Park expect a boost from the increased foot traffic as shoppers and spectators congregate downtown. While some residents view the traffic as nightmarish due to ongoing I-95 construction and race-related detours, many runners expressed excitement about the atmosphere.

Despite the impending stormy weather, participants remain undeterred, with many traveling from out of town to experience the course for the first time. The heaviest rainfall is expected to develop during the evening hours on Sunday. Looking ahead to the work week, conditions shift back to dry, breezy, and milder weather on Monday, with another storm system expected to bring wind and rain by Thanksgiving.
