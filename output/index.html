HMD Unveils Nokia 2360 Flip Featuring Dedicated SOS Button and Remote Control

HMD has launched the Nokia 2360 Flip in the Philippines for PHP 2,790, offering 2G connectivity and specialized tools for students and seniors.

A single press of the rear-mounted SOS button initiates an automatic call to a primary emergency contact while simultaneously broadcasting alerts to up to five pre-set numbers. This hardware-integrated safety feature anchors a device designed specifically for school students and senior citizens who require simpler communication tools.

The phone introduces the Family Assist feature, which enables designated relatives to remotely manage the device settings. This allows guardians or family members to add contacts, set daily reminders, and adjust volume levels from afar. These capabilities are intended to ease the burden of technical management for users who may be less comfortable with complex smartphone interfaces.

According to GSMArena and YugaTech, the device sports a 2.8-inch main display, a 1.77-inch external screen, and a removable 1,450mAh battery capable of providing up to 27.5 hours of talk time. Other hardware specifications include a QVGA camera, a 3.5mm headphone jack, 4MB of internal storage, and support for microSD cards up to 32GB. While the phone features Hearing Aid Compatibility, its 2G-only connectivity limits functionality in regions where such networks have already been discontinued.

The Nokia 2360 Flip is currently available in the Philippines via the official Shopee store in Burgundy and Grey color options for PHP 2,790. HMD has not yet provided information regarding broader global availability or pricing beyond the initial Philippine launch.
