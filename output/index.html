Naga City bettor snatches 115.99 million peso 6/45 Mega Lotto jackpot

A lone player from Camarines Sur successfully matched all six numbers in the Friday night 6/45 Mega Lotto draw to claim the top prize.

The winning ticket, which secured a grand total of P115,989,634.14, was purchased at an outlet located in Barangay Triangulo, Naga City. The lucky bettor correctly identified the winning combination of 08-22-19-10-35-27, outperforming a field of players that saw 65 other individuals each walk away with P18,461.53 for matching five of the six drawn numbers. This life-changing win was officially confirmed by the Philippine Charity Sweepstakes Office on Friday evening, following the draw results from the Megalotto 6/45 game.

For regular participants, this outcome highlights the unpredictable nature of the draw and the significant payouts available even when the headline jackpot is claimed by a single person. While one player celebrates their massive windfall, the pool for other active games continues to climb. The Mega Lotto 6/45 draw remains a staple for hopeful players, occurring on a weekly schedule of every Monday, Wednesday, and Friday.

Meanwhile, the jackpot for the 6/58 Ultra Lotto remains unclaimed and continues to grow. During the most recent session, no player managed to guess the combination of 21-12-30-04-36-41, leaving the massive prize pot of P401,335,541.99 untouched. The 6/58 Ultra Lotto draws are held on Tuesdays, Fridays, and Sundays, offering another chance for those looking to compete for the substantial remaining jackpot.

The Philippine Charity Sweepstakes Office continues to oversee these games, with winners advised to verify their tickets at local outlets. As the Ultra Lotto jackpot rolls over, the next scheduled drawings will determine if the current multi-million peso prize finds a new winner or continues to accumulate for future participants.
