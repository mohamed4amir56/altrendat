MLB Proposes 154-Game Regular Season Plus Expanded Seven-Game Division Series Format

Major League Baseball has submitted a proposal to the MLB Players Association to reduce the regular season to 154 games while restructuring the postseason.

Major League Baseball’s latest proposal to the MLB Players Association seeks to overhaul the sport's structure by shortening the regular season to 154 games. This reduction aims to introduce a weekly off-day for players while establishing a new national broadcast window designed to showcase top teams and players, according to MLB spokesman Glen Caplin.

For fans, this change intends to minimize weekday afternoon games and create a platform for longer postseason series. The league suggests that a shorter regular season allows for a more significant October, characterized by an expansion of the Division Series from a best-of-five to a best-of-seven format. By modifying the schedule, the league states it can better feature the game's top pitchers on the postseason stage.

The proposal introduces significant shifts in playoff logistics, specifically regarding how opponents are selected. Under the plan, the top seeds in both the Wild Card and Division Series rounds would be granted the power to choose their opponents. In the Wild Card round, the No. 3 seed would select between the No. 5 or No. 6 seed. For the Division Series, the No. 1 seed would select which Wild Card winner they face after that round concludes. MLB expects this mechanism to amplify fan interest, heighten the importance of regular-season records, and fuel new rivalries.

While the league emphasizes player health and broadcast drama as primary drivers, industry analysis from MLB Trade Rumors suggests the focus remains on long-term financial goals, noting the increased revenue potential of expanded postseason broadcast rights. The proposal remains under consideration by the Players Association, with discussions ongoing regarding the impact on total games and the structure of the postseason calendar.

British Covid cases surge 230 per cent as XFG strain spreads worldwide

The XFG variant, known as the American strain, has driven a sharp increase in UK infections and hospital admissions since August.

Patients diagnosed with the XFG variant frequently present with a characteristic hoarse, croaky voice that distinguishes it from previous iterations of the virus. While other symptoms remain consistent with earlier variants, such as sore throat, cough, fever, and loss of taste or smell, this specific vocal change serves as a common identifier for the strain first identified in January 2025.

Understanding the behavior of XFG is essential for managing the current surge, as health authorities monitor rising transmission rates. The UK Health Security Agency reports that between August and September, infections in Britain climbed from 1,374 to 4,589, a 230 per cent increase. With hospitalizations rising by 15 per cent—and other data points suggesting even higher admission spikes—the strain is now considered the most common in North America and a significant factor in global circulation.

According to the UKHSA, the rise in infections is linked to waning population immunity roughly a year after the previous significant wave. Additionally, the shift toward colder, wetter weather has forced more people indoors, facilitating easier transmission. Experts state that the current Covid-19 vaccine is specifically engineered to target XFG and its related strains to provide protection against severe illness.

To curb the spread, the UKHSA advises individuals experiencing symptoms, such as high temperatures, to remain at home. Those unable to isolate are encouraged to wear well-fitting or surgical face masks in public, avoid crowded spaces, and practice stringent hygiene, including frequent handwashing. Vaccination efforts have currently prioritized those aged 75 and over, residents in care homes, and individuals with weakened immune systems.

    إنذار أحمر: أمطار غزيرة ورياح عاتية تضرب مكة المكرمة اليوم الخميس

    أصدر المركز الوطني للأرصاد إنذارًا أحمر لمحافظتي رهاط ومدركة بمكة المكرمة حتى الثامنة مساءً بسبب الأمطار والرياح.

    تنبيهات شديدة اللهجة أطلقها المركز الوطني للأرصاد في السعودية، تزامناً مع رصد حالة جوية مضطربة تتركز في محافظتي رهاط ومدركة بمنطقة مكة المكرمة. هذه الحالة الجوية المتوقعة، والمصنفة ضمن الإنذار الأحمر، تحمل في طياتها مخاطر متعددة تشمل الرياح شديدة السرعة، وهطول أمطار غزيرة، بالإضافة إلى تساقط البرد، وخطر جريان السيول، مع احتمالية حدوث صواعق رعدية تؤدي إلى انعدام في مدى الرؤية الأفقية.

    تمثل هذه التحذيرات أهمية قصوى لسكان هذه المناطق والمسافرين عبرها، حيث يتوجب أخذ الحيطة والحذر نظراً لتأثيرات الطقس العنيفة على الطرق والأنشطة اليومية. إن توقعات الأرصاد لا تقتصر على الأمطار فقط، بل تمتد لتشمل تقلبات جوية حادة تتطلب متابعة دقيقة للنشرات الرسمية لضمان السلامة العامة خلال ساعات التأثير.

    من ناحية أخرى، شهدت درجات الحرارة في مختلف مدن المملكة تفاوتاً ملحوظاً، حيث سجلت المدينة المنورة والدمام أعلى درجات حرارة عند 41 درجة مئوية، تليها مكة المكرمة بـ 40 درجة مئوية. وفي المقابل، سجلت أبها أدنى درجة حرارة عند 15 درجة مئوية، والباحة 17 درجة مئوية، فيما تراوحت درجات الحرارة في بقية المدن مثل جدة والرياض وجازان وحائل بين معدلاتها المعتادة لهذا الموسم.

    من المقرر أن تستمر الحالة الجوية في محافظتي رهاط ومدركة حتى الساعة الثامنة من مساء اليوم الخميس، وفقاً لما نقله المركز الوطني للأرصاد عبر وكالة الأنباء السعودية "واس". يُنصح المواطنون والمقيمون بالبقاء على اطلاع مستمر بآخر تحديثات الأرصاد الجوية واتباع تعليمات الجهات المختصة في المناطق المتأثرة لتجنب المخاطر الناجمة عن السيول والرياح القوية.
