DC Studios Ousts James Gunn and Peter Safran, Names Jeremy Latcham President

James Gunn and Peter Safran are stepping down as co-CEOs of DC Studios on December 1st, replaced by Jeremy Latcham.

James Gunn described his four-year tenure at the helm of DC Studios as trying, highlighting his dislike for the corporate nature of the executive role. The departure arrives after Gunn and Safran oversaw the formation of a new shared universe that produced titles including Superman, Supergirl, and Clayface. During their leadership, the studio also launched television projects such as Creature Commandos, Peacemaker, and Lanterns. The exit of the pair is described as unexpected, as they were recently included as part of David Ellison’s business leadership team following a major studio merger. Fans who followed the development of the new DCU now look to a significant shift in creative direction as Jeremy Latcham assumes the role of president and chief creative officer. Latcham, a former Marvel executive, takes the reins at a time when the studio has already begun releasing initial projects under the Gods and Monsters slate. For Gunn, the transition marks the end of a period he called a dream come true, citing his childhood experience drawing comics in Manchester, Missouri, as the root of his passion for the characters. Gunn remains attached to the upcoming film Man of Tomorrow, which is scheduled to hit theaters next summer. His final day in the office alongside Safran is confirmed for December 1st.

Two Lucky Winners Split Massive $12.2 Million Toto Jackpot In Singapore

Two winning tickets shared the $12,198,558 Group 1 jackpot following the Toto cascade draw held on Nov 9.

The winning numbers drawn were 5, 27, 29, 38, 40 and 43, with 4 serving as the additional number. Each of the two triumphant ticket holders earned a share worth more than $6 million from the total prize pool, which reached $12,198,558. These winning entries were purchased through the Singapore Pools Account Betting Service, consisting of one QuickPick System 7 entry and one QuickPick Ordinary entry. Beyond the top prize, 12 shares were awarded in Group 2, with each share valued at $115,990. The significant payout followed a series of draws where the jackpot failed to find a winner, causing the funds to snowball from just over $1.1 million on Oct 29. By the time of the third draw on Nov 5, the total had already swelled to more than $5.5 million. The cascade draw process dictates that if no one claims the Group 1 prize after four consecutive draws, the accumulated money is split among winners in the subsequent prize group. This particular event marks the fifth time in 2026 that the jackpot has reached or exceeded $10 million, echoing a similar occurrence in July when two tickets shared a $12.8 million prize.

Ensuring the game is downloaded before the official release date allows players to begin their experience immediately when it becomes available. By pre-ordering through the PlayStation Store or participating retailers, users secure their access and can take advantage of the pre-download window starting at local midnight on November 10. According to Rockstar Games, those new to the PlayStation platform should verify their account information in advance to prevent any delays during the launch window. For those looking to upgrade their setup, accessories such as the DualSense Edge wireless controller and Pulse wireless audio products are available for use with the new game.

Rockstar Games maintains that the full launch for both PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S remains set for November 19. Additional details regarding the game and its features are expected to be shared by the developer as the release date approaches.
