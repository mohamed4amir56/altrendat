Arsenal hosts an unbeaten Leeds United at the Emirates following a three-week international break in the Premier League.

Following a three-week hiatus from top-flight action, Arsenal returns to the pitch with a significantly bolstered squad. This match marks a crucial return for the defending champions, who look to move past a difficult loss against Brighton suffered just before the league pause. The atmosphere surrounding the club remains complex as the broader Premier League continues under the shadow of ongoing investigations into Manchester City.

Squad depth provides the primary storyline for Saturday's kickoff. Rice sat out the entire international break to manage lingering fatigue, a decision Arteta described as essential given the heavy volume of matches the midfielder has played over the last three years.

Not every injury update was positive for the Gunners. While Arteta hopes Kai Havertz and Ezri Konsa will be fit enough to play, the manager confirmed that Christos Tzolis will miss the match and several subsequent fixtures due to a muscle injury. Tzolis sustained the issue during Greece's international fixture against the Netherlands. Arteta emphasized that he maintained close communication with various national team managers to monitor player health, noting that the return of key defensive personnel will be vital as Arsenal attempts to stifle a much-improved Leeds side that has found early-season form.
