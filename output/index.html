Celtic eyes crucial Europa League points against Torreense in Portuguese league showdown

Celtic faces Torreense in the Europa League on Thursday, November 26, 2026, at 8pm in Leiria.

Celtic arrives at the Dr. Magalhães Pessoa Stadium facing a unique European challenge, as they prepare to play a team not currently competing in their country’s top domestic flight. Torreense, a second-division side, secured their place in this competition after a historic cup run that saw them defeat Sporting Lisbon to claim the first major trophy in their 109-year existence. The match is set to take place 60 miles away from the home ground of the Portuguese club because their own facility, with a 2,500-seat capacity, failed to meet mandatory UEFA requirements.

This fixture represents a critical juncture for Celtic as they attempt to mount a comeback in the competition. Currently sitting 32nd in the 36-team league phase, the club is striving to become the first side to reach the knockout stages after losing their opening three matches. Securing points here is vital for manager Martin O’Neill, who must also balance the squad’s workload against upcoming domestic commitments, including a weekend showdown at Tynecastle against Hearts and a clash against Dundee next Wednesday.

Manager Martin O’Neill has confirmed that Kasper Hogh and Haissem Hassan will participate in the match. Meanwhile, the club has bolstered its options by adding Jota to the Europa League squad in place of the injured Mika Baur. While Auston Trusty is expected to return to the fold following his international duty, Jota’s involvement remains a point of interest after his recent recovery from a long-term knee injury. Torreense enters the encounter in 19th position, aiming to build on their six points from the opening four games. The match will be broadcast on TNT Sports 3, with coverage beginning at 7:45pm ahead of the 8pm kick-off.
