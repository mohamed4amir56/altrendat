Kaizer Chiefs storm Loftus Versfeld to battle Sundowns for Knockout Cup final

Kaizer Chiefs face Mamelodi Sundowns in the Carling Knockout Cup semifinal on Sunday, 22 November at 15:30.

Coach Fernando Da Cruz confirmed the squad will enter the Loftus Versfeld Stadium pitch with zero excuses, aiming to break the team's long wait for a competition final appearance that dates back to 2015. The club is prepared to field both Langelihle Phili and new signing Elias Mokwana, who are both available for selection after returning to full team training sessions this week.

Advancing to the final holds significant weight for the Amakhosi, as Da Cruz remains determined to secure silverware during his first season at the helm. With the team having committed to high-intensity performances throughout the current campaign, a victory here is viewed as a vital step in proving their consistency against high-level opponents like Sundowns, whom the coach describes as a team operating at a CAF Champions League standard.

According to Da Cruz, the upcoming match represents a clean slate, marking a departure from previous league encounters. The squad continues to focus on developing the output of rising star Mfundo Vilakazi, specifically tasking the midfielder with improving his final-third productivity through increased goals and assists. While Sundowns brings deep experience in switching between domestic and international competition demands, the Chiefs maintain that their primary focus remains on their own tactical approach and execution.

Following the outcome of this semifinal, the winner will progress to the final round of the Carling Knockout Cup. The Chiefs arrive at this fixture following a comeback victory in the previous round, while the hosts look to bounce back after a loss to USM Alger prior to the international break.
