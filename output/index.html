Singapore Authorities Impound 91 Deregistered Vehicles in Massive September Islandwide Crackdown

The Land Transport Authority and Singapore Police Force seized 91 vehicles in September for operating without valid Certificates of Entitlement or road tax.

A surge in illegal road activity has prompted a sharp response, as Singapore authorities identified 245 cases of deregistered vehicles on the roads in 2025, a significant rise from 75 in 2024. These vehicles present a severe hazard because they are neither roadworthy nor covered by mandatory insurance, leaving other road users at risk.

Keeping or using these unregistered vehicles carries stiff consequences for owners. Since February, first-time offenders face maximum penalties of a S$20,000 fine, up to two years in jail, or both. Repeat offenders are subject to double these penalties, highlighting the government's push to clear these vehicles from circulation.

The recent operation follows a September enforcement blitz by the Land Transport Authority (LTA) and the Singapore Police Force. The authorities emphasized that owners remain responsible for their vehicles even after handing them over to dealers. They advise that all administrative processes, such as confirming ownership transfers on the OneMotoring website or obtaining proper disposal documents, are vital to avoiding investigation.

Legislative action is now moving forward to close existing loopholes. A new bill introduced in Parliament on September 8 proposes an Authorised Exporter Scheme, which would mandate that all deregistered vehicles be handled only by LTA-authorized exporters. Additionally, the grace period for disposing of a vehicle will be slashed from one month to 14 days, and those who fail to meet these requirements may face daily fines of up to S$500 until disposal is verified. Authorities are also seeking the power to prevent vehicle transfers to individuals flagged as high-risk for failing to dispose of vehicles properly.

Ardie Savea sustained a dislocated shoulder just 25 minutes into the third Test match against the Springboks in Soweto. The All Blacks captain had been in dominant form during the opening quarter of that game, scoring a brace of tries before the injury occurred. According to head coach Dave Rennie, Savea has already undergone an operation and now faces an extensive period of rehabilitation. The recovery timeline is significant enough that he is officially ruled out of the upcoming Bledisloe Cup series against Australia and may even miss the beginning of the Super Rugby Pacific season with his new Auckland-based club.

The loss of Savea marks a difficult period for the All Blacks, who are currently managing a heavy injury toll from their four-test series in South Africa, which concluded with a 3-1 series victory for the Springboks. Beyond Savea, the team is without several other key starters: Luke Jacobson is out following surgery on a torn elbow tendon, Caleb Clarke is sidelined with a ruptured shoulder tendon, Anton Lienert-Brown is managing a medial cruciate ligament injury, and Billy Proctor remains unavailable due to a broken shoulder blade sustained earlier in the tour.

Despite the clear impact of losing a player of his calibre, Dave Rennie views the situation as a necessary opportunity to test the depth of the squad. He noted that while Savea has been inspirational and on fire during the series, the mandatory rest period will allow other players to step into leadership roles and gain critical experience over the next few months. Savea, having played a high volume of rugby over the last two years, will now focus entirely on his recovery in preparation for his return to domestic play.

    طهران تدرس الانسحاب من معاهدة حظر الانتشار النووي وسط توترات دولية متصاعدة

    إيران تعلن أن خروجها من معاهدة عدم انتشار الأسلحة النووية أصبح خياراً جدياً ومطروحاً للنقاش السياسي في البلاد.

    أكد إسماعيل بقائي، المتحدث باسم وزارة الخارجية الإيرانية، أن مسألة الخروج المحتمل من معاهدة عدم انتشار الأسلحة النووية باتت مطروحة بجدية داخل الأوساط السياسية والنخب الفكرية في طهران. وتأتي هذه التصريحات التي أدلى بها بقائي يوم الأحد في ظل نقاشات مستمرة حول استخدام مشروع برلماني يتعلق بهذا الانسحاب كأداة دبلوماسية للتعامل مع الضغوط التي تفرضها الوكالة الدولية للطاقة الذرية على إيران.

    تكمن أهمية هذا التطور في كونه يعكس تحولاً في الخطاب الرسمي الإيراني تجاه الالتزامات الدولية، حيث يطرح المسؤولون الإيرانيون تساؤلات حول جدوى البقاء في معاهدة انضمت إليها البلاد منذ عام 1970، إذا كانت لا تضمن لها التمتع بحقوقها أو حمايتها من الضغوط والتهديدات الخارجية، وفقاً لتعبيره. ويشدد المسؤولون على أن إيران، رغم التزامها بتعهداتها، ترى نفسها عرضة لعمليات استهداف لمنشآتها النووية واغتيالات لعلمائها.

    ووفقاً لما أورده موقع "إرم نيوز"، أشار المتحدث باسم الخارجية إلى أن وزارته ستلتزم بقرارات الجهات المختصة في الدولة بشأن هذه القضية. وفي المقابل، لا تزال المواقف الرسمية الإيرانية تشير إلى بقاء البلاد عضواً في المعاهدة في الوقت الراهن، مع الإدراك الكامل لكافة الالتزامات المترتبة على هذه العضوية طالما استمرت، وذلك في وقت تتصاعد فيه الضغوط الدولية المرتبطة بالملف النووي الإيراني.
