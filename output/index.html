Kylian Mbappe Snatches Late El Clasico Winner to Close LaLiga Title Gap

Kylian Mbappe scored an 87th-minute winner to secure a 2-1 victory for Real Madrid over LaLiga leaders Barcelona at Spotify Camp Nou.

Real Madrid head coach Jose Mourinho celebrated on his knees at the final whistle as his side snapped Barcelona’s nine-game domestic unbeaten run. The tension at Spotify Camp Nou remained high until the 87th minute when Kylian Mbappe connected with an Eduardo Camavinga cutback, following a piece of individual brilliance from Jude Bellingham, to silence the home crowd and secure a vital 2-1 result for the visitors.

This victory is a significant turning point for the top of the LaLiga table. Had Barcelona won, they would have established a commanding nine-point lead over their rivals. Instead, the result pulls Real Madrid to within three points of the league leaders, effectively reigniting the championship race. The win also marks Real Madrid's first triumph in Barcelona in three years, providing a substantial psychological boost for Mourinho's squad.

According to Sky Sports, the match was a pulsating affair that saw Real Madrid take a fortuitous lead when a cross deflected off Gerard Martin and into the net. Barcelona responded through an equalizer from Raphinha, but the hosts were unable to sustain their momentum against a disciplined Real side. BBC reports that Barcelona manager Hansi Flick cited a lack of freshness as a primary factor in his team's performance, noting that his squad had endured a demanding schedule in recent weeks. For Mourinho, the result extends his personal record, making him only the third Real Madrid manager in history to remain unbeaten across six consecutive El Clasicos, a streak encompassing four wins and two draws.
