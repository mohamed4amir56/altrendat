South Africa levels series with innings-and-31-run demolition of Australia at Gqeberha

South Africa defeated Australia by an innings and 31 runs in the second Test at St George's Park on Thursday to tie the three-Test series at 1-1, setting up a series decider in Cape Town.

South Africa's bowlers dismantled Australia on the final day, completing an emphatic turnaround after the hosts were in deep trouble on the third morning. The Proteas bowled out Australia for 295 in the second innings despite missing frontline bowler Anrich Nortje to a suspected groin injury. Wiaan Mulder took three of the six wickets needed on Thursday to finish the job, dismissing Travis Head on 121, Scott Boland, and captain Pat Cummins.

The victory proves significant for both teams' World Test Championship positioning, as South Africa currently holds the WTC title while Australia ranks first in the standings. More immediately, it forces a decisive third Test with the series now level at 1-1 after Australia won the first Test in Durban by a large margin. The result sends the teams to Cape Town for the final match.

South Africa's path to victory began with captain Temba Bavuma's career-best innings of 179, which anchored a recovery from 110-6 on day three. Bavuma's match-changing partnerships with Marco Jansen (85) and Simon Harmer (82) allowed South Africa to declare on 425-9. Australia then collapsed to 99 all out in their first innings, with Jansen taking 5-33, before being forced to follow on. Although Head and Boland showed resistance in the follow-on, South Africa's pace attack proved too strong.

Bavuma described the performance as "good old Test cricket," emphasizing South Africa's focus on wearing down the opposition. Australian captain Pat Cummins acknowledged Head and Boland's fight as a "blueprint" for the third Test beginning in five days from Tuesday, live on Sky Sports Cricket.
