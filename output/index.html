While flights currently in the air were unaffected, the outage halted the departure process for grounded planes. The airline acknowledged the widespread technical failure on social media and stated that teams were working to resolve the issue as soon as possible. This technology failure directly impacts travelers' schedules, forcing passengers to wait for the transmission of data that serves as a mandatory safety requirement for flight operations. According to NBC 5 Chicago, United Airlines confirmed they are actively troubleshooting the technology issue. Air traffic control audio reported that the outage was affecting systems across the network, with no estimated time provided for when the transmission of takeoff data would be fully restored. Passengers are advised to monitor their flight status through the United mobile app or the official airline website. This incident follows a separate, rare nationwide ground stop caused by a router issue during maintenance that disrupted air traffic control communications just days earlier.
