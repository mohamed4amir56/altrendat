Golden State Valkyries and Atlanta Dream Hunt First Ever WNBA Championship Title

The Golden State Valkyries and Atlanta Dream begin their best-of-seven WNBA Finals series on Saturday, October 17, at 3:30 p.m. ET.

The Atlanta Dream have chased a title four times over their 18-year history, while the two-year-old Golden State Valkyries have become the fastest expansion franchise to reach the championship round. One of these clubs will secure their first-ever banner no later than Halloween, October 31, if the series extends to a seventh game.

This matchup represents a shift in WNBA success for 2026, where long-term head coaches struggled and defensive-minded teams took priority over postseason experience. Both teams enter the series following impressive playoff runs, with the Dream sweeping the 2024 champions and the Valkyries sweeping the back-to-back champion Aces. Success in this series will likely hinge on the defensive play of stars like Angel Reese, who leads her team with reliable scoring and rebounding, utilizing her well-known competitive nature to elevate her teammates on the sport's biggest stage.

According to Sportsnet, the Valkyries swept the regular-season series against the Dream, winning 77-66 on June 24, 78-75 on June 26, and 88-83 on July 4. While the teams were not projected to be the final two standing at the start of the season, their path to the Finals confirms their current dominance within the league.

The series opens on Saturday, October 17, in Golden State. Following the first two games at the Valkyries' home arena, the series shifts to Atlanta for Games 3 and 4. If necessary, the final three games will alternate venues, concluding with a potential Game 7 on Halloween.
