Tonga is led by captain Jason Taumalolo, while Samoa enters the match under the leadership of co-captains Jarome Luai and Junior Paulo. The game is being played at CommBank Stadium in Sydney, with fans across the region expressing intense support for their respective teams.

As anticipation reaches its peak, supporters have been vocal in their prayers for a good result. The match serves as a final group fixture for the tournament, with both sides looking to secure their progression in the Rugby League World Cup.
