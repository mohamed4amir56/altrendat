Towering Gilas routs Syria by 39 points in World Cup qualifying debut

Gilas Pilipinas cruised to a 97-58 victory over Syria on Friday to improve to a 5-4 record in the FIBA Basketball World Cup 2027 Asian Qualifiers.

Kai Sotto needed only 17 minutes and 40 seconds to post a dominant 18-point, 10-rebound performance as the Philippines secured a lopsided victory at Karisma Arena in Seremban, Malaysia. The win marked the third consecutive victory for the squad, moving them above .500 in the Group E standings with a 5-4 record, while Syria remained at the bottom of the group with a 2-7 standing. The matchup featured a commanding display of physical dominance from the Filipino frontcourt, which controlled the boards to out-rebound their opponents significantly. Following a 43-32 halftime lead, Gilas broke the game open with a 29-13 third quarter behind a relentless shooting display from downtown, ultimately leading by as many as 40 points during the contest. This triumph is vital for the Philippines as the team navigates the fifth of six windows in the qualification process. Coach Tim Cone praised the team’s ability to execute within their established systems, specifically highlighting the seamless debut of naturalized player Bennie Boatwright. Boatwright finished his first outing in a national team jersey with a double-double of 16 points and 13 rebounds. The frontcourt depth was further bolstered by the defensive efforts of Mike Phillips and Quentin Millora-Brown, who combined for 13 rebounds and two blocks. Kevin Quiambao also contributed a game-high-tying 18 points, converting 6-of-8 from beyond the arc to help solidify the lopsided result.
