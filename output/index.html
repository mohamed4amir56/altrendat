A follow-up ultrasound on Wednesday revealed movement in the small clot, leading the team to rule Hurts out for Week 6 against Carolina and determine the appropriate course of treatment.

Milton spent the first five weeks of the season on Dallas' practice squad after being waived from the Cowboys' initial 53-man roster despite being drafted in the sixth round in 2024 by New England and traded to Dallas last year.

He completed 37-of-53 passes for 424 yards with two touchdowns and two interceptions, while also rushing for 66 yards and a touchdown. Last season with Dallas, he went 15-of-24 for 183 yards, one touchdown and two interceptions across four games.

The Eagles, who have lost three straight games following a 2-0 start and fallen under .500 for the first time since 2021, will face the Panthers on Sunday. Cole Payton, a fifth-round pick in the 2026 draft from North Dakota State, remains the only other quarterback on the roster besides McKee.
