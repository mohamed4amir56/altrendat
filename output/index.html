RTÉ and other media organizations were evacuated this morning following a coordinated bomb threat sent via email.

Garda sniffer dogs were deployed to sweep the buildings, which have since been declared safe.

While some outlets like Virgin Media Television maintained their broadcast schedule after assessing the situation, the impact of the email was significant enough to trigger emergency protocols across the continent.

Kevin Bakhurst described the situation as a disgraceful attack on free media that caused considerable operational chaos. The broadcaster relied on recently reviewed contingency plans to manage the evacuation. Authorities in Ireland confirmed they are actively investigating the incident, which appears to be part of a larger, coordinated campaign of disruption affecting various European platforms. Gardaí remain in contact with affected organizations as they continue to monitor the broader security threat. The investigation into the source of the emails is ongoing, with officials liaising with security partners across Europe to trace the origins of these coordinated warnings.
