Sri Lanka Routs Pakistan by Eight Wickets to Secure Tri-Series Final Spot

Sri Lanka defeated Pakistan by eight wickets in Lahore on Tuesday, eliminating England from the tri-series final contention.

The result carries significant consequences for the tri-series structure, as it officially ends England's hopes of reaching the final. Harry Brook's side, which sits at three defeats from three matches, was eliminated from the tournament entirely due to this outcome. Thursday's upcoming fixture between Sri Lanka and England is now rendered a dead rubber, as the finalists are already determined.

Pakistan captain Babar Azam provided the top score for his team with 29 runs, though the hosts struggled to build any meaningful partnerships throughout the innings. In reply, Sri Lanka's Kamindu Mendis anchored the chase with an unbeaten 62 off 60 balls. Mendis survived an early dropped catch and a successful lbw review at 44 to guide his team to victory, striking eight fours and two sixes in the process. According to The Guardian, Sri Lanka finished the match with more than 31 overs to spare.

Sri Lanka will now prepare for the championship decider against Pakistan on Saturday. Before that showpiece event, the tournament schedule continues with the remaining group match on Thursday, marking the end of England's involvement in the series before they travel to Australia for their next tour.
