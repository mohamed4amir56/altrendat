The 6/58 Ultra Lotto draws are held on Tuesdays, Fridays, and Sundays, offering another chance for those looking to compete for the substantial remaining jackpot.

The Philippine Charity Sweepstakes Office continues to oversee these games, with winners advised to verify their tickets at local outlets.
