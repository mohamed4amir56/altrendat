MetService expects a second weather front to move up the South Island tomorrow, bringing rain and stronger winds.

This upcoming weather change is significant for residents as the front brings rain and stronger northwesterly winds across the South Island. For regions that have experienced dry conditions, such as the Canterbury High Country, this moisture provides a necessary shift in the local climate. However, the anticipated wind and rain will continue to impact travel and daily routines across the country.

According to MetService meteorologist Maddy Neal, the front will reach the lower South Island late tomorrow afternoon before progressing overnight into Thursday. Westland is expected to receive the heaviest rainfall, while Gore and Invercargill should prepare for stronger gusts. The lower North Island will likely see the front arrive by midday Thursday, with Wellington, Wairarapa, and Auckland experiencing showers throughout Wednesday and Thursday.

Temperatures are expected to turn noticeably cooler following last week's highs, which reached 29C in places like Hastings. While some sheltered areas on the North Island's east coast may remain warm on Wednesday and Thursday, overall temperatures will remain lower than previous records. Travelers are advised to monitor the situation, as the earlier storm forced the cancellation or diversion of about 40 flights at Wellington Airport and caused widespread power and road disruptions.
