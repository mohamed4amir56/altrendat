Brentford Routs Everton 4-0 as Tarkowski Sees First Career Red Card

Brentford climbed to sixth in the Premier League table after a 4-0 win against Everton, marked by Keane Lewis-Potter’s double and a contentious red card for James Tarkowski.

James Tarkowski’s dismissal at the Gtech Community Stadium ended a remarkable Premier League record; the Everton captain had avoided a red card in all 350 of his previous appearances. His first-ever red card occurred after a VAR review upgraded a yellow card for a follow-through challenge on Yehor Yarmoliuk, with officials citing high studs and significant force. The sending-off leaves Tarkowski suspended for Sunday’s upcoming Merseyside derby.

The 4-0 defeat represents the heaviest loss for Everton since David Moyes returned to the club at the start of last year. For Moyes, the night was particularly difficult as it marked his 593rd game as Everton manager, setting a new club record for the most matches in charge, yet the result saw his side drop to ninth in the standings.

Keane Lewis-Potter secured the victory for the hosts with two deflected strikes within four minutes during the first half. Vitaly Janelt added a third goal with a 25-yard rocket in the 67th minute, and substitute Fabio Carvalho scored a fourth in stoppage time to complete the rout. According to the Premier League match centre, the referee ultimately ruled that Tarkowski’s challenge endangered the safety of his opponent.

Brentford now sits in sixth place, one point ahead of Everton. Moyes will look to recover in his next outing, while Brentford prepares to maintain their position in the league following this comprehensive performance.
