Cristiano Ronaldo dropped as Al-Nassr reshuffles squad for Pakhtakor AFC clash

Al-Nassr travels to face Pakhtakor in the AFC Champions League Elite this Monday without Cristiano Ronaldo, Kingsley Coman, and Mohamed Simakan.

Cristiano Ronaldo arrives in Uzbekistan with 985 career goals, yet he will remain off the pitch for this crucial continental encounter. The omission of the veteran forward, alongside teammates Kingsley Coman and Mohamed Simakan, marks a significant departure from the squad that secured a recent 5-3 win over Al-Khaleej in the Saudi Roshn League. Head coach Ange Postecoglou has opted to leave the trio out of the travelling party to manage physical loads during a congested schedule of fixtures and travel requirements. The decision is further influenced by wider security concerns within the region as the Saudi club heads to Tashkent.

For Al-Nassr, this rotation is a strategic move to preserve squad fitness for upcoming domestic and continental commitments. Sitting in ninth place in the West group with three points from two matches, the club currently looks to climb the table to improve its standing in the AFC Champions League Elite. Securing a win in Uzbekistan is vital to shifting their position before they eventually address a postponed fixture against the UAE's Al-Wasl.

Tactically, the visitors will look to bolster their defensive stability in the absence of Simakan. According to reports cited by the Saudi newspaper Arriyadiyah, Abdulelah Al-Amri is expected to return to the starting line-up to partner Inigo Martinez in central defence. The squad also receives a boost with the return of Portugal's Samu Costa, who is available for selection again after serving a one-match suspension following yellow cards accumulated in domestic play.
