Flick adjusted his tactics at halftime, introducing Frenkie de Jong at right-back to bolster the defense. Following these changes, the coach noted a marked improvement in the second half, describing the performance as a mature display that yielded the vital three points.

Historically, this result continues a strong run for Barcelona at the Metropolitano, marking their fourth consecutive away win against Atletico Madrid in La Liga. The club is now positioned to potentially match historical records for successive away victories against the Madrid side should they continue this streak in future encounters.
