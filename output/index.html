Strangers Raise Nearly 600,000 Dollars After Viral Prank Targets Pizza Worker

A Michigan grocery store employee received massive financial support after a video of him confronting a prankster went viral.

Walt Z., a Red Baron worker stocking pizzas at a Meijer store, told an unidentified prankster that his life is hard enough as he works long hours to support his four children. The confrontation occurred when the individual pretended to steal money from him using a fake Apple Pay transaction sound, prompting Walt to explain he had missed time with his family and faced early morning shifts at ten other locations.

The emotional response triggered a global reaction online, with thousands of people donating to a GoFundMe account to assist him with his financial struggles, including his mortgage payments. The support underscores a widespread public rejection of public pranking behavior that targets hardworking service employees already managing personal and professional stress.

According to Yahoo and NBC 5 Chicago, the fundraiser has surpassed 480,000 dollars, with NBC 5 reporting the total is nearing 600,000 dollars as of October 30. Red Baron publicly stood by their employee, stating on social media that "employee of the month" nominations are now closed because they already have their winner.

Walt Z. stated through the fundraiser that he is overwhelmed by the kindness shown to him and his family. While he has received numerous interview requests, he is currently prioritizing his family's privacy and considering his next steps carefully to ensure he is treated with respect.

      على أرض الميدان، حسمت باكستان اللقاء في 23 كرة فقط بفارق أربعة ويكيت، بعد أداء كارثي لقائد المنتخب الهندي كرونال بانديا. فقد تلقى بانديا 31 نقطة في أول «أوفر» بعد أن سدد عباس أفريدي خمس رميات ستية (sixes)، ليحسم الأخير جائزة أفضل لاعب في المباراة. من جهته، أنهى أفريدي اللقاء برصيد 35 نقطة من 8 كرات، بينما ظل خاواجا نافاي دون هزيمة برصيد 41 نقطة من 12 كرة، ليقودا بلادهما لانتصار ساحق.

      وفي أعقاب التغريدة التي هنأ فيها رئيس الوزراء لاعبي المنتخب ورئيس مجلس الكريكت الباكستاني محسن نقفي، انهالت التعليقات الساخرة، حيث وصف مشجعون تصرفه بـ«المحرج»، مطالبين إياه بالتوقف عن تضخيم فوز في بطولة سداسيات كما لو كان فوزًا بكأس العالم، وهو ما دفع العديد من الإعلاميين والمتابعين للتعبير عن استغرابهم من الموقف الرسمي.
