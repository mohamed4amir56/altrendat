Alexandra Eala roars past Talia Gibson to clinch Tokyo quarterfinal rematch berth

Alexandra Eala advanced to the Toray Pan Pacific Open quarterfinals after a three-set victory against Talia Gibson on Wednesday.

After dropping the opening set, Alexandra Eala roared through 12 of the final 14 games to secure a 4-6, 6-2, 6-0 victory over Talia Gibson. This impressive comeback win in Tokyo lasted exactly 1 hour and 53 minutes at the Ariake Coliseum, moving the Filipina into the quarterfinals for the first time since her maiden title run in Washington this past August.

This victory is a significant momentum shift for Eala, who had previously struggled during the Asian Swing. Having notched only one match win in her first three events in China and Singapore, Eala now faces a critical test against Diana Shnaider. The matchup carries added weight as the two rising talents meet for the second time in as many weeks, following Shnaider’s 7-5, 6-4 win at the BNB Ningbo Open.

According to the WTA, both players have reached eight quarterfinals this year. Shnaider arrives at this clash after a demanding 2-hour and 31-minute battle against Aliaksandra Sasnovich. While Eala and Shnaider are currently establishing a professional rivalry—with Shnaider holding a 1-0 lead at the pro level—the history between them dates back to a 2019 junior encounter in Osaka, which Eala won 6-1, 6-3.

Eala, currently ranked World No. 19, looks to maintain her form as she prepares for the quarterfinal showdown. Shnaider, the tournament's No. 3 seed, enters the match following a strong showing in both Wuhan and Ningbo during the current season.
