The intensity spiked shortly after when the visitors had a goal ruled out following a video assistant referee review, followed by an on-pitch altercation between Al-Nassr defender Inigo Martinez and a Neftchi player that resulted in yellow cards for both.

With this performance, Ronaldo has reached five goals across all competitions this season. He now stands 19 goals away from his stated career milestone of 1,000 goals.
