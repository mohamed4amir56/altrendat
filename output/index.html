Manchester United hunt first win in six as Como test looms in Champions League

Manchester United visit Como on Wednesday aiming to end a six-game winless run in the Champions League after drawing 1–1 at Leeds.

Manchester United's six-game winless streak reaches a critical juncture on Wednesday at Como's Stadio Giuseppe Sinigaglia, where manager Michael Carrick desperately seeks his third victory of the season. The Red Devils drew 1–1 at Leeds United over the weekend, a result Carrick described as a fresh start for his turbulent outfit, though the performance offered scant comfort given their previous losses post-international break.

The clash matters significantly for United's European ambitions. After losing to Atlético Madrid last week, they risk extending their domestic slump into the Champions League. Carrick acknowledged the stakes, noting United could lose another season if their inconsistency persists. The team showed defensive solidity at Leeds but remains a work in progress.

Como, managed by Cesc Fàbregas, arrive as confident opponents despite their own injury crisis. They defeated RB Leipzig emphatically on Matchday 1 but fell at Feyenoord. In Serie A, they moved fifth after beating Fiorentina 3–1 on Sunday. Fàbregas insists Manchester United are not in crisis, calling the match historic for Como. He confirmed Jesus Rodriguez faces a lengthy absence after a training injury, Chalobah will miss two weeks, and Moise Kean's availability remains uncertain ahead of the clash.

EA shuts down BioWare, cancels next Mass Effect game in 3% workforce cut

Electronic Arts is closing BioWare studio and cutting approximately 400 jobs, equal to 3% of its workforce, months after going private through an $55 billion leveraged buyout.

BioWare, the legendary studio behind Dragon Age and Mass Effect, is being shuttered by Electronic Arts in a major restructuring announced Wednesday. The closure affects about 50 of the roughly 400 positions being eliminated across EA, with the Edmonton workspace also closing. Chief Studios Officer Cam Weber and Chief Operating Officer David Tinson said in a memo that early groundwork on a new Mass Effect game will be abandoned as part of the decision.

The move reflects EA's push to cut costs after becoming a private company in August through a $55 billion leveraged buyout led by Saudi Arabia's Public Investment Fund. The company is carrying roughly $18 billion in debt and aims to save as much as $700 million, partly through reduced spending on research and development. BioWare's recent title Dragon Age: The Veilguard fell short of sales expectations, prompting the steep cuts at the studio last year.

EA will continue investing in other major projects. Respawn and DICE studios are joining forces on what Bloomberg reports is probably a new Star Wars: Battlefront installment. The company is also advancing Marvel's Iron Man, the next Star Wars Jedi game, and new Battlefield titles. For The Sims franchise, EA is pausing Project Lotus to prioritize Project Evergreen, while the skate team is being shrunk to focus on a clearer roadmap. At EA Sports, two new projects are launching, including Project Gridiron, an open-world American football game, and a return of the Fight Night franchise in pre-production.
