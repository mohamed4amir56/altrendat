After her opening match against Parks, Eala is set to navigate a path that could include a second-round meeting with Czech player Tereza Valentova. The outcome of this event will close out the competitive schedule for Eala before the season concludes, as the professional tour shifts focus toward the upcoming WTA Finals held in Indian Wells from November 8 to 15.
