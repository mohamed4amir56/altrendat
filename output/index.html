Wolves Topple Burnley as 21-Game Home Winless Streak Hits One Year

Burnley suffered a 2-1 defeat against Wolves, marking a full year without a league victory at Turf Moor across 21 matches.

Burnley's home league drought reaches the grim 365-day milestone after a 2-1 defeat, leaving the club winless at Turf Moor for 21 consecutive matches. The hosts squandered an early lead provided by Jamie Vardy’s first goal for the club, ultimately succumbing to defensive errors that allowed Wolves to complete a comeback victory.

This result deepens the crisis at Turf Moor, where fans have not seen a home league victory since Burnley defeated Leeds on 18 October 2025. The failure to secure points in front of home supporters has left head coach Nicky Hayen facing intense scrutiny regarding his future, though he maintains he will not give up on turning the form around. For the club, the pressure continues to mount as they approach the record of 23 home games without a win established by Sheffield Wednesday last season.

According to The Guardian, the match turned when Wolves substitute Tawanda Chirewa scored within seconds of his 72nd-minute introduction. The winning goal followed a crucial mistake from Burnley captain Kyle Walker, which allowed Mateus Mané to set up the Zimbabwe international. Fer López had previously capitalized on another defensive lapse to equalize before the halftime break. The BBC notes that the visitors, despite facing their own injury challenges including the loss of Raúl Jiménez and Adam Armstrong, were able to capitalize on Burnley’s recurring errors.

Burnley now faces a daunting schedule to try and break the streak, with an upcoming home clash against Portsmouth. The pressure will intensify significantly when bitter rivals Blackburn arrive at Turf Moor for a derby match on 22 November.
