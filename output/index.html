David Corenswet Transforms Into NFL Underdog John Tuggle for Mr. Irrelevant Movie

David Corenswet stars as the last 1983 NFL draft pick John Tuggle in the upcoming biographical sports drama hitting theaters December 25, 2026.

After flying high as Superman, David Corenswet trades his cape for a jersey to portray the unlikely journey of John Tuggle, the final player selected in the 1983 NFL draft by the New York Giants. The film showcases Tuggle's defiance of expectations both on the gridiron and in his personal life, a performance that is already generating significant buzz as potential Oscar-worthy work ahead of its release. Corenswet stars alongside Michael Shannon, who plays Giants coach Bill Parcells, and Isabel May, who portrays Tuggle's love interest, Katie.

For football fans and cinema-goers, this true story offers an intimate look at the dedication required to defy the label of an underdog. The production highlights the intense bond between a player and a head coach, capturing the moment the New York Giants picked Tuggle in the final round—a decision that forever altered the trajectory of his professional career. According to Yahoo, the film explores how remarkable perseverance can overcome even the most daunting challenges in the sports world.

Directed by Jonathan Levine with a script by Nick Santora, the film features an ensemble cast including David Krumholtz, Tuwaine Barrett, Thaddeus LaGrone, Alexander England, Spencer House, and Tandi Wright. Produced by a large team including David Ellison and Dana Goldberg, the project has already begun promotional activities.

Fans looking for an early look can participate in ticket giveaways through Audacy, which is offering seats for an advanced screening. This special showing is scheduled to take place at the Cinemark Egyptian Theater in Arundel Mills Mall on December 16, 2026, at 7pm. Following these promotional screenings, the full theatrical release is slated for December 25, 2026.
