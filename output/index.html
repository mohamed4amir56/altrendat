Authorities in Ireland confirmed they are actively investigating the incident, which appears to be part of a larger, coordinated campaign of disruption affecting various European platforms. Gardaí remain in contact with affected organizations as they continue to monitor the broader security threat. The investigation into the source of the emails is ongoing, with officials liaising with security partners across Europe to trace the origins of these coordinated warnings.
