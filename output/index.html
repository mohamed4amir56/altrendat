MetService will manage the installation and ongoing operation of the facility, which will feature modern Doppler dual-polarisation technology. The selection of the Horoirangi Peak location followed a rigorous evaluation of approximately 10 potential sites across the eastern hills near Nelson, the central hills around Moutere, and areas reaching toward Motueka and Kahurangi National Park. The chosen site was prioritized for its optimal altitude and the support of local landowners willing to host the infrastructure.

Operations at the new site are expected to be fully functional by mid-2027. Once active, the radar will require an annual operating budget of approximately $260,000 to maintain its service to the community.
