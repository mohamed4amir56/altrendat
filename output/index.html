Kimi Antonelli Beats Max Verstappen to Secure Surprise United States Grand Prix Pole

Mercedes driver Kimi Antonelli secured pole position at the Circuit of the Americas, putting him in position to clinch the F1 world championship on Sunday.

Kimi Antonelli snatched pole position at the United States Grand Prix with a lap time of 1:34.035s, edging out Red Bull’s Max Verstappen by 0.179 seconds. This performance marks Antonelli’s seventh pole of the season and his first since July’s Belgian Grand Prix, defying the recent momentum of both McLaren and Red Bull. After the session, the 20-year-old Mercedes driver noted that while the goal remains to win, the gusty and unpredictable wind at the Circuit of the Americas made Q3 particularly tough.

Securing this pole is a significant step for Antonelli, who is now on the brink of becoming Formula 1’s youngest world champion. The championship could be mathematically sealed this weekend if he wins the race on Sunday, provided his team-mate George Russell finishes lower than eighth and Ferrari’s Lewis Hamilton finishes below fourth. For Antonelli, the pressure of the title decider has been managed by keeping a close circle of friends nearby throughout the race weekend to help him relax.

Behind the front row, Red Bull’s Isack Hadjar equalled his career-best qualifying result by taking third place. Ferrari’s Charles Leclerc will start fourth, followed by Lewis Hamilton in fifth and George Russell in sixth. McLaren’s Oscar Piastri qualified seventh, while his team-mate Lando Norris dropped to eighth following the deletion of his final lap time due to track limit breaches. Liam Lawson takes ninth, and Arvid Lindblad completes the top ten, despite a five-place grid penalty for exceeding his engine component allowance.

Antonelli enters Sunday’s race focused on maintaining his momentum from the start. While he acknowledges the title scenario requires specific results from others, he intends to lead from Turn 1 and set the pace for the remainder of the event.
