Senegal chases first win under Vieira against Ethiopia in AFCON qualifier

Senegal faces Ethiopia on Tuesday in Bahir Dar in the second matchday of the 2027 AFCON qualifiers, seeking Patrick Vieira's first victory after drawing 1-1 with Mozambique.

Senegal dominated their opening match against Mozambique but could not convert chances in a 1-1 draw, Nicolas Jackson's early goal cancelled by Geny Catamo's first-half equalizer. Now the Lions of Teranga travel to Bahir Dar International Stadium to face Ethiopia, who fell 1-0 to Sudan in the qualifying opener when Mohamed Eisa scored in the 86th minute.

Viera has made intensity and clinical finishing his priorities. "We'll continue to play with more intensity and capitalise on the chances we've created," he told reporters. "It's important for us to play a good match, a complete match." Despite the long journey to Ethiopia, Vieira said the team will be "competitive in terms of tactics, physical condition, and mental strength." Senegal will be without Sadio Mané due to a physical issue but retain Nicolas Jackson, Kalidou Koulibaly, Idrissa Gana Gueye, and Pape Matar Sarr. Saudi Pro League defender Malang Sarr is set for his Senegal debut.

Ethiopia coach Yohannes Sahilu stressed focus after his strikers missed chances against Sudan. "We should be focused on the game. We should avoid any costly mistakes. We should finish off the chances we create," he said. Bahir Dar, which has hosted the Waliyas only once in five years, is considered a fortress where Ethiopia beat Ivory Coast in 2021 AFCON qualifying.

After matchday one, Sudan leads Group J with three points. Senegal and Mozambique have one point each, while Ethiopia has none. The top two teams will qualify directly for the tournament in Kenya, Tanzania, and Uganda.

Matt Rife credits TikTok viral clips for career breakthrough after Hollywood rejection

Comedian Matt Rife says his 20.3 million TikTok followers and reported $40-50 million net worth came from viral crowd work clips on the platform, after years struggling in Los Angeles's traditional comedy circuit earned him only $40-60 thousand annually.

Matt Rife earned between $40,000 and $60,000 annually in Los Angeles before discovering TikTok, a salary that qualified him as "very low income" by the city's cost of living standards. He moved to Ohio to Los Angeles expecting to follow the traditional path: become a regular at famous comedy clubs like the Laugh Factory, Comedy Store, or the Improv, where talent scouts would spot him and offer television or sitcom roles.

Instead, early success on MTV's "Wild 'N Out" faded, leaving him at professional "rock bottom." That's when social media changed everything. Despite telling Fox News he "hated" TikTok and "never wanted to be a part of it," Rife posted viral crowd work clips that accomplished what years of chasing Hollywood's traditional pipeline could not.

Today, Rife has 20.3 million TikTok followers and a reported net worth of $40-50 million. "The reach on there is incomparable," he told Fox News at the Walmart Arkansas Music Pavilion ahead of a show. His viral success has led to roles he credits to social media, including a starring role alongside Owen Wilson in the upcoming "Rolling Loud: The Movie," scheduled for release on October 2. "Owen Wilson is one of the people that made me want to be an actor," Rife said.

    Warwick Davis Returns as Professor Flitwick for HBO's Harry Potter Series

    Original Harry Potter film actor Warwick Davis will reprise his role as Professor Flitwick in HBO's new Harry Potter television adaptation, calling it "a huge honour" ahead of the series premiere.

    Warwick Davis, who portrayed Professor Flitwick across the Harry Potter film franchise, is returning to the character for HBO's upcoming television adaptation. The actor called the opportunity "a huge honour" and said he remains "excited to retell these stories because they're fantastic stories."

    Davis has been connected to the Harry Potter world for decades. He worked on the films for over 11 years and has continued collaborating with the team at the studio tour in Watford. "Harry Potter has become part of my professional life since the late 90s throughout until now," he told the Press Association. "So it's a constant. It's fabulous."

    The actor described Flitwick as one of his favourite characters to play in his career. "He's a very warm, kind of likeable character," Davis said, explaining that he based the professor on some of his own teachers—the kind who would be understanding if a student forgot their homework. Davis remained tight-lipped about details of the new series, noting "it's all very top secret."

    Davis joins a cast that includes Paapa Essiedu as Severus Snape, Janet McTeer as Minerva McGonagall, Nick Frost as Rubeus Hagrid, and John Lithgow as Albus Dumbledore. The series will premiere on December 25, 2026, on HBO Max.
