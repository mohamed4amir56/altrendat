Sonay Kartal Stuns Ostapenko To Clinch Historic First WTA 1,000 Career Final

British tennis player Sonay Kartal reaches her first WTA 1,000 final at the Wuhan Open after defeating Jelena Ostapenko in straight sets.

Sonay Kartal survived six set points across two tense tie-breaks, clawing back from 4-1 deficits in both sets to topple Jelena Ostapenko 7-6, 7-6. The 24-year-old is now the lowest-ranked player at world number 170 to reach a WTA 1,000 final since the tournament format began in 1990.

This performance marks a meteoric rise for the British athlete, who spent six months sidelined with a back injury earlier this year. The victory ensures Kartal will return to the world's top 50 rankings and become the British number one player when the official list is updated on Monday. For tennis followers, her rapid return to form signals a significant shift in the competitive landscape as she prepares for the biggest match of her professional career.

Kartal’s journey to the final has been defined by extreme tenacity. In addition to defeating the former Grand Slam champion Ostapenko, she secured notable victories over world number 6 Elina Svitolina, Olympic champion Belinda Bencic, and Wimbledon finalist Jasmine Paolini. The BBC reports that the victory over Ostapenko was one of the toughest matches of her career, with Kartal acknowledging the mental strain of riding the momentum against such high-caliber opponents.

Kartal will face Maja Chwalinska in the Wuhan Open final this Sunday. Having already surprised herself with how quickly her game has returned, Kartal remains focused on maintaining her current level as she looks toward the remainder of the season.
