Alexandra Eala faces French competitor Diane Parry in the Round of 32 at the WTA 500 Ningbo Open on October 19.

The matchup against French player Diane Parry marks the start of Eala’s campaign at the Ningbo Tennis Center, located in the Zhejiang province of eastern China. This tournament appearance comes as Eala officially misses out on the season-ending WTA Finals in Indian Wells, scheduled for November 8-15. This gap emerged after Eala opted to compete in the Aichi-Nagoya Asian Games instead of the WTA 1000 China Open, a decision that prioritized representing her country over securing additional ranking points. She ultimately earned a bronze medal in Nagoya for her efforts.

Parry, who reached a career-high ranking of World No. 30 in September, enters the match coming off a first-round exit at the Wuhan Open. Known for her one-handed backhand, the French player has recently demonstrated resilience on the court, including saving multiple set points during her Monterrey title run. The outcome of this Round of 32 clash will determine who advances in the Ningbo draw.
