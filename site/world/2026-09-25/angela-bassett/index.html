Angela Bassett Shares Her Reaction to David Jonsson's Black Panther Casting

Angela Bassett, the veteran Black Panther actress, gave her verdict on David Jonsson taking on the iconic superhero role in the MCU.

Angela Bassett, who portrayed Queen Ramonda across multiple Black Panther films, has publicly responded to David Jonsson's casting as the new Black Panther in the Marvel Cinematic Universe. According to Yahoo Entertainment, Bassett shared her honest reaction to the franchise's latest major casting decision, offering an official message welcoming the actor into the MCU's superhero family. Bassett, a franchise veteran who appeared in Black Panther, Black Panther: Wakanda Forever, and other MCU projects, carries significant credibility within the fanbase regarding the role's importance and legacy. Her public endorsement signals continuity and respect for the character's evolution within the franchise. Meanwhile, David Jonsson, known for his role in The Long Walk, is actively preparing for the demanding physical requirements of the Black Panther role. According to Tekedia, Jonsson has been documenting his fitness transformation and training regimen as he prepares to embody the iconic superhero. The actor's commitment to rigorous physical preparation demonstrates the serious approach required for the role, which demands extensive action sequences and athletic performance. Jonsson's casting represents a significant transition for the Black Panther franchise following Chadwick Boseman's passing in 2020. The announcement of a new actor stepping into the role has generated substantial fan interest and discussion, making both Bassett's supportive commentary and Jonsson's preparation noteworthy for MCU followers worldwide.
