Brazil Faces Australia In First Post-World Cup Friendly Match Friday

Brazil plays Australia on Friday, September 24, 2026, in Townsville as part of an international friendly fixture with coach Carlo Ancelotti fielding young talents Endrick and Estêvão in the starting lineup.

Brazil's national football team takes on Australia on Friday, September 24, 2026, at 7 a.m. Brasília time in Townsville, marking the squad's first match following the World Cup. According to Globo and UOL, this friendly encounter represents Brazil's return to competitive action during the FIFA international window, with the Seleção scheduled for two matches during this break. Coach Carlo Ancelotti has confirmed his starting lineup, highlighting the prominent roles of young Brazilian talents Endrick and Estêvão, with Vitor Reis earning a notable inclusion in the squad, as reported by UOL. The match will be broadcast live across multiple Brazilian networks including Globo, SporTV, and Ge TV, ensuring widespread coverage for Brazilian audiences. This fixture serves as an important preparation opportunity for Brazil to assess player form and tactical adjustments following the World Cup cycle. The friendly against Australia provides Ancelotti an opportunity to evaluate emerging players and maintain team cohesion during the international break. Globo noted that specific details regarding lineup absences, match officials, and other pertinent information will be confirmed closer to kickoff. The encounter in Townsville continues Brazil's tradition of scheduling international friendlies to maintain squad fitness and evaluate talent depth ahead of upcoming competitions.
