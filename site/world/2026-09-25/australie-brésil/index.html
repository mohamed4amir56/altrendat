Brazil Faces Australia in Friendly Match With Endrick and Vinicius Starting

Brazil plays a friendly match against Australia on Friday at Queensland Country Bank Stadium, with young talents Endrick and Vinicius starting in Carlo Ancelotti's post-World Cup lineup.

Brazil is taking on Australia in an international friendly match on Friday at 12 p.m. French time at Queensland Country Bank Stadium. According to Foot Mercato, this marks Brazil's first match composition following the World Cup, with coach Carlo Ancelotti fielding emerging talents Endrick and Vinicius as starters. The friendly serves as preparation for both nations as they continue their post-tournament rebuilding. Paris Saint-Germain defender Marquinhos, 32, represents PSG's interests in the Brazilian squad, according to ParisFans. The match comes as Australia reunites for the first time since its World Cup elimination, with the team using the friendly as part of a double-header against Brazil to maintain competitive momentum. According to Yahoo Actualités, Australian captain Jackson Irvine and rising talent Nestory Irankunda are setting standards for the Socceroos as they prepare for the two encounters. The friendly represents an important opportunity for both teams to develop their squads and test new combinations ahead of upcoming international competitions.
