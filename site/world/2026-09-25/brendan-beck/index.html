Yankees Recall Pitcher Brendan Beck For Doubleheader Against Orioles

The New York Yankees recalled right-handed pitcher Brendan Beck from Triple-A Scranton/Wilkes-Barre to the active roster ahead of a Friday doubleheader.

The New York Yankees recalled right-handed pitcher Brendan Beck (#89) from Triple-A Scranton/Wilkes-Barre prior to Game 1 of Friday's doubleheader against the Baltimore Orioles, according to MLB.com. Beck was summoned for a spot start as the Yankees made roster adjustments to finalize their pitching rotation for the final stretch of the regular season. The recall came as the Yankees wrapped up their 2026 regular-season schedule, with Friday's doubleheader against the Orioles and a single game scheduled for Sunday, per NJ.com. Beck's promotion marks a significant opportunity for the pitcher to contribute at the major league level during the critical final games of the season. CBS Sports reported that Beck was specifically summoned ahead of the spot start, indicating the Yankees' confidence in his ability to perform in this important matchup. The roster move also included the recall of RHP Yerry de los Santos (#73), expanding the Yankees' pitching depth for the doubleheader. These late-season recalls are typical as teams optimize their rosters for final games that may have playoff implications. Beck's elevation to the active roster reflects the competitive nature of the end-of-season push, as teams make strategic adjustments to their lineups and pitching staffs.
