Yankees Recall Pitcher Brendan Beck for Doubleheader Spot Start

The New York Yankees recalled right-handed pitcher Brendan Beck from Triple-A Scranton/Wilkes-Barre ahead of a doubleheader, positioning him for a spot start.

The New York Yankees recalled right-handed pitcher Brendan Beck from their Triple-A affiliate Scranton/Wilkes-Barre on September 25, 2026, according to MLB.com. Beck, wearing uniform number 89, was brought up along with fellow right-handed pitcher Yerry de los Santos (number 73) prior to the first game of a doubleheader. According to CBS Sports, Beck was summoned specifically to make a spot start for the Yankees during the two-game set. This roster move reflects the team's need for additional pitching depth during the final stretch of the regular season. Spot starts are common late in the season when teams manage their rotation or face unexpected pitching shortages. Beck's recall indicates the Yankees viewed him as a viable option to address their rotation needs at this critical juncture. Triple-A players being recalled for roster spots typically signal either performance-based merit or pressing circumstances within a Major League team's pitching staff. The timing of this move, coming in late September, suggests the Yankees were preparing for playoff considerations while managing their roster during the regular season's final weeks.
