Missing Two-Year-Old Virginia Girl Bridget Maeve Zeigler Found Safe

Bridget Maeve Zeigler, a two-year-old girl who walked away from her Cumberland County, Virginia home on Thursday evening, has been found safe after authorities launched a large-scale search.

Bridget Maeve Zeigler, a 2-year-old girl from Cumberland County, Virginia, was reported missing after walking away from her home on Hidden Valley Road Thursday evening. According to WSET, authorities initiated a large search operation following her disappearance. Virginia State Police issued a CODI Alert late Thursday night to assist in locating the toddler, as reported by CBS 19 News.

The search involved multiple agencies and resources working to locate the young child. Given her age and the circumstances of her disappearance, the case generated significant attention and urgency among law enforcement. WSET reported that the missing girl has been found safe, concluding the search operation. CBS 19 News confirmed the update that the Cumberland County toddler has been located, bringing relief to her family and the community that mobilized to help find her.

The case highlighted the coordinated response of Virginia State Police and other emergency services in responding to missing child cases. The CODI Alert system, designed to quickly disseminate information about missing children, played a role in coordinating the search efforts. The successful resolution underscores the importance of rapid alert systems and community awareness in locating missing young children.
