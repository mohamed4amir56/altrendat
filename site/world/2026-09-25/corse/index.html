Corsica Addresses Severe Drought Crisis With National Emergency Plan

Corsica's prefects are implementing a €1.3 billion national emergency plan to combat exceptional drought and wildfires affecting the island's agricultural sector.

Corsica is facing an unprecedented drought crisis that has prompted state officials to mobilize emergency resources across the island. According to Stampa Paese, the prefect of Corse and the prefète of Haute-Corse convened major agricultural stakeholders—including the Chamber of Agriculture, ODARC, MSA, SAFER, and various syndicates—to detail deployment of the CASI plan (Climate, Adaptation, Drought, and Fire Plan). The exceptional drought and heat episodes of recent months have necessitated this coordinated response to protect Corsica's agricultural economy. The state representatives outlined the €1.3 billion national emergency fund and specified how it will be applied locally to address the crisis. This funding is designed to support farmers and rural communities facing severe water scarcity and increased wildfire risk. Beyond drought management, Corsica is also experiencing positive developments in its sports sector. Corse Matin reported that the Corsican Handball Excellence Center (Pôle espoir) is expanding its female participation, reflecting broader efforts to develop women's athletics on the island. These parallel initiatives—emergency drought response and youth sports development—illustrate how Corsican authorities are simultaneously managing crisis response while investing in community development. The combination of environmental challenges and sports initiatives demonstrates Corsica's multifaceted approach to regional governance during a critical period of climate stress.
