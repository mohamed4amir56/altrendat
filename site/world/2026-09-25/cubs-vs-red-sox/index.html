📰 Full Coverage & Verified Details:

The Boston Red Sox adjusted the start time for Game 2 of Friday's doubleheader against the Chicago Cubs at Fenway Park, moving the first pitch from 6:05 p.m. ET to 5:35 p.m. ET (4:35 p.m. CT), according to MLB.com. The schedule change was made in consultation with Major League Baseball due to anticipated weather conditions that could affect the game later in the evening.

The weather concerns extend beyond the doubleheader adjustment. According to the Boston Herald, the Red Sox have also discussed the possibility of relocating Saturday's final game of the series against the Cubs, as a major coastal storm is forecasted to impact New England. The organization is actively monitoring the meteorological situation to ensure player safety and game playability throughout the series at Fenway Park.

These proactive scheduling adjustments reflect MLB's commitment to managing games around severe weather events. By moving Friday's second game earlier and considering relocation options for Saturday, the Red Sox and MLB aim to complete the Cubs series while minimizing disruptions from the approaching nor'easter. Fans attending games should monitor official Red Sox channels for any additional updates regarding the weekend schedule.
