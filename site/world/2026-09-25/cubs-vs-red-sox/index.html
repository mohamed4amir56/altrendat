Red Sox Move Game 2 Start Time Against Cubs Due To Weather

The Boston Red Sox have moved Game 2 of their doubleheader against the Chicago Cubs from 6:05 p.m. to 5:35 p.m. ET on Friday to avoid potential weather impacts later in the evening.

The Boston Red Sox have adjusted their schedule for Friday's doubleheader against the Chicago Cubs at Fenway Park, moving Game 2's start time up by 30 minutes. According to MLB.com, the second game will now begin at 5:35 p.m. ET (4:35 p.m. CT) instead of the originally scheduled 6:05 p.m. ET. The change was implemented in consultation with Major League Baseball due to expected weather conditions that could affect gameplay later in the evening.

This scheduling adjustment highlights the challenges teams face when managing games against unpredictable weather, particularly late in the season. By moving the start time earlier, the Red Sox and Cubs aim to complete both games of the doubleheader before adverse weather arrives. According to Over the Monster, the Red Sox and Cubs are likely to play three games to conclude their regular season, though the exact venue breakdown—specifically how many contests will take place at Fenway Park—remains undetermined.

Doubleheaders are relatively uncommon in modern Major League Baseball, typically scheduled only when weather or other circumstances force games to be rescheduled. The adjustment to Game 2's start time demonstrates MLB's proactive approach to protecting the integrity of competition while ensuring both teams and fans can experience complete games. The timing of this series suggests it plays a significant role in both teams' final push toward the end of the regular season.
