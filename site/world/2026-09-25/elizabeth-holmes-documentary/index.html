Nathan Fielder's Elizabeth Holmes Documentary Releases Trailer Ahead of October Premiere

Nathan Fielder's documentary 'You Can See Everything' follows Elizabeth Holmes at her home and features intimate interviews exploring the Theranos founder's life after her fraud conviction.

Nathan Fielder's documentary 'You Can See Everything' is generating significant interest as it offers an unprecedented look at Elizabeth Holmes, the disgraced Theranos founder. According to Variety, the film's full trailer has been released, showcasing Fielder's intimate approach to documenting Holmes's life. The trailer reveals scenes of Fielder living with Holmes and conducting extensive interviews, including interrogations of her husband, providing viewers with rare access to the controversial figure.

Elizabeth Holmes founded Theranos, a blood-testing company that promised revolutionary diagnostic technology but was ultimately exposed as fraudulent. Holmes was convicted of fraud and conspiracy for deceiving investors about the company's capabilities and financial performance. According to ClickOnDetroit, the documentary is set for theatrical release in October 2026, marking a significant moment in media coverage of one of Silicon Valley's most notorious scandals.

Fielder's documentary approach differs from typical crime documentaries by embedding himself directly into Holmes's daily life, capturing candid moments that humanize or contextualize her perspective. The trailer's emotional elements—including scenes of Holmes becoming tearful—suggest the film explores both her public scandal and private responses to her conviction and imprisonment aftermath. This documentary represents a notable shift in how the Holmes story is being told, moving beyond courtroom drama to examine the person behind the fraud case that captivated public attention for years.
