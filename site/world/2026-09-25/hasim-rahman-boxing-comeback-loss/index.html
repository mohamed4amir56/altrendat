Hasim Rahman Loses Heavyweight Comeback Fight at Age 53

Former two-time heavyweight champion Hasim Rahman, 53, was defeated by 40-year-old club fighter Phil Triantafillo in a six-round bout during his attempted boxing comeback.

Hasim Rahman, the legendary 53-year-old former two-time heavyweight champion, suffered a loss in his comeback fight against Phil Triantafillo on Thursday. According to BoxingScene, Rahman was outworked and outlasted by the 40-year-old club fighter over six rounds in what marked an unexpected result for the veteran boxer.

Rahman is renowned for orchestrating one of the greatest upsets in boxing history—his knockout victory over Lennox Lewis in 2001, which earned him the WBC and IBO heavyweight titles. Despite his storied career and legendary status in the sport, his return to the ring proved unsuccessful against the relatively unknown Triantafillo. As reported by FightNews, the unheralded challenger demonstrated superior conditioning and output throughout the bout, ultimately securing the decision.

The defeat highlights the significant challenges aging fighters face when attempting comebacks, particularly against younger opponents. Rahman's loss generated widespread interest among boxing fans and analysts, drawing over 5,000 searches globally. While comebacks are common in professional boxing, Rahman's loss at 53 underscores the physical demands of the sport and the difficulty of reclaiming form years after stepping away from competition. The fight serves as a sobering reminder that even legendary champions face considerable obstacles when returning to professional boxing after extended absences.
