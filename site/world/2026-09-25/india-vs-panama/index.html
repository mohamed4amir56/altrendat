📰 Full Coverage & Verified Details:

India is competing against Panama in an international friendly football match, with live coverage available through BBC Sport's text commentary and match statistics. According to The Times of India, this fixture holds special significance as it coincides with the twilight of Sunil Chhetri's storied international career. Chhetri, India's most celebrated football icon and long-serving captain, is preparing to transition from active player to supporter. The Times of India reported that Chhetri has made peace with stepping away from international football, stating "My time is up, I'm a fan now," reflecting on his anticipated retirement. The match at Sree Kanteerava Stadium represents an emotional milestone for Indian football, as Chhetri has been the driving force behind the national team's performances for nearly two decades. His departure marks the end of an era for Indian football, during which he became the country's all-time leading international scorer and captain. The friendly against Panama provides an opportunity for India to continue building form while allowing fans and teammates to celebrate Chhetri's contributions to the sport.
