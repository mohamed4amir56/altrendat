📰 Full Coverage & Verified Details:

Mark Consuelos, co-host of the daytime talk show 'Live with Kelly and Mark,' revealed that he underwent surgery for an arthritic toe that had caused him significant pain. According to TV Insider, Consuelos announced the 'major surgery' during an on-air health update, admitting he had postponed the procedure for almost a year before finally deciding to move forward. Yahoo reported that the pain from his arthritic toe had become severe enough to make the surgery necessary, despite his initial reluctance to undergo the procedure.

During the announcement on 'Live with Kelly and Mark,' Consuelos's wife and co-host Kelly Ripa couldn't resist making light of the situation, poking fun at the surgery despite its significance to her husband's health. According to SuaraGarut.ID, Consuelos followed standard pre-surgery protocols, including a 24-hour fast before the operation, which Ripa revealed to the audience during a subsequent broadcast. The decision to finally undergo the procedure highlights how arthritis-related foot conditions can progressively worsen, eventually requiring surgical intervention when conservative treatments prove insufficient. The public announcement of his health procedure adds to the ongoing transparency that Consuelos and Ripa maintain with their audience on their daily talk show.
