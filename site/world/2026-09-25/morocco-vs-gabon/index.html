📰 Full Coverage & Verified Details:

Morocco and Gabon are preparing for a crucial 2027 African Cup of Nations (AFCON) qualifier match scheduled for Friday in Rabat. According to Goal.com, the Royal Moroccan Football Federation has announced the inclusion of Ilias Akhomach, a forward from Spanish club Villarreal, in the Morocco squad. However, the federation did not immediately clarify which player would be dropped to accommodate Akhomach's inclusion, sparking speculation among fans and analysts about potential squad rotation, with some sources suggesting midfielder Brahim Diaz could be omitted.

Gabon, competing as the Panthers, is preparing for the encounter with determination. According to Morocco World News, Gabon's Minister of Sports, Paul Ulrich Kessany, visited the national team at its hotel in Rabat to encourage the squad ahead of the Friday fixture. The minister's visit underscores the importance of the match for Gabon, as the team seeks to secure points in their AFCON qualifying campaign.

The match represents a significant moment in both nations' pursuit of qualification for the 2027 AFCON tournament. Morocco's strategic squad adjustments reflect their competitive approach, while Gabon's high-level governmental support demonstrates the political and national significance of the encounter. The fixture will provide both teams an opportunity to make progress in the continental qualifying stage.
