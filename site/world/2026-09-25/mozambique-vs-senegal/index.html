Senegal Begins AFCON 2027 Qualifying Campaign Against Mozambique Under New Coach Vieira

⚡ Real-Time Coverage & Breaking Insight
🔥 Inside the Story: What Just Happened

Senegal kicks off their Africa Cup of Nations 2027 qualifiers with an away match against Mozambique on Friday, marking the debut of new coach Patrick Vieira as the team seeks to rebuild after World Cup disappointment.

📰 Full Coverage & Verified Details:

Senegal is competing in the opening match of AFCON 2027 qualifying against Mozambique at the Maputo National Stadium, beginning a new era under French coach Patrick Vieira. According to Seneweb, this crucial away game represents Senegal's first official test under their new management, with the team aiming to deliver results for the Senegalese public following recent setbacks.

Patrick Vieira rejected favorite status in his pre-match comments, telling reporters that such terminology is not useful, and instead emphasized the need for "humility, seriousness, and concentration" against a tactically disciplined Mozambican side. As reported by CAFOnline.com, Senegal will be without key playmaker Sadio Mané due to injury during this international break. However, Vieira stated his intent to use this opportunity to showcase young talent, praising the depth of the squad and expressing confidence in emerging players to seize their chance at this level.

Defender Moussa Niakhaté acknowledged Senegal's recent World Cup failure while insisting that disappointment now belongs to the past. According to Seneweb, Niakhaté stated, "We took a beating, but that's in the past now," and emphasized that the team's goal remains making the Senegalese people proud. Despite limited preparation time due to staggered player arrivals, Niakhaté expressed optimism about the new coaching staff, crediting them for bringing fresh energy and a shared football philosophy. Vieira concluded pre-match preparations by pledging to play "with pride and determination to give the nation what it deserves" as Senegal launches their 2027 AFCON qualifying campaign.
