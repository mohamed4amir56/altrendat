📰 Full Coverage & Verified Details:

Nick Bosa, the San Francisco 49ers' star edge rusher, was placed on the team's injury report on September 24 ahead of their Week 3 matchup against the Arizona Cardinals scheduled for September 27, according to Yahoo Sports and NBC Sports Bay Area. The injury report indicates that Bosa is dealing with multiple lower-body injuries, including both a calf and knee issue, which forced him to miss media availability on September 23.

According to Bleacher Report, the calf injury is expected to cause Bosa to miss time, presenting a significant challenge for the 49ers despite their strong 2-0 start to the 2024 season. Bosa, a premier pass rusher and defensive anchor for San Francisco, is a critical component of the team's defense. His absence could impact the team's ability to generate pressure on opposing quarterbacks, particularly as they prepare to face the Arizona Cardinals in Week 3.

The timing of the injury comes at a crucial juncture for the defending NFC West contenders, who have begun their season positively. The extent of Bosa's injury and his expected timeline for return remain under evaluation, with teams typically assessing lower-body injuries carefully to prevent long-term complications. The 49ers' medical staff will determine his availability for Sunday's game and beyond as they navigate the demanding NFL schedule.
