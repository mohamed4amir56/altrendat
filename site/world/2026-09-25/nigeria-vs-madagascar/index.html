Nigeria Faces Madagascar in CAN 2027 Qualifier Without Star Forward Osimhen

Nigeria plays Madagascar in the opening match of Group L in African Cup of Nations 2027 qualifying, with Victor Osimhen unavailable due to injury.

Nigeria and Madagascar faced off in the first match of Group L in the 2027 African Cup of Nations qualifying campaign, according to Africa Top Sports. The official squad compositions for the fixture were released ahead of the competition, setting the stage for what promised to be a competitive opener in the qualification phase.

A significant challenge for Nigeria came with the absence of star striker Victor Osimhen. According to Foot Mercato, the Nigerian national team had to proceed without Osimhen for the opening matches of CAN 2027 qualification. The Galatasaray forward has been sidelined since early September, limiting his availability for international duty. Yahoo Actualités reported that Osimhen's request for protective exemption from club duties to participate in international matches was rejected, leaving the Nigerian Football Federation to plan without him for this crucial qualifier.

The match represented an important opening fixture in the qualifying campaign, as teams jostled for positioning within their respective groups. Nigeria's squad selection reflected the need to compensate for Osimhen's absence while maintaining competitive strength against Madagascar. The qualifier forms part of the broader 2027 CAN qualification process, where African nations compete for spots in the continental tournament. With Osimhen unavailable, Nigeria relied on alternative attacking options to secure points in what would prove to be a consequential match in their qualification journey.
