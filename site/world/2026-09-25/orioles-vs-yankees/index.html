Orioles and Yankees Play Doubleheader Friday Due to Nor'easter

The Baltimore Orioles and New York Yankees moved their Saturday game to Friday and are playing a doubleheader to avoid a forecasted nor'easter affecting the New York area.

The Baltimore Orioles and New York Yankees have rescheduled their matchup to accommodate severe weather, with both teams now playing a single-admission doubleheader on Friday instead of their originally planned Saturday contest. According to MLB.com, the postponement stems from a forecasted nor'easter expected to impact the New York area over the next two days. First pitch for the doubleheader at Yankee Stadium is set for 4:05 p.m. on Friday, September 25.

The schedule change has added significance beyond the regular season matchup, as CC Sabathia's number retirement ceremony—honoring the legendary Yankees pitcher—will now take place one day earlier than originally scheduled. Newsday reported that this acceleration has created complications for fans, as Saturday tickets can only be exchanged for games during the next season rather than being honored for the Friday doubleheader.

According to MASN, the Orioles entered the doubleheader having been eliminated from the Wild Card chase, making the games less consequential for Baltimore's playoff hopes. The Orioles' Game 1 lineup featured Dylan Beavers leading off in right field, with Christian Franklin in left and Coby Mayo serving as designated hitter. The doubleheader format ensures both teams complete their series despite the adverse weather conditions forecasted for the region.
