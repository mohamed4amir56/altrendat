Rob Lowe Explains Why He Was Happy During Will Smith Oscars Slap

Rob Lowe, who hosted an infamously bad 1989 Oscars ceremony, reflected on being the only person pleased when Will Smith slapped Chris Rock at the 2022 Academy Awards.

Rob Lowe recently opened up about his unusual perspective on the viral Will Smith and Chris Rock Oscars incident, according to Yahoo News Canada. Lowe explained that he was "the only person happy" when Smith walked on stage and slapped Rock during the 2022 Academy Awards ceremony. His distinct reaction stems from personal experience—Lowe himself hosted the notoriously problematic 1989 Oscars, which became one of the most criticized ceremonies in the event's history. Having endured that infamous hosting experience decades earlier, Lowe found an ironic silver lining in the Smith-Rock confrontation, which briefly shifted negative attention away from his own troubled hosting performance.

Beyond his Oscars commentary, Lowe has been reflecting more broadly on his remarkable career trajectory. Speaking with Chicks in the Office, as reported by People.com, the actor opened up about his experiences as a famous and single man during the 1980s, describing that era as "better than you dream." According to Martin Cid Magazine, Lowe's professional journey has been marked by significant comeback narratives. The outlet noted that despite surviving a serious tape scandal and battling alcoholism for a decade, Lowe successfully rebuilt his career, achieving major success with his role in The West Wing and continuing to maintain relevance well into his 60s. At 62 years old, Lowe has demonstrated remarkable resilience and career longevity, proving that his comeback "never stopped coming back" throughout multiple decades in Hollywood.
