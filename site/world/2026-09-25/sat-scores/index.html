14-Year-Old Georgia Student Achieves Perfect SAT Score of 1600

A 14-year-old Fulton County student named Ashrit Talluri earned a perfect 1600 on the official SAT after consistently scoring perfectly on practice tests, becoming one of the youngest test-takers to achieve this milestone.

Ashrit Talluri, a 14-year-old student from Fulton County, Georgia, has achieved a perfect score of 1600 on the SAT, according to FOX 5 Atlanta. His accomplishment is particularly noteworthy because most students typically take the SAT during their high school years, making Talluri significantly younger than the average test-taker. Before taking the official exam, Talluri had already demonstrated his mastery of the material by scoring a perfect 1600 on three separate practice SATs, establishing a consistent pattern of excellence.

Beyond his individual achievement, Talluri has become a resource for other students preparing for the exam. FOX 5 Atlanta reports that the 14-year-old now dedicates some of his free time to writing SAT practice questions and helping fellow students develop effective test-taking strategies. This mentoring work reflects a broader commitment to supporting academic excellence in his community.

The trending interest in SAT scores has also been driven by recent reports from Michigan, where MLive.com published an analysis of the state's highest-scoring schools for the 2025-26 academic year. According to the report, schools in West Michigan and Metro Detroit have dominated rankings of Michigan's highest average SAT scores, highlighting significant regional variations in student test performance across the country.
