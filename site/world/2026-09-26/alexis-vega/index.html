Alexis Vega Eyes Mexico Exit After Snub by Coach Rafael Márquez

⚡ Real-Time Coverage & Breaking Insight
🔥 Inside the Story: What Just Happened

Diablos Rojos forward Alexis Vega announces plans to retire from Mexico's national team following his exclusion from Rafael Márquez's squad selection.

📰 Full Coverage & Verified Details:

Alexis Vega, the Diablos Rojos striker, has signaled his intention to step away from Mexico's national team after failing to receive a call-up from newly appointed coach Rafael Márquez, according to MARCA. The forward's potential retirement from the Tricolor marks a significant moment in Mexican football as the squad undergoes transition under Márquez's leadership.

Vega's decision reflects broader considerations about making space for emerging talent within the Mexican national program. According to Claro Sports, the striker is analyzing a possible retirement to open opportunities for a new generation of footballers under Márquez's management. This move aligns with Mexico's ongoing squad rebuilding efforts as the federation prepares for future competitive cycles.

The development comes as Rafael Márquez, the legendary defender and newly installed Mexico coach, shapes his squad around younger prospects and his tactical vision. Vega's potential departure would represent another chapter in Mexico's generational shift in international football. The forward's consideration reflects the competitive pressure within Mexican football, where opportunities for experienced players can shift rapidly under new coaching regimes. Whether Vega formally announces his retirement or eventually receives future call-ups remains to be seen, but his current stance underscores the evolution taking place within Mexican national team selection under Márquez's tenure.
