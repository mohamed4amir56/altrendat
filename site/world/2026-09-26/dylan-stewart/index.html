📰 Full Coverage & Verified Details:

South Carolina's star edge rusher Dylan Stewart found himself at the center of controversy during the Gamecocks' matchup against Alabama when he threw a punch in plain sight of a game official without facing ejection. According to AL.com, rules analysts expressed shock at the decision, as such conduct typically results in automatic ejection under college football regulations.

The incident was not Stewart's only disciplinary moment of the contest. Yahoo Sports reported that South Carolina coach Shane Beamer was caught on sideline cameras ripping into Stewart as he returned from the field, visibly frustrated with his star defensive player's conduct during the game.

Stewart, a key defensive weapon for the Gamecocks, appeared to lose his composure during the intense SEC matchup. The combination of the punch incident and Beamer's sideline rebuke highlighted mounting frustration within South Carolina's defensive unit as the team battled the Crimson Tide. The fact that Stewart avoided ejection despite the apparent infraction left observers questioning the consistency of penalty enforcement during the high-profile contest.

Beamer's public display of frustration suggested he was attempting to rein in his star pass rusher's aggressive behavior. The sideline confrontation underscored the coach's efforts to maintain discipline while managing the pressure of competing in one of college football's marquee rivalries. Both incidents—the unpunished punch and the coaching rebuke—became focal points of postgame discussion surrounding South Carolina's performance.
