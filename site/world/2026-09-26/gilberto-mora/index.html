📰 Full Coverage & Verified Details:

Gilberto Mora has been entrusted with one of international football's most prestigious honors: Mexico's No. 10 shirt. According to LiveScore, Mora's assignment to this iconic number carries immense pressure, pride, and historical baggage that comes with representing Mexico's attacking legacy.

Manager Javier Aguirre has made his position crystal clear on the decision. "Who better for the No 10?" Aguirre declared, as reported by OneFootball, expressing unwavering confidence in Mora's ability to handle the responsibility. The statement underscores Aguirre's strategic vision for Mexico's future and his belief that Mora possesses the technical skill and mentality required to succeed in such a demanding role.

The No. 10 jersey represents more than statistics for Mexico—it symbolizes creativity, leadership, and the burden of carrying the team's offensive ambitions. Previous wearers have left indelible marks on Mexican football history, creating sky-high expectations for any successor. Mora now joins an exclusive lineage, tasked with delivering performances that match the shirt's legendary reputation.

Aguirre, focused on his upcoming managerial challenge with Valencia in Spain, nonetheless prioritized clarifying his faith in Mora before transitioning to his new club. This public endorsement signals that Mora will be central to Mexico's tactical approach moving forward, particularly in upcoming continental competitions and World Cup qualifications.
