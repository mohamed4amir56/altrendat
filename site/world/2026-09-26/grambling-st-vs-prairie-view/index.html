Grambling State's football team will launch its Southwestern Athletic Conference (SWAC) season against Prairie View A&M University in a highly anticipated matchup, according to the Lincoln Parish Journal. The rivalry game, known as the State Fair Classic, returns to Dallas for the 2026 season and represents a critical early-conference test for the G-Men.

The contest will pit Grambling's defensive unit against Prairie View's offensive firepower, particularly quarterback Kevin Carter, who has emerged as a key threat for the Panthers. According to the Lincoln Parish Journal, Grambling's defensive lineup—anchored by Marcellius Johnson and defensive back Katrevick Davis—will focus on containing Carter's production throughout the matchup.

As Yahoo Sports reports, the State Fair Classic is an annual tradition in Dallas, drawing significant attention from SWAC and national college football audiences. The game carries weight beyond typical regular-season importance, functioning as both a conference opener and a showcase event for both programs.

For Grambling State, the season-opening conference challenge provides an immediate opportunity to establish defensive dominance and set the tone for SWAC play.
