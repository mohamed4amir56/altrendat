📰 Full Coverage & Verified Details:

Henry Cejudo, the decorated former UFC double champion, made a stunning crossover debut in professional boxing by defeating Javon Walton in his Misfits Boxing premiere. According to Yahoo Sports, Cejudo's commanding performance against the younger fighter showcased why he remains one of combat sports' most dominant athletes.

Walton, best known for his breakout role in the HBO series Euphoria, has been quietly building a boxing career alongside his acting pursuits. The BBC reported that the young fighter has deep roots in the sport before his unexpected rise to television fame, making Saturday's clash against Cejudo a career-defining test.

The fight marked a significant moment for both athletes: Cejudo's transition into the lucrative Misfits Boxing platform, which has become a major player in celebrity boxing and crossover events, and Walton's attempt to prove himself against elite-level competition. Cejudo's victory underscores his enduring athleticism and competitive hunger years after leaving the UFC, while Walton's defeat highlights the steep learning curve when facing battle-tested champions.

Misfits Boxing, founded by KSI and Logan Paul, continues to blur the lines between traditional combat sports and entertainment, attracting mainstream attention and unprecedented viewership numbers. Cejudo's participation elevates the promotion's credibility by bringing genuine championship pedigree to its card. The win positions Cejudo as a formidable force in the boxing exhibition space and hints at potential high-profile matchups ahead.
