📰 Full Coverage & Verified Details:

Jon Watts, the acclaimed director behind Marvel's Spider-Man: No Way Home, is set to take the helm of Lucasfilm's next Star Wars movie, according to The Hollywood Reporter. The move marks a significant shift for Watts, who has spent years building the Marvel Cinematic Universe's web-slinger franchise, as he ventures into the galaxy far, far away.

The new Star Wars project is part of a larger trilogy being written by screenwriter Simon Kinberg, reportedly continuing the Skywalker Saga narrative that has defined the franchise for decades. The Direct confirmed Watts is transitioning from his Marvel commitments to this major Star Wars undertaking.

However, industry insiders have raised questions about the official confirmation status. According to Bespin Bulletin, citing reliable Hollywood sources, the trilogy written by Kinberg remains without a formally announced director as of recent reports, suggesting the deal may still be in negotiation stages or awaiting official announcement.

Watts' selection represents Lucasfilm's strategy to bring established blockbuster directors into the Star Wars universe. His proven track record with the Spider-Man franchise—delivering critical and commercial success—positions him as a strong candidate to revitalize the Star Wars cinematic slate following mixed fan and critical reception of recent trilogy entries. The project signals Disney and Lucasfilm's commitment to expanding the Skywalker Saga with fresh creative vision.
