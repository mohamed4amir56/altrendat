Arkansas State is gearing up for a rematch against Kennesaw State's relentless offensive system in a non-conference battle set for Saturday, September 26. The key challenge facing the Red Wolves centers on tempo and execution. Kennesaw State's uptempo system is designed to limit defensive substitutions and create confusion on the field, a strategy that proved particularly effective early in last year's contest. Arkansas State coaches have had an offseason to develop counter-strategies, focusing on communication and situational preparation to manage the pace of play.

This matchup marks the conclusion of Kennesaw State's two-game road swing, according to Kennesaw State University Athletics. The Owls are wrapping up non-conference play before transitioning into conference competition. For Arkansas State, the game represents an opportunity to break through against a system that previously caused early-game difficulties.

Both teams bring different expectations into the contest. Kennesaw State has built its program identity around high-tempo football, while Arkansas State must execute a defensive game plan specifically tailored to slow the Owls' pace and prevent the first-quarter offensive explosions that characterized their previous encounter.
