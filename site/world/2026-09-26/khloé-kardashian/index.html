📰 Full Coverage & Verified Details:

Khloé Kardashian is scaling up her beauty business empire while offering a candid look at the longevity of the family's flagship television franchise. According to The Hollywood Reporter, the media personality and businesswoman recently sat down to discuss her latest ventures, including her expanding Sephora fragrance line and upcoming TV and podcast projects. SuaraGarut.ID reported that Kardashian has confirmed her perfume collection is now rolling into Sephora stores, marking a significant retail expansion for her beauty brand.

In a separate interview covered by Yahoo Entertainment, Kardashian addressed the future of 'The Kardashians,' the family's long-running Hulu reality series currently in its eighth season. Rather than announcing an imminent end date, Kardashian explained what could eventually bring the show to a close, leaving the door open for continued production. The Hollywood Reporter noted that Kardashian discussed the potential longevity of the series alongside her other entrepreneurial pursuits, signaling the family's continued commitment to both entertainment and business ventures.

Kardashian's strategic moves underscore the family's diversification beyond reality television. Her fragrance expansion into Sephora—a major beauty retail destination—positions her alongside established luxury fragrance brands and reflects the broader monetization of the Kardashian brand across multiple consumer categories. With 'The Kardashians' showing no signs of stopping and her beauty line gaining retail prominence, Kardashian is reinforcing her status as both entertainer and savvy businesswoman during a pivotal moment for the franchise.
