📰 Full Coverage & Verified Details:

Deportivo LSM, the club co-founded by Luis Suárez and Lionel Messi, announced a major managerial change as the team navigates its inaugural campaign in the Uruguayan divisional structure. According to El Observador, the club appointed 37-year-old Álvaro Apólito, a former player for Nacional, as its new head coach. Apólito assumed his position alongside his complete technical staff.

The managerial shift comes on the heels of the club's separation from its previous head coach, as reported by El Intransigente. That coaching cycle ended after Deportivo LSM suffered a defeat to Mar de Fondo and confronted mounting challenges during its debut season competing at the divisional level.

The appointment of Apólito represents a strategic pivot for the ambitious project backed by two of football's most decorated players. At 37, the former defender brings experience from his time at Nacional, one of Uruguay's most prominent clubs, where he likely gained insights into both playing and potentially coaching structures. His hiring signals Suárez and Messi's commitment to stabilizing the club's competitive performance despite early-season setbacks.

Deportivo LSM's inaugural campaign has tested the ambitions of its high-profile ownership. The move to bring in Apólito and his technical team underscores the owners' determination to establish winning traditions and institutional stability as they build the club's long-term identity in Uruguayan football.
