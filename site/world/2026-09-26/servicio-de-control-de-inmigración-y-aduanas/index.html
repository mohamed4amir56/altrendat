📰 Full Coverage & Verified Details:

Hispanic entrepreneurs across America are reeling from the economic fallout of intensified Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) operations, prompting a desperate national rescue effort. According to Univision, the "Compra Latino / Buy Latino" campaign has mobilized to counteract the drastic financial impact hammering Latino-owned businesses as fear of ICE raids spreads through immigrant communities.

The crisis extends beyond immediate enforcement actions. As reported by El País, activists and legislators are sounding the alarm about what they call the "ICE Tax"—a surge in product and service prices driven by severe labor shortages. "No hay gente para trabajar" (There's no one to work), business owners lament, citing the exodus of workers due to heightened immigration enforcement under Trump administration policies.

The confluence of mass deportation fears and labor market disruption is strangling small enterprises that depend on immigrant workforces and immigrant consumer bases. Latino-owned shops, restaurants, and service providers face a double squeeze: customers staying home due to deportation anxiety, and workers disappearing from payrolls. Univision's reporting underscores how ICE operations have created a chilling effect throughout Hispanic communities, translating directly into lost revenue and closing doors.

Legislators and community activists are explicitly linking Trump's aggressive immigration enforcement to what amounts to an invisible tax on American consumers and entrepreneurs. The "Compra Latino / Buy Latino" initiative represents grassroots resistance, urging citizens to support endangered Hispanic businesses before the economic damage becomes irreversible.
