SNL Season 52 Kicks Off With Knicks Star Jalen Brunson's Hosting Debut Tonight

⚡ Real-Time Coverage & Breaking Insight
🔥 Inside the Story: What Just Happened

Saturday Night Live returns September 26 with New York Knicks point guard Jalen Brunson making his first appearance as host, alongside musical guest KATSEYE and two fresh featured cast members.

📰 Full Coverage & Verified Details:

Saturday Night Live roars back for Season 52 on September 26 with a high-profile debut lineup, according to NBC News and NBC. Jalen Brunson, the dynamic New York Knicks point guard, takes the stage as host for his first time, bringing star power from the hardwood to Studio 8H. KATSEYE, the rising K-pop group, performs as the musical guest for the premiere episode.

The new season marks significant cast reshuffles, as reported by E! News. Two fresh featured players—Saidah Belo-Osagie and Grace Reiter—join the ensemble, hungry to establish themselves on live television. Meanwhile, longtime cast member Chloe Fineman departs the show, closing out her tenure after multiple seasons.

Brunson's hosting gig represents another chapter in his explosive rise since joining the Knicks, while KATSEYE continues building momentum following their recent debut activities. The introduction of new featured players signals SNL's commitment to rotating fresh comedic talent into the legendary sketch-comedy format. With Brunson's NBA profile and the new cast members stepping into the spotlight, NBC is banking on the premiere to capture substantial viewership as the show enters its 52nd season of live comedy and music.
