📰 Full Coverage & Verified Details:

Kazuchika Okada cemented his reign as AEW International Champion by defeating Tommaso Ciampa in a high-stakes title defense at AEW All Out on September 26 in Chicago, according to POST Wrestling. The match marked the first championship defense for Okada against Ciampa in a collision between two of wrestling's most accomplished technical performers.

The bout carried significant personal weight for Ciampa, who had family members ringside supporting him during the clash, as reported by Cageside Seats. Despite the emotional backdrop and his quest to capture AEW gold, Ciampa fell short against the composed champion known as "The Rainmaker."

The loss represents another frustrating chapter in Ciampa's AEW pay-per-view history. According to Cageside Seats, this defeat extends Ciampa's unfortunate streak of being unable to secure victories on major AEW pay-per-view cards, a trend that continues to plague his championship aspirations on the biggest stages.

Okada's retention underscores his status as a dominant force holding the AEW International Championship. The victory demonstrates why he remains one of wrestling's most credible titleholders, successfully fending off a challenger of Ciampa's caliber. For Ciampa, the loss signals he must find a new path forward in AEW, with his pay-per-view performance becoming an increasingly glaring obstacle in his championship pursuits.
