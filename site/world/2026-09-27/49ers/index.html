📰 Full Coverage & Verified Details:

The San Francisco 49ers and Arizona Cardinals clash in a pivotal Week 3 matchup at Levi Stadium, with both teams navigating significant roster adjustments ahead of Sunday's contest. According to the official 49ers website, star pass rusher Nick Bosa has been ruled out for the game against Arizona, alongside defensive back Romello Height, marking a notable absence in San Francisco's front seven during a critical early-season test.

The 49ers enter Week 3 seeking to validate their fast start after opening the season competitively, per CBS Sports reporting. However, the loss of Bosa—one of the NFL's premier edge rushers—presents a considerable challenge to their defensive scheme against a Cardinals offense that has produced mixed results through the season's opening weeks.

On Arizona's side, the injury picture looks considerably brighter. According to the Arizona Cardinals' official statement, cornerstone cornerback Garrett Williams is set to make his season debut against San Francisco, bolstering the secondary after missing early contests. Additionally, CBS Sports reports that Cardinals wide receiver Roy Lopez has been cleared to play in Week 3, providing offensive depth for Arizona's passing attack.

Mike Evans remains available for the 49ers despite recent health concerns, according to the official inactives list. The matchup represents a crucial early-season barometer for both NFC West contenders, with San Francisco looking to maintain momentum and Arizona attempting to stabilize an inconsistent start. Broadcast details are available through the 49ers' official streaming partners for nationwide audiences.
