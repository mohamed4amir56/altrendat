📰 Full Coverage & Verified Details:

New Orleans Saints running back Alvin Kamara is facing a dramatic reduction in playing time as the team heads into Week 3 against the Las Vegas Raiders, according to Yahoo Sports. The elite playmaker managed only a 29.7% snap share in his season debut, a stark decline from his usual elite usage patterns that has fantasy managers and Saints fans questioning his availability.

Kamara's limited role stems from a knee injury that has hampered his preparation and practice participation, Yahoo Sports reported. Despite receiving substantial touches in Week 2, the workload appears unsustainable given his current health status. Pro Football Network ranks Kamara 41st among running backs for Week 3, a shocking drop for a player who has consistently been among the league's most productive offensive weapons when healthy.

Heavy.com noted the inconsistency in his role, with the star back receiving a significant touch total in the first half of Week 2, only to see that production pattern fail to materialize heading into the Raiders matchup. The Saints appear to be managing his return cautiously rather than deploying him in his traditional three-down role.

This cautious approach reflects growing concerns about Kamara's availability as New Orleans navigates early-season injury management. Fantasy football players and bettors closely monitoring the Saints' backfield must weigh Kamara's name recognition against his current diminished snap counts. The team's handling of his usage suggests the knee issue remains serious enough to warrant restricted playing time despite his Pro Bowl pedigree.
