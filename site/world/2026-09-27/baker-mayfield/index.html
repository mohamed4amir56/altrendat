📰 Full Coverage & Verified Details:

Two of college football's most decorated quarterbacks will meet again on the NFL's biggest stage when Tampa Bay Buccaneers starter Baker Mayfield faces Minnesota Vikings quarterback Kyler Murray in Week 3. The matchup carries special significance as both players won the Heisman Trophy during their tenure at the University of Oklahoma, where they pushed each other to excellence as teammates.

Murray's participation in the game adds intrigue after he suffered a concussion in Week 1, leaving his availability uncertain heading into the Vikings' contest against Tampa Bay. According to NBC Sports, the quarterback's return from injury presents a separate complication to an already noteworthy divisional clash.

Both Mayfield and Murray were selected as No. 1 overall picks in consecutive NFL drafts, underscoring their status as generational talent at the quarterback position. The Pioneer Press reported that the two former Oklahoma stars pushed each other throughout their college careers en route to winning football's most prestigious individual award.

As the two prepare to face off as starting NFL quarterbacks, Heavy.com noted that Mayfield recently opened up about Murray and their shared history before the Week 3 showdown. Their reunion represents a rare convergence of college football's elite—two Heisman winners competing at the professional level—and marks a significant moment in both players' NFL journeys as they vie for divisional supremacy.
