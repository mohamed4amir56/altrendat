📰 Full Coverage & Verified Details:

Brandon McNulty of Phoenix captured the elite men's road race at the 2026 UCI Road World Championships in Montreal on a chaotic 273.4-kilometer course, securing the rainbow jersey and marking the United States' first world road race title in 33 years, according to NBC Sports.

McNulty launched a decisive solo breakaway with 25 kilometers remaining in the grueling race, a tactical move that proved decisive against the international field, Cyclingnews reported. The American's attack showcased both individual brilliance and the strength of his team's support throughout the demanding race.

The victory represents a historic moment for U.S. cycling. McNulty becomes only the third American man to win the road race world championship, according to NBC Sports. The last American to claim this honor was 33 years prior, making this triumph a watershed moment for the nation's professional cycling program.

Cycling Weekly noted that McNulty's team displayed exceptional tactical execution in the chaotic race environment, with American riders coordinating effectively to isolate their leader and control the race dynamics. This team-oriented approach ultimately proved the difference, allowing McNulty to launch his winning attack when it mattered most.

The Phoenix native's victory at the world championships elevates him among cycling's elite and signals a resurgence in American road cycling competitiveness on the sport's grandest stage.
