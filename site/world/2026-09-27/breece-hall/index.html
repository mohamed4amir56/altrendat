📰 Full Coverage & Verified Details:

New York Jets running back Breece Hall has established himself as a critical fantasy asset heading into Sunday's Week 3 matchup against the Detroit Lions, according to Yahoo Sports. The 22-year-old back arrives ranked as the RB12 in PFN's weekly rankings after two consecutive productive outings that showcase his dual-threat capability.

Hall accumulated 102 rushing yards and one touchdown on 22 carries against Tennessee in Week 2, demonstrating his role as the primary ballcarrier in the Jets offense. That performance was followed by an equally impressive receiving line, where he caught all five targets for 63 yards in New York's overtime loss to Green Bay, per Yahoo Sports.

The matchup grades favorably for Hall's fantasy ceiling. Detroit's defense has surrendered nine touchdowns across just 10 red zone opportunities through two weeks, creating an inviting scoring environment for visiting offensive players. Yahoo Sports notes the overall matchup grades out as a plus-one advantage for the Jets running back in fantasy contests.

Bleacher Nation's analysis of player prop betting suggests Hall carries significant anytime touchdown odds heading into the 1 p.m. ET kickoff at Ford Field. His combination of carries, passing-game involvement, and the Lions' defensive vulnerability in the red zone positions Hall as a reliable fantasy start across most league formats this week.
