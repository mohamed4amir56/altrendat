📰 Full Coverage & Verified Details:

Brian Burns is active for the New York Giants' Week 3 showdown against the Tennessee Titans on Sunday, according to Yahoo Sports and Giants.com. The star linebacker, who suffered a sprained ankle late in Week 2's loss to the Los Angeles Rams, did not practice throughout the week but has been cleared to play.

The decision represents a significant lift for the Giants defense, which has been battered by injuries. New York lost quarterback Jaxson Dart to injury, making the defensive unit's availability even more critical, according to the New York Post.

Burns, a key pass-rusher and run defender, had been a question mark entering Sunday after the ankle injury. His absence from practice sessions suggested uncertainty about his availability. However, the Giants medical staff cleared him to participate, indicating the injury is manageable enough that he can contribute on game day.

This development comes as New York scrambles to maintain competitive balance during a challenging stretch. With quarterback issues compounding defensive concerns, Burns' presence in the lineup provides defensive leadership and pressure capabilities essential to containing Tennessee's offense.

The Titans present a formidable test, making Burns' availability a notable advantage for the Giants' defensive schemes. His participation could prove decisive in a game where New York cannot afford further defensive lapses given their offensive instability. The linebacker's clearance marks one of few positive roster updates for the team heading into Week 3.
