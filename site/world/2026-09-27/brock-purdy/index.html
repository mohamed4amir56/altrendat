49ers Backup Mac Jones Draws Trade Interest From Giants Amid Purdy Injury

⚡ Real-Time Coverage & Breaking Insight
🔥 Inside the Story: What Just Happened

The New York Giants are actively pursuing a trade for San Francisco 49ers backup quarterback Mac Jones, who led the team to a 5-3 record while starter Brock Purdy recovered from a toe injury.

📰 Full Coverage & Verified Details:

The New York Giants have emerged as a serious suitor for San Francisco 49ers backup quarterback Mac Jones, according to reports from NBC Sports Bay Area and Yahoo Sports. Jones stepped into a significant role for the defending conference champion 49ers during the 2024 season, guiding the team to a 5-3 record while starting quarterback Brock Purdy sidelined himself with a toe injury.

The Giants' interest in acquiring Jones signals their search for quarterback stability heading into the offseason. Grant Cohn of Yahoo Sports highlighted the trade possibilities, noting that Mac Jones has demonstrated his capability to manage a competitive offense despite limited previous starting experience.

Jones' performance with San Francisco came after he was acquired via trade from the New England Patriots, where he had struggled in recent seasons. His success filling in for Purdy represents a compelling audition for other NFL teams seeking proven quarterback depth or a potential starter.

The 49ers' quarterback situation remains fluid as Purdy recovers from his injury, creating both uncertainty and opportunity around Mac Jones' future with the organization. If San Francisco decides to move forward primarily with Purdy as their franchise quarterback, Jones becomes an attractive trade asset given his recent performance and lower salary cap implications compared to established starters elsewhere in the league.

With multiple teams hunting for quarterback solutions, the Giants appear positioned to make a meaningful offer for Jones before the trade deadline closes.
