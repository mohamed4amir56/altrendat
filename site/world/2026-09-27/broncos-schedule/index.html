📰 Full Coverage & Verified Details:

The Denver Broncos announced that wide receiver Marvin Mims Jr. will be inactive for their Week 3 Sunday Night Football contest against the Los Angeles Rams, according to the Denver Broncos official website. Mims, who had been listed as questionable heading into the game, will not suit up as the team navigates a pivotal early-season matchup.

The loss of Mims represents a significant blow to Denver's passing attack as the Broncos seek to establish momentum in the competitive AFC West. According to NBC Sports, both squads will be without key receiving weapons, with the Rams also missing star receiver Puka Nacua on the same evening. This dual absence shapes the strategic landscape for both offensive coordinators preparing game plans for the primetime showcase.

The inactivity list was made official by both teams prior to kickoff, per Yahoo Sports reporting. Mims' unavailability forces the Broncos to lean more heavily on their remaining receiver corps as they confront an LA defense looking to exploit any weaknesses in Denver's aerial assault. The Sunday night fixture underscores the unpredictable nature of NFL scheduling, where injuries and availability decisions can dramatically shift competitive balance week-to-week. With Mims sidelined, backup receivers and tight ends will shoulder expanded responsibility in what figures to be a closely contested divisional battle on national television.
