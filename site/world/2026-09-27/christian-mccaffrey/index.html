📰 Full Coverage & Verified Details:

Christian McCaffrey enters Week 3 as Pro Football Network's RB5 in fantasy rankings, with the San Francisco 49ers hosting the Arizona Cardinals. According to Yahoo Sports, McCaffrey's carries have dropped to exactly 10 in each of his first two games this season—a stark contrast to his 311 carries last year—though this appears partly attributable to subtle load management and the team's dominant performances in consecutive 20-plus-point victories.

Despite the reduced rushing volume, McCaffrey's receiving work remains intact, positioning him as a reliable fantasy asset. RotoBaller notes that the 49ers' running back is set for a bounce-back opportunity against Arizona's defense. The Cardinals visit San Francisco in a matchup that could showcase McCaffrey's full complement of skills, particularly in the passing game where he continues to command target volume.

The workload management strategy deployed by head coach Kyle Shanahan reflects San Francisco's ability to dominate games early, reducing the need for McCaffrey to shoulder a heavy rushing burden. Fantasy managers are advised to maintain confidence in McCaffrey despite the carry decline, as his receiving opportunities and upcoming matchup against a beatable Cardinals secondary suggest production upside. The elite running back remains a core piece of the 49ers' offensive system regardless of game script, making him a solid fantasy play for Week 3.
