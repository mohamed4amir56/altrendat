Raiders Elevate WR Cody White Third Time as Kubiak Opportunity Arrives

📰 Full Coverage & Verified Details:

The Las Vegas Raiders have activated wide receiver Cody White from the practice squad for the third elevation of the season, according to Silver and Black Pride. This marks the organization's final opportunity to call up White under NFL practice squad elevation rules, which allow teams to temporarily promote players a maximum of three times per season before requiring a permanent roster move.

White, a former Seattle Seahawks receiver, is receiving a significant opportunity with the Raiders' offense under the leadership of offensive coordinator Klint Kubiak, as reported by Heavy.com. The elevation comes after White demonstrated a breakout performance that caught the attention of the Raiders' coaching staff.

The 26-year-old wideout originally signed with the Seahawks but has cycled through multiple organizations seeking consistent playing time. With Kubiak's track record of developing wide receiver talent, the Raiders are banking on White's potential to contribute meaningfully to their receiving corps down the stretch.

This final elevation carries high stakes for White's NFL future. If the Raiders wish to retain him beyond this temporary assignment, they would need to sign him to the active roster permanently, taking up valuable salary cap space. The decision reflects the team's belief in White's abilities, even as they navigate competitive roster decisions during the critical latter stages of the season.
