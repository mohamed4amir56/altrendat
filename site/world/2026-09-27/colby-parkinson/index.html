Rams Tight End Colby Parkinson Faces Sunday Uncertainty With Knee Injury

📰 Full Coverage & Verified Details:

The Los Angeles Rams' offensive lineup faces mounting injury concerns heading into their Week 3 contest against the Denver Broncos. Colby Parkinson, a veteran tight end, has been officially listed as questionable for Sunday due to a knee injury, according to FantasyPros. His status remains uncertain, adding to the team's growing depth chart challenges.

The injury timing is particularly problematic given that the Rams are already managing significant personnel losses. Wide receiver Puka Nacua, a crucial component of the Rams' offensive strategy, is unavailable this week due to a hip injury sustained earlier in the season. Sports Illustrated reports that Nacua's absence has already forced the Rams to adjust their game plan, as evidenced during Monday Night Football's Week 2 victory over the New York Giants.

If Parkinson cannot play against Denver, Terrance Ferguson is expected to assume a larger role in the tight end rotation, according to FantasyPros. Fantasy football analysts caution that even if Parkinson is active on Sunday, he should not be started in any leagues given the uncertainty surrounding his participation and the expected elevation of Ferguson.

The Rams' offensive adjustment dilemma reflects the broader challenge facing the team as injuries mount. Navigating these absences effectively will prove critical as Los Angeles attempts to maintain momentum in a competitive AFC West matchup.
