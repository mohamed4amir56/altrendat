📰 Full Coverage & Verified Details:

Cooper Kupp has been cleared for action Sunday against the Washington Commanders, according to FantasyPros, ending uncertainty surrounding a back injury that sidelined him partially during the week. The Seahawks' elite receiver participated in full practice Thursday, per CBS Sports, signaling his fitness ahead of the crucial divisional contest.

Kupp's availability comes as quarterback Sam Darnold prepares for his own return after a glute injury, reuniting the offensive weapons for Seattle's Week 3 contest. FantasyPros reported the back ailment was never considered serious enough to threaten his participation, though the injury did raise early-week red flags for the franchise.

Despite the green light to play, fantasy analysts at RotoBaller expressed cautious optimism about Kupp's Week 3 output. Even with a favorable matchup on paper against Washington's secondary, experts flagged the receiver as "hard to trust" this particular week, suggesting the back issue may linger in his movement and explosiveness. The Commanders defense has shown vulnerability against elite receivers, yet Kupp's injury status and overall offensive rhythm could temper expectations.

Kupp remains one of the NFL's most dynamic receiving threats when fully healthy, but Sunday's contest presents a complex fantasy calculus. His confirmed participation eliminates the game-time decision drama, but his production ceiling may be slightly diminished as he works through the lingering back tightness and reconnects with Darnold's timing.
