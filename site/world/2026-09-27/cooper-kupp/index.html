📰 Full Coverage & Verified Details:

Cooper Kupp has been cleared to suit up for the Seattle Seahawks' Week 3 matchup against the Washington Commanders on Sunday, according to FantasyPros. The elite wide receiver dealt with a back injury earlier in the week but medical staff determined the issue is not serious enough to sideline him.

Kupp practiced in a limited capacity Wednesday, per CBS Sports, as the Seahawks monitored his condition heading into the divisional contest. Despite receiving clearance, fantasy football analysts remain cautious about his outlook. RotoBaller reports that Kupp is "hard to trust" in this particular matchup, even with the Commanders representing a relatively favorable defensive pairing for receivers.

The calculus shifts slightly with the expected return of Seahawks quarterback Sam Darnold, who has been dealing with a glute injury but is set to resume his starting role. Darnold's presence typically boosts Kupp's usage and production potential. However, FantasyPros notes that the back injury and the specific defensive matchup combine to make Kupp a risky fantasy play this week.

Kupp has established himself as one of the NFL's premier receiving threats in recent seasons. His availability will be critical to Seattle's offensive firepower as the Seahawks seek to stay competitive in their NFC West division. The team will likely manage his workload carefully given the injury, balancing his return with long-term health considerations.
