Corey Heim, the soon-to-be full-time NASCAR Cup Series driver, found himself at the center of a violent altercation following the ValleyStar Credit Union 300 Late Model stock car race at Martinsville Speedway on Saturday night. According to Motorsport.com, Heim was confronted and jumped by multiple crew members from the Chad Bryant Racing team, who punched him several times during the melee.

Yahoo Sports reported that amateur video footage captured the incident, showing Heim being struck repeatedly by team members in what escalated from on-track contact during the race. The physical confrontation unfolded immediately after the checkered flag, highlighting rising tensions between competitors at one of NASCAR's most storied venues.

The incident marks a significant moment for Heim, who is preparing for his transition to NASCAR's premier Cup Series. The altercation raises questions about race-day conduct and safety protocols at major events. Martinsville Speedway, known for its tight racing and intense competition, has long been the site of heated rivalries, but physical altercations involving multiple participants remain rare.

The circumstances surrounding the on-track contact that sparked the post-race confrontation remain under review, as NASCAR officials and track management assess the incident. This development comes at a critical juncture in Heim's career trajectory as he advances to full-time Cup Series competition.
