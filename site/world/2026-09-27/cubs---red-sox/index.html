📰 Full Coverage & Verified Details:

The Boston Red Sox swept the Chicago Cubs in a doubleheader Friday, securing their playoff berth and confirming a wild-card round matchup against the New York Yankees, according to The Boston Globe. Roman Anthony delivered a standout performance in Game 1, while Wilyer Abreu proved decisive in Game 2, propelling Boston to back-to-back victories.

Despite having relatively little on the line entering Friday's contest, the Red Sox refused to coast. Boston.com noted that the team "could've easily phoned in Friday's doubleheader," yet instead built substantial momentum as it heads into the postseason. The sweep demonstrated the club's competitive mentality and hunger despite the playoff bracket already being largely determined.

With the doubleheader victories locked in, Boston now turns its attention to Game 162 on Sunday in Florida, the final regular-season contest. The Red Sox have secured their wild-card position and will face the Yankees in the opening round of October baseball, setting up a high-stakes rivalry matchup that has captivated fans across both franchises.

The doubleheader sweep marks a crucial confidence-builder for Boston's pitching and lineup ahead of the playoffs, proving the team can execute under pressure and maintain focus even when the immediate outcome feels predetermined. Anthony and Abreu's heroics Friday showcase the offensive firepower the Red Sox will rely upon against New York.
