📰 Full Coverage & Verified Details:

Dak Prescott stands on the brink of NFL history as the Dallas Cowboys prepare for a high-stakes matchup against the Baltimore Ravens in Rio. A victory would tie him with Cowboys legend Roger Staubach for second place on the franchise's all-time wins list, according to NBC Sports. The milestone comes after Prescott recently etched his name into Cowboys lore by setting the team's all-time passing touchdown record last week.

The upcoming contest carries enormous significance beyond individual accolades. Russell Wilson, a Super Bowl-winning quarterback, has publicly endorsed Prescott's readiness for the moment. "Dak Prescott is ready to explode," Wilson told Yahoo Sports, predicting the Cowboys signal-caller will dominate against Baltimore's fierce defense led by Lamar Jackson. Wilson emphasized that Dallas must establish an early scoring advantage to manage Jackson's dual-threat capabilities, which have historically posed matchup nightmares for opposing defenses.

Sports Illustrated reports that Wilson expects Prescott to "go crazy" in what promises to be a historic Cowboys-Ravens clash in Rio. The convergence of Prescott's individual accomplishment pursuit and the high-pressure playoff-caliber environment creates a perfect storm for explosive quarterback performance. With Lamar Jackson's running prowess threatening to overwhelm Dallas's defensive schemes, early offensive touchdowns become critical. If Prescott executes at the level Wilson predicts, he could simultaneously achieve his franchise wins record while proving the Cowboys belong among the NFL's elite contenders heading into the season's final stretch.
