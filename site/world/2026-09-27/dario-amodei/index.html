📰 Full Coverage & Verified Details:

Dario Amodei, CEO of AI safety company Anthropic, addressed the UN Security Council alongside other technology leaders to advocate for establishing global AI governance frameworks. According to BBC reporting, Amodei and OpenAI's Sam Altman both called for international risk evaluation standards to manage artificial intelligence development. Hugging Face CEO Clement Delangue echoed similar concerns about the need for coordinated global oversight.

The appeals came as tech leaders faced mounting pressure to align on safety protocols amid rapid AI advancement. However, the US government rejected these pleas for binding international AI standards, according to BBC sources. The rejection underscores tensions between tech companies seeking regulatory certainty and the US administration's position on AI governance.

Democracy Now! reported that the UN Security Council hosted this unprecedented gathering of "AI Tech Billionaires" to debate whether the organization should take a lead role in setting global AI rules. The discussion reflected growing international concern that unilateral national approaches to AI regulation could create fragmented markets and safety gaps.

Amodei's intervention signals Anthropic's commitment to responsible AI development through institutional frameworks. His call for standardized risk evaluation protocols aligns with the company's stated mission of building AI systems that are safe, steerable, and interpretable. The contrasting US position suggests deepening divisions between Silicon Valley's calls for global coordination and Washington's preference for maintaining domestic regulatory flexibility in the competitive AI race.
