Davis Webb Bounces Back Spectacularly After Disastrous Debut as Broncos' New Play-Caller

📰 Full Coverage & Verified Details:

Davis Webb seized control of Denver's offense in Week 2 and delivered an immediate resurgence, leading the Broncos to a decisive 20-13 victory over Jacksonville after a humiliating Week 1 collapse. The win marked a dramatic turnaround following the team's disastrous 31-10 shutout loss to Kansas City, where the Broncos managed just 176 total yards of offense, according to Yahoo Sports.

Webb's promotion to play-calling duties came after Sean Payton's original decision to retain offensive responsibilities drew criticism from analysts who labeled it a "mistake from jump," as reported by Yahoo Sports. The Jacksonville win demonstrated immediate improvement despite the Jaguars entering Week 2 riding a nine-game regular-season win streak, a significant accomplishment that Webb's offense dismantled decisively.

Mile High Report noted that Webb executed precisely what the Broncos needed to restore offensive credibility and momentum heading into subsequent contests. While Denver Sports 104.3 The Fan acknowledged the victory lacked complete polish and offensive sophistication, analysts emphasized the importance of granting Webb patience as he navigates his inaugural play-calling responsibilities. The broadcaster's midday show stressed that the progress demonstrated against Jacksonville was genuine and tangible, even if the execution remained imperfect.

Webb's 20-13 triumph over Jacksonville proved the Broncos could function competently under renewed offensive direction, offering Denver fans legitimate hope that replacing Payton's play-calling would unlock dormant offensive potential for the remainder of the season.
