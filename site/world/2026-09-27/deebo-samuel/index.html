📰 Full Coverage & Verified Details:

Kyle Shanahan expressed uncertainty about Deebo Samuel's future with the San Francisco 49ers, yet a cascade of injuries has forced the 49ers offensive coordinator to reimagine the wide receiver's importance to the team's offense. According to NBC Sports Bay Area, Shanahan wasn't initially convinced the 49ers would retain Samuel or utilize him prominently in their scheme.

The situation has shifted dramatically as the 49ers' receiving corps has been decimated by injuries entering Week 3. With key targets sidelined, Samuel has emerged as a critical component of San Francisco's passing attack, forcing Shanahan to expand his role and target distribution.

Samuel's versatility—his ability to line up as a receiver while also contributing on jet sweeps and other creative plays—makes him invaluable during this depleted roster period. Fantasy analysts at DraftKings Network flagged Week 3's matchup against the Arizona Cardinals as a potential breakout opportunity, noting that the thinned-out 49ers receiver room significantly elevates Samuel's usage rate and touchdown probability.

The 49ers' injury crisis has inadvertently validated Samuel's value as a Swiss Army knife offensive weapon. Shanahan's initial skepticism has given way to necessity, making Samuel a focal point as San Francisco navigates a challenging stretch. For fantasy players, the combination of injuries and increased opportunity positions Samuel as a strong start candidate against Arizona, with DraftKings Network projecting elevated target volume and scoring potential.
