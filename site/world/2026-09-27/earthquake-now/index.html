📰 Full Coverage & Verified Details:

A significant M6.6 earthquake jolted the Loyalty Islands near New Caledonia at 21:23 UTC on September 25, 2026, according to the United States Geological Survey (USGS). The agency reported an unusually shallow depth of just 10 kilometers (6.2 miles), which typically amplifies ground shaking intensity across the region. The Loyalty Islands, situated in the South Pacific, sit atop an active seismic zone where tectonic plates collide, making the area particularly vulnerable to strong temblors.

Simultaneously, the South Bay region near California experienced its own seismic event on Sunday morning, with two earthquakes striking southeast of San Jose near Westley, according to USGS data reported by KRON4. The initial quake hit the area just after 7:20 a.m. local time, with additional seismic activity following shortly after. Yahoo News confirmed communities across the South Bay and surrounding areas felt the sudden tremors.

A secondary M5.1 earthquake also impacted New Caledonia around September 27, 2026, further rattling the island nation. The combination of Pacific rim seismic events reflects heightened tectonic activity across the Ring of Fire, the horseshoe-shaped zone encircling the Pacific Ocean responsible for approximately 90 percent of global earthquakes. Authorities continued monitoring the situation as aftershocks remained possible in both affected regions.
