📰 Full Coverage & Verified Details:

Google is celebrating its 28th birthday on September 27, 2026, according to The Times of India. The search engine giant was founded by Stanford University PhD students Larry Page and Sergey Brin, who started the venture in a modest garage setting.

The milestone anniversary highlights a remarkable financial origin story. In 1998, an early investor wrote a $100,000 cheque to the struggling startup, which prompted founders Larry Page and Sergey Brin to formally establish Google Inc. as a legal business entity the very next day, according to The Economic Times. This decisive moment transformed the company from a research project into an official commercial operation.

From its garage-based beginnings, Google has expanded into a global technology empire. The company now oversees a vast ecosystem of products and services including Gmail, YouTube, Android, and Gemini AI, according to The Times of India. Today, Google's market value exceeds $4 trillion, representing one of the most successful transformations in technology history.

Google marked the 28th birthday with a special Doodle on its homepage, bringing back its early logo to commemorate the occasion. The annual celebration underscores the company's significance in shaping the digital landscape over nearly three decades, from pioneering web search technology to developing cutting-edge artificial intelligence solutions that impact billions of users worldwide.
