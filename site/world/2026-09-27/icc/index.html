Virat Kohli Announces Cricket World Cup 2027 Will Be His Final Tournament

⚡ Real-Time Coverage & Breaking Insight

📰 Full Coverage & Verified Details:

Virat Kohli, holder of the most centuries in ODI cricket history, has declared that the ICC Men's Cricket World Cup 2027 (CWC27) will be his final World Cup appearance, according to an official ICC statement. The 36-year-old Indian superstar aims to secure a fifth successive World Cup berth, capping an extraordinary international career that has defined modern cricket.

Kohli's announcement comes as fellow Indian captain Rohit Sharma, who turns 40 soon, also remains central to India's World Cup planning. The Times of India reports that both Sharma and Kohli—described as "The Rolling Stones of Indian cricket"—are determined to deliver one final championship push, with Kohli turning 38 in the coming months.

Kohli's record stands unmatched: he holds the distinction of most ODI centuries across cricket history, a testament to his consistency and excellence across multiple decades. His five consecutive World Cup appearances represent an elite tier of longevity rarely achieved in professional cricket.

The 2027 tournament will provide Kohli a final opportunity to add to his World Cup legacy, having previously competed in 2011, 2015, 2019, and 2023 editions. His confirmation signals India's intent to build a competitive squad around experienced leadership for what could be a crowning achievement before retirement. The ICC's announcement underscores Kohli's enduring status as one of global cricket's most celebrated athletes heading into his final chapter on sport's grandest stage.
