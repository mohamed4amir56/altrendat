📰 Full Coverage & Verified Details:

The Indiana Fever's dynamic backcourt made history on September 27, 2026, when guards Caitlin Clark and Kelsey Mitchell were selected to the Associated Press All-WNBA First Team. Clark's individual honor extends further: she has been named the AP Comeback Player of the Year, recognizing an extraordinary return to form after missing 31 of 44 regular season games in 2025 and the entire Fever playoff run due to injury.

Clark's 2026 campaign proved transformative for the league. After sitting out most of the previous season, she returned to the court and posted career-best and league-record performances, according to the Indiana Fever. Yahoo Sports reported that the Clark-Mitchell duo combined to average 47 points per game—the most in league history for any paired guards.

The honors reflect the Fever's resurgent 2026 season, powered by their elite backcourt. Mitchell's inclusion on the First Team underscores her consistent excellence alongside Clark's dramatic comeback. Bleacher Report noted that Clark and A'ja Wilson headline the 2026 AP All-WNBA first team, placing the Indiana guard among the league's elite performers alongside other standout selections including Angel Reese, Paige Bueckers, and Azzi Fudd.

Clark's Comeback Player of the Year award caps a remarkable redemptive arc, transforming a season of absence and uncertainty into record-breaking individual performances and team success. Her recognition by the AP signals validation of her recovery and supremacy on the court.
