The Indiana Fever's backcourt tandem of Caitlin Clark and Kelsey Mitchell has been named to the 2026 Associated Press All-WNBA First Team, marking a landmark achievement for the franchise, according to the Indiana Fever's official announcement on September 27, 2026.

Clark's selection carries additional significance with her designation as AP Comeback Player of the Year. The accolade recognizes her remarkable return to form after a devastating 2025 season in which she missed 31 of the team's 44 regular season games and the entire playoff run due to injury. Despite the lost time, Clark rebounded spectacularly in 2026, setting both career and league records during the regular season with 22.3 points per game, according to Yahoo Sports.

The dynamic duo's combined production reached historic heights. Clark and Mitchell averaged 47 points per game together—the most ever recorded by any backcourt pair in WNBA history, per Yahoo Sports reporting. This unprecedented offensive output underscores the Fever's offensive firepower and the individual excellence both guards demonstrated throughout the 2026 campaign.

Clark joins fellow All-WNBA First Team selections including A'ja Wilson, according to Bleacher Report, while the broader AP honors recognized standout performances from across the league's elite talent pool. The recognition caps a triumphant season for the Fever organization, validating Clark's comeback narrative and Mitchell's sustained excellence.
