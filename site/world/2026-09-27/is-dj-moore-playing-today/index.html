📰 Full Coverage & Verified Details:

DJ Moore's Week 3 status against the Los Angeles Chargers hangs in the balance as the Buffalo Bills manage their injury roster heading into a pivotal divisional clash. According to Yahoo Sports, the latest Bills injury update centers on Moore's availability for the upcoming game, with the team working to confirm his participation for Sunday's AFC West battle.

Moore has been a critical offensive weapon for Buffalo so far this season. Yahoo Sports reported that the star wide receiver led the Bills with a dominant 24.1% target share in Week 1, establishing himself as quarterback Josh Allen's primary receiving option. His involvement in the passing game has made his status a key fantasy football consideration for Week 3, as ESPN's analysts are weighing whether to start or sit the playmaker.

The Buffalo Bills' official injury report, released via their website, reveals that head coach Joe Brady has already ruled out two players entirely and designated three additional athletes as questionable for Sunday's matchup. This tiered approach suggests Moore may fall into the questionable category, leaving his participation uncertain until closer to kickoff.

For fantasy football managers, Moore's target share through the first two weeks suggests he remains a high-value play when healthy. However, his potential absence would dramatically shift offensive responsibilities to backup options like Khalil Shakir, who Yahoo Sports noted has seen limited opportunities, running routes on just 58.6% of pass plays in recent contests.

The Bills are expected to provide a final injury designation by Sunday morning.
