📰 Full Coverage & Verified Details:

Jacksonville Jaguars wide receiver Jakobi Meyers will take the field against his former team, the New England Patriots, in a Week 3 matchup that holds far more significance than a typical NFL game. According to Yahoo Sports, Meyers revealed the emotional weight of facing New England, likening the experience to "seeing your ex," underscoring the complicated feelings surrounding his departure from Foxborough.

Meyers emerged as a reliable target for the Patriots during the post-Tom Brady era, establishing himself as a steady presence in New England's offense before eventually signing a lucrative deal with Jacksonville. Heavy.com reports that the veteran receiver has evolved from catching passes from Brady into a $60 million Jaguars receiver, marking a significant chapter in his professional trajectory.

The reunion game at EverBank Stadium carries personal stakes beyond standard divisional competition. Pats Pulpit examined the roster connections between both squads heading into Sunday's clash, noting multiple ex-Patriots and ex-Jaguars scattered across both rosters, creating an intricate web of former teammates and colleagues facing off.

For Meyers specifically, Sunday represents an opportunity to prove his worth to Jacksonville while simultaneously demonstrating his progress to the organization that drafted him as an undrafted free agent. The psychological dimension of playing against former colleagues and coaches adds an extra layer of intensity to what promises to be a compelling AFC East showdown.
