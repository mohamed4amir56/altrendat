📰 Full Coverage & Verified Details:

Jordan Addison's start to the 2026 NFL season has disappointed fantasy managers and Vikings fans alike. According to FantasyPros, the wide receiver has recorded two receptions or fewer in each of the first two games, despite drawing five targets in the Vikings' Week 2 victory. This underperformance highlights a broader problem plaguing Minnesota's offense early in the season.

The Athletic reported that the Vikings' 2-0 start masks a frustrating wide receiver problem that demands immediate attention. With quarterback Kyler Murray returning to his starting role in Week 3 against the Tampa Bay Buccaneers, there is hope for improvement in the passing game. However, Addison remains a high-risk, pass-catching option in all fantasy formats, according to FantasyPros.

Fantasy analysts at RotoBaller are already fielding questions about whether managers should drop Addison ahead of Week 3. His inability to convert targets into receptions has made him a liability rather than an asset in most fantasy leagues. The Vikings' receiving corps needs significant improvement if they hope to maintain their undefeated record and establish a consistent passing attack. Addison will have an opportunity to prove his worth in the coming weeks, but his early-season struggles have left his fantasy outlook uncertain and his role in Minnesota's offense in question.
