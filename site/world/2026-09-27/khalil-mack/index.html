Khalil Mack, one of the NFL's most dominant pass rushers, has become the focal point of trade speculation as multiple contenders circle the Los Angeles Chargers veteran. According to Sports Illustrated, the Chicago Bears are being evaluated as a logical trade suitor for Mack, with the potential move described as capable of creating an "epic reunion" between the star edge rusher and the franchise. The analysis suggests Chicago's defensive needs and salary cap flexibility make them a credible candidate in any potential deal.

Meanwhile, Heavy.com reports that the San Francisco 49ers have also examined whether acquiring Mack represents a sound strategic move. The 49ers' assessment of a potential trade underscores how multiple playoff-contending teams view Mack as an elite solution to bolster their defensive pass rush.

Mack, widely recognized as one of the league's premier edge rushers, brings championship-caliber experience and proven production to any defensive line. His market value reflects his elite status, yet teams are actively exploring whether the investment aligns with their championship windows. The emergence of both the Bears and 49ers as serious suitors signals that Mack's availability could reshape the defensive landscape across the NFL. Any trade would represent a significant move with major cap implications and depth chart ramifications for both the acquiring team and the Chargers.
