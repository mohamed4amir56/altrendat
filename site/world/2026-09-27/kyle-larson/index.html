He led an impressive 235 laps, breaking his own previous track record at Kansas Speedway, demonstrating control and consistency across the majority of the 400-mile race. Cindric's second-place finish and Denny Hamlin's involvement rounded out the top finishers at a track where Larson has increasingly established himself as the dominant force.
