Fernandez Stages Epic Comeback To Reach Singapore Final After Losing First Set

📰 Full Coverage & Verified Details:

Leylah Fernandez showcased her trademark resilience at the Singapore Tennis Open, battling back from a crushing first-set loss to defeat Maja Chwalinska 1-6, 6-4, 6-2 in a thrilling semifinal clash. The Canadian star, after dropping the opening set in one-sided fashion, completely shifted momentum in the second frame, reclaiming control of the baseline and forcing errors from her opponent. Fernandez's aggressive groundstrokes and improved serve performance in the final two sets proved decisive, ultimately propelling her to her first tour-level final of the season, according to WTA Tennis.

The 23-year-old's comeback victory exemplifies her fighting spirit on tour. Down and seemingly outplayed early, Fernandez adjusted her tactics, mixing pace and placement to unsettle Chwalinska's rhythm. The second-set tiebreaker proved pivotal, shifting psychological momentum entirely in Fernandez's favor as she broke through to claim it 6-4.

In the championship match, Fernandez will face Australia's Talia Gibson, who advanced through her own semifinal path. Last Word On Sports highlighted the final as a compelling WTA showdown, with both players seeking a career-defining title at the prestigious Asian tournament. Fernandez enters the final on strong form, having demonstrated the mental fortitude and technical adaptability that have defined her rise in professional tennis since her breakthrough performances at major championships.
