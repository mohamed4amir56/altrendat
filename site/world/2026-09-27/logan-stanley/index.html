📰 Full Coverage & Verified Details:

The New York Islanders bolstered their defensive roster by signing unrestricted free agent defenseman Logan Stanley to a one-year contract, according to announcements from TSN and Sportsnet. The deal carries a cap hit of $1.5 million, with both the team and Stanley expressing mutual interest in the partnership.

Stanley's signing comes as the Islanders continue to fortify their back-end for the season ahead. The defenseman had been available on the free-agent market and found a home in New York after exploring his options. TSN reported the contract was officially announced on Sunday, marking a completed negotiation following preliminary discussions about terms and structure.

The one-year structure of the deal suggests both sides view this as a prove-it opportunity, allowing Stanley to demonstrate his value while providing the Islanders with short-term flexibility. At $1.5 million, the contract represents a reasonable investment for depth on the blue line, particularly for a club seeking to strengthen its roster composition. Sportsnet's reporting emphasized that mutual interest facilitated swift negotiations, indicating Stanley and the organization aligned on expectations.

This signing reflects the Islanders' strategic approach to free agency, targeting experienced defensemen who can contribute immediately without long-term salary cap commitments. Stanley joins the team as it prepares for competition in the upcoming season, adding veteran depth to New York's defensive core.
