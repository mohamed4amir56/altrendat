Mark Andrews Returns to Elite Form: Ravens Tight End Cracks Top 12 Rankings

📰 Full Coverage & Verified Details:

Mark Andrews is heating up at precisely the right moment for Baltimore. The Ravens' star tight end has posted matching 49-yard outings through the season's first two weeks, re-establishing himself as a legitimate fantasy asset after a sluggish opening month. According to Yahoo Sports, Andrews now ranks 10th among all tight ends in Pro Football Network's Week 3 rankings—his first appearance inside the top 12 this season.

The consistency is striking. CBS Sports reported that Andrews hauled in six catches during Week 2's loss, demonstrating the high-volume opportunity rate that has historically defined his game. This dual 49-yard performance marks a significant milestone: it's the first time since October 2023 that Andrews has strung together consecutive games with at least four receptions and 49 yards, Yahoo Sports noted.

As Baltimore prepares to face Dallas this week, the timing of Andrews' resurgence cannot be overlooked. After weeks of disappointing production relative to his pedigree, the 28-year-old tight end appears ready to capitalize on the opportunities flowing his way. RotoBaller highlighted that Andrews is specifically hoping to convert his elevated target share into fantasy production in Week 3's matchup.

For fantasy managers who benched the Ravens tight end during his early-season struggles, his reentry into the scoring elite presents a crucial decision point. With the Dallas defense representing a manageable challenge and momentum clearly shifting in his favor, Andrews has positioned himself squarely back in must-start territory heading into Week 3.
