Yellowstone Stars Return To Marshals Season 2 As CBS Cancels Planned Stories

⚡ Real-Time Coverage & Breaking Insight
🔥 Inside the Story: What Just Happened

Luke Grimes and Jefferson White rejoin the Yellowstone franchise for Marshals season 2 premiering October 4, even as CBS cancels major story arcs set up for the new season.

📰 Full Coverage & Verified Details:

Marshals season 2 is returning to CBS on Sunday, October 4, with major casting news shaking up the Yellowstone universe. Luke Grimes returns as Kayce Dutton, the last surviving member of the Dutton family still appearing on screen, according to 3DVF. Joining him is Yellowstone actor Jefferson White, who will reprise his role as Jimmy Hurdstrom in a recurring capacity.

White's involvement marks a significant reunion with the franchise after earlier uncertainty. The actor was previously expected to headline the 6666 spinoff, but that project was shelved from active development, Yahoo Entertainment reported. His pivot to Marshals season 2 comes just three weeks after CBS officially confirmed the casting announcement.

However, the season faces creative headwinds. Screen Rant reveals that CBS has cancelled multiple storylines originally set up for season 2's narrative arc, suggesting the network is reshaping the show's direction. With the Dutton Ranch itself not returning until 2027, according to 3DVF, Marshals appears positioned as the immediate continuation of the Yellowstone saga.

The casting of Grimes and White signals CBS's commitment to maintaining continuity with the original series, even as planned plot developments are shelved. Fans of the franchise will see these familiar characters navigate new territory when Marshals premieres this October.
