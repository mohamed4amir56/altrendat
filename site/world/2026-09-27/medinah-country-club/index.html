📰 Full Coverage & Verified Details:

Donald Trump made his fourth golf tournament appearance of 2026 Sunday at Chicago's prestigious Medinah Country Club, arriving amid heightened security to rally USA fans during the final day of the Presidents Cup. According to ABC7 Chicago, the visit sparked organized demonstrations and protests, with additional security measures deployed as Trump touched down at Chicago O'Hare Airport. Yahoo Sports reported the USA team trailed the International Team 10.5-7.5 heading into Sunday's matches, making the American comeback bid critical to avoiding a rare home-soil defeat. Trump's presence was intended to inspire the home squad in their pursuit of victory, leveraging the energy of the crowd at one of America's most storied golf venues. Medinah Country Club, located in the western suburbs of Chicago, has hosted numerous major golf championships, including previous Presidents Cups, making it a fitting stage for high-profile appearances. The event drew significant public attention, with protests and counter-demonstrations organized around Trump's attendance, according to ABC7 Chicago's reporting. Security personnel were visibly increased throughout the venue and surrounding areas. The Presidents Cup, held biennially, traditionally features twelve players from the USA competing against twelve players from the International Team, consisting of professionals from outside Europe. Sunday's conclusion would determine whether the American squad could complete a comeback from their deficit or if the International Team would secure an upset victory on American soil.
