📰 Full Coverage & Verified Details:

Mike Washington Jr.'s rookie season began with intrigue but has quickly deflated into a cautionary tale of preseason optimism meeting NFL reality. The Las Vegas running back, who generated significant fantasy buzz during training camp and the exhibition slate, has seen his stock plummet through the first two weeks of the 2026 season.

According to Yahoo Sports, Washington carried the ball seven times in Week 1 for minimal production, then managed just 7 total rushing yards across Week 2, effectively erasing any standalone fantasy value the prospect once promised. The Athletic's preseason coverage highlighted Washington as a potential breakout candidate, but a generational talent ahead of him on the depth chart has relegated the young back to a secondary role.

Heading into Week 3's matchup against the New Orleans Saints on September 27, Washington ranked 43rd among NFL running backs in PFN's fantasy rankings, according to Yahoo Sports' analysis. Fox Sports 990 and RotoBaller both classified him as a "bench stash" at best—a speculative hold for managers banking on future opportunity rather than current production.

The dream of standalone value has effectively died before the end of September, per Yahoo Sports' blunt assessment. Fantasy managers who rode Washington's preseason momentum must now pivot their lineup construction, recognizing that his limited touches and backup designation make him an unreliable play in Week 3 and beyond. The rookie's path to relevance now depends entirely on injuries or a dramatic shift in the Vegas offensive hierarchy.
