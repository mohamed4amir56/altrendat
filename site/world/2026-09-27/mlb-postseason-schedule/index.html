📰 Full Coverage & Verified Details:

Major League Baseball has officially released its Wild Card Series playoff schedule, setting the stage for dramatic October baseball across the nation. According to ABC7 Chicago, the Chicago Cubs will travel to San Diego to face the Padres in their Wild Card Series, with games scheduled for 9 p.m. CT starts. Meanwhile, the Chicago White Sox will begin their playoff journey at 4 p.m. CT, heading to Houston to take on either the Astros or Rangers, depending on final regular-season standings.

The schedule reflects MLB's continued push into primetime slots for maximum viewership, with the Cubs' evening starts designed to capture national audiences. However, fans seeking to watch all playoff action face a new hurdle: streaming availability. The Boston Globe reported that while marquee matchups featuring the Boston Red Sox and New York Yankees are expected to receive prominent national placement on NBC, several other Wild Card Series games will be exclusive to Peacock, NBC's streaming service.

This distribution strategy means casual fans and subscribers may need multiple platforms to follow their teams through the postseason. The shift toward streaming exclusivity marks another evolution in how MLB distributes playoff content, balancing traditional television broadcasts with digital-first offerings. The Wild Card Series represents baseball's most unpredictable playoff format, with single-elimination intensity making every pitch crucial. Teams competing across multiple time zones will face the added challenge of adjusting to varying start times throughout their series matchups.
