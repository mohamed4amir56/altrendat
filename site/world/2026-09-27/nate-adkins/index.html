📰 Full Coverage & Verified Details:

The Denver Broncos have found unexpected depth at the tight end position as the 2026 NFL season unfolds. Head Coach Sean Payton expressed enthusiasm about the development of Nate Adkins alongside veteran Evan Engram, citing their impressive early contributions to the team's offensive scheme.

Adkins has captured Payton's attention through productive outings in the season's opening weeks, signaling a potential breakout campaign for the young tight end. The Broncos' coaching staff has integrated both Engram and Adkins into their passing attack with tangible results, as both players have demonstrated reliability and execution in the early portion of the schedule.

Engram, the veteran presence in Denver's tight end room, posted a strong Week 1 performance before a quieter Week 2 outing in the Broncos' comeback victory. Through two games, Engram has accumulated five receptions for 60 yards and one touchdown, according to FantasyPros. In Week 2 specifically, Engram hauled in a 17-yard reception, further showcasing his value as a receiving option.

With Payton's offensive system requiring dynamic tight end play, the emergence of Adkins alongside Engram provides the Broncos with multiple attacking options in the passing game. As Denver prepares for Week 3 against the Los Angeles Rams, the dual-threat tight end approach may prove crucial to sustaining offensive momentum. The early-season success of both players suggests the Broncos have developed genuine depth at a position that can significantly impact playoff positioning.
