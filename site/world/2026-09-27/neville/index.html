📰 Full Coverage & Verified Details:

Gary Neville, the legendary Manchester United defender and prominent pundit, has issued a scathing assessment of the club's transfer strategy in the post-Ferguson era. According to Goal.com and The Peoples Person, Neville singled out Antony as Manchester United's most disappointing signing since 2013, a striking indictment of the Brazilian winger who arrived at Old Trafford with considerable fanfare.

In his analysis, Neville also identified Angel Di Maria as another catastrophic acquisition, cementing his view that Manchester United's recruitment of South American talent has yielded dire returns. The Peoples Person reported that both players were specifically highlighted for their failure to deliver on their promise at the club.

Antony's tenure at Manchester United has been marked by inconsistency and underperformance despite his expensive transfer fee and early expectations. The Brazilian's struggles to adapt to the Premier League's intensity and his inability to replicate his Ajax form have frustrated supporters and club officials alike.

Di Maria's brief and turbulent spell at United, spanning just one season, remains a cautionary tale of how quickly a marquee signing can deteriorate both on and off the pitch. His early-season form promised much, but injury and adaptation issues saw his influence wane dramatically.

Neville's critique, reported by Metro.co.uk, reflects broader frustrations among Manchester United's fanbase regarding the club's transfer decisions under multiple regimes since Ferguson's departure. His assessment carries particular weight given his legendary status at the club and his current role as a respected analyst of the sport.
