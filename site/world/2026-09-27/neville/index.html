📰 Full Coverage & Verified Details:

Gary Neville, the iconic Manchester United defender turned pundit, has delivered a damning verdict on the club's recruitment strategy, naming Antony as Manchester United's worst signing since Sir Alex Ferguson's 2013 retirement, according to Goal.com and The Peoples Person.

The Brazilian winger, signed for a club-record fee in 2022, has failed to deliver the consistent performances expected of a marquee acquisition. Neville's assessment reflects widespread frustration among the fanbase regarding Antony's inability to justify his substantial investment and replicate the form that caught United's attention.

In a striking dual critique, Neville also identified Argentine winger Angel Di Maria as another catastrophic recruitment failure in the post-Ferguson era, per The Peoples Person. Di Maria's 2014 arrival promised much but yielded disappointment, with the player ultimately departing after just one tumultuous season at Old Trafford.

Both South American signings represent the club's broader struggles in the transfer market over the past decade. While Di Maria's brief tenure ended swiftly, Antony's extended underperformance has proven more costly to United's ambitions. The winger has failed to establish himself as a consistent creative force or goal contributor at the level demanded by elite Premier League competition.

Neville's candid assessment underscores Manchester United's challenge in identifying and nurturing world-class talent since Ferguson's departure. The club's spending power has repeatedly failed to translate into sustained on-pitch success, with these two signings epitomizing the disconnect between investment and actual performance under successive managerial regimes.
