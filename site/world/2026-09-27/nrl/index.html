Nathan Cleary Suffers Compound Fracture as Crossland Sent Off in NRL Upset

📰 Full Coverage & Verified Details:

Nathan Cleary's NRL preliminary final nightmare unfolded in the first half when Newcastle Knights hooker Phoenix Crossland executed a hip-drop tackle that left the Penrith Panthers quarterback with a compound fracture, according to The Guardian. Cleary was immediately transported to hospital, ending his involvement in the contest and triggering a domino effect across the season's final stages.

The catastrophic injury proved the decisive turning point. Phoenix Crossland was sent off for the illegal tackle, as reported by NRL.com, leaving Newcastle at a numerical disadvantage. Despite playing with 12 men for much of the match, the Knights powered through to secure a 22-14 victory and claimed their first NRL grand final berth since 2001, according to ABC News.

The compound fracture ruling—confirmed by The Guardian—eliminates Cleary from international competition immediately. The two-time Dally M Medal winner and Penrith's creative fulcrum faces an extended rehabilitation period that extends beyond the immediate NRL calendar. His absence represents a seismic loss for the Panthers' championship aspirations and the broader rugby league landscape heading into the world game's marquee tournament.

Newcastle's breakthrough grand final appearance marks a stunning turnaround for the franchise, which has endured a 25-year drought at the sport's pinnacle. The Knights will now face either the Brisbane Broncos or Gold Coast Titans, with the other preliminary final determining the opposing finalist in rugby league's championship decider.
