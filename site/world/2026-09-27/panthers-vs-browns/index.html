The Carolina Panthers announced their inactives for Sunday's matchup against the Cleveland Browns, ruling out key offensive and defensive contributors. Wide receiver Xavier Legette, who was listed as questionable on the injury report, will not play this week, according to the official Panthers announcement.

Despite uncertainty surrounding multiple receivers heading into the contest, Jalen Coker is expected to suit up against the Browns, NBC Sports reported. Coker carries a questionable designation but appears to have cleared the team's medical protocols after limited practice participation during the week.

Beyond the offensive lineup changes, the Panthers will also be without two defensive starters. Safety Nick Scott and linebacker Devin Lloyd are both inactive, creating notable gaps in the secondary and linebacker corps heading into Week 3.

These absences come as the Panthers navigate the injury management challenges that accompany the NFL season's early stages. The loss of Legette removes a receiving option from quarterback's play-calling sheet, while Coker's availability provides some depth at a position dealing with personnel questions. The defensive losses of Lloyd and Scott will test Carolina's ability to execute coverage schemes and run-stopping assignments against a Browns offense seeking early-season momentum. The Panthers will look to maintain competitive performance despite the shortened roster, with Coker's participation potentially proving crucial to offensive execution in Cleveland.
