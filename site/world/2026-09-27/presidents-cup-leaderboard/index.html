📰 Full Coverage & Verified Details:

The 2026 Presidents Cup reached its decisive moment Sunday at Medinah Country Club, with the United States mounting an aggressive comeback against the International team's halftime advantage. According to Yahoo Sports, the Americans entered singles play trailing 10½ to 7½, but unleashed a coordinated assault on the final day to claw back into contention.

The International squad had controlled much of the competition through the opening sessions, building what appeared to be a commanding buffer. However, the U.S. team's depth and experience in match-play formats meant the outcome remained far from settled heading into Sunday's 12 singles matches.

Golf Channel reported a critical wrinkle in the competition's format: if both teams finish tied at exactly 15 points each, neither would automatically claim the trophy. This tiebreaker scenario loomed as a genuine possibility given the razor-thin margins typical of Presidents Cup history, adding extra urgency to every point contested on Sunday.

The International team, seeking only their second Presidents Cup victory in the event's modern era, faced intense pressure from a U.S. squad hungry to reassert dominance in this biennial showcase. With the trophy potentially on the line in individual matches throughout the day, both sides fielded their strongest lineups for the singles format, where head-to-head competition traditionally showcases golf's most compelling drama.
