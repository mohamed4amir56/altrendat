📰 Full Coverage & Verified Details:

Comedy legend Rachel Dratch has officially claimed her spot as a Spice Girl in a fresh Dunkin' advertisement that premiered ahead of the MTV Video Music Awards. The former Saturday Night Live star starred in the hilarious new commercial alongside Emma "Baby Spice" Bunton and Geri Halliwell, bringing her signature comedic flair to the iconic girl group lineup.

According to The Hollywood Reporter, Dratch introduced a "Long-Lost Spice Girl" character centered around pumpkin spice—Dunkin's signature fall flavor—during the awards show on Sunday. The collaboration cleverly blended nostalgia with current pop culture, leveraging the enduring appeal of the Spice Girls while tapping into consumer enthusiasm around seasonal beverages.

Just Jared reported that this campaign marks Dratch's creative expansion into branded content, positioning her as the sixth member of the beloved '90s group. The commercial capitalized on the MTV VMA platform to reach millions of viewers, combining entertainment with product placement in a way that entertained rather than jarred audiences.

Dratch's appearance alongside Bunton and Halliwell underscores her continued relevance in entertainment, nearly three decades after her SNL tenure. The pumpkin spice angle taps into Dunkin's massive seasonal push, a promotional strategy that generates substantial revenue during fall months. The collaboration represents a savvy marketing move that merges celebrity nostalgia with contemporary comedy, proving that legacy acts can successfully navigate modern branded partnerships.
