Boca Faces Racing Threat in Copa Argentina Quarterfinal as Rivals Extend Unbeaten Streak

📰 Full Coverage & Verified Details:

Boca Juniors and Racing Club meet this afternoon in Rosario for a high-stakes Copa Argentina quarterfinal, with the winner advancing to face Banfield in the semifinals. According to TyC Sports, Boca manager Arruabarrena will field his strongest lineup, though the Xeneize face a significant blow with defender Álvaro Montero sidelined.

Racing enters the clash as dangerous underdogs. Clarín reports that Racing has extended an impressive six-match unbeaten streak against Boca—a dramatic reversal of the historical balance. While Boca maintains a commanding record in head-to-head meetings, recent years have tilted dramatically in Racing's favor. The Avellaneda side has claimed victories in several recent classics and captured two national cup titles against the Xeneize, though Boca countered by winning the Copa Libertadores twice.

This quarterfinal represents a critical juncture for both clubs' domestic cup aspirations. La Nación confirms the match will be broadcast live, with the venue set at Rosario's Arroyito stadium—neutral ground that removes any home-field advantage. For Boca, breaking Racing's recent momentum becomes paramount; for Racing, extending their streak could spark a monumental upset. The winner's path to the Copa Argentina final runs through Banfield, setting the stakes for what promises to be a fiercely contested Argentine classic.
