📰 Full Coverage & Verified Details:

The Denver Broncos have executed a series of strategic roster moves in preparation for their Week 3 showdown against the Los Angeles Rams on Sunday Night Football, signaling potential depth concerns and injury questions ahead of the primetime clash.

According to the Denver Broncos' official announcement, the team promoted long snapper Mitchell Fraboni from the practice squad to the active roster, a move that typically indicates they needed roster depth at a critical position. Simultaneously, the Broncos elevated running back Adam Prentice and wide receiver Kolbe Katsis from the practice squad for the Rams game, per Denver Sports 104.3 The Fan.

The elevation of Katsis carries particular significance. Denver Sports 104.3 The Fan reported that the move could signal game-day availability questions surrounding wide receiver Marvin Mims Jr., suggesting the Broncos may be preparing contingency plans at the receiver position.

These roster adjustments underscore Denver's preparation strategy as they face a divisional rival in Los Angeles. The promotions and elevations reflect both proactive depth management and potential injury responses as the Broncos navigate a critical stretch of the 2024 season. Head coach Sean Payton continues to shape the roster week-to-week based on performance, personnel health, and matchup requirements against the Rams' defensive scheme.
