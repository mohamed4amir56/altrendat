📰 Full Coverage & Verified Details:

Los Angeles Rams wide receiver Puka Nacua faces a doubtful designation for Week 3's high-stakes Sunday night matchup against the Denver Broncos, according to NFL.com. The star receiver missed the Rams' Week 2 victory with a groin injury and remains questionable heading into the primetime contest.

Nacua's absence extends beyond immediate game-day concerns. NBC Sports reports that his contract situation looms large as questions mount about his return timeline. The injury, sustained in early September, has already forced the Rams to navigate their passing attack without one of their top offensive weapons in their season's second game.

The timing of Nacua's injury creates strategic challenges for Los Angeles as they prepare to face Denver's defense. With the receiver potentially sidelined for a second consecutive game, the Rams must adjust their offensive scheme and redistribute targets among their remaining receivers. NFL.com's doubtful designation suggests Nacua remains unlikely to suit up Sunday, though his status could shift during the week as the Rams evaluate his progress.

The contract implications cited by NBC Sports add another layer of complexity to Nacua's recovery, suggesting broader organizational discussions about his health and role going forward. The Rams face a critical decision balancing immediate competitive needs against long-term roster management as they await clarity on their receiver's availability and expected return date.
