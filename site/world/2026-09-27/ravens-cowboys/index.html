📰 Full Coverage & Verified Details:

The NFL is taking its global expansion to new heights as the Baltimore Ravens travel to Brazil to face the Dallas Cowboys on September 27 during Week 3 of the regular season. According to The Hollywood Reporter, the matchup will be contested at Maracanã Stadium in Rio de Janeiro, one of the world's most storied sports venues.

This clash represents a landmark moment for American football's international reach. Yahoo Sports reports that this marks the Cowboys' first-ever home international game, positioning Dallas as pioneers in the NFL's aggressive push to expand its fanbase beyond North America.

The Maracanã Stadium, which hosted the 1950 and 2014 FIFA World Cups, provides an iconic backdrop for what promises to be a high-profile encounter between two franchises with passionate global followings. The Ravens bring a strong defensive tradition, while the Cowboys enter as one of the league's most recognizable franchises.

According to The Hollywood Reporter, fans can access the game through various online platforms, making this historic contest available to worldwide viewers. The Brazil game underscores the NFL's strategic commitment to cultivating international audiences and establishing permanent footholds in key global markets.

This international Week 3 contest signals that regular-season games played outside American soil are no longer novelties but core components of the NFL's long-term commercial and cultural strategy to cement American football's position as a truly global sport.
