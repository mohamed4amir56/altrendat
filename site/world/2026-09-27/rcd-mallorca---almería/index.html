📰 Full Coverage & Verified Details:

Almería and Mallorca clash on Sunday, September 27 at 16:15 CET at Estadi Mallorca Son Moix in a pivotal Jornada 7 showdown of LaLiga Hypermotion, according to MARCA. The fixture represents a critical battle in Almería's bid for direct promotion, as Canal Sur reports the Andalusian side seeks to "assault the longed-for direct ascent" through a demanding road contest.

Almería manager Xavier García Pimienta faces significant squad constraints heading into the encounter. The club's official website confirms that UEFA youth champion Yaakobishvili and midfielder Quim Junyent return to availability, while academy prospects Popovic and Koné earn their first senior call-ups. These reinforcements arrive as García Pimienta navigates the impact of what UD Almería describes as "FIFA virus" absences—players departing for international fixtures—compounded by ongoing injury concerns.

The timing presents a delicate balancing act for the Andalusian outfit. One emerging talent departing for Spanish U-20 duty immediately after Sunday's contest adds further logistical pressure. Despite these obstacles, Almería enters as a promotion-contending force in Spain's second tier, making the away trip to Son Moix a pivotal examination of their direct-ascent credentials. The broadcast will be available through MARCA's listed television channels and regional Spanish coverage, ensuring widespread fan access to this LaLiga Hypermotion showdown.
