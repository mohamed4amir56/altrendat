📰 Full Coverage & Verified Details:

UFC Vegas 121 delivered a brutal highlight Saturday night at the Meta Apex as Mexican fighter Raul Rosas Jr. secured victory over Brazilian bantamweight Roani Barcelos in an intense, back-and-forth war. The fight tested both competitors' durability across all three rounds, but Barcelos's night ended in bitter fashion when a heavy fall in the championship round left him visibly injured.

According to reports from GE and UFC.com.br, the clash was among the event's most physically demanding contests. Barcelos, a key representative of Brazil's competitive bantamweight roster, brought relentless pressure throughout, yet Rosas Jr. showed the composure and technique needed to navigate the Brazilian's aggression and secure the decision. The injury in the final frame complicated what had been a competitive matchup.

UOL reported that all 24 fighters on the UFC Vegas 121 card passed the official weigh-ins Friday without incident, including Barcelos and the six-member Brazilian contingent competing that night at the Meta Apex. The seamless weigh-in process set the stage for Saturday's action. The event also featured a women's bantamweight bout headlining the undercard, where Norma Dumont faced Argentine opposition, though that fight reportedly failed to deliver on its promise amid excessive showboating. Despite the mixed quality across the card, Rosas Jr.'s victory over Barcelos solidified his rising bantamweight credentials in the UFC's competitive division.
