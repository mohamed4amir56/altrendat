📰 Full Coverage & Verified Details:

Roman Wilson, the Pittsburgh Steelers' second-round draft pick, is set for a sudden increase in offensive snaps after the team's starting wide receiver was ruled inactive for their matchup against the New England Patriots, according to A to Z Sports.

The Arizona State product now faces pressure to deliver in an expanded role he may not have anticipated this early in his NFL tenure. Wilson's ascension comes as the Steelers adjust their receiving corps on short notice, forcing head coach Mike Tomlin to lean heavily on his young talent rather than proven veterans.

However, RotoBaller's fantasy analysis flags Wilson as a potential "faller" candidate for Week 3, cautioning that despite the increased opportunity, he may not immediately translate elevated volume into production. The report notes Wilson has fallen back down the depth chart with teammates returning to full health, suggesting his current role may be temporary rather than indicative of a long-term shift in offensive usage.

For fantasy football managers, Wilson presents a classic boom-or-bust scenario: explosive upside due to sudden offensive snaps against a vulnerable Patriots secondary, but compounded by his status as a raw rookie adjusting to NFL speed and coverage schemes. His performance against New England could significantly impact his role going forward and determine whether he earns permanent starter consideration or reverts to reserve duty as the Steelers' receiving depth stabilizes.
