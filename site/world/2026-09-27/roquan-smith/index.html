Roquan Smith drew a significant fine from the NFL following a controversial fourth-down play that decided the Ravens' matchup against the Saints. According to NBC Sports, Smith was fined for committing a facemask penalty on Saints quarterback Tyler Shough during Shough's game-winning quarterback sneak touchdown.

The play itself proved highly contentious, with questions immediately surrounding whether Shough actually crossed the goal line for the score. The Baltimore Ravens official website noted that the NFL subsequently explained its decision on the call, addressing widespread fan and team concerns about the controversial fourth-down touchdown that ultimately cost Baltimore the game.

Shough's sneak play, which resulted in the go-ahead touchdown, became the focal point of debate following the Saints' victory. While Shough reached paydirt on the controversial drive, Smith's facemask infraction compounded the Ravens' frustration, as the penalty upheld the play and sealed the outcome.

The incident highlights the razor-thin margins in professional football, where split-second decisions and penalty flags can determine championship implications. Smith's subsequent fine underscores the NFL's commitment to enforcing contact rules around the head and neck area, even in high-pressure, game-deciding moments. For the Ravens, the loss and the accompanying controversy added fuel to ongoing debates about officiating consistency in critical fourth-quarter situations.
