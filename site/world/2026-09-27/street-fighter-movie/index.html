📰 Full Coverage & Verified Details:

The Street Fighter movie is ramping up its promotional push with a major gameplay-to-film comparison trailer featuring WWE's biggest names, according to Bleacher Report. The footage, unveiled Monday, showcases how the legendary video game's signature martial arts moves translate to the big screen, building momentum ahead of next month's theatrical release.

Roman Reigns and Cody Rhodes, two of wrestling's most prominent figures, star in the upcoming film, bringing crossover appeal from sports entertainment to the fighting game franchise. The comparison trailer strategy directly highlights the movie's commitment to authenticity, pitting in-game animations against real-world action choreography to demonstrate the filmmakers' dedication to honoring the source material.

Merchandise tie-ins are already rolling out through AMC theaters nationwide. The exclusive Street Fighter popcorn bucket, per Yahoo Entertainment, features a plastic bust of Guile's iconic gravity-defying hair, complete with a chin strap and dog tag necklace—transforming the character's most recognizable feature into wearable memorabilia that fans can take home.

For viewers eager to experience similar action-packed entertainment before the Street Fighter release, ScreenRant recommends a curated list of comparable films that share the franchise's martial arts intensity and competitive spirit. The movie's multi-platform promotional strategy—combining gameplay footage, celebrity partnerships, and collectible merchandise—indicates studio confidence in capturing both nostalgic gaming enthusiasts and mainstream action audiences.
