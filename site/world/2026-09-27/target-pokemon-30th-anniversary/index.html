📰 Full Coverage & Verified Details:

Target and Best Buy have announced coordinated Pokémon 30th Anniversary events designed to celebrate three decades of the beloved franchise with exclusive merchandise and giveaways.

Target's celebration features custom card crafts, free goodie bags, and limited-edition 30th Celebration Trading Card Game drops, according to Mashable. Customers visiting participating Target locations can expect to claim commemorative items while supplies last, with the event designed to attract both longtime collectors and new fans.

Best Buy is running a parallel official Pokémon 30th Anniversary TCG Trade & Play event, reported by Polygon. The retailer's promotion includes a Mewtwo promo card as the headline giveaway, alongside additional free 30th Anniversary merchandise for attendees. Mewtwo's selection as the featured promo card holds symbolic weight, as the legendary psychic-type Pokémon has remained central to the franchise since its introduction in the original 1996 games.

These coordinated retail initiatives mark a significant push by The Pokémon Company to engage fans across multiple channels during the milestone anniversary year. The free promo card distribution strategy aims to drive foot traffic while building excitement around the Trading Card Game, which has experienced explosive growth in recent years.

Both retailers are positioning their events as limited-time offerings, suggesting customers should act quickly to secure items before inventory depletes. The dual-retailer approach underscores the Pokémon Company's confidence in sustained consumer interest across the 30th anniversary season.
