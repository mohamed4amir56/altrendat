📰 Full Coverage & Verified Details:

Target has announced a major Pokémon 30th Anniversary event designed to celebrate three decades of the iconic franchise with in-store experiences and exclusive merchandise. According to Mashable, the retailer is offering custom card crafts where attendees can create personalized Pokémon trading cards, free goodie bags packed with commemorative items, and access to limited-edition 30th Celebration TCG drops that won't be available elsewhere.

The event capitalizes on surging collector interest in Pokémon trading cards, with retailers nationwide competing to offer exclusive anniversary experiences. Polygon reports that Best Buy is simultaneously launching its own official Pokémon 30th Anniversary TCG event, where fans will receive a Mewtwo promo card and additional free 30th Anniversary merchandise as part of the celebration.

These simultaneous retail activations underscore the massive consumer appetite for Pokémon's milestone anniversary. The custom card crafting component allows fans to engage directly with the TCG experience, while limited-edition product drops create urgency among serious collectors. Free promotional cards—including the Mewtwo promo—serve as collectible keepsakes that drive foot traffic to physical stores during a period when digital retail dominates.

Both events run concurrently, giving Pokémon enthusiasts multiple opportunities to participate in 30th Anniversary festivities. Target and Best Buy's coordinated approach reflects the franchise's cultural significance and the trading card game's resurgence among both longtime collectors and new players discovering Pokémon's legacy.
