📰 Full Coverage & Verified Details:

Deion Sanders publicly criticized the Jacksonville Jaguars' deployment of Travis Hunter, the No. 2 overall pick in the 2025 NFL Draft, according to Yahoo Sports. Sanders, Hunter's coach at Colorado, voiced bewilderment at the team's usage patterns, stating "I Have No Idea What's Going On" regarding how the Jaguars are utilizing their prized defensive talent.

Hunter entered the NFL as one of the most hyped prospects in his draft class, carrying elite credentials and massive expectations from scouts and analysts. His selection in the top five positioned him as a cornerstone piece for Jacksonville's defensive rebuild. However, early deployment decisions have raised eyebrows around the league.

Sanders' public critique adds significant pressure on Jaguars coaching staff to clarify their strategic approach with Hunter. As a Hall of Fame defensive back himself, Sanders brings credibility to the discussion about optimal player usage. The Jacksonville organization addressed questions about their game plan in an O-Zone column via their official website, though detailed responses remain scarce.

Hunter's sophomore year in the NFL now carries heightened scrutiny, with the Jaguars facing questions about whether they're maximizing one of football's brightest emerging talents. Sanders' commentary reflects broader concern among NFL observers that Jacksonville may be mismanaging a generational defensive prospect during his critical early career window.
