📰 Full Coverage & Verified Details:

Trinidad and Tobago face a critical test Sunday night as they host the Dominican Republic in Concacaf Nations League action, seeking to recover from an opening-match disappointment. The match kicks off at 9 pm at Hasely Crawford Stadium in Mucurapo, Port-of-Spain, according to Trinidad Guardian.

Head coach Derek King's squad suffered a 3-2 defeat to Haiti in their Nations League opener at Sabina Park in Jamaica, setting the stage for an urgent home fixture. Despite the setback, King remains confident his team has learned from the mistakes and is prepared to execute a stronger tactical approach, Trinidad Guardian reported.

The Soca Warriors face injury concerns ahead of the critical encounter. Key attacker Tyrese Spicer, described as another vital component of King's offensive arsenal by Trinidad Express, is currently unavailable and under close monitoring by his club ahead of the home match. His status remains uncertain as the squad prepares for the Dominican Republic clash.

The Nations League contest carries significant stakes for both nations competing in the Concacaf confederation's league structure. Trinidad and Tobago's early loss to Haiti has intensified pressure to secure three points at home, where they typically enjoy strong support.
