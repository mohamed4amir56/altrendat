📰 Full Coverage & Verified Details:

Tua Tagovailoa has left the Miami Dolphins organization that drafted him, joining the Atlanta Falcons in a significant roster move that reshapes the quarterback landscape across the AFC East and NFC South. According to Heavy.com, the Dolphins were the only NFL team Tagovailoa had ever known until this season, making his departure a watershed moment in his career trajectory.

When Tagovailoa inked his deal with Atlanta, the Falcons appeared to offer him a clear path to the starting role. Yahoo Sports reported that the quarterback faced a summer competition against Michael Penix Jr., another left-handed signal-caller on the roster. However, when Penix remained sidelined and unavailable for Week 1 action, Tagovailoa suddenly emerged as the starter—a golden opportunity many believed would validate his decision to leave Miami.

The transition has not been entirely smooth. Yahoo Sports indicated that Tagovailoa "Might Be In Trouble," suggesting complications have arisen during his tenure with the Falcons. Despite initial optimism about his role, the quarterback has encountered obstacles in Atlanta.

On the injury front, the Atlanta Falcons official website confirmed that Tagovailoa has returned to full participation in team activities, signaling progress in his integration with the team. Starting right tackle Jawaan Taylor and defensive end Brandon Dorlus also resumed full practice duty alongside the quarterback, bolstering the Falcons' offensive line depth as they navigate the season.
