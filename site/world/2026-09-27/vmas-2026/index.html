📰 Full Coverage & Verified Details:

The MTV Video Music Awards are making their triumphant return to Los Angeles for 2026, with the star-studded ceremony set for Sunday, September 27. According to Yahoo Entertainment, the prestigious awards show will air on CBS and simulcast across MTV and Paramount+, bringing the iconic music celebration to fans across multiple platforms.

The event will emanate from the Peacock Theater in downtown Los Angeles, one of the city's premier entertainment venues. CBS Los Angeles is amplifying the excitement with a dedicated red carpet special, launching at 3 p.m. on Sunday to capture all the fashion moments and celebrity arrivals ahead of the main broadcast.

This return to Los Angeles marks a significant moment for the VMAs, which have historically been held in various U.S. cities. The multi-platform distribution strategy—combining traditional television on CBS and MTV with streaming on Paramount+—reflects the modern media landscape and ensures maximum accessibility for viewers worldwide. The decision to broadcast simultaneously across these channels underscores MTV's commitment to reaching audiences regardless of their preferred viewing method.

Fans can tune in via their preferred platform to catch performances, award presentations, and all the unexpected moments that have made the VMAs a cultural institution since their debut in 1984. The combination of CBS LA's red carpet coverage and the main event promises comprehensive entertainment coverage for music lovers globally.
