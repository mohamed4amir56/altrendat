📰 Full Coverage & Verified Details:

A fierce Nor'easter is battering the Northeast this weekend, bringing life-threatening conditions across a sprawling coastal corridor. According to The Weather Channel, the storm will persist into Monday, dumping heavy rain, unleashing strong winds, and triggering significant coastal flooding from North Carolina through New England. CBS News reported the Nor'easter has already turned deadly as the brunt of the system advances toward the New York City area, with teams deployed across the region documenting the escalating impacts. CNN's live coverage underscores the storm's power, with meteorologists tracking the system as it digs deeper into the East Coast. Major metropolitan areas including Boston and New York City face the most severe conditions, with residents warned to prepare for prolonged disruptions. The early-season timing of this intense storm marks an unusually aggressive start to severe weather season in the Northeast. Coastal communities are under heightened alert for life-threatening surge, while inland areas face flooding risks from torrential rainfall. Power outages and travel disruptions are expected to mount as the system maintains its grip through the weekend. Emergency management officials urge residents to secure property, stock supplies, and heed evacuation orders where issued. The storm's duration through Monday means the region faces two to three days of hazardous conditions, making this a significant weather event requiring immediate preparedness.
