📰 Full Coverage & Verified Details:

Fantasy managers face critical roster decisions heading into Week 3, with wide receiver Garrett Wilson of the New York Jets presenting a compelling start option despite modest target volume. According to Yahoo Sports, Wilson ranks ninth among all receivers in Pro Football Network's Week 3 rankings as the Jets travel to Detroit for a 1 p.m. ET Sunday matchup.

Wilson has demonstrated efficiency in the early season despite receiving just seven targets in each of the first two games—a number Yahoo Sports characterizes as "on the fringe of acceptable." Remarkably, the Jets wide receiver has converted that limited volume into 11 total catches, showcasing elite conversion rates. His first passing touchdown came in recent action via a 5-yard reception, signaling the scoring potential that has made him a focal point despite the team's offensive struggles.

Running back decisions also dominate Week 3 start-or-sit conversations. FantasySP reports that Seattle's Jadarian Price, a first-round selection, has taken the lead role in the backfield after backing up Jeremiyah Love at Notre Dame. Price logged 23 carries for 104 yards in recent action and has cleared a chest injury sustained the previous week, returning to full health.

Bleacher Nation's analysis of player prop odds adds additional context for Wilson's matchup against the Lions' secondary, with oddsmakers posting anytime touchdown props for the receiver's scoring upside potential. For fantasy owners wrestling with weekly lineup decisions, Wilson emerges as the safer floor play despite ranking outside the top eight at his position.
