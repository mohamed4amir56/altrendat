📰 Full Coverage & Verified Details:

Zay Flowers' availability for the Baltimore Ravens' Week 3 clash against the Dallas Cowboys remains up in the air following a hamstring injury that has sidelined the young wide receiver. According to CBS Sports, Flowers is officially listed as questionable ahead of the contest.

The Ravens provided a glimmer of hope regarding Flowers' participation, with the team indicating he has a "real opportunity" to play versus Dallas, per the Baltimore Ravens' official injury report. However, if the explosive receiver does take the field, he could be operating under a pitch count as the team manages his recovery, CBS Sports reported.

Fantasy football managers are closely monitoring the situation as they prepare their rosters for Week 3. Yahoo Sports' Fantasy Football Live crew emphasized the importance of clarity on Flowers' status before making lineup decisions, noting that his participation level could significantly impact his fantasy production.

The Ravens will be without offensive lineman Ronnie Stanley, who has been ruled out after missing all week of practice due to a toe injury. Defensive lineman Nnamdi Madubuike, meanwhile, is poised to make his debut for Baltimore.

Flowers, a key component of the Ravens' passing attack, had emerged as a reliable target for quarterback Lamar Jackson. His potential absence or limited snap count could reshape Baltimore's offensive gameplan heading into their divisional showdown with the Cowboys.
