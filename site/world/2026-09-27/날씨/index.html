Korea Faces Treacherous Autumn Holiday Weather: Rain and Dense Fog Blanket Nation

⚡ Real-Time Coverage & Breaking Insight
🔥 Inside the Story: What Just Happened

As South Korea's Chuseok holiday reaches its final day, meteorologists warn of widespread rainfall and thick morning fog across the country, creating hazardous travel conditions for millions returning home.

📰 Full Coverage & Verified Details:

South Korea is experiencing unsettled autumn weather during the final day of Chuseok, the nation's most important holiday period, according to reporting from Chosunbiz. Heavy rain and dense fog are expected across multiple regions, particularly affecting the southern provinces and Jeju Island, as reported by KBS News.

The central regions of South Korea are forecast to remain mostly clear throughout the day, providing some relief for travelers in Seoul and surrounding areas. However, the southern regions and Jeju Island face persistent cloudy conditions with intermittent showers and gusty wind patterns expected through the evening hours, according to KBS News meteorological forecasts.

The combination of rainfall and dense morning fog poses significant challenges for the millions of South Koreans returning to their hometowns and commuting back to major cities as the Chuseok holiday concludes. Visibility on major highways is expected to decrease substantially during early morning hours, with thick fog persisting throughout central and southern regions.

Weather authorities are urging travelers to exercise extreme caution during their journeys, particularly those heading southward toward Jeju Island and southern coastal provinces. The unstable atmospheric conditions typical of autumn weather patterns in Korea are creating conditions that could delay transportation networks and affect travel safety. Drivers are advised to reduce speed and increase following distances during fog advisories, particularly on major expressways connecting Seoul to provincial destinations.
