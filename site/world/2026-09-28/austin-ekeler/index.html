Commanders Bring Back Austin Ekeler Despite Torn Achilles Injury

⚡ Real-Time Coverage & Breaking Insight
🔥 Inside the Story: What Just Happened

The Washington Commanders are expected to re-sign running back Austin Ekeler after he suffered a torn Achilles early in the 2025 season.

📰 Full Coverage & Verified Details:

Austin Ekeler is set to rejoin the Washington Commanders in a move that underscores the team's confidence in the veteran running back's recovery prospects. According to NFL.com, the Commanders are expected to bring back Ekeler, who spent the past two seasons with the organization. The signing comes despite a significant setback earlier this season when Ekeler sustained a torn Achilles injury in early 2025, an injury that typically sidelines running backs for extended periods. CBS Sports confirmed that Ekeler is poised to return to Washington. The decision reflects the Commanders' assessment that Ekeler remains a valuable asset to their offensive plans, even as he enters his recovery phase. Ekeler has been a dependable presence in the NFL, and his familiarity with the Commanders' system likely played a role in the organization's choice to retain him. The timing of his return and the scope of his role in the 2025 season remain unclear, but the re-signing signals the team's long-term commitment to the running back. As Ekeler works through his rehabilitation, the Commanders will monitor his progress closely to determine his availability for upcoming games.
