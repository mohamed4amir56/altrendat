France Faces Belgium in Nations League Showdown Without Star Captain Mbappe

⚡ Real-Time Coverage & Breaking Insight
🔥 Inside the Story: What Just Happened

Two-time world champions France travel to Brussels for a crucial UEFA Nations League clash against Belgium, notably missing captain Kylian Mbappe due to injury.

📰 Full Coverage & Verified Details:

France arrives at King Baudouin Stadium in Brussels on Monday, September 28, for the second matchday of UEFA Nations League 2026/27 Group A1 without their talismanic captain. According to Al Jazeera, Kylian Mbappe's absence due to injury marks a significant blow to Les Bleus' attacking firepower as they seek to build on their opening victory.

The French side enters the contest riding momentum from a 1-0 win over Turkey in their Nations League opener, as reported by Yahoo Sports. Despite losing their captain, France remains among the tournament favorites, having claimed the World Cup twice in their storied history.

Belgium, hosting the encounter, will field their own star-studded lineup featuring midfielder Kevin De Bruyne, according to Al Jazeera's team roster reports. The Belgian squad will look to exploit France's injury troubles and claim a statement victory at home.

BBC Sport is providing live text commentary, score updates, and comprehensive match statistics from the encounter. This Nations League fixture carries additional significance as part of the prestigious international competition's Group A1 stage, where points are crucial for qualifying ambitions.
