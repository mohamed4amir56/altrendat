Bronson Reed signs to face Oba Femi at WWE Money in the Bank on October 10

By Karim Fouad · Published 29 Sep 2026, 02:00 UTC

Bronson Reed agreed to a contract match against Oba Femi at WWE Money in the Bank premium live event on October 10 in New Orleans after Femi issued the challenge on Monday Night Raw.

Reed has already destroyed Femi twice in the ring, yet signed the contract without hesitation, declaring he came back to WWE to conquer The Ruler. Femi responded by saying Reed had just sealed his fate with his signature.

The match caps a tense rivalry that intensified when Reed returned from a torn biceps injury at Sunday Night's Main Event and rejoined The Vision faction. Femi, who won the King of the Ring, had been focused on his months-long feud with The Vision before Reed's return changed the dynamic.

Femi arrived on Raw in Milwaukee with a contract already approved by Raw General Manager Adam Pearce and WWE Chief Content Officer Paul "Triple H" Levesque. He called Reed to the ring, and Reed signed without hesitation, putting pen to paper before making a quick exit. The segment ended with both men acknowledging the other's credibility—Femi admitting Reed had "some balls" for accepting, and Reed claiming he would dethrone The Ruler.

Money in the Bank will mark Reed's first televised match since his injury in May. The event at the Smoothie King Center will also feature Roman Reigns defending the World Heavyweight Championship against LA Knight, alongside the annual men's and women's ladder matches.
