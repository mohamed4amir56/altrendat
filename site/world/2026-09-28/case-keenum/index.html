The Chicago Bears' playoff hopes face a significant test Monday night against the Philadelphia Eagles, but they'll do so without their franchise centerpiece. According to the Chicago Bears organization, quarterback Caleb Williams has been ruled out due to a hamstring injury, forcing the team to turn to backup options for one of the season's most pivotal games.

Tyson Bagent, the Bears' third-string quarterback, cleared concussion protocol Saturday and is eligible to play, according to NFL.com and head coach Ben Johnson. Bagent was sidelined following a previous head injury but has returned to full practice participation ahead of the Monday night showdown.

However, the Bears have not yet announced their starting quarterback decision between Bagent and veteran Case Keenum, as reported by the Chicago Bears. The choice between the two backups carries significant implications for Chicago's offensive execution and game plan against Philadelphia's formidable defense.

This injury to Williams marks a major blow to the Bears' momentum entering a critical divisional contest. The team now must rely on inexperienced or veteran backup options to navigate one of the NFL's toughest defensive matchups. Head coach Ben Johnson will need to gameplan accordingly, potentially simplifying the offensive scheme to accommodate the backup quarterback's familiarity with the system. The decision between Bagent and Keenum will likely hinge on practice performance and comfort level with the game plan heading into Monday evening.
