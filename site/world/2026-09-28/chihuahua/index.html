Morena Party Set To Announce Chihuahua Governor Candidate Amid Seven-State Selection

⚡ Real-Time Coverage & Breaking Insight
🔥 Inside the Story: What Just Happened

Mexico's Morena party will reveal its chosen gubernatorial candidate for Chihuahua and six other states Monday, with party leadership making final decisions after internal polling surveys.

⚡ The Story in 30 Seconds (Quick Takeaways)

📰 Full Coverage & Verified Details:

Morena, Mexico's ruling party, is poised to announce its official candidate for Chihuahua's governorship on Monday, September 28, 2026, alongside selections for six additional gubernatorial races. According to Crónica del Poder, the party will simultaneously nominate precandidates for governorships in Nuevo León, Sonora, San Luis Potosí, Baja California Sur, Nayarit, and Tlaxcala.

The decision rests with Morena's national leadership, including party president Ariadna Montiel and Citlalli Hernández, president of the National Elections Commission, as reported by El Diario. The announcement follows closed-door meetings in Mexico City where leading Chihuahua gubernatorial aspirants gathered to learn results from internal party surveys that determined the final selection.

Mayra Chávez, one of the primary contenders, issued a statement asking supporters for patience while awaiting the official announcement, per 860 Líder Informativo. This process represents Morena's internal vetting mechanism ahead of the 2027 gubernatorial elections, a critical political cycle as multiple states prepare to elect new leaders.

The timing is significant for Mexico's political landscape, as these seven states represent substantial electoral battlegrounds. Chihuahua, in particular, has been a competitive region, and Morena's selection signals the party's strategic priorities for the northwestern state. The announcement marks the final stage of candidate selection before the party's formal campaign launches for 2027.
