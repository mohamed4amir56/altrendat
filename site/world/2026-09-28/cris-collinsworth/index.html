📰 Full Coverage & Verified Details:

Cris Collinsworth's broadcasting style has become a flashpoint for frustrated NFL viewers, who are increasingly vocal about his perceived favoritism toward certain players and teams. During Week 3 of the 2026 NFL season, the NBC analyst drew sharp criticism for his Sunday Night Football commentary on the Broncos-Rams matchup, with fans flooding social media to express their displeasure. According to Yahoo Sports, viewers described Collinsworth's work as "truly the most insufferable" and complained that "the guy is unbearable."

The controversy extends to Collinsworth's well-documented admiration for Kansas City Chiefs quarterback Patrick Mahomes. The Big Lead reported that Collinsworth has been openly expressing his "deep love" for Mahomes as the star returns to peak form this season. This pattern of lavish praise didn't go unnoticed during a recent Chiefs-Colts Sunday Night Football broadcast.

Comedian Nikki Glaser took aim at the analyst during the SNF introduction, jokingly calling out Collinsworth's "gushing" over Mahomes, according to The Mirror US. The public ribbing highlights a broader sentiment among fans that Collinsworth's commentary often strays from neutral analysis into overt favoritism. As the 2026 NFL season progresses, the scrutiny surrounding his broadcast work suggests networks may need to reconsider his on-air presence or adjust his role to balance entertainment with professional objectivity.
