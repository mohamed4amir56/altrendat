📰 Full Coverage & Verified Details:

The Denver Broncos announced that wide receiver Marvin Mims Jr. will be inactive for Sunday Night Football's Week 3 clash against the Los Angeles Rams, according to the Denver Broncos official announcement. Mims had been listed as questionable heading into the game, but the team ultimately ruled him out for the primetime matchup.

The Broncos are not alone in their receiving corps challenges. According to NBC Sports, the Rams will also be without a key receiver, as Puka Nacua has been ruled inactive for the same contest. Both teams will operate at reduced firepower at the wide receiver position during the nationally televised game.

Mims' absence represents a significant blow to Denver's offensive weapons as the Broncos look to improve their record in Week 3. The young receiver had been a part of the team's offensive plans this season, making his unavailability a notable factor in Sunday night's matchup. The Rams, similarly impacted by Nacua's absence, will need to adjust their passing attack accordingly.

The gameday inactives list, compiled by Mile High Report, highlights the various health concerns both teams are managing heading into the Week 3 contest. With both squads missing established receiving talent, the game figures to be heavily influenced by how each team's remaining offensive weapons perform under the primetime spotlight on NBC.
