Massive Five-Alarm Blaze Engulfs Roxbury's Edison Building as Firefighters Battle Inferno

⚡ Real-Time Coverage & Breaking Insight
🔥 Inside the Story: What Just Happened

Boston firefighters are battling a rapidly escalating five-alarm fire at the Edison Building in Roxbury's Nubian Square, forcing street closures and prompting residents to evacuate the area.

📰 Full Coverage & Verified Details:

A devastating fire erupted at the Edison Building in Roxbury on Monday morning, quickly intensifying into a major emergency that stretched Boston's fire department resources. According to The Boston Globe, the blaze escalated rapidly, with the fire department confirming that "very quickly it has climbed to 5 alarms." Firehouse.com reported that Boston firefighters deployed ladder pipes in active operation to combat the multi-story commercial structure fire in the landmark building.

The conflagration has created significant traffic disruptions across the neighborhood. NBC Boston reported that drivers should expect major congestion near Warren and Dudley streets in the Nubian Square area, with Boston police urging motorists to use alternate routes to avoid the emergency zone.

The five-alarm designation indicates an exceptionally serious fire requiring maximum response from multiple fire departments and mutual aid resources. Such classifications are typically reserved for large-scale structural fires with significant risk to surrounding properties and public safety. Firefighters continued their aggressive suppression efforts throughout the morning as emergency crews worked to contain the blaze and prevent spread to adjacent buildings in the densely populated commercial district.
