📰 Full Coverage & Verified Details:

Episode 7 of DC's Lanterns, titled "The Jordan Boys' Legacy," pivoted the narrative squarely onto Hal Jordan despite his death, according to The Ringer's recap. The penultimate episode thrust the deceased Lantern into the spotlight, delivering what The Ringer described as a "close-up" moment for the character even in his departed state.

The episode expanded the show's mythological scope considerably. IndieWire reported that Episode 7 introduced "remarkable oddities" including dark matter portals, dead alien worlds, and a metaphysical journey across the river Styx—elements that deepened the supernatural and cosmic dimensions of the DC universe being constructed.

Hal Jordan's appearance, whether as a hologram, force ghost, or alternate manifestation, forced both characters and viewers to grapple with his unfinished business and the weight of his legacy as a Green Lantern. The reveal positioned his story as central to understanding the current conflict and the choices facing the remaining heroes.

With Episode 7 serving as the penultimate installment of Season 1, the introduction of Hal's spiritual or spectral presence sets up what IndieWire called "a charged finish." The episode's exploration of legacy, sacrifice, and the persistence of power beyond death suggests that resolving Hal Jordan's arc will prove crucial to the series' conclusion. The stakes have clearly elevated as the season approaches its climax, with dead worlds and mystical passages signaling that no boundary—not even death itself—remains fixed in this DC adaptation.
