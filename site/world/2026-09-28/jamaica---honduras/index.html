Honduras begins Concacaf Nations League campaign with four matches starting September 25

By Karim Fouad · Published 29 Sep 2026, 00:00 UTC

Honduras will play four matches in the Concacaf Nations League beginning September 25 against Surinam, Jamaica, Martinica, and Jamaica again, aiming to advance to the quarterfinals.

Quick facts

Tournament start date
September 25
Opponents
Surinam, Jamaica, Martinica, Jamaica
Target
Quarterfinals and Copa Oro 2027 qualification

Honduras faces a critical window in its pursuit of the Concacaf Nations League quarterfinals and positioning toward the 2027 Copa Oro. The national team will open against Surinam on September 25, followed by matches against Jamaica, Martinica, and Jamaica again. The campaign carries significance beyond the pitch: according to Neilsberg Research data from 2020-2024, approximately 135,000 people of Honduran origin live in the New York metropolitan area and another 135,000 in Houston. Miami hosts around 97,000 Honduran-origin residents, Washington D.C. has over 69,000, and Los Angeles exceeds 62,000. Additional communities exist in Dallas, Charlotte, Atlanta, Austin, and Nashville. Honduran players currently in MLS include Andy Nájar and Bryan Acosta at Nashville SC, Joseph Rosales at Austin FC, David Ruíz at Red Bull New York, and Oscar Avilez at D.C. United. These players and their communities will follow the national team's progress through the tournament, with Honduras playing not only in traditional venues but also through the homes, restaurants, bars, and communication groups of Honduran expatriates across MLS cities.
