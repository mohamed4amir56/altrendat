Garage Beer valued at $200 million as Kelce brothers expand nationwide

By Karim Fouad · Published 29 Sep 2026, 02:00 UTC

Travis and Jason Kelce's beer company Garage Beer reached a $200 million valuation after receiving strategic growth investment from Durational Capital Management, with plans to expand marketing and distribution.

Garage Beer has increased its volume over 400% in the past year and expanded nationally to become one of the fastest growing beer brands in the U.S., according to CEO Andy Sauer. The company's valuation now exceeds $200 million following a strategic growth investment from Durational Capital Management.

The investment will be used to accelerate marketing, expand the sales force, and strengthen relationships with distributors, retailers and customers. For consumers, this means broader access to Garage Beer's products: the brand has already secured placement at Red Robin restaurants nationwide, featuring its Classic and Lime varieties.

Travis Kelce, the Kansas City Chiefs tight end, and his older brother Jason Kelce, former Philadelphia Eagles star, became minority owners of Garage Beer in 2024. In a statement, they said the brand represents their values and their approach to business: "In a world where everyone tries to be the smartest person in the room, we go full send on the dumb ideas that are a blast." The brothers also noted that "a lot of our best memories, celebrations and conversations have come with a beer by our side." Beyond the Red Robin partnership, Garage Beer has launched creative marketing campaigns, including a recent collaboration with Liquid Death and a wedding promotion offering couples a $2,000 gift card for rings and $5,000 for a honeymoon at a Garage Beer tailgate in Philadelphia.
