Lola Vice wins Money in the Bank qualifier in Raw main roster debut

By Karim Fouad · Published 29 Sep 2026, 00:31 UTC

Lola Vice, promoted from NXT after four and a half years, won her first WWE main roster match and qualified for the Women's Money in the Bank ladder match.

Quick facts

Air time
8 ET/5 PT on Netflix
Previous tenure
Four and a half years on WWE NXT
Qualifying match opponents
Raquel Rodriguez and Kelani Jordan

Lola Vice won her opening match on WWE Raw, defeating Raquel Rodriguez and Kelani Jordan in a Money in the Bank qualifying contest. The victory came days after the former NXT Women's Champion signed her contract to join the red brand, only to be interrupted by rival Roxanne Perez in an intense verbal confrontation that nearly escalated into a brawl.

The promotion marks a significant step for Vice, who spent four and a half years developing her character on NXT and gained additional experience in AAA. She now competes against Perez and other Superstars for the Money in the Bank briefcase, a prize that could define her early main roster career.

Vice said the transition still feels surreal. "I stood for a second, and I was like, 'Wow, I'm on Monday Night Raw. I can't believe this,'" she told Busted Open Radio. She credited her time on the developmental brand as essential preparation, noting she was "mentally and physically at my best" when receiving the call. Vice emphasized her focus on establishing herself independently while representing Cuban culture on the main roster, though she expressed interest in potentially working with Paul Heyman if the opportunity arose.

Vice and Perez will both compete in the Money in the Bank Ladder Match, which airs on Netflix.
