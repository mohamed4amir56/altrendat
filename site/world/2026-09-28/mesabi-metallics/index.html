Trump Unveils Historic $15 Billion Steel Mill in Iowa, Creating 1,750 Jobs by 2030

⚡ Real-Time Coverage & Breaking Insight
🔥 Inside the Story: What Just Happened

Mesabi Metallics will build the largest steel plant in U.S. history in Iowa, targeting production launch in 2030 and generating nearly 1,800 permanent jobs.

📰 Full Coverage & Verified Details:

The Trump administration announced a transformative manufacturing investment as Mesabi Metallics plans to construct the largest steel mill in American history in Iowa, according to NBC News and CBS News. The $15 billion facility represents a landmark commitment to domestic steel production and reshoring manufacturing capabilities.

The plant is expected to generate 1,750 permanent jobs for Iowa workers, addressing significant workforce needs in the region and strengthening the state's industrial base, CBS News reported. This job creation underscores the project's scale and economic impact across the state.

Mesabi Metallics has set an ambitious timeline, targeting the start of production in 2030, according to a White House official cited by NBC News. The multi-year construction and development phase signals the company's serious commitment to the venture despite the substantial capital requirements.

The announcement aligns with the Trump administration's stated focus on American manufacturing and reducing dependence on foreign steel production. The Iowa location positions the facility in a region with existing industrial infrastructure and workforce capabilities. The initiative reflects broader policy goals around tariffs and domestic manufacturing competitiveness in the steel sector, though specific operational details and financing structures remain under development.
