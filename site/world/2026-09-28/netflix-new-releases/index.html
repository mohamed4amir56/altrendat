📰 Full Coverage & Verified Details:

Netflix is delivering a heavyweight lineup of new releases this week, headlined by the arrival of all five Jason Bourne films starring Matt Damon. According to AOL, The Bourne Identity, The Bourne Supremacy, The Bourne Ultimatum, The Bourne Legacy, and Jason Bourne are hitting the platform during the week of September 28–October 7, 2026. The espionage thriller series follows a CIA assassin navigating high-stakes missions across the globe.

Beyond the Bourne franchise, Netflix is simultaneously rolling out three seasons of the long-running procedural drama NCIS (Seasons 20–22), giving subscribers access to dozens of crime-solving episodes. Yahoo Entertainment highlighted that the week's releases also include the literary adaptation East of Eden and the documentary Doing Life, rounding out a diverse content menu.

Netflix's official Tudum guide confirmed additional titles entering the catalog this period, including LEGO ONE PIECE, The Arena, The Widower: 'Til Death Do Us Part, The Final Problem, and others. The staggered rollout reflects Netflix's strategy of blending prestige dramas, action franchises, and niche content to appeal to its global subscriber base. With over 5,000 searches worldwide for Netflix new releases, the platform's September-to-October programming slate is clearly capturing audience attention across multiple genres and demographics.
