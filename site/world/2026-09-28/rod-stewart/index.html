Rod Stewart Confirms Final Encore Tour After Heart Procedure and Health Scares

⚡ Real-Time Coverage & Breaking Insight
🔥 Inside the Story: What Just Happened

The 81-year-old rock legend announces his last-ever tour next year, ending a touring career spanning six decades after recent cardiac intervention.

📰 Full Coverage & Verified Details:

Rod Stewart has confirmed his "Final Encore Tour" will mark the end of his legendary touring career, according to Fox News. The announcement comes following a coronary stent procedure and recent health setbacks that prompted the iconic performer to reassess his future on stage.

At 81 years old, Stewart is wrapping up a touring legacy that stretches back to the early 1960s when he was still in his mid-teens, The Guardian reported. The British rock icon will play dates across the United Kingdom and Europe during what he has declared to be "most definitely my last" tour, scheduled for 2027.

This retirement marks the culmination of more than six decades of touring, WCVB noted. Stewart's decision reflects both his age and the health challenges he has recently faced, which made continuing an intensive touring schedule increasingly difficult.

The "Maggie May" and "Sailing" hitmaker has been a cornerstone of rock music for generations, known for his raspy voice and energetic stage presence. His final tour announcement gives fans a window to experience one last opportunity to see the legendary performer live before he steps away from the road permanently. The exact dates and venues for the Final Encore Tour are expected to be announced in the coming months.
