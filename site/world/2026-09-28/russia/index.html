Russia Suffers Staggering Losses As Ukraine War Grinds Into Fifth Year

📰 Full Coverage & Verified Details:

Russia continues to sustain devastating losses in its ongoing war against Ukraine, with Ukrainian military officials documenting mounting casualties across the battlefield. According to Ukraine's General Staff, reported by Українська правда, Russia lost 1,710 soldiers killed and wounded over a 24-hour period, along with 96 artillery systems destroyed.

The cumulative toll has reached catastrophic levels. Ukrinform reports that total Russian combat losses since the full-scale invasion began on February 24, 2022, now exceed 1.5 million personnel—approximately 1,528,970 troops eliminated as of late September 2026. Defense Express corroborates these figures, noting that Russian forces have suffered persistent attrition across 1,677 days of active combat operations.

Beyond personnel, the destruction of Russian military hardware continues at a relentless pace. Ukrainian defenders have neutralized 541,763 air targets belonging to Russian forces, according to Defense Express, while destroying thousands of tanks, armored vehicles, and support trucks. The daily losses of 1,710 troops and 96 artillery pieces underscore the grinding, attritional nature of the conflict now in its fifth year.

These figures, compiled by official Ukrainian military command structures, reflect the sustained defensive operations and counteroffensive capabilities Ukraine has maintained despite Russia's numerical advantages and industrial capacity. The war has become a war of attrition, with Russia facing continuous replenishment challenges as losses mount across all combat branches.
