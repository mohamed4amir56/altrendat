Slipknot Announces Massive 2027 Worldwide Stadium Tour With Major Support Acts

⚡ Real-Time Coverage & Breaking Insight
🔥 Inside the Story: What Just Happened

The heavy metal giants have unveiled an ambitious global stadium tour for 2027, featuring Marilyn Manson and Bring Me The Horizon as support acts across multiple continents.

📰 Full Coverage & Verified Details:

Slipknot has officially confirmed an expansive worldwide stadium tour launching in 2027, capping off a carefully orchestrated reveal campaign, according to Knotfest. The band completed a cryptic rollout involving strategically positioned posters branded with their tribal imagery, video teasers referencing the upcoming year, and projection displays at focal international locations before the formal announcement.

The (sic)est Stadium Tour will see the Iowa-based metal pioneers performing at major venues across the globe. According to the BBC, Marilyn Manson will join Slipknot as a support act during their London show, marking a significant collaboration between two of alternative metal's most iconic acts. The musical pairing brings together two legacy artists known for theatrical, provocative live performances.

Additional dates already confirmed include a headline performance at San Francisco's Golden Gate Park next summer, with Bring Me The Horizon appearing on that billing, Yahoo Entertainment reported. Tickets for the Golden Gate Park show went on sale this week.

The 2027 tour represents Slipknot's most ambitious touring schedule in years, capitalizing on renewed momentum in the metal community. The band's decision to span multiple continents and utilize stadium-sized venues demonstrates confidence in their continued drawing power and fanbase dedication.
