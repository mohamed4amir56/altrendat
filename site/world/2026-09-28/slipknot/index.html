Slipknot Teases Massive Stadium Tour Across 2026 and 2027 Dates

⚡ Real-Time Coverage & Breaking Insight
🔥 Inside the Story: What Just Happened

Slipknot is orchestrating a global stadium takeover, with mysterious posters and cryptic teasers hinting at a major tour spanning multiple years and iconic venues worldwide.

📰 Full Coverage & Verified Details:

Slipknot is building momentum for what appears to be their most ambitious touring cycle yet. Wembley Stadium in London has posted a cryptic image of the band's iconic logo paired with the date 09.28.26, according to Louder, signaling a major show at the legendary 65,000-capacity arena. The teaser carries the enigmatic message "If you're 555," referencing the band's numerical symbolism.

The London hint is just the tip of the iceberg. Multiple stadiums across the globe are simultaneously teasing the band's return, suggesting a coordinated summer-season stadium run, according to Theprp.com. This synchronized rollout indicates Slipknot is planning a sweeping international tour rather than isolated dates.

The mystery deepens further into 2027. Knotfest reports that Slipknot has shared a new teaser visual pointing toward 2027 plans, following an active week of social media posts celebrating the band's storied live history. Mysterious posters have appeared in cities worldwide, directing fans to a cryptic website displaying a burning tribal S—the band's signature symbol reimagined in flames.

The layered approach—combining stadium announcements, social media campaigns, and international street marketing—demonstrates Slipknot's confidence in their drawing power and suggests a release of new music or major tour announcement is imminent. Fans have been speculating about the band's next chapter for months, and these coordinated teasers finally confirm something substantial is coming.
