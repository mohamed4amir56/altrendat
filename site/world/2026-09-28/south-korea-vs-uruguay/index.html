South Korea's national football team will clash with Uruguay tonight at Seoul World Cup Stadium at 8 p.m. in a high-stakes friendly match, according to Chosunbiz. Head coach Robert Moreno has named captain Son Heung-min in the starting XI alongside Lee Kang-in in a two-striker formation, with Oh in the lineup as well.

Son, currently playing for LAFC, is just one goal away from matching South Korea's all-time record for goals scored in men's A-matches, according to StarNews Korea. The veteran forward will have a prime opportunity to etch his name into history on home soil at Seoul World Cup Stadium.

Lee Kang-in, speaking at an official press conference held at the stadium a day before kickoff, expressed confidence in the squad's cohesion. "All players worked hard for the team," Lee told reporters via SBS News. "I have high expectations as I am playing a match in Korea after a long time."

This friendly serves as a crucial test for Moreno's tactical approach following his managerial debut against Ecuador. The two-striker system featuring Son and Lee Kang-in represents an aggressive attacking strategy designed to penetrate Uruguay's defense. With Son hunting the historic goal milestone and Lee Kang-in energized by playing domestically, South Korea aims to deliver a commanding performance in front of their home crowd.
