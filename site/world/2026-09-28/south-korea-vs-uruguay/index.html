Son Heung-min Chases Historic Record as South Korea Takes On Uruguay Tonight

⚡ Real-Time Coverage & Breaking Insight
🔥 Inside the Story: What Just Happened

Captain Son Heung-min is one goal away from matching South Korea's all-time scoring record as Coach Moreno fields a two-striker lineup against Uruguay in a high-stakes friendly at Seoul World Cup Stadium.

📰 Full Coverage & Verified Details:

South Korea's national football team faces Uruguay tonight at 8 p.m. at Seoul World Cup Stadium in a friendly match that carries significant implications for Son Heung-min's legacy. According to Star News Korea, the LAFC forward stands just one goal shy of equaling South Korea's all-time men's A-match scoring record, making this encounter a potential turning point in his international career.

Head coach Robert Moreno has crafted a two-striker formation to maximize attacking threats, pairing Son with Lee Kang-in and Oh in advanced positions, as reported by Chosunbiz. This tactical setup reflects Moreno's intent to give his star captain every opportunity to etch his name deeper into South Korean football history.

Son's pursuit of the all-time goal record underscores his evolution as one of Korean soccer's greatest talents. Playing for LAFC in MLS, the 32-year-old has maintained his elite output at international level, demonstrating the sustained excellence that defines his career trajectory, per 매일경제 (Maeil Business Newspaper).

The friendly against Uruguay provides the perfect platform for Son's milestone moment. With the entire nation watching from Seoul, the stakes extend beyond a simple exhibition match—this is an opportunity for the captain to cement his place among South Korea's football immortals while helping his team build momentum heading into crucial upcoming competitions.
