SpaceX Stock Poised for Explosive Growth as Colossus AI Empire Scales to 1.44 Million GPUs

⚡ Real-Time Coverage & Breaking Insight
🔥 Inside the Story: What Just Happened

Evercore projects significant EBITDA upside for SpaceX as its Colossus supercomputer expands to 2.3–2.7GW capacity, potentially doubling revenue by 2027.

📰 Full Coverage & Verified Details:

SpaceX's aggressive pivot into artificial intelligence infrastructure is reshaping investor expectations for the aerospace giant's financial trajectory. According to Evercore, the company's Colossus GPU data center initiative could unlock major EBITDA upside as the facility scales its computational power, with 2027 revenue projections climbing substantially higher than previous forecasts.

Tom's Hardware reports that SpaceX is adding 660,000 AI GPUs this year alone, bringing its total operational capacity to 1.44 million units. The company is simultaneously constructing a dedicated 1.2-gigawatt power plant to keep these systems fully operational, underscoring the massive infrastructure commitment required to support its AI ambitions.

The Motley Fool highlights that Colossus 2 supercomputer could double its Nvidia GPU deployment by year-end, delivering substantial gains for the semiconductor manufacturer while signaling SpaceX's determination to become a dominant player in the data center race. Yahoo Finance identifies this AI expansion as a key "green flag" for investors, though acknowledging unspecified headwinds remain.

Despite SpaceX's impressive operational fundamentals, The Motley Fool notes the stock itself has delivered inconsistent returns historically. Yet with Colossus ramping to 2.3–2.7GW capacity and generating forecasted revenue surges, analysts suggest the equity could experience meaningful appreciation over the next five years as these AI investments mature into revenue engines.
