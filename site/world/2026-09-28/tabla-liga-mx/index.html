The Apertura 2026 season delivered a dramatic reversal at the summit on Matchday 10, as América surged back into contention for the Liga MX title. According to Fox Sports, the capital club equalized with Toluca atop the general standings, matching both points and goal differential after Sunday's conclusion of the tenth date. The move represents a crucial momentum shift for one of Mexico's most prestigious franchises as the tournament enters its decisive phase.

Meanwhile, the individual accolade race heated up dramatically. Mediotiempo reported that Henry Martín, América's prolific forward, struck a stunning hat-trick during the matchday, propelling himself into serious contention for the tournament's Golden Boot award. The three-goal performance underscores Martín's growing importance to his club's championship ambitions and signals he has emerged as one of Liga MX's premier finishing talents this season.

The tactical battle for supremacy now intensifies between América and Toluca, with both squads level on points and separated only by marginal goal-differential calculations. As the Apertura 2026 progresses beyond the halfway point, the dual storylines—the title chase and Henry Martín's individual scoring heroics—have captured widespread fan attention across Mexico and beyond. The coming matchdays promise to reveal whether América can sustain this resurgence or if Toluca will reassert dominance at the league's apex.
