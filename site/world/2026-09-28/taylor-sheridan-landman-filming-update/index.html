Taylor Sheridan's Landman Season 3 Officially Rolls Cameras in Texas

⚡ Real-Time Coverage & Breaking Insight
🔥 Inside the Story: What Just Happened

Production on Taylor Sheridan's hit oil industry drama Landman has officially commenced with cameras rolling around Fort Worth, Texas.

📰 Full Coverage & Verified Details:

Landman season 3 is now in active production, with cast member James Jordan confirming the milestone through a set photo shared publicly. According to Fox News, filming is taking place in and around Fort Worth, Texas, marking the official start of the highly anticipated third season. The production update comes after The Independent reported that Jordan had received his season three script last month, signaling the show's progression toward the new installment. The oil and gas-focused drama, created by Taylor Sheridan, continues to expand his entertainment empire alongside other successful projects like Lioness. The confirmation of active filming resolves earlier uncertainties about the show's timeline and provides fans with concrete evidence that new episodes are in development. Sheridan's signature storytelling, which has made Landman a cornerstone series for its network, will continue with the established cast moving forward into production. The Texas-based shoot location remains consistent with the show's authentic oil industry backdrop, allowing production to capture the real landscapes and environments central to the series' narrative. With cameras now rolling, the path toward a season 3 release appears firmly on track, ending the speculation that had surrounded the show's development schedule in recent months.
