📰 Full Coverage & Verified Details:

A dangerous nor'easter is pummeling the Northeast with unprecedented fury, bringing deadly conditions and catastrophic coastal damage to the region. According to FOX Weather, the storm is generating severe coastal flooding, beach erosion, and damaging winds that pose immediate threats to life and property across multiple states.

Massachusetts' South Shore has emerged as a particular flashpoint, with WCVB reporting that coastal communities in the region experienced significant impacts over the weekend. Scituate, a vulnerable coastal town, suffered direct damage to its seawalls as powerful waves and strong winds battered the shoreline. The storm surge—the abnormal rise in water level during the nor'easter—has proven especially destructive to coastal infrastructure designed to protect homes and businesses.

FOX Weather is actively tracking live updates as the nor'easter continues its assault on the Northeast. The network is monitoring real-time radar data, compiling power outage reports, and issuing updated weather forecasts as conditions evolve. Dangerous winds are whipping through the region, compounding hazards from the rising water and erosion.

The combination of storm surge, coastal flooding, and high winds creates a perfect storm scenario for coastal communities. Residents in affected areas face threats ranging from property damage and beach erosion to power outages and dangerous travel conditions. Emergency management officials are urging coastal residents to remain vigilant and follow evacuation orders as the nor'easter continues its destructive passage.
