Madagascar and Tanzania seek first points in AFCON 2027 qualifying after opening defeats

By Karim Fouad · Published 29 Sep 2026, 15:00 UTC

Madagascar and Tanzania face each other on Tuesday, September 29, in Group L of the 2027 Africa Cup of Nations qualifiers, both searching for their first points after losing their opening matches.

Quick facts

Match date and time
Tuesday, September 29, 4:00pm East Africa Time
Venue
Stade d'Honneur in Meknès, Morocco
Madagascar's opening result
Lost 2-1 to Nigeria
Tanzania's opening result
Lost 2-0 to Guinea-Bissau
AFCON 2027 co-hosts
Tanzania, Kenya, and Uganda

Both teams enter Matchday 2 without a single point, making the encounter in Morocco one of the most consequential of their campaigns so far. Madagascar lost 2-1 to Nigeria despite taking an early lead through Ehsan Kari in the 16th minute, while Tanzania suffered a 2-0 home defeat to Guinea-Bissau in Zanzibar. Tanzania's coach Miguel Ángel Gamondi acknowledged his team dominated possession but failed to convert chances into goals, challenging his players to improve their efficiency in the final third.

The stakes differ significantly between the two teams. Tanzania has already qualified for AFCON 2027 as one of three co-hosts alongside Kenya and Uganda, so the qualifiers serve primarily as competitive preparation. For Madagascar, however, the situation is urgent. Only the highest-ranked non-host nation in Group L will earn the group's additional qualifying spot, meaning Nigeria, Guinea-Bissau, and Madagascar must compete for one berth. After Matchday 1, Guinea-Bissau and Nigeria both held three points, widening the gap.

A second consecutive defeat would significantly damage Madagascar's qualification prospects in a six-match campaign. Tanzania's automatic qualification removes the pressure of results, allowing them to experiment and build form. The match kicks off at 4:00pm East Africa Time on Tuesday in Meknès, Morocco.
