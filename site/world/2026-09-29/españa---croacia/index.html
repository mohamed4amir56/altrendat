España recibe a Croacia en Sevilla por la segunda fecha de Nations League

By Karim Fouad · Published 29 Sep 2026, 18:15 UTC

España y Croacia disputan este martes 29 de septiembre en el estadio Ramón Sánchez-Pizjuán de Sevilla la segunda fecha del Grupo A3 de la Nations League.

Spain will host Croatia on Tuesday, September 29 at the Ramón Sánchez-Pizjuán stadium in Seville, where the Spanish national team plays without losses in 25 matches. The stadium has recorded 20 victories and 5 draws as Spain's home venue, with no defeats across more than 10 games played there.

Spain arrives in strong form after a dramatic 3-2 comeback against England in Wembley, extending an unbeaten streak to 39 matches. Goals from Lamine Yamal, Álex Baena, and Mikel Oyarzabal secured the victory despite England's Harry Kane missing a penalty that could have sealed the match. Croatia also began their Nations League campaign successfully, defeating the Czech Republic 2-1 away, with Luka Modric opening the scoring and Mario Pašalić sealing the victory.

The match will kick off at 20:45 Spanish time on Tuesday. In Spain, the game will be broadcast live on TVE La 1 with streaming available through RTVE Play, Movistar+, and fuboTV España. For U.S. viewers, Fox Sports 1, fuboTV, and ViX will carry the match, while Mexican fans can watch on Sky Sports Mexico and Sky+.
