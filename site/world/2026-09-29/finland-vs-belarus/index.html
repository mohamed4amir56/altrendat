Finland hosts Belarus in UEFA Nations League seeking to build on 7-0 opening win

By Karim Fouad · Published 29 Sep 2026, 17:15 UTC

Finland, ranked top of Group 1 after defeating San Marino 7-0 in their opener, faces Belarus at Helsinki's Olympiastadion in UEFA Nations League C, with Belarus looking to recover from a 2-0 loss to Albania.

Quick facts

Competition

Current standings
Finland first place, Belarus third place

Finland arrived in Helsinki with commanding momentum after a 7-0 victory over San Marino in their Nations League opener, a result that settled nerves following heavy defeats to Germany and Hungary in friendly matches leading into the tournament.

For Belarus, this match carries urgency. Viktor Goncharenko's side lost their opening fixture 2-0 to Albania and sits in third place, already under pressure to recover points. Despite encouraging pre-tournament form that included three wins from five matches and a 4-1 victory over Syria, Belarus must respond quickly to stay competitive in Group 1.

The stakes are clear for both coaches. Finland's Jacob Friis will look to build on the San Marino performance with another controlled display at home. Goncharenko needs his squad to show the quality they displayed in the months before the competition began. The gap in current standings gives this fixture genuine edge, with the result potentially defining each team's Nations League campaign trajectory.

Finland sit atop the group after matchday one, while Belarus find themselves under immediate pressure. A win here could shift the group's shape significantly for either side.
