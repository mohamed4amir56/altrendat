Game Informer reveals Grand Theft Auto VI's advanced weather system and Florida setting

By Karim Fouad · Published 29 Sep 2026, 15:15 UTC

Game Informer published a 14-page cover story on Grand Theft Auto VI featuring exclusive interviews with Rockstar Games designers about the game's advanced weather systems, wildlife, and its Florida-inspired setting called Leonida.

Grand Theft Auto VI graces the cover of Game Informer this month in a 14-page feature that explores how Rockstar Games brought the game's Florida parody, Leonida, to life. The cover story includes exclusive interview quotes with several lead designers at Rockstar, alongside 12 new screenshots showing off the game's sights and citizens.

Rockstar's approach to creating Leonida involved researching Miami and Florida to ensure the most culturally and aesthetically accurate world possible. The cover story focuses particularly on two major features: advanced weather systems that simulate Florida's less sunny weather phenomena, and the staggering amount of wildlife—native and otherwise—roaming Leonida on land, air, and sea. According to Game Informer, these weather effects could include tropical storms and hurricanes.

The magazine issue, numbered 382, features the work of a Game Informer editor who is a lifelong native of South Florida and offers homegrown perspective on how accurately the game captures the region. The cover artwork, created by Rockstar Games, features protagonists Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos. Beyond the GTA VI coverage, the issue includes previews from Gamescom, an interview with voice actor Maggie Robertson about Warlock: Dungeons & Dragons, behind-the-scenes details on Persona 4 Revival, and coverage of Tides of Annihilation from a recent trip to China.
