Ghana faces Gambia in Africa Cup of Nations qualifier with fans urged to show belief

By Karim Fouad · Published 29 Sep 2026, 16:04 UTC · Updated 29 Sep 2026, 16:46 UTC

Ghana's Sports Fund Administrator calls on supporters to trust the Black Stars ahead of their Africa Cup of Nations qualification match against Gambia today.

Dr. David Kofi Wuaku, Administrator of the Ghana Sports Fund, has urged Ghanaians to give the Black Stars their full support ahead of today's match against Gambia, saying players perform best when they feel the nation is behind them.

Wuaku acknowledged that Ghanaian football fans are passionate about lineups and tactics in the days before matches, but emphasized a shift in responsibility once play begins. "Every Ghanaian is a coach in the days before a big match," he said. "But once the whistle blows, our job changes. Our job is to believe."

The administrator pointed to how players respond to crowd energy, noting that young players feel every pass and missed chance deeply. "A player who hears his name chanted after a mistake plays the next ball without fear. A player who hears silence or insults hesitates," Wuaku explained, underscoring how national support shapes performance. He emphasized that football careers are shaped by moments of confidence and doubt, with a supportive nation able to tip the balance.

Wuaku also called for maturity in how fans respond after the final whistle, whatever the result. "If we win, let us celebrate with humility. If we do not, let us respond with respect and encouragement," he said. He stressed that lasting success requires a nation standing with its team across every season, not only on match day.
