Yankees activate Giancarlo Stanton for Wild Card Series after months sidelined

By Karim Fouad · Published 29 Sep 2026, 15:00 UTC

The New York Yankees activated Giancarlo Stanton from the injured list for their Wild Card Series against the Boston Red Sox, marking his return after calf injuries kept him sidelined since late April.

Giancarlo Stanton has not played since April 24, when a calf strain began what became a two-injury ordeal. He suffered his initial calf injury in April, then injured his other calf while attempting to return. After weeks of speculation about whether his season had ended, the Yankees activated him Tuesday, according to MLB Network's Jon Morosi.

Stanton's return matters for October baseball. Home runs carry extra weight in postseason play, and the Yankees get one of baseball's best home run hitters back, even if limited. Manager Aaron Boone said Stanton would likely be used primarily as a pinch hitter off the bench rather than in a starting role.

On Monday, the day before the roster decision, Stanton took five at-bats in live batting practice at an empty Yankee Stadium against pitchers Ryan Weathers, Elmer Rodríguez, and prospect Kyle Carr. He walked once, struck out twice, and hit two ground balls. Boone watched the entire session and said beforehand it would be important for determining the final roster call. The slugger showed no visible discomfort during the workout, running curves in the outfield and taking leads off first base, though he was not going full-speed, a purposeful approach to avoid further injury.

Stanton does not typically use rehab assignments after injuries, preferring instead to find his timing in the cage. This approach may help him contribute immediately for the Yankees, as his bat could be ready without a lengthy minor league stint.
