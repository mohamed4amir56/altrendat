Nigeria battles Guinea-Bissau in 2027 Africa Cup of Nations qualifier

By Karim Fouad · Published 29 Sep 2026, 16:04 UTC · Updated 29 Sep 2026, 16:46 UTC

Nigeria played Guinea-Bissau in an Africa Cup of Nations qualification match as the Super Eagles continued their 2027 tournament campaign following their win over Madagascar.

Quick facts

Tournament
2027 Africa Cup of Nations Qualification
Previous result
Nigeria won against Madagascar

Nigeria's Super Eagles faced Guinea-Bissau in a 2027 Africa Cup of Nations qualification match, pushing forward in their campaign for continental tournament qualification. The match came after Nigeria's previous victory over Madagascar, as they worked to maintain momentum in the qualifying rounds.

The encounter held importance for Nigeria's bid to secure a spot in the 2027 Africa Cup of Nations finals. With qualification continuing across multiple matchdays, each result carried weight in the competitive African qualifying stage.

On the pitch, Nigeria controlled much of the play but faced a disciplined Guinea-Bissau side. Early in the match, around the 36-minute mark, Nigeria attempted to penetrate the defense through midfield. Onyeka played a long ball forward seeking Osayi-Samuel, but Guinea-Bissau's Embadje reached the loose ball first, snuffing out the attacking opportunity. Nigeria's rhythm remained somewhat predictable during this period, with their approach lacking the incisiveness needed to unlock Guinea-Bissau's compact defensive setup. The visitors' stubborn resistance prevented the Super Eagles from creating clear-cut scoring chances in the opening stages.
