Jason Kelce slips with 'we' talk during Bears-Eagles broadcast, quickly apologizes

By Karim Fouad · Published 29 Sep 2026, 19:15 UTC

The retired Philadelphia Eagles center apologized live on ESPN's Monday Night Football on September 28 after repeatedly using "we" when discussing his former team during their 27-7 loss to Chicago.

Jason Kelce caught himself breaking broadcasting's cardinal rule: maintaining objectivity. During halftime commentary of the Eagles' matchup against the Bears, the 38-year-old analyst referred to Philadelphia as "we," saying "We played really well offensively. We had opportunities. We shot ourselves in the foot, but we've moved the ball."

Pat McAfee immediately flagged the slip, remarking, "A lot of 'we' talk out of Jason for Philly." Realizing his error, Kelce responded at once: "Sorry, sorry. I forget. Sorry, Bears fans."

The Eagles fell 27-7 in Chicago, dropping to 2-1 on the season. Kelce spent his entire 13-year NFL career with Philadelphia before retiring in 2024, making neutrality challenging even for a seasoned broadcaster. His attachment to the organization is well-documented; in 2023, he described Philadelphia as "a blessing" and said he had fully integrated himself and his family into the city.

Philadelphia will host the Rams on Sunday at 1 p.m. E.T.
