Luzardo starts Game 1 for Phillies against Braves after three weeks off

By Karim Fouad · Published 29 Sep 2026, 17:45 UTC

Jesús Luzardo will open the wild-card series for Philadelphia against Atlanta on Tuesday afternoon, his first appearance since September 7 due to left shoulder inflammation.

Luzardo threw four fastballs in a row to close out the eighth inning Sunday night, needing just one pitch per batter. It was his first game action in three weeks because of inflammation in his left shoulder. The Phillies had no choice: their ace Zack Wheeler was used in Sunday's must-win regular-season finale, and Aaron Nola started on two days' rest Saturday. Catcher J.T. Realmuto said Luzardo's stuff looked good despite the long layoff, though he appeared rusty warming up in the bullpen.

The Phillies face an unfavorable matchup. Atlanta went 9-4 against Philadelphia in the regular season and outscored the Phillies 66-43 in their 13 games. Chris Sale, the Braves' starter, finished second in the National League with a 2.16 ERA and was dominant against Philadelphia, going 3-0 in four starts with a 2.08 ERA. However, Luzardo gives the Phillies hope: he pitched a shutout in his only appearance against Atlanta, allowing just two hits with 12 strikeouts.

Manager Don Mattingly acknowledged the uncertainty. Luzardo is not expected to pitch deep into the game because he has not started in more than three weeks and has pitched only once in that span. "I don't think we really know what to expect," Mattingly said. Yet the risk is worth taking: without a starting pitcher, the Phillies allowed 30 runs in three games using an opener while Luzardo was out. The Phillies clinched the third National League wild-card spot on the final day of the regular season.
