Jimmy Kimmel mocks Trump's White House ban reversal and Xi state visit

By Karim Fouad · Published 29 Sep 2026, 04:45 UTC

Late night host Jimmy Kimmel joked about journalists returning to the Oval Office after Trump's media ban was overturned during Chinese President Xi Jinping's state visit.

The White House lifted its press ban on Thursday, forcing journalists back into the Oval Office whether the president likes it or not. Jimmy Kimmel seized on the moment with a sharp quip: "The only thing more unwelcome in the Oval Office than CNN is a salad."

Kimmel used the timing of Xi Jinping's state visit to highlight the irony of Trump's reversed media restrictions. The host compared the return of journalists to an unstoppable force, likening them to Trump and a beauty pageant dressing room: "They're going in anyway, they will not be stopped."

During the same state visit, Trump gave Xi a tour of the White House, bragging about details like the gold decor and what he called "the most beautiful helipad ever." Kimmel impersonated Trump's tendency to boast about trivial matters regardless of broader concerns: "Guys, I know that gas is $6 a gallon, but look at how beautiful my helipad is."

When Xi announced plans to gift two giant pandas to the US as a goodwill gesture, Kimmel warned this was a "very bad idea," joking that Trump would likely eat them. The host also criticized Trump's guest list for the state dinner, which included Jeff Bezos, Elon Musk, Sam Altman and Tim Cook, calling them "everyone who's ruining everything."
