Jimmy Kimmel jokes Trump bragged about helipad during Xi Jinping White House visit

By Karim Fouad · Published 29 Sep 2026, 04:45 UTC · Updated 29 Sep 2026, 05:30 UTC

On his late-night show Thursday, Kimmel mocked Trump for showing off the White House's gold decor and helipad to Chinese President Xi Jinping during a state visit, comparing it to a child showing off toys to a visiting friend.

Quick facts

Trump showed Xi Jinping around the White House like a nine-year-old displaying his toys to a friend for the first time, Kimmel said on Thursday's Jimmy Kimmel Live!. The host watched as Trump guided the Chinese leader through gold-painted walls, a half-built ballroom, and what Trump called "the most beautiful helipad ever."

Kimmel used the visit to highlight what he saw as Trump's compulsive need to boast about his possessions. "There really is nothing that guy will not brag about," the host said, before impersonating Trump: "Guys, I know gas is $6 a gallon, but look at how beautiful my helipad is."

Kimmel also joked about the White House lifting its media ban during the same visit. "The only thing more unwelcome in the Oval Office than CNN is a salad," he quipped, noting that journalists were being reinstated whether the president liked it or not.

The state dinner that followed included tech and business leaders including Jeff Bezos, Elon Musk, Sam Altman and Tim Cook—guests Kimmel described as "everyone who's ruining everything."
