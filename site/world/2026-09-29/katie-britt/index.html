U.S. Senator Katie Britt to address Mobile County Republicans on October 5

By Karim Fouad · Published 29 Sep 2026, 19:01 UTC

U.S. Senator Katie Britt will join the Mobile County Republican Executive Committee on October 5, 2026, at Regency Church of Christ in Mobile, Alabama, alongside other GOP candidates and nominees.

Quick facts

Date
Monday, October 5, 2026
Meet and Greet Time
5:45–6:15 p.m.
Program Start Time
6:15 p.m.
Location
Regency Church of Christ, 501 S. University Boulevard, Mobile, Alabama

Senator Katie Britt will be the featured guest at a Mobile County Republican event bringing together the party's slate of candidates and officeholders. The gathering includes U.S. House District 1 nominee Jerry Carl, Secretary of State nominee Caroleene Dobson, Agriculture Commissioner nominee Corey Hill, State Rep. Chris Pringle, and Sheriff Paul Burch, according to the Mobile County Republican Executive Committee.

The event creates an opportunity for Republican candidates to unite their supporters and reach voters in the region. The public is invited to attend, and the committee will serve refreshments throughout the gathering.

The schedule includes a meet and greet from 5:45 to 6:15 p.m., followed by the formal meeting and program beginning at 6:15 p.m. Members of the press are welcome to attend and cover the event. The gathering takes place at Regency Church of Christ, located at 501 S. University Boulevard in Mobile, Alabama. This event brings together multiple levels of GOP leadership and candidates on a single evening.
