MLB Wild Card Series kicks off with four games on Tuesday

By Karim Fouad · Published 29 Sep 2026, 15:00 UTC

The MLB playoffs begin Tuesday, September 29, with all four Wild Card Series matchups getting underway, including Phillies vs. Braves at 2:00 PM ET, White Sox vs. Astros at 5:00 PM ET, Red Sox vs. Yankees at 8:00 PM ET, and Cubs vs. Padres at 10:00 PM ET.

Four Wild Card Series games tip off Tuesday as October baseball arrives early in the season. The slate begins with Phillies vs. Braves at 2:00 PM ET and concludes with Cubs vs. Padres at 10:00 PM ET, with White Sox vs. Astros and Red Sox vs. Yankees filling the afternoon and evening windows.

The postseason debut carries high stakes for teams seeking to advance. The White Sox face particular pressure: the vast majority of their hitters are getting their first taste of postseason play after several key contributors slumped down the stretch. Munetaka Murakami and Colson Montgomery posted two of the lowest batting averages in the Majors since August 1, despite their strong early-season performances. For the Astros, Yordan Alvarez enters as the American League MVP frontrunner, hitting .400 with four home runs and a 1.300 OPS across his last 11 games before the playoffs.

The Phillies will turn to Jesus Luzardo as their opener despite his limited recent work—he has thrown just one inning since September 7 due to injury. Luzardo owns a career-best 2.85 ERA and posted a complete-game shutout with nine strikeouts against the Braves in his last full outing. The Braves counter with Chris Sale, who at 36 years old posted a 2.16 ERA this season while averaging 96.2 mph on his fastball, his hardest velocity since his rookie year.

All four Wild Card Series will air on Peacock and NBCSN, with select games on NBC. Game 2 matchups follow on Wednesday, with Game 3s, if necessary, scheduled for Thursday.
