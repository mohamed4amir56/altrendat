Kevin Durant expresses happiness with Rockets, hints at long-term future

By Karim Fouad · Published 29 Sep 2026, 15:00 UTC

Houston Rockets GM Rafael Stone says Kevin Durant told the team at the end of the 2025-26 season that he is happy and wants to remain with the franchise before his 2027-28 player option.

Kevin Durant made clear his contentment with the Houston Rockets at the end of the 2025-26 season, telling the organization "how happy he is here" and that he wants to stay, according to GM Rafael Stone.

The 37-year-old's commitment matters because Durant has a player option for 2027-28, meaning his long-term future has been uncertain. Stone's optimism signals the Rockets may be able to retain their star forward beyond the upcoming season and build a sustained contender in the Western Conference.

Durant's first year in Houston delivered strong individual numbers: he averaged 26.0 points, 5.5 rebounds and 4.8 assists per game while shooting 52 percent from the field and 41.3 percent from three-point range, earning All-NBA Second Team and All-Star honors. The Rockets made the playoffs with him, though they lost to the Los Angeles Lakers in the first round after Durant was limited by injury in the postseason.

Stone emphasized Durant's fit with Houston's culture, saying "His fit's been really good. It feels like he's really happy to be here, he's been a good teammate. He just wants to hoop, which fits great with our culture." With Fred VanVleet returning alongside Alperen Şengün, Amen Thompson and Jabari Smith Jr., the Rockets are positioned to contend again if Durant remains healthy.
