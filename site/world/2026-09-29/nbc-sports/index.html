NBC Sports to provide extensive coverage of Keeneland Fall Meet and Breeders' Cup

By Karim Fouad · Published 29 Sep 2026, 18:39 UTC

NBC Sports will extensively cover Keeneland's Fall Meet starting Oct. 2 and the Breeders' Cup World Championships on Oct. 30-31 in Lexington.

NBC Sports is ramping up its horse racing broadcasts for one of the year's busiest racing weekends, with Keeneland's Fall Meet opening Friday, Oct. 2, and eight "Win and You're In" qualifying races for the Breeders' Cup filling the opening weekend through Oct. 4.

The event matters because Keeneland is hosting the Breeders' Cup World Championships for the fourth time, and organizers expect 42,000 fans daily during the championship event, which features 14 races worth $34 million. The Breeders' Cup Classic alone carries a $7 million purse and will attract top horses including a World Cup winner, Dubai World Cup winner, Kentucky Derby winner, and the Japan Horse of the Year competing against each other.

The Fall Meet runs through Oct. 24 and includes new features such as dining rooms in the new Paddock building and loge boxes available throughout the meet. Organizers have added about 5,000 temporary seats along with additional staff and volunteers to handle crowds. According to Dora Delgado, executive vice president and chief racing officer for the Breeders' Cup, the event is described as the "Olympics of horse racing." The championship event is expected to generate an estimated $75 million to $100 million in economic impact for Lexington.

The Breeders' Cup World Championships will take place Oct. 30-31, with general admission tickets still available but requiring advance purchase along with parking.
