Odell Beckham Jr. plays six snaps, catches zero passes in Giants win over Titans

By Karim Fouad · Published 29 Sep 2026, 17:15 UTC

Odell Beckham Jr. played six snaps with one target and no catches Sunday as Giants fans chanted his name during the team's 12-0 shutout win over the Titans.

MetLife Stadium erupted with "OBJ! OBJ!" chants as Beckham jogged onto the field for his fifth snap of Sunday's fourth quarter, with the crowd drowning out other noise. It was a stark moment: fans calling for involvement from a player who has barely taken the field.

Beckham hasn't caught a pass in three games with the Giants, appearing on just 11 percent of offensive snaps (11, 3, and 6 snaps respectively through week three). Head coach John Harbaugh said Friday he "would be all for" getting Beckham more involved Sunday, calling it "the perfect time." Offensive coordinator Matt Nagy countered that they were "trying to probably slowly package him in some things offensively" rather than expand his role broadly.

On the one target Beckham received Sunday, Titans safety Amani Hooker nearly intercepted the pass in the first quarter. Beckham last caught a pass on December 8, 2024, as a member of the Dolphins and last scored a touchdown in 2023 with the Ravens. He signed a one-year, $1.3 million deal with the Giants in June after they drafted him 12th overall in 2014.
