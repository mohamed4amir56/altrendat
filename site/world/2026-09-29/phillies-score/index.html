MLB announces 2026 postseason field with 12 teams competing

By Karim Fouad · Published 29 Sep 2026, 18:39 UTC

The 2026 Major League Baseball postseason field has been set, with 12 teams now preparing for high-stakes playoff baseball.

The 2026 postseason field is officially locked in, with 12 teams ready to compete for championship glory. Players across these rosters are now in prime position to make their marks on October baseball, with some entering the playoffs in peak form after recovering from earlier-season injuries.

The postseason format rewards durability and sustained excellence through the regular season. Teams have qualified based on their performance, and analysts are now evaluating which players are most likely to deliver big performances when the stakes are highest. Health status plays a crucial role—some elite performers will miss the playoffs entirely due to injury, reshaping how teams approach their matchups.

Notable absences include Yankees slugger Aaron Judge, a back-to-back American League MVP currently on the injured list with a calf strain who may not participate in the Yankees' Wild Card Series clash against the Red Sox. His injury opens opportunities for other players to step into larger roles. Across the 12 qualifying teams, players like Matt Olson, who posted a 40-homer season, and others who have returned to health after mid-season injuries are positioned to influence their teams' postseason runs.

The month-long playoff drama will test which players can sustain their regular-season excellence under October pressure, with both individual legacies and team championships on the line.
