Uzuni doubles Albania's lead over hapless San Marino in Nations League rout

By Karim Fouad · Published 29 Sep 2026, 19:30 UTC

Albania defeated San Marino 2-0 in a UEFA Nations League match, with Anis Mehmeti and Myrto Uzuni scoring, extending Albania's perfect record against San Marino to four consecutive victories.

Quick facts

Final Score
San Marino 0-2 Albania
Goalscorers
Anis Mehmeti (1st half), Myrto Uzuni (2nd half)
Kick-off Time
19:45
Competition
UEFA Nations League C Group 1

Myrto Uzuni's close-range finish from a set piece sealed Albania's dominant performance over San Marino, who remain without a goal across all four recorded meetings between these nations. The second-half strike followed Anis Mehmeti's opener from near the goal line, set up by Rey Manaj from a corner, as Albania controlled a match that reflected the vast gulf in form between the two sides.

The result leaves San Marino rooted at the bottom of UEFA Nations League C Group 1 with little prospect of improvement. San Marino have lost four of their last five matches, conceding thirteen goals while scoring just two across that run, including a 7-0 defeat to Finland. Albania, by contrast, sit in second place and showed their credentials by building on a recent 2-0 win over Belarus, with genuine stake in the promotion race.

Albania's dominance in this fixture is complete: they have won all four recorded meetings, including a 5-0 victory in World Cup qualification in September 2021 and two 3-0 wins in earlier encounters. San Marino created few chances, with Nicola Nanni's attempt from 35 yards missing the target and San Marino goalkeeper Edoardo Colombo forced to save efforts from Rey Manaj and Juljan Shehu to keep the scoreline from worsening.
