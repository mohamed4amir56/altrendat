Spain hosts Croatia in Nations League with 39-game winning streak on the line

By Karim Fouad · Published 29 Sep 2026, 17:45 UTC

World champions Spain face Croatia on Tuesday in Seville in a UEFA Nations League Group A3 match, with Spain seeking to extend their record 39-game European winning streak.

Quick facts

Kick-off time
Tuesday, September 28, at 8:45pm (18:45 GMT)
Stadium
Ramon Sanchez-Pizjuan Stadium, Seville, Spain
Spain's winning streak
39 consecutive games (record for European team)
Lamine Yamal's age
19 years old
Luka Modric's age
41 years old

Spain's 39-game unbeaten run—a record for any European national team—faces its sternest test yet as world champions host Croatia in Seville on Tuesday evening. The streak has transformed La Roja into what observers describe as an imperious side, yet Croatia arrives fresh from a 2-1 victory over Czechia under new manager Slaven Bilić.

For Spanish fans, this match marks a homecoming celebration. Spain won the World Cup in New Jersey, and this is their first competitive home fixture since that triumph. The world champions already defeated England 3-2 in their opening Nations League game after coming from behind, while Croatia's midfield—led by the 41-year-old Luka Modric—will attempt to disrupt Spain's rhythm.

The generational clash features 19-year-old Barcelona winger Lamine Yamal against Modric, who has not slowed despite his age. Yamal scored within two minutes against England and has seven goals in La Liga this season. Modric scored Croatia's opener against Czechia and remains the architect of his team's play. Spain's attack also features Barcelona midfielder Dani Olmo, who provided an assist in their England win.

Spain will be without goalkeeper Joan García due to a knee injury and defender Pedro Porro, who was hurt in Tottenham's match against Aston Villa. De la Fuente must adjust Spain's right defensive flank. Despite Croatia's improvements under Bilić, most analysis suggests Spain's form makes them heavy favorites in what is expected to be a celebratory evening in Seville.
