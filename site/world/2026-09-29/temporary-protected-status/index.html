101,000 Ukrainians face Oct. 19 TPS deadline with no extension decision yet

By Karim Fouad · Published 29 Sep 2026, 19:45 UTC

As Temporary Protected Status expires for Ukrainian refugees in the U.S. on Oct. 19, an automatic six-month extension is in effect, but the Trump administration has not yet decided whether to formally extend the program.

Gaylena Shpilman drove through minefields to escape Ukraine four years ago, but now faces a different crisis: losing her legal right to work. The Sacramento resident and approximately 101,000 other Ukrainians living in the U.S. are waiting to learn if their Temporary Protected Status will continue beyond Oct. 19, when the current designation expires.

The stakes are immediate. While an automatic six-month extension through April 19, 2027 triggered under federal law when the Department of Homeland Security missed an Aug. 20 deadline to decide on the program, USCIS has provided no clear guidance on how this affects employment documents. "In the next few weeks, I'll maybe lose my job. If I lose my job, what am I going to do? I need to pay rent," Shpilman said. California alone is home to approximately 60,000 Ukrainian TPS recipients. Many have applied for alternative protections through the Uniting for Ukraine program, which was suspended last year and whose two-year humanitarian parole is now ending.

A bipartisan group of 31 U.S. senators urged the Trump administration to extend TPS for 18 months, citing both humanitarian and economic reasons. According to the lawmakers, Ukrainian TPS holders contribute approximately $2.6 billion to the U.S. economy annually and pay about $716 million in taxes. The New York Legal Assistance Group reports that Ukrainian clients fear being forced to return to a war-torn country as Russia continues intensive attacks.

Expiring TPS does not immediately force deportations, but creates obstacles as recipients seek alternative legal status. "It does create a problem as they seek other ways to remain here safely," said an immigration advocate. Ukraine was initially designated for TPS on April 19, 2022.
