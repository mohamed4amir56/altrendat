South Africa faces 130-place ranking gap against Eritrea in Afcon qualifier

By Karim Fouad · Published 30 Sep 2026, 15:46 UTC · Updated 30 Sep 2026, 16:33 UTC

Bafana Bafana, coached by Pitso Mosimane, play Eritrea—ranked 130 places below them—in an Africa Cup of Nations qualifier at Cairo's Al-Salam Stadium on Wednesday at 6pm SA time.

Eritrea enters Wednesday's match as a massive underdog, sitting 130 places below South Africa in the world rankings. The Red Sea Camels, as Eritrea are nicknamed, will face Bafana Bafana at Cairo's Al-Salam Stadium under the direction of coach Pitso Mosimane in this Africa Cup of Nations qualification encounter.

The stark ranking disparity suggests that South Africa should have a comfortable evening against their opponents. With such a significant gap between the teams' competitive levels, Bafana Bafana are heavily favored to control possession and create scoring opportunities throughout the match. The massive gulf in rankings indicates that Mosimane's side could be in for an easy night against the lower-ranked Red Sea Camels.

The qualifier takes place at Cairo's Al-Salam Stadium on Wednesday evening, with the match kicking off at 6pm SA time. This Africa Cup of Nations qualification fixture represents a crucial opportunity for South Africa to secure three points against an opponent ranked so far below them in the global standings.
