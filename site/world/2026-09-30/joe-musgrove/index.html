Two years removed from surgery, Musgrove returns to Padres' playoff roster vs. Cubs

By Karim Fouad · Published 30 Sep 2026, 04:45 UTC

Joe Musgrove, the 33-year-old Padres right-hander who underwent Tommy John surgery in October 2024 and missed nearly two years from major-league action, was added to San Diego's Wild Card Series roster as a reliever.

Musgrove last pitched in a major-league game on October 2, 2024, when he exited a Wild Card Series start against the Braves with the elbow injury that led to reconstructive surgery. Nearly two years later, he's back on a postseason roster, this time as a reliever after a recovery marked by multiple setbacks.

The 33-year-old San Diego native has shown encouraging signs in recent appearances. Across three August rehab starts at Triple-A, Musgrove averaged 94.4 mph with his fastball, up from 92.9 mph in 2024. He expressed optimism about his progress, saying "I do feel the most optimistic I've felt at any time in the last two years."

Musgrove's track record with the Padres suggests he remains a capable pitcher despite the extended layoff. In four seasons with San Diego, he posted a 123 ERA+, a 3.68 FIP, and a 4.18 K/BB ratio across 97 starts. He threw the first no-hitter in Padres history in 2021. Over his full MLB career, he has 26 relief appearances, giving him experience in that role.

The Padres added him to their 11-man pitching staff as they prepare to face the Cubs in the Wild Card Series. Game 1 is scheduled for Tuesday at 7 p.m.
