Phillies survive extra innings to force Game 3 as White Sox sweep Astros

By Karim Fouad · Published 01 Oct 2026, 00:34 UTC

The Phillies beat the Braves 4-3 in 10 innings Wednesday to stay alive in their wild-card series, while the White Sox eliminated the Astros with a 7-3 victory to advance to the ALDS.

The Phillies clawed back from the brink of elimination Wednesday night, defeating the Braves 4-3 in 10 innings to force a winner-take-all Game 3 on Thursday. After dropping Game 1 on Tuesday, Philadelphia's season now comes down to a single game in Atlanta.

Meanwhile, the White Sox secured their first playoff series win since 2005, sweeping the Astros 7-3 in Game 2 to earn a berth in the ALDS against the Guardians. The Chicago victory marks a significant milestone after two decades without a postseason series triumph.

The wild-card round continues to tighten as Game 3s loom. The Yankees host the Red Sox on Thursday at 8 p.m. on NBC/fubo, while the Padres face the Cubs at 10 p.m. on Peacock/NBCSN. The Phillies take on the Braves at 2 p.m. on NBC/fubo. The higher-seeded Brewers (103 wins) and Dodgers (100 wins) have already secured berths in the NLDS after earning byes as top seeds, and will host their respective wild-card winners starting Saturday.

The Rays, Guardians, Brewers and Dodgers all earned first-round byes. The Rays, as the AL's top seed, will face either the Yankees or Red Sox in the ALDS, while the Guardians await the White Sox or Astros winner despite posting an 85-77 regular-season record, the 10th-best among 12 playoff teams.
