Phillies force Game 3 after 10-inning thriller; White Sox sweep Astros into ALDS

By Karim Fouad · Published 01 Oct 2026, 00:30 UTC

The Phillies beat the Braves 4-3 in 10 innings Wednesday to stay alive in their Wild Card Series, while the White Sox eliminated the Astros with a 7-3 victory to claim their first playoff series win since 2005.

The Phillies refused elimination Wednesday night, outlasting the Braves 4-3 in 10 innings to force a decisive Game 3 on Thursday. After dropping Game 1 on Tuesday, Philadelphia clawed back in a back-and-forth extra-inning affair to keep their postseason alive.

Meanwhile, the White Sox advanced directly to the ALDS by sweeping the Astros 7-3 in Game 2, marking their first playoff series victory since winning the 2005 World Series. The sweep secured Chicago a matchup with the Guardians, the AL's No. 2 seed who finished with an 85-77 record.

Elsewhere, the Yankees crushed the Red Sox behind strong performances from Cam Schlittler and Ben Rice, while the Padres dominated the Cubs with Michael King pitching a no-hitter through seven innings in a blowout victory. The Rays, Guardians, Brewers and Dodgers all earned byes to the Division Series after finishing as their league's top seeds or division winners with the best records.

The Brewers won 103 games and the Dodgers 100 games and will host NLDS Game 1s on Saturday. The Brewers will face the Padres-Cubs winner, while the two-time reigning champion Dodgers await the winner of the Phillies-Braves series.
