World No. 18 Eala settles for bronze after losing to Wang in Asian Games semifinals

By Karim Fouad · Published 01 Oct 2026, 11:04 UTC

Alex Eala, the top seed and highest-ranked player at the 2026 Asian Games in Nagoya, lost 3-6, 6-3, 6-2 to China's Wang Xiyu in the women's singles semifinals on October 1, replicating her bronze finish from the 2023 edition.

Alex Eala arrived at the 2026 Asian Games as the title favorite and top seed, ranked 18th in the world—the highest-ranked player in the women's singles field. She began her semifinal match against China's Wang Xiyu with a dominant 5-0 lead in the opening set and won it 6-3. But Wang, ranked 85th globally, staged an upset by winning the second set and forcing a decider.

The result stung for Eala because she had chosen to compete in Nagoya rather than defend ranking points at the China Open in Beijing, a mandatory WTA 1000 event. She risked a potential US$10,000 fine from the tour to represent the Philippines at the Asian Games, calling the events of representing her country "very close to my heart."

In the decisive third set, Eala trailed 1-4 before breaking Wang in the sixth game, but could not sustain the momentum. Wang won the final two games to clinch victory in 2 hours and 34 minutes. A forehand error by Eala ended her gold bid. This marks the second consecutive Asian Games where Eala has settled for bronze after losing to a Chinese opponent in the semifinals—she finished with the same medal in 2023 in Hangzhou, when Zheng Qinwen beat her in the semis.
