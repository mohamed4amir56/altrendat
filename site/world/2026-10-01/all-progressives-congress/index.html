APC sweeps four of five legislative by-elections across Nigeria on September 19

By Karim Fouad · Published 01 Oct 2026, 11:04 UTC

The All Progressives Congress won four of five legislative seats in by-elections held on September 19, 2026 across Gombe, Kano, Bauchi and Delta states, prompting questions about the party's prospects in the 2027 general elections.

The sole victory outside the APC came in Bauchi's Sakwa State Constituency, where APM candidate Adamu Yakubu defeated the ruling party with 8,515 votes. The by-elections tested the APC's strength across different regions, from the northern strongholds of Gombe, Kano and Bauchi to the southern Delta state, offering early signals ahead of Nigeria's 2027 general elections. Political observers are examining whether the results reflect broader voter sentiment or regional variations that could shape the next presidential and legislative contests.

The elections involved constituencies in five states spanning Nigeria's north and south, with margins ranging from decisive wins in Gombe and Kano to a tight race in Bauchi. The APC's performance in Delta, a state with significant PDP influence in the past, drew particular attention among political analysts tracking the party's trajectory.
