Ondo APC leaders predict 2027 victory for President Bola Ahmed Tinubu

By Karim Fouad · Published 01 Oct 2026, 11:04 UTC · Updated 01 Oct 2026, 15:01 UTC

APC elders and Senator Adeniyi Adegbonmire have declared their full support for President Tinubu's 2027 reelection bid, citing his courage to implement necessary national reforms.

While acknowledging the current economic hardship facing Nigerian families, the PBAT Mandate Elders Forum maintains that President Bola Tinubu is the only leader with the courage to tackle structural issues that have accumulated over several decades. During a press briefing in Oba-Ile, the forum chairman, Demola Ijabiyi, argued that the President deliberately chose the long-term survival of the national economy over temporary political convenience. According to the elders, external factors, including the ongoing conflict involving the United States and Iran, have further driven up global energy prices, intensifying the burden of the transition period. They insist that the administration’s foundation must be repaired before lasting prosperity can be established.

For party supporters and voters, these endorsements signal a unified push toward the upcoming election cycle. The APC leadership in Ondo State is emphasizing that these reforms are essential for long-term stability, urging Nigerians to look beyond immediate economic pressures to the eventual results of the government's policy direction.

Senator Adeniyi Adegbonmire, representing Ondo Central, echoed this confidence at the Akure Airport on Monday. He dismissed concerns regarding internal party friction following primary elections, noting that such disagreements are natural and have not hindered previous electoral successes. Adegbonmire further characterized the political opposition in the state as effectively non-existent, claiming that rivals are either dead or currently in an Intensive Care Unit status. He specifically warned against the rainbow coalition being championed by FCT Minister Nyesom Wike, labeling any participation in such efforts as anti-party activity against the APC's interests.

The party is now working to resolve outstanding internal grievances ahead of the 2027 polls. Senator Adegbonmire maintains that the APC will remain unified, asserting that the party has no credible opposition to challenge its dominance in the upcoming general election.
