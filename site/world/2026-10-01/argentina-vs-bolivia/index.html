4-0 rout sends Argentina into post-Messi era with emphatic Cordoba victory

By Karim Fouad · Published 01 Oct 2026, 11:04 UTC

Argentina defeated Bolivia 4-0 in a friendly in Cordoba, with goals from Lautaro Martinez, Nico Paz, Cristian Romero, and Jose Lopez, marking the team's first match since Lionel Messi announced his international retirement.

Argentina hammered Bolivia 4-0 at Estadio Mario Alberto Kempes in Cordoba, dominating a friendly that marked the start of the post-Messi era. Lautaro Martinez opened the scoring in the 19th minute with a drilled finish, followed by Nico Paz's dink to make it 2-0. Cristian Romero headed in Argentina's third from Paz's free-kick in the 54th minute, before Jose Lopez added a late fourth from the bench after initially seeing a strike ruled offside for his maiden Argentina goal.

The emphatic victory comes as Argentina moves forward without Lionel Messi, who recently confirmed his international retirement. Coach Lionel Scaloni praised the integration of fresh talent, expressing emotion about giving debuts to young players. Scaloni highlighted the joy of watching debutants sing the national anthem and specifically commended Jose Lopez for his pressing work rate, tactical movement, and professional attitude.

Argentina's dominance was overwhelming. They had 18 attempts to Bolivia's three shots, none of which were on target. Expected goals favored Argentina 2.24 to Bolivia's 0.11, underscoring their control throughout. Nico Paz and Alcaraz led with three shots each, while Martinez recorded seven touches in the opponent's box. Paz also crafted three chances, joint-leading with Franco Mastantuono, another young talent helping fill the void left by Messi's departure.

With Messi no longer part the setup, much of the attacking responsibility now falls on Martinez, Paz, and Julian Alvarez. Argentina will face Benin next week, which will serve as a farewell match for the eight-time Ballon d'Or winner, though tougher tests lie ahead for Scaloni's reconstructed team.
