Pennsylvania Students Brake Runaway School Bus After Driver Suffers Medical Emergency

By Karim Fouad · Published 01 Oct 2026, 11:04 UTC · Updated 01 Oct 2026, 13:32 UTC

Sophomore Alexis Smith and senior Skyla Horton successfully halted their school bus in Western Pennsylvania after their driver became incapacitated behind the wheel.

Quick facts

Location
Hollidaysburg Area School District, Pennsylvania
Key Participants
Alexis Smith, Skyla Horton
Outcome
No passengers injured

Students realized the severity of the situation when the bus failed to stop at its scheduled location and continued moving with the driver slumped over the steering wheel. As the vehicle rolled forward with the driver's foot still depressing the gas pedal, sophomore Alexis Smith and senior Skyla Horton rushed to the front of the cabin to intervene. Smith applied full force to the brakes while Horton engaged the parking brake to secure the vehicle and prevent further movement. Following the immediate stop, the students checked the driver's pulse and waited for emergency assistance. No passengers sustained injuries during the event. The students credit the training they received in their school health science class for providing them with the necessary first aid skills to handle the medical emergency safely. The driver, who has since recovered and returned to his duties, thanked the students for their immediate actions during the encounter. Surveillance footage from the school bus security system captured the incident, showing the rapid coordination between the students to control the bus. Officials from the Hollidaysburg Area School District released the footage, highlighting the quick thinking of those on board during the ride home.
