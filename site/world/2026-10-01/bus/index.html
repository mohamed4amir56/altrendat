Pennsylvania Students Save Moving School Bus After Driver Suffers Sudden Medical Emergency

By Karim Fouad · Published 01 Oct 2026, 11:04 UTC · Updated 01 Oct 2026, 11:41 UTC

High school students in Hollidaysburg stopped a runaway bus after the driver collapsed behind the wheel during a route last month.

Quick facts

Location
Hollidaysburg, Pennsylvania
Involved parties
Alexis Smith, Skyla Horton
School District
Hollidaysburg Area School District

Sophomore Alexis Smith and senior Skyla Horton relied on training from their health science class to manage the emergency as the bus continued to roll after missing a stop. When they noticed the driver limp over the steering wheel with his foot still on the gas pedal, the students initiated a rapid intervention to prevent a collision. Smith applied full force to the service brakes while Horton engaged the parking brake to bring the vehicle to a complete stop, ensuring none of the student passengers were injured during the incident.

The actions of the students ensured the safety of everyone on board and provided the driver with the necessary time to receive medical attention. Their quick response turned a potentially dangerous situation on a busy route into a controlled stop, demonstrating the practical value of their emergency first-aid education. By staying calm under pressure, the students were able to secure the bus immediately upon realizing the driver was unresponsive.

According to CBS News, the school bus security camera captured the entire event, which took place in central Pennsylvania. After being assisted by the students, who also checked his pulse, the driver recovered and has since returned to his job at the Hollidaysburg Area School District. The driver expressed his gratitude to the students for their decisive intervention, noting that the first thing he said upon regaining consciousness was a direct thank you to those who helped him. The video, originally recorded last month, was released recently to highlight the students' efforts during the medical emergency.
