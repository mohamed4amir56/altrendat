Alcaraz advances to Tokyo quarterfinals after tiebreak win over Michelsen

By Karim Fouad · Published 01 Oct 2026, 08:00 UTC · Updated 01 Oct 2026, 11:17 UTC

Carlos Alcaraz defeated American Alex Michelsen 7-6, 4-6, 6-1 in a closely contested match at the ATP 500 Tokyo tournament on October 1, securing his place in the round of 16.

Alcaraz battled for two and a half hours on the hard court of Ariake Coliseum against Michelsen, the world No. 34, in a match that saw the Spanish player overcome early service struggles and multiple break points. The packed stadium, filled with Spanish flags and occasional cheers of "Vamos, Carlitos" from Japanese fans, provided the backdrop for a tightly contested first set that went to a tiebreak, which Alcaraz won 7-1.

The victory ensures Alcaraz continues his push toward the title at Tokyo's ATP 500 event, where several higher seeds have already fallen. Top-10 players Taylor Fritz, Casper Ruud, Tommy Paul, and Brandon Nakashima were eliminated in the first round, clearing the path for the third-ranked Alcaraz.

Alcaraz will face his next opponent in the round of 16 on either Friday, October 2, or Saturday, October 3, when the second set of round-of-16 matches concludes. Other Spanish players Jaume Munar, Jódar, and Davidovich are also competing in the tournament, with matches scheduled in the coming days. In Spain, all ATP 500 Tokyo matches air on Movistar Plus+, with Alcaraz's matches shown on channel 7 or #Vamos.
