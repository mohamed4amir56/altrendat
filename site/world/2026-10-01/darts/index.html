Luke Littler toppled by Luke Woodhouse in shock World Grand Prix opener

By Karim Fouad · Published 01 Oct 2026, 11:04 UTC

World No 1 Luke Littler lost 2-1 to Luke Woodhouse in the first round of the 2026 World Grand Prix on Monday in Leicester, ending his title defence.

The defending champion fell to an upset that few saw coming. Littler, who won last year's tournament with a commanding 6-1 final victory over Luke Humphries, found his reign cut short by world No 18 Woodhouse in a tightly contested match where both players averaged over 82.

Littler's loss matters because the world No 1 had returned to competition after a difficult week in Amsterdam, where he faced booing and whistling during the World Series of Darts and contemplated boycotting Dutch tournaments. The Leicester crowd proved more supportive, but it was not enough to lift him past the opening round.

Woodhouse took control in the final set, moving 2-0 ahead and holding off Littler's late fightback before sealing victory on double 12. The first set had gone to Littler, who raced ahead early despite playing below his standards, hitting a double 15 for a 106 checkout. But Woodhouse's nerve held in the decider. Littler averaged 82.41 to Woodhouse's 82.51 in a match that showcased the double-in, double-out format's demand for precision from the opening throw. Woodhouse progresses to face Nathan Aspinall in the last 16.
