Portugal Snatches 4-2 Victory Over Denmark In Life After Cristiano Ronaldo

By Karim Fouad · Published 01 Oct 2026, 20:31 UTC · Updated 02 Oct 2026, 00:01 UTC

Portugal maintained their perfect Nations League start by defeating Denmark 4-2 in Copenhagen as Vitinha and Joao Felix secured late goals.

Vitinha celebrated his first ever international goal with a header while Joao Felix added a late strike to help Portugal overcome Denmark 4-2, maintaining a perfect record in Nations League Group A4. The victory arrived just days after 41-year-old Cristiano Ronaldo left the national team camp following a tenure on the bench during a previous match against Norway.

This outcome signifies a potential shift in the national team's tactical approach under head coach Jorge Jesus. By securing a win without the record-holding forward, the squad demonstrated a transition toward a team-oriented structure, with Jesus emphasizing that internal rules remain identical for every player regardless of their individual status or history.

Joao Cancelo opened the scoring for Portugal in the 13th minute before Mikkel Damsgaard equalized for the hosts. Gonçalo Ramos regained the lead for the visitors after 25 minutes, though Rasmus Højlund leveled the score at 2-2 early in the second half. The match remained tense until the final stages when Portugal's attacking depth ultimately overwhelmed the Danish defense. Ronaldo, who has recorded 146 goals and 234 caps for his country, departed the setup after reports emerged of a disagreement with his former club manager, Jorge Jesus.

Portugal will look to continue their momentum in the group stage following this performance, which the coaching staff characterized as a performance of high standard. The match also drew attention for the atmosphere surrounding the team, with Ronaldo’s departure serving as a major talking point in international football circles.
