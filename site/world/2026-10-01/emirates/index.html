Emirates Airbus A380 diverts to Frankfurt after mid-air passenger medical emergency

By Karim Fouad · Published 01 Oct 2026, 16:31 UTC

Emirates flight EK31 from Dubai to London Heathrow made an emergency landing at Frankfurt Airport on Thursday after a passenger required medical attention.

Emergency Code

Diversion Airport
Frankfurt Airport

Six hours into a 7-hour and 20-minute flight, the crew of the double-decker Airbus A380-800 transmitted the international transponder code Squawk 7700 while cruising at 40,000 feet. The aircraft initiated a rapid descent over European airspace, shifting its course from London Heathrow to Frankfurt to address the unfolding situation.

The diversion serves as a direct response to a health crisis on board, with Emirates confirming that the cabin crew provided assistance while the plane was in the air. For the hundreds of passengers on the flight, the unscheduled stop meant an abrupt change in travel plans, though the airline has since worked to rebook connecting flights and provide updated itineraries for those affected.

According to Emirates, the aircraft landed safely in Frankfurt, where the passenger disembarked to receive further medical support. While flight tracking data initially identified the alert as a general emergency, the airline subsequently clarified the specific nature of the event. The carrier issued an apology to customers, stating that the health and safety of both passengers and crew remains its highest priority.

Following the landing, the airline coordinated a recovery leg to transport remaining passengers to their original destination. Emirates scheduled the aircraft to depart Frankfurt for London Heathrow at 17:30 local time, with a projected arrival at London Heathrow shortly thereafter.
