Kenya seeks redemption in Guinea after Eritrea stalemate

By Karim Fouad · Published 01 Oct 2026, 16:31 UTC

Kenya's national team faces Guinea on Thursday at 7 pm in Conakry, looking to recover from a 1-1 draw with Eritrea in their Africa Cup of Nations qualifying campaign.

Kenya drew 1-1 with Eritrea in their opening qualifier, a result that frustrated head coach Benni McCarthy despite the performance showing the growing competitiveness of smaller African nations. The draw highlighted that underestimated teams can pose real threats when they are tactically disciplined and dangerous on the counter-attack.

McCarthy's warning to South Africa about respecting Eritrea underscores what Kenya learned firsthand: overlooking opponents in this qualifying round carries real cost. Kenya cannot afford another slow start as they bid to reach the 2027 Africa Cup of Nations. The Guinea match offers an immediate chance to correct course and build momentum in the group stage.

Kenya must now travel to Conakry to face Guinea, a team they hope presents a different challenge than the stubborn Eritrean side. The Harambee Stars need a positive result to stay competitive in their qualifying group and demonstrate they can adapt after the opening stalemate.
