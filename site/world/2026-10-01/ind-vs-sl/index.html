India defend Asian Games title bid in cricket semi-final against Sri Lanka

By Karim Fouad · Published 01 Oct 2026, 04:00 UTC

India face Sri Lanka in the Asian Games 2026 cricket semi-final at Korogi Athletic Park on Thursday, with the toss scheduled for 9:30 a.m. IST.

India enter Thursday's semi-final at Korogi Athletic Park as defending champions, seeking to reclaim gold with an A-team squad led by captain Shreyas Iyer. The side includes Vaibhav Sooryavanshi, Jasprit Bumrah, and other established players in a bid to extend India's dominance in Asian Games cricket.

Both teams reached the semi-final without playing their quarterfinals, which were washed out. Under the tournament format, they were declared winners by virtue of their higher seeding, meaning Thursday marks the first competitive cricket either side will play in the competition.

India are favored to win, with betting odds placing them at 1.10 on Dafa, while Sri Lanka sit as underdogs at 6.60. India's predicted XI includes Sanju Samson as wicketkeeper, Ishan Kishan, Tilak Varma, Shivam Dube, Axar Patel, Washington Sundar, Ravi Bishnoi, and Arshdeep Singh, alongside fast bowler Bumrah. Sri Lanka will counter with Niroshan Dickwella behind the stumps and captain Sahan Arachchige leading their batting lineup.

The toss is set for 9:30 a.m. IST on Thursday, with the winner advancing directly to the final.
