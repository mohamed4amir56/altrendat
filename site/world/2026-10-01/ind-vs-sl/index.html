Ishan Kishan smashes 80 as India demolishes Sri Lanka by 124 runs for gold-medal clash with Pakistan

By Karim Fouad · Published 01 Oct 2026, 04:00 UTC · Updated 01 Oct 2026, 11:16 UTC

India routed Sri Lanka by 124 runs at the Asian Games on October 1, with Ishan Kishan scoring an unbeaten 80, to advance to the T20 cricket final against Pakistan.

Jasprit Bumrah struck in the first five balls, trapping one batter lbw and catching another for nought. Sri Lanka collapsed to three for two, then continued crumbling as two more run-outs followed. The final tally: Sri Lanka bowled out for 45 in 9.5 overs, chasing India's 170.

For India, the demolition sets up a gold-medal decider against Pakistan after the team's depleted squad already made the second-highest successful chase the day before, beating West Indies by eight wickets. The final will test both sides on what Ishan Kishan called a difficult pitch—cracked and spinner-friendly.

Ishan anchored India's innings with an unbeaten 80 from 46 balls, while 15-year-old opener Vaibhav Sooryavanshi impressed on his Games debut with 38 runs. Left-arm spinner Abhishek Sharma finished with 2-2 from five balls. Pakistan advanced to the final with a six-wicket win over Bangladesh in a match reduced to 13 overs a side due to a wet outfield, with Abdul Samad blasting an unbeaten 30 off 15 balls.
