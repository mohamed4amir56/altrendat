Undefeated Home Streak on the Line as JackJumpers Host Melbourne United

By Karim Fouad · Published 01 Oct 2026, 11:56 UTC

Tasmania puts its perfect home record on the line against Melbourne in this Thursday night Round 3 clash.

Quick facts

Matchup
Tasmania JackJumpers vs Melbourne United
Round
3
Day
Thursday

The Tasmania JackJumpers have remained untouchable on their home court throughout the NBL27 season, entering Thursday’s game as the team to beat on Tasmanian soil. Their opponents, Melbourne United, arrive with their own impressive track record, having secured victories in 23 of their last 24 Thursday night appearances across the league. This matchup serves as a significant hurdle for both squads, with Melbourne coach Jacob Chance describing the upcoming physical test as a challenge similar to his team's recent encounters with South East Melbourne.

For spectators and league observers, this game represents a key indicator of current form for two sides with deep history, including their previous NBL24 Championship Series clash. Fans can expect a tactical battle on the floor, as neither side has managed to post a score exceeding 97 points in any of their meetings since March 18, 2024. The outcome remains difficult to predict, as both teams hold a 5-5 split across their last 10 head-to-head encounters.

Attention will focus on the high-stakes point guard duel between Taran Armstrong and Cole Anthony, who both serve as the primary offensive engines for their respective rosters and rank among the league's top assist providers. Melbourne’s big man Josh Oduro heads into his first interstate NBL game looking to build on his 21-point performance from Throwdown 33. The JackJumpers face a lengthy injury list, including Majok Deng, who is sidelined until at least Round 8, and Anthony Drmic, whose status is still to be confirmed. Melbourne remains without Chris Goulding until Round 5, though Shea Ili is expected to be available for the rotation.
