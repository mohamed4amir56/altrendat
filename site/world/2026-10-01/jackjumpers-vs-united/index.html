Taran Armstrong Dagger Clinches 84-82 JackJumpers Thriller Over Melbourne United

By Karim Fouad · Published 01 Oct 2026, 11:56 UTC · Updated 01 Oct 2026, 13:01 UTC

Burnie-born Taran Armstrong drilled a game-winning jump shot with 5.5 seconds remaining to secure a two-point victory for the Tasmania JackJumpers on Thursday night.

Burnie-born point guard Taran Armstrong delivered a clutch pull-up jumper with just 5.5 seconds remaining to break an 82-82 deadlock at Hobart's MyState Bank Arena. After Melbourne United’s Sam Waardenburg hit a three-pointer to level the score with 14.2 seconds on the clock, Tasmania responded with a time-out before Armstrong’s decisive shot. United had a final opportunity to steal the game, but Malith Machar’s long-range attempt rimmed out, leaving the home side victorious. The result marks a significant defensive hold for Tasmania, which maintained its unbeaten record on home soil in NBL27. For Melbourne, the loss serves as a rare setback on a Thursday night, a time slot where the team has historically dominated with 23 wins in its last 24 appearances. The game highlighted the intense rivalry between these two clubs, following their history in the NBL24 Championship Series. The JackJumpers initially built a commanding 19-point lead by halftime, largely due to a dominant rebounding performance that saw them outrebound Melbourne 33-14 in the first half. Will Magnay celebrated his 100th game by contributing 17 points and 12 rebounds to the effort. Despite trailing 52-35 at the break, United surged back behind the efforts of Josh Oduro, who finished with 22 points, and Joe Ingles, who added 18. Melbourne chipped away at the deficit throughout the second half, narrowing the margin to 14 points by the final break before forcing the dramatic fourth-quarter finish. Tasmania coach Scott Roth praised Armstrong’s toughness and grittiness following the win, describing the performance as a reflection of the team's character in a high-stakes, foul-ridden matchup.
