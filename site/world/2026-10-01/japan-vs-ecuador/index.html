Japan and Ecuador Clash at Yokohama Stadium in 2026 Kirin Cup

By Karim Fouad · Published 01 Oct 2026, 09:46 UTC · Updated 01 Oct 2026, 14:30 UTC

Japan faces Ecuador in the 2026 Kirin Cup semifinal on October 1 at Nissan Stadium in Yokohama.

Quick facts

Date
October 1, 2026
Venue
Nissan Stadium, Yokohama
Kickoff Time
7:10 p.m. JST
Japanese Broadcaster
TBS
Ecuadorian Broadcasters
Teleamazonas and TC Televisión

The match kicks off at 7:10 p.m. JST, marking a significant test for both nations following the 2026 FIFA World Cup. While the encounter serves as a competitive semifinal for the Kirin Cup, it also highlights international cultural connections, as Ecuador has unveiled a special-edition yellow jersey for the event. This unique kit features red line-art graphics depicting an Andean condor facing a Japanese red-crowned crane, symbolizing a duel between the cultural icons of both countries. The jersey includes dark navy trims on the collar and sleeve cuffs, maintaining the team's traditional yellow aesthetic while serving as part of a bespoke series for the Asian tour. The kit follows a similar collector's edition white jersey worn against South Korea one week prior. For fans following the action, Japan’s starting lineup includes Suzuki, Itakura, Seko, Ito, Doan, Sano, Tanaka, Nakamura, Kubo, Y. Suzuki, and Tanimura. Ecuador counters with a lineup featuring Galindez, Alcivar, Pacho, Estupinan, Yeboah, Arroyo, Vite, Angulo, Franco, Mercado, and Porozo. The game is broadcast in Japan via TBS and streaming through TVer, with coverage beginning at 6:30 p.m. Ecuadorian viewers can tune into Teleamazonas or TC Televisión for live coverage of the proceedings.
