Ecuador sports special commemorative kit for Kirin Cup showdown against Japan

By Karim Fouad · Published 01 Oct 2026, 09:46 UTC · Updated 01 Oct 2026, 12:31 UTC

Ecuador makes its debut in the Kirin Cup tournament against Japan this Thursday starting at 05:00.

Quick facts

Match
Japan vs Ecuador
Tournament
Kirin Cup
Kickoff
Thursday at 05:00
Location
Yokohama

The Ecuadorian national team will take the field against Japan wearing a unique commemorative jersey featuring the Andean condor alongside the traditional Japanese crane. This collector's edition garment, a fusion of symbols from both cultures, was created exclusively for the team's international tour in Asia and is now available for purchase by fans.

For Ecuador, this match represents a significant test against a national side with a long-standing developmental process. Midfielder Alan Franco emphasized the importance of the fixture, stating that the team is focused on collective effectiveness across defense, midfield, and the forward line to achieve better results than in previous encounters. The squad aims to use this competition to refine their play and build on their tactical cohesion.

According to Diario AS, the match serves as a pivotal friendly in the Kirin Cup, with both teams competing to secure a spot in the tournament final. The players have been working closely with the coaching staff, emphasizing human connection and team chemistry as they prepare for the challenge. While the team moves forward, they have expressed respect for Enner Valencia's decision to step aside from the current squad.

The match serves as the official debut for La Tri in the Kirin Cup. Following this encounter, the team will continue to integrate new dynamics as they finalize their preparations for upcoming international commitments.
