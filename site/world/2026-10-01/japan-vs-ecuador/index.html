Ecuador debuts special Condor-Crane kit in Japan friendly at Yokohama

By Karim Fouad · Published 01 Oct 2026, 09:46 UTC · Updated 01 Oct 2026, 11:16 UTC

Ecuador faces Japan on October 1, 2026, at Yokohama International Stadium wearing a limited-edition yellow jersey featuring an Andean condor and Japanese red-crowned crane in cultural symbolism.

Quick facts

Date
October 1, 2026

Ecuador unveiled a special-edition yellow jersey for the Japan friendly, with intricate red line-art depicting an Andean condor facing a Japanese red-crowned crane across the lower front. The artwork symbolizes a duel between the cultural icons of both nations, following Ecuador's white collector's kit worn against South Korea a week earlier.

Mediacampista Alan Franco emphasized the importance of the match for Ecuador's squad. "It's a very good selection, and for us it's positive to face a team with a longer process," Franco said. "It's going to be a match for us to think about big things. We're going to take the match with the importance it deserves every time we wear the country's shirt."

Franco highlighted Ecuador's collective strength across all lines. "I can say with great calm that we are a selection that has good characteristics, good players in defense, midfield, and forward. Our focus will go much further so that both defensively and offensively we can complement each other to do better than in the past."

The Copa Kirin debut marks Ecuador's second challenge on their Asian tour. The special jersey, produced in limited quantities by Marathon Sports, features dark navy trims on the v-neck collar and sleeve cuffs alongside patterned side panels. Franco also addressed the team's connection with new coach Marcelo Gallardo, noting the group's priority is making him and his technical staff feel comfortable and part of the squad.
