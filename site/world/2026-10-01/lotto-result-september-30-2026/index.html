Grand Lotto Jackpot Hits P236 Million As September 30 Draws Yield Nothing

By Karim Fouad · Published 01 Oct 2026, 11:04 UTC · Updated 01 Oct 2026, 15:04 UTC

No bettors matched the winning combinations for the Grand Lotto 6/55 and Megalotto 6/45 draws held on September 30, 2026.

The odds of hitting the Grand Lotto 6/55 jackpot remain steep at 1 in 28,989,675. Bettors failed to secure the top prize after no one selected the six winning numbers of 44-28-48-38-23-42 during the September 30 session. The unclaimed jackpot for this specific draw has climbed to P236,623,866.82, according to the Philippine Charity Sweepstakes Office (PCSO). These results mean the prize money remains available for future participants looking to match all numbers in the specified 1 to 55 range. As the jackpot continues to grow, it draws significant attention from those hoping to beat the mathematical probability. Beyond the Grand Lotto, the Megalotto 6/45 draw also saw no winners on the same day. Participants missed out on the P83,372,178.85 jackpot after failing to choose the required combination of 41-35-29-23-20-06. The PCSO confirms that both major lottery prizes remain unclaimed, keeping the pools active for subsequent draw dates. Players can continue to monitor official channels for upcoming draw schedules and updated prize amounts as they evolve following these recent results.
