Grand Lotto jackpot surges to P236M as no winners hit September 30 draw

By Karim Fouad · Published 01 Oct 2026, 11:04 UTC

No winners claimed either major Philippine lotto jackpot on September 30, 2026, pushing the Grand Lotto 6/55 prize to over P236 million.

The Grand Lotto 6/55 jackpot swelled to P236,623,866.82 after no one matched the winning numbers 44-28-48-38-23-42 on September 30, according to the Philippine Charity Sweepstakes Office (PCSO). The odds of winning this prize are 1 in 28,989,675, requiring bettors to match all six numbers chosen from a field of 1 to 55.

No winners emerged from the other major draw either. Megalotto 6/45 also went unclaimed, as no one picked the combination 41-35-29-23-20-06 that would have yielded a jackpot of P83,372,178.85. The combined failure to produce winners across both draws means larger prizes await the next round of players.

For players, the mounting jackpots represent growing odds of a life-changing payout in upcoming draws, though the mathematical likelihood of winning remains extremely low.
