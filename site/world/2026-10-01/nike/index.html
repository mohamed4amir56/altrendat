Caitlin Clark signature shoe Caitlin 1s launch as Nike shares plunge 44%

By Karim Fouad · Published 01 Oct 2026, 14:30 UTC · Updated 01 Oct 2026, 15:03 UTC

Nike releases the new Caitlin 1 signature shoe on October 1 as the company faces declining revenue and a difficult financial year.

Quick facts

Release date
October 1
Stock performance
Down 44% this year
Key product
Caitlin 1s
Location
Clinton Street, Iowa City

Investors are watching closely as the Caitlin 1, featuring the star's personal CC branding, arrives in stores while the company grapples with a 44% decline in share price this year. The launch serves as a high-profile attempt to regain momentum after the sportswear giant fell out of the S&P 100 index.

For Nike, the success of this signature release is critical as CEO Elliot Hill enters his third year at the helm without the turnaround performance stakeholders originally expected. Both earnings and revenue are forecast to drop, intensifying the pressure on the brand to find new growth drivers through its partnerships in leagues like the WNBA and the NFL.

According to Yahoo Finance, the company has struggled to move the needle despite its ongoing supply of professional football jerseys and other athletic gear. While the previous quarter benefited from World Cup-related spending, current market conditions have been more challenging for the apparel manufacturer.

In Iowa City, the rollout is being marked by a major public display on Clinton Street, where local crews constructed a site in front of the Old Capitol. This event coincides with a busy week for the area, as the city also prepares to host ESPN’s College GameDay this Saturday morning ahead of the matchup between the Hawkeyes and Ohio State.
