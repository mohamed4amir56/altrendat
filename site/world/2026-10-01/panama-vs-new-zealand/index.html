New Zealand women's team heads to Panama for two-match series in October

By Karim Fouad · Published 01 Oct 2026, 06:45 UTC · Updated 01 Oct 2026, 11:17 UTC

The New Zealand Football Ferns will play Panama on October 11 and 14 at Estadio Rommel Fernández Gutiérrez in Panama City, with both matches broadcast live on TVNZ, TVNZ+, and FIFA+ via DAZN.

New Zealand's Football Ferns will face Panama twice in mid-October as they build toward the FIFA Women's World Cup 2027. The two-match series marks part of an extended international window where the team will play four games instead of the usual two before the year ends.

The matches form a crucial preparation period ahead of the World Cup. NZ Football confirmed that the Ferns have two additional international fixtures scheduled for later in 2026 against opponents still to be announced, though details have not yet been released.

Both Panama games will be broadcast live and free to New Zealand audiences through TVNZ free-to-air television, plus streaming on TVNZ+ and FIFA+ via the DAZN platform. The first match kicks off on October 11 at 10am New Zealand time, with the second on October 14 at 1:30pm New Zealand time, both at Estadio Rommel Fernández Gutiérrez in Panama City. The live coverage ensures fans can follow the team's progress during this critical build-up phase to next year's World Cup.
