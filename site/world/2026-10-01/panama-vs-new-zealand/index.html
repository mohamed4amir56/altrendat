Panama and New Zealand clash in Kirin Cup semi-final at Yokohama

By Karim Fouad · Published 01 Oct 2026, 06:45 UTC · Updated 01 Oct 2026, 15:03 UTC

The New Zealand All Whites face Panama in a Kirin Cup semi-final match scheduled for October 1, 2026, at the Yokohama International Stadium.

Quick facts

Match Date
1 October 2026
Venue
Yokohama International Stadium, Tokyo, Japan

7:10pm
Broadcast
TVNZ, TVNZ+, FIFA+ via DAZN

The Yokohama International Stadium plays host to a rare intersection of CONCACAF and Oceania football as Panama and New Zealand meet on the pitch for a high-stakes Kirin Cup semi-final. With a spot in the tournament final on the line, the match pits Panama’s high-tempo, vertical style against the disciplined, defensive structure of the New Zealand side. Fans looking to follow the action can catch the broadcast on TVNZ, TVNZ+, or through the FIFA+ platform via DAZN. This fixture is part of a broader international schedule that sees the All Whites also competing in Japan later in the month. Panama enters the encounter as a technically polished unit known for applying consistent pressure in the opponent’s half, while New Zealand relies on compact mid-blocks, aerial dominance, and dangerous counterattacking play to disrupt their opposition. This meeting offers a distinct stylistic contrast, as the physical, wide-play tactics of the Central American team challenge the resilient organizational approach of the Oceanian representatives. The match represents one of nine international fixtures for the All Whites and Ford Football Ferns set for broadcast before the end of 2026. Following this tournament, the national teams will continue their respective campaigns, with the Ford Football Ferns also set for a two-match series in Panama City in mid-October. Supporters are advised to monitor the official match timings, as the All Whites move from this Japanese tournament toward their November home series against India in Auckland and Christchurch.
