Patrick Garcia breaks 18-year silence on split with Jennylyn Mercado in viral interview

By Karim Fouad · Published 01 Oct 2026, 11:04 UTC

Patrick Garcia spoke publicly about his past relationship with actress Jennylyn Mercado in a recent viral interview with Ogie Diaz, revealing details he said had been private for 18 years.

Quick facts

Years since split
18 years

Patrick Garcia opened up in a viral interview with Ogie Diaz about his relationship history with actress Jennylyn Mercado, addressing the past directly for the first time in 18 years. The actor clarified in the conversation that he did not leave Mercado while she was pregnant, according to reports of the interview.

Mercado and Garcia share an 18-year-old son, Alex Jazz. Garcia is now married to Nikka Martinez, with whom he has a 13-year-old daughter, Michelle Garcia. Mercado is currently in a relationship with actor Dennis Trillo and they have a child together named Dylan, as well as a stepson named Calix.

Mercado's manager, Becky Aguila of Aguila Entertainment, noted Garcia's comments in response. Through her talent management company, Mercado chose not to share her perspective publicly, citing respect for her family's privacy and peace of mind. Mercado requested that the public stop directing negativity toward Garcia's family, particularly his daughter Michelle.

Dennis Trillo responded on Facebook, stating he does not support bashing but expressed appreciation that the discussion reminded people of Alex Jazz, and called for spreading love rather than hate.
