Patrick Garcia's 18-year-old split claims reignite clash with Jennylyn Mercado's camp

By Karim Fouad · Published 01 Oct 2026, 11:04 UTC · Updated 01 Oct 2026, 15:02 UTC

Actor Patrick Garcia's recent interview about his past relationship with Jennylyn Mercado sparked pushback from her manager Becky Aguila, who denied his account of their breakup.

Patrick Garcia's sit-down interview with Ogie Diaz has revived an 18-year-old controversy between the actor and his former partner Jennylyn Mercado. During the viral conversation, Garcia claimed he did not leave Mercado while she was pregnant, according to the sources.

Mercado's manager Becky Aguila responded forcefully to Garcia's statements. "You will never earn my respect," Aguila fired back at the actor, rejecting his version of events. The dispute centers on the circumstances of their breakup and Garcia's role in it.

Mercado and Garcia share an 18-year-old son, Alex Jazz. Rather than engage directly, Mercado chose silence on the matter. Through her talent management company Aguila Entertainment, she asked the public to stop attacking Garcia's family, particularly his 13-year-old daughter Michelle with wife Nikka Martinez. Mercado's camp emphasized she is living a "good and happy life" with her current husband Dennis Trillo and their children.

Trillo posted on Facebook in response to the controversy: "Hindi ako pabor sa bashing, pero salamat at naalala mo si Jazz dahil sa pag bash sa anak mo. Spread LOVE not HATE." Mercado's representatives stated she chose not to share her side of the story to protect the privacy and peace of mind of both families.
