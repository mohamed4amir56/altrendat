Tom Kim storms to T-7 start chasing Asian Games military exemption

By Karim Fouad · Published 01 Oct 2026, 14:01 UTC

PGA Tour star Tom Kim sits two shots off the lead at the Asian Games in Japan, aiming for a gold medal to secure a mandatory military service exemption.

Quick facts

Asian Games
Location
Kasugai Country Club East Course, Japan

Military Service Duration
18 to 21 months

After arriving in Japan on Monday following his performance at the Presidents Cup in Medinah, 24-year-old Tom Kim carded a 3-under 67 to open his campaign at the Kasugai Country Club East Course. Kim is currently tied for seventh place, trailing leaders Wei-hsuan Wang and Kazuki Higa by only two strokes after the first round of play near Nagoya.

Securing a gold medal in either the individual or team competition at these games represents a critical milestone for Kim. Under South Korean law, all able-bodied men must complete between 18 and 21 months of mandatory military service before reaching age 28. A gold medal win would provide the necessary exemption to bypass active-duty service, allowing the professional golfer to avoid a significant disruption during the prime years of his career.

Professional golfers have previously seen their momentum halted by these requirements, most notably Bae Sang-moon. In 2015, Bae was a rising star in the top 100 of the Official World Golf Rankings with two PGA Tour victories to his name. Following his mandatory enlistment, he returned to the tour in 2017 with his world ranking plummeting to 1,869th, as he noted that he had lost the feel for competitive golf during his service.

Kim is looking to replicate the success of compatriots Sungjae Im and Si Woo Kim, who earned their own exemptions by winning the 2023 Asian Games by a 25-stroke margin. As the competition progresses, Kim remains in a strong position to challenge for the top of the leaderboard and secure his future on the PGA Tour.
