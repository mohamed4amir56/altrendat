Pro Golfer Phil Mickelson Checks Into Treatment Center for Addiction and Trauma

By Karim Fouad · Published 01 Oct 2026, 14:30 UTC

Professional golfer Phil Mickelson voluntarily sought treatment for family trauma and addiction following recent misconduct allegations and personal health issues.

The 56-year-old golf champion recently entered and completed a stint at an addiction treatment center to address complex personal challenges. A representative for Mickelson confirmed the decision to seek professional help for both addiction and family trauma, noting that the golfer has already been discharged from the facility. This clinical intervention follows a difficult year that saw his private life thrust into the public eye through a series of professional absences and external claims regarding his conduct. In February, Mickelson announced his departure from competition, later clarifying in April that he would miss the Masters tournament to prioritize a family health matter. His representatives maintained in June that his primary focus remains on his role as a husband and father. The decision to enter treatment comes after multiple allegations surfaced against the athlete, including claims by a female golf course employee who reported nonconsensual physical contact. Additionally, Ashley Perez alleged that Mickelson made inappropriate sexual gestures toward her in 2015. Mickelson has publicly labeled these reports as a misleading narrative and described the allegations as a drive-by shooting, while his wife, Amy, has expressed her support throughout the period. Mickelson has previously spoken openly about his history with gambling, admitting in 2023 that his habits had shifted from moderate to addictive, which significantly impacted his personal life. While he has acknowledged some past mistakes, his spokespeople have explicitly denied the more recent accusations of misconduct, stating that many claims are false or distorted. The golfer continues to navigate these personal health and legal matters after stepping away from his professional career earlier this year.
