Police ban six activists from Perth defence expo security zone ahead of event

By Karim Fouad · Published 01 Oct 2026, 11:04 UTC

Western Australian police have banned at least six people from protesting inside the security zone for the Land Forces defence expo opening next Tuesday, using newly granted temporary powers that allow stop-and-search and facial recognition technology.

At least six people have received police letters banning them from the security zone around Perth's Land Forces expo, which opens next Tuesday at the Perth Convention and Exhibition Centre with an expected 20,000 delegates. Climate activist Will Shanahan, 18, posted their police ban to social media, becoming the first to publicly reveal the restrictions. Those who violate the ban face arrest, three years in prison, and a $36,000 fine.

The bans follow violent clashes at Melbourne's 2024 Land Forces expo, where 24 police officers were injured by protesters throwing rocks, cans, and acid. The WA government budgeted $11.5 million for security and passed legislation last month giving police temporary powers until October 10 to manage the event. Within the security zone covering the convention centre and Elizabeth Quay bus port, police can stop and search anyone, remove face coverings, and confiscate banned items including speakers, microphones, and bottles.

Police have refused to reveal why specific people were targeted or how many total are on the exclusion list. WA Police Commander Darren Sivright described the security setup as a "fort." Facial recognition technology will track if banned individuals enter the zone. Those banned can still protest outside the security perimeter.

Activists and the Greens have criticized the police for a "lack of transparency." Several groups, including the WA Anti-War Collective, have announced plans to protest against the event. Land Forces is Australia's biggest defence expo, with major international weapons manufacturers attending and about 10,000 overseas and interstate visitors expected between October 6 and 8.
