Singapore must beat Malaysia to reach ASEAN Cup final or bronze medal round

By Karim Fouad · Published 01 Oct 2026, 13:31 UTC

Singapore face Malaysia on October 1 in a decisive FIFA ASEAN Cup Group A match where a win guarantees progression to either the Final or Bronze Final, with the Lions currently third on three points, one behind Malaysia's four.

Midfielder Harhys Stewart has emerged as Singapore's unlikely scoring weapon this season, netting three goals in 2026 including a first-half header in the Lions' 1-0 victory over Bangladesh on September 28. The 25-year-old has scored more than the team's two recognised strikers, Ilhan Fandi and Shawal Anuar, who have both gone scoreless so far in the tournament.

The match determines Singapore's path forward. A victory guarantees a place in either the Final or Bronze Final, while Malaysia enters as group leaders with four points from two matches—a 3-0 win over Bangladesh and a goalless draw against hosts Indonesia. Singapore sits third on three points, one adrift of Malaysia.

Head Coach Gavin Lee stressed that Singapore's game plan will not change, saying the team will "focus on ourselves, identify Malaysia's potential threats, try to neutralise them and put ourselves in a position to be successful." Lee acknowledged Malaysia's strengthened squad, noting the additions of forward Arif Aiman, Bergson in attack, and defenders Brad Tapp and Dion Cools, who plays at a high level in Japan's J1 League.

This rematch carries added significance because Lee, now 36, previously faced Malaysia as interim coach in his first match in charge last September, resulting in a 2-1 loss—though FIFA later overturned the result to a 3-0 Singapore win after Malaysia fielded ineligible players. Lee emphasised that the two Malaysia teams are entirely different, with the current side featuring newly naturalised players.
