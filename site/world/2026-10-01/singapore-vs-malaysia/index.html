Malaysia Routs Singapore 6-0 to End Lions' FIFA ASEAN Cup Campaign

By Karim Fouad · Published 01 Oct 2026, 13:31 UTC · Updated 01 Oct 2026, 17:34 UTC

Singapore suffered a historic 6-0 defeat against Malaysia at the Si Jalak Harupat Stadium on October 1, eliminating the Lions from the FIFA ASEAN Cup.

Quick facts

Singapore experienced a crushing end to their inaugural FIFA ASEAN Cup campaign on October 1, falling 6-0 to their Causeway rivals, Malaysia, in Bandung. The result marked Singapore’s heaviest defeat at a senior level since 1978, when they lost to Malaysia by the same margin. Brazil-born striker Bergson led the offensive for Malaysia with a hat-trick, supported by additional goals from Stuart Wilkin, Paulo Josue, and Syahir Bashah.

This lopsided scoreline serves as a significant reality check for coach Gavin Lee’s side, particularly with the Asian Cup approaching in three months. While Singapore entered the match with three points after an earlier 1-0 win over Bangladesh, they were unable to contain Malaysia’s attack. Coach Lee acknowledged the disappointment after the game, stating that his team failed to do enough to qualify and must now reflect on the performance levels required for future competitions.

Following the victory, Malaysia finished second in Group A behind Indonesia. Despite the emphatic win, Malaysia fell short of securing the top spot due to a lower goal tally compared to the hosts. Indonesia secured their place in the final with a 9-2 victory over Bangladesh. Malaysia is now expected to face Vietnam in the Division 1 third-place play-off on October 5, while the group winners, Indonesia, prepare to challenge Thailand for the tournament title.

Singapore concludes the group stage in third place with three points. The squad now turns its attention to long-term preparation, as the team looks to move forward after this defeat.
