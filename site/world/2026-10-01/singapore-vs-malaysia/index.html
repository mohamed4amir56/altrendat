Bergson's hat-trick sinks Singapore 6-0 in Malaysia showdown

By Karim Fouad · Published 01 Oct 2026, 13:31 UTC · Updated 01 Oct 2026, 15:02 UTC

Malaysia thrashed Singapore 6-0 on October 1 in a FIFA ASEAN Cup Group A match, with Brazil-born striker Bergson scoring three goals in the Lions' heaviest defeat under coach Gavin Lee.

Bergson's three goals and strikes from Stuart Wilkin, Paulo Josue, and Syahir Bashah dismantled Singapore at Si Jalak Harupat Stadium in Bandung. The 6-0 loss marked Singapore's worst defeat to Malaysia at senior level since 1978, ending their inaugural FIFA ASEAN Cup campaign after they had secured their first tournament victory against Bangladesh 1-0 in their previous match.

The rout eliminates Singapore from contention for the final or bronze medal, a dramatic reversal from their pre-match position where a victory would have guaranteed progression. Head coach Gavin Lee had prepared his team to "focus on ourselves" and neutralize Malaysia's threats, but the Causeway rivals' attacking firepower, bolstered by new signings including Bergson upfront and Brad Tapp in defense, proved overwhelming.

Malaysia's dominant display leaves them level on seven points with group leaders Indonesia, though with an inferior goal difference. They will likely face Vietnam in the third-place play-off on October 5. Singapore finished third in Group A with three points, while hosts Indonesia topped the group after thrashing Bangladesh 9-2 simultaneously in Jakarta. Indonesia will likely meet Group B winners Thailand in the final.
