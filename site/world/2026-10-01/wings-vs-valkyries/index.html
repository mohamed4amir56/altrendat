Arike Ogunbowale Drops Record 45 Points As Dallas Wings Force Game 3

By Karim Fouad · Published 01 Oct 2026, 11:04 UTC · Updated 01 Oct 2026, 15:04 UTC

The Dallas Wings defeated the Golden State Valkyries 108-100 in overtime on Wednesday, extending their playoff series to a deciding third game.

Arike Ogunbowale delivered an unprecedented 45-point performance to lead the Dallas Wings to a 108-100 overtime win against the Golden State Valkyries. The four-time All-Star erased a three-point deficit in the final seconds of regulation by hitting an off-balance three-pointer to force the extra period, eventually securing the highest point total in a single WNBA postseason game. She surpassed the previous record of 42 points held by Napheesa Collier, Breanna Stewart, and Angel McCoughtry.

This victory was crucial for the Wings, as it kept their playoff aspirations alive after a 24-point blowout loss in the series opener. By forcing a deciding game, Dallas maintains its pursuit of a second semifinal appearance since 2016. For the second-year Golden State Valkyries, the result prevents an early elimination as the franchise seeks its first-ever playoff series win.

Ogunbowale’s efficiency defined the game, as she converted 12 of 23 shots, including five of eight from beyond the arc, and 16 of 18 from the free-throw line. According to CBS Sports, she also contributed seven rebounds, four assists, and three steals. The game saw a significant turnaround from Game 1, with Dallas maintaining intensity throughout, including a strong second-quarter effort that gave them a halftime lead. The Valkyries had previously won the opening matchup 104-80 in San Francisco.

The series now heads to San Francisco for a winner-take-all Game 3 on Friday night. The victor will advance, while the loser’s season will come to an end.
