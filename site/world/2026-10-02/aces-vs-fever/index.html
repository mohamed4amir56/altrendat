Fever Force Decider As Caitlin Clark Masterpiece Stuns Defending Champion Aces

By Karim Fouad · Published 02 Oct 2026, 04:00 UTC

The Indiana Fever defeated the Las Vegas Aces 99-89 in Game 2, forcing a winner-take-all Game 3 on Thursday.

Caitlin Clark made history in Indiana’s 99-89 victory over the Las Vegas Aces, becoming the first player in WNBA playoff history to record a points-assists double-double in a single half. Her 27-point, 15-assist performance propelled the Fever to a series-tying win, ensuring the sixth-seeded team survives to see another day despite facing elimination. The victory was bolstered by Kelsey Mitchell’s 26 points and Aliyah Boston’s 22, as the trio combined for 75 total points to outplay the defending champions.

This series shift is vital for Indiana, which entered the postseason as underdogs against a Las Vegas squad led by MVP favorite A’ja Wilson. The Aces, who boasted the league’s second-highest scoring offense, now face the pressure of a home-court decider after Indiana demonstrated newfound toughness and offensive balance in Game 2. For the Fever, the performance was a necessary response after being thoroughly outplayed in a 102-85 defeat during the series opener.

The game also saw a tense moment in the fourth quarter when A’ja Wilson confronted a fan in the stands after a foul on Sophie Cunningham. Security and teammates intervened to separate the two, an incident Wilson later described as unacceptable after the match. While Clark eventually fouled out with 2:45 remaining, her teammates maintained their composure to consolidate the lead and force a return to Sin City.

The two teams are now scheduled to meet for the decisive Game 3 this Thursday in Las Vegas. The winner of this matchup will advance to the WNBA semifinals, ending the road for the losing side.
