Amazon Unleashes Kindle Colorsoft With Seamless Edge-to-Edge Color Across New Lineup

By Karim Fouad · Published 02 Oct 2026, 07:03 UTC

Amazon introduced the new Kindle Colorsoft and redesigned e-reader lineup on October 1, with shipping scheduled for late October.

The Kindle Colorsoft arrives as the centerpiece of a complete hardware overhaul that marks Amazon's 20th anniversary in the e-reader market. Beyond the introduction of color, the new models feature a redesigned frame that measures 6.8 mm in thickness, significantly lighter and thinner than their predecessors to improve handling during long reading sessions. Amazon has also integrated a new display stack across the entire family, ensuring consistent performance and visual quality for every device in the updated range.

For readers, this transition toward a slimmer, lighter, and more durable aluminum construction represents a significant shift from the traditional plastic builds of the past. The inclusion of color, combined with the retained waterproof design and adjustable warm reading lights, positions the Colorsoft as a premium option for those seeking both functionality and advanced display technology. These updates aim to address long-standing user demands for faster, more portable devices capable of handling complex visual content.

According to Amazon, the entire new Kindle family includes the basic Kindle, the Kindle Paperwhite, and the Kindle Colorsoft, along with specialized versions for younger readers. Wired reports that the base models utilize plastic, while premium Signature Editions incorporate recycled aluminum. The new lineup also debuts the Kindle Click, a dedicated page-turning remote designed to work with the updated e-readers. While some base models are available for immediate purchase, Mashable notes that the Kindle Colorsoft and other advanced variants are currently available for preorder.

The official launch for the Kindle Colorsoft and the Kindle Paperwhite is set for the final week of October. Users interested in the kid-friendly versions can also access additional benefits including a one-year subscription to Kids+ and a comprehensive damage guarantee.
