Amazon unveils first Kindle Colorsoft featuring seamless edge-to-edge color display technology

By Karim Fouad · Published 02 Oct 2026, 07:00 UTC

Amazon introduced the new Kindle Colorsoft as part of a redesigned lineup, with pre-orders available ahead of a late October launch.

The Kindle Colorsoft arrives as a major upgrade to the e-reader family, offering users seamless edge-to-edge color for the first time. The device features a redesigned display stack housed within a chassis that is notably thinner and lighter than its predecessors, measuring 6.8 millimeters in thickness and weighing 204 grams.

This release marks a significant transition for Amazon, which is refreshing its entire e-reader range to commemorate its 20th anniversary. Beyond the color screen capabilities, the new models incorporate adjustable warm reading lights and retain the waterproof design expected by loyal Kindle users. For those seeking a more premium feel, the Kindle Colorsoft is available in a Signature Edition that utilizes a construction featuring recycled aluminum, providing a more robust build compared to the standard plastic versions.

According to reports from Mashable and WIRED, consumers can secure their devices now through pre-order programs, although the hardware is scheduled for shipment at the end of October. The lineup also includes specialized Kids versions of the Colorsoft, which come bundled with artist-designed cases, a one-year worry-free damage guarantee, and a subscription to Kids+ for unlimited ad-free reading material.

While the basic Kindle and its aluminum variants are available for purchase starting today, the Kindle Colorsoft and the Kindle Paperwhite models are rolling out as pre-orders. These updates aim to address demand for faster performance, with Amazon noting that the new software interface allows for books to open 30 percent quicker than the previous generation of readers.
