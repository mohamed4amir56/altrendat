Braves Face Dodgers In Best-Of-Five NL Division Series Starting This Saturday

By Karim Fouad · Published 02 Oct 2026, 11:30 UTC

The Atlanta Braves advance to the NLDS to play the Los Angeles Dodgers in a best-of-five series beginning Saturday at 4:00 p.m. ET.

TV Channel

The Atlanta Braves officially punched their ticket to the National League Division Series after a decisive 6-2 victory against the Philadelphia Phillies in the final game of their Wild Card series. Following this win, the team now turns its attention to a familiar postseason rival in the Los Angeles Dodgers, the reigning back-to-back world champions who are aiming for their third consecutive title. This high-stakes matchup marks a return to the postseason stage for Atlanta after a five-year hiatus. While the Dodgers rested by avoiding the Wild Card Series, the Braves enter the Division Series with momentum and a historical edge, having won five of the six regular-season meetings between the two teams this year while holding the Dodgers to a .580 OPS. Veterans like Kiké Hernández have noted the Dodgers' confidence, but both teams must now shift focus to the opening pitches at Dodger Stadium. For the Braves, the challenge is to maintain their regular-season dominance over Los Angeles across a tight best-of-five format. All games for the series will be televised on FS1 and available for streaming on FOX ONE. Should the competition remain deadlocked after the first three games, the series will extend to a fourth game on Wednesday, October 7, in Atlanta, with a potential deciding fifth game scheduled for Friday, October 9, in Los Angeles. Players on both sides are emphasizing the need to manage pressure while taking care of business on the field to advance further in the postseason.
