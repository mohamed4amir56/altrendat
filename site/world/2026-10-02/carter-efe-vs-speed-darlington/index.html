Carter Efe Dominates Speed Darlington In Chaos In The Ring Boxing Bout

By Karim Fouad · Published 02 Oct 2026, 04:00 UTC · Updated 02 Oct 2026, 06:01 UTC

Carter Efe secured a technical knockout victory against Speed Darlington at the Chaos in the Ring event held on October 1, 2026.

Carter Efe maintained his unbeaten record in the celebrity boxing series by forcing a stoppage against Speed Darlington. The four-round crossover contest ended prematurely when Efe dominated the proceedings, causing the musician to cease movement and resulting in a technical knockout win for the skit maker.

The Chaos in the Ring event, organized by Balmoral Promotions at the Federal Palace Hotel in Victoria Island, Lagos, showcased celebrity bouts that drew significant attention. For fans of the viral content creators, this matchup provided a definitive conclusion to the ongoing crossover series that pits social media personalities against one another in a professional ring setting.

Beyond the Efe and Darlington bout, the event featured historic moments as reality television star Phyna defeated Nkechi Blessing via knockout in Nigeria’s first female celebrity boxing match. Phyna, who claimed a ₦50m prize for her performance, immediately turned her sights to actress Etinosa Idemudia. After Phyna publicly stated her intent to settle personal disputes in the ring, Etinosa accepted the challenge from ringside. The main event of the evening saw Seth "Freezy Macbones" Gyimah dethrone Godday "Zodsolo" Appah.

While Carter Efe has solidified his standing within this specific celebrity circuit, the organizers have not yet announced the dates for subsequent events. Following the vocal exchange between Phyna and Etinosa at the ringside, spectators now wait to see if the proposed bout between the two celebrities will be finalized for a future edition of the Chaos in the Ring series.
