PRC Releases September 2026 Civil Engineer Licensure Exam Results Nationwide

By Karim Fouad · Published 02 Oct 2026, 11:30 UTC

The Professional Regulation Commission released the list of successful civil engineering examinees on October 2, 2026.

Quick facts

Exam Dates
September 26-27, 2026
Release Date
October 2, 2026
Examining Body
Professional Regulation Commission Board of Civil Engineering

For the thousands of examinees who took the tests, the publication of these results is a critical milestone in their professional development, granting them the credentials required to practice as civil engineers across the country. The examination was administered simultaneously across multiple testing centers to accommodate candidates from various regions, including NCR, Baguio, Butuan, Cagayan de Oro, Calapan, Cebu, Davao, Iloilo, Koronadal, Legazpi, Lucena, Pagadian, Palawan, Pampanga, Rosales, Tacloban, Tuguegarao, Zamboanga, and Ilocos Sur.

Candidates can access the complete Roll of Successful Examinees through the official PRC channels. The publication of this list provides the definitive confirmation for those who have satisfied the licensure requirements established by the board. This specific set of results is distinct from other professional examinations held concurrently, such as the Geodetic Engineers Computer-Based Licensure Examination, where high-ranking performers have also recently been recognized for their academic achievements.

While the Board of Civil Engineering has fulfilled the requirement to post the results within the designated five-day window, successful candidates are encouraged to check the official government platforms for further instructions regarding the registration process and the upcoming oathtaking ceremony, which serves as the final formal step before entering professional practice.
