ChatGPT launches global virtual try-on feature to revolutionize your online shopping

By Karim Fouad · Published 02 Oct 2026, 07:00 UTC

OpenAI introduced new global shopping tools on October 1 that allow users to virtually try on clothing using personal photos and save favorite products to an in-app library.

Quick facts

Launch Date
October 1
Model Used
ChatGPT Images 2.5

Starting October 1, OpenAI has expanded its retail capabilities by enabling users to upload selfies or full-body photos to visualize clothing and accessories on their own bodies. This virtual try-on functionality, which is triggered by a new "Try On" button within shopping results, also allows users to upload screenshots of items from any website for the chatbot to process. The feature is powered by the company's ChatGPT Images 2.5 model, released in September, which reportedly delivers improved lighting, texture quality, and faster generation times compared to previous iterations.

These shopping tools aim to integrate the chatbot more deeply into the consumer retail experience, mirroring similar features introduced by Google in 2025. By allowing users to save discovered items into a personal Library within the app, OpenAI is positioning the platform as a competitor to existing fashion discovery destinations like Pinterest. For shoppers, this provides a centralized way to curate potential purchases and visualize products before deciding to commit to a transaction.

Technically, the process involves selecting a clothing item, tapping the "Try On" button, and providing the necessary personal images. OpenAI notes that saved products and try-on results are stored together, though users should be aware that uploaded images may be used for model training by default unless they opt out of the setting. The rollout follows the company's previous, unsuccessful experiment with an instant checkout feature, signaling a continued effort to find viable consumer use cases for its AI technology.
