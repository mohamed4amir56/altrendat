Chery snatches local identity, names new one-tonne bakkie the Grootman in South Africa

By Karim Fouad · Published 02 Oct 2026, 07:00 UTC

Chery has confirmed its upcoming double-cab KP31 bakkie will be named Grootman following a public competition, with a launch scheduled for early 2027.

Quick facts

Launch Date
First quarter of 2027
Powertrain
2.5 l turbodiesel engine with an electric motor
Performance
350kW and 800Nm
Towing Capacity
3,500kg braked

Following a public campaign that drew over 20,000 name submissions, Chery South Africa has officially settled on the name Grootman for its first-ever one-tonne double-cab bakkie. The name, which is rooted in Afrikaans and translates to “big man,” was selected from a shortlist of six finalists that also included Bakkie, Brahman, Chief, Orca, and Nduna. According to the manufacturer, the choice reflects a common South African expression of respect for individuals who demonstrate responsibility and experience through their actions.

For South African consumers, the Grootman represents a significant entry into the highly competitive bakkie market, where it is positioned to rival established players like the Toyota Hilux and Ford Ranger. General Manager of Chery South Africa, Jay Jay Botes, noted that allowing the public to shape the vehicle's identity creates meaningful expectations for the brand. While the vehicle is marketed as the Grootman in South Africa, it will carry the name Stockman in Australia.

Technical preliminary specifications for the new hybrid bakkie have also been released, detailing a powertrain that combines a 2.5-liter turbodiesel engine with an electric motor. The four-wheel drive unit is expected to deliver a combined output of 350kW and 800Nm of torque, while offering a braked towing capacity of up to 3,500kg.

The Grootman is scheduled to launch in South Africa during the first quarter of 2027. Initially, the bakkie will be imported as a full unit, though Chery is currently evaluating plans for local assembly at the company's newly acquired Rosslyn plant. Further details regarding the final model line-up, exact pricing, and local specifications are expected to be shared closer to the official launch date.
