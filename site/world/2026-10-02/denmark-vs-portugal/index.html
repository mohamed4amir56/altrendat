Portugal silences Denmark 4-2 in Copenhagen to maintain perfect group record

By Karim Fouad · Published 02 Oct 2026, 04:00 UTC

Portugal secured a 4-2 win against Denmark in the UEFA Nations League Group A4 to extend their winning streak.

Quick facts

Match result
Denmark 2-4 Portugal
Competition
UEFA Nations League Group A4
Venue
Copenhagen

Vitinha netted his first senior international goal for Portugal, breaking a 2-2 deadlock to help steer the visitors to a 4-2 victory in Copenhagen. This result marked a third consecutive win for the side under manager Jorge Jesus, keeping them undefeated in their Nations League group campaign. Despite the high-profile absence of Cristiano Ronaldo, who departed the squad prior to the match, the team demonstrated significant attacking depth and tactical discipline throughout the ninety minutes.

For the Selecao, maintaining this momentum is critical as they navigate a highly competitive group stage. The victory not only confirms their current form—boasting nine wins across their last 13 matches—but also solidifies their standing in the tournament after dealing with multiple equalizing efforts from the hosts. For Denmark, the match highlights the challenge of containing a multi-faceted attack, even after Rasmus Højlund leveled the score early in the second half.

João Cancelo opened the scoring in the 13th minute before Mikkel Damsgaard responded for Denmark. Gonçalo Ramos restored Portugal's lead in the 25th minute, though the hosts drew level again via Højlund shortly after the break. Following Vitinha’s decisive header, João Félix sealed the result with a late strike. According to The Guardian, Jorge Jesus praised the performance as a display of the highest quality that set a new standard for his squad. Flashscore reports that the victory keeps the Selecao in command of Group A4, building on a record that has seen them suffer only one defeat in their last 13 outings.
