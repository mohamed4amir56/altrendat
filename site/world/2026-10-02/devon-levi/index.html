Devon Levi storms the net for first career Edmonton Oilers start

By Karim Fouad · Published 02 Oct 2026, 04:30 UTC

The 24-year-old goaltender makes his first start for the Oilers against the Vancouver Canucks on Thursday at 10 p.m. ET.

Devon Levi prepares for his 37th National Hockey League start with a mindset built on more experience than he held during his tenure with the Buffalo Sabres. After recording a 2-7-0 record with a 4.12 goals-against average and an .872 save percentage in nine regular-season appearances last year, the young netminder claims he now carries more tools in his kit, both on and off the ice. Edmonton coach Mike Babcock emphasizes that confidence is earned rather than given, noting that Levi is relishing this specific opportunity. With veteran leaders like Leon Draisaitl already praising his play during training camp and captain’s skates, the Oilers hope Levi can provide the stable goaltending the team has been seeking. This change in the crease comes as the Oilers look to respond following an overtime loss to the Vancouver Canucks on Tuesday. The team continues to navigate early-season hurdles, including a recent upper-body injury to forward Zach Hyman, who will miss up to two weeks of action. Levi will take the ice at Rogers Arena in Vancouver for the Thursday night matchup. He steps into the role as the organization searches for consistency after a rocky performance in the season opener.
