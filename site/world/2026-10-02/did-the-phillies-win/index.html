Phillies Force Winner-Take-All Game 3 After Dramatic Extra-Inning Victory Against Braves

By Karim Fouad · Published 02 Oct 2026, 10:30 UTC

The Phillies evened the NL Wild Card Series at one game apiece with a 4-3 comeback win in Game 2, forcing a decisive third game on Thursday.

Alec Bohm clinched the victory for Philadelphia with a game-winning home run in the extra innings of Game 2. This performance followed a challenging start to the series, where the Braves secured a win in Game 1. "Just win a game," Bohm said of the team's mindset heading into the final matchup. "Turn the page, and move on to tomorrow and show up ready to do the same thing."

The stakes for the upcoming Game 3 at Truist Park are significant, as the winner secures a spot in the NLDS to face the Dodgers. For the Phillies, a victory would mark a successful comeback after dropping the series opener, a feat rarely achieved by postseason teams. Both rosters face pitching challenges, forcing managers to rely on deep rotations and potential relief appearances from their aces to navigate the nine innings.

According to MLB.com and CBS Sports, the Phillies will start Aaron Nola, who recorded a 4.50 ERA against Atlanta during the regular season. The Braves plan to open with left-hander Raymond Kerr on the mound. While the Phillies rely on Nola to carry the initial load, there is speculation that ace Zack Wheeler could enter the game in relief if the situation requires it. Other pitchers, including Jesús Luzardo and Andrew Painter, have confirmed their availability for the bullpen as well.

The decisive Game 3 is scheduled for Thursday at 8:00 p.m. ET. The broadcast will be available on NBC and Peacock, with additional coverage on Universo and Univision Radio for Spanish-speaking fans. ESPN Radio will also provide live national audio coverage of the high-stakes matchup.
