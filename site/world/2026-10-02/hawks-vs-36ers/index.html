Winless Hawks target first victory in high-stakes home opener against Adelaide 36ers

By Karim Fouad · Published 02 Oct 2026, 11:00 UTC

The winless Illawarra Hawks face the Adelaide 36ers this Friday night as they look to recover from an 0-3 season start.

Quick facts

Matchup
Illawarra Hawks vs Adelaide 36ers
Date
Friday night
Hawks standing
0-3 (10th place)

Adelaide forward Nick Rakocevic enters the match with significant momentum after recording a double-double of 21 points and 12 rebounds during Sunday's victory against Cairns. The 36ers head into Wollongong aiming for consecutive wins, while the home side remains under pressure following three straight road losses that have left them sitting in 10th place on the NBL ladder. NBL analyst Derek Rucker has described the Hawks' early performance as a mess, prompting coach Justin Tatum to promise that the team will change drastically and swiftly in their first game back at their home arena.

The outcome carries heavy implications for the Hawks, who are struggling to find stability after an winless start to their campaign. While the team has added size with the reported signing of Makur Maker, they continue to face scrutiny over the lack of a pure point guard and an unfilled third import spot. Conversely, the 36ers are looking to maintain their historical dominance in this matchup, having won eight of their last nine meetings against the Hawks, despite losing their most recent in-season clash by a single point.

According to the Adelaide 36ers, the visitors will be without Flynn Cameron, who is sidelined for at least three games due to an ankle injury. Isaac Humphries and Bul Kuol remain out of the lineup while managing ongoing knee injuries. The Hawks face their own health concerns, with Todd Blanchfield and Mason Peatling both listed as day-to-day with hand and foot injuries, respectively. Both teams are familiar with each other's style, having faced off during the pre-season Code Sports Blitz, a game decided by a late Milton Doyle three-pointer.
