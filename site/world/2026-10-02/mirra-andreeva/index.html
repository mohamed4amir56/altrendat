French Open Champion Mirra Andreeva Faces Yue Yuan in Beijing Clash

By Karim Fouad · Published 02 Oct 2026, 04:30 UTC · Updated 02 Oct 2026, 05:01 UTC

Mirra Andreeva is set to play Yue Yuan in the China Open round-of-64 on Friday in Beijing.

Yue Yuan secured her spot in the round-of-64 by upsetting 47th-ranked Oleksandra Oliynykova with a 6-2, 6-3 victory on Wednesday. The win ended a drought for the 135th-ranked Yuan, who had not claimed a main-draw victory since early August. She navigated the match in 91 minutes, saving all three break points faced while winning over 58 percent of her return points. This performance earns her a high-profile meeting against the Russian standout.

The upcoming match between Yuan and Andreeva presents a significant challenge for the local favorite. According to analysis from Last Word On Sports, Andreeva is viewed as the superior player in the matchup despite recent struggles in her form. The contest is widely expected to be one-sided in favor of the French Open champion, with projections favoring an Andreeva victory in straight sets.

For tennis followers, the match represents a clash between a player finding fresh momentum on home soil and one of the sport's most prominent young talents. The result will determine who advances further in the 1000-level China Open, a tournament that saw several other players secure progression on Wednesday, including Katie Boulter and Aliaksandra Sasnovich. The round-of-64 matches are scheduled to begin on Friday, following the conclusion of the first-round fixtures on Thursday.
