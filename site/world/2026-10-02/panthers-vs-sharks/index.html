Undefeated Florida Panthers invade San Jose for high-stakes second game clash

By Karim Fouad · Published 02 Oct 2026, 04:00 UTC

The 1-0-0 Florida Panthers continue their California road trip against the San Jose Sharks tonight at 10 p.m. ET.

Quick facts

Time
10 p.m. ET
Venue
SAP Center
TV Channels
Scripps, NBCSCA, TVAS

The Florida Panthers arrive at the SAP Center carrying momentum from a 1-0 overtime victory against the Carolina Hurricanes. That season-opening win, secured by Gustav Forsling’s decisive strike with only 4.3 seconds remaining, saw goaltender Jacob Markström stop all 15 shots faced to earn his 26th career shutout. While Florida celebrates its successful start, the team prepares for a distinct tactical shift as they navigate the first leg of a three-game California road trip.

This matchup serves as a significant test for the Panthers, who are shifting gears from the defensive style they faced in Carolina to the Western Conference-style trapping zone employed by San Jose. Head coach Paul Maurice noted the importance of this transition, highlighting the Sharks' notable speed and skill as key challenges for his group. With Florida playing only three of their first 13 games at home, adapting to this rigorous travel schedule and varying tactical environments remains a top priority.

San Jose enters the contest with high expectations following a 34-point improvement last campaign. The team is spearheaded by superstar captain Macklin Celebrini, who totaled 115 points last season, and forward Will Smith. For the Sharks, this game marks their official season opener. Ivar Stenberg is expected to make his NHL debut for San Jose. Meanwhile, the Panthers are managing lineup adjustments, including the injury status of Brad Marchand and Jonah Gadjovich, though Brady Tkachuk is expected to suit up despite missing a recent practice.
