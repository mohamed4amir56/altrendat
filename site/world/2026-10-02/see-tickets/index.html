Glastonbury 2027 coach tickets vanish in under 30 minutes of release

By Karim Fouad · Published 02 Oct 2026, 10:00 UTC

Organizers confirmed all available coach packages for the 2027 festival sold out in less than half an hour on Thursday.

The mad scramble for Glastonbury 2027 has officially begun, as the first wave of tickets was snatched up in under 30 minutes. Following a fallow year in 2026, demand proved relentless as the initial batch of coach and ticket packages disappeared almost immediately after the 18:00 BST release. For those still hoping to attend, the pressure now shifts to the general admission sale this Sunday morning. Getting a ticket requires navigating an automatic queuing system where any attempt to game the site with multiple devices or tabs risks a complete lockout. Experts advise having payment details ready for rapid auto-fill, as buyers face a strict five-minute window to finalize transactions once they reach the front of the line. The 2027 festival, set for 23-27 June at Worthy Farm, arrives with a price tag of £408, the first time entry costs have breached the £400 barrier. While the figure sparked online outrage, some industry observers argue that when compared to individual stadium concerts for major acts, the five-day event offers significant value across its 100 stages of music, comedy, and theatre. Participation remains restricted to those who completed the mandatory pre-registration process before the 25 September deadline. Fans entering the Sunday morning sale must be prepared for stiff competition, as registration will not reopen until after these final October ticket slots are claimed.
