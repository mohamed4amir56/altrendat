South Korea welcomes back Hwang In-beom but faces Kim Min-jae injury scare

By Karim Fouad · Published 02 Oct 2026, 11:00 UTC

South Korea prepares for a friendly match against Venezuela as coach Moreno assesses Kim Min-jae's fitness following his recent injury.

Coach Moreno is currently evaluating the physical condition of defender Kim Min-jae during team training sessions to determine if he can take the pitch. The uncertainty surrounding the team's defensive core comes despite the positive news of Hwang In-beom returning to the squad ahead of the upcoming friendly match against Venezuela.

For supporters and the coaching staff, the potential absence of Kim represents a significant tactical hurdle in the preparation for this international fixture. The decision on his participation remains pending as medical staff monitor his recovery status closely following reports of his recent injury concern.

According to reports from the Chosun Ilbo, the South Korean national football team is balancing the relief of regaining a key playmaker in Hwang with the urgent need to shore up the defense. Moreno has committed to a final assessment of all players on the roster before finalizing the lineup for the game.

This friendly serves as a critical period for the squad to integrate returning talent while addressing holes in the lineup. Fans await the final roster confirmation to see if the team will be at full strength when they face the Venezuelan side.
