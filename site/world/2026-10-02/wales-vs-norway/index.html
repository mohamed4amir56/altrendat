Neco Williams strike fuels Wales comeback to topple 10-man Norway

By Karim Fouad · Published 02 Oct 2026, 04:00 UTC

Wales secured their first Nations League A victory of 2026 by rallying to defeat Norway 2-1 in Cardiff.

Quick facts

Final Score
Wales 2-1 Norway
Venue
Cardiff
Red Card
Torbjørn Heggem

Seventeen seconds after the halftime break, the match turned decisively when Norway defender Torbjørn Heggem was shown a red card for fouling Daniel James. That pivotal moment opened the door for Neco Williams to curl home the decisive free-kick, capping a night where Wales successfully neutralized a frustrated Erling Haaland to claim their first win of the year.

For manager Craig Bellamy, the result serves as a massive psychological breakthrough following a bruising run of results, including a March World Cup playoff defeat to Bosnia and Herzegovina. The victory provides a necessary cooling-off period for a squad that faced significant pressure and public scrutiny following a listless performance against Denmark just days prior.

Norway took an early lead through an Oscar Bobb goal, but Daniel James leveled the score before the sending-off shifted the momentum in favor of the hosts. According to The Guardian, this loss marks the first time in four years that Norway has suffered consecutive defeats, leaving captain Martin Ødegaard to describe the result as a stinging blow for his side.

Looking ahead, the Welsh squad aims to build on this performance against top-tier nations. Neco Williams noted after the match that the team had focused on reinforcing their principles during training, and the players are now setting their sights on preparing for their next fixture in the Nations League campaign.
