TV host Alison Hammond rushes to hospital after live broadcast health scare

By Karim Fouad · Published 03 Oct 2026, 06:00 UTC

Alison Hammond left the ITV show This Morning midway through Friday’s episode to undergo heart checks after experiencing palpitations.

Quick facts

Date of incident
Friday, October 3, 2026
Program
This Morning
Time of departure
11:30 BST
Host age
51

While presenting a segment about pets, the 51-year-old television personality was observed clutching her chest before being assisted off the set by her co-host, Dermot O'Leary. Viewers expressed immediate concern as she abruptly exited the live broadcast, leading O'Leary to inform the audience that his co-presenter had to "duck out" due to feeling unwell. This unexpected departure represents a significant disruption for the program, as Hammond noted she would never normally abandon a live show under standard circumstances. The presenter's health status is of direct interest to the millions who follow her career across popular platforms like This Morning and The Great British Bake Off. Following her departure, Hammond posted an update from a hospital bed to her 1.9 million Instagram followers. She explained that she was having her heart checked after experiencing a palpitation, stating that she remained hopeful the event was merely a panic attack. Despite the situation, Hammond maintained a lighthearted tone, jokingly captioning her video "anything to have a day off" and noting that she was still wearing her television make-up while under medical observation. O'Leary confirmed to the audience that he would continue hosting the remainder of the Friday broadcast alone. Hammond expressed gratitude for the supportive messages received from viewers and praised the medical staff attending to her, while stating she feels "fine" as she undergoes the necessary tests.
