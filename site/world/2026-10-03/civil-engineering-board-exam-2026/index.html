6,337 Civil Engineers Clear Licensure Exam as Top Passer Hits 93.95%

By Karim Fouad · Published 03 Oct 2026, 04:00 UTC

The Professional Regulation Commission reported a 37.14% passing rate for the September 2026 Civil Engineers Licensure Examinations.

Jude Bernard Villarba Miñoza from the Cebu Institute of Technology – University led the latest board exam rankings, securing the top spot with a 93.95 percent rating. The exam, conducted across 19 testing centers, saw 17,062 candidates face the test, with 6,337 ultimately succeeding to join the professional ranks. Results were released just five working days after the final day of testing on September 27.

This high-stakes licensure exam serves as the gateway for graduates to legally practice as civil engineers. For the institutions involved, the results also serve as a benchmark of excellence, with several universities achieving impressive performance metrics. Notably, the University of the Philippines-Los Baños achieved a perfect 100 percent passing rate, with all 75 of its examinees successfully passing the board requirements.

According to the Professional Regulation Commission (PRC), the examinations were held on September 26 and 27, 2026. While thousands celebrated their success, the PRC noted that the results for eight examinees have been withheld pending a final determination of their liabilities under current licensure regulations. Among the top-performing schools with at least 50 examinees, De La Salle University-Manila, the University of Pangasinan, and Pangasinan State University-Urdaneta also recorded passing rates exceeding 80 percent.

The successful examinees now await the official oath-taking ceremonies to formally enter the profession. Those interested can review the complete list of successful candidates through official channels provided by the Professional Regulation Commission.
