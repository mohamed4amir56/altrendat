Coco Gauff Begins China Open Title Defense Against Camila Osorio This Friday

By Karim Fouad · Published 03 Oct 2026, 04:00 UTC · Updated 03 Oct 2026, 05:01 UTC

No. 3 seed Coco Gauff faces Maria Camila Osorio Serrano in the Round of 64 at the China Open on October 2.

Bookmakers have set Gauff as the heavy favorite for this matchup, placing her at -1667 odds to advance against Osorio Serrano, who carries +750 odds. This upcoming contest represents the first career meeting between the two players on the professional circuit. The match follows a dominant first-round performance by Osorio, who snapped a four-match losing streak to defeat Lanlana Tararudee in straight sets, 6-3, 6-3, to earn her place in the bracket against the American star.

For Gauff, the tournament serves as a critical defense of her title, as she enters the second round after receiving an opening-round bye. The No. 4 ranked player looks to maintain her momentum as part of a high-profile lineup of top seeds competing in the Beijing event this week. The outcome of this match will determine who advances further in the tournament as the field of top-ranked players continues to be thinned through competitive play.

According to reports from the WTA and tournament organizers, the schedule of play remains packed with marquee fixtures beyond the Gauff match. Other notable second-round action includes Tenth-seeded American Iva Jovic facing Harriet Dart and a clash between Maria Sakkari and Storm Hunter. The tournament, which has already seen intense three-set matches in the opening stages, continues to draw attention as these major seeds officially begin their campaigns.

The match is scheduled for Friday, October 2, serving as a primary focal point for the second-round action in the Chinese capital. Fans are looking to see if Gauff can navigate this first-time encounter to keep her repeat championship hopes alive.
