Alessandro Bastoni Equalizer Denies France Victory in Sold-Out Stade de France Draw

By Karim Fouad · Published 03 Oct 2026, 06:00 UTC

France and Italy finished in a 1-1 deadlock during their UEFA Nations League match after Alessandro Bastoni canceled out Michael Olise's opening strike.

Gianluigi Donnarumma faced a barrage of pressure, forced to make brilliant saves against Eduardo Camavinga’s long-range efforts in the final 15 minutes of play. France controlled 65 percent of possession and utilized a high-intensity pressing scheme throughout the evening, yet could not secure the win against Italy's low defensive block.

This result leaves both teams assessing their tactical progress under pressure in the international break. For France, the match served as a test for fresh attacking talent like Michael Olise and Desire Doue, who were tasked with executing head coach Zinedine Zidane’s philosophy in front of home supporters.

Michael Olise broke the deadlock ten minutes into the second half with a strike past the Italian goalkeeper, according to Goal.com. Managing Madrid reports that Alessandro Bastoni leveled the score in the 68th minute following an error by Lucas Da Cunha. The match also saw France forced onto the back foot late in the game after Dayot Upamecano received a red card for a second yellow-card offense.

Despite the disappointment of the draw, France forward Desire Doue noted that the team remains committed to the instructions provided by Zidane. Les Bleus are now preparing for their next fixture of the international break, which is scheduled for Monday night against Belgium.
