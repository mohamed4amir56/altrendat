Olise goal cancelled by Bastoni strike as France draws with Italy

By Karim Fouad · Published 03 Oct 2026, 04:00 UTC

Michael Olise scored for France before Alessandro Bastoni equalized to earn Italy a 1-1 draw in the Nations League at Stade de France.

Michael Olise opened the scoring at the Stade de France with a shot from outside the box that crashed in via the underside of the bar, following a free-kick rolled to him by Rayan Cherki. Italy replied as Alessandro Bastoni stole the ball off Lucas da Cunha and beat the advanced goalkeeper Mike Maignan from the edge of the penalty area to secure a point for the visitors.

This match represented Zinedine Zidane's first home game as France manager after securing two previous victories on the road during the current international break. The result marks a continuation of Italy's campaign since Roberto Mancini returned as head coach, with the team now holding a record of one win, one draw, and one loss in the competition.

Performance metrics highlight the impact of the scorers, as Bayern Munich's Olise has now been involved in 14 goals in 15 games for France throughout this year. France faced difficulty maintaining their advantage late in the fixture when Dayot Upamecano was shown a second yellow card, resulting in his expulsion and leaving the team with ten men to finish the game. Elsewhere in Group A1 action, Belgium secured a 3-0 victory against Turkey with goals from Kevin de Bruyne and Romelu Lukaku.
