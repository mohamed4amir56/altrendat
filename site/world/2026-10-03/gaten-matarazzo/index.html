Gaten Matarazzo stars as hit musical Rent returns to London's West End

By Karim Fouad · Published 03 Oct 2026, 10:00 UTC

Stranger Things actor Gaten Matarazzo leads the 30th anniversary revival of Jonathan Larson’s Rent, which began preview performances on 26 September.

Fans can secure front-row seats for the debut at the Tom Stoppard Theatre for just £20, mirroring the show's original pioneering Broadway rush policy. This rare price point for the musical's opening night requires an in-person visit to the box office, with availability strictly capped at two tickets per person starting at 12 noon on the day of the show.

This revival marks a significant moment for theatre-goers as it celebrates three decades of the iconic rock musical. By bringing the story of East Village artists navigating love and poverty to the West End stage, the production aims to capture the same raw energy that earned the original show a Pulitzer Prize and multiple Tony Awards. The production is overseen by award-winning director Luke Sheppard, who previously guided the highly acclaimed version at the Hope Mill Theatre.

Following the opening, the production continues its 30th-anniversary celebration with a specialized ticketing system. From now until 7 October, audiences can purchase front-row seats at the box office for £30 each, starting daily at 10:30 a.m. This effort to keep the show accessible builds upon the musical's historical roots in the early 1990s.

Looking toward the future, the production team has confirmed a shift in ticketing strategy. Starting Friday 9 October, the theatre will transition to a digital lottery system for day seats. Further details regarding the digital process are expected to be announced soon, providing a new way for audiences to catch the production as its run continues.
