Sports Reporter Grace Hayden Defends Career Against Persistent Nepotism Claims

By Karim Fouad · Published 03 Oct 2026, 06:00 UTC

Grace Hayden, the 24-year-old daughter of cricket legend Matthew Hayden, has hit back at critics who argue her broadcasting success is due solely to her family name.

Grace Hayden spent her younger years hearing that she only earned spots on sporting teams because she was the daughter of an Australian cricket great. Now a 24-year-old sports reporter and model, she continues to battle those same claims regarding her career, which spans coverage of cricket’s Big Bash League and IPL, motorsports, tennis, and the NRL. In a recent interview with Stellar, Hayden insisted that her professional journey is defined by thorough research and hard work rather than her famous surname. People will always find things to throw at her, she noted, but she stands by the effort she invests in her work. While she acknowledged that growing up in a prominent family brought public recognition, she rejected the idea that her professional standing is simply a result of being Matthew Hayden’s daughter. She recalled wrestling with these labels throughout her youth, but she has since followed advice to embrace her family identity rather than fight it. Hayden currently lives on a farm in Moree, New South Wales, with her partner, business executive Wilson Statham. She credits her success to surrounding herself with people who know her true character and are willing to provide honest feedback. Her father, who played 103 Test matches for Australia, remains an influence as she continues her career in sports media, including her recent role as a sideline reporter for Fox League.
