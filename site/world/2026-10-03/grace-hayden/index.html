Reporter Grace Hayden Hits Back at Trolls Disputing Her Career Success

By Karim Fouad · Published 03 Oct 2026, 04:00 UTC · Updated 03 Oct 2026, 04:31 UTC

Grace Hayden defends her broadcasting career against claims that her professional achievements are solely due to her father's cricket legacy.

Grace Hayden is shutting down accusations that her media career rests on her father Matthew Hayden’s sporting reputation rather than her own effort. The 24-year-old presenter rejects the narrative that she relies on her surname, emphasizing that her roles in high-profile sports coverage are built on extensive research and hard work. While she acknowledges the challenges of growing up as the daughter of a cricket legend, she confirms that she has learned to balance these external opinions with her own professional reality.

For Hayden, these criticisms are not new; she recalls facing similar skepticism since childhood, including claims that her positions on sports teams were unearned. Addressing these remarks is significant for her as she continues to expand her footprint in sports media, moving from cricket coverage in the Big Bash League and India’s IPL to sideline reporting for the NRL. Her defense highlights the internal pressure she has navigated while establishing her identity in a competitive industry.

According to Stellar, Hayden maintains that she does the hard yards necessary to succeed in broadcasting. She currently works across various disciplines, including tennis and motorsports. Reflecting on her path, she notes that she once wrestled with the constant association to her father, but now chooses to embrace her family background while remaining firm about her personal contribution. Outside of her professional life, she resides on a farm in Moree, New South Wales, with her partner, business executive Wilson Statham, and continues to rely on her inner circle to stay grounded despite public scrutiny.
