Hasan Nawaz hits belligerent 96 but Pakistan falls short against India

By Karim Fouad · Published 03 Oct 2026, 08:01 UTC · Updated 03 Oct 2026, 08:31 UTC

Hasan Nawaz scored 96 runs in the Asian Games cricket final, but Pakistan finished 19 runs behind India's 212-run total.

Quick facts

Match result
Pakistan 192/6, India 212
Hasan Nawaz score
96 runs from 51 balls
Venue
Korogi Athletic Park, Nisshin, Japan
Date
Saturday

Hasan Nawaz struck seven sixes and five fours in a defiant 51-ball innings, eventually falling on the final ball of the match. His departure left Pakistan on 192 for six, unable to overcome India's target of 212 at Korogi Athletic Park. The innings began under pressure, with Pakistan struggling at 39 for three after the first six overs before a recovery effort led by Hasan and Saim Ayub. The pair formed an 87-run partnership for the fourth wicket, providing Pakistan a brief path toward contention during the middle overs of the final. Hasan was particularly effective against the spin attack, forcing Ravi Bishnoi to concede 44 runs across three overs. By the 14th over, Pakistan had reached 126 for three, requiring 86 runs from the final 36 balls to secure the gold medal. The match reached a climax in the final over, where Pakistan required 33 runs for a win. Hasan successfully hit Shivam Dube for a boundary and a six, but a subsequent yorker and two dot balls left him needing four runs for a century on the final delivery. His attempt to clear the infield resulted in a catch by wicket-keeper Sanju Samson, sealing the 19-run defeat. The result awards Pakistan the silver medal in the Asian Games men's cricket competition, while India takes the gold.
