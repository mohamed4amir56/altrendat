Hyundai Surges Past Honda to Claim US Hybrid Market Share for First Time

By Karim Fouad · Published 03 Oct 2026, 04:00 UTC

Hyundai Motor Group overtook Honda Motor in U.S. hybrid vehicle sales during the first half of the year as Americans increasingly turn to Asian brands.

Quick facts

Market Leader Shift
Hyundai Motor Group overtakes Honda Motor
Time Period
First half of the year
Event Date
October 1

Hyundai Motor Group has officially overtaken Honda Motor in the U.S. hybrid vehicle market during the first half of the year. This shift marks the first time the South Korean automaker has surpassed its Japanese rival, signaling a notable change in consumer preference within the automotive sector.

The growing consumer shift toward hybrid technology is driven by sustained high gasoline prices across the country, which have pushed Americans to reconsider their vehicle choices. As demand for these fuel-efficient models continues to rise, major Asian manufacturers are positioned at the forefront of the market, with Hyundai successfully capturing a larger portion of the growing hybrid customer base.

Nikkei Asia reports that while Hyundai is rapidly expanding its sales volume, Japanese automakers are currently facing difficulties meeting the heightened demand for hybrid inventory. This struggle to match production with market interest has allowed Hyundai to exert significant pressure on its competitors. To maintain this momentum, Hyundai Motor CEO Jose Munoz recently showcased the redesigned Tucson SUV during an event in New York on Oct. 1.

Industry analysts and consumers alike are watching to see how Japanese rivals will adjust their supply chains to combat this market shift. With the appetite for hybrid cars remaining strong among U.S. buyers, the competitive landscape between these international giants is expected to intensify throughout the remainder of the fiscal year.
