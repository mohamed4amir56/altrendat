Brazil and India meet for first time ever in Kolkata international friendly

By Karim Fouad · Published 03 Oct 2026, 14:01 UTC

India and Brazil face off in a senior men’s international friendly at Vivekananda Yuba Bharati Krirangan this Saturday.

Quick facts

Match Date
Saturday
Venue
Vivekananda Yuba Bharati Krirangan, Kolkata
Brazil Head Coach
Carlo Ancelotti
Squad Size
26 players

Brazil arrives at the Salt Lake Stadium boasting a recent record of four wins in their last six matches, including a 4-2 victory against Australia. The Seleção now play under head coach Carlo Ancelotti as they look to build momentum following their round-of-16 elimination at the 2026 World Cup. The upcoming fixture marks the inaugural senior men's international clash between the two nations, providing a rare test for the home side against the five-time world champions.

For football fans, the match serves as a marquee opportunity to witness world-class talent on Indian soil. Despite facing injury setbacks, the Brazilian squad remains formidable, anchored by stars like Vinicius Junior, Endrick, and Estevao Willian. The result carries significance for both teams as they continue their respective paths in the international circuit, with Brazil seeking to solidify their tactical approach under Ancelotti's 4-3-3 system.

The visiting roster has seen changes, with Barcelona winger Raphinha ruled out due to a right-thigh injury sustained against Australia. He joins Joao Pedro and Wesley on the sidelines. To compensate, Fluminense forward Gabriel Martinelli received a late call-up to join the traveling group. Ancelotti has opted not to name a replacement for Raphinha, relying instead on the existing depth of his 26-player experimental squad.

Defensive veterans Marquinhos and Gabriel Magalhaes are expected to anchor the backline, while Bruno Guimaraes remains the primary choice to control the midfield tempo. Ancelotti will look to capitalize on wide areas throughout the match at the Vivekananda Yuba Bharati Krirangan, commonly known as Salt Lake Stadium, where the crowd expects an intense tactical display from both sides.
