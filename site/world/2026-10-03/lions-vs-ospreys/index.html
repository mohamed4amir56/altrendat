Boeta Chamberlain try secures bonus point as Lions surge past Ospreys

By Karim Fouad · Published 03 Oct 2026, 14:01 UTC

The Lions secured a bonus point victory over the Ospreys at Ellis Park following a decisive try from Boeta Chamberlain.

Quick facts

Venue
Ellis Park
Kick-off time
1.45pm
Competition
United Rugby Championship

A cross-field kick by Chris Smith initially evaded Kelly Mpeku, but the winger recovered to find Francke Horn, whose offload set up Boeta Chamberlain for a crucial score. TMO Gianluca Gnecchi reviewed the play for a potential forward pass but found insufficient evidence to overturn the on-field decision, confirming the Lions' bonus point. Smith later attempted to extend the lead but saw his wide-angle conversion strike the post.

This victory marks a significant continuation of momentum for the Lions, who entered the fixture off a narrow season-opening win against Leinster. Assistant coach Jaque Fourie emphasized that despite the recent success, the team had to reset their focus to address the specific challenges posed by an Ospreys side that has consistently proven difficult to overcome over the past five years.

The Lions lineup featured strategic adjustments, including the first URC start for centre Ethan Adams, who replaced the concussed Henco van Wyk. The team also received a notable boost with the inclusion of Springboks Morne van den Berg and Quan Horn on the bench. Changes in the forward pack saw Siba Qoma and JC Pretorius enter the starting flanker positions, while Batho Hlekani was omitted from the match-day 23.

With this result at Ellis Park, the Lions continue their campaign in the United Rugby Championship. The coaching staff remains focused on refining team performance after identifying areas for improvement following their previous outing against Leinster. The squad maintains their preparations as they look toward subsequent fixtures in the competition.
