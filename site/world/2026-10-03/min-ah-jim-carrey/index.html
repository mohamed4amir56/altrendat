Jim Carrey weds girlfriend Min Ah in private Los Angeles ceremony at 64

By Karim Fouad · Published 03 Oct 2026, 05:01 UTC

Canadian actor Jim Carrey, 64, married his girlfriend Min Ah, 32, in a small private ceremony in Los Angeles, seven months after publicly thanking her as his 'sublime companion' at the César Awards in Paris.

Jim Carrey has married again after decades of avoiding the altar. The 64-year-old Ace Ventura star tied the knot with Min Ah, 32, in a private Los Angeles ceremony, according to his spokesperson's confirmation to People on September 30. The couple kept the wedding date, venue, and guest list confidential.

Carrey's relationship with Ah remained largely private until their public debut together in February at the César Awards in Paris, where the actor accepted an honorary award. During his acceptance speech, delivered in French, Carrey publicly acknowledged Ah for the first time, calling her his "sublime companion" and declaring his love for her in front of his daughter Jane and grandson Jackson.

The couple was first photographed together in February 2022 after a charity comedy show hosted by Judd Apatow in Los Angeles. Carrey has previously been married twice: to Melissa Womer from 1987 to 1995, with whom he shares daughter Jane, and to Dumb and Dumber costar Lauren Holly from 1996 to 1997. The actor has been notably private about his personal life throughout his career, and tends to keep relationships out of the public spotlight.
