Nathan Carter becomes youngest inductee to Late Late Country Music Hall

By Karim Fouad · Published 03 Oct 2026, 04:00 UTC · Updated 03 Oct 2026, 07:31 UTC

Country music star Nathan Carter was surprised with an induction into The Late Late Show Country Music Hall of Fame during Friday night's special broadcast.

Nathan Carter

At 36 years old, Nathan Carter is now the youngest recipient ever to receive the honor, marking a significant milestone in a career that began in Liverpool pubs nearly 16 years ago. The singer was visibly caught off guard when the award was presented, describing the experience of having his parents, Ian and Noreen, and his grandmother, Anne, join him on stage as an unbelievable moment he simply did not expect. The induction represents a high-profile recognition of his lasting impact on the country music scene, cementing his status alongside legends who previously earned their spots in the Hall of Fame. For fans, the segment provided a full-circle moment as the artist performed "Time of Your Life" to open the show, the same track he first sang on the program 13 years ago. According to RTE and Evoke, the induction ceremony was part of The Late Late Show Country Music Special, which also featured performances by Ashley Campbell, Cliona Hagan, Michael English, and Una Healy. During the broadcast, Carter reflected on his journey since leaving his home city, noting the profound contrast between his early days playing in small venues and receiving this national recognition. The event also included a tribute to Dolly Parton, rounding out a night dedicated to the genre's heritage and current stars. Carter took to social media following the broadcast to express his pride in being welcomed into the Hall of Fame in recognition of his achievements in the business.
