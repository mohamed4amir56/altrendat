Netflix secures library of five Ice Age movies from rival Disney

By Karim Fouad · Published 03 Oct 2026, 06:00 UTC · Updated 03 Oct 2026, 10:01 UTC

Disney Entertainment and Netflix have signed an expansive licensing deal to bring various library titles and TV shows to the Netflix streaming platform.

Quick facts

Netflix user base
Over 325 million
Key inclusions
All five Ice Age movies
Featured series
Will Trent, Percy Jackson and the Olympians, and Felicity

Netflix will add all five Ice Age movies to its library as part of an expansive new content licensing agreement with Disney Entertainment. The deal includes a wide variety of titles, ranging from current-season television shows to long-running series like Felicity, Will Trent, and Percy Jackson and the Olympians. While both companies continue to compete directly for streaming subscribers, Disney is leveraging the reach of Netflix to monetize its content further. With Netflix boasting a customer base exceeding 325 million users, Disney views the arrangement as a strategic opportunity to promote its intellectual property to a massive global audience. The agreement effectively sees Netflix paying for the rights to host these popular titles for varying periods of time, creating an eclectic mix of programming that spans genres and production eras. This move highlights a shifting landscape in streaming media, where direct competitors increasingly collaborate to maximize the value of their entertainment catalogs. By distributing content across multiple platforms, Disney aims to drive engagement with its titles beyond its own flagship streaming service. For subscribers, the partnership ensures that high-profile content originally produced by Disney will now be accessible on Netflix. While the financial details of the agreement remain undisclosed, the deal signals a significant investment from Netflix to bolster its library with established franchises and popular TV series. Further content additions are expected as the licensing agreement takes effect across the platform for the specified durations.
