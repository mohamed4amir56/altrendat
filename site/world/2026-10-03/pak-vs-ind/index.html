India Clinches Gold Medal Showdown Against Pakistan in Asian Games Cricket

By Karim Fouad · Published 03 Oct 2026, 04:00 UTC

India secured a spot in the 2026 Asian Games men's cricket gold medal match after crushing Sri Lanka by 124 runs.

Quick facts

Event
Asian Games 2026 Men's Cricket Final
Date
October 1, 2026
Venue
Korogi Sports Park, Nisshin
Score
124-run victory

Sri Lanka folded for a paltry 45 runs against India's bowling attack in the second semi-final at Korogi Sports Park. The total was a sharp contrast to India's score of 169/7, which was anchored by Ishan Kishan's unbeaten 80 runs off 46 balls. After winning the toss and choosing to field on a spin-friendly pitch, Sri Lanka allowed India to recover from an early 24/2 struggle to set a daunting target. Teenage prodigy Vaibhav Sooryavanshi provided early momentum with 38 runs off 21 balls, but it was Kishan’s masterclass of five sixes and four boundaries that proved to be the knockout blow. For cricket followers, this victory sets up a highly anticipated gold medal clash between regional rivals India and Pakistan. The final serves as the culmination of the 2026 Asian Games men's cricket competition, drawing intense interest given the history between the two teams. According to Cricbuzz, India's bowling attack was led by Jasprit Bumrah, who delivered two early strikes before Sri Lanka could even register a run. Axar Patel then dismantled the middle order, taking the wickets of captain Sahan Arachchige and Ashen Bandara in the span of four balls. This disciplined performance ensured a swift conclusion to the match, with Sri Lanka struggling to cope with the spin-friendly conditions that defined the day's play in Nisshin. The gold medal match is scheduled as the next step for both teams, bringing the continental tournament to its definitive conclusion with the most awaited encounter in the sport.
