Simba Coach Steve Barker Demands International Recall For Captain Rushine De Reuck

By Karim Fouad · Published 03 Oct 2026, 11:40 UTC

Simba SC head coach Steve Barker is pushing for defender Rushine De Reuck to return to the Bafana Bafana squad following consistent performances in Tanzania.

Rushine De Reuck has successfully reinvented his career in the East African top flight, logging 34 appearances across all competitions for Tanzanian giants Simba SC. Simba coach Steve Barker is now publicly advocating for the 30-year-old centre-back to be considered for a return to the South African national team setup, citing the defender's reliable form and leadership as clear evidence of his readiness. De Reuck last featured for Bafana Bafana in 2022 during his tenure with Mamelodi Sundowns.

The potential recall holds significant weight, as injury setbacks and a subsequent lack of game time previously forced the defender out of the national team picture. By maintaining consistent health and performance levels since moving abroad, De Reuck has emerged as a focal point for his current club. Barker’s endorsement highlights the tactical value of incorporating players who have proven their resilience and consistency in competitive continental football.

Speaking to media at the club’s mid-season camp in Polokwane, Barker confirmed his stance on the player's international prospects. He emphasized that the defender has demonstrated both quality and leadership abilities throughout the matches played since January. Barker noted that the club has managed the player's physical condition effectively, allowing him to play consistently week in and week out. The coach remains hopeful that selectors will take note of these solid performances when finalizing future squads. The call marks a push for a veteran player to reclaim a spot on the international stage after overcoming previous physical challenges.
