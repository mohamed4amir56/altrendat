Roughriders Hunt Playoff Berth Against Stampeders in Crucial West Division Clash

By Karim Fouad · Published 03 Oct 2026, 04:00 UTC

The Saskatchewan Roughriders aim to clinch a postseason spot Friday at 9:30 p.m. ET by defeating the Calgary Stampeders.

Quick facts

Matchup
Calgary Stampeders vs. Saskatchewan Roughriders
Kickoff
Friday, 9:30 p.m. ET
TV Channel
TSN

This matchup carries heavy stakes as both teams navigate the final stretch of the regular season. For Saskatchewan, the focus remains on fixing recent red-zone inefficiencies that saw them come up empty on four possessions in a 31-24 loss to BC last week. Despite the recent dip in efficiency, quarterback Trevor Harris insists the team is focused on execution rather than panic. Head coach Corey Mace emphasized that while the team discusses playoff implications, their primary goal is simply playing winning football at home.

Calgary enters the game with momentum, riding back-to-back wins over the Ottawa REDBLACKS. Receiver Jalen Philpot has been a key driver of that success, recently surpassing 1,000 yards for the first time in his career. Defensively, Calgary will look to limit Saskatchewan’s ground game, specifically running back AJ Ouellette, who returned from injury last week to record 77 yards on 19 carries. The Stampeders' defense will also be tasked with protecting late-game leads, having surrendered an average of 19 points in the fourth quarter over their last four outings.

The game is scheduled for a 9:30 p.m. ET kickoff in Regina. Fans in Canada can follow the action on TSN, while international and U.S. viewers can tune in via CFL+.
