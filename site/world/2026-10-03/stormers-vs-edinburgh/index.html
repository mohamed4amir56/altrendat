Edinburgh Stuns Stormers as Lineout Errors Derailed 17-13 Away Defeat

By Karim Fouad · Published 03 Oct 2026, 04:00 UTC

The Stormers lost 17-13 to Edinburgh at Hive Stadium on Friday after failing to secure 11 lineouts during the second round of the URC.

The Stormers were undone by their own set-piece, surrendering possession on 11 separate occasions at the lineout in a match decided by fine margins in the wind. Trailing 3-0 at half-time, the visitors fought back to briefly claim the lead, only to see the hosts secure the victory by stealing a final lineout attempt at the death. Coach John Dobson expressed deep frustration with the performance, noting that the team’s game plan relied heavily on the maul, which became impossible to execute under the prevailing weather conditions. The loss was compounded by the early exit of hooker André-Hugo Venter, who was forced off the field midway through the first half following a failed HIA. Dobson highlighted that the loss of Venter significantly impacted the team's ability to maintain set-piece stability throughout the remainder of the contest. While Yaqeen Ahmed contributed eight points with his boot and Hacjivah Dayimani scored a try, defensive discipline issues at the breakdown allowed Edinburgh to capitalize on critical scoring opportunities. This defeat leaves the Cape Town side looking to recover from their inaccuracy as they prepare for upcoming fixtures. The team is currently shifting focus toward regrouping after this disappointing result in Scotland. Moving forward, the club is engaged in a long-term internal strategy known as Project 2029, which aims to strengthen development structures and rebuild the team as a consistent international trophy contender, emphasizing a stronger connection between the players and their support base.
