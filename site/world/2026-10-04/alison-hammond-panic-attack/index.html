TV presenter Alison Hammond rushes off live show after massive panic attack

By Karim Fouad · Published 04 Oct 2026, 08:00 UTC

Presenter Alison Hammond left ITV's This Morning on Friday after suffering a panic attack during a live broadcast segment about pets.

Quick facts

Date
Friday, October 3, 2026
Show
This Morning
Time of departure
11:30 BST
Diagnosis
Panic attack

It was a startling turn of events on Friday morning when 51-year-old television presenter Alison Hammond was escorted off the This Morning set while appearing to clutch her chest. The incident occurred during a segment on the ITV programme involving a veterinarian and pets, forcing the show to abruptly cut to an advertising break. After initial concerns regarding her heart health, medical staff at the NHS performed comprehensive tests on her heart, lungs, and blood, all of which returned clear results.

For those watching at home, the departure was jarring as Hammond had been co-hosting alongside Dermot O'Leary since the 10:00 BST start. Understanding the impact of such a visible health scare, Hammond later shared an update on Instagram to address the worry of her audience. She confirmed that while the experience felt intensely physical, she is now home, safe, and resting. Her candid admission serves as a personal account of navigating what she described as a 'new world' of panic attacks.

The timeline of the broadcast shows that Hammond left at 11:30 as the symptoms intensified. According to the BBC and RTE, O'Leary initially informed viewers that his colleague was 'feeling a little unwell' and might return. He later confirmed to the audience that he would be hosting the remainder of the show alone. During the segment, she was heard breathing heavily before O'Leary assisted her off-camera.

Looking ahead, Hammond expressed her gratitude to the medical teams and her colleagues at This Morning for their quick support during the ordeal. She stated that she plans to take stock of the situation and prioritize rest before returning to her presenting duties. Her fans have received confirmation that she is recovering well after the medical checks.
