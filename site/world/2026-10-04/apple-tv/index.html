Florence Pugh Topples Expectations in Netflix Ambitious East of Eden Series Premiere

By Karim Fouad · Published 04 Oct 2026, 07:00 UTC

The seven-part adaptation of John Steinbeck’s classic novel featuring Florence Pugh arrived on Netflix this Thursday.

Quick facts

Platform
Netflix
Release Date
Thursday, October 1
Format
7-part limited series
Starring
Florence Pugh

A girl like that does not just fall out of the sky. This central tension defines the arrival of Cathy Ames, portrayed with icy sweetness by Florence Pugh, on the doorstep of the Trask family farm. The seven-part adaptation, created by Zoe Kazan, shifts the narrative focus to provide a more ambiguous look at a character defined not just as a sociopathic destroyer, but as a ruthless survivor.

This release matters for viewers looking for standout fall television, as the performance is already being cited as one of Pugh's best. The series offers a new perspective on the Steinbeck study of free will and family, moving away from purely villainous tropes to explore the character's survival instincts.

According to TheWrap, the entire series became available for streaming on Thursday. The show joins other major streaming premieres this week, including the return of the thriller series Sacrifice on Paramount+ and a new crime project starring Catherine Zeta-Jones. The Guardian notes that while the script occasionally struggles with its complex philosophical themes, the production remains a must-watch for those tracking the fall television season.

With the full series now available on Netflix, audiences can view the multigenerational story of the Trask family in its entirety.
