Giuliano Simeone Header Seals Dominant 7-0 Argentina Rout Over Burkina Faso

By Karim Fouad · Published 04 Oct 2026, 04:00 UTC

Argentina secured a commanding 7-0 victory against Burkina Faso in an international friendly at the Estadio Monumental.

Quick facts

Result
Argentina 7, Burkina Faso 0
Venue
Estadio Monumental, Buenos Aires
Argentina FIFA Ranking
2nd
Burkina Faso FIFA Ranking
62nd

Giuliano Simeone hammered home a close-range header to finalize a relentless 7-0 scoreline, punctuating a night where Argentina displayed its vast offensive depth. The match, defined by sustained pressure from the hosts, saw Burkina Faso goalkeeper Razack Guira repeatedly tested by long-range efforts, including a difficult strike from Franco Mastantuono and a left-footed attempt from Santiago Castro that rattled the woodwork. The visitors struggled to gain momentum, with their attacking attempts thwarted throughout the evening and their defense unable to contain the relentless Argentine buildup led by Valentín Barco. The result underscores the significant gap between the two sides as Argentina prepares for upcoming international commitments. With this comfortable win, Argentina continues to test its squad depth following their previous 4-0 success over Bolivia. The match served as another venue for coach Lionel Scaloni to evaluate younger talent, including Simeone and Mastantuono, while veteran figures like Emiliano Martínez saw action before being substituted. Burkina Faso, currently ranked 62nd by FIFA, remains focused on rebuilding after this lopsided defeat. Lionel Messi, who is resting during this international window, was absent from the squad as he awaits his scheduled farewell match against Benin on October 6. The Argentine side, currently second in the FIFA rankings, demonstrated the tactical flexibility that has kept them at the top tier of international football despite the absence of their legendary forward. Attention now shifts for the Argentine squad as they look ahead to their final fixture of the current window, while Burkina Faso returns to its development path following this difficult encounter at the Monumental.
