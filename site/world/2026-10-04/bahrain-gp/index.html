Max Verstappen Snatches First Pole as Hamilton Returns to Front Row

By Karim Fouad · Published 04 Oct 2026, 06:01 UTC

Max Verstappen claimed pole position for Sunday's Bahrain Grand Prix in Malaysia, finishing 0.298 seconds ahead of Lewis Hamilton.

Quick facts

Pole position time
1:35.130
Qualifying margin
0.298s
Race start time
8am Sunday
Broadcast channel
Sky Sports F1 and Main Event

Max Verstappen leads the grid after securing his first pole position of the year at the Sepang International Circuit, with Lewis Hamilton joining him on the front row. Qualifying saw Verstappen post a time of 1:35.130, leaving Hamilton nearly 0.3 seconds behind in second place. This marks the first time the two drivers have shared a front row since the 2023 Hungarian Grand Prix.

The race at the Sepang track, which hosts Formula 1 for the first time since 2017, presents significant tactical challenges. Drivers are bracing for high tyre wear on a surface described as perhaps the roughest of the season, combined with intense tropical heat. For Hamilton and Ferrari, the primary concern remains a deficit in straight-line speed that may hinder their ability to challenge for the win against Red Bull's current form.

According to BBC, Red Bull's performance gains are attributed to a successful floor upgrade, a stark turnaround for a team that spent the first half of the season more than 0.5 seconds slower per lap than their competitors. Verstappen noted the progress as an incredible result given the team's early-season struggles. Meanwhile, championship leader Kimi Antonelli will start the race from third position.

Lights out for the Bahrain Grand Prix in Malaysia is scheduled for 8am on Sunday. Fans can follow the live broadcast on Sky Sports F1 and Main Event, with pre-race build-up coverage beginning at 6.30am.
