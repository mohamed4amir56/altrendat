Munster rookie Charlie O’Shea masterclass topples Bulls in 26-22 URC clash

By Karim Fouad · Published 04 Oct 2026, 09:01 UTC

The Bulls fell to a 26-22 loss against Munster at Thomond Park on Saturday night as 20-year-old Charlie O’Shea scored two tries.

Quick facts

Final Score
Munster 26-22 Bulls
Date
Saturday night
Venue
Thomond Park, Limerick
Bulls Try Scorers
Ruan Vermaak, Francois Klopper, Jeandre Rudolph

The 20-year-old pivot Charlie O’Shea dominated the match in only his second United Rugby Championship appearance and his first start, scoring two tries and setting up another to dictate the pace of the game. Munster controlled the match through additional tries from Diarmuid Kilgallen and Alex Kendellen, successfully holding off the visitors in the final minutes.

The result impacts the Bulls' standing as they move through their northern hemisphere tour, despite showing enough form to remain serious contenders for the season. The match proved a high-quality tactical battle that ended in a narrow four-point deficit for the South African side.

The contest was deadlocked at 14-14 at half-time after Ruan Vermaak and Francois Klopper touched down for the Bulls. After Curwin Bosch slotted a penalty in the 44th minute to briefly give the Bulls the lead, Munster regained control. A late glimmer of hope emerged in the 72nd minute when Jeandre Rudolph scored a try in the right-hand corner, but the missed conversion left the Bulls trailing 26-22 entering the final six minutes of play. According to News24, Munster utilized superior possession and territory to squeeze the life out of the game in the second half.

Heading into this match, head coach Johan Ackermann had implemented a reshuffle of the match-day 23, including rotating the backline and incorporating prop Khutha Mchunu following an ankle injury to Mornay Smith. While the starting forward pack had remained unchanged from the previous victory over Zebre Parma, the team could not overcome the Irish opposition’s late-match performance at Thomond Park.
