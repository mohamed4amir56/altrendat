Canucks Host Flames as Young Defensemen Parekh and Buium Face Off Saturday

By Karim Fouad · Published 04 Oct 2026, 05:01 UTC

The Vancouver Canucks face the Calgary Flames at Rogers Arena Saturday following a high-scoring two-game opening series against Edmonton.

Zayne Parekh and Zeev Buium, two standout 20-year-old defensemen from the 2024 NHL Draft, headline the matchup at Rogers Arena as the Calgary Flames visit the Vancouver Canucks. While Calgary looks to recover from a 6-1 opening defeat to the Seattle Kraken, Vancouver enters the game with momentum after netting 13 goals across their first two contests against the Edmonton Oilers. This Saturday night encounter marks the first of four meetings this season between the two clubs.

For both organizations, these games carry significant weight regarding their long-term rebuild strategies. With both teams identified as potential competitors for the top spot in the 2027 NHL Draft, the results of this season series may prove influential in determining their future draft odds. Fans looking to catch the action can tune in for the broadcast starting at 8 p.m. MT on Sportsnet West and QR Calgary, or via the 10 p.m. ET broadcast on Sportsnet and TVAS2.

The Flames, led by head coach Ryan Huska, are shuffling their defensive pairings and starting goaltender Devin Cooley for this road appearance. On the Vancouver side, head coach Manny Malhotra has been experimenting with line changes throughout practice sessions at UBC following their intense opening stretch against Edmonton. The matchup brings a renewed sense of tension to the rink as the teams aim to establish their standing early in the 2026 campaign.

The two clubs are scheduled to continue their season series throughout the coming months, with scouts and fans watching to see which roster can establish more consistency on the ice. The outcome of Saturday’s game serves as the first benchmark in this revived divisional rivalry.
