Channel 9 staff face October 18 deadline for new controversial employment contracts

By Karim Fouad · Published 04 Oct 2026, 09:01 UTC

Nine Network employees are currently holding secret meetings with unions regarding new workplace conditions that take effect on October 18.

Quick facts

Contract deadline
October 18
Project name
Future News
NRL grand final teams
Roosters and Knights

Nine staff have been warned that new contracts will be considered accepted through conduct if they continue working after October 18, regardless of whether the documents are physically signed. Employment lawyer Michael Byrnes states that if employees do not explicitly object to the new terms, their continued presence in the workplace may be interpreted by the company as formal agreement to the changes introduced under the Future News project.

This development holds significant weight for the workforce, as it marks a contentious shift in employment conditions. Staff are currently engaging in secret meetings with unions to clarify their legal standing and determine how to effectively register their dissent. Byrnes advises that for employees to protect their rights, they must clearly and persistently communicate their refusal to accept the new contract terms to their employer.

Separately, the network is preparing its broadcast team for Sunday's NRL grand final, a decision that has sparked heated debate among viewers. The commentary line-up, which features Mat Thompson, Andrew Johns, and Billy Slater, has left fans divided over the exclusion of veteran analyst Phil Gould from the main event. While fans speculate on the fairness of the roster, the network remains focused on the upcoming clash between the Roosters and the Knights.

The situation at the network remains fluid as the October 18 deadline approaches. Employees who wish to challenge the new conditions are being encouraged to maintain a clear record of their objections rather than simply opting not to sign the provided paperwork.
