Ruthless England storms past Croatia in 7-0 Nations League demolition rout

By Karim Fouad · Published 04 Oct 2026, 04:00 UTC

England secured a dominant 7-0 victory against Croatia in Rijeka on Saturday to claim their second win in three Nations League group matches.

The atmosphere at Stadion HNK Rijeka was a stark contrast to typical footballing hostility, with only 6,000 local schoolchildren and 100 England supporters present due to crowd trouble sanctions dating back to 2025. Despite the serene proximity of the Adriatic Sea, England displayed a relentless clinical edge that left Croatia completely unable to cope with their pace and tactical movement. Harry Kane, who extended his national record goal tally to 89, noted that the team performed at a Premier League level from start to finish.

For England manager Thomas Tuchel, the win marks a significant milestone as the largest victory of his tenure. The performance underscores the team's ongoing development under his leadership, particularly in utilizing the tactical fluidity of his attacking quartet to dominate opposition defenses. Securing these points is vital for England's campaign as they look to finalize a top-two finish in their group to guarantee a spot in the Nations League quarterfinals this coming March.

According to Al Jazeera, England’s offensive masterclass was led by Kane and Bukayo Saka, who both finished with two goals each. Jude Bellingham added to the scoring and provided three assists in a commanding midfield display, while Anthony Gordon and substitute Morgan Rogers also registered goals. BBC Sport reported that England's defensive setup, featuring Jarell Quansah at right-back and a balanced back four, effectively stifled Croatia throughout the match, allowing the attacking players the freedom to dismantle the host side.

England will now return to Wembley on Tuesday to conclude the current international break with a fixture against the Czech Republic. The team aims to maintain this momentum to secure their progression path in the competition.
