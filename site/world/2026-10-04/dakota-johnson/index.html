Dakota Johnson Returns to Saturday Night Live Studio 8H for Third Time

By Karim Fouad · Published 04 Oct 2026, 04:00 UTC

Dakota Johnson hosts the second episode of Saturday Night Live Season 52 on October 3, 2026.

Quick facts

Host
Dakota Johnson
Date
October 3, 2026
Time
11:30 p.m. ET
Network
NBC

Dakota Johnson makes her third appearance as host tonight, bringing her closer to the Five Timers Club robe. Her return to Studio 8H follows her initial hosting gig in 2015 and a second appearance nine years later in 2024. Promotional materials for the episode feature Johnson alongside cast member Ashley Padilla, signaling the next installment of Season 52.

For viewers, this episode serves as the follow-up to the season premiere, which featured New York Knicks captain Jalen Brunson. The show continues its run during a period of transition, as fans adjust to the departure of Chloe Fineman and Martin Herlihy and the addition of new featured players Saidah Belo-Osagie and Grace Reiter.

According to TVLine, Johnson is using this platform to promote the upcoming film Verity, an adaptation of the Colleen Hoover novel that also features Anne Hathaway and Josh Hartnett. The show will air live at 11:30 p.m. ET on NBC.

Following the live broadcast, the full episode will be available for streaming on Peacock starting Sunday. This airing maintains the schedule established during the Season 52 premiere last week.
