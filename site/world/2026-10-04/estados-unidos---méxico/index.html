USA and Mexico clash in Glendale for 80th North American Classic

By Karim Fouad · Published 04 Oct 2026, 04:00 UTC · Updated 04 Oct 2026, 05:01 UTC

The United States hosts Mexico at State Farm Stadium this Saturday, October 3, 2026, in their 80th historical encounter.

Quick facts

Match
80th North American Classic
Date
October 3, 2026
Time
20:00
Venue
State Farm Stadium, Glendale, Arizona

The United States enters this 80th meeting against Mexico with high momentum, having secured back-to-back victories by scoring eight goals in two matches. Mauricio Pochettino’s squad enters the contest following a 4-2 win over Chile and a 4-1 thrashing of Peru during the current FIFA window. In stark contrast, Mexico’s head coach Rafa Márquez arrives in Glendale seeking his first win after drawing both of his recent fixtures against Colombia and Peru, with both games ending 1-1.

This match carries significant weight as Márquez faces his historical rival in his first 'Clásico' as a head coach. While the veteran strategist experienced both triumphs and controversial dismissals as a player in 14 previous matchups against the Americans, he must now rely on his starting lineup to break the team's stalemate. The availability of Gil Mora, who was cleared after recovering from a recent injury, provides a tactical boost for the Mexican side as they look to challenge a surging United States team.

According to Mediotiempo, the game is scheduled for 20:00 local time. The historic rivalry dates back decades, with Márquez having been part of iconic moments such as the 2011 Gold Cup final comeback, where Mexico overcame a 0-2 deficit to win 4-2. The match will be broadcast across multiple channels including TUDN, Canal 5, and Azteca 7, with digital options available through Claro Sports and Amazon Prime Video. Fans will be watching to see if Márquez can navigate his squad to a win or if the United States continues their dominant scoring streak.
