Glastonbury 2027 tickets go on sale this week after festival hiatus

By Karim Fouad · Published 04 Oct 2026, 07:00 UTC

Tickets for the five-day Glastonbury festival go on sale starting Thursday evening for coach packages and Sunday morning for general admission.

Quick facts

Coach and ticket sale
Thursday, 18:00 BST
General admission sale
Sunday, 09:00
Ticket limit
6 per transaction
Payment window
5 minutes

After a one-year break in 2026, thousands of festival-goers are preparing to compete for tickets to the upcoming 2027 five-day event. To maximize your chances of success, you can book up to six tickets per transaction, and the most effective strategy involves splitting into groups of six who are all logged on to the official website before the sale begins at 9:00 on Sunday. Joining the waiting room earlier than the advertised time may improve your standing, even though the queue system is randomized. Conversely, logging on late will place you at the back, significantly reducing your opportunity to secure a spot. Once you reach the booking page, you will have a strict five-minute window to complete your purchase, making it essential to have payment information and authentication methods, such as banking apps or two-factor text codes, ready for immediate use. Avoid using multiple tabs or devices, as the festival website may interpret this activity as a bot, potentially banning you from the site or kicking you out of the queue entirely. Ensure that you and everyone in your group have sufficient funds available to place a deposit for each ticket requested. Coach and ticket packages launch at 18:00 BST this Thursday, followed by general admission tickets on Sunday.
