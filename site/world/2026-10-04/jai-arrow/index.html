Stars unite for emotional Jai Arrow tribute at NRL Grand Final show

By Karim Fouad · Published 04 Oct 2026, 08:30 UTC

Australian music artists will perform a tribute to Jai Arrow and Nathan Charles during the NRL Grand Final pre-game show on Sunday night.

Quick facts

Tribute Recipient
Jai Arrow and Nathan Charles
Performance Song
You'll Never Walk Alone
Key Performers
Amy Shark, The Screaming Jets, Grinspoon, Teen Jesus and the Jean Teasers

An emotional rendition of Gerry and the Pacemakers' anthem You'll Never Walk Alone will serve as the centerpiece of Sunday night's pre-game entertainment. The performance honors Jai Arrow, who has been supported by the rugby league community since announcing his motor neurone disease diagnosis earlier this year. Arrow, a former forward for the Rabbitohs, Broncos, and Titans, was recently awarded the Provan-Summons Medal at the 2026 Dally M Awards for his spirit.

This tribute carries deep significance for both the rugby league and cross-code communities, as former Wallaby Nathan Charles will also be honored alongside Arrow. Charles, who revealed his own motor neurone disease diagnosis during the week, will unite with his cross-code rival for the performance. The NRL's decision to focus on these figures highlights a pivot toward community-centered recognition during the season's final event.

According to Fox Sports, the pre-game lineup features pop star Amy Shark, who is joined by members of The Screaming Jets, Grinspoon, and Teen Jesus and the Jean Teasers. While rumors previously suggested an appearance by electronic duo Peking Duk, the pair confirmed on Instagram that they were never contacted to perform. Instead, the artists will gather to support Arrow, who has been a central figure in the sport's recent efforts to raise awareness for the condition. NRL boss Peter V'Landys confirmed the details of the tribute on 2GB, emphasizing the collaborative nature of the performance as the league marks its grand finale.
