James Tedesco and Kalyn Ponga clash in NRL grand final showdown

By Karim Fouad · Published 04 Oct 2026, 09:30 UTC

Fullbacks James Tedesco and Kalyn Ponga lead their respective teams into the NRL grand final this Sunday at Stadium Australia.

James Tedesco’s path to the NRL grand final stands in stark contrast to his counterpart’s early trajectory. While Kalyn Ponga entered the league with immediate, widespread acclaim after viral highlights and a high-profile debut, the 33-year-old Tedesco endured a far more arduous start. During his 2012 debut with the Wests Tigers, Tedesco suffered a devastating knee injury just 30 minutes into the match, requiring him to be stretchered off in tears. This incident initiated a grueling three-season period defined by injury setbacks and persistent doubts from critics, which he ultimately overcame to secure his status as an every-week first-grade player. According to ABC News, the two captains now meet at Stadium Australia, representing vastly different journeys to the peak of the sport.

The upcoming match is significant as it pits these two distinct career narratives against one another on the biggest stage in rugby league. For supporters, the final serves as a definitive test of two different styles of leadership and development. While Ponga’s career was propelled by early expectations and natural talent, Tedesco’s rise is defined by his persistence in silencing naysayers and rebounding from early-career medical trauma.

Reports from ABC News confirm that both players are confirmed to lead their teams this Sunday. Meanwhile, in related news, Newcastle player Dane Gagai has been cleared to join Ponga in the New Zealand international squad following an IRL appeals committee ruling. Despite previously representing the Kangaroos, Gagai received support from ARLC executive chairman Peter V’landys and coach Kevin Walters to make the switch, as officials noted special circumstances for players not recently capped by Australia.
