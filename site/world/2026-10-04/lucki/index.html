Rapper Lucki Bloodied Following Backstage Altercation At Los Angeles ComplexCon Event

By Karim Fouad · Published 04 Oct 2026, 07:31 UTC

Lucki suffered an apparent injury after an alleged fight involving associates of Playboi Carti during the Saturday event.

Quick facts

Location
ComplexCon, Los Angeles Convention Center
Day
Saturday
Involved parties
Lucki, Playboi Carti and associates

Video footage shows Lucki attempting to enter a vehicle with blood visible on his neck and face after an alleged physical clash behind the scenes at ComplexCon. While initial reports on social media suggest the rapper may have been stabbed or slashed, the precise nature of the wound remains unconfirmed at this time. The altercation reportedly involved the Chicago artist and individuals associated with Playboi Carti’s entourage. One circulated video depicts Playboi Carti standing nearby while the confrontation unfolds, though there is no evidence that he personally participated in or directed the alleged attack against Lucki. AllHipHop reports that their correspondent witnessed the incident, which occurred on Saturday at the Los Angeles Convention Center. This event, currently celebrating its 10th anniversary, features Carti as an artistic director alongside designer Hiroshi Fujiwara. The clash has revived public attention regarding a previously reported long-standing personal beef between Lucki and Carti. As of now, there is no official update regarding the physical condition of the Chicago rapper or a definitive account of what specifically triggered the fight. ComplexCon continues its programming through Sunday, with Playboi Carti still scheduled to headline a concert performance at the Crypto.com Arena featuring other members of the Opium collective.
