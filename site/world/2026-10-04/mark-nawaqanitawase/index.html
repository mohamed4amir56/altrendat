Mark Nawaqanitawase eyes NRL return after two-year rugby stint in Japan

By Karim Fouad · Published 04 Oct 2026, 09:01 UTC

Roosters winger Mark Nawaqanitawase will depart the NRL following Sunday's grand final against the Newcastle Knights to begin a two-year contract with the Saitama Wild Knights.

More than 70 million people watched the winger's spectacular Try of the Year against the Bulldogs last season, cementing his reputation as one of the most watchable athletes in the game. That highlight-reel ability has now sparked serious discussions regarding his future, with the 26-year-old entertaining a potential comeback to rugby league once his time in Japan concludes. A secret meeting held last Friday in Brisbane between Roosters chairman Nick Politis and Nawaqanitawase's management confirmed the player has spent the last six weeks considering a return to the club that provided him a home over the past two years. His mother, Fiona, expressed strong support for a potential homecoming, praising the way the Roosters treated their players and families during his time at the club.

While his future beyond Japan remains a possibility, Nawaqanitawase must first complete his final appearance for the Sydney Roosters this Sunday at Accor Stadium. Wallabies coach Les Kiss, who is closely monitoring Nawaqanitawase, warned that no players are guaranteed selection upon their return to rugby union. Despite high expectations for his potential role in next year's Rugby World Cup in Australia, the coach emphasized that every athlete must earn their spot through hard work rather than relying on their previous league achievements.

Nawaqanitawase has already indicated he is unlikely to participate in the upcoming Nations Championship Tests in November. Instead, he plans to spend the following month transitioning before formally starting with his new Japanese club. This departure marks the end of a two-year stint in the NRL, during which Nawaqanitawase established himself as an elite playmaker capable of defying standard defensive efforts on the field.
