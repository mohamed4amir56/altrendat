Natalia Silva Defeats Wang Cong to Clinch Vacant UFC Flyweight Championship Title

By Karim Fouad · Published 04 Oct 2026, 05:01 UTC

Natalia Silva captured the vacant UFC women’s flyweight title with a unanimous decision victory over Wang Cong at UFC 332 on Saturday.

Natalia Silva extended her undefeated UFC record to 9-0 after a masterful five-round performance, securing the flyweight belt by unanimous decision with judge scores of 48-47, 48-47, and 49-46. The victory marks her 14th consecutive win, cementing her status as the fifth champion in the history of the 125-pound division.

This title victory represents a significant milestone for Silva, who utilized her varied striking and movement to dictate the pace throughout the night. By overcoming Wang Cong at the Delta Center, Silva ascends to the top of the flyweight rankings. For the division, the result fills the vacancy left by former champion Valentina Shevchenko, setting a new direction for the women’s 125-pound weight class.

According to UFC.com, Silva maintained an early lead by frustrating her opponent over the first two rounds. Although Wang attempted a comeback in the championship rounds and landed notable shots in the final minutes of the fifth, Silva remained in control. MMA Fighting reports that the main event capped off a high-action card in Salt Lake City that featured a total of 11 knockouts throughout the evening. Following her win, Silva stands as the fourth woman to hold this specific championship belt.
