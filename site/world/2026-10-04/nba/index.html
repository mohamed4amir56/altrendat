Giannis Antetokounmpo Storms Into Miami Debut With 129-105 Preseason Blowout Rout

By Karim Fouad · Published 04 Oct 2026, 04:00 UTC

Giannis Antetokounmpo scored 9 points and 7 assists in his first appearance for the Miami Heat during a 129-105 preseason victory against the Toronto Raptors.

Quick facts

Final Score
Miami 129, Toronto 105
Location
Centre Vidéotron, Québec City
Antetokounmpo Stats
9 points, 7 assists, 4 rebounds

Giannis Antetokounmpo made an immediate impact in his first game as a member of the Miami Heat, playing 14 minutes and flirting with a double-double before resting for the entire second half. His presence was felt early as he opened the game by setting up Andrew Wiggins for a three-pointer, establishing a rhythm that saw the Heat surge to a 20-point lead by halftime. Antetokounmpo finished his limited outing with 9 points, 4 rebounds, and 7 assists, showing perfect 3-for-3 shooting from the field.

This performance offers the first glimpse of how the Heat intend to utilize their new-look roster, which underwent a major transformation over the summer. By pairing the size of Antetokounmpo with established shooters like Klay Thompson and Tim Hardaway Jr., Miami aims to create a dynamic offense that can either play physically in the paint or rely on perimeter spacing. For the Heat, the preseason is a vital window to integrate these high-profile additions and evaluate depth players like Pelle Larsson and Ryan Conwell while preparing for the 2026-2027 season.

According to Yahoo Sports, the game held in Québec City served as the official debut for Antetokounmpo following his trade from the Milwaukee Bucks. While Miami cruised to the 129-105 win, the matchup was also part of the NBA Canada Series. The game highlighted a tactical shift for the Heat, as they utilized their frontcourt size to dictate play. Raptors HQ notes that the Raptors countered with the defensive athleticism of Scottie Barnes and Kawhi Leonard, though the Heat's depth and size ultimately proved too much in the exhibition opener.
