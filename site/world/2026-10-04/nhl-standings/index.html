Novak Snaps Overtime Winner to Topple Canadiens in Pittsburgh Thriller

By Karim Fouad · Published 04 Oct 2026, 06:01 UTC

Tommy Novak scored his second goal of the game at 1:33 of overtime to lead the Pittsburgh Penguins to a 6-5 victory over the Montreal Canadiens on Saturday night.

Tommy Novak proved to be the difference-maker as the Pittsburgh Penguins narrowly defeated the Montreal Canadiens in a back-and-forth overtime contest on Saturday. Novak secured the win by converting a 2-on-1 feed from Evgeni Malkin just 1:33 into the extra period, capping off a dramatic comeback for the home side. The Penguins were forced to recover after squandering a four-goal lead earlier in the second period, a lapse that allowed Montreal to climb back into the game and force the game beyond regulation time.

For the Penguins, the win marks a significant recovery after allowing their multi-goal advantage to slip away under pressure from the Canadiens. The result is a critical demonstration of resilience for Pittsburgh early in the season. For Montreal, the loss highlights defensive vulnerabilities in the second period that allowed the Penguins to regain momentum despite the Canadiens' efforts to level the score.

According to TSN, the overtime sequence began after both teams pushed hard through the final stages of regulation. The Penguins' ability to regain their composure following Montreal's mid-game surge served as the catalyst for the final goal. Both clubs had entered the matchup with high expectations, with Montreal coaching staff noting the importance of preparing for the elite level of play displayed by the Pittsburgh roster.

Looking ahead, teams are continuing to evaluate their rosters and early-season performance trends. The Canadiens are assessing lineup adjustments, including discussions regarding the role of Alex Newhook, while other teams across the NHL navigate injury concerns, such as the Florida Panthers monitoring an injury to Aleksander Barkov.
