Prospect Payton Talbott Stuns Deiveson Figueiredo With Early Straight Right Hand

By Karim Fouad · Published 04 Oct 2026, 04:00 UTC

Rising bantamweight contender Payton Talbott clashed with former flyweight champion Deiveson Figueiredo in the UFC 332 co-main event at the Delta Center.

Quick facts

Event
UFC 332
Location
Delta Center, Salt Lake City
Division
Bantamweight

In the opening round of their highly anticipated bantamweight bout, Payton Talbott wasted no time establishing his striking dominance by catching veteran Deiveson Figueiredo with a sharp, straight right hand that sent the former champion reeling. This aggressive start by the 11-1 prospect underscored the significant stakes of the matchup, as Figueiredo entered the cage seeking to regain his championship form following consecutive losses to Umar Nurmagomedov and Song Yadong. The Salt Lake City crowd watched as the younger, taller Talbott applied consistent pressure immediately after the opening bell, living up to his status as the odds-on favorite.

For the 38-year-old Figueiredo, this bout represented a crucial crossroads in a career currently marked by a difficult stretch of losses. While former champions and peers such as Henry Cejudo had praised Talbott’s length and superior distance recognition, high-profile fighters like Dustin Poirier had publicly backed the veteran to overcome the surging prospect. The clash at the Delta Center was widely viewed as a pivotal moment for Talbott, who aimed to secure a top-five position in the division with a statement victory against an established name.

The co-main event, officiated by Jason Herzog, drew intense attention from the MMA community due to the striking contrast in the careers of the two athletes. Talbott entered the evening with momentum from recent decision wins over Felipe Lima and Henry Cejudo, while Figueiredo attempted to shake off a recent submission loss. The fight remained the focus of heated analysis, with peers like Chris Weidman and Jorge Masvidal weighing in on the likelihood of a breakthrough win for the younger fighter against the experienced former titleholder.
