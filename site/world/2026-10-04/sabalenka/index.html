Nikola Bartunkova Stuns World No. 2 Aryna Sabalenka at China Open

By Karim Fouad · Published 04 Oct 2026, 06:01 UTC

Nikola Bartunkova defeated second-seeded Aryna Sabalenka 6-4, 6-3 in the third round of the Beijing WTA 1000 event on Sunday.

Quick facts

Match Score
6-4, 6-3
Match Duration
1 hour, 35 minutes
Tournament Stage
Third round
Location
Beijing

Nikola Bartunkova delivered the biggest win of her career by systematically dismantling world No. 2 Aryna Sabalenka in just 1 hour and 35 minutes. The 26th-seeded Czech player dominated throughout the match, notably saving five break points in the sixth game of the first set to protect her early lead. By the second set, Bartunkova's momentum grew as she faced zero break points, eventually forcing Sabalenka to the point of throwing her racket onto the court in frustration during the fourth game.

This upset holds significant weight for the rankings and the progression of the WTA 1000 tournament in Beijing. With world No. 1 Elena Rybakina already eliminated from the competition, the exit of the second seed marks a major shift in the bracket. Bartunkova’s performance confirms her rising status, as this victory represents only the second time she has reached the round of 16 at a WTA 1000 level event, matching her previous success in Rome.

According to WTA Tennis, Sabalenka struggled significantly under pressure, failing to convert any of the five break points she earned in the opening set. Bartunkova, meanwhile, remained composed throughout the match, breaking her opponent twice in the second set to finalize a straight-sets victory. This win marks the second time in her career that Bartunkova has defeated a top-10 player, with her only prior success coming against Belinda Bencic at the Australian Open.

Looking ahead, Bartunkova moves forward to face Austrian Sinja Kraus in the fourth round. Kraus secured her own path to the next stage by defeating Dayana Yastremska in straight sets, 6-1, 6-4.
