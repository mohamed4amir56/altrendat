Teen prodigies Cavan Sullivan and Gilberto Mora headline USA versus Mexico clash

By Karim Fouad · Published 04 Oct 2026, 04:00 UTC

Seventeen-year-olds Cavan Sullivan and Gilberto Mora lead a new era in the USMNT and Mexico rivalry tonight in Glendale.

Quick facts

Location
Glendale, Arizona
Time
10pm ET
Key Players
Cavan Sullivan and Gilberto Mora

The USMNT and Mexico are set for another intense encounter as the latest generation takes center stage. While 17-year-old phenoms Cavan Sullivan and Gilberto Mora represent the future of this historic rivalry, the stakes remain as high as ever for the senior squads. Sullivan joins his first senior camp with the U.S. while Mora has already featured for El Tri at the World Cup level. Their emergence marks a significant transition for both national teams as they look toward the next decade of competition.

This match serves as a crucial test for the U.S. side and coach Mauricio Pochettino. Following a painful loss in the 2025 Gold Cup finale, Pochettino remains focused on asserting the team's presence on home soil. Despite the rising level of collaboration between MLS and Liga MX in club-level tournaments, the intensity remains unchanged when these nations meet. For the players, the match carries significant emotional weight, with veterans like Antonee Robinson emphasizing that regardless of the friendly status, the hostility and the desire to win remain constant.

Hostility is expected to be a staple of this rivalry, as matches have frequently been characterized by heated collisions and emotional confrontations. With Javier Aguirre replaced by Rafa Marquez, the Mexican side enters this collision with a refreshed identity under a legendary figure. The atmosphere is expected to be overwhelmingly pro-Mexico even in the United States, a factor that USMNT staff and players are preparing for as they navigate this new era of competition. The match in Arizona marks the first head-to-head meeting of this transition period for both coaching staffs.
