AFL Trade Period Starts With Star Pursuits And Pick Swap Deals

By Karim Fouad · Published 05 Oct 2026, 04:00 UTC · Updated 05 Oct 2026, 05:01 UTC

The 2026 Continental Tyres AFL Trade Period officially opened today with clubs across the league actively negotiating player movements and draft selections.

The activity holds significant implications for team rosters as clubs look to refine their lists before the upcoming season. For fans and stakeholders, the period serves as a critical window where personnel decisions and pick swaps directly impact the competitive outlook of teams across the league.

Recent reports indicate several high-profile negotiations are underway. According to Fox Sports, St Kilda is seeking a first-round pick and a player from Geelong in exchange for 31-year-old ruckman Rowan Marshall. Meanwhile, Gryan Miers has declined a move to West Coast despite completing a medical, and Jayden Short is reportedly interested in joining Collingwood. Elsewhere, Port Adelaide is exploring pick swap options to maximize the return on Zak Butters' potential move to the Western Bulldogs, with the club considering a compensation package involving pick 5. Fremantle has also been identified as a team interested in Lawson Humphries.

Negotiations are expected to intensify throughout the week. Clubs are actively working to finalize trade details and manage draft assets as the deadline approaches, with official announcements regarding player signatures and pick movements anticipated as the Trade Period continues.
