Braves seize momentum as bullpen masterclass topples Dodgers in Game 2

By Karim Fouad · Published 05 Oct 2026, 04:00 UTC

The Atlanta Braves tied their NLDS series at 1-1 by defeating the Los Angeles Dodgers 3-2 on Sunday behind a six-pitcher bullpen effort.

The Braves chased two-time Cy Young winner Blake Snell after only 3 2/3 innings, effectively dismantling the Los Angeles starter before relying on their own defensive depth to hold the lead. While the Dodgers were limited to just two runs, a late solo home run by Max Muncy in the ninth inning provided the only major resistance against the Atlanta bullpen in the final frame. The victory at Dodger Stadium marked a significant shift in the series, as Atlanta successfully reclaimed home-field advantage from the reigning World Series champions. The strategic win relied heavily on a collective pitching performance featuring six different arms, including Ray Kerr, who delivered 2 2/3 scoreless innings, and Didier Fuentes, who managed three perfect innings starting in the sixth. This balanced approach allowed the Braves to silence the Dodgers' offense for the majority of the night, setting a competitive tone for the upcoming matchups. As the series moves to Atlanta, the Braves are positioned to capitalize on their newfound momentum. With the series now tied, ace Chris Sale is expected to take the mound for the Braves in Game 3 on Tuesday at Truist Park. The Dodgers, meanwhile, look to bounce back in the critical road environment with Yoshinobu Yamamoto set to pitch in the third game of the best-of-five series.
