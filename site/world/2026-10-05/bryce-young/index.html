Bryce Young Sizzles With 11 Straight Completions To Topple Lions 32-26

By Karim Fouad · Published 05 Oct 2026, 07:30 UTC

Bryce Young threw for 329 yards and two touchdowns on Sunday night to lead the Panthers past the Lions.

Bryce Young opened the game with 11 consecutive completions, marking the longest streak to start a game in his professional career. This high-efficiency start powered the Carolina Panthers to a 32-26 victory over the Detroit Lions on Sunday night, overcoming a roster heavily impacted by injuries throughout the week.

For the Panthers, the win represents a critical performance fueled by a balanced offensive attack. Bryce Young connected with Tetairoa McMillan for two touchdowns, while teammate Chuba Hubbard delivered a season-high 122 rushing yards on 20 carries, crossing the goal line twice. This offensive output proved vital in navigating the team through a challenging week of physical setbacks.

According to TSN, Young finished the game completing 29 of 41 pass attempts for his 329-yard total. On the other side, Pride of Detroit reports that the Lions struggled with discipline, committing nine penalties throughout the contest—seven of which occurred on the offensive side of the ball. The Lions faced significant hurdles during a pivotal fourth-quarter drive, where consecutive delay of game and false start penalties forced the offense into a difficult fourth-and-20 situation. Despite a desperate attempt at a hook-and-ladder play, the Lions failed to convert, effectively ending their comeback hopes.

With this result, the Detroit Lions fall to a 2-2 record after the first month of the season. The team now enters a period of evaluation as they address defensive performance and recurring penalty issues that hindered their ability to sustain drives against Carolina.
