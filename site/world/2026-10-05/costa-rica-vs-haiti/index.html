Costa Rica battles Haiti Sunday to clinch Nations League Category A spot

By Karim Fouad · Published 05 Oct 2026, 04:00 UTC

Costa Rica hosts Haiti this Sunday at Estadio Ricardo Saprissa to secure its position in the Concacaf Nations League Category A.

Costa Rica faces a high-stakes showdown this Sunday at 6 p.m. at Estadio Ricardo Saprissa Aymá, with the national team fighting to retain its place in Category A of the Concacaf Nations League. The match marks the final game of the group stage, serving as a critical hurdle for interim coach Bryan Ruiz’s squad.

Securing a result is essential for Costa Rica to maintain its status within the top tier of regional competition. For Haiti, the stakes are equally significant, as the visiting side aims to capture the top spot in Group 1, a position that would grant them a path to face Panama in the next stage of the tournament.

Interim head coach Bryan Ruiz has made tactical adjustments to his roster to compensate for player injuries and the suspension of Jossimar, who is sidelined due to yellow card accumulation. Ruiz confirmed that Mayron George has been called up as a direct replacement for the attacking vacancy. Additionally, Anthony “Pikachu” Hernández returns to the fold after recovering from a previous injury, with Ruiz noting that Hernández is fully fit and capable of providing significant impact on the wing. Experienced left-back Ronald Matarrita also rejoins the selection, drawing praise from Ruiz for his consistent performance in his position during recent matches.

The defensive execution will be under scrutiny following previous matches where set-piece vulnerabilities proved costly. The match will be broadcast live across various platforms, including Teletica 7 and Canal 6 Repretel in Costa Rica, while viewers in the United States can follow the action via Fox Sports 2, TUDN, and FOX One. In Mexico and South America, coverage will be available through the official CONCACAF YouTube channel.
