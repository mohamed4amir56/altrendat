Flávio Bolsonaro and Lula face off in high-stakes presidential runoff vote

By Karim Fouad · Published 05 Oct 2026, 05:00 UTC

Senator Flávio Bolsonaro and President Lula will compete in a second-round presidential runoff on October 25.

Quick facts

Election date
October 25
Candidates
Flávio Bolsonaro and Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva
Final vote date
January 5, 2027
Official Body
Tribunal Superior Eleitoral

More than 56 million voters cast their ballots for Flávio Bolsonaro, while Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva secured over 53.7 million votes, according to the Tribunal Superior Eleitoral. The official results confirmed the runoff late Sunday evening as the nation prepares to choose between two vastly different political paths. With 96.78% of the urns counted, Bolsonaro maintained a lead with 47.07% of the vote against Lula's 45.12%, leaving the final outcome to be determined in three weeks. This contest marks a significant moment for the country, as it finalizes the direction for the next four years of governance following a campaign characterized by deep polarization. Voters will return to the polls on October 25 to decide the winner of the executive seat. If successful, Flávio Bolsonaro would take office at age 45 on January 5, 2027, succeeding the role previously held by his father. Conversely, a victory for Lula would secure him a fourth term at age 81, making him the first Brazilian to preside over the country four times. The electoral commission expects the final count to provide the definitive measure of national support for each candidate. As the campaigns shift focus toward the final round, the results depend on the choices of millions of citizens who will decide whether to maintain the current administration or transition to new leadership. All official tallies are provided by the Tribunal Superior Eleitoral as the voting districts continue to process the ballots.
