Deonte Banks 98-Yard Pick-Six Vaults Giants Into First Place NFC East

By Karim Fouad · Published 05 Oct 2026, 08:30 UTC

The New York Giants secured a 36-24 victory over the Arizona Cardinals on Sunday to claim sole possession of the NFC East lead with a 3-1 record.

Quick facts

Final Score
New York Giants 36, Arizona Cardinals 24
Date
October 4, 2026
Team Record
3-1
Key Play
98-yard interception return for touchdown by Deonte Banks

Deonte Banks sealed the win in dramatic fashion during the final play of the game at MetLife Stadium, jumping a route to intercept Jacoby Brissett on fourth-and-goal at the four-yard line. Banks returned the interception 98 yards for a touchdown, turning a narrow defensive stop into a 12-point margin victory. This performance helped the Giants overcome an early 14-6 deficit and ended a nine-plus quarter touchdown drought for the offense.

The win carries significant weight for the Giants, as it vaults the team into sole possession of first place in the NFC East for the first time since 2015. With the Philadelphia Eagles falling to the Los Angeles Rams, the Giants now sit atop the division standings at 3-1, moving ahead of both the Eagles and the Dallas Cowboys, who each hold 2-2 records.

According to CBS Sports, Jameis Winston finished the day 18 of 29 for 250 yards, throwing three touchdown passes to Malachi Fields, Malik Nabers, and Theo Johnson. Despite throwing two interceptions, Winston successfully stepped in for injured starter Jaxson Dart. Malik Nabers was a standout for the offense, recording 112 yards on six catches, more than doubling his total receiving yardage for the season. J.J. McCarthy, acquired in a trade with Minnesota six days prior, dressed for the game but did not participate, as coach John Harbaugh noted he was not close to entering the contest.

The Giants now look to maintain their position as they build on their two-game winning streak following their Week 2 loss to the Los Angeles Rams.
