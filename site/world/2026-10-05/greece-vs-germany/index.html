Germany dominates possession but settles for frustrating goalless draw in Greece

By Karim Fouad · Published 05 Oct 2026, 04:00 UTC

Germany played to a 0-0 stalemate against Greece in their Nations League match at Toumba Stadium.

Germany controlled the ball with 70.1% possession and attempted 21 shots, yet failed to secure a victory during their Nations League trip to Thessaloniki. The visitors completed 640 passes compared to Greece's 267, but the team's lack of verticality in the final third left the scoreline deadlocked at 0-0. A first-half strike from Jonathan Burkhardt was ruled out by the video assistant referee following a handball by Tom Bischof, marking a frustrating end to the international window for new head coach Jurgen Klopp. Despite the territorial dominance, Germany tested the Greek goalkeeper only four times throughout the match.

The result leaves Germany third in Group A2, sitting three points behind leaders the Netherlands and unbeaten Greece. For Germany captain Florian Wirtz, the inability to convert pressure into a decisive goal was a source of frustration, though he noted the squad has only been together for a week. The match featured few clinical moments, with Greece also struggling to find the net despite early attempts from Fotis Ioannidis and later efforts by Konstantinos Mavropanos and Giorgos Masouras.

Under Jurgen Klopp's leadership, Germany has now managed one win from their opening four matches. The coach acknowledged the team's need for growth while defending his tactical decisions and player selections. Germany's defense remained largely untroubled, with goalkeeper Jonas Urbig making two saves to preserve the clean sheet, although defenders Malick Thiaw and others were cautioned for tactical challenges as the game progressed into a one-sided possession exercise. Germany currently holds third place in the group standings as they look ahead to future international fixtures.
