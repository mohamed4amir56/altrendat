1-1 Nations League draw between Republic of Ireland and Israel turns hostile

By Karim Fouad · Published 05 Oct 2026, 05:30 UTC

Republic of Ireland and Israel played a tense 1-1 Nations League draw in Serbia marred by allegations of spitting.

Quick facts

Result
1-1
Venue
TSC Arena, Serbia
Ireland Goal Scorer
Troy Parrott
Israel Goal Scorer
Dor Peretz

A voice on television broadcast was clearly audible during the tunnel walk at TSC Arena stating, "he spat on me," as Republic of Ireland staff member Damien Doyle alleged he was targeted by an Israel player during the half-time break. The 1-1 stalemate in the Nations League Group B3 match in Serbia, held behind closed doors, served as the backdrop for a series of heated confrontations between the two squads.

Manager Heimir Hallgrímsson has publicly urged UEFA to prevent future matchups between the two nations after enduring two weeks of high-pressure fixtures. Hallgrímsson stated that his players were subjected to intimidation tactics from kickoff but managed to avoid disciplinary action by maintaining their composure despite the antagonistic atmosphere. The Irish manager emphasized that no other national team should be forced to undergo the difficult circumstances his squad encountered during this international window.

On the pitch, Troy Parrott secured an early lead for the Republic of Ireland by converting a penalty in the 39th minute, marking his 10th goal in seven competitive international appearances. The lead held until the 47th minute, when Israel’s Dor Peretz equalized to level the match. Israel coach Ran Ben-Shimon denied witnessing the half-time incident, noting that he was in his dressing room with his staff when the reported confrontation occurred.

Tensions between the sides remained high throughout the lead-up to the match, with a pre-match press conference cut short on Saturday due to what Hallgrímsson described as a verbal attack from media representatives. Both teams wore black armbands during the encounter as a mark of respect for those killed in the October 7, 2023, attacks.
