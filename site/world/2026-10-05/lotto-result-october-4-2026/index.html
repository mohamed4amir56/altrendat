The Philippine Charity Sweepstakes Office held the Superlotto 6/49 and Ultra Lotto 6/58 draws on Sunday, October 4, 2026.

The 9 p.m. draw on Sunday marks the latest round of official lottery results released by the Philippine Charity Sweepstakes Office. Players across the nation tuned in as the agency revealed the winning combinations for its primary evening games.

These results are essential for participants of the Superlotto 6/49 and Ultra Lotto 6/58 games who must verify their tickets against the official numbers drawn by the PCSO. Verification ensures that winners can claim their prizes according to the established rules and timelines set by the organization.

According to ABS-CBN and The Manila Times, the draws conducted on October 4, 2026, were part of the standard nightly operation of the Philippine Charity Sweepstakes Office. Both sources confirmed that the proceedings followed the scheduled 9 p.m. time slot, providing the necessary data for all ticket holders waiting for the outcome of these specific draws.

Participants should check their physical tickets carefully against the official PCSO results to confirm if they hold a winning combination. Prizes must be claimed following the guidelines provided by the Philippine Charity Sweepstakes Office, which serves as the sole governing body for these lottery games in the Philippines. Winners are encouraged to verify their results through official channels to ensure accuracy before visiting any local agency branch.
