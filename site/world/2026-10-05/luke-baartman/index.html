Baartman forced off with injury in South Africa U23's 1-1 draw with Algeria

By Karim Fouad · Published 05 Oct 2026, 09:00 UTC

Kaizer Chiefs attacker Luke Baartman was substituted during the first half of South Africa U23's international friendly against Algeria on Sunday due to a knock, leaving the club concerned ahead of upcoming fixtures.

Luke Baartman's breakthrough season at Kaizer Chiefs hit a setback when the 20-year-old was forced off the pitch near the end of the first half during South Africa U23's match against Algeria on Sunday afternoon. The midfielder appeared in significant discomfort before the medical team signaled for his immediate removal, with Stellenbosch FC striker Quwan Plaatjies brought on as his replacement.

The injury comes at a concerning time for Kaizer Chiefs, who are already dealing with growing fitness worries in their squad. The loss of Baartman ahead of the club's upcoming commitments from next weekend adds pressure to their medical staff as they prepare for competitive matches.

The South Africa U23 side, managed by interim boss Karabo Mogudi in place of head coach Raymond Mdaka, finished the match in a 1-1 draw. South Africa found the net through Kutlwano Letlhaku's work on the left flank in the 34th minute, with an Algerian defender deflecting the ball into his own net. Algeria equalized shortly after when Benahmed Kohili capitalized on a defensive lapse.

Baartman had established himself as a key player for Kaizer Chiefs this season, featuring in eight matches across all competitions. The young attacker was called up to the U23 squad for the Algeria friendlies after missing out on previous national team opportunities due to injury.
