Lauren Hemp scores twice as Manchester City rally to beat Arsenal 4-2

By Karim Fouad · Published 05 Oct 2026, 10:30 UTC

Reigning champions Manchester City defeat Arsenal 4-2 at Etihad Stadium to move 10 points clear at the top of the table.

Quick facts

Final Score
Manchester City 4-2 Arsenal
Points clear
10 points ahead of Arsenal

Lauren Hemp scored twice as Manchester City stormed back from a deficit to beat Arsenal 4-2 at Etihad Stadium. The victory extends the reigning champions' lead over Arsenal to 10 points, cementing their position at the top of the table.

The comeback win comes at a crucial time for City, who bounced back after a challenging stretch of fixtures. The resilience shown in this match demonstrates the team's ability to compete at the highest level despite the demanding schedule they have faced recently.

Hem's two goals were the standout moments in a match that saw City flex their attacking muscle against one of their closest rivals. The victory underlines City's credentials as title contenders and their capacity to deliver when it matters most against top opposition. Chelsea midfielder Keira Walsh highlighted the importance of such performances, noting that the team needed to produce a good result during this difficult block of games.
