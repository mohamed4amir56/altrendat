Golden State Valkyries Clinch WNBA Semifinal Game 1 Win Over Las Vegas

By Karim Fouad · Published 05 Oct 2026, 04:00 UTC

The Golden State Valkyries defeated the Las Vegas Aces 71-60 on Sunday afternoon to take a 1-0 lead in the WNBA Semifinals.

Chelsea Gray cemented her place in league history during Sunday's matchup by becoming the WNBA's all-time postseason assists leader. Despite the milestone, the Aces fell 71-60 to the Valkyries in the Bay Area, trailing the series 0-1 as they struggled to maintain control on the boards against the hosts.

For fans and those following the league, this opening victory marks a critical start for the Valkyries in the pursuit of a semifinal series win. The game showcased top-tier talent, with A'ja Wilson recording 24 points for Las Vegas while Jackie Young added 11 points, 5 assists, and 3 steals in the losing effort.

The Las Vegas Aces are now focused on reclaiming their momentum as the WNBA Semifinals continue, while the Valkyries look to defend their current series advantage. Elsewhere in professional basketball, the New Orleans Pelicans have finalized their broadcast team for the upcoming season, appointing Kelly Crull and Kalia Butler to join veteran announcers Joel Meyers and Antonio Daniels. The Pelicans plan to broadcast 80 of their 82 regular-season games through the Gulf Coast Sports & Entertainment Network, with additional streaming options available on Amazon Prime and Pelicans+ for fans.

As the basketball calendar advances, the Golden State Warriors also remain in the spotlight following their October 4, 2026, contest against the LA Clippers. Teams are preparing for the remainder of their respective schedules as playoff intensity continues to rise on the court.
