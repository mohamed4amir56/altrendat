With six fresh faces already confirmed in the 23-man squad, the selection of the halves remains the final puzzle for the coaching staff to solve before the tournament gets underway. The buildup to the World Cup has been intense, following an NRL season that saw high-stakes drama and the emergence of several players who are now testing for international honors for the first time. The final decision rests on whether the selectors want to lean on established combinations or test the chemistry of players like Walker against the world's best.
