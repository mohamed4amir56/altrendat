Strathbogie Shire man claims $1.2 million TattsLotto jackpot after dentist appointment

By Karim Fouad · Published 05 Oct 2026, 08:01 UTC

A Victorian man discovered he won $1,247,390.16 in TattsLotto draw 4715 while driving home from the dentist on Saturday, September 26.

The winning ticket holder from Strathbogie Shire was still experiencing the lingering numbness from his dental procedure when he received the call that changed his life. He confirmed he was completely numb, jokingly clarifying it was not from the dentist, as he processed the news that his marked 10-game entry had secured him a $1,247,390.16 prize. The winner is one of five division one-winning entries from the September 26 draw, according to The Lott. For the winner, this windfall represents a significant shift in his financial reality. While he previously spent time imagining how he would spend lottery winnings, the sudden arrival of the $1.2 million sum left him feeling stumped. He plans to take a few weeks to let the news settle before deciding on his next steps, though he has already identified a desire to help his family and purchase a new four-wheel drive. The winning ticket was purchased at Euroa News and Lotto, located on Binney St, Euroa, approximately 160km northeast of Melbourne. Shop owner Lorraine Mawson expressed her excitement over the sale, noting that it is the first division one-winning entry the store has sold since 2018. The winning numbers for draw 4715 were 6, 25, 27, 2, 31, and 40, with supplementary numbers 24 and 35. The winner expressed plans to share the news with his wife via video to capture her reaction to the life-changing amount.
