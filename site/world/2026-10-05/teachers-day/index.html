President Marcos leads mass promotion for over 4,000 teachers and education staff

By Karim Fouad · Published 05 Oct 2026, 04:00 UTC

President Ferdinand Marcos Jr. celebrated National Teachers' Day on Monday by swearing in more than 4,000 promoted educators and personnel in Santiago, Isabela.

Quick facts

Event

Location

Promoted Personnel
Over 4,000

More than 4,000 education professionals received a significant career advancement as President Ferdinand Marcos Jr. led a mass oath-taking ceremony in Santiago, Isabela. This milestone event coincided with the national observance of Teachers' Day, highlighting the administration's focus on the development of the country's teaching workforce.

The promotions signify a vital step for the thousands of individuals involved, reflecting efforts to strengthen the education sector's leadership and capacity. By recognizing these professionals through a formal promotion process, the government aims to reinforce the dedication of educators who serve as the backbone of the national school system.

According to reports from GMA News Online, the President officiated the swearing-in ceremony to honor the service and commitment of the teaching staff and education personnel. This direct engagement with educators underscores the administration's ongoing investment in human capital within the public school system.

In a separate message marking National Teachers' Day, as noted by ABS-CBN, President Marcos issued a call to the broader teaching community to remain committed to their core mission. He urged educators to continue their work in shaping Filipinos to become discerning and principled citizens. This mandate emphasizes the long-term role teachers play in fostering national character and integrity among the youth of the Philippines.
