Temi Nkem Snatches ₦160 Million Grand Prize After 70 Days of Competition

By Karim Fouad · Published 05 Oct 2026, 04:00 UTC

The 21-year-old content creator secured victory in the Big Brother Naija Season 11 grand finale on Sunday after outlasting 23 other housemates.

Following 70 days of living under constant surveillance, Temi Nkem beat out 23 other housemates to emerge as the latest Big Brother Naija champion. The London-born contestant, known for her confidence and distinctive British accent, defeated runners-up Aikou and Sheba to clinch the top spot during the season's grand finale. Her rise to the top was marked by a memorable fake eviction moment where she calmly smiled and uttered the word “Chakam,” a scene that quickly became a fan favorite.

For the viewers and the broader Nigerian entertainment landscape, this victory signals the conclusion of a season that drew heavy attention for its unique “Show Ya Sef” format. According to Channels Television, the grand prize package is valued at ₦160 million. This reward includes ₦100 million in cash and a car valued at ₦60 million, a massive career-launching windfall for the former lifestyle content creator who entered the house back on July 26.

Reporting from the finale, Channels Television noted that the path to victory required Temi to survive multiple rounds of nominations and complex house tasks. She gained a crucial advantage in the final weeks when fellow contestant Barry utilized his Ultimate Veto Power to trigger an “Immunity for All” status, which effectively cleared the final housemates of the threat of elimination.

Fans of the winner, who have dubbed themselves “Teminators,” followed her journey closely as she navigated the house's unpredictable environment. The event concluded a long-running competition that initially saw 24 participants compete for the title. With the winner now officially crowned, the season effectively shutters its doors, ending the high-stakes battle that kept audiences tuned in for over two months of reality television.
