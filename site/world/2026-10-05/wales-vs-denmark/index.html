Rasmus Hojlund penalty fires Denmark to 1-0 Nations League victory over Wales

By Karim Fouad · Published 05 Oct 2026, 04:30 UTC

Wales suffered a 1-0 defeat to Denmark in Cardiff as Rasmus Hojlund scored the game's only goal from a 75th-minute penalty.

Quick facts

Final Score
Wales 0-1 Denmark
Goal Scorer
Rasmus Hojlund
Goal Time
75th minute
Venue
Cardiff City Stadium

Denmark forward Rasmus Hojlund coolly converted a penalty in the 75th minute to secure a 1-0 win, marking a dominant double for the visitors after their previous 2-0 victory in Copenhagen. The result leaves Craig Bellamy’s side with a third defeat in four Nations League fixtures, following a week where they also secured a win against Norway. The match hinged on a decisive second-half error when Wales goalkeeper Danny Ward gifted the ball to Gustav Isaksen inside the penalty area before fouling the forward to concede the spot-kick. Despite maintaining 54 per cent possession in the first half, the Welsh side struggled to break down the Danish defense and managed only a single shot on target throughout the entire match. For Denmark, the victory was achieved despite losing captain Pierre-Emile Hojbjerg to injury within the opening 10 minutes of play. Craig Bellamy’s team now faces the task of regrouping after failing to convert their periods of possession into significant attacking threats, with Brennan Johnson and Ethan Ampadu both failing to capitalize on limited opportunities during the contest. The loss marks a setback for Wales as they navigate a challenging Nations League campaign, while Denmark’s efficient performance ensures they maintain their momentum in Group A4. Bellamy opted for continuity in his starting lineup, making only one change from the side that defeated Norway, but the squad ultimately could not replicate the attacking sharpness required to overcome the Danish backline. The result reflects a clinical display from the visitors, who punished a rare defensive lapse to leave the Cardiff City Stadium with all three points.
