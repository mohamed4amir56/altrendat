Teachers join 250,000 public sector workers in nationwide work-to-rule protest

By Karim Fouad · Published 05 Oct 2026, 08:01 UTC · Updated 05 Oct 2026, 09:01 UTC

Primary and secondary school teachers began industrial action today, ending pre- and post-school supervision as part of a pay dispute.

Quick facts

250,000
Primary schools affected
3,300

Previous pay deal expiration
June 30th

Teachers across Ireland are pulling back from all duties outside official school hours, including pre- and post-school supervision, staff meetings, and formal parent-teacher conferences. This work-to-rule initiative, affecting over 3,300 primary schools, forms part of a broader standoff involving more than 250,000 public sector employees seeking a new national pay deal. The current dispute follows the expiration of the previous public sector agreement on June 30th.

The industrial action creates immediate logistical challenges for working parents, as insurance companies have advised that schools should restrict student access solely to core hours when no alternative supervision is available. Boards of management have been directed to conduct safety assessments to determine if school grounds remain secure without the additional monitoring previously provided during 'Croke Park' hours. While extracurricular sports are currently expected to continue, electronic communication and staff meetings are largely being suspended by union members.

INTO general secretary John Boyle stated that the action is a necessary response to declining real wages, noting that families will have to adjust to these new restrictions while the dispute continues. Unions are demanding that cost-of-living concerns be prioritized in any new negotiations. Meanwhile, Minister for Public Expenditure Jack Chambers has criticized the move, arguing that industrial action is unfair to the public given the government's expressed willingness to engage in talks.

The situation is set to escalate significantly next week, with a nationwide one-day strike scheduled for October 14th that is expected to close almost every school in the country. A second strike date is anticipated on October 21st.
