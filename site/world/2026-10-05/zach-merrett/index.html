Merrett ends standoff with Bombers, joins Hawks for picks 15 and 25

By Karim Fouad · Published 05 Oct 2026, 06:30 UTC

Zach Merrett, Essendon's former captain, will leave the club to join Hawthorn in exchange for draft picks 15, 25, and a 2028 second-rounder, ending a year-long contract dispute.

Essendon's president Andrew Welsh held firm through months of negotiations, warning Hawthorn not to "fumble" talks or get "too cute," insisting the Bombers would not be "crumbling" and reminding the rival club that Merrett was contracted and their best player. Last year's attempt to prise him away had failed. But one fact proved decisive: Merrett wanted out.

The midfielder's departure marks a turning point for a club that has cycled through coaches, executives, and strategies over two decades without the results to show for it. After holding Merrett to his deal in 2025, sacking a coach midway through the season, and finishing dead last, the Bombers chose to stop fighting. The deal closes a saga that consumed much of the AFL off-season, with both clubs aware that a resolution was inevitable once Merrett made clear his desire to leave.

Merrett's move to Hawthorn brings the Hawks prospect of adding a midfielder of elite pedigree while Essendon recoup draft capital. The picks acquired—15th and 25th in the current draft plus a future second-rounder—provide the Bombers ammunition for their own roster rebuild as they look to move forward under new direction.
