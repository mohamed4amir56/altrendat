Amazon Prime Big Deal Days Returns With Millions Of Seasonal Savings Events

By Karim Fouad · Published 06 Oct 2026, 11:00 UTC

Amazon Prime members can access millions of deals during the 48-hour Prime Big Deal Days event running October 6-7, 2026.

Quick facts

Event Name
Prime Big Deal Days
Dates
October 6-7, 2026
Event Duration
48 hours
Discount range
Up to 75% off

While Prime members prepare for the 48-hour event, consumers can find competing discounts of up to 75% off at other retailers, including Target, Walmart, and Nordstrom. These sales cover a wide range of categories, from tech and kitchen appliances to beauty and home goods, offering alternatives during the October shopping window.

For shoppers, this period serves as a strategic start to fall and holiday purchasing. By utilizing new AI shopping tools, Prime members can track and secure items from a vast selection, including Amazon devices, Kindle, and Amazon Music. Retailers outside of Amazon are also leveraging the high-traffic week to clear stock and attract customers with highly-rated items, many of which carry at least a 20 percent discount compared to original prices.

According to Amazon, the event features expert-curated lists, including over 100 toys aimed at simplifying holiday gifting. NBC News notes that other major retailers are simultaneously offering significant markdowns on products ranging from wireless earbuds with 30-hour battery life to lightweight carpet cleaners designed for pet messes and hardwood floors. These competing sales feature items with strong customer ratings, such as a coffee maker programmable up to 24 hours in advance and an instant-print camera suitable for travel.

As Prime Big Deal Days proceed through October 7, shoppers are encouraged to monitor retail platforms for newly added deals. Analysts and shopping editors continue to update lists of products that maintain high customer ratings to assist consumers in identifying items that provide actual value during the surge of seasonal sales.
