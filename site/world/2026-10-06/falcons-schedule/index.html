Falcons list two defensive starters as questionable for Monday Night Football clash

By Karim Fouad · Published 06 Oct 2026, 04:00 UTC

Atlanta Falcons defensive starters Divine Deablo and Samson Ebukam are both listed as questionable with hamstring injuries for Monday's game against the New Orleans Saints.

Samson Ebukam achieved full participation in practice this Friday for the first time since suffering his hamstring injury. This development follows his absence during the team's Week 3 victory against the Green Bay Packers. Meanwhile, fellow linebacker Divine Deablo has remained a limited participant in practice throughout the week while addressing his own hamstring issue. No player has been ruled out of Monday night's matchup at this time.

Monitoring these injury statuses is critical for fans and fantasy participants ahead of the prime-time game against the Saints. Defensive continuity serves as a vital factor for the Falcons as they prepare for the division rival. The team's injury and game status report, released Saturday, provides the clearest look at the defensive depth currently available for the coaching staff as kickoff approaches.

In addition to the defensive starters, the Falcons reported that linebacker Yasir Abdullah is also questionable due to an illness, although he was a full participant in Friday's practice. Several other players, including Jake Matthews, Za’Darius Smith, and Jawaan Taylor, were absent or resting for non-injury related reasons. Across the field, the New Orleans Saints confirmed that tight end Noah Fant will be inactive for the Week 4 contest due to an abdominal injury.

The game is set to take place on Monday night. Further updates will determine if the questionable starters will be cleared to suit up before the opening whistle.
