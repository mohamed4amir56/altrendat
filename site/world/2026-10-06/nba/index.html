Lakers open revamped roster against Kings with Kessler in focus

By Karim Fouad · Published 06 Oct 2026, 04:00 UTC · Updated 06 Oct 2026, 05:01 UTC

The Los Angeles Lakers begin their preseason Monday against the Sacramento Kings at Golden 1 Center, with new center Walker Kessler and young wings Cameron Carr and Adou Thiero getting significant playing time.

Los Angeles brought in seven new players this offseason to rebuild its roster depth, making Monday's preseason opener against Sacramento a chance to see how the pieces fit together. The Lakers signed Williams and Thybulle for their defensive prowess and three-point shooting, while Sexton will anchor the bench as a scoring option. Kessler, who the team acquired for two unprotected first-round picks and two pick swaps, will play limited minutes but offers the most intrigue as he pairs with stars Dončić and Reaves.

Monday's game matters because preseason provides the first look at how these acquisitions blend on the court. Star players like Dončić and Reaves will see limited minutes, typical for exhibition games, allowing the roster's new wing prospects and younger players to showcase their abilities. Carr and Thiero impressed during the 2026 NBA Summer League, averaging 18 and 15.4 points per game respectively, and both will get significant looks.

The Lakers are banking on their hybrid wings to defend and stretch the floor next to Dončić. The team also acquired Kessler, Looney, Mamukelashvili, Grimes, and Sexton, along with Williams and Thybulle, creating a nearly complete roster overhaul from the offseason. Kessler's rim protection and chemistry with the guards will be watched closely despite his expected limited playing time.
