Daniil Medvedev disqualified for hitting spectator during China Open semi-final against Djokovic

By Karim Fouad · Published 06 Oct 2026, 12:00 UTC

Daniil Medvedev was defaulted from his China Open semi-final match against Novak Djokovic on Monday after striking a spectator with a ball.

Quick facts

Event
China Open semi-final
Date
Monday

The incident occurred while Novak Djokovic was serving for a spot in the final, when Medvedev reacted to a ball ricocheting off the back of the court by swiping it into the stands. The ball struck a spectator directly in the face, leading to the disqualification of the Russian player just a few minutes later. Under tennis regulations, players are strictly barred from hitting a ball in a dangerous or reckless manner, or with negligent disregard for the consequences regarding the safety of officials, ball kids, and spectators. While Medvedev maintains that he is a calm individual off the court and that his tennis-related aggression fades quickly once he departs the venue, this disqualification marks the end of his run at the China Open. The BBC reports that the decision was deemed necessary given the nature of the action. Medvedev has a history of on-court outbursts, previously facing fines at the 2019 US Open for his treatment of a ball person and making offensive gestures toward fans. In a 2017 Wimbledon match, he also caused controversy by sprinkling coins at the feet of umpire Mariana Alves during a heated exchange. Despite his tendency for explosive behavior during matches, Medvedev remains a popular figure with the media for his humorous and reflective interviews after play concludes. His frustration often manifests as irritation directed toward umpires and officials, sometimes resulting in game penalties throughout his career as a junior and professional athlete.
