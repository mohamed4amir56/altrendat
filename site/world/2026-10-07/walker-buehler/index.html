Walker Buehler returns to start must-win Padres Game 4 against Brewers

By Karim Fouad · Published 08 Oct 2026, 02:00 UTC

Walker Buehler makes his Game 4 start for the Padres on Wednesday night as they face elimination against the Milwaukee Brewers.

Right-hander Walker Buehler takes the mound for the San Diego Padres with a 9-6 record and a 4.63 ERA, rejoining the rotation after missing the first two games of the National League Division Series while on the paternity list. He was officially reinstated to the roster on Monday, providing a critical arm for San Diego as they attempt to force a deciding Game 5. The Padres kept their season alive by winning Tuesday’s contest 4-3, trailing the best-of-five series 2-1 heading into this pivotal matchup at Petco Park.

For the Padres, this game represents a make-or-break moment to stay in the postseason race. The Brewers, holding a series lead, are sending left-hander Robert Gasser to the mound for his first career postseason start. Gasser enters the game with significant rest, having not pitched since September 25. His performance on 11 days of rest is a key focus, as he recorded a 2.74 ERA in eight starts this season when given six or more days between outings.

The Padres also gain a significant boost with the return of star closer Mason Miller from the paternity list. Miller proved effective during the regular season, successfully converting 38 of 41 save opportunities with a 1.67 ERA. Meanwhile, the Brewers are managing their bullpen depth closely; with Abner Uribe having appeared in every game of the series so far, pitching coach Chris Hook and manager Murphy are balancing personnel to ensure coverage, including the potential availability of Jacob Misiorowski for relief duties.

Should the Padres secure a victory in this must-win scenario, the series will extend to a deciding Game 5. That final matchup is scheduled to take place this Friday night at American Family Field in Milwaukee.
