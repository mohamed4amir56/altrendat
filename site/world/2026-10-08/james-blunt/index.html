James Blunt Evades Early Exit in Tense Second Series of Celebrity Traitors

By Karim Fouad · Published 08 Oct 2026, 20:30 UTC

Tidworth-born singer James Blunt remains in the running for charity on the BBC’s The Celebrity Traitors after surviving the opening episodes.

James Blunt has navigated the opening episodes of the second series of The Celebrity Traitors without being selected as a secret Traitor or suffering an early elimination. While fellow contestants have already fallen under suspicion in Claudia Winkleman’s game of deception, the chart-topping musician has avoided becoming a target, keeping him firmly in the hunt for victory among the 21 participating celebrities. The show, which premiered on October 1, tasks participants with identifying hidden Traitors while completing challenges to build a prize fund for charity. Despite fans labeling him one of the series's "biggest windup merchants" and expressing frustration that he was not selected as a Traitor, Blunt continues to participate as a contestant while maintaining his composure. The singer's presence has drawn significant attention from viewers online, with many praising his performance in challenges and suggesting his self-deprecating sense of humor could make him a formidable asset in the game. Tension within the group has already spiked following a slip-up by contestant Richard E. Grant, who mistakenly admitted his role as a Traitor during a conversation with Joe Lycett. As the group continues to gather at dramatic round table meetings to debate identities, viewers across Wiltshire and beyond remain focused on whether Blunt can continue to outwit his peers. The contestants are competing over the course of five weeks to determine who will ultimately emerge as the winner.
