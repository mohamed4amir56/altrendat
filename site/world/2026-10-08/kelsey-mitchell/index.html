WNBA Scoring Machine Kelsey Mitchell Declares Intent To Be Selfish In Free Agency

By Karim Fouad · Published 08 Oct 2026, 19:31 UTC

Free agent Kelsey Mitchell is weighing her future after recording a league-record 1,086 total points for the Indiana Fever this season.

Kelsey Mitchell arrives at free agency following a career-best campaign where she set a WNBA record with 1,086 total points. Averaging 24.7 points per game, the 30-year-old guard also led the league with a 45% 3-point shooting percentage. As she prepares to navigate her first experience as an unrestricted free agent, Mitchell stated she plans to be selfish regarding her next career move, noting that she must prioritize what is best for her future after nine seasons with the same franchise.

This impending decision carries significant weight for the Indiana Fever organization, which views re-signing the four-time All-Star as a major priority. Teammate Caitlin Clark has publicly expressed a strong desire for Mitchell to remain in Indiana, citing the importance of their established backcourt chemistry. The two players navigated a high-pressure season together, enduring intense public scrutiny while forming an elite scoring duo.

Basketball Hall of Famer Shaquille O'Neal has weighed in on the situation, advising Mitchell to leverage the increased spotlight that comes with playing alongside a high-profile teammate like Clark. O'Neal suggested that staying within that orbit could offer professional and financial advantages, though he emphasized he would not interfere with her personal decision-making process.

Mitchell’s choice remains one of the most anticipated moves of the upcoming offseason. While the Fever possess the salary cap space necessary to retain her, competition from other organizations is expected to be fierce when free agency officially opens in late January.
