Guardians stall White Sox playoff push with dominant 9-3 road victory

By Karim Fouad · Published 09 Oct 2026, 00:30 UTC

The Cleveland Guardians forced a Game 4 after defeating the Chicago White Sox 9-3 in Wednesday's playoff matchup at Rate Field.

The White Sox offense struggled under the pressure of a sold-out crowd in Bridgeport, as eight of the team’s first nine hitters struck out during Wednesday's loss. Despite the high expectations of fans hoping to see the South Side team clinch their first postseason series at home in 120 years, the momentum shifted decisively toward Cleveland. The 9-3 final score narrowed the White Sox’s American League Division Series lead to 2-1, pushing the celebration for local supporters back at least one more day.

For the thousands of fans who arrived hours before the 3 p.m. first pitch, the outcome was a stark contrast to the energy that filled the stadium. The defeat means the team must now regroup to secure the single win needed to reach the American League Championship Series for the first time since 2005. As noted by attendees, the team appeared overly hyped, yet the focus remains on the upcoming opportunity to close out the series.

According to Yahoo Sports and Block Club Chicago, the White Sox have a second chance to clinch the series on their home turf. Pitcher Hagen Smith is slated to take the mound for Chicago, while Cleveland looks to build on its momentum with Parker Messick starting. If the series continues, a fifth and final game is scheduled to take place in Cleveland this Saturday.

Players and fans alike are shifting their attention to the next contest, aiming to clear the nerves that impacted Wednesday's performance. With the series on the line, the atmosphere at Rate Field is expected to remain intense as the team attempts to validate their playoff run.
