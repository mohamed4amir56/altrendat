Alabama Targets No. 2 Georgia Ranking With Potential Hurricane Looming Over Tuscaloosa

By Karim Fouad · Published 09 Oct 2026, 09:30 UTC

The No. 6 Alabama Crimson Tide remain scheduled to host No. 2 Georgia at 6:30 p.m. CT Saturday despite incoming tropical weather.

A home victory against the No. 2-ranked Georgia Bulldogs would provide a massive +0.89 boost to Alabama’s resume score. Currently, the Crimson Tide sit at No. 2 in the latest College Football Playoff resume rankings, trailing only Texas, and are within reach of taking the top spot after a recent win over Mississippi State. Because an average No. 25-ranked team would only be expected to defeat Georgia 11% of the time, the meritocratic ranking system weighs such a win heavily. Conversely, a loss would only result in a -0.11 hit to Alabama's ledger.

This matchup holds immense weight for Alabama’s playoff positioning, regardless of the weather conditions currently brewing in the Gulf of Mexico. With Tropical Storm Isaias expected to strengthen into a hurricane, the game faces potential disruptions, yet school officials maintained on Wednesday evening that the contest remains set for its original time. Safety remains the priority, and the university is coordinating with the National Weather Service, the Southeastern Conference, and the University of Georgia to monitor conditions.

The logistical challenge of the weekend extends beyond the gridiron, with meteorologist James Spann labeling the convergence of the game, a presidential visit, and regional events like the National Shrimp Festival a potential nightmare. President Donald Trump is currently expected to attend the game, though he noted on Wednesday that the incoming storm could impact those plans. Fans are advised to monitor official Alabama athletics and SEC channels for updates as Saturday approaches.
