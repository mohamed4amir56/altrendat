England face four-match detour through Greece to reach 2027 World Cup finals

By Karim Fouad · Published 09 Oct 2026, 16:00 UTC

England begins its European play-off journey against Greece today in Crete after finishing behind Spain in their qualification group.

Quick facts

Match location
Crete
Tournament
2027 World Cup qualification play-offs
Manager
Sarina Wiegman

The road to Brazil requires a perfect performance from the Lionesses as they navigate a grueling four-match path in the European play-offs. Following a group stage loss to Spain, England must now secure victories in these extra matches to keep their tournament dreams alive, starting with today's opening leg in Crete. The stakes are clear as any slip-up could derail the team’s quest to qualify for the 2027 World Cup.

For England, this playoff series is a test of consistency and resolve. Defender Esme Morgan has emphasized that the team must maintain high standards throughout this series, noting that the squad needs to be perfect against their Greek opponents to progress through the bracket.

According to the BBC, England was consigned to this additional qualification route after failing to top their group. Manager Sarina Wiegman is tasked with steering the team through this challenging detour. Fans can follow the progress of this high-stakes encounter through BBC Radio 5 Live commentary and real-time text updates.

Following this initial round in Crete, the Lionesses must continue to win to advance through the remaining stages of the play-offs. Success in this path is the only way for England to secure their place in the upcoming World Cup finals.
