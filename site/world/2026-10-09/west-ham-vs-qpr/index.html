Tomáš Souček returns as West Ham hosts QPR in London derby

By Karim Fouad · Published 09 Oct 2026, 18:32 UTC

West Ham United plays Queens Park Rangers in an EFL Championship match tonight at 20:00 BST at London Stadium.

Competition

Tomáš Souček is back in the starting XI for West Ham United after the Championship returns from a 19-day break. Players from across the division have spent this period representing their countries, with many competing in four matches during the hiatus. This London derby marks the resumption of domestic football with both clubs vying for position at the top of the table.

The outcome of this fixture carries significant weight for the promotion race. According to BBC reporting, the winner is guaranteed to finish the night in one of the two automatic promotion spots. Fans have waited nearly three weeks to see these teams return to club action, making this encounter a pivotal moment in the Championship season.

West Ham United confirms that the match takes place at London Stadium. The BBC is providing live text commentary and radio analysis throughout the game. The fixture serves as the first match back for both clubs following the international window.

Kick-off is scheduled for 20:00 BST. Supporters can follow the live updates via the BBC's digital platforms as the teams compete for crucial points in the standings.
