Caicedo and Henderson start as Chelsea hosts Bournemouth at Stamford Bridge today

By Karim Fouad · Published 10 Oct 2026, 13:30 UTC

Chelsea faces Bournemouth today at Stamford Bridge as Moises Caicedo makes his first Premier League start of the season.

Quick facts

Date
Saturday, October 10
Time
15.00 BST
Venue
Stamford Bridge
Referee
Sam Barrott

Moises Caicedo returns to the starting lineup for the first time this season, partnering Jordan Henderson in midfield for his debut appearance at Stamford Bridge. This tactical adjustment by manager Xabi Alonso comes as the squad aims to return to winning ways following an extended three-week break in domestic action. The match marks the resumption of the Premier League campaign for Chelsea at their home ground. The visitors arrive at Stamford Bridge seeking their first top-flight victory of the current season. This fixture provides Chelsea a significant opportunity to secure points after making three alterations to the XI that faced Brentford. The tactical configuration sees Emi Martinez in goal, protected by a defensive unit featuring captain Levi Colwill, Josh Acheampong, and Maxence Lacroix. The attacking setup includes Cole Palmer, Morgan Rogers, and the returning Joao Pedro. For Bournemouth, the starting XI includes Petrović in goal with Evanilson, Adams, and Christie featuring prominently in the lineup. Matchday squad additions also include Marco Palestra, who returns to the bench for the first time since the preseason. Fans globally can access the game via various streaming services and broadcasters, though the match is not scheduled for television broadcast in the United Kingdom. Referee Sam Barrott will oversee proceedings on the pitch with John Brooks handling VAR responsibilities. The match serves as a pivotal encounter in matchday 6, offering both teams a chance to shift their momentum as the league schedule intensifies following the international break.
