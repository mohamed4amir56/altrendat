Max Verstappen Masters Singapore Rain as Russell Crashes and McLarens Collide

By Karim Fouad · Published 10 Oct 2026, 12:30 UTC

Max Verstappen won the wet Sprint race in Singapore after a half-hour rain delay and a dramatic first-lap crash by George Russell.

Quick facts

Winner
Max Verstappen
Location
Marina Bay Street Circuit
Race Type
Sprint

George Russell initially surged past polesitter Max Verstappen at the start, only to undo all that work by crashing at the final corner on the very first lap. The race started thirty minutes late due to a pre-race downpour that soaked sectors of the Marina Bay Street Circuit, forcing all drivers to manage intermediate tyres on a track that flickered between wet and dry conditions. After the race restarted, Verstappen maintained the lead, managing his tyres steadily while pulling away from the chasing pack. The victory marks a significant continuation of his momentum, following his previous win at the Bahrain Grand Prix in Malaysia. Lewis Hamilton capitalized on the chaos, jumping from sixth to second, while championship leader Kimi Antonelli secured a fourth-place finish. The closing stages of the race turned chaotic when McLaren teammates Lando Norris and Oscar Piastri collided at Turn 5. Norris tagged Piastri into a spin, causing Piastri to stop outright and eventually finish 14th, while Norris slipped back to eighth place. Other drivers, including Liam Lawson, Ollie Bearman, and Nico Hulkenberg, moved up the field to take advantage of the late-race incident. As the track dried out, teams faced difficult choices regarding tyre wear, with Charles Leclerc noting his intermediate tyres were heavily degraded before the finish. Qualifying for the Singapore Grand Prix is scheduled to follow these results as the racing weekend at the Marina Bay Street Circuit continues.
