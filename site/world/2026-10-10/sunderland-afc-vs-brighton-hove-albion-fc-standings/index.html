10-man Brighton clinches 2-0 win over Sunderland at Stadium of Light

By Karim Fouad · Published 10 Oct 2026, 16:30 UTC

Brighton secured all three points against Sunderland to remain in the Premier League top four despite a 57th-minute red card for Pascal Struijk.

Quick facts

Match result
Sunderland 0-2 Brighton
Red card
Pascal Struijk (57th minute)
Goalscorers
Maxim De Cuyper, Jaouen Hadjam
Venue
Stadium of Light

Brighton & Hove Albion successfully defended their lead for over 30 minutes with only 10 men, capping a resilient performance with a breakaway goal in the third minute of stoppage time. The result forces Sunderland to remain just above the relegation zone while Brighton continues an exceptional start to their campaign, keeping the club firmly inside the Premier League's top four. The breakthrough arrived early in the second half when Maxim De Cuyper scored to put the visitors ahead. The game reached a chaotic tipping point in the 57th minute as a VAR review resulted in a red card for Brighton defender Pascal Struijk, forcing his side to play the remainder of the match a man down. Despite the numerical advantage, Sunderland failed to convert their pressure into an equalizer. According to The Independent, the hosts were punished for their inability to break down the resilient Brighton defense during the dying moments of the match, as Jaouen Hadjam finished a decisive Brighton counter-attack to seal the 2-0 victory. Earlier in the match, Sunderland goalkeeper Robin Roefs performed several key interventions, including saving a strike from Diego Gomez, while Brighton’s Bart Verbruggen was required to stop a long-range effort from Nordi Mukiele to maintain the clean sheet. Sunderland manager Regis Le Bris had rotated his squad following a 5-3 defeat to Manchester City, but Brighton’s consistency—carrying over from a recent 3-0 win against Arsenal—ultimately proved too much for the Black Cats to overcome at their home stadium.
