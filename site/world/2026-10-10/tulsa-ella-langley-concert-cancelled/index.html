Ella Langley cancels sold-out Tulsa concert following reported serious security threat

By Karim Fouad · Published 10 Oct 2026, 06:31 UTC

Country music star Ella Langley scrapped her Friday night performance at the BOK Center after receiving a threat claiming great harm would occur at the venue.

Quick facts

Venue
BOK Center, Tulsa
Cancellation Date
Friday
Reason cited
Serious security threat
Police findings
No credible threat found

A caller warned that shots would be fired and bombs would be used at the 19,000-seat arena, prompting the 27-year-old singer to cancel her performance just two hours before the doors were scheduled to open. Despite the severity of the claim, Cpt Richard Meulenberg of the Tulsa Police Department confirmed that authorities conducted multiple sweeps of the BOK Center, including the use of sniffer dogs, but found no credible threat.

For the thousands of fans who had already gathered or planned to attend the sold-out show, the sudden cancellation resulted in a complete disruption of the event. The artist stated that the decision was made in coordination with local authorities and her security team, emphasizing that she refused to put her fans, staff, or team in harm's way.

Both Langley and the BOK Center management issued statements apologizing for the disappointment caused to ticket holders. While the police investigation concluded that the venue was safe and ready for the performance, the show was ultimately scrapped. The artist, known for her record-breaking success on the US charts this year, did not provide further details regarding the source of the phone call.

Langley’s management team confirmed that information regarding ticket refunds will be provided to fans as soon as possible. No further updates on rescheduled tour dates or specific investigation outcomes have been released at this time.
