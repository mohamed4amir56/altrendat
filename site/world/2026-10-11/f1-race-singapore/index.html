Max Verstappen Snatches Singapore Grand Prix Pole Position Following Sprint Race Victory

By Karim Fouad · Published 11 Oct 2026, 09:30 UTC

Max Verstappen secured pole position for Sunday's Singapore Grand Prix after also winning the sprint race on the Marina Bay circuit.

Quick facts

Pole position margin
0.05 seconds
Championship leader advantage
89 points
George Russell qualifying position
6th

Max Verstappen continues his late-season resurgence, clocking a lap five-hundredths of a second faster than Ferrari's Charles Leclerc to secure his 50th career pole position. This performance follows his sprint victory in Singapore earlier on Saturday, marking a dominant run after his recent win at the Malaysia Grand Prix. Verstappen credited the car's improved balance for his ability to push to the limit, noting that he is incredibly happy to start from the front after a positive string of races.

The momentum shift is significant for the championship, though title leader Kimi Antonelli maintains an 89-point cushion over teammate George Russell. As Verstappen finds his rhythm, the focus shifts to whether he can protect his lead at the start, an area where the Red Bull driver has faced inconsistency in recent outings. The race remains a high-stakes tactical battle, especially with overtaking opportunities at a premium on the Marina Bay streets.

For George Russell, the weekend proved disastrous. After leading the sprint on lap one, he crashed into the barriers following a miscalculation that pushed his car onto the white lines in wet conditions. Forced to start Sunday's grand prix from the back of the grid due to engine penalties and the repair work required after his crash, Russell’s championship hopes have taken a severe blow. Meanwhile, Antonelli qualified fourth and is positioned to potentially seal his first world title if he extends his lead to 101 points by the end of this race.

Verstappen remains cautious heading into Sunday, acknowledging that the long race leaves room for errors despite his current form. He will be joined on the front row by Ferrari drivers, who hope to challenge his pace in the race.
