Max Verstappen Clenches Maiden Singapore Grand Prix Pole Position at Marina Bay

By Karim Fouad · Published 11 Oct 2026, 04:00 UTC

Max Verstappen qualified first for the Singapore Grand Prix, narrowly beating Ferrari’s Charles Leclerc at the Marina Bay Street Circuit.

Max Verstappen clinched pole position at the Marina Bay Street Circuit for the first time in his career, clocking a lap of 1m 31.373s. This performance secured him the top spot for the Singapore Grand Prix, effectively pushing Ferrari’s Charles Leclerc into second place by a margin of only 0.054s. Lewis Hamilton took third position after the qualifying hour, having led the pace during the initial segments of the session. The result follows a volatile weekend that included a rain-hit sprint race featuring a record nine retirements. Verstappen’s latest success at the circuit comes after he also claimed victory in the 20-lap sprint event held on Oct 10. The qualifying hour saw varying performances from the field, as drivers navigated the drying Marina Bay streets following earlier rain. Lewis Hamilton and Charles Leclerc lead the immediate chasing pack, while George Russell finished in P6, though he carries an incoming grid penalty into the main race. Further down the order, Isack Hadjar faced significant reliability issues with his Red Bull, finishing the session at the bottom. Meanwhile, drivers like Fernando Alonso and the Cadillac pair of Sergio Perez and Valtteri Bottas failed to advance through the grid in a highly competitive qualifying session. With the starting order largely finalized, attention now shifts to the main Singapore Grand Prix event. The grid positions will be adjusted to account for existing penalties, such as the one applied to George Russell, which will see those ranked behind him move up a place. Teams are now preparing for the main race, looking to capitalize on positions earned during this qualifying hour on the Marina Bay layout.
