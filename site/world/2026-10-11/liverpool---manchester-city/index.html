Lewis Koumas earns first Premier League start as Liverpool hosts Manchester City

By Karim Fouad · Published 11 Oct 2026, 15:00 UTC

Liverpool faces Manchester City at Anfield today with Lewis Koumas starting in the absence of Cody Gakpo and Alexander Isak.

Lewis Koumas leads the Liverpool attack today at Anfield, marking his first start in the Premier League. He receives support from teammates Bradley Barcola and Victor Munoz as the team adjusts to the absence of forwards Cody Gakpo and Alexander Isak. The confirmed starting XI also features Alisson, Van Dijk, Jeremy Jacquet, Kerkez, Wirtz, Mac Allister, Araujo, and Gravenberch. Dominik Szoboszlai is notably absent from the matchday squad for the home side.

This matchup at Anfield holds significant weight as Manchester City plays its first game since being found guilty of more than a hundred Premier League charges. The Guardian notes that the venue serves as a focal point for the club's biggest modern rivals, adding intense pressure to an encounter already known for its febrile atmosphere. City enters the contest following a perfect start of five wins from their first five Premier League matches.

According to Liverpool FC, the bench includes Mamardashvili, Gomez, Endo, Chiesa, Tsimikas, Frimpong, Nyoni, McConnell, and Ngumoha. While Manchester City manager Enzo Maresca prepares his team for the hostile reception at Anfield, both clubs navigate different tactical challenges. Liverpool continues to adapt to the coaching philosophy of Andoni Iraola, while City deals with the fallout of the recent league findings. The game is being monitored as a high-stakes moment for the visitors amid public scrutiny surrounding the club's standing in the competition.
