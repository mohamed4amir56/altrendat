The Diplomat Returns for Season 4 Alongside Ben Affleck Thriller Animals

By Karim Fouad · Published 11 Oct 2026, 10:01 UTC · Updated 11 Oct 2026, 10:31 UTC

Netflix debuts the fourth season of The Diplomat and the crime thriller Animals during the week of Oct. 9–15.

Ambassador Kate Wyler, played by Keri Russell, grapples with a potential culture of lawlessness as the delicate relationship between the UK and US threatens to implode in the fourth season of The Diplomat. This season uncovers the fallout from the pivotal Season 3 finale, where President Grace Penn and Vice President Hal Wyler navigated a high-stakes nuclear threat. The series explores whether the diplomatic steps taken remain within bounds or have crossed into uncharted territory.

For viewers, this week’s lineup offers a mix of high-stakes political drama and suspenseful crime storytelling. The variety of new content provides options ranging from tense kidnappings to lighthearted competition series, allowing subscribers to choose between heavy political intrigue and seasonal entertainment.

Also premiering is Animals, a crime thriller starring Ben Affleck as a Los Angeles mayoral candidate. After his son is kidnapped, he and his wife, played by Kerry Washington, must scramble to raise a ransom while secrets emerge that threaten to dismantle their lives. Directed by and co-written by Affleck, the film also features Gillian Anderson and Steven Yeun. Beyond these dramas, Netflix has expanded its library with the return of reality favorite Love Is Blind for Season 11, the sophomore season of Is It Cake? Halloween, and documentaries including TYSON and The First Monday in May.

Subscribers can also access new content released throughout the week of Oct. 9–15, including the kids' series Spike and Hollywood Arts, and the second season of Haunted Hotel. The platform continues to rotate its catalog with these additions to provide a broad range of viewing experiences throughout the month.
