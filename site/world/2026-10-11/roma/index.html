Roma Travel North to Face Como in Pivotal Serie A Title Clash

By Karim Fouad · Published 11 Oct 2026, 11:00 UTC

Roma faces Como at Stadio Giuseppe Sinigaglia in a crucial match as both teams contend for the top of the Serie A standings.

Quick facts

Venue
Stadio Giuseppe Sinigaglia
Kick-off time
11.30 UK time

Three points currently separate these rivals, as Roma enters the weekend tied for first place in Serie A with Inter while Como aims to bounce back from a recent 2-0 loss to Frosinone. This showdown represents a major test for Gian Piero Gasperini’s squad, which must balance the intensity of this league clash against an upcoming Champions League match against Real Madrid. The Giallorossi find themselves in a three-horse race for the league title, making every point in the standings essential.

For readers, this match serves as a barometer for how Roma manages squad rotation while competing across multiple high-stakes tournaments. With Real Madrid looming mid-week, Gasperini faces the challenge of maintaining his team's momentum while ensuring key players remain fit for European duties. A victory in Como is considered necessary for Roma to solidify its position at the top of the table.

According to Chiesa Di Totti, the match at Stadio Giuseppe Sinigaglia carries significant implications for the league hierarchy, as Como looks to prove it belongs among the Italian elite. Sporting director Carlalberto Ludi stated that Como intends to dominate the game through possession and quality. The home side, however, will be without striker Moise Kean, who was ruled out due to an injury sustained while playing for Italy. Yahoo Sports reports that while Como’s manager Cesc Fàbregas previously expressed frustration regarding the national team's medical handling of the injury, Ludi maintains that the club’s rapport with the Azzurri staff remains excellent.

Following this encounter, Roma will turn its attention to a highly anticipated Champions League match against Real Madrid scheduled for Wednesday.
