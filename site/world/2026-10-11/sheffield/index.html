Patrick Bamford nets double as Sheffield United storms past Lincoln City 3-0

By Karim Fouad · Published 11 Oct 2026, 08:31 UTC

Sheffield United ended a three-game losing streak by defeating Lincoln City 3-0 at Bramall Lane.

Quick facts

Final Score
3-0
Venue
Bramall Lane
League Position
13th
Key Scorers
Patrick Bamford (2), Tahith Chong (1)

Patrick Bamford scored twice and Tahith Chong added his first goal for the club as Sheffield United snapped a three-game losing streak with a dominant 3-0 victory over Lincoln City. The result marks a significant turnaround for the Blades, who entered the match under pressure following a difficult start to their Championship campaign and an early EFL Cup exit at the hands of Fleetwood Town. The win propels Chris Wilder’s side to 13th in the league standings, placing them one point and two positions above the previous season's League One title winners. For Wilder, the performance was a necessary response to mounting external criticism and the internal challenges facing the South Yorkshire club, which is currently the subject of an English Football League investigation following the liquidation of its parent company in August. Despite holding possession, Sheffield United struggled to find an opening until the 43rd minute, when Bamford clinically finished a low cross from Sam McCallum. Lincoln City missed a golden opportunity to take the lead earlier when Freddie Draper headed over the bar from a Rob Street delivery. The momentum firmly shifted in the second half. Chong, making his first Championship start after returning from international duty with Curacao, squeezed a tight-angled shot past goalkeeper George Wickens. Bamford sealed the win shortly after, volleying home a deep cross from Ben Brereton Diaz to complete a comfortable afternoon at Bramall Lane. Manager Chris Wilder noted the importance of the result, stating that every individual involved at the club needed to improve their game following a challenging period marked by off-field instability and poor form.
