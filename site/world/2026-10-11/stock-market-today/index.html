Dow Surges 400 Points as Wall Street Breaks Losing Streak Friday

By Karim Fouad · Published 11 Oct 2026, 10:01 UTC · Updated 11 Oct 2026, 10:31 UTC

Major stock averages rebounded on Friday as AI trade jitters eased and tech stocks recovered their footing.

Humana shares surged 12% following a boost to its largest Medicare Advantage contract rating, while Alignment Healthcare cratered 23% in the same sector. These sharp moves among managed care providers occurred after the Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services released 2027 Star Ratings for upcoming open enrollment. Elsewhere, Park Aerospace reported earnings of 21 cents per share on $20.8 million in revenue, a notable increase from the 12 cents per share and $16.4 million recorded during the same period last year.

The market movement signals a stabilization as the 10-year Treasury yield dropped to 5.24% and investors processed new developments in the technology sector. The rebound was supported by reports that OpenAI projects its annualized revenue will exceed $70 billion by the end of 2026, which helped alleviate previous market concerns regarding the company's growth trajectory. As sentiment shifted, major averages moved to snap a two-day losing streak.

Specific sectors faced distinct pressures throughout the trading session. Telecom providers saw a significant decline after Grain Management announced an agreement for SpaceX to purchase its 800 megahertz spectrum portfolio. In response, T-Mobile shares dropped 6%, Verizon lost more than 6%, and AT&T fell nearly 7%. Meanwhile, Brent crude oil futures traded near $103 per barrel and WTI remained below $92 following comments regarding US policy toward Iran. Tech sector performance remains a key focus for market participants as chip manufacturers including Nvidia, Micron, and SK Hynix bounced back during the Friday session. Bitcoin prices held steady, trading just below $83,000 per token as broader market conditions began to stabilize across the major indices.
