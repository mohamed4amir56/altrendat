10-man Vancouver Whitecaps hold firm in tense 1-1 road draw against LAFC

By Karim Fouad · Published 11 Oct 2026, 07:31 UTC

Oliver Larraz scored for a shorthanded Vancouver Whitecaps side in a 1-1 tie against Los Angeles FC on Saturday night.

Tristan Blackmon was shown a straight red card in the seventh minute, forcing Vancouver to battle for the remainder of the contest with only 10 men on the pitch. Despite the numerical disadvantage, the Western Conference leaders secured a point against LAFC, maintaining their place near the top of the league standings with 51 points this season.

For the Vancouver Whitecaps, this result is critical as the club pursues a berth in the Audi 2026 MLS Cup Playoffs. Holding the conference lead requires sustained defensive discipline, particularly after losing a key defender early in the game. For LAFC, the draw marks a continuation of a winless streak that has now spanned four consecutive matches, including two losses during that stretch.

Oliver Larraz provided the breakthrough for the visitors in the 43rd minute, pouncing on a rebound from an arcing pass by Édier Ocampo to blast a first-touch shot into the top-net. Goalkeeper Yohei Takaoka recorded three saves to preserve the draw for the Whitecaps. LAFC responded shortly after halftime when Denis Bouanga headed home a cross from Sergi Palencia in the 46th minute. This goal was Bouanga's 15th of the season, extending his streak of scoring at least 15 goals in four consecutive campaigns.

Vancouver now turns its attention to a midweek trip to face St. Louis on Wednesday. LAFC will look to break its winless run when the team returns home to host Austin, also scheduled for Wednesday.
