Christian Yelich returns to Brewers designated hitter spot for NLCS Game 2

By Karim Fouad · Published 12 Oct 2026, 23:01 UTC

The Milwaukee Brewers have restored Christian Yelich to the lineup for Monday’s second game of the NLCS against the Los Angeles Dodgers.

Quick facts

Game
NLCS Game 2
Opponent
Los Angeles Dodgers
Opposing Pitcher
Yoshinobu Yamamoto
Yelich Position
Designated hitter

Manager Pat Murphy is opting for a tactical shift as the Brewers prepare to face Dodgers starting pitcher Yoshinobu Yamamoto. While Yelich watched from the sidelines during Sunday's thrilling Game 1 victory, he now returns to the field to anchor the team's offensive efforts in the designated hitter position. This change comes as the Brewers attempt to build on their early momentum in the postseason series. For fans, this lineup adjustment signals a clear strategic pivot as the team attempts to counter Yamamoto’s pitching repertoire. The return of a franchise icon like Yelich provides the Brewers with an experienced hitter during a high-stakes playoff environment. Beyond the inclusion of Yelich, the Brewers have made additional changes to the defensive configuration. Sal Frelick is set to replace Luis Lara in right field, while David Hamilton will take over third base duties from Joey Ortiz. The move to sit Lara, despite his role in knocking in the game-winning run during the series opener, reflects the team's willingness to shuffle personnel to secure the best possible matchups. These maneuvers are central to the team's approach as they aim to seize a commanding position in the NLCS. The coaching staff continues to balance current production with strategic planning, specifically adjusting their roster to address the challenges posed by the opposing starting pitcher. The Brewers carry their one-game lead into Monday's contest, looking to capitalize on their recent success while integrating these new faces into the starting nine.
