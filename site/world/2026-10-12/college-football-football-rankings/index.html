Georgia Holds Top Spot While Texas Plummets After Ugly Rivalry Defeat

By Karim Fouad · Published 12 Oct 2026, 11:00 UTC

Georgia retained its number one ranking in the NCAA Re-Rank 1-138 following Week 6, while Texas dropped to seventh after losing to Oklahoma.

UCLA surged up the rankings, jumping nine spots to reach number nine following a gritty 17-15 win over Oregon. Meanwhile, the SEC continues its dominance with four teams represented in the top eight positions: Georgia at number one, Alabama at number six, Texas at number seven, and Mississippi at number eight. Alabama maintained its position despite a narrow three-point loss.

These standings hold significant weight for program momentum as the regular season reaches the midpoint. For teams like Kentucky, the latest re-rank offers a massive boost, climbing 20 spots to number 20 following a victory over LSU. Conversely, the shifting landscape highlights the volatility of the season, with programs like Minnesota dropping 16 spots to number 46 and Texas A&M sliding to number 55. These movements are based on the latest NCAA Re-Rank 1-138 after Week 6, as reported by USA TODAY Sports.

Georgia solidified its grip on the top position by defeating Alabama, effectively separating itself from contenders Notre Dame and Miami. Despite the loss to Oklahoma, Texas managed to avoid a sharper decline due to an earlier comeback victory against Ohio State, though the Longhorns were still surpassed by the fifth-ranked Buckeyes in the latest iteration of the rankings.

Looking ahead, teams such as UCLA are positioned for potential 10-win seasons under coach Bob Chesney, leveraging remaining schedules to build on their newfound rankings. Other programs, including Nebraska, continue to linger on the periphery of the top 25, while the US LBM Coaches Poll remains an ongoing weekly assessment for head coaches across the Football Bowl Subdivision.
