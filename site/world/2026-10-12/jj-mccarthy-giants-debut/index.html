J.J. McCarthy storms onto field for Giants debut in Washington clash

By Karim Fouad · Published 12 Oct 2026, 10:31 UTC

Quarterback J.J. McCarthy made his New York Giants debut Sunday, recording five snaps and two carries during a 27-27 tie against the Washington Commanders.

J.J. McCarthy entered the game during the team’s first offensive possession in Washington, marking his first appearance for the Giants. His debut was brief and limited, as he ultimately recorded five total snaps throughout the Week 5 matchup. While his entry was intended to provide a shift in momentum, Washington appeared prepared for the personnel change. Reports indicate that each time McCarthy stepped onto the field, the defense anticipated a designed quarterback run or an RPO, holding him to just two yards on two carries. During one specific sequence, he attempted a pass to Darnell Mooney that resulted in a five-yard loss, though the team was bailed out by a facemask penalty. For the Giants, integrating new personnel like McCarthy remains a tactical challenge as the team navigates a shifting rotation at the quarterback position. The use of backup signal-callers during high-stakes drives forces the coaching staff to balance offensive rhythm with the need to test new roster depth. Following the game, the team's snap count data revealed that the offense utilized 89 total plays in the overtime tie, with McCarthy seeing only a small fraction of the action. The Giants ultimately fought back from a 14-point second-half deficit to secure a 27-27 tie at Northwest Stadium. Jameis Winston handled the bulk of the passing duties, finishing with 326 yards and three touchdowns, while the offense relied on heavy contributions from Malik Nabers and Isaiah Likely. The team moved to a 3-1-1 record after a late fumble by Cam Skattebo in overtime halted a potential game-winning drive. Despite the team's overall offensive production, McCarthy’s limited role did not yield significant yardage, leaving questions about how the backup will be utilized in future game plans as the season progresses.
