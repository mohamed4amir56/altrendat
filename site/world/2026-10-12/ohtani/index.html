Dodgers Star Shohei Ohtani Faces Power Shortage During Struggles Against Milwaukee Brewers

By Karim Fouad · Published 12 Oct 2026, 23:31 UTC

Shohei Ohtani is battling a postseason slump and injury concerns while hitting a .188 average.

Quick facts

Postseason Batting Average
.188
NLCS Game 1 Result
Dodgers lost 2-1
NLDS Performance
2-for-13 with 4 strikeouts

Shohei Ohtani enters the NLCS searching for the explosive form he showcased against the Milwaukee Brewers just one year ago. During that previous series, Ohtani produced a masterpiece with three home runs and ten strikeouts across six scoreless innings to help sweep the Dodgers toward the World Series. Now, the team is looking for a similar resurgence after Ohtani struggled through the NLDS, recording only a .188 postseason batting average.

The decline in performance has become a serious concern for the Los Angeles Dodgers as they navigate a competitive postseason. With Ohtani limited to just one RBI in his last 23 games, manager Dave Roberts moved him down to the fifth spot in the lineup. The power shortage is palpable, with the slugger primarily hitting opposite-field singles rather than the authoritative strikes that typically anchor the Dodgers' offense.

Concerns remain tied to lingering injuries to his right biceps and left knee, which have persisted since June and potentially disrupted his swing mechanics. According to MLB.com, Ohtani confirmed he must manage these physical ailments through the remainder of the postseason, though additional rest during his September stint on the injured list was intended to aid his recovery. Reporter Bob Nightengale noted that the lack of power suggests Ohtani is not swinging freely, raising questions about whether he will require surgery after the season concludes.

Despite the slump, the Dodgers maintain confidence in their star player. Hitting coach Aaron Bates reported that Ohtani's work on the field ahead of the NLCS opener was great. As the Dodgers search for answers following a 2-1 loss in Game 1, the team continues to treat the situation with focus and urgency.
