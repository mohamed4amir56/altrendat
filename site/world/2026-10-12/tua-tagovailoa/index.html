Tua Tagovailoa primed to start for Falcons following Michael Penix rib injury

By Karim Fouad · Published 12 Oct 2026, 05:30 UTC

Tua Tagovailoa stepped in during overtime of Sunday's 30-23 loss to the Ravens after starter Michael Penix Jr. sustained a rib injury.

Quick facts

Game Date
Sunday
Final Score
Ravens 30, Falcons 23
Next Opponent
Bears

Tua Tagovailoa's Falcons debut saw him take the field during an overtime possession, ending in an interception by Ravens defender Kyle Hamilton that sealed a 30-23 defeat. Tagovailoa entered the game to finish the match after starter Michael Penix Jr. was sidelined following a hard sack by linebacker Tavius Robinson with only 27 seconds remaining in regulation.

Should imaging confirm that Penix is unable to play, Tagovailoa is slated to take the starting role for the Falcons in their upcoming Week 6 home game against the Bears. This shift in the depth chart puts Tagovailoa ahead of Cooper Rush, who handled duties during the first two weeks of the season while Penix was recovering from previous surgery. The potential transition comes at a critical time for the team, as Penix was managing the offense through a competitive performance that included 240 passing yards and a touchdown.

Head coach Kevin Stefanski noted that the team expects to receive more definitive information on Penix's status in the coming days. Penix, who attempted to return to play during the overtime session, ultimately felt he could not perform at the level required to secure a victory. The injury occurred during a two-minute drill while the score was locked at 23-23, forcing the Falcons to rely on their backup options to close out the contest. The organization is now focused on the evaluation process as they prepare for the Bears matchup.
