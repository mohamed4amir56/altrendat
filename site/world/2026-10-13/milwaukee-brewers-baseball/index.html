William Contreras leads Brewers past Dodgers to clinch commanding 2-0 NLCS lead

By Karim Fouad · Published 13 Oct 2026, 15:00 UTC

The Milwaukee Brewers secured a 7-4 victory over the Los Angeles Dodgers in Game 2 of the NLCS on Monday to take a 2-0 series lead.

William Contreras cemented his place in postseason history on Monday, becoming only the fourth player to record a trio of game-tying or go-ahead hits in a single playoff game. His relentless offensive display included a first-inning single, a solo home run in the fifth, and a decisive RBI single in the sixth, helping propel the Milwaukee Brewers to a 7-4 win over the Los Angeles Dodgers at American Family Field.

This victory puts the Dodgers' three-peat ambitions on life support as the best-of-seven series transitions to Dodger Stadium. For the Brewers, the result was anchored by both their offensive spark and a flawless performance from four relief pitchers who retired 15 consecutive batters, maintaining the team's momentum in front of 42,516 vocal home fans.

Contreras joined an elite list of postseason performers, including Hall of Famer George Brett, Sandy Alomar Jr., and Steve Garvey, by consistently producing in high-leverage situations. His sixth-inning go-ahead hit followed an intentional walk to teammate Brice Turang, an aggressive sequence that underscored the tension of the matchup. Brewers rookie shortstop Cooper Pratt praised the performance, describing the catcher as a dedicated competitor who thrives in the most critical moments.

The series now moves to Los Angeles, where the Brewers look to extend their advantage. Despite the Dodgers' early attempts to gain ground through defensive errors and opportunistic base running, the Milwaukee bullpen successfully neutralized the Los Angeles offense, providing 4.2 scoreless innings to preserve the win and secure a significant lead in the quest for the National League pennant.
