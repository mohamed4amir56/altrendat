Pirates hunt first place as Galaxy seeks turnaround after three-game drought

By Karim Fouad · Published 13 Oct 2026, 17:01 UTC

Orlando Pirates host TS Galaxy on Tuesday at Orlando Amstel Arena as league football resumes following a three-week international break, with Pirates sitting second on 19 points and Galaxy winless in their last three outings.

Orlando Pirates have won all three home league games this season, each by a 2-0 scoreline against Milford, Sekhukhune United, and Durban City. They sit on 19 points from seven games, just one point behind log leaders Mamelodi Sundowns with a game in hand.

The match matters because Pirates are defending their league title and chasing a return to first place, while Galaxy desperately need points after collecting just one in their last three matches, including back-to-back home defeats to Kruger United and Chippa United.

Galaxy arrive with fresher legs, having not played since the international break. Pirates, meanwhile, are still recovering from Saturday's MTN8 final defeat in Durban. Both sides enter Tuesday's fixture looking to erase recent disappointments. Galaxy's well-known giant-killing form makes them a threat despite their poor run, according to the Pirates preview. The match returns domestic action to Orlando Amstel Arena in Soweto for the first time in nearly a month after the lengthy recess.
