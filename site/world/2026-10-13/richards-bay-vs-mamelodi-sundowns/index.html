MTN8 champions Sundowns chase league lead at Richards Bay on Tuesday

By Karim Fouad · Published 13 Oct 2026, 17:01 UTC

Mamelodi Sundowns, fresh from beating Orlando Pirates 2-0 in the MTN8 final on Saturday, face Richards Bay on Tuesday evening at Princess Magogo Stadium as they prepare for a demanding schedule of games every three days.

Mamelodi Sundowns returned to training the day after their MTN8 triumph to prepare for Tuesday's clash, reflecting the relentless pace of competing on multiple fronts. Goalkeeper Ronwen Williams highlighted the challenge: at Sundowns, players face a game every three days, leaving just Sunday and Monday to prepare after Saturday's final win.

The match carries weight for both teams in the Betway Premiership. Sundowns sit top of the table, one point ahead of second-place Orlando Pirates. Richards Bay remain winless after seven games, with three draws and four losses, but a victory could lift them as high as seventh position.

Goalkeeper Williams expects Richards Bay to test Sundowns' defenses. He described the KwaZulu-Natal side as energetic and willing to take the game to their opponents. The encounter takes place at Princess Magogo Stadium, eliminating travel demands for Miguel Cardoso's squad.

Sundowns' recent form provides confidence: they defeated Pirates 2-0 at Moses Mabhida Stadium on Saturday with a clean sheet, collecting their second trophy in quick succession. The club's ability to perform on consecutive days will face its first real test of the week.
