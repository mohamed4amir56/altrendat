Breaking Bad star RJ Mitte dies at age 34 in Texas

By Karim Fouad · Published 13 Oct 2026, 04:01 UTC

The actor who portrayed Walter White Jr. in the hit television series passed away on Monday in Brownsville.

Breaking Bad episodes
56

Born in 1992 in Jackson, Mississippi, RJ Mitte gained international fame for his portrayal of Walter White Jr., the son of a chemistry teacher turned drug kingpin, in the television series Breaking Bad. Appearing in 56 episodes between 2008 and 2013, Mitte brought personal authenticity to his performance as he lived with cerebral palsy, the same condition as his iconic character. Beyond his acting career, he was a dedicated philanthropist and activist who campaigned for the rights of people with disabilities, working to ensure that such figures were defined by their personality and strength rather than their limitations.

The loss of the actor is felt deeply by fans and the entertainment industry alike, as confirmed in a statement from his management team to outlets including Variety and Deadline. While the management confirmed the death on Monday, the official circumstances are currently under investigation by authorities. Reports from TMZ indicate that the actor may have died by suicide, though definitive information regarding his passing remains limited as the investigation proceeds.

Mitte's personal life had recently reached a significant milestone when he proposed to his longtime partner, Kennedy Suarez, during a charity gala in San Diego in October 2025. In one of his final public appearances just days before his death, Mitte spoke on a podcast about his early career struggles in Hollywood, noting that his first audition was for an anti-drug commercial, a ironic precursor to his career-defining role in a show focused on the drug trade.
