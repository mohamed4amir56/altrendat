Status Yellow wind warning hits Ireland with gusts expected this Wednesday morning

By Karim Fouad · Published 13 Oct 2026, 04:01 UTC

Met Éireann issued a Status Yellow wind warning for Donegal, Galway, and Mayo starting at 6am on Wednesday, October 14.

Quick facts

Warning Level
Status Yellow
Warning Area
Donegal, Galway, Mayo
Start Time
6am, Wednesday October 14
End Time
6pm, Wednesday October 14

Southwesterly winds will turn very strong across Atlantic coastal regions this Wednesday, with forecasters warning of potential fallen trees, branches, and loose objects being displaced. Coastal areas face the additional risk of large waves and moderate surges that may lead to wave overtopping in exposed locations throughout the duration of the weather event.

For residents and travelers, the warning signifies difficult conditions for driving and cycling across the affected counties. Officials have also issued a Small Craft Warning for all Irish coasts throughout the period, advising those on the water to remain cautious as gusty winds become a major factor in daily activity.

According to Met Éireann, the wind warning remains in effect until 6pm on Wednesday. The broader outlook for the week indicates a mixed pattern, with heavy rain expected to push eastwards on Wednesday night and clear by Thursday afternoon. While the start of the week remains mild with temperatures reaching up to 19°C, the incoming weather system will bring a noticeable shift in wind intensity and localized instability, particularly for those in Ulster where thunder remains a possibility on Tuesday.

Following the windy conditions on Wednesday, Thursday will see rain clear the country by the afternoon. Drier and brighter conditions are expected to follow the departure of the wet weather system as it moves eastward across the island.
