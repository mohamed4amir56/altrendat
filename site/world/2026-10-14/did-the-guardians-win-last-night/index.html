Guardians seize 1-0 series lead by crushing Rays in ALCS opener

By Karim Fouad · Published 14 Oct 2026, 10:01 UTC

The Cleveland Guardians took a 1-0 lead in the American League Championship Series after defeating the Tampa Bay Rays 7-3 in Game 1.

The Guardians jumped ahead early in the best-of-seven series, securing a 7-3 victory over the Rays in the opening matchup. This win gives Cleveland the initial advantage as the teams compete for a spot in the World Series. Following the conclusion of Game 1 on Monday, the focus for the Guardians turned to Game 2, as they prepare to continue their push through the playoffs. Manager Stephen Vogt designated Parker Messick to take the mound for the second contest of the series on Tuesday at Tropicana Field. Messick, who grew up attending games at this venue, returns to the stadium following two previous regular-season appearances against the Rays in Florida. During the Monday opener, the Rays experienced a moment of concern during the ninth inning when third baseman Junior Caminero and outfielder Jonny DeLuca were involved in a collision while chasing a foul ball. Both players remained in the lineup for the subsequent game on Tuesday, with manager Kevin Cash confirming their availability. Looking ahead, the series shifts to Cleveland for Game 3 on Thursday. The Rays have announced that Griffin Jax will start that game, while the Guardians are expected to send right-hander Gavin Williams to the mound.
