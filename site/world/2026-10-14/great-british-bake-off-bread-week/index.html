Double Departure Rocks Great British Bake Off During Demanding Bread Week Challenge

By Karim Fouad · Published 14 Oct 2026, 04:01 UTC

Molly was eliminated and Nikki exited for personal reasons during Tuesday's episode.

Quick facts

Show
The Great British Bake Off
Network
Channel 4
Broadcast Time
Tuesdays at 8pm
Star Baker
Tom
Departing Contestants
Molly and Nikki

The tent saw a double exit during bread week as multiple contestants departed the competition after a disastrous technical challenge involving filled flatbreads. While Molly was voted off by judges Paul Hollywood and Nigella Lawson for failing to impress, Nikki left the series mid-episode following news that her mother, Joyce, had taken a turn for the worse. The programme concluded the broadcast with a photo tribute and a message of condolence to Nikki and her family for the loss of her mother. Nikki, an advertising post-producer, stated she had no regrets about leaving to be with her mother during her final days. Despite the departures, hairdresser Tom secured the title of Star Baker for the second time after impressing the judges with his showstopper creation. Molly, an accessibility co-ordinator from London, reflected on her experience after her elimination, noting she had learned to put less pressure on herself throughout the process. She expressed pride in her time on the show, having successfully competed through biscuit and bread weeks. Paul Hollywood initially confirmed that the judging would proceed as scheduled for the remaining bakers despite Nikki's sudden departure to maintain the integrity of the competition format. The series continues to air on Channel 4.
