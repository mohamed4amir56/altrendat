Tsimikas captains Liverpool in Austria for Champions League clash at LASK

By Karim Fouad · Published 14 Oct 2026, 16:30 UTC

Liverpool made four changes for its Champions League match against LASK in Austria, with Kostas Tsimikas taking the captain's armband and Jeremie Frimpong, Trey Nyoni, and Rio Ngumoha entering the starting lineup.

Kostas Tsimikas captained Liverpool at Raiffeisen Arena in Austria for the Champions League encounter, marking a significant rotation from the squad that fell to Manchester City on Sunday. Four players entered the starting eleven: Frimpong, Nyoni, Ngumoha, and Tsimikas himself, while Virgil van Dijk, Milos Kerkez, Alexis Mac Allister, and Victor Munoz were rested. The lineup showed Alisson in goal, with Frimpong, Araujo, Jacquet, and Tsimikas across the back line. Nyoni and Gravenberch anchored the midfield, while Ngumoha, Wirtz, Barcola, and Koumas operated in advanced positions. Liverpool deployed a full bench of substitutes including Van Dijk and other regulars available to enter the match. The rotation reflected manager Andoni Iraola's approach to balancing European competition with domestic demands after the recent Manchester City defeat, ensuring fresh legs for the Austrian test.
