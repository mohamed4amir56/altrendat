Erling Haaland Chases Champions League Record Against Visiting Paris Saint-Germain Tonight

By Karim Fouad · Published 14 Oct 2026, 19:31 UTC

Manchester City hosts Paris Saint-Germain in tonight's Champions League clash with Erling Haaland aiming to become the club's all-time top scorer in the competition.

Quick facts

Date
October 14, 2026
Kick-off time
8pm BST
Haaland current goals
36 goals in 40 appearances
Recent form
7 wins from 7 matches

Erling Haaland enters tonight's match against Paris Saint-Germain with 36 goals in 40 Champions League appearances, currently level with Sergio Agüero who reached the same tally in 64 games. A single goal against the visitors would see the Norwegian striker set a new Manchester City record, while a hat-trick would lift him above Ruud van Nistelrooy in the all-time Champions League scoring charts. The home side enters the match on a perfect run under manager Enzo Maresca, who has masterminded seven wins from seven matches, including a recent 3-0 victory at Liverpool.

This fixture carries significant weight as City seeks to build upon their 2-0 matchday one win against FC Porto. The meeting marks the ninth encounter between the two clubs, with City having won four of the previous eight matches and remaining unbeaten in all four home games against the French side. For the hosts, Elliot Anderson makes his first Champions League start, while Phil Foden returns to the squad following a suspension.

Manchester City has confirmed three changes to their starting eleven from the weekend win at Liverpool, with Jérémy Doku, Abdukodir Khusanov, and Joško Gvardiol entering the line-up. Rúben Dias and Marc Guéhi continue to anchor the central defense, supporting an attacking setup featuring Doku and Antoine Semenyo on the wings behind Haaland. PSG arrives with a lineup featuring Marquinhos and Ousmane Dembélé, looking to contest a series where City has historically enjoyed a dominant home record. The match kicks off at 8pm BST as City aims to maintain their momentum in the current European campaign.
