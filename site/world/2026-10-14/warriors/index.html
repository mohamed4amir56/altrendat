Golden State Warriors Cruise Past Lakers in 101-89 Preseason Win at T-Mobile

By Karim Fouad · Published 14 Oct 2026, 05:01 UTC

The Golden State Warriors defeated the Los Angeles Lakers 101-89 at T-Mobile Arena on Tuesday night.

Quick facts

Final Score
Warriors 101, Lakers 89
Venue

Next Game
Friday against Denver Nuggets
Regular Season Opener
October 21 vs Warriors

While center Walker Kessler made his long-awaited debut for the Lakers, the team faced a tough night against the Golden State Warriors, falling 101-89 in Las Vegas. The result marked another preseason victory for Golden State, who previously beat Los Angeles 124-98 at the Chase Center earlier this month. The Lakers were without six-time All-Star Luka Doncic, who sat out the matchup for rest despite coach JJ Redick confirming the star guard is not nursing any current injuries. Redick characterized the game as part of a developmental process, aiming to refine the team's sequencing and scout presentation ahead of the regular season opener. Austin Reaves paced the Lakers offense, contributing 28 points on 10-for-18 shooting, though the team could not overcome the Warriors' defensive adjustments. Kessler showed promise in his first appearance, recording 11 points and nine rebounds, including a sequence where he blocked a shot by Charles Bassey from behind. However, the Warriors forced Kessler to defend the perimeter when they moved to a small-ball lineup, testing the center's mobility outside of his preferred paint area. The Lakers look ahead to one final preseason contest against the Denver Nuggets this Friday at Crypto.com Arena. The two teams are scheduled to meet again for the regular season opener on October 21 at the Lakers' home arena.
