Heavy rainfall surges through Awakino Gorge forcing highway down to single lane

By Karim Fouad · Published 15 Oct 2026, 04:00 UTC

Flooding on State Highway 3 in the Awakino Gorge reduced the route to one lane today following intense rainfall.

Motorists traveling between New Plymouth and Hamilton are facing significant travel delays after heavy rain turned the Awakino Gorge into a restricted zone. Stop/go traffic management is currently active on the route to ensure vehicle safety through the water-logged corridor after initial closures earlier today. The situation remains volatile as a cold snap sweeps across the country, bringing not only persistent rain but also the threat of plummeting temperatures and alpine snowfall. This regional disruption highlights the impact of a broader weather pattern moving across the North Island. The New Zealand Transport Agency Waka Kotahi is advising drivers to exercise extreme caution when navigating the gorge. With the weather event shifting north, authorities are monitoring conditions closely to manage traffic flow and keep the vital highway link accessible despite the inundation. While the worst of the rain is expected to clear by Friday, the immediate focus remains on maintaining the functionality of state highways as cold air moves in from the Southern Ocean. Travelers should prepare for potential delays throughout the day as emergency measures remain in place to manage the flooded lane. Further heavy rain is also forecast for regions including Wairoa and northern Bay of Plenty tomorrow, alongside strong winds affecting Wellington and the coast south of Taranaki. Motorists are urged to avoid driving through standing floodwaters and to stay updated on road status alerts before departing. The combination of localized flooding and rapidly dropping temperatures across the nation marks a significant challenge for travel safety during this spring weather shift.
