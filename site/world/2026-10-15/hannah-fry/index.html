Math Professor Hannah Fry Deploys High-Stakes Double Bluff on The Celebrity Traitors

By Karim Fouad · Published 15 Oct 2026, 21:00 UTC

Professor Hannah Fry is utilizing a sophisticated double bluff strategy to avoid banishment on the second series of The Celebrity Traitors.

Standing at a model height of 6ft, Professor Hannah Fry is currently navigating the intense psychological landscape of a Scottish castle. While many players on The Celebrity Traitors rely on stealth, Fry is leaning into the suspicion directed at her by openly agreeing with fellow contestants who identified her as an obvious recruitment choice for the traitor team. This counter-intuitive approach serves as a high-stakes double bluff, designed to convince the faithful that a true traitor would never be so blatant about their position.

For viewers at home, this tactical display offers a rare look at how academic logical thinking can be applied to competitive reality television. By acknowledging the heat on her at the recent roundtable, Fry is attempting to neutralize the threat of banishment that previously claimed predecessor Richard E. Grant, who failed in a similar attempt to use extreme honesty as a defense. Her success in the game now hinges on whether this sophisticated maneuver can outwit her peers' perceptions of her intelligence.

According to Heart Radio, Fry’s background as the first Cambridge University professor of Public Understanding of Mathematics has informed her public profile, leading to her roles in broadcasting, including her podcast The Rest Is Science and documentary work. Outside of the game, she is a mother of two who maintains an active public career. Good Housekeeping notes that her current standing in the game remains precarious, as she continues to balance the scrutiny of being perceived as a logical threat against the necessity of blending into the cast.

The next episodes will determine if Fry’s strategy successfully keeps her safe from the upcoming roundtables or if the increased attention will lead to her removal from the competition.
