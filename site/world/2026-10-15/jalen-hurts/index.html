Philadelphia Eagles bench quarterback Jalen Hurts after blood clot movement detected

By Karim Fouad · Published 15 Oct 2026, 17:31 UTC

Jalen Hurts will miss the upcoming game against the Panthers following medical findings regarding a small blood clot in his calf.

vs. Panthers

Status
Out for Sunday's game

A follow-up ultrasound conducted on Wednesday night revealed movement in a small blood clot in Jalen Hurts's calf, leading the Philadelphia Eagles to sideline the quarterback for this Sunday’s matchup against the Panthers. The team confirmed that while Hurts played without limitation during the previous game in London, his health and safety remain the highest priority as they determine the appropriate course of treatment. The situation began on October 8th when Hurts sought treatment for a calf injury he reported sustaining during the October 4th game against the Rams. Despite practicing in full, the team performed an ultrasound in London on October 10th following a team walkthrough. That initial scan identified a superficial blood clot, though medical staff at the time concluded that activity and mobility were safe, allowing him to participate fully in the game. According to the Eagles, the decision to sit Hurts for the Panthers game follows internal reviews and ongoing communication with the NFL office. While Hurts previously described the injury as a unique experience during a press conference, the team's official statement clarifies that the decision to bench him is based on the results of the latest diagnostic imaging. As the team manages his recovery, the league’s medical protocols regarding player participation and injury reporting remain in effect. The organization stated they are currently evaluating the necessary medical steps to ensure his return to health before he resumes active play.
