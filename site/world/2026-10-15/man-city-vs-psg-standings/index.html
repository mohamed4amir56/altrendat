PSG storms Etihad as holders topple Manchester City in 4-2 thriller

By Karim Fouad · Published 15 Oct 2026, 04:00 UTC

Paris Saint-Germain secured a 4-2 victory over Manchester City in a Champions League clash featuring a brace from Erling Haaland.

Quick facts

Final Score
Manchester City 2-4 PSG
Competition
Champions League
City Record
Erling Haaland 38 goals in 41 appearances
Venue
Etihad Stadium

Erling Haaland etched his name into the record books despite the loss, scoring a double to reach 38 goals in 41 Champions League appearances. This clinical performance made him Manchester City's all-time leading scorer in the competition, though his efforts could not overcome the three-goal deficit established in a frantic first half.

The defeat marks a difficult home return for Manchester City following an independent commission's guilty verdict regarding widespread financial irregularities. Supporters filled the stadium with banners and chants of solidarity, while former manager Pep Guardiola watched from the stands alongside club leadership in a high-profile show of support for the embattled club.

Paris Saint-Germain dominated the opening period with a masterclass of attacking football. Desire Doue opened the scoring after 13 minutes, with Ousmane Dembele adding a second just two minutes later. Vitinha extended the lead to 3-0 by the 32nd minute. Although Haaland twice pulled goals back for the hosts, Maghnes Akliouche added a fourth for the visitors to cement the victory, even as the French side missed a late penalty.

City remains focused on domestic competition, having recently secured a 3-0 victory against Liverpool on Sunday. The club currently sits at the top of the Premier League table despite the ongoing uncertainty surrounding their future and potential sanctions related to the financial charges.
