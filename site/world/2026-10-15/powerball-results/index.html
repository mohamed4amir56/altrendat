Tennessee Powerball player snatches $150,000 prize after opting for 3X Power Play

By Karim Fouad · Published 15 Oct 2026, 10:31 UTC

A Nashville Powerball player won $150,000 in the Monday drawing by matching four white-ball numbers and the Powerball.

The $150,000 prize claimed in Nashville resulted from a base win of $50,000 that was tripled through the Power Play feature. This upgrade requires an additional $1 investment per ticket, allowing winners to multiply non-jackpot prizes significantly when the 3X multiplier is drawn. The winning ticket was purchased at Laverte’s Market located on 51st Ave. North.

For lottery participants, these results underscore the impact of optional multipliers on final payouts. While the jackpot remains the primary draw for most players, secondary prizes offer substantial returns for those who correctly select four white-ball numbers and the Powerball, especially when choosing the Power Play option to boost their potential earnings.

The Tennessee Lottery confirmed the successful ticket matched the numbers 22, 28, 38, 51, 69 and Powerball 9 during the Monday drawing. Additionally, a separate winning ticket for the Daily Tennessee Jackpot worth $490,000 was sold at Fast Stop on Highway 22 North in Wildersville for the October 12 drawing, which featured the winning numbers 18, 26, 30, 32 and 34.

Players are now looking ahead to the next Powerball drawing. The jackpot has climbed to an estimated $562 million for the Wednesday, October 14, 2026 drawing, with a cash value estimated at $230.4 million before taxes.
