James Talarico leads Republican Ken Paxton in competitive Texas US Senate race

By Karim Fouad · Published 15 Oct 2026, 07:01 UTC

Democratic nominee James Talarico is currently holding a narrow lead over Republican Ken Paxton as he campaigns for a Texas Senate seat.

The Texas Senate race has tightened significantly, with recent polling data placing Democrat James Talarico ahead of Republican candidate Ken Paxton. The Democratic hopeful is attempting to break a decades-long losing streak for his party, which has not secured a Senate seat in the state since the late 1980s. Talarico’s campaign is banking on newcomer energy and a push to attract voters disillusioned by current political dynamics. According to The Guardian, Paxton declined an invitation to participate in the recent CNN town hall event where Talarico faced direct questioning from undecided voters.

This contest carries high stakes as both candidates vie for the support of independent voters in a state traditionally dominated by the Republican Party. The outcome hinges on Talarico’s ability to frame himself as a moderate voice while navigating significant policy disagreements on issues like reproductive rights and foreign aid. The ability to bridge these divides is essential for Talarico, who is attempting to capture a seat that has remained out of reach for Democrats for nearly 40 years.

During a televised town hall on Wednesday evening, Talarico addressed voter concerns regarding his religious remarks and his pro-choice position. When confronted by a voter about the biblical stance on abortion, Talarico argued that reducing unplanned pregnancies could serve as a shared public policy goal, despite differing moral conclusions. He also addressed a past provocative comment regarding his description of God, stating he intended to emphasize the theological concept that divinity cannot be limited by human categories. Throughout the session, the candidate consistently positioned himself against the interests of the ultra-wealthy, attempting to establish common ground with everyday Texans.
