Trump tells press secretary Katie Zacharia to reconsider role after five days

By Karim Fouad · Published 16 Oct 2026, 18:31 UTC

President Trump is transitioning Katie Zacharia out of the White House press secretary role just five days after her initial appointment.

Quick facts

Duration in role
5 days
First appearance length
Less than 15 minutes
Questions taken on Tuesday
3

Katie Zacharia’s tenure as White House press secretary lasted less than 15 minutes during her first public appearance on Tuesday, a debut that saw her take only three questions from reporters. Following a week of intense scrutiny, President Trump stated publicly that he told her she does not have to remain in the position if she does not want to, advising her that if he were in her shoes, he would reconsider the job. The White House has confirmed that Zacharia is currently transitioning out of her post.

The departure follows widespread questions regarding Zacharia’s professional background. According to the BBC, the primary point of contention involves her legal credentials; President Trump had originally introduced her as an attorney, despite the fact that Zacharia has never passed the bar exam and does not meet the legal definition for the title. Her week in the role was largely defined by fielding aggressive questioning from conservative news outlets regarding her preparedness and professional qualifications.

For the administration, the resignation highlights the ongoing difficulties in maintaining stable leadership within the communications office. The rapid turnover of a key spokesperson affects how the White House manages its messaging and interaction with the press corps. The scrutiny directed at Zacharia during her short time in office centers on the accuracy of the biographical information provided during her hiring process. While the White House has announced her transition out, there has been no formal timeline provided for the naming of a successor.
