Quentin Tarantino Returns to Graham Norton's Sofa After Over a Decade

By Karim Fouad · Published 16 Oct 2026, 22:30 UTC

Director Quentin Tarantino makes his first appearance on The Graham Norton Show in more than 10 years to discuss his new West End farce, The Popinjay Cavalier.

Quick facts

Program
The Graham Norton Show

Featured Project

Quentin Tarantino is breaking a long hiatus from the popular BBC One chat show, marking his first return to the red sofa in more than a decade. The director is set to discuss his latest creative project, a West End stage production titled The Popinjay Cavalier, which features actor Sacha Baron Cohen in a starring role. The rare television interview provides a look into the director's transition from cinema to live stage comedy.

Fans of the director can expect insights into his work on the farce, which represents a shift in medium for the filmmaker. Tarantino’s appearance is scheduled for the 10.40pm broadcast on BBC One, occupying a high-profile slot on the network. His presence on the program highlights the show's focus on major entertainment industry figures and their current projects.

Alongside the director, the episode features a diverse group of guests. Chat show host Ellen DeGeneres will join the conversation, as will actor Colin Farrell, who is appearing to discuss a documentary about a friend. Additionally, actress Gemma Chan is scheduled to appear to promote her film Josephine, in which she stars with Channing Tatum. Musical entertainment for the evening will be provided by Suede, who are performing a track from their latest album, Antidepressants.
