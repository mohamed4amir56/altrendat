Wallabies hunt redemption against All Blacks in final 2026 Bledisloe Cup clash

By Karim Fouad · Published 17 Oct 2026, 05:00 UTC · Updated 17 Oct 2026, 05:31 UTC

Australia hosts New Zealand at Accor Stadium in Sydney today for the second and final match of the 2026 Bledisloe Cup.

Quick facts

Date
17 October 2026
Venue
Accor Stadium, Sydney
Kick-off Time
4pm local time
Event
Bledisloe Cup

The Wallabies enter this final fixture looking to bounce back from a loss in Auckland last weekend, a defeat that marked their first setback in six matches. While the outcome of the silver trophy series may already be decided, the Sydney encounter represents Australia's final chance to perform on home soil for the 2026 season.

This match serves as a critical test for coach Les Kiss, who is navigating a roster impacted by injuries, suspensions, and the absence of European-based players. The team faces significant depth concerns following the loss in New Zealand, with the squad needing to reshuffle its playmaker options at No 10 to remain competitive against a top-tier opponent.

According to The Guardian, the Wallabies will be forced to compete without key players including Will Skelton, Len Ikitau, and Carter G. The personnel shortage places additional pressure on the remaining squad members to demonstrate resilience after a difficult performance last week. The match will determine whether Australia can end their home campaign with a victory before finishing their seasonal commitments. This physical test against the All Blacks will reveal if the current group can sustain intensity despite the evident lack of rotational depth throughout the roster.
