Head injury forces Martínez off in Chelsea's match against Everton

By Karim Fouad · Published 17 Oct 2026, 13:30 UTC

Chelsea goalkeeper Emiliano Martínez suffered a head injury from a collision with Everton defender Jarrad Branthwaite during the Premier League fixture and was unable to continue.

Emiliano Martínez's afternoon came to an abrupt end when he collided with Everton defender Jarrad Branthwaite in a frightening moment that brought play to a halt. The 33-year-old Argentine, who had already made several saves in the highly physical contest, required immediate medical attention and was unable to continue playing.

Martínez has established himself as Chelsea's first-choice goalkeeper since arriving in London during the summer transfer window. His absence creates a personnel issue for Chelsea during a busy period of the season, and the club has not yet announced an official diagnosis or timeline for his return.

Martínez arrived at Chelsea from Aston Villa on a three-year contract in August 2026 and switched to the No.1 shirt shortly after joining. The goalkeeper had started in the seventh-round Premier League fixture against Everton at Hill Dickinson Stadium before the collision forced him from the field. Chelsea have not disclosed the exact severity of the injury, leaving uncertainty about when the World Cup winner might return to action.
