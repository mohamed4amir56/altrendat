All Blacks Rout Wallabies 43-21 to Clinch 24th Successive Bledisloe Cup Title

By Karim Fouad · Published 17 Oct 2026, 19:01 UTC

New Zealand secured a dominant 43-21 win over Australia in Sydney on Saturday, completing a clean sweep of the Bledisloe Cup series.

Quick facts

Final Score
43-21
Location
Accor Stadium, Sydney
Attendance
73,000
Bledisloe Cup streak
24th successive year

The Wallabies suffered a devastating collapse at Accor Stadium, coughing up 18 turnovers as the All Blacks surged to a 43-7 lead by the 42nd minute. While the match began with breathless, end-to-end rugby that resembled a heavyweight boxing contest, New Zealand’s clinical finishing ultimately left the hosts punch drunk and struggling to maintain their footing on the pitch. By the time Josh Moorby crossed for his second try, the visitors had effectively dismantled the Australian defence, showcasing a level of speed and cohesive handling that proved impossible for the home side to contain.

This victory marks a 24th successive year of Bledisloe Cup dominance for New Zealand, confirming their status as the superior force in the annual series. For Australia, the result serves as a stark reflection of a difficult campaign, as they were swept in the series for the sixth year in a row. The match highlights the ongoing disparity between the two sides, leaving the Wallabies to confront significant tactical shortcomings following another comprehensive defeat.

New Zealand’s performance was particularly notable given their injury challenges, losing Jordie Barrett to a calf issue before kickoff and Quinn Tupaea to a head injury within two minutes. Despite these setbacks, Beauden Barrett scored twice, spearheading a rampant attack that produced seven tries in front of 73,000 spectators. The All Blacks’ momentum finally slowed late in the match when they played the final 12 minutes shorthanded due to a yellow card for debutant Chay Fihaki and an injury to Samisoni Taukei'aho that exhausted the bench, leaving the team with only 13 players on the field for a five-minute stretch.
