90% Rain Chance Threatens India and West Indies Series Deciding Fifth T20I

By Karim Fouad · Published 17 Oct 2026, 13:30 UTC

India and West Indies face off in the fifth T20I in Bengaluru on Saturday with the series tied at 2-2.

Quick facts

India vs West Indies 5th T20I
Date

Location
M Chinnaswamy Stadium, Bengaluru
Series status
Tied 2-2
Rain probability
90%

AccuWeather projects a 90% chance of precipitation for Saturday’s series decider at M Chinnaswamy Stadium, with risks climbing steadily from the afternoon onwards. While rain chances sit at 11% at noon, they jump to 34% by 2 PM and 40% by 3 PM, putting the final match of the five-game contest in jeopardy. The India Meteorological Department has added to the uncertainty by issuing a thunderstorm and lightning warning for the city.

With the series locked at 2-2, the outcome in Bengaluru carries significant weight. West Indies recently gained momentum, placing added pressure on the hosts to secure a win on a pitch historically known for high-scoring totals. Given the nature of the previous four matches—which saw massive totals like 250 and 248 chased or defended—both sides are prepared for a high-intensity run-fest should the weather hold.

According to Yahoo Sports, the stadium features a SubAir drainage system designed to manage surface water, which may be vital given the forecast of cloudy skies and potential thundershowers. Temperatures are expected to remain between 20 and 29 degrees Celsius throughout the day. The match serves as the final fixture of the series, with India and West Indies previously trading wins across venues in Lucknow, Ranchi, Indore, and Hyderabad.

Spectators and officials await final confirmation on whether play can proceed. The series remains finely balanced after the fourth game saw India fall short by 24 runs while chasing a target of 248, setting the stage for this ultimate deciding clash.
