India and West Indies clash in winner-takes-all fifth T20 series decider

By Karim Fouad · Published 17 Oct 2026, 16:01 UTC

India hosts West Indies in the final T20 International on Saturday at M. Chinnaswamy Stadium with the five-match series locked at 2-2.

2-2

The five-match series reaches its peak on Saturday as India and West Indies face off in Bengaluru to settle a series currently tied at 2-2. The decider follows a string of high-scoring encounters, most recently a 24-run victory for the tourists in Hyderabad where West Indies captain Shai Hope notched his second century of the series. Hope has been the central figure for the visitors, including an unbeaten 102 off 45 balls in the second T20I that set the record for the highest successful run chase for his side.

For India, the match carries the weight of history as the team looks to avoid its first home series loss since 2019. The hosts are currently recalibrating their lineup after struggling to contain the West Indies batting order in recent games. According to Yahoo Sports, the team management is considering bringing back Kuldeep Yadav to bolster the bowling attack, while the squad is also monitoring the fitness of left-arm pacer Arshdeep Singh, who sustained a blow to his bowling shoulder during the fourth match.

According to Al Jazeera, the tour has featured record-breaking performances that have pushed the T20 World Cup holders to their limits. While India maintained an early advantage, their narrow 14-run win in the third game proved to be the last time they held the lead in the series. West Indies momentum shifted significantly with Hope's dominant form, supported by recent contributions from Keemo Paul, who claimed a five-wicket haul in the most recent victory.

Officials confirmed that the match will take place at the M. Chinnaswamy Stadium with the toss scheduled for 6:30 p.m. Local fans can access live coverage through the Star Sports Network and the JioHotstar platform.
