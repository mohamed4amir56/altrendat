Kaizer Chiefs storm Zambia to face Red Arrows in continental cup clash

By Karim Fouad · Published 17 Oct 2026, 14:01 UTC

Kaizer Chiefs begin their CAF Confederation Cup campaign against Red Arrows in the first leg of the second preliminary round in Ndola, Zambia.

Quick facts

Opponent
Red Arrows
Venue
Levy Mwanawasa Stadium, Ndola
Second leg date
23 October
Second leg venue
Moses Mabhida Stadium

Nineteen-year-old defender Kabelo Nkgwesa is preparing for his first taste of competitive African club football, facing the reality that the continental arena is far more aggressive and competitive than domestic fixtures. The young left-back, a graduate of the club's youth academy, aims to contribute to a winning strategy as Amakhosi seek a vital result at the Levy Mwanawasa Stadium to boost their group stage qualification hopes.

For Kaizer Chiefs, this match represents a significant opportunity to shift momentum after a frustrating start to their domestic season. While the team remains unbeaten in the Premier Soccer League, they currently sit nine points off the summit following five draws in eight fixtures. With the league title race appearing difficult, success in the CAF Confederation Cup has taken on increased importance for the club and its supporters.

Manager Fernando Da Cruz faces a challenging task as he grapples with a persistent injury crisis. The squad will be missing several key players, including midfielders Lebohang Maboe, Siphesihle Ndlovu, and Adolf Mtasingwa, alongside winger Langelihle Phili and goalkeeper Brandon Petersen. Despite these absences, Da Cruz remains optimistic, noting that the squad for this clash will remain largely unchanged from their recent domestic outings.

The two clubs have never met before, adding an layer of uncertainty to this first-leg encounter. Red Arrows reached this stage after defeating Madagascar's FC Rouge 4-0 on aggregate. Following the match in Ndola, the two teams will conclude the tie with a second leg scheduled for Friday evening, October 23, at the Moses Mabhida Stadium in South Africa.
