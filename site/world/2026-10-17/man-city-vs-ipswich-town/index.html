Ayyoub Bouaddi makes first Premier League start as Manchester City hosts Ipswich

By Karim Fouad · Published 17 Oct 2026, 13:30 UTC

Manchester City faces Ipswich Town in a Premier League match featuring four changes to the home side's starting lineup.

Quick facts

Venue
Etihad Stadium
Competition
Premier League

Ayyoub Bouaddi enters the Manchester City starting lineup for the first time in his Premier League career to face Ipswich Town. He is joined in the midfield by Mateo Kovacic, representing a shift in experience for the reigning champions at the Etihad Stadium. This selection follows a rigorous runaround for the team against Paris Saint-Germain just three days ago. Manager Pep Guardiola has opted for fresh legs, resting central midfielders Elliot Anderson and Enzo Fernandez to manage the physical toll of a dense schedule. Phil Foden and Jeremy Doku also return to the starting fold for this encounter. The inclusion of Bouaddi alongside the experienced Kovacic highlights the club's reliance on squad depth during a high-stakes period of the domestic season. For Manchester City, maintaining momentum remains critical in the title race, with every point vital against newly promoted opponents. Sky Sports reporter Laura Hunter noted from the ground that these four changes signify a necessary rotation following the recent midweek European test. Fans are following the match via live text commentary and score updates as the action unfolds at the Etihad. The outcome of this fixture will influence the standings at the top of the Premier League table as the campaign enters its next crucial phase.
