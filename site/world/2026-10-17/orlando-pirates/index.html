Orlando Pirates Storm Botswana Aiming for CAF Champions League Group Stage Return

By Karim Fouad · Published 17 Oct 2026, 13:30 UTC

Orlando Pirates face Namibia’s African Stars this Saturday in the first leg of the CAF Champions League Second Preliminary Round at Botswana National Stadium.

Quick facts

Match
Orlando Pirates vs African Stars
Venue
Botswana National Stadium
Competition
CAF Champions League Second Preliminary Round
Date
Saturday

Orlando Pirates have navigated their domestic season since August, playing their third opponent in eight days as they arrive in Gaborone for this high-stakes continental encounter. This two-legged series against the reigning Namibian Premiership champions represents the final hurdle between the Soweto giants and a return to the prestigious group stage of the CAF Champions League. Head coach Abdeslam Ouaddou has publicly set qualification for the group stage as a primary objective, noting that the team’s elimination in the previous campaign by FC Saint-Éloi Lupopo has provided valuable experience for the current squad. The match at the Botswana National Stadium serves as a critical opening leg, where the visitors aim to secure a result that puts them in control before the return match held in Soweto. According to official club reports, the Buccaneers reached this stage after defeating Mauritius’ La Cure Waves with a 3-1 aggregate score, a series that featured all four goals during a single performance at Orlando Amstel Arena. While African Stars enter the match having conquered their own domestic league, the Pirates bring the momentum of a season already in full swing. This venue is a familiar backdrop for the South African club, which has played there multiple times in recent years, including a 3-0 victory and a 1-0 defeat against Jwaneng Galaxy. Beyond their continental duties, the team continues to balance domestic commitments, including preparations for a fixture against Milford FC in the Carling competition. The club continues to manage a demanding schedule as they pursue deep success in Africa's premier club tournament.
