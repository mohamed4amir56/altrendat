Allison Janney and The Diplomat Cast Push for Intense Season 5 Renewal

By Karim Fouad · Published 18 Oct 2026, 14:31 UTC

Following the dramatic fourth season finale where Kate Wyler resigns from the Grace Penn administration, the cast is hoping for a series return.

Quick facts

Role of Allison Janney
President Grace Penn
Streaming Platform
Netflix

Allison Janney, who portrays the shrewd President Grace Penn, has openly expressed her desire for the political drama to return for a fifth season. Her call for renewal is shared by co-star Rufus Sewell, who joined her in hoping the series continues after the high-stakes events of the fourth installment.

The potential for further episodes hinges on the aftermath of a tense season 4 conclusion, where Ambassador Kate Wyler publicly resigns from her post during the G7 summit in Italy. Wyler stepped down in protest of the Grace Penn administration’s aggressive military posture, specifically the president’s decision to deploy warships to block Russian vessels from British waters. This departure triggered a cascade of resignations, including those of the Chief of Staff, the Secretary of State, and Vice President Hal Wyler.

Showrunner Debora Cahn is currently evaluating how the story might proceed, maintaining that she wants the series to remain a living organism rather than a static production. According to ELLE, Cahn has a vision for the characters but remains open to evolving the narrative based on their growth throughout the series. While a formal renewal has yet to be announced, the fourth season is currently streaming on Netflix, highlighting the confrontation between Wyler and the president portrayed by Janney.

Fans are left waiting for confirmation on whether the show will return to explore the fallout of the coup and the persistent threat of nuclear conflict. The production team and the cast continue to emphasize their hope that the story of these characters will move forward.
