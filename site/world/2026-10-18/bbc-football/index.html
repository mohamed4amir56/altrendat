Rangers Miss Chance to Topple Celtic After Late Dundee Equaliser Stuns Ibrox

By Karim Fouad · Published 18 Oct 2026, 15:01 UTC

Rangers drew 2-2 against Dundee on Saturday, failing to overtake Celtic at the Scottish Premiership summit due to a late defensive error.

Quick facts

Final Score
Dundee 2-2 Rangers
League
Scottish Premiership
Venue
Dens Park

A costly mistake by Dan Neil in the 69th minute felt like a balloon popping, according to Rangers manager Derek McInnes, after the defender's mishit backpass allowed Callum Jones to secure a 2-2 draw for Dundee at Dens Park. The result prevented the visitors from climbing to the top of the Scottish Premiership, leaving them one point behind Celtic instead of capitalising on the opportunity to move ahead. Rangers had arrived in Dundee buoyed by a streak of seven consecutive victories, but their failure to maintain intensity for the full 90 minutes proved decisive in the title race.

This draw is significant for the league leaders, as Celtic now has the opportunity to extend their cushion to four points when they face Hearts on Sunday. For Rangers, the performance underscored ongoing concerns about consistency, with former midfielder Derek Ferguson noting on BBC Sportsound that 25-minute bursts of dominance are insufficient to secure league titles. McInnes admitted that while the team showed flashes of their potential, they cannot expect to win away from home if they are forced to score three goals to overcome defensive lapses.

According to Sky Sports and BBC reports, the match was a back-and-forth affair. Bradley Fink initially put Dundee ahead by capitalising on a long ball to round the goalkeeper, before Badredine Bouanani equalised with a curling effort before half-time. Nicolas Raskin appeared to have shifted momentum in the 60th minute, firing in a long-range goal that rattled the underside of the crossbar. However, Dundee maintained their pressure until Jones punished the Rangers defence to earn a share of the points.
