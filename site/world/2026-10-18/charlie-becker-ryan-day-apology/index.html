Indiana Receiver Charlie Becker Apologizes to Ryan Day After Heartbreaking 19-15 Loss

By Karim Fouad · Published 18 Oct 2026, 11:01 UTC

Indiana wide receiver Charlie Becker sought out Ohio State head coach Ryan Day after Saturday's game to apologize for a viral taunt from last season.

Quick facts

Final Score
Ohio State 19, Indiana 15
Venue
Memorial Stadium
Next Game
Indiana at Michigan, Saturday, noon ET

Before approaching Ryan Day on the field at Memorial Stadium, Charlie Becker hauled in nine catches for a career-high 165 yards during Indiana's narrow 19-15 defeat to Ohio State. This statistical performance came in the same stadium where the Hoosiers' 22-game winning streak finally came to an end after their undefeated run last season. Despite the intensity of the matchup, Becker made a deliberate effort to find the Buckeyes coach to address a controversy stemming from the previous year's Big Ten Championship Game. During that trophy presentation, Becker had gone viral for mocking the Ohio State fan base with an offensive chant.

The apology carries weight due to Becker’s history as a lifelong Ohio State fan who grew up in an Ohio State household. He told Day that his previous actions were uncharacteristic and expressed deep regret for the disrespect shown to the program he once dreamed of playing for. According to FOX Sports, Day appeared to accept the gesture, giving the receiver a friendly slap on the helmet before the two parted ways.

This incident highlights a moment of personal accountability following a heated conference rivalry game. For the Buckeyes, the win served as revenge for their 13-10 loss in last year's Big Ten title game. As the college football season progresses, both programs remain in contention for a potential rematch in the upcoming Big Ten Championship, depending on the status of Ohio State's roster following recent injury reports. Indiana prepares to face Michigan in Ann Arbor next Saturday at noon ET, while Ohio State heads into a bye week before traveling to play USC on Halloween.
