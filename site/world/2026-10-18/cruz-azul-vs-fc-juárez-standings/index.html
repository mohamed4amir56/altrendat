Cruz Azul Routs FC Juarez 5-0 to Surge Into Fifth Place Standings

By Karim Fouad · Published 18 Oct 2026, 06:31 UTC

Cruz Azul secured a commanding 5-0 home victory against FC Juarez on matchday 12 of the Liga MX season.

Five different players found the back of the net as Cruz Azul dismantled their opponents to secure a vital win at Banorte Stadium. Montaño, Charly Rodríguez, Campos, Palavecino, and González all contributed goals to the dominant 5-0 scoreline, helping La Máquina climb to fifth place in the league standings.

This emphatic result marks a crucial turning point for the current Liga MX champions as they navigate the final stretch of the regular season. After failing to capitalize on a man-advantage against Pumas UNAM in their previous outing, the team required this performance to regain momentum and confidence, moving past their recent struggles in offensive efficiency.

For FC Juarez, the heavy defeat serves as another setback in a difficult Apertura 2026 campaign. Previously marked by an unwanted record as the first team in Liga MX history to lose their opening eight matchdays, the visitors arrived in Mexico City hoping to build on isolated positive results against Tigres and Tijuana. However, those performances occurred exclusively at their home ground, and they were unable to contain Cruz Azul's surging attack throughout the match.

With this outcome, Cruz Azul solidifies its position among the league's top sides, while FC Juarez remains at the lower end of the table. The victory provides a necessary cushion for the hosts as they look to maintain their form and secure a favorable seeding heading into the postseason phase of the competition.
