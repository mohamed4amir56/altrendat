Leeds eye first consecutive league wins over Manchester United in 54 years

By Karim Fouad · Published 18 Oct 2026, 08:31 UTC

Leeds United host Manchester United at Elland Road this Sunday at 2pm with a chance to secure back-to-back league victories against their rivals for the first time since 1972.

Quick facts

Kick-off time
2pm
Venue
Elland Road
Leeds league position
8th
Manchester United league position
14th

Leeds United have not secured consecutive league victories over Manchester United since the 1971/72 campaign, but Sunday offers an opportunity to break that 54-year wait. The club enters the clash sitting eighth in the Premier League table, marking the first time since September 2002 that they have hosted this specific fixture while ranked above their visitors, who currently sit in 14th place.

This matchup carries significant weight for the hosts, who are looking to capitalize on their strong home form to maintain their position in the standings. A win would not only provide three points but also serve as a definitive statement regarding their progress this season under Daniel Farke. For fans, the fixture represents the latest chapter in one of English football’s fiercest rivalries, intensified by the home side's current standing relative to their opponents.

According to the official Leeds United website, the Whites are currently on an unbeaten run of seven Premier League home games, including four wins and three draws. Daniel Farke’s team successfully snapped a 10-game winless streak against Manchester United in April with a 2-1 victory at Old Trafford, where Noah Okafor scored twice. Defensively, Leeds have proven resilient at Elland Road, conceding only twice across three home matches this season and keeping clean sheets in four of their last seven home fixtures. They have also avoided conceding more than one goal in any of their last 10 home league games.

Following the 2pm kick-off on Sunday, Leeds will look to continue their unbeaten home streak, which stands as their longest since a 15-game run between January and December 2001. The team aims to utilize the Elland Road crowd to build on the momentum gained from their recent victory at Old Trafford.
