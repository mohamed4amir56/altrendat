Johan Manzambi Snatches Winner As Aston Villa Downs Struggling Newcastle United

By Karim Fouad · Published 18 Oct 2026, 04:01 UTC

Johan Manzambi scored the decisive goal in a 1-0 victory for Aston Villa against Newcastle United on Saturday.

Quick facts

Match Result
Newcastle 0-1 Aston Villa
Match Date
Saturday
Transfer Fee
Over £50m
Goalscorer
Johan Manzambi

Johan Manzambi marked his 21st birthday week by scoring his third goal in Aston Villa colors, heading home the winner from eight yards out after an excellent cross from Joao Gomes. The midfielder, who was heavily linked to a move to St James' Park before completing a club-record deal exceeding £50m at Villa Park, proved his quality to the home crowd in a result that left Newcastle fans voicing their frustration with audible boos at the final whistle. Manzambi previously contributed to a 3-2 victory over Tottenham and a 6-0 thrashing of Fenerbahce in the Champions League, cementing his status as a key part of Unai Emery’s current rebuild.

The defeat underscores recent difficulties for Newcastle, as the home side struggled to create chances throughout the afternoon. According to Sky Sports reporter Keith Downie, the host performance appeared tepid, with the team frequently overrun and outnumbered in the middle of the pitch. Manager Unai Emery praised Manzambi’s intelligence, physicality, and defensive work rate, noting that the player's immediate impact has been vital for the squad's offensive and defensive setups.

For Newcastle, the afternoon ended with a late, unsuccessful tactical shift that saw defender Dan Burn moved into an attacking role to chase an equalizer. The loss highlights ongoing challenges for the club as they look to address midfield vulnerabilities, while Villa continues to find form under Emery’s guidance. Manzambi, who previously helped Freiburg reach the Europa League final, remains in the spotlight as a nominee for the Kopa Trophy, awarded to the best player in the world under the age of 21.
