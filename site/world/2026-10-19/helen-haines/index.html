Vehicle crash injures independent MP Helen Haines near Wangaratta office

By Karim Fouad · Published 19 Oct 2026, 08:01 UTC

Australian federal MP Helen Haines was injured in a vehicle crash on Murphy Street near her Wangaratta electorate office in regional Victoria shortly before 4pm.

Quick facts

Time of incident
Shortly before 4pm
Location
Murphy Street, Wangaratta, regional Victoria
MP tenure
Federal member for Indi since 2019

Emergency services responded to a vehicle crash on Murphy Street in Wangaratta before 4pm, injuring independent MP Helen Haines near her electorate office. The street was closed in both directions as police responded to the incident.

Haines has represented the Victorian regional electorate of Indi as a federal MP since 2019, when she became the first independent to succeed another MP, taking the seat from Cathy McGowan. The 65-year-old former nurse, midwife and health researcher has been an advocate for better telecommunications infrastructure and the establishment of the National Anti-Corruption Commission.

In a statement, Haines's family said she was receiving medical care and thanked bystanders and health workers who assisted her, as well as police for their response. The family asked that their privacy be respected by the public and media during this time.
