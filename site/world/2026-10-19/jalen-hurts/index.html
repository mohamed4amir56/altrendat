Blood clot sidelines Hurts for three months; backup McKee faces Eagles chaos

By Karim Fouad · Published 19 Oct 2026, 15:01 UTC

Jalen Hurts is out with a superficial blood clot for an estimated three months, leaving backup Tanner McKee to lead the injury-ravaged Eagles through a crucial stretch against NFC East rivals.

Jalen Hurts has been diagnosed with a superficial blood clot and placed on injured reserve, sidelining the Eagles quarterback for an estimated three months while undergoing treatment. McKee, named as Hurts' replacement, takes over an offense already reeling from three straight losses, including two heartbreaking defeats.

The timing compounds Philadelphia's crisis. The Eagles are 2-3 and face a brutal schedule: the Panthers this Sunday, then consecutive divisional matchups against the Cowboys on Monday night, the Commanders, and the division-leading Giants before the Week 10 bye. With 24 players on this week's injury report and five offensive players already on injured reserve, McKee inherits a depleted roster.

McKee's task is steep. A pocket passer without Hurts' mobility, McKee has started only two career games—both meaningless Week 18 contests. Last season against the Commanders, he completed 21 of 40 passes for 241 yards, one touchdown and one interception in a loss. His pocket mobility has been a weakness; he took three sacks in his Week 18 start and two against the Giants this year.

The Eagles' offensive line remains fragile. Right tackle Lane Johnson retired abruptly, left guard Cam Jurgens was traded away, and center Drew Kendall is being monitored for a knee injury. McKee, whose 13 career rushing attempts include multiple kneeldowns, will struggle to compensate for the line's instability. Andy Dalton, the previous backup, was cut after an interception against the Bears in Week 3.
