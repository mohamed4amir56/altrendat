Kylian Mbappe Snatches Brace As Real Madrid Routs Sevilla 4-1 Victory

By Karim Fouad · Published 19 Oct 2026, 05:01 UTC

Kylian Mbappe scored twice to lead Real Madrid to a 4-1 win over Sevilla, keeping the team within six points of La Liga leaders Barcelona.

Quick facts

Final Score
Real Madrid 4-1 Sevilla
Next Match
El Clasico at Barcelona on 25 October
Mbappe Seasonal Tally
11 goals in 11 games
League Standing
6 points behind leaders Barcelona

Kylian Mbappe has now surged to 11 goals in just 11 appearances across all competitions, underscoring a blistering start to his season that shows no signs of slowing down. While the scoreboard reflected a dominant performance, the match featured a triumphant return for Eder Militao, who stepped onto the pitch to a standing ovation from the Bernabeu crowd after being sidelined since April 21.

This victory serves as a vital momentum builder for Jose Mourinho’s squad as they maintain their pursuit of league leaders Barcelona. With the gap currently fixed at six points, the result ensures that Real Madrid remains firmly in the title conversation heading into the most high-stakes fixture on their upcoming calendar.

Arda Guler ignited the scoring in the 30th minute with a spectacular strike from the edge of the box following a lightning-fast counterattack. Mbappe extended the lead seven minutes later after a brilliant individual move that saw him outmaneuver the defense and round the goalkeeper. After the break, Mbappe earned and converted a penalty to bring his tally for the night to two. Although Sevilla’s Manu Bueno found a late consolation goal in the 80th minute, Yan Diomande immediately responded with a powerful solo effort to cement the 4-1 scoreline for Real Madrid.

All eyes now turn to October 25, when Real Madrid travels to face Barcelona in the highly anticipated El Clasico. This upcoming clash will be the true test for the team's ability to narrow the current six-point deficit.
