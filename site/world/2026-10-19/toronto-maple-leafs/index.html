Leafs and Sharks Clash at Scotiabank Arena in Vital Puck Drop

By Karim Fouad · Published 20 Oct 2026, 02:31 UTC

The Toronto Maple Leafs face the San Jose Sharks tonight at 7:30 p.m. ET following a recent 4-1 loss.

The Toronto Maple Leafs return to the ice tonight carrying a 3-4-2 record, looking to regain momentum after a 4-1 regulation loss to the New York Islanders on October 17. Their opponents, the San Jose Sharks, arrive with a 4-3-1 record despite a recent 4-3 shootout defeat against the Detroit Red Wings, providing a narrow difference in point percentages between the two sides as they seek to climb the standings.

This matchup carries significant implications for the Maple Leafs' depth, as the team grapples with a wave of roster instability. Key players including Dakota Joshua, Max Domi, Chris Tanev, and John Tavares are currently sidelined with various injuries. The team faces further uncertainty regarding William Nylander, who missed the morning skate due to illness and is listed as a game-time decision for tonight's contest.

According to NHL.com, Toronto has responded to these absences by recalling forward Vinni Lettieri from the American Hockey League on an emergency basis, while goaltender Artun Akhtyamov has also been brought up to serve as a backup. For the Sharks, Yaroslav Askarov is the projected starter in net. Luca Cagnoni is set to re-enter the lineup after being a healthy scratch in the Sharks' previous game against Detroit.

The game will be available for viewing via SN+ and TVAS, as the Maple Leafs aim to stabilize their early-season performance in what remains a critical stretch of home games.
