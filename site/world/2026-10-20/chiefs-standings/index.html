Cleveland Browns climb the mountain to lead the competitive AFC North division

By Karim Fouad · Published 20 Oct 2026, 04:01 UTC

The Cleveland Browns secured a comeback win against the Baltimore Ravens in Week 6 to take first place in the AFC North.

The NFL landscape shifted dramatically this week as the Cleveland Browns surged to the top of the AFC North following their wild comeback victory over the Baltimore Ravens. The division has quickly solidified its reputation as the most competitive in the league, forcing a total reshuffle of the standings. While the Browns now sit in first place, the Cincinnati Bengals, who entered the week with a 3-2 record and tied for second, have dropped to the bottom of the division standings after the Sunday results. The AFC North's high level of competition is highlighted by the contrast with the NFC East, which officially became the first division in NFL history to record an 0-6-2 winless streak across all four teams over two consecutive weeks. For fans and analysts, the rapid climb of the Browns signals a significant volatility in the AFC race this season. According to CBS Sports, the AFC North is now being viewed as the premier division in the NFL, turning the standard expectations of the season upside down as mid-tier teams struggle to maintain their footing. The surge of the Browns and the slide of the Bengals emphasize the razor-thin margins currently defining the playoff hunt. Analysts are tracking how these shifts will impact the post-season outlook as the league progresses beyond the mid-point of the schedule. Next steps for the division will be determined in the upcoming week as teams continue to adjust to the aggressive playstyles emerging within the AFC North.
