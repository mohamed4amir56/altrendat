Diana Shnaider Awaits Alexandra Eala Following Dominant First Round Victory in Ningbo

By Karim Fouad · Published 20 Oct 2026, 06:02 UTC · Updated 20 Oct 2026, 09:31 UTC

Alexandra Eala defeated Diane Parry 6-3, 6-1 in Ningbo to advance to a second-round matchup against sixth-seeded Diana Shnaider or Yulia Putintseva.

Before the 2026 season began, Alexandra Eala had tallied only 15 total tour-level wins in her professional career. On Monday, October 19, the 21-year-old climbed to 34 victories for the season by dispatching France's Diane Parry in straight sets during the first round of the WTA 500 event in Ningbo, China. The match lasted one hour and 17 minutes, with Eala turning a 3-3 deadlock into a dominant finish by winning seven consecutive games to secure a 6-3, 6-1 scoreline. The victory successfully snapped a three-match losing streak for Eala following early exits in Singapore and Wuhan.

For Eala, the win serves as a crucial rebound after recent setbacks, including a bronze medal finish at the 2026 Asian Games in Nagoya and a loss to Shi Han at the Wuhan Open. Maintaining consistency proved to be the difference on court, as Eala struck 12 winners while limiting herself to nine unforced errors. Conversely, Parry struggled to find rhythm, recording 25 unforced errors throughout the match. Eala's ability to capitalize on break points allowed her to dictate play after the initial exchange of serves.

The tournament bracket now turns toward the round of 16, where Eala awaits her next challenge. Her upcoming opponent will be decided by the result of the match between the sixth-seeded Diana Shnaider of Russia and qualifier Yulia Putintseva of Kazakhstan. This second-round encounter will mark the first time Eala has faced either Shnaider or Putintseva on the professional circuit. As the Ningbo Open progresses, Eala continues to draw significant support from fans in attendance, a factor she credited for helping her maintain focus during difficult segments of her matches this season.
