England Storms Rawalpindi for High-Stakes ODI Tri-Series Clash Against Pakistan

By Karim Fouad · Published 20 Oct 2026, 07:31 UTC · Updated 20 Oct 2026, 08:01 UTC

England faces Pakistan in the second match of the 50-over tri-series following the hosts' opening 37-run win over Sri Lanka.

Salman Ali Agha powered Pakistan to a confident start on Sunday, smashing an unbeaten 107 off 90 balls to help his side defend a total of 305 against Sri Lanka. The pace attack completed the job, bowling out their opponents for 268 to secure a 37-run victory at the beginning of the tournament. This momentum places significant pressure on the visiting English side as they prepare to face the hosts at the Rawalpindi Cricket Stadium. England coach Brendon McCullum noted the team must adapt quickly to subcontinent conditions, acknowledging that playing against local teams on their home ground requires a distinct strategic approach rather than a fixed process. Captain Harry Brook echoed these sentiments, emphasizing that his squad must be ready from the very first ball. The tri-series serves as crucial preparation for next year’s 50-over World Cup, with England entering the contest riding a wave of three consecutive ODI series victories throughout 2026. However, the visitors face the challenge without key personnel, as Jos Buttler remains sidelined by injury and Jofra Archer has been rested for this tour. The format features a double round-robin stage where each team plays the others twice. The performance of both squads in Rawalpindi will be vital in determining who secures one of the top two spots required to qualify for the final scheduled for October 31 in Lahore.
