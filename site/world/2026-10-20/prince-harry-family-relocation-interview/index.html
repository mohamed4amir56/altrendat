Prince Harry Seeks Reconciliation With Brother William And King Charles III

By Karim Fouad · Published 20 Oct 2026, 17:31 UTC

Prince Harry confirmed in a recent GQ interview that he hopes to resolve his complicated family conflicts following his return to the UK.

Quick facts

Interview recording location
New York

Return to UK date
Late August

Prince Harry stated he wants to mend his strained relationships with King Charles III and Prince William, describing the process of reconciliation as the most important step amid global instability. Despite acknowledging that scars remain, he expressed a desire to move past historical grievances and encouraged others to let go of the past as well. During his interview with GQ, he noted that issues regarding sibling disagreements and broken bonds between parents and children are massively relatable, as they frequently emerge in his conversations with others.

This message comes as the Duke of Sussex settles back into life in the UK with his wife, Meghan, and their two children. The couple relocated from California to the UK in late August, six years after their initial departure from working royal roles. Harry emphasized that being able to place his children in a British school has held significant importance for him personally, marking a shift in his family's living situation after years of distance.

While Harry previously expressed a desire to spend more time with his father, who is undergoing treatment for an undisclosed form of cancer, there has been no visible change in the emotional closeness between him and his brother, Prince William. The interview was recorded in late September in New York. A spokesperson for the couple clarified that the timing of the publication was the decision of the magazine and stated that Harry's comments regarding those who might not want the situation to work out were not intended as a swipe at his family.
