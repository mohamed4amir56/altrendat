James Maddison strikes as Tottenham clinches first Premier League win of season

By Karim Fouad · Published 20 Oct 2026, 04:01 UTC

Tottenham Hotspur moved off the bottom of the Premier League table after beating Coventry City 3-1 at home.

Quick facts

Final Score
Tottenham 3-1 Coventry
Maddison's goal time
14th minute
Next opponent
Fulham

James Maddison marked his first start in 18 months with a brilliant 20-yard right-foot finish in the 14th minute, setting the tone for a crucial victory. The 29-year-old, who missed much of last season due to an anterior cruciate ligament injury and a subsequent shoulder fracture, celebrated his opening goal against his former club with his trademark imaginary dart throwing motion. This victory is a significant turning point for Tottenham, who had averaged less than a point per game since his last top-flight goal in February 2025. According to the BBC, the result provides a much-needed lift for manager Roberto de Zerbi, who had employed various strategies from team bonding dinners to a sunshine break in Marbella to spark his struggling side. Before this win, Spurs had won just four league matches throughout 2026. Despite the result, the team remains cautious as they have secured only six wins in their last 37 home league games. Coventry manager expressed pride in his side's personality and competitive spirit throughout the match, noting that his team pushed for an equalizer even when trailing 2-1. He maintained that the squad would not be defined by this away defeat as they prepare for a challenging match against Fulham. The match marked the first time Tottenham scored three or more goals in a home league fixture since the opening day of the previous season. The team now looks to maintain this newfound consistency as they aim to build on the performance delivered against Coventry.
