81st-minute Vitinha strike and Ruiz's late finish sink unbeaten Barcelona 4-2

By Karim Fouad · Published 21 Oct 2026, 19:01 UTC

PSG defeated previously-unbeaten Barcelona 4-2 at home on Tuesday with two late goals, extending the defending Champions League champion's winning streak to three matches.

PSG's relentless pressure in the final minutes proved decisive against Barcelona, with midfielder Vitinha striking in the 81st minute before Fabian Ruiz sealed victory in the 86th at the Parc des Princes on Tuesday night. The victory capped a breathless 14-minute spell that saw both sides trade four goals after Barcelona equalized through Lamine Yamal's mesmerizing solo effort in the 79th minute.

The result ends Barcelona's 11-game winning streak and hands PSG three vital points as the reigning European champions defend their continental crown during the league phase. PSG has now won three consecutive matches in the competition, scoring 14 goals overall, including a 4-2 win at Manchester City the previous week. The French side demonstrated its capacity to overcome adversity despite losing Ousmane Dembélé and Khvicha Kvaratskhelia to injury.

PSG opened the scoring through Désiré Doué's 38th-minute goal, which Barcelona equalized through Xavi Espart in the 55th minute. Lucas Hernandez restored PSG's lead in the 72nd minute before the frantic final stages. Yamal, the 19-year-old Spain sensation, scored his 10th goal of the season when he chested down a botched clearance and ghosted past Warren Zaire-Emery to equalize. Barcelona finished with 50 goals in 12 games this season, but the late collapse denied them another victory.
