Nico Paz scores in under 2 minutes as Como stuns Manchester United in Champions debut

By Karim Fouad · Published 21 Oct 2026, 16:31 UTC · Updated 21 Oct 2026, 17:32 UTC

Argentine winger Nico Paz scored a spectacular goal in under two minutes as Como shocked Manchester United 1-0 in Champions League matchday three on October 21, marking the Italian club's historic first European competition appearance.

Nico Paz needed less than two minutes to make an impact in European football. The Argentine winger struck a goal cutting inside onto his left foot and firing a shot past Manchester United's goalkeeper that the keeper could do little to stop. Como took a 1-0 lead in their debut Champions League appearance, a moment coach Cesc Fàbregas called historic for the city.

The early strike put pressure on Manchester United, who entered the match having gone six consecutive games without a win across all competitions. For Fàbregas, however, talk of crisis was overblown. "The United is a fantastic team, they have great players and an incredible history," the Como manager said before the match. "Crises don't exist in football. We are professionals who want to compete."

Paz's opening goal showcased the technical quality the Argentine has displayed throughout Como's unexpected Champions League run. Playing his number 10 role, he resolved the play with precision and personality, catching the English defense off guard in one of the first real chances of the match. The goal forced Manchester United to chase the game as Como sought to capitalize on their advantage in their historic debut in Europe's elite competition.
