Osimhen returns to France as Lille and Galatasaray draw 1-1 in UCL

By Karim Fouad · Published 21 Oct 2026, 16:31 UTC · Updated 21 Oct 2026, 17:01 UTC

Victor Osimhen and Galatasaray drew 1-1 with Lille in the Champions League on Wednesday, with both sides seeking their first points after opening losses.

Quick facts

Result
Lille 1, Galatasaray 1
Date
Wednesday
Competition
UEFA Champions League

Victor Osimhen led Galatasaray's attack as the Turkish champions faced his former club Lille, adding intrigue to a match where both teams desperately needed points. Galatasaray arrived in France under pressure after losing 3-1 to Sporting CP and 4-1 to Barcelona in their first two league-phase matches. Lille, meanwhile, had fallen to Real Betis and Arsenal and was also searching for their first victory.

The draw leaves both teams in precarious positions in the Champions League standings. Galatasaray hoped to revive their European campaign with a positive result, while Lille aimed to capitalize on home support. Osimhen, who previously scored 18 goals in 38 appearances for Lille during the 2019/20 season before joining Napoli, was expected to be a major test for the French defense.

Yunus Akgün gave Galatasaray an early lead with a left-footed shot from the centre of the box. Lille equalized through Soriba Diaoune, whose left-footed effort from the left side of the box found the bottom left corner, assisted by Alexsandro with a through ball. Osimhen had a chance to put Galatasaray ahead but his header from the centre of the box went too high, assisted by Ismail Jakobs. The match ended 1-1, leaving both sides without the victory they needed to climb the league-phase table.
