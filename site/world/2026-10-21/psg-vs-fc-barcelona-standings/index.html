PSG Routs Barcelona 4-2 To Snap Unbeaten Run In Champions League Thriller

By Karim Fouad · Published 21 Oct 2026, 04:01 UTC

Paris Saint-Germain defeated Barcelona 4-2 in a UEFA Champions League classic, handing the visitors their first loss of the season.

Quick facts

Result

Competition
UEFA Champions League
Venue

Next opponent for Barcelona
Real Madrid

A staggering 40 attempts on goal defined this high-octane encounter at the Parc des Princes, where the reigning European champions ultimately outgunned their visitors to claim a crucial victory. The match saw both teams trade goals before Paris Saint-Germain surged ahead in the final stages, effectively ending Barcelona’s 11-game winning streak.

For Barcelona, the defeat represents a significant setback in their domestic and European campaign, as Hansi Flick’s side tasted loss for the first time in 12 games this season. The loss carries extra weight as the LaLiga leaders prepare for the first El Clasico of the season against Real Madrid this coming Sunday.

According to Sky Sports, the match was characterized by relentless offensive pressure, though it was marred by multiple player withdrawals. Ballon d'Or holder Ousmane Dembele departed in the first 20 minutes with a suspected hamstring injury, while teammate Khvicha Kvaratskhelia was helped off the pitch in the second half. Barcelona also lost Dani Olmo to injury during the first half, further complicating their current fitness crisis.

On the field, Desire Doue and Lucas Hernandez found the back of the net for the hosts, but Barcelona repeatedly leveled through Xavi Espart and Lamine Yamal. The deadlock was finally broken when Vitinha restored PSG's lead late in the second half, followed quickly by a Fabian Ruiz tap-in that sealed the 4-2 win. With this result, Paris Saint-Germain moves to the top of the Champions League table, according to Al Jazeera.
