Temba Bavuma hits career-best 179 as South Africa battles back against Australia

By Karim Fouad · Published 21 Oct 2026, 08:30 UTC

Captain Temba Bavuma scored 179 runs and led a record eighth-wicket partnership to help South Africa reach 425-9 before declaring against Australia on day three.

Captain Temba Bavuma rescued South Africa from a perilous 110-6 to post a dominant 425-9 against Australia in Gqeberha. Battering the touring attack for 552 minutes, Bavuma secured his fifth Test century and his highest career score, effectively dragging the hosts back into the series. The innings was bolstered by a 183-run partnership with Simon Harmer, marking the highest eighth-wicket stand in South African Test history.

For the Proteas, this performance is a massive shift in momentum following earlier batting struggles against Australia's elite bowling unit. Bavuma’s personal form has seen a sharp climb since he took the captaincy, with his average rising from 34.53 to 54.36 across his tenure. The innings proved critical in setting a challenging target, though critics questioned whether the side could have declared earlier to exploit the evening conditions.

The hosts finally closed their innings after Bavuma was caught behind off Mitchell Starc. South Africa immediately capitalized on the fading light, with Kagiso Rabada dismissing Australia’s Nic Maddinson for a duck in the opening over. Umpires eventually called an end to play after deciding the conditions were too dark for continued seam bowling, leaving Australia to resume their pursuit on day four.

Australia now faces the difficult task of batting through the remainder of the match to whittle down South Africa's lead. While the visitors aim to hold out for a draw to secure the series trophy, South Africa’s bowlers will look to exploit the pitch and maintain pressure when play resumes.
