Shai Gilgeous-Alexander Leads Thunder Past Spurs in Statement 116-104 Season Opening Win

By Karim Fouad · Published 21 Oct 2026, 14:30 UTC

The Oklahoma City Thunder defeated the San Antonio Spurs 116-104 on October 20, 2026, as Shai Gilgeous-Alexander scored 32 points.

Quick facts

Date
October 20, 2026
Final Score
116-104
Top Scorer
Shai Gilgeous-Alexander (32 points)
Wembanyama Stats
12 points, 1-for-13 field goals

Victor Wembanyama endured a difficult night, missing his first 12 shots and finishing with only 12 points on 1-for-13 shooting. The Spurs star's only successful field goal did not arrive until an alley-oop pass from De’Aaron Fox as time expired in the third quarter. Wembanyama also saw a separate dunk attempt blocked by 7-foot-3 rookie Aday Mara during a contest dominated by the Thunder’s defensive pressure.

The game served as a heated rematch following the teams' seven-game Western Conference Finals series last May. For the Thunder, the victory signaled a strong start to the season and offered a reminder of their championship pedigree. By leading for nearly the entire game, Oklahoma City proved they remain a top force in the West, even against a rival that ended their title hopes just months ago.

According to CBS Sports, Oklahoma City pulled ahead early with a 31-16 second-quarter performance, securing a 65-44 lead at halftime. Tensions flared three minutes into the second quarter when a scuffle broke out after De'Aaron Fox received a Flagrant 1 foul for dragging Chet Holmgren to the floor. The incident resulted in technical fouls for Wembanyama and three Thunder players—Jalen, Jaylin, and Kenrich Williams. Despite a second-half rally where the Spurs cut the deficit to single digits, Jared McCain added 20 points for the Thunder to help seal the win.

Head coach Mark Daigneault noted that the team continues to evolve its understanding of the opponent through lessons learned from their intense history of matchups. The Thunder successfully navigated the hostile environment in San Antonio to claim the season-opening win.
