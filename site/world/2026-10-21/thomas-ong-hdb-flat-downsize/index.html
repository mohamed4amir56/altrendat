Actor Thomas Ong downsizes to 38 sq m HDB flat for efficiency

By Karim Fouad · Published 21 Oct 2026, 11:31 UTC · Updated 21 Oct 2026, 12:31 UTC

Local actor Thomas Ong moved from a three-room flat to a two-room Flexi unit near Canberra MRT to eliminate redundant space.

Quick facts

Property type
HDB two-room Flexi flat

Location
Near Canberra MRT station

Thomas Ong reveals his 38 sq m home is so compact that cleaning the entire unit takes just 10 minutes of vacuuming and mopping. The 57-year-old actor opted for this smaller footprint after realizing he rarely used the spare bedroom in his previous three-room flat, leaving it empty for nearly 360 days of the year due to his frequent travels.

This move prioritizes practical lifestyle benefits, with the actor noting that smaller living spaces prevent belongings from getting lost in storage. By keeping his possessions within a specific range, he finds that maintaining an organized home is significantly easier. The shift also aligns with his current preferences, as he designed the interior to mimic a sleek, dark-toned gentlemen's club instead of maintaining a traditional living area.

According to Mediacorp's #JustSwipeLah, the unit is located near Canberra MRT station and cost approximately $140,000. Ong customized the layout by removing a sliding door to create a seamless transition between the living and bedroom areas. He utilizes multi-functional furniture, such as a height-adjustable desk that serves as both a coffee and dining table, to maximize the functionality of his new residence.

Ong explained that the dark aesthetic of the new home is specifically tailored for his current stage of life, focusing on rest and comfort. The open-plan design and compact proportions were praised for creating an environment comparable to a hotel room or a high-end train suite, allowing the actor to focus on a simplified daily routine.
