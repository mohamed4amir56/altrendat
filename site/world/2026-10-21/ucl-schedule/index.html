18-year-old Ngumoha inspires Liverpool to thrilling 4-3 Champions League comeback victory

By Karim Fouad · Published 21 Oct 2026, 19:01 UTC

Liverpool secured their third consecutive Champions League league phase win after overcoming an initial two-goal deficit against Villarreal at Anfield.

Quick facts

Match score
Liverpool 4-3 Villarreal
Match day
Tuesday
League phase record
Three wins from three
Player of the Match
Rio Ngumoha

Rio Ngumoha registered 12 touches in the opposition box and completed seven out of eight dribbles during a standout performance that saw the teenager named Uefa's player of the match. The 18-year-old proved decisive in overturning a first-half two-goal trail, assisting the equaliser and setting up the go-ahead goal by outmaneuvering Villarreal defenders Sergi Cardona and Juan Foyth.

This victory is significant for Liverpool as it marks their third win from three matches in the Champions League league phase. The result maintains their momentum in the competition following a rainswept evening that required a collective effort from the squad to recover from their slow start.

According to BBC, Alexis Mac Allister scored two goals, while Ronald Araujo and Florian Wirtz added one each to finalize the 4-3 scoreline. Ngumoha, who was substituted in the 74th minute to cheers from the Anfield crowd, described the encounter as one of the best games he has played. Liverpool's official sources confirmed the match took place on Tuesday, with the club now turning their attention to subsequent fixtures in the ongoing league phase format.
