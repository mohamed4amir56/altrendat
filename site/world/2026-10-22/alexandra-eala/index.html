Alexandra Eala Routs Diane Parry to Reach Ningbo Round of 16

By Karim Fouad · Published 22 Oct 2026, 09:31 UTC

Alexandra Eala secured her 34th tour-level victory of 2026 by defeating Diane Parry 6-3, 6-1 in the first round of the WTA 500 event in Ningbo.

Before the 2026 season began, Alexandra Eala had managed just 15 career tour-level wins; now, she has surged to 34 victories in a single year. The 21-year-old dominated her opening match in China, winning seven consecutive games after the score was tied 3-3 to finish the contest in 1 hour and 17 minutes.

This performance is a significant turnaround for Eala, who entered the match following a three-match losing streak that included early exits in Singapore and Wuhan. For the Filipino tennis star, this win provides critical momentum as she looks to solidify her position in the competitive WTA 500 field.

According to WTA Tennis, Eala displayed superior consistency throughout the match, recording a winner-to-unforced-error ratio of 1.33 with 12 winners and only nine unforced errors. In contrast, her opponent, Diane Parry, struggled with accuracy, committing 25 unforced errors to just 11 winners for a 0.44 ratio. Eala credited the vocal crowd for fueling her play during the difficult stretches of the match.

Eala is now set to compete in the Round of 16 on Thursday. She will face world No. 13 Diana Shnaider, who stands in her path to the quarterfinals, as reported by ABS-CBN.
