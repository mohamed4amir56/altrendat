Bournemouth doubles lead against 10-man Milan in Europa League clash

By Karim Fouad · Published 22 Oct 2026, 20:09 UTC

Bournemouth built a two-goal advantage over AC Milan in their Europa League match after Milan were reduced to 10 men, according to BBC live coverage.

Bournemouth seized control of their Europa League encounter against AC Milan by establishing a double lead after the visitors were sent down to 10 players. The hosts' ability to capitalize on the numerical advantage demonstrated their dominance in a crucial league phase matchday.

The result matters significantly for both clubs' Europa League campaigns. Bournemouth had already impressed by beating Real Sociedad and Sturm Graz in their first two league phase matches, though their domestic form had been patchy until a recent victory over Sunderland. AC Milan, meanwhile, arrived unbeaten in seven Serie A games and had hammered RB Salzburg 4-0 away in their previous Europa outing, making them a considerable threat despite the red card.

Milan's man disadvantage came as a major turning point in the match. The Italian side had been unbeaten in their opening Europa League games aside from an initial loss to Benfica, but the dismissal allowed Bournemouth to press their advantage at the Vitality Stadium. AC Milan's absence of Filippo Terracciano, already ruled out after a red card in the Salzburg win, compounded their defensive problems on the night.

The match represented a significant test for Marco Rose's Bournemouth side, who were seeking to maintain their impressive Europa League record while building momentum in their domestic campaign.
