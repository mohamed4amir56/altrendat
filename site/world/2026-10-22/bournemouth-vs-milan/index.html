Bournemouth doubles lead against 10-man AC Milan in Europa League showdown

By Karim Fouad · Published 22 Oct 2026, 20:06 UTC

Bournemouth holds a two-goal advantage over AC Milan as the Italian side plays with one man down in their Europa League clash.

Quick facts

Competition
Europa League
Venue
Vitality Stadium

The Cherries established their dominance early at the Vitality Stadium, forcing AC Milan into a defensive scramble while playing with ten men following a red card incident. This numerical advantage allowed the home side to pull away and double their lead in a pivotal matchday three fixture of the Europa League phase. Bournemouth entered the match with momentum, having already secured two previous European victories this season against Real Sociedad and Sturm Graz under manager Marco Rose.

For Bournemouth, securing points against a high-caliber Italian opponent is essential to maintaining their perfect start in the competition. Conversely, the result puts immense pressure on AC Milan, who arrived in England unbeaten in seven Serie A games this season but currently struggling to contain the hosts' attacking output with a reduced squad.

According to BBC Sport, the live commentary confirmed Bournemouth effectively doubled their lead in the second half of the contest. Pre-match analysis from LiveScore noted that the visitors were missing key personnel, including Filippo Terracciano, who received a red card in their previous European outing against RB Salzburg. The Italian squad also entered the match without injured players Matteo Gabbia and Alexis Saelemaekers, limiting their options to respond to Bournemouth's pressure.

As the clock ticks down toward the final whistle, AC Milan faces a significant uphill task to salvage any points from their trip to the Vitality Stadium. Bournemouth will look to protect their lead and move closer to securing progression from the league phase, building on their recent domestic form that saw them claim a win against Sunderland just days prior to this European test.
