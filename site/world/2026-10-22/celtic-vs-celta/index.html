Financial Heavyweight Celtic Faces Impoverished Celta Vigo in Europa League Clash

By Karim Fouad · Published 22 Oct 2026, 18:31 UTC

Celtic faces Celta Vigo tonight in the Europa League while currently holding zero points from two matches this season.

Celtic enters tonight’s Europa League fixture against Celta Vigo with a significant financial edge, boasting double the turnover of their opponents. While Celta Vigo faces economic constraints, Celtic’s CEO Michael Nicholson oversees a club with vastly superior resources, including a total wage bill of £37.2 million. The contrast in financial standing puts pressure on Nicholson, as failure to secure a victory would further highlight the club's struggles on the European stage after twice failing to qualify for the Champions League in recent years.

This match represents a critical opportunity for Celtic to recover from a difficult start to their European campaign. With zero points from their first two matches this season and a record of only 11 points from 24 in the group phase last season, the pressure is mounting on the club’s leadership to justify their spending and squad management. Critics point to the decision-making of the CEO, noting that the squad remains under scrutiny following unsuccessful attempts to replace key players like Kyogo Furuhashi and the departure of Nicolas Kuhn.

Manager Martin O’Neill has already confirmed one change to the starting eleven for the encounter. The focus remains on whether Celtic can finally leverage their superior financial position to deliver a much-needed result on the pitch. While other clubs with smaller budgets, such as Club Brugge, Bodo Glimt, and Sabah, have shown an ability to compete and grow within European competitions, Celtic faces internal criticism for what observers describe as a culture of fear and conservatism. Tonight’s match is seen as a definitive test of the current management’s ability to turn these financial advantages into tangible success.
