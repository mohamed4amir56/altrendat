Nasdaq tumbles 2.2% as AI stocks crumble on interest rate fears

By Karim Fouad · Published 22 Oct 2026, 15:24 UTC

The Nasdaq composite fell 2.2% on Thursday as artificial intelligence stocks dragged markets down worldwide, with oil prices climbing to their highest since May amid Iran war concerns.

The technology-heavy Nasdaq composite plunged 2.2% on Thursday as AI stocks faltered globally. Meta Platforms reported negative free cash flow for the first time since 2012, a warning sign that rattled investors already nervous about the sector's prospects. AI stocks have grown shakier as investors worry that high interest rates threaten the boom that drove their gains.

The selloff matters because tech stocks dominate major indexes, amplifying losses across broader markets. The S&P 500 sank 1.4% while the Dow Jones Industrial Average fell a more modest 0.6%, or 295 points, according to Springfield News-Sun. This divergence shows how concentrated the damage was in technology.

Beyond earnings concerns, oil prices added pressure to markets. Brent crude oil touched its highest price since May, driven by worries about the war with Iran. Markets worldwide felt the impact, with Asia experiencing even sharper losses in its AI-heavy tech sector than the United States.
