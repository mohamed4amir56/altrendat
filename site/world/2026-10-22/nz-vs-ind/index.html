India Favored to Clinch Opening T20I Victory Over New Zealand at Christchurch

By Karim Fouad · Published 22 Oct 2026, 06:31 UTC · Updated 22 Oct 2026, 07:01 UTC

India faces New Zealand in the first of five T20Is on October 22 at Hagley Oval starting at 8:00 p.m. NZDT.

India enters the Hagley Oval with a win probability reflected in odds of 1.55, aiming to overcome the host nation on a ground where the visiting side has never played a men's T20I. The match serves as a high-stakes return to action for New Zealand, who look to move past a 96-run defeat suffered against India in their last T20 World Cup meeting in March.

This encounter marks the beginning of a 12-match tour that spans across all formats and extends through December 1. The outcome of the series opener holds significant weight for both squads as they navigate a sequence of five T20Is before shifting to Wellington, Auckland, and Hamilton for the remainder of the short-format schedule.

Both teams face roster challenges heading into the series. India arrives without Jasprit Bumrah and Hardik Pandya, while New Zealand’s preparations have been hindered by the absence of Rachin Ravindra, Mark Chapman, and Zak Foulkes. Mitchell Santner captains the New Zealand side, while Shreyas Iyer leads the Indian team, with the evening match expected to take place in dry but cooling conditions as temperatures drift toward 14°C.

The series continues through November 1, with broadcasts available on the Sony Sports Network in India and Sky Sport NZ within New Zealand. Coverage for the opening match also includes free-to-air access via Three and ThreeNow for local fans.
