Emery emerges as leading target for Manchester United after 4-1 Como humiliation

By Karim Fouad · Published 22 Oct 2026, 17:31 UTC

Unai Emery and Julian Nagelsmann have been identified as top candidates to replace Michael Carrick at Manchester United following the club's worst start to a season since 1986.

Unai Emery has impressed at Aston Villa since taking over in 2022, winning the Europa League—the club's first major trophy since 1996—and finishing fourth in the Premier League, one place ahead of United. The 39-year-old manager has also won trophies with Sevilla, Paris Saint-Germain and Villarreal, though his spell with Arsenal was less successful.

Carrick's job is in the balance after two wins from 11 games this season, with Wednesday's 4-1 defeat to Como in the Champions League intensifying scrutiny on his position. Carrick is expected to remain in charge for Sunday's visit of Bournemouth, with his pre-game media conference scheduled for Friday.

Emery and Nagelsmann have admirers at Manchester United's board level. Nagelsmann, who guided Germany to the Euro 2024 quarter-finals, is unemployed after leaving his position as Germany's manager. He is highly regarded by United's head of recruitment, Christopher Vivell, and is understood to be interested in an approach from the club.

A compensation package could complicate Emery's move: he is contracted to Aston Villa until 2029 and earns between £10m and £12m annually. Other potential candidates being considered include Antonio Conte, Eddie Howe and Kieran McKenna, all currently out of work. Fabian Hürzeler at Brighton is not open to leaving at this stage, according to sources.
