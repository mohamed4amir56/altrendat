Storm Austen floods Ireland: orange warnings for six counties tonight and Friday

By Karim Fouad · Published 22 Oct 2026, 06:31 UTC

Storm Austen brings intense rain and flooding to Ireland from Thursday evening, with orange warnings in place for Cork, Kerry, Waterford, Dublin, Wexford, and Wicklow through Friday morning.

Quick facts

Orange warning period 1
Cork, Kerry, Waterford: 10pm Thursday to 5am Friday
Orange warning period 2
Dublin, Wexford, Wicklow: 2am Friday to 8am Friday
Yellow warning
All counties: 7pm Thursday to 10am Friday
Temperature
10 to 14°C
Storm name
Austen (first named storm of 2026/2027 season)

Met Éireann has issued two Status Orange rain warnings as Storm Austen, the first named storm of the 2026/2027 season, approaches Ireland. The storm will bring intense rainfall and difficult travelling conditions to six counties, with flooding considered a substantial risk, particularly where leaves and blocked drains may worsen water accumulation in smaller river catchments.

The first orange warning covers Cork, Kerry, and Waterford from 10pm Thursday until 5am Friday. A second orange warning extends to Dublin, Wexford, and Wicklow from 2am Friday until 8am. A Status Yellow warning for wind and rain applies to all counties from 7pm Thursday through 10am Friday. Met Éireann Senior Forecaster Gerry Murphy cautioned that despite relatively lower water levels from the dry summer, localised flooding remains possible in affected areas.

Disruption is expected to public transport and road travel. Head of the National Directorate for Fire and Emergency Management Keith Leonard warned road users not to drive through flooded roads. Local authorities have activated severe weather assessment teams and Dublin City Council confirmed its flood response team is preparing for potential heavy rainfall impacts.

Storm Austen is developing in the Atlantic Ocean with help from a strong jet stream as it approaches western Europe. Coastal communities will experience strong winds as the storm passes south of Ireland. Temperatures will reach 10 to 14°C.
