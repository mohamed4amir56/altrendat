Bournemouth Rout Seven-Time European Champions Milan In Historic 3-0 Europa League Victory

By Karim Fouad · Published 23 Oct 2026, 04:01 UTC

Bournemouth secured a 3-0 win against 10-man AC Milan in the Europa League after Mike Maignan was sent off for a first-half confrontation.

Bournemouth boss Marco Rose witnessed his side dismantle seven-time European champions AC Milan in a 3-0 masterclass that moves the Cherries to a perfect three-from-three record in the Europa League. The victory was defined by a chaotic first half that saw Milan captain Mike Maignan receive a red card for shoving Tyler Adams in the face, a reckless moment that allowed the hosts to dominate the match. Justin Kluivert converted the first penalty following a Koni De Winter handball, while Marcus Tavernier calmly slotted home the second after the Maignan incident. Amine Adli capped the performance with a precise free-kick that sailed through a fragile Milan wall.

For Bournemouth, this result marks a monumental milestone in their first-ever European campaign, proving they can compete at the highest level despite their modest history. The tension of the night extended beyond the pitch, as heated exchanges occurred between members of the Italian club's staff and several Bournemouth players, including Adam Smith. Even manager Marco Rose engaged in a sideline argument with Ruben Amorim's staff, underscoring the intense frustration of the visitors during their dismal return to England.

Amorim, now struggling to overcome the south coast side, has failed to secure a win against Bournemouth in five attempts. The Cherries entered the match with renewed confidence following their first Premier League victory of the season against Sunderland just days prior. As the club climbs the Europa League table, the focus remains on sustaining this momentum. Rose praised his squad for their brilliant performance from the first minute to the last, noting that the team handled the pressure of playing against 11 men and the subsequent man-advantage with total composure, securing a treasured scalp that will be remembered by their supporters for years to come.
