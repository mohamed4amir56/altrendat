Herro's homecoming debut fuels Bucks against injury-hit Bulls in Milwaukee

By Karim Fouad · Published 23 Oct 2026, 19:14 UTC · Updated 23 Oct 2026, 20:02 UTC

Tyler Herro makes his home debut for the Milwaukee Bucks against the Chicago Bulls on Wednesday at 4:10 p.m. ET after scoring 24 points in Milwaukee's 128-116 loss to Washington, while the Bulls arrive fresh off a 135-92 rout by Toronto.

Tyler Herro's return to his home state figures to draw attention Wednesday night at Fiserv Forum. The Milwaukee product, acquired in the Giannis trade, scored 24 points and nine assists in the Bucks' season-opening loss to Washington. Now he faces Norman Powell, the All-Star signing who was supposed to share Miami's backcourt with Herro last season but never consistently played alongside him due to injuries.

The Bucks are without Giannis Antetokounmpo on the roster for the first time since the 2012-13 season. Head coach Taylor Jenkins looks to bounce back after his team surrendered a 128-116 defeat despite leading 96-90 in the fourth quarter. Jaime Jaquez Jr. added 22 points and seven rebounds in that loss.

Chicago arrives bruised. The Bulls suffered the most lopsided defeat in a season opener in franchise history, losing 135-92 to Toronto while giving up 43 first-quarter points. Matas Buzelis led Chicago with 17 points, but rookie Caleb Wilson scored 16 while committing nine turnovers. Point guard Josh Giddey managed only 1-for-10 shooting and is probable with a wrist injury. Zach Collins is also probable with a right quadriceps strain.

The Bucks trail Chicago 138-137 all-time in regular season meetings but won three of four last season, capturing both home contests. Chicago's most recent victory came March 1 with a 120-97 win, as Giddey posted a triple-double.
