Celtic collapse in Europe as Javi Rodriguez double stuns home side

By Karim Fouad · Published 23 Oct 2026, 04:01 UTC

Celtic suffered their third consecutive Europa League defeat after falling 2-1 to Celta Vigo at Celtic Park.

Celtic remains anchored near the bottom of the 36-team Europa League standings, sitting in 35th place after three straight losses. The Scottish champions, who currently lead the Scottish Premiership by four points, have conceded 14 goals over their last four European fixtures, mirroring a struggle to translate domestic form onto the continental stage. This recent defeat marks a return to disappointment following a dominant 7-1 weekend victory against Hearts.

The inability to maintain momentum during European ties has become a recurring issue, according to former Scotland attacker James McFadden. He noted that the squad frequently retreats into defensive shells when facing adversity, inviting pressure instead of playing forward. Manager Martin O'Neill pointed to missed opportunities, specifically a Liam Scales header that struck the crossbar, as moments that could have secured points for his team.

After Mika Baur opened the scoring for Celtic in the 47th minute, the game shifted dramatically. Javi Rodriguez responded by netting two goals in three minutes, delivering an unstoppable drive to secure the comeback win for the Spanish visitors. The refereeing also drew focus during the match, with O'Neill citing a missed foul before Celta's equalizer, and earlier penalty appeals for a challenge on Callum Osmond going unrewarded.

Celtic now prepares to return to domestic action this weekend. The team will face Hibernian at Easter Road, with the match scheduled for a 12pm kick-off on Sunday.
