Jablonec stalemate snaps Brighton’s six-game winning streak in UEFA Conference League clash

By Karim Fouad · Published 23 Oct 2026, 04:01 UTC

Brighton failed to score for the first time in 10 matches after a goalless draw against Jablonec in the UEFA Conference League.

Brighton recorded 26 total efforts at goal but could not find the net during their visit to the Stadion Strelnice, marking a sharp contrast to their recent form of 23 goals in seven matches. Goalkeeper Bart Verbruggen secured the point for the visitors with a sensational one-handed fingertip save in the 75th minute to deny Jiri Slama’s curling drive. This scoreless result marks the end of a six-game winning run for the Premier League side, who currently sit third in the English top flight. Fabian Hurzeler, marking his 100th match in charge, made eight changes to the starting lineup that recently defeated Crystal Palace 5-0. Despite the side maintaining 73 per cent possession throughout the evening, they struggled to break down a resilient Jablonec defense. The hosts actually finished the match the stronger of the two sides, creating late danger when Antonin Rusek and Cedidla both missed clear-cut chances to snatch a victory in the final stages. Hurzeler noted after the match that the team did not play at their highest level and acknowledged that the result was not what the club wanted. The draw leaves the Seagulls looking for better efficiency in upcoming continental fixtures after failing to convert any of their heavy pressure into a goal against the Czech team in the Jizera Mountains.
