Wind wreaks havoc at Portman Road as Ipswich host struggling Forest

By Karim Fouad · Published 23 Oct 2026, 19:01 UTC

Ipswich Town face Nottingham Forest in a Premier League match at Portman Road where strong wind conditions are affecting play, with Forest having failed to score in four top-flight games this season.

Strong wind at Portman Road is already shaping the contest, holding up goalkeeper Walton's kicks from his own area and sending Gibbs-White's knock out of play in the opening minutes. The conditions are turning into a factor both teams must manage.

Nottingham Forest arrive with a glaring weakness in attack. The visiting side has failed to score in four different Premier League games this season—more than any other team in the top flight. Manager Oliver Glasner acknowledged the issue this week, saying "I don't see it as a massive problem because we're creating enough situations," though Forest supporters may be less convinced.

The problem centers on the club's £45m summer signing from Chelsea, Liam Delap. While Delap scored with his third shot for Forest this term, he has since managed only one goal in his last 15 attempts. Ipswich's top scorer from their previous Premier League campaign in 2024-25, Delap was brought in as a key attacking addition but has struggled to replicate his early impact as the season has progressed.

The match unfolds amid these attacking difficulties for Forest, with Ipswich hosting at home where conditions are proving unpredictable.
