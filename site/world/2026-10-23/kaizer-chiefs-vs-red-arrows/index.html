Kaizer Chiefs Chase One-Goal Deficit Against Red Arrows For Group Stage

By Karim Fouad · Published 23 Oct 2026, 17:01 UTC

Kaizer Chiefs host Red Arrows in the CAF Confederation Cup second-leg tie on Friday to overturn a 1-0 first-leg deficit.

Kaizer Chiefs must navigate a delicate 1-0 deficit as they host Zambia’s Red Arrows at Moses Mabhida Stadium. The Glamour Boys have struggled offensively, managing only two goals in their last four matches, yet head coach Fernando Da Cruz faces pressure to secure a path into the CAF Confederation Cup group stages for the second consecutive year. Goalkeeper Renaldo Leaner highlighted the need for home support, noting that the atmosphere created by Zambian fans in the first leg was intimidating. Leaner urged local supporters to act as the team's 12th man for the entire 90 minutes to help the squad overcome their current form.

Progression is critical for the club’s standing, as an early exit would add significant pressure to the coaching staff. Defensively, Chiefs have recorded only two clean sheets across ten matches this season, making the goal of maintaining a shutout while hunting for a win a difficult balance to strike. Should Red Arrows manage to score an away goal, the South African side would be forced to net three times to remain in the competition.

Preparations received a boost as goalkeeper Brandon Petersen and midfielder Lebohang Maboe returned to training following injury layoffs. The team conducted their final preparations at Moses Mabhida Stadium on Thursday, signaling their readiness to capitalize on weaknesses identified during the first-leg loss. The decisive encounter takes place on Friday, 23 October, with kickoff set for 18:00.
