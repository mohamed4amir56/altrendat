Miranda Hart opens up about ME diagnosis that forced her off stage

By Karim Fouad · Published 23 Oct 2026, 19:14 UTC

Celebrity Traitors star Miranda Hart revealed she was diagnosed with ME (chronic fatigue syndrome) in 2024, which prevented her from enjoying her career and left her fearing she would never walk again.

Miranda Hart feared she would never enjoy a gentle walk again after her chronic health battle intensified. The comedian was diagnosed with ME (Myalgic encephalomyelitis), also known as chronic fatigue syndrome, in 2024, a condition that prevented her from pursuing her career.

ME is a chronic condition affecting multiple body systems, according to Doctor Amir Khan, who explained the illness on ITV's Lorraine show on Tuesday, 20 September. "ME is a chronic, serious condition that affects lots of parts of the body, including tiredness, the immune system, the brain function," Khan said. "Even a small amount of physical activity can flare up your symptoms."

Hart has also dealt with Lyme disease, which led to periods of being bedbound and housebound. On Celebrity Traitors, she spoke about how her health struggles affected her life. "I've had times in my life when I haven't had that, through illness, and it's brought up that silly, sensitive soul," Hart said, reflecting on the importance of connection and laughter. The emotional vulnerability she displayed on the show—particularly when her fellow contestant Joe Lycett was banished—resonated with viewers and fellow Faithfuls in the Scottish castle.

Hart's openness about her condition came as her odds of winning the BBC series improved to second favourite at 4/1, climbing the leaderboard following episode six.
