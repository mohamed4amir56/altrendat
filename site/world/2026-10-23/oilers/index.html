Connor McDavid reaches 30 points in 10 games, fourth player in history

By Karim Fouad · Published 23 Oct 2026, 04:01 UTC

Edmonton Oilers captain Connor McDavid secured his 30th point of the season during a match against the Carolina Hurricanes to join an elite NHL record group.

Connor McDavid reached the 30-point mark in his first 10 games of the 2026-27 season, matching a milestone previously achieved only by hockey legends Wayne Gretzky, Mike Bossy, and Mario Lemieux. The Oilers star hit the threshold by recording four points during his team's matchup against the Carolina Hurricanes, including a critical goal in the third period that tied the game 5-5. This performance also marked his third five-point game of the young season, an achievement shared only by Lemieux in league history.

This early production places McDavid on a statistical trajectory for 260 points this season, far exceeding his previous career standards. Beyond individual accolades, the Oilers have utilized a strong team structure under head coach Mike Babcock to maintain a 7-1-1 record, a start mirroring the 1983-84 campaign that preceded the franchise’s first Stanley Cup era. The team remains undefeated on the road, with significant supporting contributions from Evan Bouchard, who leads NHL defensemen with 18 points, and Leon Draisaitl, who has tallied 16 points and eight goals.

According to Sportsnet, McDavid’s consistent output has included 23 assists and eight goals through his first 10 appearances. The Globe and Mail notes that the Oilers' only regulation loss occurred against the Utah Mammoth, while their season opener was an overtime defeat to Vancouver. Goalie Devon Levi, filling in for the injured Freddie Andersen, has recorded a 5-1 record to bolster the Oilers' defensive efforts.

Looking ahead, the Edmonton Oilers will continue their five-game homestand at Rogers Place. The team is scheduled to host the Toronto Maple Leafs this coming Saturday in a nationally televised game.
