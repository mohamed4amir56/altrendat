One goal away: Pirates seek CAF group stage berth against African Stars

By Karim Fouad · Published 23 Oct 2026, 19:01 UTC

Orlando Pirates hold a 1-0 advantage from the first leg and need to avoid defeat Friday at Orlando Amstel Arena to reach the CAF Champions League group stage for the first time since last season's failure.

Pirates' home defensive record this season
Conceded 2 goals

Tshepang Moremi's 75th-minute goal in Gaborone gave Orlando Pirates a slender 1-0 first-leg victory, but the Soweto giants know Friday's return leg at Orlando Amstel Arena will test their nerve in a competition where they stumbled at the same hurdle last season.

The stakes are stark: Pirates need only avoid defeat to advance, while African Stars require a two-goal win to progress outright. A one-goal Namibian victory sends the tie to penalties. Head coach Abdeslam Ouaddou stressed the importance of patience and intelligent movement, telling players that "this season Orlando Pirates is a giant in Africa" with stakeholders expecting the club's return to the group stage.

Defender Tapelo Xoki acknowledged last season's disappointment has sharpened focus. "We learnt from our lessons from last season," he said. "We have a lead, but we are playing at home. For us, it's all about the energy tomorrow, getting through." Pirates' defensive record at Orlando Amstel Arena provides encouragement—they have conceded just twice this season, with only Durban City and La Cure Waves breaching their defence.

African Stars arrive as formidable opponents. The teams have met three times previously, producing just two goals, both by Pirates. In their 2018/19 Champions League preliminary-round tie, Pirates won 1-0 on aggregate. The Namibian side showed defensive discipline in the first leg, absorbing pressure for 75 minutes before Pirates scored, and they engineered a comeback against AS Mangasport in the previous round.
