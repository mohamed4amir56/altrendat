Tampa Bay pitchers are currently controlling the pace at the start of the 2026 World Series, with Nick Martinez inducing weak contact to keep the Milwaukee Brewers scoreless in the opening frames. This matchup between the American League’s best team and the National League’s best team serves as a test of talent development and roster depth over high-spending payrolls. The Rays, ranked 28th in payroll, and the Brewers, ranked 19th, successfully navigated their paths to the Fall Classic by outplaying and sweeping heavyweights like the Yankees and Dodgers in their respective league championships. This series matters because it showcases two organizations that have maintained consistent success, with Milwaukee ranking second and Tampa Bay ranking fifth in total regular-season wins since 2020. The tactical approach for the Rays includes utilizing Nick Martinez to start the first game while giving their ace, Drew Rasmussen, an extra day of rest to start in Game Two. The Brewers are expected to rely on starter Jacob Misiorowski, who possesses one of the most dominant four-seam fastballs in the league, averaging 100.6 mph. The current series unfolds amid uncertainty regarding the sport's collective bargaining agreement, which concludes after this season. Both teams have spent the year focusing on marginal advantages, such as aggressive baserunning, disciplined plate approaches, and elite defensive play to secure their place on baseball's biggest stage.
