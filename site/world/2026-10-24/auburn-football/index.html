Auburn Tigers Host No. 18 LSU After Upset Over Georgia Bulldogs

By Karim Fouad · Published 24 Oct 2026, 17:01 UTC

Auburn football returns to Jordan-Hare Stadium this Saturday, October 24, at 11 a.m. CT to face No. 18 LSU.

Quick facts

Game Date
Saturday, Oct. 24
Kickoff Time
11 a.m. CT
Opponent
No. 18 LSU
Location
Jordan-Hare Stadium
Discount Window
8:45 a.m. to 9:30 a.m.

Fans arriving between 8:45 a.m. and 9:30 a.m. at Jordan-Hare Stadium can claim a 50 percent discount on food and non-alcoholic drinks at Auburn-operated concession stands. This initiative, launched in partnership with Aramark, aims to alleviate the significant congestion historically seen at entry gates during 11 a.m. kickoffs. During that same window, 12-ounce Michelob Ultras will be available for $5 at portable brew carts throughout the venue.

Encouraging an early arrival is critical for the fan experience as Auburn Athletics works to mitigate long lines and heavy foot traffic. Gates are expected to see a surge in activity by 10 a.m. as the team hosts its first home game in a month. Attendees are advised to utilize the official Auburn Tigers app for real-time information regarding stadium entry and campus logistics.

Following a bye week, the Auburn Tigers enter Saturday’s contest after a victory over the No. 1 ranked Georgia Bulldogs in Athens. According to Yahoo Sports, the Auburn Daily staff unanimously predicts a second consecutive win for the home team against No. 18 LSU. The stadium will also host the "All Auburn, All Orange" event, marking a return for the team to its home turf.

Navigation around the stadium remains a focus for officials, particularly near the intersection of Donahue Drive and Heisman Drive. Extended road closures following the Tiger Walk, now scheduled 2.5 hours before kickoff, have increased congestion. Additionally, the temporary relocation of visiting team bus operations due to ongoing construction has further impacted traffic patterns. Fans are urged to plan their travel accordingly to ensure a smooth transition into the stadium before the morning start.
