Winless Bucks clash with unbeaten 76ers in Philadelphia Saturday night showdown

By Karim Fouad · Published 24 Oct 2026, 23:01 UTC

The Milwaukee Bucks face the Philadelphia 76ers Saturday night as they seek their first win of the 2026-27 NBA season.

Milwaukee enters this contest desperate for a win after dropping its first two games of the season, surrendering at least 125 points in each outing including a 132-125 loss to Chicago. In sharp contrast, the 76ers currently boast a 2-0 record after securing victories against the New York Knicks and the Cleveland Cavaliers.

This matchup serves as a critical test for both Eastern Conference teams aiming to establish early-season positioning. For the Bucks, the priority is addressing the defensive vulnerabilities that led to back-to-back high-scoring losses, while the 76ers look to maintain their winning momentum in front of a home crowd at Xfinity Mobile Arena.

According to Yahoo Sports, the Bucks are coming off a quick turnaround following their Friday night loss. The 76ers have navigated their early schedule with Tyrese Maxey leading the backcourt and Joel Embiid anchoring the frontcourt. However, roster management is a key factor for Philadelphia; as reported by NBC Sports Philadelphia, LeBron James is listed as questionable for the game due to low back and sciatica management. Dean Wade is also questionable with an ankle injury, while teammates Dominick Barlow, Tacko Fall, and Ariel Hukporti remain out.

Looking ahead, Joel Embiid has indicated he will sit out one game of the team's current back-to-back schedule, meaning he is expected to miss either this clash with Milwaukee or the subsequent game against the Detroit Pistons on Sunday. The team will determine his availability based on their internal load management plan.
