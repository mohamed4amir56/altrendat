Chelsea targets seventh consecutive Premier League victory over rivals Tottenham at home

By Karim Fouad · Published 24 Oct 2026, 16:01 UTC

Chelsea manager Xabi Alonso faces Tottenham at Stamford Bridge today while pursuing a third straight top-flight win.

Quick facts

Venue
Stamford Bridge
Chelsea Goalkeeper

Chelsea Captain
Moises Caicedo

Mike Penders makes his first Premier League start for Chelsea as he replaces Emi Martinez, who is sidelined following jaw surgery. The Blues enter the match aiming to secure their seventh consecutive league win against Tottenham, building on recent victories over Bournemouth and Everton. Manager Xabi Alonso has opted to keep Moises Caicedo as captain, pairing him with Jordan Henderson in midfield as the team looks to maintain its momentum in the table. The defensive line features Josh Acheampong, Maxence Lacroix, Levi Colwill, and Jorrel Hato. Pedro Neto, coming off a two-goal performance, retains his place in an attacking quartet alongside Cole Palmer, Geovany Quenda, and Morgan Rogers. Joao Pedro was excluded from the match-day squad. On the opposing side, Tottenham arrives with a starting lineup led by Kinsky in goal and featuring James Maddison, Sandro Tonali, and Omar Marmoush. The bench for Spurs includes options such as Dominic Solanke and Dejan Kulusevski. For Chelsea, the substitutes list includes Gusto, Fofana, and Lavia, providing depth if the manager needs to adjust tactics during the match. This encounter marks a significant test for Chelsea as they attempt to extend their historic run of success against their London rivals in top-flight competition. The outcome of this fixture will influence the current standings for both clubs as the Premier League season progresses toward the winter schedule.
