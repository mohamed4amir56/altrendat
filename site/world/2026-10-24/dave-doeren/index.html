NC State fires Dave Doeren following 31-28 loss to Stanford

By Karim Fouad · Published 24 Oct 2026, 22:32 UTC

NC State dismissed head football coach Dave Doeren on Saturday after 14 seasons following a loss to Stanford.

The 54-year-old coach compiled a record of 98-74 during his time with the Wolfpack, finishing his tenure as the winningest coach in program history. His departure arrives less than 24 hours after a 31-28 defeat at Stanford, a game in which the Wolfpack trailed early by 14-0 and never held the lead. NC State currently sits at 3-4 overall and 1-3 in conference play.

This decision carries significant financial weight for the university. Under the terms of his contract, which was scheduled to run through December 31, 2029, the school could owe Doeren approximately $9.75 million in remaining base salary. The change in leadership follows a season that failed to meet expectations, starting with a blowout loss at Virginia and a defeat at Vanderbilt, despite initial hopes of competing for an ACC championship.

Athletics director Boo Corrigan announced the firing on Saturday, noting his appreciation for the leadership and passion Doeren brought to the role over 14 years. While rumors regarding Doeren's future had circulated previously, the coach had stated he was not considering retirement. The team's performance has struggled throughout the month, including a 38-14 home loss to Wake Forest earlier in October.

Running backs coach and special teams coordinator Todd Goebbel will assume head coaching duties for the remainder of the season. NC State still has five games remaining on its schedule, including home matchups against California, Duke, and Syracuse, before concluding the season against Florida State and UNC. Meanwhile, the university has officially initiated a national search to find a permanent replacement for the head coaching position.
