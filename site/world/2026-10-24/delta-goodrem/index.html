Delta Goodrem returns to Strictly Come Dancing floor after fractured rib recovery

By Karim Fouad · Published 24 Oct 2026, 19:32 UTC

Australian singer Delta Goodrem is set to perform on Saturday after missing one week of the BBC competition due to a rib injury.

Quick facts

Show return date
Saturday, 24 October
Injury
Fractured rib
Performance
Rumba to Celine Dion's 'It's All Coming Back To Me Now'
Broadcast channel
BBC One

Delta Goodrem captured the emotional toll of her recovery in an Instagram post, sharing hospital clips that documented her progress over the past two weeks. The 41-year-old singer described the experience of being sidelined by a fractured rib as gutting, noting that she had to learn to appreciate small steps while remaining patient during her absence from the ballroom. In her message to fans, she declared that she is back to fight, telling those facing their own battles that she sees them and remains with them as a fighter.

Her return is critical for her continued participation in the competition. BBC rules stipulate that contestants are permitted to skip only a single week of the show before they are required to withdraw. With her health now cleared by medical professionals, Goodrem is set to rejoin professional dancer Kai Widdrington to perform a rumba to Celine Dion's “It's All Coming Back To Me Now” during the upcoming live show.

Goodrem missed last week's performance, which was known as Icons Week. During her absence, Widdrington stepped in to dance with fellow professional Amy Dowden in a tribute to the late musical legend Dolly Parton. The BBC officially confirmed on Monday that the singer had been given the all-clear to dance again this coming weekend. The return of the pair, affectionately dubbed Team DeltaKai by fans, will see all 12 remaining contestants on the floor for week five of the current series. The program will continue its broadcast schedule this Saturday night as the competition moves forward following the mandatory week-long rest period.
