Delta Goodrem returns to Strictly Come Dancing after missing show with injury

By Karim Fouad · Published 24 Oct 2026, 19:30 UTC

Australian singer Delta Goodrem returns to the Strictly Come Dancing ballroom this Saturday after a fractured rib forced her to miss week four.

Delta Goodrem shared intimate video clips from her time in the hospital, revealing the emotional toll of a fractured rib that sidelined her from competition. The 41-year-old singer admitted to feeling "gutted" when the injury occurred, noting that she struggled through days of pain while documenting small steps toward her recovery. In one emotional post, she thanked fans for their support while expressing her determination to push through the frustration of being forced to step back from the experience she said she was truly loving.

Her return is critical for her continued participation in the BBC production. According to show rules, contestants are only permitted to miss a single week of competition before they are required to withdraw entirely. Goodrem’s successful return ensures she remains in the running alongside the 11 other remaining contestants as the series moves into week five.

Goodrem is partnered with professional dancer Kai Widdrington. The pair is scheduled to perform a rumba set to Celine Dion’s "It's All Coming Back To Me Now" during Saturday night's broadcast. Widdrington spent the previous week dancing with fellow professional Amy Dowden in a tribute performance while Goodrem focused on her recovery.

As the competition intensifies, dancers are adjusting to a week without a specific theme, requiring them to rely solely on their technical skills. Judges have faced criticism from fans this week, with many viewers describing the recent feedback as notably harsher than in previous rounds of the competition. Following last week's elimination of Dame Sarah Storey, remaining contestants like actress Bethany Antonia are under pressure to avoid the bottom two as the show continues on BBC One.
