Heat seek redemption against Hornets in first road clash of season

By Karim Fouad · Published 24 Oct 2026, 21:31 UTC

The Miami Heat face the Charlotte Hornets at the Spectrum Center this Saturday at 6:00 p.m. following early season losses.

Quick facts

Date
Saturday, October 24
Time
6:00 p.m.
Location
Spectrum Center, Charlotte

The Charlotte Hornets enter this matchup coming off a 47-point loss to the Atlanta Hawks, which stands as the largest blowout in that franchise’s history. Meanwhile, the Miami Heat are winless this week, despite Giannis Antetokounmpo averaging over 35 points in each of his first two appearances for the team. This game marks the first of four meetings between the two Eastern Conference teams this regular season.

For Miami, the game serves as an immediate opportunity to reset after a difficult home stand that included a 25-point loss to the Minnesota Timberwolves and a defeat against the Detroit Pistons. Coach Erik Spoelstra dismissed the need for rest after the team’s back-to-back schedule, stating that the roster just needs to get back on the floor and compete. The outcome of this game is critical for the Heat as they look to turn around their early season form.

Historically, the Heat maintain an 83-50 record against the Hornets in the regular season. Last year, the teams met five times, with the Hornets securing a one-point overtime victory in the Play-In Tournament. Charlotte continues its rebuilding phase following the offseason trade of LaMelo Ball, while the Hornets have listed Christian Anderson as probable for Saturday’s game due to an ankle injury. Klay Thompson, Bobby Portis, Tim Hardaway Jr., and Nick Richards debuted for their respective squads this week, with Thompson struggling on Friday to find rhythm from beyond the arc. The Heat, who won seven of the last ten overall against Charlotte, aim to improve their 35-31 road record against the Hornets.
