Julio Enciso strikes to hold Nottingham Forest in 1-1 Premier League draw

By Karim Fouad · Published 24 Oct 2026, 04:01 UTC

Ipswich Town and Nottingham Forest shared the points at Portman Road after Julio Enciso neutralized Neco Williams’ opening goal.

Quick facts

Final Score
Ipswich 1-1 Nottingham Forest
Goalscorers
Neco Williams (Forest), Julio Enciso (Ipswich)
Referee
Thomas Bramall

Nottingham Forest finished the match with 17 shots compared to Ipswich's seven, yet managed only a single goal from 2.31 worth of expected goals. Forest have now scored just six times from 13.4 worth of chances created, marking the team as the biggest underperformer in expected goals data this season. The result leaves Forest with only one win from their opening eight games.

This draw highlights a recurring theme for Ipswich Town, who have now secured seven points from losing positions this season, more than any other team in the Premier League. The point earned at Portman Road matches the total points Ipswich achieved during their entire 2024-25 campaign in the competition.

Neco Williams opened the scoring for Forest before a mistake from Nikola Milenkovic allowed Julio Enciso to equalize just before halftime. The match featured significant VAR controversy, as referee Thomas Bramall reviewed a challenge by Ipswich defender Issa Diop on James McAtee. Despite being sent to the pitchside monitor, Bramall opted to issue only a yellow card, ruling that Diop did not deny an obvious goal-scoring opportunity. Forest manager Oliver Glasner expressed frustration with the decision, calling it a clear red card.

Late-game action saw Leif Davis clearing the ball off the line twice for Ipswich. Meanwhile, Liam Delap and Callum Hudson-Odoi both struggled to convert, and Forest suffered a setback when Neco Williams exited the match with an arm injury late in the second half. The draw keeps both sides searching for consistency as the league season progresses.
