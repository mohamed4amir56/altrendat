15-year-old prodigy Vaibhav Sooryavanshi debuts as India bowls in Christchurch T20

By Karim Fouad · Published 24 Oct 2026, 07:01 UTC · Updated 24 Oct 2026, 10:31 UTC

India won the toss and elected to bowl in the second T20 international against New Zealand after inserting teenager Vaibhav Sooryavanshi into the lineup.

Quick facts

Venue
Hagley Oval, Christchurch
Date
Saturday, October 24
India Captain
Shreyas Iyer
New Zealand Captain
Mitchell Santner

The first match of the series featured a staggering 455 runs, marking India's highest successful run chase in T20 international history. Following that four-wicket victory, India returns to the Hagley Oval pitch looking to maintain momentum despite an injury to wicketkeeper-batter Ishan Kishan, who has been ruled out with a groin strain.

For Indian fans, the inclusion of 15-year-old Vaibhav Sooryavanshi is the central narrative of this second clash. His appearance gives supporters in the Southern Hemisphere their first look at the young talent. Meanwhile, India’s bowling attack sees a tactical shift as Prince Yadav enters the XI to replace Mayank Yadav, aiming to stifle a New Zealand side hungry for a series equalizer.

According to The Toronto Star, the Indian lineup features Abhishek Sharma, Sanju Samson, Vaibhav Sooryavanshi, captain Shreyas Iyer, Tilak Varma, Naman Dhir, Nitish Kumar Reddy, Axar Patel, Bhuvneshwar Kumar, Arshdeep Singh, and Prince Yadav. New Zealand, seeking to recover from their opening loss, has named Jacob Duffy to replace Adam Milne in their bowling ranks, rounding out a squad led by captain Mitchell Santner.

Following today's encounter at Hagley Oval, the tour is set to progress as both teams prepare to move to Wellington, Auckland, and Hamilton for the final three matches of the series.
