Kamario Taylor returns as Oklahoma linemen return for SEC Saturday clash

By Karim Fouad · Published 24 Oct 2026, 19:01 UTC

Oklahoma faces Mississippi State in a Week 8 matchup at 3:30 p.m. ET, bolstered by the return of key offensive linemen.

Mississippi State quarterback Kamario Taylor is available to start today after being left off the final injury report, anchoring a Bulldogs offense that averages 32.5 points per game. Meanwhile, the Oklahoma Sooners received a crucial boost as offensive linemen Michael Fasusi and Heath Ozaeta were cleared to play, providing needed stability for an offense currently averaging 21.3 points per outing. While these key starters return to action, Oklahoma will be without defensive back Eli Bowen and wide receiver Elijah Thomas. The team will also wear special helmet stickers today to support offensive lineman Sean Hutton, who remains hospitalized in stable condition following a rare medical event on Wednesday. This matchup is critical for both programs, as Mississippi State looks to end a two-game skid and maintain their position in the SEC standings. The Bulldogs carry a 4-2 record into the game, while Oklahoma enters with a 3-3 mark, still searching for their first road victory of the season. Mississippi State’s passing attack, led by Taylor’s 17 touchdowns, will be tested against an Oklahoma defense that has allowed only 18.1 points per game and recorded 18 sacks this year. Oklahoma aims to reach a .500 record and gain momentum as they navigate a difficult conference schedule. The game kicks off at Davis Wade Stadium at 3:30 p.m. ET, with coverage available on the SEC Network.
