Alexander Volkanovski Defends Featherweight Title Against Undefeated Movsar Evloev at UFC 333

By Karim Fouad · Published 24 Oct 2026, 08:01 UTC · Updated 24 Oct 2026, 09:01 UTC

Two-time featherweight champion Alexander Volkanovski faces challenger Movsar Evloev this Saturday at Etihad Arena in Abu Dhabi.

20-0

Movsar Evloev arrives at the Etihad Arena carrying a staggering 20-0 professional record, seeking to secure his first championship victory after 10 consecutive wins since entering the UFC. Standing in his path is the Australian veteran Alexander Volkanovski, who enters the Octagon aiming for a record ninth win in UFC featherweight title fights, a feat that would surpass the benchmark set by Jose Aldo. This return to Abu Dhabi marks Volkanovski's first appearance in the city in three years. For Volkanovski, this main event represents a pursuit of legacy and challenge rather than simple statistics. After suffering consecutive knockout losses to Ilia Topuria and Islam Makhachev, the 38-year-old took a 14-month hiatus before reclaiming his belt. He now looks to prove his longevity against the undefeated Russian contender, viewing his status as an older competitor as fuel for his performance. The UFC 333 card features a co-main event rematch between bantamweight rivals Petr Yan and Merab Dvalishvili. The event, which takes place in the United Arab Emirates, follows Volkanovski's successful title defense against Diego Lopes earlier this year. According to UFC, the event is scheduled for a special start time, with prelims beginning at 10am ET and the main card set for 2pm ET.
