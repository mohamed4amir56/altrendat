Storm Boelo Triggers 80mph Winds Across Scotland Following Destructive Storm Austen

By Karim Fouad · Published 24 Oct 2026, 05:31 UTC

An amber weather warning is in place for Scotland, Northern Ireland, and northern England as Storm Boelo brings potential travel chaos and power cuts this weekend.

Gusts reaching up to 80mph are expected to lash exposed coastal areas as Storm Boelo arrives just one day after Storm Austen caused widespread destruction. While the National Grid managed to restore power to over 60,000 properties following Friday's winds, more than 10,000 homes remained in the dark Friday evening as emergency teams prepare for a second bout of extreme weather. In Brighton, the structural integrity of the iconic 1866 West Pier failed under the pressure of the previous storm, resulting in a section collapsing directly into the sea.

Residents across much of Scotland, northern England, and parts of Northern Ireland face significant disruption as the storm sweeps through. The severe weather threatens to topple trees, damage buildings, and trigger further power outages. Beyond the immediate risk of structural damage, the combination of heavy rain and high winds creates hazardous conditions for commuters, with authorities cautioning that flying debris could pose a danger to life throughout the weekend.

The Met Office has issued an amber weather warning effective from 21:00 Saturday until 21:00 Sunday, noting the unusual length of the alert over such a wide geographical area. ScotRail has confirmed cancellations for some train services, and Network Rail is implementing speed restrictions across large parts of Scotland. Police Scotland have urged drivers to avoid unnecessary journeys as officials coordinate through the government's resilience room.

Conditions are expected to remain volatile through Sunday, with the strongest winds initially impacting the west before moving eastwards. Meteorological reports indicate that winds will begin to ease gradually from the west as the system passes through the region by Sunday evening.
