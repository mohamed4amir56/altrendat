Barcelona and Real Madrid clash in crucial El Clasico title race

By Karim Fouad · Published 25 Oct 2026, 20:01 UTC

Barcelona faces Real Madrid this Sunday in a decisive match that could effectively end the La Liga title race.

Quick facts

Match
Barcelona vs Real Madrid
Referee
Juan Martinez Munuera
VAR
Javier Iglesias Villanueva
Potential Gap
9 points

Barcelona enters the upcoming El Clasico as clear favorites, holding a significant advantage that makes this match a potential championship decider. If Barcelona secures a victory against Real Madrid, they would establish a nine-point gap, leaving no statistical precedence for a title comeback. Real Madrid identifies this as a must-not-lose scenario, noting that avoiding defeat is essential to keeping their hopes alive before Barcelona's schedule becomes more difficult next month.

The atmosphere surrounding Real Madrid has shifted significantly compared to last season. According to Managing Madrid, team morale is at its highest point in over a year, with manager Jose Mourinho receiving credit for fostering a new sense of brotherhood within the squad. Mourinho has reportedly moved away from past methods that failed to produce results, opting to reshape the team's tactical approach, including utilizing Fede Valverde as an unchained two-way force on the right wing.

Off-field tensions remain high ahead of the kickoff. Sky Sports reports that Barcelona previously submitted a formal complaint to the Spanish FA regarding referee Juan Martinez Munuera, stemming from his officiating during a Copa del Rey semi-final loss to Atletico in February. Real Madrid has also criticized both Martinez Munuera and VAR official Javier Iglesias Villanueva in the past through their own media channels. Mourinho was recently presented with leaked information concerning the match officials assigned to this encounter, adding another layer of scrutiny to the officiating team before the players take the pitch.
