Surging Panthers host struggling Buccaneers in divisional clash

By Karim Fouad · Published 25 Oct 2026, 16:20 UTC

The Carolina Panthers, ranked first in the NFC South at 3-2 with back-to-back wins, face the Tampa Bay Buccaneers at 1-5 on October 25, 2026 at 1:00 PM EDT in Charlotte.

The Panthers arrive at Bank of America Stadium as the league's most prolific passing attack, averaging 295.6 yards per game and 309.8 receiving yards per game through five contests. Bryce Young has orchestrated that dominance with 1,549 passing yards and 11 touchdown passes, putting Carolina's offense on a trajectory that has generated 29.2 points per game.

For Tampa Bay, this divisional matchup represents a chance to arrest a skid that has left the Buccaneers fourth in the NFC South. At 1-5 and coming off a loss, the Buccaneers rank 30th in passing yards per game and 25th in scoring, though their ninth-ranked rushing attack and fifth-ranked defense in interceptions offer potential paths to compete. Baker Mayfield returns as starter after missing three games, while receivers Emeka Egbuka, tackle Luke Goedeke, and safety Antoine Winfield Jr. are inactive for Week Seven.

The strategic battle centers on whether Young can avoid Tampa Bay's takeaway-generating defense, which has six interceptions through six games, while the Buccaneers must generate explosive offensive plays against a Carolina defense that has shown vulnerability. Carolina's two-game winning streak and commanding offensive metrics position the Panthers as clear favorites entering their home divisional game.
