Joao Pedro misses Chelsea match day squad for Tottenham Hotspur home clash

By Karim Fouad · Published 25 Oct 2026, 09:31 UTC

Joao Pedro is absent from the Chelsea squad for the Premier League fixture against Tottenham Hotspur at Stamford Bridge.

Quick facts

Match

Venue

Date

Chelsea Captain

Joao Pedro is not included in the match-day squad for Chelsea’s evening Premier League meeting with Tottenham Hotspur today. His exclusion comes as head coach Xabi Alonso makes tactical adjustments to a side currently targeting a third consecutive top-flight victory at Stamford Bridge.

For supporters and followers of the club, the absence of a key attacker like Joao Pedro forces a significant reshuffle in the offensive lineup. With the team seeking an unprecedented seventh Premier League win over Tottenham, the performance of the starting attacking quartet becomes critical to maintaining their recent momentum following wins against Bournemouth and Everton.

According to the official Chelsea team announcement, the starting XI features Pedro Neto alongside Cole Palmer, Geovany Quenda, and Morgan Rogers. The squad also sees Mike Penders making his first Premier League start in goal, necessitated by Emi Martinez being sidelined after undergoing jaw surgery following the previous match against Everton. The defense remains anchored by Josh Acheampong, Maxence Lacroix, Levi Colwill, and Jorrel Hato, while Moises Caicedo captains the side in a midfield partnership with Jordan Henderson.

While Joao Pedro is out of the match-day squad, Chelsea maintains depth on the bench with substitutes including Gusto, Fofana, Lavia, Estevao, and Welbeck. The outcome of this London derby will determine if Chelsea can successfully extend their winning streak in the league.
