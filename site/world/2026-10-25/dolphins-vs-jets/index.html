Dolphins seek first win against Jets in AFC East bottom-two clash

By Karim Fouad · Published 25 Oct 2026, 16:20 UTC

The Miami Dolphins, winless through five games, travel to MetLife Stadium to face the New York Jets (2-4) on Sunday at 1 p.m. ET in an NFL Week 7 matchup.

The Dolphins arrive in East Rutherford, New Jersey, with an offense averaging just 14 points per game—tied for last in the NFL—and eight turnovers already this season. The Jets, fresh from a 26-19 loss to the New England Patriots, present a chance for Miami to break through after its bye week following a 24-32 defeat to the Cincinnati Bengals.

A win matters for Miami's rebuild, as the team has struggled to execute in its young 2026 campaign. The Dolphins' turnover margin sits at minus-4, double the Jets' plus-4, making ball security critical. Quarterback Malik Willis has thrown for 849 yards and rushed for 186, but inconsistency on offense has plagued the team.

The Jets come in as three-point favorites at MetLife Stadium, with their defense ranking 11th in yards allowed at 308 per game and eighth in points allowed at 20.3. Geno Smith threw for 252 yards against New England, and wide receiver Garrett Wilson has compiled 418 receiving yards and three touchdowns with six receptions exceeding 20 yards. New York could get running back Breece Hall back from injury. Historically, Miami leads the all-time series 63-57-1 and won 34-10 in New York last season, though the Jets have taken home meetings in 2022 and 2024.
