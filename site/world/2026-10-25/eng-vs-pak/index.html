Pakistan eyes tri-series final victory as England searches for first win

By Karim Fouad · Published 25 Oct 2026, 08:31 UTC

England battles Pakistan in Lahore during a one-day international tri-series match following a recent defeat to Sri Lanka.

Shaheen Afridi reached a milestone of 150 ODI wickets as he dismissed England opener Tom Banton during the clash in Lahore. Banton attempted an early drive off a delivery from Shaheen, only to find Arafat Minhas at cover, handing Pakistan a crucial early breakthrough in the tri-series contest. This match represents a critical juncture for England, which is attempting to rebound from a crushing loss to Sri Lanka last Thursday. The team’s recent form includes a high-scoring 430-run effort where Harry Brook scored a double-century, yet the side ultimately failed to defend that total, raising significant concerns for the squad less than a year before the World Cup. For England, a third consecutive defeat in this format would intensify pressure on the team to find a complete performance. Pakistan enters the match holding a psychological advantage after winning the previous meeting between the two sides by 87 runs. In that encounter, England was bowled out for just 215, struggling against Arafat Minhas who claimed six wickets. A victory for Pakistan at their Lahore headquarters would secure their place in the tournament final with a match still to play. Following this series, both teams continue to refine their strategies as they navigate the complexities of one-day international cricket under intense competitive scrutiny.
