Inter Milan Targets Top Spot Against Lowly Fiorentina in Serie A Clash

By Karim Fouad · Published 25 Oct 2026, 11:33 UTC · Updated 25 Oct 2026, 12:31 UTC

Inter Milan hosts Fiorentina at 12:30 CET on Sunday with a win putting the reigning champions level on points with league leaders Lazio.

Quick facts

Match Start Time
12:30 CET
Day
Sunday
Fiorentina Record
1 win, 1 draw, 5 losses

Fiorentina arrives in Milan searching for only their second league victory of the season, currently holding a record of one draw and five losses. In contrast, Inter Milan enters the match under the guidance of Cristian Chivu, aiming to capitalize on home advantage to climb the Serie A table. A victory for the reigning champions would propel them to 21 points, matching the current summit position held by Lazio, who recently edged past Parma in a 2-1 result. The pressure is on the hosts to secure all three points as the league standings tighten at the top. For Fiorentina, the match represents a crucial attempt to reverse their difficult start to the domestic campaign. The outcome of this fixture is vital for Inter’s momentum as they look to maintain their status among the league's elite challengers. Fans and observers will be watching to see if the reigning champions can navigate past the struggling Viola to claim the joint-top position in Italy's primary competition.
