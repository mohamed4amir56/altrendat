Winless Manchester United hosts Bournemouth as Michael Carrick faces mounting pressure

By Karim Fouad · Published 25 Oct 2026, 13:31 UTC

Manchester United takes on Bournemouth in a Premier League fixture as the club struggles through a winless streak lasting nearly two months.

Quick facts

League
Premier League
Manager
Michael Carrick

It has been nearly two months since Manchester United recorded a league victory, a dry spell punctuated only by a win against newly promoted Ipswich. The club now finds itself positioned five places below Ipswich in the league standings, marking what is described as a miserable start to the season that continues to decline.

This match against Bournemouth represents a critical moment for the club, as pressure mounts on manager Michael Carrick to secure a result. With the manager’s future in doubt and little sense of a clear path forward for the team, the performance against Bournemouth serves as a pivotal test for a side currently struggling to secure points against any opposition.

Ahead of the match, Michael Carrick addressed team selection concerns, specifically noting the absence of Bruno Fernandes. Carrick stated the decision was based on pure football considerations, emphasizing the need to identify the right team to deliver a necessary performance. Furthermore, Carrick confirmed that Lisandro Martinez was not deemed fit enough to start the game, noting that the risk to the player was not worth taking, leading to the selection of Ayden in his place.

As the team prepares to take the pitch, the club remains focused on turning around their form. Having managed only one win over Sabah, Champions League debutants, the squad is under intense pressure to deliver both a quality performance and a favorable result to alleviate the growing crisis surrounding the organization.
