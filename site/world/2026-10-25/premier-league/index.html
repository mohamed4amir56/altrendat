Hull City seeks fifth point at home as unbeaten Brentford visit MKM Stadium

By Karim Fouad · Published 25 Oct 2026, 15:01 UTC

Hull City hosts Brentford in a Premier League match where the Tigers aim to extend their strong home record against a Bees side that remains unbeaten on the road.

Quick facts

Hull City home record
5 points from 3 Premier League matches at MKM Stadium
Brentford away record
3 draws, unbeaten on the road this season
UK kick-off time
2:00pm GMT on Sky Sports Golf
US kick-off time
10:00am ET on Peacock
Australia kick-off time
12:00am AEDT Monday on Stan Sport

Hull City have taken five points from their opening three Premier League matches at the MKM Stadium, building what FourFourTwo describes as strong home form. That record will face its test against Brentford, who start the weekend as one of just four top-flight sides yet to taste defeat away from home this term. The Bees have drawn all three of their away games so far, showing resilience on the road despite not yet securing a victory.

For Hull City, continuing their unbeaten Premier League home record matters as they bid to climb the table. Brentford, meanwhile, will look to break their away-game pattern and secure three points after drawing their first three road matches.

Sergej Jakirovic's side host the visitors at MKM Stadium. The match will be shown on Sky Sports in the UK, with live coverage on Sky Sports Golf at kick-off of 2:00pm GMT. Peacock will broadcast the game in the US at 10:00am ET, while Australian viewers can watch on Stan Sport at midnight AEDT on Monday morning.
