Emil Forsberg seals 2-0 win as New York Red Bulls topple Miami

By Karim Fouad · Published 25 Oct 2026, 04:01 UTC

The New York Red Bulls ended Inter Miami's three-match winning streak with a 2-0 victory at Red Bull Arena on Saturday.

Quick facts

Result
New York Red Bulls 2, Inter Miami CF 0
Location
Red Bull Arena, Harrison, New Jersey
Next Match
Wednesday

The New York Red Bulls snapped Inter Miami’s three-match winning streak on Saturday night, securing a 2-0 victory at Red Bull Arena. The hosts grabbed an early advantage just seven minutes into the contest when a defensive lapse saw Casemiro inadvertently deflect a Cade Cowell cross into his own net. Emil Forsberg later cemented the win for New York with a clinical right-footed strike from the center of the box into the high center of the goal, following an assist from Cameron Harper. This result marks a significant setback for Cristian “Kily” González’s side, which struggled to find a breakthrough despite commanding 62.5% of the possession. While Inter Miami generated several opportunities, including a strike that hit the bar from Telasco Segovia and a blocked effort from Lionel Messi, they were limited to only two shots on target throughout the match. Lionel Messi began the game on the bench following a trip to Spain to accept the Princess of Asturias Award for Sports. Introduced in the 56th minute, the Argentine icon attempted to spark a comeback, but the defeat ended his remarkable run of 13 consecutive matches with either a goal or an assist. The tactical decision to start Messi as a substitute was pre-planned to manage his physical condition ahead of upcoming fixtures. With this defeat, Inter Miami now looks to regroup quickly. The team faces their next challenge on Wednesday, as they attempt to recover from the loss and return to their prior winning form.
