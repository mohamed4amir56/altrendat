Prince William reveals he tried to remove Travis Kelce from royal selfie

By Karim Fouad · Published 25 Oct 2026, 10:01 UTC

Prince William admitted on The Graham Norton Show that he attempted to exclude Travis Kelce from a 2024 photograph taken with Taylor Swift and his children.

Quick facts

Event
The Graham Norton Show appearance
Date
October 23
Location
Wembley Stadium, London
Participants
Prince William, Taylor Swift, Travis Kelce

Prince William revealed his comedic struggle during a surprise appearance on the Oct. 23 episode of The Graham Norton Show. While reflecting on his family's attendance at the first night of Taylor Swift’s eight-night London residency at Wembley Stadium, the 44-year-old Prince of Wales confessed he kept trying to move the Kansas City Chiefs tight end out of the frame. William intended for the photo to feature only himself, the singer, and his two eldest children, Prince George and Princess Charlotte. “Travis kept coming back in, and I couldn't work out why he kept coming back in!” the Prince joked to the host.

This incident is notable because the photograph, which features a smiling Kelce with his hand on the Prince's back, eventually served as the singer’s public confirmation of her relationship with the NFL star on Instagram. William admitted that he did not grasp the weight of the moment at the time, noting that he only recently came to appreciate its significance. He did not realize he was witnessing the start of a major chapter in the couple's romance.

Despite the initial confusion during the photo session, the meeting between the royal and the athlete left a lasting impression. Following their encounter in June 2024, Prince William made a guest appearance on the “New Heights” podcast hosted by Travis and his brother Jason. During that July appearance, Travis reflected on the meeting, describing it as one of the coolest moments of his life. William also appeared to hold no hard feelings, laughing along with the lengthy, formal introduction provided by Jason Kelce before their interview began.
