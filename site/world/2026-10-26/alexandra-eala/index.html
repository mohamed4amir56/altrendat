Alexandra Eala chases breakthrough performance during first trip to Tokyo tournament

By Karim Fouad · Published 26 Oct 2026, 09:06 UTC

Alexandra Eala enters the Toray Pan Pacific Open in Tokyo this week for her first appearance at the event, facing teenager Mika Stojsavljevic in the opening round.

Quick facts

Tournament
Toray Pan Pacific Open
Event Level
WTA 500
Opponent
Mika Stojsavljevic

After a summer that included reaching the fourth round at Wimbledon, a maiden WTA Tour-level title in Washington, and a third-round finish at the US Open, Alexandra Eala now seeks to stabilize her recent form. The 21-year-old enters the Tokyo tournament after a demanding schedule that saw her win only one match across three recent WTA events during her current Asian swing. She enters the competition following a second-round exit in Ningbo, a result she described as positive regarding her footwork and overall level of play.

Securing a result in Tokyo is critical for Eala as she balances the physical toll of her high-intensity season against the need to regain momentum. This event represents the final WTA 500 tournament of the season, providing a late-year opportunity for the rising star to reset before the schedule concludes. Success here would validate the adjustments she has been making on the court during this stretch of matches.

According to Yahoo Sports, Eala is set to face 17-year-old British wildcard entrant Mika Stojsavljevic in the first round. Eala noted that while she is unfamiliar with the teenager’s game, she recognizes her reputation as a big hitter with a strong junior background. In addition to her singles campaign, Eala will compete in the doubles bracket alongside a Grand Slam champion. Last Word On Sports confirms the match is part of the opening day schedule for the Toray Pan Pacific Open, as the field competes to close out the final WTA 500 event of the year.
