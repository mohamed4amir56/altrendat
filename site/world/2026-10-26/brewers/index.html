Robert Gasser Hurls Brewers Into World Series Game 3 Spotlight at Tropicana

By Karim Fouad · Published 27 Oct 2026, 02:01 UTC

The Milwaukee Brewers face the Tampa Bay Rays in Game 3 of the 2026 World Series on Monday night at Tropicana Field.

Forty years after his father Jim Gasser was drafted by the Milwaukee Brewers, Robert Gasser is living out the family dream by taking the mound in the World Series. Jim Gasser, who was selected in the 27th round by Milwaukee in 1984 before a later arm injury ended his own professional aspirations, will watch his son compete on the sport's biggest stage. The emotional weight of the moment is palpable, with Jim describing the family's anticipation as their heart rates climb with every passing hour before the first pitch.

This opportunity carries significant weight for the Gasser family, who have traveled to support Robert throughout his postseason journey. Robert Gasser has already proven his mettle during these playoffs, allowing only a single run across nine innings of work during his previous starts in the National League Division Series and the Championship Series. His success on the road has been buoyed by the presence of his parents, who have made it a tradition to greet him near the bullpen before he takes the mound to face opponents like the San Diego Padres and the Los Angeles Dodgers.

According to MLB.com, Robert Gasser’s path to the Brewers organization came via a trade rather than the amateur draft. This route brought him to a pivotal Game 3 matchup against the Tampa Bay Rays. Fans across the country can catch the action on Fox or Fox One, with audio coverage provided by ESPN Radio. The atmosphere at Tropicana Field is expected to be intense as both clubs continue their quest for the championship title on Monday evening.
