Former Canucks Captain Quinn Hughes Returns To Vancouver For First Time Sunday

By Karim Fouad · Published 26 Oct 2026, 04:30 UTC

The Minnesota Wild defenseman visits his former home rink at Rogers Arena following his trade last December.

Quinn Hughes recorded 432 points in 459 games for the Vancouver Canucks, a franchise record for a defenseman that stands as a testament to his tenure before a trade sent him to Minnesota on Dec. 12, 2025. The 27-year-old returns to Rogers Arena on Sunday as a member of the Minnesota Wild, entering an environment where he expects a potential mix of reactions from the local fanbase.

This visit is significant because it marks the first time Hughes has played in Vancouver since the franchise traded him 10 months ago. His departure followed a period of intense public discourse in a market that remains one of the most challenging in the NHL. For supporters and followers of the league, the return highlights the complicated legacy of a player who served as captain while simultaneously navigating contract stalemates and shifting team dynamics.

According to NHL.com, Hughes remains the team’s all-time leading scorer among defensemen. Sportsnet reports that his time in Vancouver included winning the Norris Trophy at age 24. While online sentiment has been critical, particularly following his role in Team USA's Olympic gold-medal victory against Canada, Hughes expressed that he holds no ill will. He stated that the passion of the fans is a primary reason he enjoyed his time with the organization.

Regarding his professional future, Hughes is currently in the final year of a six-year, $47.1 million contract. Having been eligible to sign an extension with Minnesota since July 1, he noted that he is taking his time with the decision but would not be surprised if an agreement is reached soon.
