PSG looks for first Monday win while Lyon eyes past success

By Karim Fouad · Published 26 Oct 2026, 19:05 UTC · Updated 26 Oct 2026, 20:01 UTC

Paris Saint-Germain faces Olympique Lyonnais in a Ligue 1 clash tonight at 20:30.

Quick facts

Match
PSG vs OL
Kick-off
20:30
Competition
Ligue 1, Round 8

The Paris Saint-Germain squad takes the field tonight without a single goal scored in its last three official Monday matches against Ligue 1 opponents. During this stretch, the capital club recorded one draw and two defeats, struggling to find the net when the schedule shifts to the start of the work week.

For Lyon, the Monday slot evokes memories of a high-scoring 4-3 comeback victory against Lille on May 6, 2024. That result marked the last time the club secured an official away win after trailing at halftime. Bringing that momentum into today's fixture, Lyon aims to capitalize on a historical trend that favors them on Monday nights.

According to Sport.fr, the official lineups feature Safonov, Hakimi, Zabarnyi, Pacho, Zaïre-Emery, Neves, Vitinha, Ruiz, Akliouche, Torres, and Doué for Paris Saint-Germain. Olympique Lyonnais enters the match with Greif, Athekame, Bacher, Niakhaté, Abner, Tessmann, Morton, Tolisso, Nuamah, Merah, and Duranville. The match represents a critical test for both sides as they navigate the rare Monday night scheduling.

While PSG seeks to break its scoreless streak in these conditions, Lyon players will look to replicate their performance from their last Monday outing. The match is part of the 8th round of Ligue 1, with both sides hoping to secure points in a matchup where statistical history heavily favors the visitors.
