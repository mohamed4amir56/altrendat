Police orders PSG versus Lyon match moved to Monday night slot

By Karim Fouad · Published 26 Oct 2026, 19:05 UTC · Updated 26 Oct 2026, 19:58 UTC

The Ligue 1 match between PSG and OL has been rescheduled by 24 hours to Monday at 20:30 due to security requirements in Paris.

Quick facts

New match date
Monday, October 26, 2026
New kick-off time
20:30
Points tally
14 points each

The 8th round of Ligue 1, originally scheduled for Sunday at 20:45, will now take place on Monday night. This decision comes from the Paris police prefecture, which is managing an exceptional mobilization of security forces due to planned demonstrations in the capital and a regular-season NFL game occurring at the Stade de France. This rescheduling mirrors previous security-related adjustments in the league, such as the shifting of the Toulouse-Troyes match and the Montpellier-Red Star fixture in Ligue 2.

For football supporters and stakeholders, this scheduling change adds uncertainty regarding player availability for the upcoming Ballon d'Or ceremony. The event is slated for Monday in London, mirroring a previous conflict that prevented several PSG players from attending the ceremony when a match against Marseille was moved. While key players Ousmane Dembélé, Marquinhos, and Nuno Mendes are sidelined with injuries, the club must now navigate potential travel conflicts for other squad members should they be required at the awards gala.

Both teams enter the fixture with 14 points, with Lyon currently holding a better goal difference of +8 compared to Paris's +5. Paris Saint-Germain is riding a streak of seven consecutive victories across all competitions but must contend with a thinning roster following their recent Champions League win over Barcelona. Injuries have mounted, with Kvaratskhelia, Dembélé, and Marquinhos joining long-term absentee Nuno Mendes in the infirmary. Meanwhile, Lyon aims to bounce back after a 3-0 defeat to Hoffenheim in their previous outing, looking to repeat their 1-2 victory at the Parc des Princes from last April.
