Alexandra Eala Routs Mika Stojsavljevic in Tokyo to Reach Second Round

By Karim Fouad · Published 27 Oct 2026, 08:01 UTC

Alexandra Eala advanced at the Toray Pan Pacific Open after defeating Mika Stojsavljevic 6-1, 6-2 on Monday.

Alexandra Eala needed only 75 minutes to dismantle 17-year-old wild card Mika Stojsavljevic, surrendering just three games in a dominant 6-1, 6-2 victory. While Stojsavljevic managed to fire off 24 winners compared to Eala's eight, the 21-year-old Filipina maintained control by forcing the Briton into 41 unforced errors. Eala’s composure was most evident when she faced a 0-40 deficit early in the second set, recovering immediately with two service winners to hold her serve. This victory for the 2022 US Open junior champion effectively halted the momentum of her younger opponent, who was making her main-draw debut at the event.

The clinical performance highlights Eala’s maturation on the professional circuit as she navigates the Tokyo field. By keeping her error count low and landing 68% of her first serves, Eala demonstrated the tactical discipline required to sustain deep tournament runs, setting a strong pace as competition intensifies.

According to the WTA, Eala’s serve remained a key asset, winning 72% of points on her first delivery. The match saw a contrast in styles between the consistent ball-striking of the No. 8 seed and the high-risk, high-reward approach of the World No. 411. The tournament also saw significant shifts in the draw, as No. 2 seed Maja Chwalinska withdrew with a left thigh injury and defending champion Belinda Bencic pulled out due to a right ankle issue.

Eala will face Talia Gibson in the second round. Gibson earned her spot after securing a 6-0, 7-5 victory over Daria Kasatkina in an all-Australian clash. This next match represents a critical test for Eala, as Gibson carries the momentum of a hot start, having won the first eight games of her previous match.
