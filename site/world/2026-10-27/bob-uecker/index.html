Brewers reach 2026 World Series as family remembers iconic voice Bob Uecker

By Karim Fouad · Published 28 Oct 2026, 02:31 UTC

The Milwaukee Brewers have advanced to the 2026 World Series, prompting a public tribute from the family of legendary broadcaster Bob Uecker.

Quick facts

Event
2026 World Series
Team
Milwaukee Brewers

For decades, the sound of Bob Uecker on the radio defined the Milwaukee Brewers, and as the team advances to the 2026 World Series, his family is honoring the connection he shared with the city. The Uecker family expressed their profound elation and pride in the team's achievement, noting that while they long for his physical presence, they feel his spirit in every stadium and living room where fans gather to listen to the games. They emphasized that the Brewers, the city of Milwaukee, and the fan base held a central place in his life, describing the current playoff run as a moment worth the years of hope held by generations of supporters.

This World Series appearance serves as a validation of the team's commitment to the franchise and the city. The Uecker family publicly thanked the coaching staff, the ownership group led by the Attanasio family, and the current broadcast team for honoring the traditions that Bob helped establish. They also highlighted the role of the fans, who have sustained his legacy through shared stories, laughter, and an enduring dedication to the team.

The impact of Bob Uecker’s persona extends across generations, as evidenced by young fans who continue to be named in his honor. Even as the organization celebrates its return to the biggest stage in baseball, the focus remains on the community bonds that he helped foster through his years behind the microphone. Brewers captain Christian Yelich noted that the team’s current success reflects the spirit and dedication that defined Uecker’s career as Mr. Baseball. As the franchise prepares for the upcoming series, the focus shifts to a city united behind its team.
