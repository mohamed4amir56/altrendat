Milwaukee Brewers Eye First Franchise World Series Title With Game 4 Sweep

By Karim Fouad · Published 28 Oct 2026, 02:01 UTC

The Milwaukee Brewers send Dustin May to the mound Tuesday night against the Tampa Bay Rays to clinch their first championship.

Dustin May takes the ball in a bullpen game plan Tuesday night as the Brewers hunt for their first-ever World Series title. Milwaukee enters the matchup with a 3-0 series lead, attempting to finish off a clean sweep of the Tampa Bay Rays on their home turf. May, who tossed a scoreless inning in the team's narrow 5-4 victory during Game 2, faces a Rays lineup led by right-hander Griffin Jax, who last saw action on October 15 against the Cleveland Guardians.

This sweep attempt carries historical weight for the Milwaukee organization. Should they emerge victorious, the Brewers would become the first team to sweep both the League Championship Series and the World Series since the LCS transitioned to a best-of-seven format in 1985. They would join the ranks of the 1976 Cincinnati Reds as one of the few clubs to complete such a dominant postseason run.

According to TSN, May enters the contest following a regular season where he posted a 7-9 record with a 4.70 ERA and 144 strikeouts. His postseason performance over eight innings across four appearances reflects a 5.63 ERA. The decision to utilize a bullpen strategy underscores the team's focus on securing the final four wins required to finalize their title campaign.

As the series progresses, the atmosphere remains intense, with recent reports indicating a back-and-forth struggle as both teams fight for every run. Milwaukee aims to close the gap on history by finishing the series tonight, relying on their depth to contain the Rays and secure the franchise's first championship trophy.
