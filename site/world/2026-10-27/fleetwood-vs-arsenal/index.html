Arsenal faces League Two Fleetwood Town in Carabao Cup fourth round clash

By Karim Fouad · Published 27 Oct 2026, 19:33 UTC

Arsenal travels 250 miles northwest to face Fleetwood Town tonight in a last-16 Carabao Cup encounter.

Quick facts

Competition
Carabao Cup fourth round
Fleetwood League Position
18th
Date
October 27, 2026

Fleetwood Town has navigated the Carabao Cup tournament without conceding a single goal, dispatching Chesterfield, Shrewsbury, and second-tier Sheffield United on their path to this meeting. While they currently sit 18th in League Two with 14 points from 13 games, their defensive record in the cup sets the stage for a difficult test for the English champions at their seaside ground, where local conditions mean the wind blows in all directions. For Arsenal fans, the match at Highbury Stadium, located 250 miles from Islington, offers a unique destination to support the team as they face an opponent they have never played before. The Gunners arrive following a win over Everton and a 2-2 Champions League draw against Bayern Munich. With a match at Anfield scheduled for Sunday, manager Mikel Arteta is expected to rotate his squad, providing an opportunity for young talents including Marli Salmon, Ceadach O’Neill, and Max Dowman to start. Despite the potential for a depleted lineup, Arsenal remains the favorite to advance to the next stage of the competition. The game represents a classic midweek cup challenge for the visitors as they balance high-stakes league and continental commitments. This fixture marks the final hurdle for both teams before the quarter-final spots are determined.
