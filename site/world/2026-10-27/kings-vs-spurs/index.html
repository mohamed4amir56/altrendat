Shorthanded Kings face grim odds as defending champions Spurs host Tuesday tilt

By Karim Fouad · Published 27 Oct 2026, 23:31 UTC

The winless Sacramento Kings travel to face the San Antonio Spurs this Tuesday while struggling with four key players ruled out due to injury.

The Sacramento Kings enter Tuesday's matchup against the San Antonio Spurs riding a three-game losing streak that has left them ranking last in the league for blocks, assists, and Player Impact Estimate. While the Kings search for their first victory of the 2026-27 season, they must contend with the defending Western Conference champions who are simultaneously looking to climb back to a .500 record after opening their own campaign with one win in three outings.

For the Kings, the challenge is compounded by a significant injury report that has sidelined four players. The team confirmed that starting forward Keegan Murray will remain out for two weeks, while backup centers Dylan Cardwell and Maxime Raynaud are unavailable for the next week to 14 days. Veteran Ben Simmons also remains out for the duration of the season following Achilles tendon repair surgery. These absences force the Kings to rely heavily on role players like Precious Achiuwa and Jonathan Mogbo to fill the void, as the team attempts to improve a defense that has conceded over 120 points in each of their three previous contests.

According to Yahoo Sports, the Spurs maintain a clear advantage heading into the contest, as they deal with fewer health concerns than their opponents. Pounding The Rock notes that the Sacramento backcourt faces a thin roster of available guard talent, increasing the pressure on established scorers like De’Andre Hunter and Malik Monk to dictate the pace. Both teams are looking to secure a win on the first leg of San Antonio’s second back-to-back series of the season.

The game serves as a critical junction for the Kings, who have struggled to replicate their previous postseason form, while the Spurs aim to capitalize on the matchup to find momentum.
