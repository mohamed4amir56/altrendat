Mitchell Starc Claims Six-for as Australia Dismisses South Africa for 329

By Karim Fouad · Published 27 Oct 2026, 16:33 UTC

Mitchell Starc took six wickets on day one of the third Test as Australia bowled out South Africa for 329 in Cape Town.

Mitchell Starc Performance

Mitchell Starc spearheaded the Australian bowling effort on the opening day at Newlands, claiming six wickets to curb South Africa’s momentum. The visitors successfully dismissed the hosts for a total of 329 after a testing start to the series decider. Australia, who entered the match following a series-leveling loss in the second Test, faced early difficulties as their bowling unit attempted to navigate a flat surface in Cape Town.

The performance of the pace attack proved vital as the series currently sits at 1-1. Following a difficult morning session that drew comparisons to Australia's 2005 experience at Edgbaston, the team relied on Starc’s precision to prevent South Africa from building an even larger first-innings total. This match marks a critical juncture for the tourists, who entered the game having made four changes to their starting XI.

Selection drama surrounded the match, with Steve Smith ruled out shortly before play due to a neck injury, while Scott Boland was omitted despite his strong career record. Cooper Connolly, aged 23, joined the side for his second Test appearance as the team sought to address recent struggles. The crowd at Newlands, the site of the 2018 series controversy, remained a vocal presence throughout the day's play.

Australia’s bowling dominance provided a necessary shift in the contest as they look to regain control of the decider. With the South African innings closed at 329, the focus now shifts to the Australian top order to stabilize the game and respond to the target set by the hosts.
