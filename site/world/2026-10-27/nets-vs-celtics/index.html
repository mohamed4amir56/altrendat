Winless Nets Storm TD Garden Seeking Upset Against Reigning Atlantic Division Champions

The Boston Celtics host the Brooklyn Nets at 7:30 p.m. ET as the home team looks to keep the visitors winless on the season.

The Brooklyn Nets arrive at TD Garden in search of their first victory, currently carrying the league's fifth-worst net rating with an average margin of 11.8 points lost per 100 possessions. Brooklyn enters the matchup with an 0-3 record, reeling from a 136-109 defeat to the Indiana Pacers. Head coach Jordi Fernandez described the team's performance following the first quarter against Indiana as unacceptable, noting that surrendering 112 points over three quarters makes winning nearly impossible.

For the Celtics, the game serves as a test on the second night of a back-to-back following a 134-99 win over the Chicago Bulls. While Boston’s Jayson Tatum leads the team with averages of 24 points and eight assists, the status of starters Paul George and Mitchell Robinson remains a situational decision for head coach Joe Mazzulla. Given their limited minutes during Monday’s blowout victory, the pair may see action against a Brooklyn squad led by Julius Randle, who currently averages 22 points, seven rebounds, and 6.3 assists per game.

Boston enters the contest as a 12.5-point favorite with an over/under set at 222.5 points. The SportsLine Projection Model, which has simulated the matchup 10,000 times, is currently favoring the Under, projecting a combined 219 points as the total falls short of the line 57% of the time. This clash marks the first meeting of the season between the two Atlantic Division teams.

Following tonight's action, the Celtics maintain a brief two-day break before returning to the court on Friday for a rematch against the Bulls.
