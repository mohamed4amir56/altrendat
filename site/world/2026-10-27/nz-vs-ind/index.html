India eyes series clinching victory as injury-hit New Zealand faces 2-0 deficit

By Karim Fouad · Published 27 Oct 2026, 07:31 UTC

India aims to secure the five-match T20I series against New Zealand today in Wellington after establishing a 2-0 lead.

New Zealand’s seam attack arrives in Wellington with significantly reduced firepower as bowlers Matt Henry and Lockie Ferguson sit out the third match of the series. The hosts are struggling for consistency, having claimed only four wickets across the first two fixtures in Christchurch. This lack of pressure has allowed India to remain comfortable, with the visitors currently leading 2-0 and eyeing a decisive series victory.

The conditions in Wellington could further favor the tourists, as the dew and late-day starts have historically made batting second a simpler task. New Zealand’s performance has been hampered by sloppy fielding, compounding the difficulty of overcoming a side that has remained unbeaten in bilateral series against the Blackcaps since the 2018/19 season. For the visiting Indian squad, this match serves as a golden opportunity to wrap up the series early.

Yahoo Sports reports that India enters the contest as the favorites with odds of 1.74, while New Zealand is priced at 2.06. The Blackcaps will look to debut a revised lineup featuring Finn Allen and Daryl Mitchell to counter India’s roster, which includes Shreyas Iyer and Arshdeep Singh. With the current series record standing at 2-0, India has the momentum to extend their dominance in New Zealand, where the bilateral series record between the two nations is currently deadlocked at 2-2.

Should India secure a win at Sky Stadium, they will add to their historical success in New Zealand, where they previously recorded a 5-0 sweep in 2019/20 and a 1-0 series win in 2022/23. New Zealand will need a quick turnaround to prevent the series from ending in the third match.
