United flight forced back to Las Vegas after passenger threatens to hijack

By Karim Fouad · Published 27 Oct 2026, 12:01 UTC

A 25-year-old man was arrested after writing he would hijack a flight and crash it, forcing a return to Las Vegas on Saturday.

The passenger allegedly communicated a plan to "gonna hijack" the flight and "crash ur row only" while the aircraft was midair. This note forced the United flight, which carried 150 passengers, to turn around and return to Las Vegas less than one hour after its initial departure.

This incident highlights a growing issue in aviation security, with the Federal Aviation Administration reporting over 1,400 unruly passenger incidents recorded throughout 2026. Such events create significant disruptions, forcing flight crews to prioritize safety by diverting planes and delaying travel for hundreds of passengers.

According to the criminal complaint, the man was taken into custody by Las Vegas police following the report of terroristic threats made during the Saturday night flight to Newark. The suspect now faces charges of making a threat or providing false information regarding an act of terrorism, a crime that carries a potential sentence of 1 to 20 years in prison. United confirmed the plane landed safely, and the flight was eventually delayed overnight before departing for its destination on Sunday morning.

The investigation into the security breach remains ongoing, with the Federal Aviation Administration officially confirming it will look into the circumstances of the flight. The suspect is scheduled to make his initial appearance in court this Tuesday to address the charges filed against him.
