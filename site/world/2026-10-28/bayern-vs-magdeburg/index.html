Five Bayern stars sidelined for DFB-Pokal clash against visiting Magdeburg side

By Karim Fouad · Published 28 Oct 2026, 19:31 UTC

Interim coach Aaron Danks confirmed Bayern Munich will miss five players for Wednesday night’s DFB-Pokal second-round match at the Allianz Arena.

Jonas Urbig will step in for his sixth start of the season as Manuel Neuer remains out with a foot tendon irritation, marking one of five major absences for Bayern Munich. Beyond the captain, Jamal Musiala, Luis Díaz, Bara Sapoko Ndiaye, and Joshua Kimmich are also unavailable for the clash against 1. FC Magdeburg. Interim coach Aaron Danks announced the squad limitations as the team continues to cope with the recent hospitalization of head coach Vincent Kompany.

These absences create a high-stakes scenario for Bayern, who currently sit as favorites against the visiting second-division side. For Magdeburg defender Alexander Nollenberger, who grew up as a Bayern fan and previously played for the club's second team, the match represents a significant career milestone. Despite acknowledging the "monster task" of facing one of the world's best teams, Nollenberger maintains that his side believes in their chance to secure an upset under the floodlights.

The match remains a significant logistical and tactical hurdle for the Munich squad. While Kimmich is expected to return to training following his bereavement leave and targets the Bundesliga match against BVB, others face a longer recovery. Musiala is undergoing rehabilitation for a thigh muscle fiber tear, and Díaz is sidelined for approximately one month due to a bundle tear in his left thigh. Ndiaye remains out following an appendectomy, forcing Danks to navigate the DFB-Pokal fixture with a heavily modified roster.
