Brewers Crush Rays 10-1 To Seize Commanding 3-0 World Series Lead

By Karim Fouad · Published 28 Oct 2026, 10:01 UTC

The Milwaukee Brewers moved within one win of their first title by defeating the Tampa Bay Rays 10-1 in Game 3 on Monday.

No team in the history of the Fall Classic has ever erased a 3-0 series deficit, putting the Tampa Bay Rays in a dire position after Monday night's blowout. Milwaukee now stands just one victory away from capturing the first World Series championship in franchise history.

This 10-1 rout forces the Rays into a must-win situation for the remainder of the series. With the historical odds stacked against them, the Tampa Bay organization must now attempt an unprecedented comeback to stay alive in the hunt for the title. The scale of the Game 3 defeat emphasizes the mounting pressure on the Rays to prevent an immediate series conclusion.

According to Yahoo Sports and reporting from Bob Nightengale of USA TODAY, the Rays are turning to 31-year-old right-handed pitcher Griffin Jax to stave off elimination in Game 4. Jax, who recorded a 3.53 ERA across 34 regular-season outings, has surrendered four earned runs in six innings during this postseason. Rays manager Kevin Cash plans to utilize his bullpen early if necessary to keep the team's championship hopes from slipping away on Tuesday night at Tropicana Field.

The series resumes Tuesday night in Tampa Bay as the Brewers look to clinch the title. First pitch for the decisive Game 4 is scheduled for 8 PM ET. While the Rays aim to take the series one game at a time, they must outperform a surging Milwaukee squad that has dominated the opening three contests.
