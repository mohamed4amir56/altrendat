Mitch Starc bursts through South Africa as Australia targets Cape Town victory

By Karim Fouad · Published 28 Oct 2026, 09:31 UTC

Australia enters the second day of the series-deciding Test in Cape Town chasing South Africa's first innings total of 329.

Quick facts

Venue
Newlands, Cape Town
South Africa first innings score
329
Mitch Starc performance
6 for 10

Mitch Starc delivered a clinical finish to the first day of the Test series decider at Newlands, claiming an explosive burst of 6 wickets for only 10 runs. This late surge dismantled the South African lineup after openers Aiden Markram and Marques Ackerman had initially dominated the opening session of play.

The current Test match marks the first time Australia has returned to the Newlands venue since 2018. The outcome of this encounter is critical for the visiting side, which is currently operating with a heavily rejigged batting lineup due to the absence of key player Smith, who is sidelined with a neck injury.

According to The Guardian, the first day of play saw dramatic momentum swings, with South Africa initially reaching a strong position before the sudden collapse by stumps. The match also serves as a milestone for umpire Dick Kettleborough, who is officiating his 100th Test match. While Australia chases the 329-run target set by the hosts, the team looks to move past the history of the venue which previously saw major disciplinary sanctions following the 2018 series.

As the second day commences, the focus shifts to whether the Australian batting order can maintain composure against the South African bowling attack. The team is aiming to establish a reasonable first-innings total to regain control of the deciding match.
