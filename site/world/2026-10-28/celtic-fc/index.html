Neil Farrugia prods home late equalizer as Dundee United stuns Celtic

By Karim Fouad · Published 28 Oct 2026, 20:32 UTC

Bert Esselink and Neil Farrugia secured a late comeback goal against Celtic in the Scottish Premiership match.

Bert Esselink rose to meet a cross at the back post, directing the ball toward the goal where Neil Farrugia successfully prodded it over the line. The play resulted in an own goal off defender Liam Scales, who was unable to clear the ball away from goalkeeper Sam Johnstone on the line. This marked the first goal for the defender since joining Dundee United.

The result serves as a significant test for Celtic following Dundee United's recent form. After a difficult start to the season that included early losses to Hearts, Celtic, Dundee, and Motherwell, the visitors have now secured 10 out of a possible 12 points. This momentum highlights a sharp turnaround for the club compared to their initial struggles in the Scottish Premiership campaign.
