Malo Gusto hits 100th Chelsea start against Liverpool in Carabao Cup

By Karim Fouad · Published 28 Oct 2026, 21:01 UTC

Malo Gusto marks his 100th start for Chelsea as the team faces Liverpool in the Carabao Cup fourth round tonight at Anfield.

Quick facts

Venue
Anfield
Competition
Carabao Cup fourth round
Chelsea Captain
Moises Caicedo

Malo Gusto reaches a significant career milestone tonight, making his 100th start for Chelsea during the Carabao Cup fourth-round clash at Anfield. This fixture sees manager Xabi Alonso implement four changes to the starting lineup following the team's recent defeat to Tottenham. The squad relies on the experience of captain Moises Caicedo, who leads the side alongside Romeo Lavia and Jordan Henderson in the middle of the pitch. Mike Penders remains in goal, supported by a defensive line featuring Josh Acheampong, Maxence Lacroix, and Wesley Fofana.

For supporters, this match serves as a pivotal test in a cup competition where individual form often dictates outcomes. The inclusion of Danny Welbeck at the front of the attack, supported by Pedro Neto and Morgan Rogers, signals a tactical push for goals in an intense away environment. The team also keeps options open on the bench, with Emmanuel Emegha poised to potentially make his debut for the Blues, alongside youth prospects Mahdi Nicoll-Jazuli and Olutayo Subuloye.

According to the official club report, Liverpool has also adjusted its side, making seven changes from their victory against Brighton. Virgil van Dijk and Cody Gakpo remain in the lineup for the hosts. With Morgan Rogers arriving at the fixture boasting a record of 11 goal involvements across his last nine League Cup appearances, the encounter is set to test the depth of both squads. Following the conclusion of this tie, the focus for the participating teams will shift toward the upcoming quarter-final draw as they compete for progress in the tournament.
