League Two Fleetwood Town Stuns Arsenal in Dramatic Carabao Cup Shootout Win

By Karim Fouad · Published 28 Oct 2026, 18:32 UTC

Fleetwood Town eliminated Arsenal from the Carabao Cup after a 5-3 penalty shootout victory following a 1-1 draw at Highbury Stadium.

Quick facts

Match result
1-1 draw, 5-3 on penalties
Venue
Highbury Stadium
Competition
Carabao Cup fourth round
Next fixture
Sunday against Liverpool at Anfield

Teenager Conor Haughey secured the victory for Fleetwood Town by driving his emphatic fifth penalty into the top corner, triggering a mass pitch invasion at Highbury Stadium. The result marks one of the biggest shocks in the competition’s history, leaving Arsenal manager Mikel Arteta to reflect on a night where his side failed to capitalize on a high volume of chances despite playing for parts of the match with 10 men following a red card for Max Dowman.

For Arsenal, the defeat signifies a premature end to their Carabao Cup campaign, forcing a swift change in focus toward their upcoming Premier League fixture against Liverpool. Arteta expressed disappointment with the exit, acknowledging that while his team controlled large portions of the game, they failed to kill off the opponent when presented with superior opportunities to score.

According to Arsenal.com, the match ended 1-1 after 90 minutes, with Viktor Gyokeres scoring his first goal of the season for the Gunners, while Aaron Loupalo-Bi had initially opened the scoring. Arsenal goalkeeper Jay Lynch played a pivotal role in the shootout, saving a tame effort from Bruno Guimaraes before Haughey stepped up to seal the win. The match also saw Ceadach O'Neill make his first start for Arsenal, providing an assist while being watched by Northern Ireland senior manager Michael O'Neill.

Following the result, Fleetwood advances to the quarter-finals of the EFL Cup. Arsenal now turns their attention to Sunday's Premier League clash against Liverpool at Anfield.
