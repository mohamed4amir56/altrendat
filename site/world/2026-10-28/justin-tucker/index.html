Chiefs sign veteran kicker Justin Tucker to practice squad after Butker struggles

By Karim Fouad · Published 28 Oct 2026, 13:31 UTC

The Kansas City Chiefs signed Justin Tucker to their practice squad following Harrison Butker's inconsistent performance during the 2026 NFL season.

Justin Tucker returns to an NFL practice squad for the first time since his release from the Baltimore Ravens in May 2025. During his tenure in Baltimore, Tucker established himself as one of the league's most successful kickers, holding the second-best field goal percentage in NFL history and ranking 17th in all-time points scored. He previously earned five first-team All-Pro selections and seven Pro Bowl honors between 2013 and 2023.

The addition of Tucker follows a period of erratic performance from current Chiefs kicker Harrison Butker, who has missed five field goals this season. Butker, who connects on 11-of-16 attempts, struggled specifically from 50-plus yards, making only two of six kicks from that range. His recent output included two missed field goals during a 20-3 loss to the Seattle Seahawks, prompting the team to bring in competition for the veteran specialist.

Reports from the Kansas City Star indicate that Tucker remained unsigned since 2025 after facing accusations of sexual misconduct from multiple massage therapists. These allegations led to his release from the Ravens and a 10-game suspension during the 2025 season. Despite participating in tryouts for various organizations over the last two years, he had not secured a spot on an active roster or practice squad until this agreement with the Chiefs.

While the Chiefs have moved to add depth to their kicking position, the organization has not yet announced a timeline for when Tucker might transition to the active roster or displace Butker in the starting lineup. The team continues to navigate the remainder of the 2026 season with both kickers currently under contract.
