UAE Weather Forecast: Rain to Persist Across Northern and Eastern Regions

By Karim Fouad · Published 28 Oct 2026, 14:01 UTC

The National Centre of Meteorology predicts rainfall will continue across northern, eastern, and southern UAE for the next five days.

Quick facts

Forecast duration
5 days
Expected wind speed
10 to 50 km/h
Primary weather risk
Rain and dust

Wind speeds are expected to reach up to 50 km/h during the coming days, accompanied by rising dust and fresh to strong gusts near convective cloud formations. While conditions remain fair to partly cloudy, the presence of these clouds brings a consistent chance of rain throughout the end of the week, according to the National Centre of Meteorology (NCM).

Residents should prepare for humid mornings across coastal and inland areas, with a recurring possibility of fog or mist in the early hours. These unsettled conditions follow a recent period of heavy showers and hail that transformed landscapes from desert terrain to flowing wadis, prompting the Ministry of Interior to issue earlier safety guidelines for drivers to reduce speed and exercise caution.

Following Wednesday's forecast for rain in the north, east, and south, the NCM expects convective clouds to form over northern and eastern areas on Thursday, potentially extending into inland regions. Friday and Saturday will see similar patterns, with cloud activity focused in eastern areas bringing rainfall, while wind speeds are anticipated to taper slightly to 40 km/h. Sunday is expected to remain fair to partly cloudy with continued morning humidity, marking the end of the current five-day weather outlook.
