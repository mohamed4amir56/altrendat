Milwaukee Brewers One Win From Historic Sweep After Dominating Rays In Game 3

By Karim Fouad · Published 28 Oct 2026, 04:01 UTC

The Milwaukee Brewers hold a 3-0 World Series lead over the Tampa Bay Rays entering Tuesday's Game 4 at Tropicana Field.

Quick facts

Series Lead
Brewers 3-0
Game 3 Score
Brewers 10, Rays 1
Game 4 Venue
Tropicana Field

Milwaukee’s relentless offense put the Rays on the brink of elimination in Game 3, shattering records with eight two-out runs in a single inning to secure a lopsided 10-1 victory. The Brewers, who have yet to drop a game in the Fall Classic, now stand just one win away from becoming the first franchise in Major League Baseball history to sweep both a best-of-seven League Championship Series and the World Series in the same postseason. Brewers second baseman Brice Turang emphasized the team's urgency to finish the job, noting that players are committed to competing as hard as possible to maintain their pressure on Tampa Bay.

The stakes for Game 4 reach a historical threshold, as the Rays attempt a comeback that almost no team has ever achieved. According to MLB.com, teams trailing 3-0 in best-of-seven series have lost the series 42 out of 43 times. While the 2004 Red Sox famously overcame a 3-0 deficit in the American League Championship Series against the Yankees, no team has ever successfully forced a Game 6 after trailing 3-0 in the World Series.

Behind the Brewers' success is a philosophy rooted in internal development and roster turnover, exemplified by the performance of rookie pitcher Robert Gasser. Analysts Jordan Shusterman and Jake Mintz pointed to the contrast between Milwaukee's reliance on emerging arms and the Rays' reliance on veteran ace Freddy Peralta. Despite defensive struggles and the weight of a 3-0 series hole, Rays players like Yandy Díaz maintained that their focus remains entirely on winning the next game to extend the series. The matchup continues Tuesday night in St. Petersburg, where the Brewers look to cement their title run.
