Milwaukee Brewers Storm to 3-0 World Series Lead Over Tampa Bay Rays

By Karim Fouad · Published 28 Oct 2026, 05:01 UTC

The Milwaukee Brewers move one win away from their first championship after a 10-1 Game 3 victory over the Tampa Bay Rays.

Quick facts

Game 3 Score
Milwaukee 10, Tampa Bay 1
Series Lead
Milwaukee leads 3-0
Attendance
40,388
Location
Tropicana Field

The Milwaukee Brewers turned a fifth-inning challenge into a historic rout at Tropicana Field, scoring eight two-out runs in a 10-1 victory that pushed the Tampa Bay Rays to the brink of elimination. After a successful replay review overturned a call at second base, Milwaukee became the first team to record seven consecutive hits in a postseason game. This offensive surge leaves Milwaukee just one win shy of securing the first championship in franchise history.

The Brewers' dominance has been absolute, as they remain undefeated since October 6. For Tampa Bay, the situation is grim; no team has ever forced a sixth game in a World Series after trailing 3-0 in the best-of-seven series. Milwaukee’s pitching staff has consistently outperformed the Rays, while Tampa Bay’s own starters have struggled to a 6.77 ERA throughout the first three games.

Following the loss, Milwaukee players emphasized a focus on maintaining their momentum, with Sal Frelick noting that the team continues to play until the series is officially decided. Conversely, Tampa Bay designated hitter Yandy Díaz acknowledged the difficulty of the deficit but insisted the team must look ahead to the next game to keep their championship hopes alive.

With the Brewers leading the series 3-0, the pressure on Tampa Bay is immense. As history shows, only one team in any best-of-seven postseason series has managed to overcome such a deficit. The Rays now prepare for Game 4, knowing that a single loss will end their season and mark the first World Series sweep since 2012.
