AFL Drops Opening Round as 2027 Premiership Season Fixture Finally Lands

By Karim Fouad · Published 29 Oct 2026, 13:01 UTC

The 2027 Toyota AFL Premiership Season fixture is now live, introducing Sunday night games and removing the Opening Round.

For fans, the release provides clarity on bye schedules and critical matchups that determine the path to the finals. The difficulty rankings, determined by the 2026 finishing positions of double-up opponents, reveal the hurdles awaiting each club. Understanding this layout is essential for supporters tracking their team's chances, as a low total score in the difficulty calculation indicates a significantly tougher road ahead on paper.

A notable shift this year is the inclusion of Sunday night games, designed to offer more convivial starts in Adelaide and Western Australia. Meanwhile, the league has officially abandoned the "Opening Round" format, a move that ensures no byes for the first 10 rounds of the season. According to The Age, the league structure is expected to shift again in 2028 when the 19th team joins the competition.
