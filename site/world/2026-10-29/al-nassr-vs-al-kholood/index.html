Ronaldo and Mane Lead Al-Nassr Attack Against Al-Kholood in Saudi League

By Karim Fouad · Published 29 Oct 2026, 17:31 UTC · Updated 29 Oct 2026, 19:01 UTC

Cristiano Ronaldo and Sadio Mane head the Al-Nassr starting lineup for matchday 12 of the Saudi Roshn Pro League against Al-Kholood.

Al-Nassr arrives at this clash sitting second in the Roshn League standings with 25 points, having secured 8 wins from their opening 10 matches. In stark contrast, Al-Kholood occupies the tenth position with 12 points, accumulated through a record of 3 wins, 3 draws, and 4 defeats. The gap between the two sides highlights the pressure on Al-Nassr to secure three points as they continue their pursuit of league leaders Al-Hilal.

Manager Ange Postecoglou has implemented significant changes to the squad that previously defeated Al-Faisaly 3-1. These tactical adjustments see Angelo Gabriel, Ayman Yahya, and Mohamed Simakan enter the starting eleven, replacing Samu Costa, Awad Aman, and Abdulelah Al-Amri respectively. Supporting the attacking duo of Cristiano Ronaldo and Sadio Mane, the midfield and attacking support includes Joao Felix and Abdullah Al-Hamdan, while the defensive line features Nawaf Boushal, Mohamed Simakan, Inigo Martinez, and Ayman Yahya.

For Al-Nassr, maintaining this momentum is essential to closing the gap at the top of the table. Every point gained in this twelfth round remains vital as the team faces a title contender or seeks to consolidate their league position.
