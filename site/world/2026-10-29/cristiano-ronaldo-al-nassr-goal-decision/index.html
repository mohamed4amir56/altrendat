Fans Vote Cristiano Ronaldo’s 2008 Ballon d’Or As Greatest Of All Time

By Karim Fouad · Published 29 Oct 2026, 11:31 UTC

Cristiano Ronaldo has secured the Ballon d’Or of All Time award for his 2008 Manchester United performance following a global fan vote.

The 2008 triumph, which marked the first of Cristiano Ronaldo’s five career Ballon d’Or wins, captured the inaugural Ballon d’Or of All Time (B.O.A.T.) honor. Fans placed the 2008 victory ahead of Lionel Messi’s 2012 win and Ronaldo’s own later successes in 2016 and 2017 to secure the top spot. The vote concluded on October 27, with the result announced just days after Harry Kane claimed the 2026 Ballon d’Or.

This recognition highlights the lasting impact of Ronaldo’s breakout season at Old Trafford, where he functioned as the centerpiece of Sir Alex Ferguson’s squad. For fans and analysts, the award serves as a formal acknowledgment of his peak form during a campaign that saw him transform from a teenage winger into a dominant global force. While multiple-time Ballon d’Or winners were eligible for the fan-led contest, the B.O.A.T. distinction carries no physical trophy.

During that iconic 2008 season, Ronaldo netted 31 goals in 34 Premier League matches to claim the Golden Boot. Across all competitions, he recorded 42 goals, including a crucial strike in the Champions League final against Chelsea. That match, which ended 1-1 before Manchester United triumphed on penalties in Moscow, was a defining moment in his career. Yahoo Sports reports that Ronaldo, who currently plays for Al-Nassr, is now closing in on a career total of 1,000 goals.

The voting process, launched by the Ballon d’Or organization, required participants to pay €1 to cast their ballot. The initiative allowed fans to differentiate this title from the Super Ballon d’Or, focusing exclusively on the best individual year produced by players who have won the award multiple times.
