Riquelme Fillipi snatches 90th-minute equalizer to rescue Inter Miami against Cincinnati

By Karim Fouad · Published 29 Oct 2026, 04:01 UTC

Inter Miami and FC Cincinnati fought to a 2-2 draw on Wednesday as Riquelme Fillipi scored in the final minute to share the points.

Quick facts

Date

Final Score
2-2
Venue

Miami Goal Scorers

Cincinnati Goal Scorers
Kenji Mboma Dem, Samuel Gidi

Riquelme Fillipi emerged from the bench in the 83rd minute to salvage a point for Inter Miami, firing home a crucial equalizer in the 90th minute. The dramatic finish followed a frantic second-half turnaround that saw all four goals scored in the final 30 minutes of play at Nu Stadium. Following a scoreless first half that saw Lionel Messi receive a yellow card in the 44th minute, Luis Suarez broke the deadlock in the 61st minute off an assist from Casemiro. The opening goal electrified the home crowd, but Miami’s lead proved short-lived as Cincinnati shifted the momentum late in the match. The visitors capitalized on a tiring defense, with Kenji Mboma Dem leveling the score in the 80th minute. Just six minutes later, Samuel Gidi connected on a Tyler Barlow assist to put Cincinnati ahead 2-1, silencing the Florida supporters. Facing a potential defeat, Inter Miami maintained pressure until Fillipi converted a delivery from Rodrigo De Paul in the dying moments of regulation. The draw highlights a test for Miami as they continue their Eastern Conference campaign, while Cincinnati’s late surge underscores their desperate pursuit of a playoff position. Goal.com reports that despite the offensive flurry, disciplinary issues plagued Miami, resulting in three separate yellow cards during the match. With the result finalized at 2-2, both teams leave the pitch having traded leads in a match defined by second-half chaos. While Suarez and Fillipi proved decisive for the hosts, the team now looks to regain defensive stability following a difficult night where they conceded two goals in quick succession.
