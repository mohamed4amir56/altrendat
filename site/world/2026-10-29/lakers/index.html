Lakers add 7-foot center Lawson Lovering in new two-way roster move

By Karim Fouad · Published 29 Oct 2026, 05:31 UTC

The Los Angeles Lakers signed 7-foot center Lawson Lovering to a two-way contract while waiving forward AK Okereke.

Lawson Lovering brings NBA experience to the Lakers frontcourt after previously appearing in two games for the Memphis Grizzlies last season. During those appearances, the 23-year-old averaged 7.0 points, 7.5 rebounds, and 1.5 assists in 24.5 minutes, highlighted by a February performance against the Utah Jazz where he recorded 11 points and 11 rebounds in 32 minutes.

This roster shift is designed to bolster the team's depth, as Los Angeles has been actively exploring additional frontcourt options early this season. The team currently features Walker Kessler and Kevon Looney at the center position, and officials viewed the two-way roster spots as a specific avenue to add necessary size to the squad.

According to Michael Scotto of HoopsHype, the organization finalized the agreement by waiving forward AK Okereke. Okereke, who joined the Lakers after going undrafted in the 2026 NBA Draft, saw his stint with the team end only a few months after his initial signing. During his final college season at Vanderbilt, the 23-year-old forward had averaged 9.6 points and 3.6 rebounds while shooting 40.0% from three-point range over 36 games.

Lovering, who played four years of college basketball split between Colorado and Utah, entered the NBA path through the Memphis Grizzlies' G League system. He spent most of last season with the Memphis Hustle before signing a 10-day contract with the Grizzlies in February. He returned to the Grizzlies on an Exhibit 10 deal ahead of the 2026-27 season but was waived in late September to make room for another two-way signing.
