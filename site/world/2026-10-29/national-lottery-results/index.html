Wednesday National Lottery Jackpot Hits 2.8 Million Pounds for Lucky Ticket Holders

By Karim Fouad · Published 29 Oct 2026, 05:01 UTC

The National Lottery held its Lotto and Thunderball draws on Wednesday, October 28, offering players a top prize of 2.8 million pounds.

A life-changing 2.8 million pounds was the headline jackpot available for players participating in this Wednesday's National Lottery draw. This significant prize pool offered participants the potential to clear debts, fund travel, or contribute to charitable causes.

For those who play, the stakes reach beyond personal gain as the National Lottery generates approximately 30 million pounds every week for good causes across the United Kingdom. With a cost of 2 pounds per play, the system provides two distinct opportunities to win per entry following recent updates to the game structure.

According to Chronicle Live, the schedule for the evening featured the primary Lotto draw at 8pm, followed immediately by the Thunderball draw at 8.15pm. These drawings represent one of the recurring weekly opportunities for individuals to participate in the nationwide event.

Players checking their tickets for Wednesday, October 28, are encouraged by The Independent to refresh their digital platforms or official sources to verify the specific winning number combinations for both the Lotto and Thunderball games. No further announcements regarding future jackpot adjustments were provided at this time.
