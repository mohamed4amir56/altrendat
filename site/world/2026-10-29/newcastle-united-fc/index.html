Newcastle United and Everton clash for EFL Cup quarter-final spot Thursday

By Karim Fouad · Published 29 Oct 2026, 11:31 UTC

Newcastle United face Everton at Hill Dickinson Stadium in the fourth round of the EFL Cup on Thursday evening.

Quick facts

Date
Thursday, October 29
Kick-off
7.45pm
Venue
Hill Dickinson Stadium
Competition
EFL Cup
TV Channel
Sky Sports+

Everton player Thierno Barry has scored six goals in ten appearances this season, including a hat-trick in a round two win at Preston, with only Erling Haaland recording more shots in the Premier League. The match marks the first of two meetings between the sides in four days, with the teams set to face off again on Monday for Premier League points. Following the midweek cup tie, the clubs will travel to St James’ Park for their subsequent fixture. With no European commitments impacting either side, the EFL Cup remains a significant priority as both teams chase a place in the quarter-finals. Newcastle, currently sitting eighth in the league, recently secured their first domestic trophy for 70 years after a victory over Liverpool in the 2025 final. Manager Matthias Jaissle leads the Magpies, who have seen recent consistent cup performances, including a semi-final run last year and a runners-up finish in the 2022-23 season. Conversely, Everton enters the match after suffering consecutive defeats to Chelsea and Arsenal in league play. The Toffees have focused on defensive stability, with five of their last six matches recording fewer than 2.5 goals. Newcastle is expected to field a strong side for the fixture, noting that winger Harvey Barnes has already drawn 15 fouls during his eight league appearances this season. The result on Thursday determines which side advances in the competition, with the game serving as a critical indicator of form ahead of their upcoming Monday encounter. The match is scheduled for a 7:45pm kick-off.
