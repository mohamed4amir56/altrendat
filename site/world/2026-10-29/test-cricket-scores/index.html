Nathan Lyon bowls Temba Bavuma as Australia tightens grip on Test decider

By Karim Fouad · Published 29 Oct 2026, 18:31 UTC

South Africa holds a slim 40-run lead with six wickets remaining at stumps on day three of the third Test against Australia.

South Africa finished the third day of the decisive third Test at Newlands in a precarious position, trailing by only 40 runs after losing captain Temba Bavuma in the penultimate over. Australia entered the final stages of the day with significant momentum, as a delivery from Nathan Lyon spun through Bavuma’s flick to bowl the batter for 35, leaving the hosts at 140 for four heading into the fourth morning. The dismissal marked the fourth time Bavuma has fallen to spin during the current series.

This final Test match carries massive stakes, as the series is currently tied at 1-1. The eventual winner of this clash will secure the top spot in the Test world rankings, a position currently held by Australia. With the pitch at Newlands showing increasing signs of wear and dust, batting is expected to become significantly more difficult as the game concludes, making the 40-run lead look insufficient for the home side to successfully defend against an aggressive Australian bowling attack.

Australia’s dominance earlier in the day was anchored by Josh Inglis, who matched his career-best score by hammering a 132-ball century. His 102, combined with Alex Carey’s 80, propelled Australia to a first-innings total of 429 and a lead of 100 runs. South Africa attempted to stabilize their innings through Marques Ackerman, who scored a vibrant 62 off 80 balls, but his departure following a venomous bouncer from Mitchell Starc halted the momentum. Ackerman was dismissed after a controversial moment where he ran out of time to request a review, with replays later confirming the ball had grazed his glove.

Play resumes on the fourth morning with Matthew Breetzke on 19 and Ryan Rickelton on eight. Australia remains wary of setting a specific target, with players suggesting a total of 200 runs could prove difficult to chase as the pitch continues to deteriorate.
