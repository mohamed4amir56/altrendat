Final James Bond Screen Tests Begin This Week Featuring Jack Lowden

By Karim Fouad · Published 30 Oct 2026, 14:10 UTC

Amazon MGM Studios is conducting final screen tests for the next 007 this week, with actors Jack Lowden and Callum Turner reportedly participating.

Final screen tests for the next 007 are scheduled to commence as early as Friday, according to sources speaking with Variety. The selection process enters its most critical phase this week as Amazon MGM Studios evaluates potential candidates for the iconic role. Among those reportedly in the mix are actors Callum Turner and Jack Lowden, both of whom have been identified as figures currently under consideration by the production studio.

The search for a new lead actor represents a significant milestone for the long-running franchise. For fans and industry followers, these sessions provide the clearest indication yet of who might step into the role previously held by Daniel Craig. As the production moves toward a decision, the involvement of established talent signals the studio's desire to secure a successor capable of carrying the legacy forward into future installments.

Callum Turner, one of the names linked to these tests, has built a diverse screen career that includes notable roles in Masters of the Air and The Boys in the Boat. According to MI6-HQ, Turner did not attend drama school and previously pursued a career in semi-professional football before transitioning into modeling and acting. He has since appeared in projects like Fantastic Beasts and received a BAFTA Television Award nomination for his performance in The Capture.

While the specific lineup for the final tests remains subject to change, the studio's schedule confirms that the search for the next James Bond is accelerating. Observers are now looking toward the conclusion of these tests as the most significant step toward an official announcement regarding the franchise's future star.
