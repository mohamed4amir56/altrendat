Carolina Panthers fire defensive coordinator Ejiro Evero after 3-4 season start

By Karim Fouad · Published 30 Oct 2026, 14:31 UTC · Updated 30 Oct 2026, 15:01 UTC

The Carolina Panthers dismissed defensive coordinator Ejiro Evero on Friday following a two-game skid and a 27-19 loss to the Green Bay Packers.

Ejiro Evero departs Carolina with his defense ranked last in the league for the 2024 season, a stark contrast to his reputation as a promising defensive mind. The team officially parted ways with Evero and defensive passing game coordinator Jonathan Cooley following an embarrassing defensive performance during their recent two-game losing streak, including the 27-19 defeat on Thursday night in Green Bay.

This coaching shift is a direct response to a unit that struggled significantly, never ranking higher than 15th in points allowed during Evero’s tenure. While the team faced challenges with injuries, including the loss of starting cornerbacks and pass rushers, head coach Dave Canales determined these absences did not excuse the defensive struggles. The team currently sits at a 3-4 record and has seen their standing in the NFC South decline during this recent stretch.

Taking over the play-calling duties is defensive line coach Todd Wash, who has been named interim defensive coordinator. Wash brings 31 years of coaching experience to the role, including five seasons as the defensive coordinator for the Jacksonville Jaguars, where he led the 2017 defense that ranked second in the league. As part of the staff reshuffle, AC Carter will see his responsibilities expand to include coaching the defensive line, while Renaldo Hill remains in charge of the defensive backs.

The move marks a total pivot for a defense that entered the Thursday night matchup ranked 27th in points allowed and 32nd in rushing yards allowed per game. The Panthers will now look to Wash to stabilize a unit that has struggled to contain opposing offenses, as the team attempts to recover from a 3-4 start that has affected all three phases of their play.
