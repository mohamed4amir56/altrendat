Hāna Evacuation Site Opens as Heavy Flooding Routs Maui Road Networks

By Karim Fouad · Published 30 Oct 2026, 04:31 UTC

Maui emergency officials opened an evacuation site at Helene Hall as rising waters forced multiple road closures in Hāna on October 28, 2026.

Quick facts

Evacuation Site
Helene Hall, 174 Keawa Place, Hāna
Road Closures
Piʻilani Highway Mile 51, Uakea Road, Keanini Street
Date
October 28, 2026

County emergency officials opened a temporary evacuation site at Helene Hall, located at 174 Keawa Place in Hāna, late on October 28, 2026, to accommodate residents affected by rising floodwaters. The facility provides a gathering space for those displaced, though officials noted that it operates differently than a full-scale shelter and does not currently stock supplies like food or cots. Residents arriving at the site are required to bring their own essentials, and pets are only permitted if secured in crates or cages.

The widespread flooding prompted significant disruptions to travel throughout the region. According to the Maui Police Department, Piʻilani Highway was shut down in both directions at Mile Marker 51, rendering that stretch of road entirely impassable. Additionally, local authorities closed Uakea Road and Keanini Street in Hāna town due to the volume of high water.

While the National Weather Service indicated that heavy rain began to ease over East Maui and Hawaii island by the evening, a statewide flood watch remained in place as a precaution. Forecasters warned that the potential for localized flooding persists, with downpours expected to affect windward areas through Friday. The evacuation site will remain active until the threat of flooding has fully subsided.

Looking toward the end of the week, weather conditions across the state are projected to improve. Meteorological reports suggest a shift toward fair, trade wind conditions by Halloween, offering a reprieve from the recent heavy showers and thunderstorm activity that forced emergency responses across the islands.
