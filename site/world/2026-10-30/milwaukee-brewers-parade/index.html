Milwaukee Brewers celebrate historic first World Series title with championship parade rally

By Karim Fouad · Published 30 Oct 2026, 15:31 UTC

The Milwaukee Brewers are hosting a championship parade and rally to commemorate the franchise's first ever World Series victory.

Quick facts

Achievement
First World Series win in franchise history
Featured Players
Jacob Misiorowski, Brandon Woodruff, Sal Frelick, Brice Turang
Season

The gleaming World Series Trophy is making the rounds in Wisconsin, appearing alongside players at a recent Green Bay Packers game against the Carolina Panthers. This appearance followed the team's historic clinching of their first World Series title in franchise history, marking a major milestone for the club and its fan base. A group of the 2026 Brewers squad, including Jacob Misiorowski, Brandon Woodruff, Sal Frelick, and Brice Turang, attended the Thursday night football game to display the trophy and celebrate the championship win among fans. This public appearance serves as a high-profile victory lap for a team that has achieved its first championship win since the franchise began. The parade and championship rally are the centerpiece events for the team as they continue to mark this unprecedented achievement with supporters. According to MLB.com, the organization is hosting these official festivities to allow fans to share in the celebration of the club's inaugural title. The team's transition from the diamond to the football stadium highlights the widespread regional excitement surrounding this victory. Supporters are invited to watch the live coverage of the parade and rally as the franchise formally celebrates the monumental 2026 season. These events offer a final chance to commemorate the historic success of the current roster before the conclusion of the celebration period.
