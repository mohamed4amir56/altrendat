India snatches 3-0 series lead as Ishan Kishan returns for fourth T20I

By Karim Fouad · Published 30 Oct 2026, 08:01 UTC

India captain Shreyas Iyer elected to bowl first against New Zealand in Auckland on Friday after winning the series 3-0.

Quick facts

Venue
Eden Park, Auckland
Series status
India leads 3-0
Toss winner
India
Match date
Friday, October 30

Despite racking up 45 runs from just 19 balls in the third T20I, Vaibhav Sooryavanshi was dropped from India's playing XI for the fourth match. The decision clears the way for the return of Ishan Kishan, who missed the previous two encounters due to a right adductor strain. The team management also refreshed its pace attack, bringing in Mayank Yadav to replace the experienced Bhuvneshwar Kumar for the Friday afternoon clash at Eden Park.

With India already holding an unassailable 3-0 lead in the five-match series, the match serves as a pivotal opportunity for both squads. India aims to solidify its dominance and push toward a series sweep, while New Zealand faces mounting pressure to avoid a whitewash on home soil. Captain Shreyas Iyer noted that the pitch displays a significant amount of grass, leading him to expect sharp bounce and movement, which dictated his decision to bowl first after winning the toss.

According to The Times of India, New Zealand also shuffled its lineup, bringing Nathan Smith into the team to replace Kyle Jamieson. The hosts are banking on a stronger showing from key players including Finn Allen, Tim Seifert, and Daryl Mitchell to disrupt India's rhythm. The match follows a hard-fought three-wicket victory for India in the third fixture, which was highlighted by an explosive century from Abhishek Sharma. As the series progresses, the focus remains on whether India can maintain their unbeaten streak or if New Zealand can finally break the cycle of defeat.
