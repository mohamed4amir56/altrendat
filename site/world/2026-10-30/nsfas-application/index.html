Over 1 Million Apply for 2027 NSFAS Funding Before October 31 Deadline

By Karim Fouad · Published 30 Oct 2026, 13:31 UTC

The National Student Financial Aid Scheme has received over one million funding applications for the 2027 cycle, with the submission period closing October 31.

Official Website

More than one million prospective students have already submitted their requests for financial assistance as the cutoff date arrives. This surge in volume highlights the heavy reliance on state support for those planning to enroll at public universities and TVET colleges in the upcoming cycle.

For many students, receiving this funding is the deciding factor in whether they can pursue higher education or are forced to delay their academic goals. The scheme serves as a critical bridge to post-school training, providing the financial means for individuals in rural communities and townships to attend public institutions that might otherwise remain out of reach.

According to the National Student Financial Aid Scheme, all applications must be completed via the official website or the dedicated mobile application. The organization explicitly stated that no extensions will be granted beyond the October 31 deadline. Officials have issued a strict warning to applicants: failing to upload mandatory supporting documentation or providing incorrect information will likely hinder the processing and verification of the funding request.

To capture any final eligible applicants, the NSFAS Board is currently participating in a final-push outreach program. This initiative is specifically targeting students who have had limited access to application support or information. The scheme continues to urge parents, guardians, and educators to ensure that all relevant forms are submitted promptly, advising that waiting until the final hours increases the risk of technical challenges that could invalidate a student's submission.
