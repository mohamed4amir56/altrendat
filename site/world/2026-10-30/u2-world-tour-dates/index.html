U2 Storms Europe and London for The Greatest Show On Earth Tour

By Karim Fouad · Published 30 Oct 2026, 07:31 UTC

U2 returns to stadiums on April 26, 2027, with a European tour opening in Stockholm followed by dates across the continent.

The band is marking a significant return to large-scale venues, with their upcoming European shows representing their first stadium tour since the 2019 Mumbai concert. Supporting act Wolf Alice will join the tour beginning with the opening night at Stockholm’s Strawberry Arena, a performance that follows the band’s recent 50th-anniversary celebrations at their former school and a surprise Dublin street set. The tour schedule includes stops in Amsterdam, Berlin, Lisbon, Milan, and Paris, plus two nights at London’s Tottenham Hotspur Stadium on June 18 and 19. Additional dates for Spain are expected soon, while the band is already developing plans to conclude the massive production with a final homecoming run in Ireland during 2028. Following instant sell-outs for the initial Latin American leg in Mexico City, São Paulo, and Buenos Aires, the band expects to reveal details regarding a North American tour in the coming weeks. For fans looking to secure spots, a Visa cardholder presale begins Wednesday, November 4, at 10 a.m. local time. An Artist Presale follows on Thursday, November 5, with the remaining tickets becoming available to the general public starting Friday, November 6, at 10 a.m. GMT via the official U2 website. The tour is tied to the band's upcoming album, Carnaval De Luz, which is scheduled for release on November 13, 2026. As The Edge noted, while specific details for the tour's final leg remain under development, the band intends to host their concluding performance in Ireland.
