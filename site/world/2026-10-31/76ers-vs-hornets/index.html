Hot 5-1 Philadelphia 76ers storm Spectrum Center to face struggling Charlotte Hornets

By Karim Fouad · Published 31 Oct 2026, 21:32 UTC

The Philadelphia 76ers face the Charlotte Hornets at the Spectrum Center on Saturday night in an Eastern Conference NBA matchup.

Tyrese Maxey enters the matchup averaging more than 28 points per game, spearheading a Philadelphia offense that has surged to a 5-1 record. The 76ers arrive in Charlotte following a gritty 98-93 victory over the New York Knicks on Friday night, marking their fourth win in five games.

This Saturday night clash carries significant implications for the Eastern Conference standings as Philadelphia looks to maintain its championship momentum. For the Charlotte Hornets, the game represents a critical opportunity to regain footing after enduring back-to-back losses against the Detroit Pistons and Washington Wizards. With the 76ers playing on the second night of a back-to-back, the Hornets aim to leverage home-court advantage at the Spectrum Center to disrupt Philadelphia's rhythm.

According to Yahoo Sports, Charlotte relies heavily on Brandon Miller, who is currently averaging more than 24 points per game. However, the Hornets face depth challenges due to recent injuries affecting their supporting cast. To secure a win, Charlotte must execute a high-level defensive game plan and maintain efficient scoring against a Philadelphia roster anchored by the paint dominance of Joel Embiid. Both teams are navigating the fatigue of consecutive nights of play.

The game is scheduled to tip off at 6 p.m. ET. Fans can watch the live broadcast on NBA TV, with additional streaming options available through platforms like Fubo for those looking to follow the action in real time.
