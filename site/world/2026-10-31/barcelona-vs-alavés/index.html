Barcelona surges to La Liga lead with dominant five goal rout over Alavés

By Karim Fouad · Published 31 Oct 2026, 21:01 UTC · Updated 31 Oct 2026, 22:01 UTC

Barcelona reclaimed the top spot in La Liga after a resounding 5-0 victory against Deportivo Alavés on October 31, 2026.

Quick facts

Date
October 31, 2026
Score
5-0
Scorers
Raphinha, Lamine Yamal, Fermín, Gordon

Raphinha powered the Catalan attack by netting a brace to dismantle the Alavés defense in a lopsided encounter. The scoreline was further padded by goals from Lamine Yamal, Fermín, and Gordon, confirming a clinical performance that left the visitors with little room to maneuver throughout the match. This commanding win marks a significant shift in the league standings, placing Barcelona back at the summit of the table under manager Hansi Flick. The team's ability to convert multiple opportunities highlights their current offensive form as they push to consolidate their position in the title race. According to El Correo, the match saw Raphinha open the scoring early with a left-footed strike from close range, setting the tone for a one-sided affair that the visitor's side struggled to counter. El País reports that while Mariano managed to soften the final result for Alavés, the sheer volume of goals from Barcelona ensured the outcome was never in doubt. The loss forces Alavés to regroup as they look for more consistency in their defensive organization against top-tier opponents. Barcelona continues their domestic campaign with the momentum gained from this substantial margin of victory, aiming to maintain their defensive and offensive efficiency in upcoming fixtures.
