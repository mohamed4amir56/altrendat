Fabio Silva 89th-minute strike snatches draw for Dortmund against Bayern Munich

By Karim Fouad · Published 31 Oct 2026, 20:01 UTC

Borussia Dortmund twice rallied to force a 2-2 draw against Bundesliga leaders Bayern Munich.

Quick facts

Final Score
2-2
Equalizer Minute
89th minute

Fabio Silva found the bottom left corner with an 89th-minute strike on the turn, silencing the crowd and denying Bayern Munich a victory in Der Klassiker. The late goal capped a frantic ending to a match that saw the lead change hands twice and both teams frequently testing the woodwork.

For Bayern Munich, the result serves as a precarious defense of their position at the top of the Bundesliga table. While they remain ahead of Borussia Dortmund, the lead is now held only by goal difference. Any slip in performance from the league leaders allows the trailing pack to tighten their grip on the standings, making every point crucial in the mid-season race.

According to the BBC, Bayern Munich initially took the lead through Konrad Laimer, who scored after his own rebound was saved. Harry Kane, fresh off winning the Ballon d'Or, struggled to convert his chances, notably hitting the crossbar with a second-half penalty. Dortmund replied through Serhou Guirassy, who leveled the match with a low effort. Dayot Upamecano restored Bayern’s lead with a close-range header, but the home side could not maintain their advantage as Silva’s late heroics secured the draw. Sky Sports confirmed that despite Bayern’s protests regarding a foul in the build-up to the final equalizer, the goal stood as the visitors clawed their way back.

The draw sees both teams separated only by goal difference at the peak of the German league. Bayern Munich continues to operate under the guidance of assistant manager Aaron Danks as head coach Vincent Kompany recovers following his discharge from the hospital earlier this week.
