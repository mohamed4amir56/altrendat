Estevao earns first Premier League start as Chelsea host Manchester United today

By Karim Fouad · Published 31 Oct 2026, 12:01 UTC

Chelsea and Manchester United face off in the Premier League with Estevao and Bruno Fernandes returning to their respective starting lineups.

Quick facts

Chelsea starting lineup
Penders, Acheampong, Fofana, Colwill, Palestra, Caicedo, Lavia, Estevao, Palmer, Neto, Rogers
Manchester United starting lineup
Lammens, Yoro, Maguire, Martinez, Dalot, Santos, Baleba, Amad, Fernandes, Cunha, Mbeumo
Chelsea manager
Xabi Alonso
Manchester United manager
Michael Carrick

Estevao makes his first Premier League start of the season for Chelsea while captain Bruno Fernandes returns to lead Manchester United after being dropped last weekend. Chelsea manager Xabi Alonso has opted for four changes to his side following their EFL Cup defeat at Liverpool, bringing in defenders Levi Colwill and Marco Palestra alongside the returning Cole Palmer. The team will start with Penders in goal, supported by a defensive line of Acheampong, Fofana, Colwill, and Palestra, while Caicedo and Lavia hold the midfield positions behind an attacking front of Estevao, Palmer, Neto, and Rogers.

Manchester United manager Michael Carrick has made three changes to his starting XI. Beyond Fernandes, Lisandro Martinez and Matheus Cunha are also back in the lineup. Lammens starts in goal behind a defense featuring Yoro, Maguire, Martinez, and Dalot. The midfield duo of Santos and Baleba will support an attack consisting of Amad, Fernandes, Cunha, and Mbeumo. Notable absences for United include Kobbie Mainoo, who remains on the bench despite recovering from an injury that sidelined him for four matches.

The tactical setup highlights distinct shifts for both clubs as they look to secure points in a congested Premier League table. Chelsea faces the challenge of adapting without Maxence Lacroix, who is sidelined with a hamstring injury sustained at Anfield, and the surprise omission of Jordan Henderson from the squad entirely. Manchester United fans have expressed mixed reactions to the team selection, specifically concerning Carrick’s use of defensive personnel in wide positions. With both teams looking for stability, the result today will carry significant weight for the remainder of the league campaign.
