Jaylen Brown remains questionable for Sixers matchup against the Charlotte Hornets tonight

By Karim Fouad · Published 31 Oct 2026, 21:35 UTC

Philadelphia 76ers forward Jaylen Brown is listed as questionable for Saturday's road game against the Charlotte Hornets due to left patellar tendinopathy.

Jaylen Brown’s availability for the Philadelphia 76ers remains uncertain as the team prepares for the second leg of a back-to-back road trip. The forward, who is dealing with left patellar tendinopathy, was listed as questionable in the team’s official injury report released Saturday afternoon following his absence during Friday’s win over the New York Knicks. Brown previously reported soreness in his left knee following a 114-109 victory against the Indiana Pacers on Wednesday. Sixers head coach Nick Nurse has stated that the timetable for Brown’s return depends on his ongoing treatment and pain tolerance, noting that there is no long-term concern regarding the injury. Through his first five games this season, Brown has averaged 20.6 points, 6.6 rebounds, and 4.2 assists. The status of other key roster members adds to the uncertainty for the Sixers in Charlotte. While LeBron James and Joel Embiid are officially ruled out for tonight’s game due to injury management, the team may see the return of Jabari Walker from a knee injury. Additionally, center Tacko Fall is listed as questionable and could potentially make his season debut. The Sixers enter this contest coming off a 98-93 victory on Friday night. The organization will continue to monitor Brown’s condition leading up to tip-off against the Hornets as they navigate a busy schedule that includes upcoming NBA Cup group stage matchups against the Cleveland Cavaliers, Miami Heat, and Indiana Pacers throughout November.
