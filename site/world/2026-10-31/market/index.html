Binance co-founder Zhao asks Elon Musk for support after $6.3 billion hack

By Karim Fouad · Published 31 Oct 2026, 08:01 UTC

Changpeng Zhao requested that Elon Musk cease mocking the cryptocurrency exchange following a $6.3 billion heist on Thursday.

Changpeng Zhao reminded the SpaceX CEO that Binance holds over $1 billion in SpaceX equity, an investment stemming from a previous $500 million stake in the takeover of Twitter. The request for support followed a series of laughing and grimacing emojis posted by Musk on X in response to the record-breaking security breach. Zhao emphasized that this was a poor time to troll the platform as it navigates the aftermath of the theft.

This incident marks the largest cryptocurrency heist ever recorded, according to reports from blockchain firms. The scale of the loss highlights the vulnerability of major digital asset exchanges to state-sponsored entities. With $3 billion in assets already frozen, the exchange is attempting to manage the fallout of an attack currently being attributed to the North Korean state-sponsored unit known as TraderTraitor.

The public dispute between Zhao and Musk follows a history of online friction between the two figures. In 2021, the pair clashed when Musk labeled Binance’s suspension of Dogecoin withdrawals as shady, prompting Zhao to draw a comparison to a massive Tesla vehicle recall. The current tension underscores the delicate relationship between major crypto infrastructure and the tech moguls who frequently comment on the industry.

Binance confirmed on Friday that it has officially restored deposits to the exchange following a temporary freeze of all user activity that was implemented immediately after the hack was detected. The exchange continues to work alongside investigators to address the stolen funds and stabilize the platform for its users worldwide.
