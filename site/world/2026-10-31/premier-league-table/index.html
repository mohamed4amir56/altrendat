Newcastle United eyes top six push against Everton at St James' Park

By Karim Fouad · Published 31 Oct 2026, 15:01 UTC

Newcastle United sits eighth in the Premier League table with 11 points and hosts Everton on Monday night.

Newcastle United enters Monday night's clash at St James' Park sitting eighth in the Premier League table, having secured 11 points from their opening eight league fixtures. A victory over Everton would propel the team to 14 points, potentially vaulting them as high as fifth place by the end of the round depending on other results involving Leeds, Brentford, and Chelsea.

The current standing challenges the narrative of a season in crisis following a summer of squad realignment and the departure of key players to meet UEFA SCR restrictions. Despite the heavy rotation toward younger, less expensive talent and a change in management, a consistent points average through the remainder of the campaign could yield 59 points, a total that would have secured a top-six finish last season.

While BBC pundit Chris Sutton has predicted a 2-1 defeat for the hosts, citing Newcastle's open tactical approach, the team looks to build on the defensive stability shown in a recent 2-0 win at Crystal Palace. Following a 3-1 Carabao Cup exit on Thursday, the focus for manager Matthias Jaissle turns to limiting the impact of Everton’s forward line, specifically Thierno Barry, who remains a focal point of their attack.

Perspective on the club's trajectory remains a subject of debate among the fanbase as they await Monday’s outcome. With 27 games remaining after this match, the squad aims to stabilize their position in the upper half of the table before the season deepens.
