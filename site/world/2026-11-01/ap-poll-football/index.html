USC Stuns Ohio State As Miami Clinches Third Consecutive Week At Number 1

By Karim Fouad · Published 01 Nov 2026, 19:31 UTC

Miami retains the top spot in the AP Top 25 poll following a weekend of upsets that saw Ohio State drop out of the top 10.

USC's upset victory over Ohio State in Los Angeles marked a turning point for the Trojans, securing Lincoln Riley his first win against a top-five opponent as head coach. The triumph, achieved in rain-soaked conditions, effectively vaulted the team back into the AP Top 25 rankings after previously falling out of the poll. The loss forced Ohio State to plummet from No. 2 to No. 11, marking their exit from the top 10 as the chaotic Week 9 schedule disrupted the rankings.

For college football enthusiasts, this week’s results carry significant weight as they set the stage for the inaugural College Football Playoff rankings. With multiple teams ranked between 17 and 25 suffering losses, the bottom of the poll is expected to see a considerable shift. The emergence of new ranked teams and the movement of established contenders highlight the high stakes of late-season conference play, where even slight margins can drastically alter a program's postseason trajectory.

Miami maintained its No. 1 status for the third straight week after a narrow 38-35 victory against North Carolina, bolstered by two touchdowns from Malachi Toney. Meanwhile, Georgia secured a 28-7 win over Florida, leaving the Gators at No. 25. New Mexico made history by appearing in the AP Top 25 for the first time in the program's history. Alongside the Lobos, Iowa, South Carolina, and USC entered the rankings, replacing Northwestern, SMU, Oklahoma State, and Wisconsin.

All eyes now turn to the upcoming Tuesday night, when the initial College Football Playoff rankings are scheduled to be released.
