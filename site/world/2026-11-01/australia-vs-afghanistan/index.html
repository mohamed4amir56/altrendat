Afghanistan Stuns Australia by Four Wickets to Clinch Maiden Hong Kong Title

By Karim Fouad · Published 01 Nov 2026, 10:01 UTC

Afghanistan secured their first-ever Hong Kong International Sixes championship on Sunday after successfully chasing down a 116-run target against Australia.

Imran Mir hammered an unbeaten 53 runs off just 14 deliveries to anchor the pursuit, while Farmanullah Safi remained not out on 27 from only seven balls. The pair combined with captain Gulbadin Naib’s 17-run contribution to overtake Australia’s first-innings score of 115 with four wickets and limited balls to spare. This clinical batting display ensured Afghanistan successfully navigated a challenging chase at the Mission Road Ground in Mong Kok to claim the tournament trophy. The victory marks a significant milestone for the team, as it represents their first title win in the history of the Hong Kong International Sixes. The Afghanistan Cricket Board lauded the result as an outstanding all-round performance from the national side. For cricket followers, this result demonstrates the growing competitive capacity of the Afghan squad in short-format matches, highlighting their ability to overhaul significant totals under pressure in tournament finals. Australia had initially set a formidable pace by posting 115 runs in their allotted six overs. However, the Afghan side’s aggressive batting approach effectively countered the Australian bowling lineup to seal the win on Sunday. The tournament concluded with the presentation of the maiden title to the Afghanistan team, cementing their successful campaign in the Hong Kong competition. This triumph adds a fresh chapter to the nation's cricketing record, confirming their momentum in international limited-overs contests.
