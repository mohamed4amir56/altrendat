Braelon Allen Draws Starting Nod As Jets Face Raiders Without Breece Hall

By Karim Fouad · Published 01 Nov 2026, 19:31 UTC

The New York Jets will start running back Braelon Allen against the Raiders today while Breece Hall remains inactive due to a quad injury.

Braelon Allen enters Sunday's matchup as the definitive starter, having already shouldered the load with 72 carries for 357 yards and a touchdown during his time in the lead role. While fans speculated about the return of Breece Hall from his quad injury, NFL Network's Ian Rapoport confirmed that Hall is officially inactive for the contest against the Raiders. Allen's continued presence in the starting lineup highlights the team's ongoing adjustment to its injury-riddled roster. For the Jets, this game carries weight beyond the scoreboard as the November 10 NFL trade deadline approaches, turning today into a platform for players to prove their value while managing a depleted depth chart. The offensive shift is compounded by defensive absences, as T'Vondre Sweat has been moved to injured reserve with a knee injury, requiring him to miss at least four games. The team has turned to newcomer Payton Page, signed from the practice squad, to fill the void on the defensive front. Additionally, the Jets will be without edge Joseph Ossai due to an ankle injury, and David Bailey is listed as doubtful with a groin ailment. Amid these roster concerns, the Raiders prepare for the clash with potential plans to challenge the Jets' ground defense, following comments from Ashton Jeanty regarding the unit's tackling. The Jets have also elevated Quinton Bell from the practice squad to bolster their options as they face the Raiders at home.
