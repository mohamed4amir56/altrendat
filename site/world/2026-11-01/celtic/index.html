Falkirk stuns Celtic with dominant two-goal victory in Scottish Premiership match

By Karim Fouad · Published 01 Nov 2026, 15:01 UTC

Falkirk defeated Celtic in the Scottish Premiership on Friday, marking the sixth loss for the visitors across their last 10 matches.

Falkirk fans erupted as their team set the tone within three minutes, with Ross capitalizing on a defensive error from Carter-Vickers to break the deadlock. The hosts secured a second goal before the end of the first half and nearly extended their lead further, though a third effort was disallowed by VAR due to a handball. Despite a late rally, Celtic failed to find their rhythm until the final stages of the match, leaving the visitors unable to bridge the gap.

This result represents a significant setback for Celtic, as the club continues to struggle for consistency in the current campaign. With this latest loss, Celtic has now recorded six defeats in their last 10 fixtures across all competitions. For Falkirk, the win underscores their strong run of form, as the hosts extend their winning streak to four consecutive matches while maintaining a third successive clean sheet.

Sky Sports reports that Falkirk goalkeeper Scott Bain delivered a commanding performance against his former team, effectively neutralizing Celtic’s offensive efforts for much of the game. The atmosphere at the stadium remained intense throughout, reflecting the significance of the result for the home side. Celtic’s inability to capitalize on possession or create meaningful chances early proved decisive, as they could not overcome the pressure applied by the disciplined Falkirk defense. As the season progresses, Celtic faces mounting pressure to address these defensive lapses and rediscover their winning form in the league.
