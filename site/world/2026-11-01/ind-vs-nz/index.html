India Clinches Series as New Zealand Looks for Finale Win in Hamilton

By Karim Fouad · Published 01 Nov 2026, 07:33 UTC

India faces New Zealand in the fifth and final T20I in Hamilton after securing the series victory by winning the first three matches.

Glenn Phillips propelled New Zealand to an 87-run win in the fourth match with a 107-run performance off 49 balls, followed by a 65-ball 65 from Michael Bracewell and four wickets from Jacob Duffy. This victory allowed the Black Caps to avoid a whitewash in Auckland, setting the stage for a competitive final encounter.

For the Men in Blue, the fifth match offers a final opportunity to demonstrate their dominance before moving to the next stage of their multi-format tour. While India has already secured the series, the outcome remains a critical measure for the squad's preparation for upcoming challenges.

According to Yahoo Sports, New Zealand captain Mitchell Santner won the toss for the final match and opted to bowl first. The teams have confirmed their lineups, with Shreyas Iyer captaining India and Mitchell Santner leading the Black Caps.

Bookmakers currently favor India to win the final game with odds at 1.69, while New Zealand sits at 2.14. These market prices are indicative and subject to shift as the final T20I progresses in Hamilton.
