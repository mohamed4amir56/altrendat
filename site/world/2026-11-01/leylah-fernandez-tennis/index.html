Diana Shnaider claims Tokyo title after surviving late Leylah Fernandez comeback bid

By Karim Fouad · Published 01 Nov 2026, 15:01 UTC

Diana Shnaider secured the Toray Pan Pacific Open crown by defeating Leylah Fernandez 6-4, 5-7, 6-2 in Tokyo on Sunday.

Quick facts

Final Score

Location

Tournament

Leylah Fernandez staged a gritty second-set rally, winning seven of eight games to push the final to a decider after trailing 6-4, 4-0. The Canadian challenger carried that momentum into the third set, immediately forcing three break points on Shnaider's serve. However, the No. 3 seed fended off the challenge to level the set and ultimately regained control to finish the match in 2 hours and 18 minutes.

This victory provides Diana Shnaider with her first title of the 2026 season and moves her total to six career singles crowns. For Fernandez, the loss concludes a notable Asian Swing, as acknowledged by Shnaider during the trophy presentation. The result also carries statistical weight in their head-to-head record; after dropping her first two matches against Fernandez, Shnaider has now secured four consecutive victories over the Laval, Quebec native.

According to WTA Tennis, Shnaider maintains an impressive record in finals, having won six consecutive championship matches on the WTA Tour. Four of her six career titles have been earned on hard courts, including this third WTA 500 crown. The win in Tokyo is expected to elevate the 22-year-old to a career-high ranking of No. 11 in the WTA PIF Rankings.

TSN reports that the final was marked by contrasting service performances. While Shnaider recorded two aces and zero double faults throughout the championship match, Fernandez struggled with five double faults and five unforced errors. Shnaider successfully converted six of 15 return games, while Fernandez managed four of 15. The match serves as a conclusion to Shnaider's difficult run of four lost semifinals earlier this season, marking a strong finish to her current campaign.
