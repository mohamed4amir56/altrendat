Shemar Stewart Returns for Bengals as Cincinnati Looks to Topple Tennessee Titans

By Karim Fouad · Published 01 Nov 2026, 17:31 UTC

First-round pick Shemar Stewart returns to the active roster for today's matchup against the Tennessee Titans.

After being a healthy scratch in the team's 35-13 victory over the Baltimore Ravens last Sunday, defensive end Shemar Stewart is back on the active roster today against the Tennessee Titans. Stewart, the Bengals' 2025 first-round selection, has struggled to find his footing during a difficult rookie season limited to just 17 snaps across three games. His initial progress was stalled by a hyperextended knee suffered during the first day of training camp in early September.

For the Bengals, the roster decision highlights the depth of a defensive line group that head coach Zac Taylor describes as both crowded and competitive. While Stewart remained sidelined last week, rookie second-round pick Cashius Howell seized the opportunity, recording his first career sack against Tyler Huntley. The contrast between the two former Texas A&M edge rushers is sharp; Stewart offers the prototypical 6-foot-5 frame the front office covets, while Howell has prioritized consistent production, racking up 11.5 sacks in his final collegiate season.

According to defensive coordinator Al Golden, Stewart’s absence against Baltimore was purely a matter of managing a deep roster rather than a critique of his effort or skillset. The coaching staff maintains optimism about his development, noting that Stewart has remained positive throughout his recovery and transition to the professional level. With Cincinnati favored by 12.5 points against Tennessee, the team hopes to secure a comfortable lead that would allow Stewart to earn critical game repetitions.

Looking ahead, the Bengals are preparing for a long flight to Madrid, Spain, where they are scheduled to face the Atlanta Falcons next week. In addition to Stewart’s return, the team confirmed that linebacker Joe Giles-Harris is active despite dealing with a calf injury, while safety Kyle Dugger remains sidelined with a quad issue.
