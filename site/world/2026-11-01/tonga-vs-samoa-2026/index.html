Tonga and Samoa clash in must-win Rugby League World Cup showdown

By Karim Fouad · Published 01 Nov 2026, 11:01 UTC

Tonga and Samoa compete in a critical final group match at Sydney's sold-out CommBank Stadium tonight.

Quick facts

Venue
CommBank Stadium, Sydney
Kick-off time
10.05pm NZT

Retailers in Auckland have moved nearly 1000 national flags over the past two weeks as communities prepare for the ultimate Pacific showdown. In Ōtāhuhu, shop owner Gary Wang noted that Tongan flags have been the most popular choice among fans, while smaller Samoan flags have completely sold out ahead of the match.

This high-stakes encounter carries significant weight for the supporters, many of whom describe the event as a vital connection to their motherland while living abroad. For fans in Auckland, the biggest Polynesian city in the world, the match has sparked weeks of spontaneous parades, with red and white attire dominating local shopping strips.

According to RNZ, both nations have named full squads for the contest. Tonga is led by captain Jason Taumalolo, while Samoa enters the match under the leadership of co-captains Jarome Luai and Junior Paulo. The game is being played at CommBank Stadium in Sydney, with fans across the region expressing intense support for their respective teams.

As anticipation reaches its peak, supporters have been vocal in their prayers for a good result. The match serves as a final group fixture for the tournament, with both sides looking to secure their progression in the Rugby League World Cup.
