Apple Targets $113 Billion Revenue Milestone as New CEO Takes Command

By Karim Fouad · Published 02 Nov 2026, 21:31 UTC

Apple reports its fiscal fourth-quarter earnings today, with expectations of $113.46 billion in revenue and earnings per share of $2.

John Ternus leads his first earnings call as CEO today, marking a significant transition for the company after taking the role from Tim Cook on Sept. 1. Investors are closely monitoring whether the tech giant can maintain its momentum after its stock surged 27% this year, a performance that currently outpaces its primary industry peers on Wall Street. This report follows a challenging period for other major technology firms that have faced intense scrutiny regarding rising capital expenditures and shrinking cash flows.

The upcoming financial disclosure serves as a crucial barometer for market sentiment toward the firm's measured approach to artificial intelligence investments. Unlike other hyperscalers that have accumulated massive infrastructure bills, Apple maintains a more conservative spending profile, which analysts highlight as a key differentiator for the company’s current financial stability.

According to Bloomberg analyst consensus estimates, Apple is projected to report earnings per share of $2, rising from $1.85 in the same period last year. Revenue expectations are set at $113.46 billion, a notable increase over the $102.46 billion reported in the previous year's fourth quarter. Projections for specific segments include iPhone sales reaching $56.48 billion, representing a 15% year-over-year increase. The services division is expected to generate $31.58 billion, while the Mac, wearables, and iPad segments are anticipated to contribute $9.69 billion, $8.99 billion, and $6.83 billion respectively. Although the iPhone 18 Pro went on sale after the fiscal quarter closed, the company may provide insight into early consumer demand.

Looking ahead, the market will assess the provided holiday guidance and performance updates regarding China, alongside the broader reaction to the company’s fiscal results.
