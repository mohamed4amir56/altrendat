Saquon Barkley Declared Active as Eagles Face Commanders on Sunday Night

By Karim Fouad · Published 02 Nov 2026, 11:01 UTC

Saquon Barkley is officially active to play against the Commanders on Sunday Night Football despite recent hamstring and back injuries.

Quick facts

Matchup
Eagles vs. Commanders
Event
Sunday Night Football
Status
Saquon Barkley active

Saquon Barkley managed just 10 snaps in the previous game due to injuries but produced 62 rushing yards and a touchdown on 9 touches before his exit. The running back missed practice sessions on Wednesday and Thursday this week but returned to the field on Friday, maintaining a personal philosophy that he would rather play through pain than wonder if he could have competed. Barkley expressed that he does not want to look back on his career and regret not giving himself a chance to play when he had the opportunity.

The availability of Barkley provides a boost to an offensive unit that faces roster challenges heading into the matchup. The team will be shorthanded at the receiver position, with Smith, Wicks, Campbell, Covey, and Jones all ruled out. Consequently, the team will rely on a receiver rotation consisting of Makai Lemon, Darius Cooper, Elijah Moore, and Hollywood Brown.

According to NBC Sports Philadelphia, the Eagles have also adjusted their running back depth by placing Dameon Pierce on Injured Reserve and signing Jaydon Blue to the active roster. Veteran Elijah Mitchell has been elevated from the practice squad for the second consecutive week to provide additional support in the backfield. On the defensive side, the team confirmed that all edge rushers, including Jonathan Greenard and Nolan Smith, are available for the game, marking a rare occurrence for the roster this season. Mason Reiger has been elevated from the practice squad and is expected to make his NFL debut during this contest.
