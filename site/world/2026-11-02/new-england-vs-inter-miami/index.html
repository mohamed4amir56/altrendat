New England Revolution chases No. 2 seed against reigning champion Inter Miami

By Karim Fouad · Published 02 Nov 2026, 05:01 UTC

New England hosts Inter Miami on Sunday in a pivotal Matchday 34 showdown with Eastern Conference playoff seeding on the line.

Quick facts

Matchday
34
League
Major League Soccer

Carles Gil reached a historic milestone for New England by becoming the eighth player in MLS history to record 100 regular-season assists. This achievement coincided with a 3-2 victory over Red Bull New York, marking the team's fourth consecutive win as they prepare for their regular-season home finale at Gillette Stadium. With a win this Sunday, New England secures the No. 2 seed in the Audi 2026 MLS Cup Playoffs, a feat that would solidify the early success of head coach Marko Mitrović's first season in charge. Conversely, Inter Miami arrives in Massachusetts seeking to break a two-game winless streak after a late 90th-minute equalizer from Riquelme Fillipi saved a 2-2 draw against FC Cincinnati. As the reigning MLS Cup champions, Miami aims to become the first team to win back-to-back titles since the LA Galaxy in 2011-12. According to MLSsoccer.com, securing a road victory remains essential for Miami to keep the No. 2 seed within their reach and maintain their path toward qualifying for the 2027 Concacaf Champions Cup. Luis Suárez, who scored a goal in the team's previous draw, remains a primary offensive threat as Miami attempts to regain momentum. The Sunday Night Soccer match serves as a direct battle for positioning in the Eastern Conference standings as the regular season reaches its final stages. Both clubs enter the contest with clear objectives: New England looks to cement their high standing, while Miami fights to remain a top contender before the postseason begins.
