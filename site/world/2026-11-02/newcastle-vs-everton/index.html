Louder boos rang out at full-time as Newcastle United players exited the pitch following a disappointing home defeat. The home crowd made their frustration clear throughout the evening, jeering the team off the field at both half-time and the final whistle. While Newcastle attempted to gain momentum after making substitutions during the second half, the efforts were insufficient to overcome the single-goal deficit.

This loss marks a difficult stretch for Newcastle as they navigate a period of transition involving both a new manager and a significant turnover in their squad. For the supporters, the current performance levels are a growing concern, with many fans openly expressing that the team's style of play is not yielding the desired results during these early stages of the season.

According to the BBC, the match ended 1-0 in favor of the visitors. Everton's Thierno Barry provided the decisive moment, clinching the victory for the away side. The match saw a series of scrappy exchanges, with Newcastle struggling to find coherence despite creating opportunities through players like Burn and Joelinton. Tarkowski played a key defensive role for Everton, clearing a snapshot from Joelinton to help maintain the clean sheet.

Everton entered the contest looking to build on previous results, having already secured a victory over Newcastle in the Carabao Cup earlier in the week. The team dynamic remains a focus for both clubs as they continue their respective campaigns, with Everton successfully securing all three points on the road to leave the north-east with a vital Premier League win.
