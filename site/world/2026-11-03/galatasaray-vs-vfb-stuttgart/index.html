Stuttgart backtracks after mocking Galatasaray’s stadium sound system before Champions League clash

By Karim Fouad · Published 03 Nov 2026, 18:01 UTC

VfB Stuttgart apologized to Galatasaray after a deleted social media post questioning the authenticity of the atmosphere at Rams-Park.

The Bundesliga club faced a swift backlash after sharing a photo of loudspeakers at the Turkish side's home ground accompanied by pensive emojis. The post, which aimed to highlight allegations that Galatasaray fans amplify their famous whistling through the stadium’s speaker system, triggered an immediate and aggressive response from the home supporters. Within ten hours, the post accumulated roughly 2,200 comments and 1.4 million views, forcing Stuttgart to delete the content and issue a formal public apology for the perceived disrespect. Galatasaray officially accepted the apology, expressing anticipation for a fair match in what they described as one of the best football atmospheres globally.

This confrontation precedes a critical fixture for both clubs as they navigate the UEFA Champions League group stage. VfB Stuttgart currently sits with seven points from three matches and views this encounter as a vital opportunity to reach ten points, which would put them in a strong position for the intermediate round. Conversely, Galatasaray prepares for the contest with a heavy focus on their offensive firepower.

Stuttgart defender Maximilian Mittelstädt emphasized the intensity of the challenge, specifically citing Nigerian striker Victor Osimhen as a primary threat. According to Goal.com, the match is scheduled for Tuesday evening at 18:45. Mittelstädt, as reported by the Daily Post Nigeria, acknowledged Osimhen’s consistent attacking quality and his impressive tally of 10 goals in 10 appearances across all competitions this season. Despite receiving treatment during a recent league match, the striker is expected to be available for the fixture. Osimhen currently holds eight Champions League goals for the Turkish club, placing him within reach of Burak Yılmaz’s record of 10 goals as the team's joint second-highest scorer in the competition.
