Ginebra eyes series sweep to topple San Miguel in PBA quarterfinals showdown

By Karim Fouad · Published 03 Nov 2026, 13:01 UTC

Barangay Ginebra attempts to eliminate San Miguel Beer in tonight’s Game 3 of their PBA Season 50 Governors’ Cup quarterfinals.

Quick facts

Event
PBA Season 50 Governors’ Cup Quarterfinals Game 3
Matchup
Barangay Ginebra vs. San Miguel Beer
Series status
Ginebra leads 2-0
Time
7:30 p.m.
Location
City of Dasmariñas Arena, Cavite

Ginebra enters tonight’s contest with a 2-0 series lead following a 126-99 rout of the Beermen last Friday. While the Kings are one win away from a semifinals berth against TNT, head coach Tim Cone warned that teams often stage comebacks, noting he has seen many unexpected shifts during his four-decade career. He demands the same intensity and aggressiveness that defined their recent victory, insisting the team must not relax or let their guard down.

The outcome carries significant stakes as the winner advances to a best-of-five semifinals series against the defending champion, TNT, which already swept its own quarterfinal matchup. For San Miguel, the game is a desperate fight for survival; June Mar Fajardo, who recorded 16 points and nine rebounds in the previous loss, maintains that the series remains open and insists his side must stay positive to force a game four.

According to the Daily Tribune and Philstar.com, the Kings have benefited from the chemistry between import Jordan Murphy and locals RJ Abarrientos, Scottie Thompson, and Stephen Holt. Meanwhile, Japeth Aguilar and Isaac Go have anchored the interior defense against Fajardo. Cone remains focused solely on the task at hand, ignoring the potential for a larger tournament run until the third win is officially secured.

The decisive clash takes place at the City of Dasmariñas Arena in Cavite. Tip-off is scheduled for 7:30 p.m. following the earlier matchup between Rain or Shine and Converge.
