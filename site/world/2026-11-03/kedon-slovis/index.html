Ravens sign Kedon Slovis to bolster quarterback room after multiple injury hits

By Karim Fouad · Published 03 Nov 2026, 15:31 UTC

The Baltimore Ravens are signing 25-year-old quarterback Kedon Slovis from the Green Bay Packers practice squad to address a depleted roster.

Kedon Slovis enters the Baltimore locker room having attempted just two passes during his two-game professional debut with the Arizona Cardinals last season. The 25-year-old signal-caller joins the Ravens following a chaotic Sunday outing against the Buffalo Bills that left the team scrambling for healthy options at the position.

This roster move provides immediate depth for Baltimore as they navigate a string of injuries affecting their core quarterbacks. Lamar Jackson is sidelined indefinitely with a thumb injury, while Tyler Huntley is managing a toe issue that kept him out of Monday’s practice. Furthermore, Joe Fagnano, who finished the game in Buffalo, appeared on the injury report with an ankle concern after absorbing a hit from defensive lineman Ed Oliver.

According to Tom Pelissero, the Ravens secured Slovis directly from the Green Bay Packers practice squad. The transition to the new environment may be eased by familiarity; Slovis is set to reunite with Baltimore’s quarterback coach, Israel Woolfork, who served as his position coach during their time together in Arizona. Additionally, Ravens wide receivers coach Keary Colbert worked with Slovis during his collegiate tenure at USC. Before entering the professional ranks as an undrafted player in 2024, Slovis completed a college career that included stints at USC, Pittsburgh, and BYU.

To further solidify their quarterback situation, the team also added Austin Reed to the practice squad on Monday. As the organization manages these mounting health concerns, the Ravens prepare to host the Jacksonville Jaguars this Thursday night.
