Mystery NSW Player Snatches Entire $60 Million Oz Lotto Jackpot in Blacktown

By Karim Fouad · Published 03 Nov 2026, 12:31 UTC · Updated 03 Nov 2026, 13:01 UTC

A single ticket sold in Blacktown, New South Wales, matched all winning numbers in Tuesday night's Oz Lotto draw 1707.

The search is officially on for Australia’s newest multi-millionaire after a single mystery entry took out the entire $60 million Oz Lotto jackpot on Tuesday night. While the winner is now $60 million richer, officials believe they may not even know they have won yet, prompting an urgent plea for anyone who purchased a ticket at a City of Blacktown outlet to check their numbers immediately. The massive windfall follows a streak of unclaimed prizes, as last week’s $50 million jackpot went without a division one winner. Beyond the top prize, more than 940,000 other winners across divisions two to seven collectively secured nearly $19 million in total winnings. Thirteen of those players shared the division two prize, with each walking away with just over $53,000. Lottery officials are now stressing the importance of registering tickets to a player card or The Lott Members Club. This system allows representatives to contact winners directly, preventing the kind of hunt currently underway in western Sydney. Without a registered entry, the mystery winner remains unidentified, waiting for the life-changing news to surface. The winning numbers for draw 1707 were 18, 19, 38, 7, 30, 22, and 27, with supplementary numbers 3, 23, and 45. While standard seven-number games cost $1.50, lottery data indicates that various system entries have historically provided broader coverage across prize divisions. For now, the jackpot winner has until they come forward to claim the funds that have effectively turned a mid-week draw into a multi-million dollar transformation for one lucky individual.
