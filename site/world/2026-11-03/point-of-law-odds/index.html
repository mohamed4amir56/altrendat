Point Of Law clinches Melbourne Cup as million dollar bet misfires spectacularly

By Karim Fouad · Published 03 Nov 2026, 05:01 UTC

Point Of Law secured victory in the 2026 Lexus Melbourne Cup at Flemington as the race favourite Hopewell Rock finished outside the top four.

A staggering $1 million bet placed by Irish billionaire JP McManus on Hopewell Rock resulted in a complete loss after the horse failed to secure a place in the top four. The gamble was set to yield a massive $9.6 million payout for the 75-year-old, but the horse finished well behind the lead pack on Tuesday night at Flemington.

This outcome reshaped the day for punters and professional bettors alike, as the race served as a major test of staying power. The event highlights the extreme volatility of high-stakes horse racing, where even significant financial backing cannot guarantee a return against the performance of the field on the track.

Winning trainer Ciaron Maher celebrated a dominant performance as he secured a trifecta, with three of his horses taking the top three spots in the race. Maher noted that while he was confident in his stable, the distance Royal Supremacy built up during the event was unexpected. According to the Sydney Morning Herald, the trainer credited his team's execution in what he described as a true test of stamina for all involved.

As the racing circuit moves forward, the results from Flemington will be analyzed for their impact on future major events and trainer rankings. The focus now shifts to the aftermath of the race for the winning stable and the notable financial fallout for high-profile investors who backed the unsuccessful favourite.
