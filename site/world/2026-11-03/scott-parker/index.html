Scott Parker Nears West Ham Manager Role After Nuno Espirito Santo Sacking

By Karim Fouad · Published 03 Nov 2026, 10:31 UTC

West Ham United have dismissed Nuno Espirito Santo after 13 Championship games, with former player Scott Parker in advanced talks to take over.

Scott Parker, who made 129 appearances for the Hammers between 2007 and 2011, is close to being appointed as the new head coach. Parker brings extensive experience in the division, having previously secured promotion to the Premier League with Fulham, Bournemouth, and Burnley. He left his position at Burnley last April when their relegation was confirmed.

The decision to change leadership comes as West Ham sit eighth in the Championship, six points behind the league leaders and four points off the automatic promotion spots. The club's board stated that a change in the dugout was necessary to ensure their promotion objectives and expectations remain on track after winning only six of their first 13 matches.

According to Sky Sports, Nuno Espirito Santo’s departure followed consecutive home losses at London Stadium, including a 3-2 defeat to Southampton and a 3-1 loss to West Brom. Supporters at the stadium voiced their frustrations during the final match of his tenure. West Ham United confirmed in a club statement that Nuno has left his position with immediate effect.

While the recruitment process for a new head coach is underway, current staff members Mark Noble, Steve Potts, and Gerard Prenderville have been tasked with preparing the squad for the upcoming matches. The team is scheduled to play Cardiff City away this Tuesday, with the match broadcast live on Sky Sports. Following the trip to Cardiff, West Ham face Blackburn on November 7, Preston on November 21, and Portsmouth on November 24.
