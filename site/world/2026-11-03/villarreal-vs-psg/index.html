Academy Graduate Senny Mayulu Starts As PSG Storms Villarreal For Champions League

By Karim Fouad · Published 03 Nov 2026, 19:35 UTC

Paris Saint-Germain faces Villarreal this Tuesday for matchday four of the Champions League league phase.

Quick facts

Matchday
4
Competition
Champions League

Senny Mayulu gets the nod over Ferran Torres up front as the PSG academy graduate joins an attack featuring Maghnes Akliouche and Désiré Doué. This tactical shift arrives as manager Luis Enrique navigates a depleted squad facing the challenge of a fourth straight Champions League fixture. The visitors enter the match with a clear objective: secure a victory to maintain their momentum during the league phase of the tournament.

Injuries have forced significant changes to the Parisian line-up, as Ousmane Dembélé, Marquinhos, Khvicha Kvaratskhelia, and Nuno Mendes remain unavailable for the contest. The absence of Kvaratskhelia, who is sidelined with an ACL injury, has also triggered speculation regarding future reinforcements at the club. Reporters suggest the team is currently monitoring potential replacements like Real Betis star Ez Abde ahead of the January transfer window to bolster their attacking options.

For the match at Villarreal, PSG has confirmed a starting eleven consisting of Safonov, Hakimi, Zabarnyi, Pacho, and Hernandez in the back line, supported by a midfield of Vitinha, Neves, and Ruiz. Villarreal counters with a squad featuring Gulacsi, Freeman, Mourino, Veiga, Romero, Comesana, Gueye, Macia, Buchanan, Pepe, and Pérez. With both sides finalizing their strategies on the pitch, the focus remains entirely on the points at stake in this critical European clash.
