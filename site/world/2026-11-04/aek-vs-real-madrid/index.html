Real Madrid Storms Greece Seeking Seventh Straight Win Against Struggling AEK Athens

By Karim Fouad · Published 04 Nov 2026, 17:05 UTC

Real Madrid faces AEK Athens at the ALLWYN Arena this Wednesday, aiming to maintain their flawless Champions League record.

AEK Athens goalkeeper Alberto Brignoli has conceded 13 goals in his last five appearances, a defensive vulnerability that Real Madrid’s high-scoring attack will look to exploit in Wednesday’s Champions League fixture. While the Greek side aims to improve upon a 5–1 European defeat against Manchester City, the visiting Spanish giants arrive with a streak of six consecutive victories across all competitions.

This match carries significant weight for Real Madrid’s ambition to secure a top-eight finish in the league phase. Maintaining a perfect European record is essential for the team as they look to capitalize on their recent momentum, including a high-profile win in the Clásico. For AEK Athens, a positive result against the reigning favorites would stand as a major achievement, especially given their difficult recent domestic form and loss to Panathinaikos.

Real Madrid manager José Mourinho will be without club captain Federico Valverde, who traveled to Greece but is unlikely to be risked due to a hamstring injury. Eder Militao makes his first start since returning from a long-term injury, bolstering a defense that faces an AEK squad led by Jović, a former Madrid player who struggled to find the net during his time in Spain. Stand-in goalkeeper Brignoli remains the focus of the home defense as Thomas Strakosha is sidelined with a wrist injury.

Following this encounter, Real Madrid will look to sustain their offensive average of 3.4 goals per game as they continue their European campaign. The match kicks off at 18:45 CET, marking the latest test for the Spanish side as they pursue continued dominance in the tournament.
