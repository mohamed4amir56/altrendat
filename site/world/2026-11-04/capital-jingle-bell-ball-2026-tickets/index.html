Capital Jingle Bell Ball 2026 tickets go on sale this November

By Karim Fouad · Published 04 Nov 2026, 21:31 UTC

Capital Plus subscribers and Barclaycard holders gain early access to Jingle Bell Ball tickets starting November 5th, with a general sale following on November 6th.

Quick facts

Pre-sale Date
Thursday 5th November, 9am
General Sale Date
Friday 6th November, 9am
Event Dates
Saturday 5th December and Sunday 6th December 2026
Venue
The O2, London
Capital Plus Subscription
£4.99 a month or £49.99 a year

Securing tickets early requires a specific subscription through the Global Player app. Once signed up for Capital Plus or Radio Plus, users will receive an email containing a secure link and code to access the ticket portal on AXS. Barclaycard customers are also eligible for a 10% discount on their tickets during this pre-sale period. For those who do not hold these subscriptions or specific credit cards, the general sale for the public will open on Friday, November 6th, at 9am via Global Player.

The initial line-up for the first night of the event features Lewis Capaldi, Teddy Swims, Jorja Smith, Becky Hill, Tinie Tempah, Aitch, December 10, Ashe, Bash Croft, and Mulaa Joans. Lewis Capaldi, a two-time BRIT award winner, is returning to the O2 stage for the first time since 2022. Organizers have also confirmed a mystery guest will appear as part of the surprise Barclaycard Out of the Blue performance.

Capital Breakfast hosts Jordan North, Chris Stark, and Siân Welby will reveal the full list of performers for the second night of the festival tomorrow morning. Fans are reminded that receiving a pre-sale link does not guarantee a ticket purchase, as demand for the two-day event is expected to be high.
