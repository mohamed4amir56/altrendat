Manchester United Overcomes Two-Goal Deficit to Clinch Thrilling 3-2 Champions League Victory

By Karim Fouad · Published 04 Nov 2026, 20:01 UTC

Manchester United completed a dramatic second-half comeback to defeat Roma 3-2 at Old Trafford in the Champions League.

Quick facts

Match result
Manchester United 3-2 Roma
Winning streak
Ends a nine-game winless run

The atmosphere inside Old Trafford shifted from a cathedral of distrust to pure celebration as Manchester United clawed back from a two-goal deficit. The 3-2 victory marked a turning point for interim coach Michael Carrick, ending a nine-game winless streak that had spanned nearly eight weeks. While the stadium was filled with protests against ownership, including tennis balls thrown onto the pitch and green and yellow balloons at the Stretford End, the players managed to separate themselves from the surrounding tension to secure a vital result.

This victory provides immediate relief for Carrick, though his long-term future remains uncertain. The club has faced numerous false peaks over the last 13 years, leading to skepticism about whether this result signals a lasting change or merely a temporary reprieve. Systemic issues remain outside of the technical area, and experts note that repeating such second-half performances on a weekly basis will require significant adjustments beyond the manager's current influence.

The match featured a return for academy product Kobbie Mainoo, who started in midfield despite spending the previous month sidelined by injury. Early in the game, United’s backline struggled, with a mistake near the halfway line allowing Marten de Roon to set up Matías Soulé for the opening goal. However, the team rallied in the second half, delivering a performance described by observers as a discrete brilliance that briefly silenced the ongoing psychodrama at the club.

Looking ahead, Manchester United faces a test against Aston Villa this Sunday. The performance in that fixture will likely determine if the momentum from this European comeback can be sustained or if the club will continue its pattern of inconsistent form.
