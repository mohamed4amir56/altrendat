Undefeated Real Betis storms Signal Iduna Park to challenge Borussia Dortmund

By Karim Fouad · Published 04 Nov 2026, 19:34 UTC

Borussia Dortmund hosts an undefeated Real Betis side on Wednesday evening for a crucial UEFA Champions League group stage encounter.

Quick facts

Match Date
Wednesday evening
Venue

Betis Champions League Record
3 wins in 3 matches

Real Betis arrives in Germany boasting a perfect record in the Champions League, having secured victories in all three of their opening matches. Their recent European campaign includes a 3-2 win against OSC Lille, followed by two narrow home triumphs over FC Porto and Feyenoord Rotterdam. Currently sitting fourth in LaLiga, the Spanish side remains within four points of the league's top three and recently defeated Real Madrid 1-0. Stefan Effenberg has issued a stern warning to Dortmund, insisting that the German club must not underestimate their Spanish opponents. He suggested that Dortmund could face a nasty surprise if they let the perceived stature of the club dictate their preparation. Beyond the immediate threat posed by Betis, the road ahead for Dortmund appears treacherous, with upcoming fixtures scheduled against Arsenal in London and Inter Milan at home. Borussia Dortmund coach Niko Kovac acknowledged the tactical challenge, describing Betis as a technically gifted side that excels at maintaining possession and executing quick, effective transitions. Kovac noted that their opponent's ability to control the ball while remaining dangerous on the counter will serve as a definitive test for his squad. Amidst these tactical preparations, the club has highlighted the consistent performances of Jobe Bellingham. Managing director for sport Lars Ricken praised Bellingham’s work ethic, noting that the midfielder’s focus on video analysis and daily improvement has made him a cornerstone of Kovac's current setup. As the team prepares for Wednesday's kickoff, the focus remains on neutralizing a Betis squad that is currently enjoying a strong domestic and continental run of form.
