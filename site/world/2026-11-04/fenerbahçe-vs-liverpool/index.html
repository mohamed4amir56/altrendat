Andoni Iraola hands five changes to Liverpool line-up for Fenerbahce clash

By Karim Fouad · Published 04 Nov 2026, 17:05 UTC

Liverpool faces Fenerbahce in Istanbul tonight for a UEFA Champions League league-phase fixture.

Quick facts

Venue
Istanbul
Competition
UEFA Champions League
Liverpool Manager
Andoni Iraola

Joe Gomez and Kostas Tsimikas enter the Liverpool starting defence for tonight's match in Istanbul as manager Andoni Iraola looks to maintain momentum. The squad shake-up also includes starts for Victor Munoz, Rio Ngumoha, and Dominik Szoboszlai. These selections mark five changes to the line-up as the club continues its Champions League campaign following previous victories against LASK and Villarreal. Alisson starts in goal, anchored by a defensive unit featuring Gomez, Van Dijk, Jacquet, and Tsimikas, while the attacking options include Barcola, Nyoni, and Ngumoha.

This rotation strategy remains a primary focus for Iraola, particularly with an intense schedule that saw the Reds play Arsenal last Sunday and travel to face Crystal Palace this coming weekend. Managing squad fatigue has become critical for the coaching staff given the growing injury list at Anfield. The current unavailable list includes Ryan Gravenberch, Jeremie Frimping, Conor Bradley, Giovanni Leoni, and Hugo Ekitike, forcing the manager to rely on squad depth to keep available players fresh through the fixture list.

Liverpool has confirmed the bench will consist of Mamardashvili, Woodman, Kerkez, Wirtz, Isak, Gakpo, Araujo, Chambers, McConnell, Koumas, and Danns. These substitutes will be ready to contribute if required during the game. Fans can track the developments from Istanbul through live text commentary and match statistics, or follow the event via the club’s provided free audio commentary in the official Match Centre. The tactical decisions made during this match are expected to be pivotal for Liverpool’s standing in the league phase of the tournament, especially as the team looks to balance European commitments with their domestic schedule.
