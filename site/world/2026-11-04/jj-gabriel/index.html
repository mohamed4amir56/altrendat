Real Madrid reach verbal agreement to snatch Manchester United teenager JJ Gabriel

By Karim Fouad · Published 04 Nov 2026, 13:31 UTC

Real Madrid expects to sign 16-year-old forward JJ Gabriel in January after the player walked out on Manchester United in September.

Expected signing window

Sixteen-year-old JJ Gabriel has not trained with Manchester United since September, when he formally requested to leave the club and asked to be de-registered to become a free agent. While he was previously slated to potentially become the club's youngest debutant during a Carabao Cup tie against Brighton, the teenager has instead opted to train independently in Spain while finalizing his move to Real Madrid. The transfer hinges on a FIFA loophole concerning players who hold European passports, a status Gabriel qualifies for through his parents. This technicality allows the Spanish giants to secure the forward despite his existing registration with the United academy. According to Sky Sports, Real Madrid has bypassed direct club-to-club negotiations, opting to secure a verbal agreement directly with the player and his representatives. This strategy has put them ahead of other interested European powerhouses including Barcelona, Paris Saint-Germain, and Bayern Munich. For Manchester United, the departure marks the loss of a highly-rated prospect who was named the Premier League's Under-18 player of the season last term at just 14 years old. While United officials have indicated they would consider legal action regarding the move, the teenager's camp has expressed little concern over such measures. United would be entitled to compensation for the academy graduate, though the amount is expected to be lower than if he were moving to another Premier League side. Real Madrid now intends to formally complete the signing of the youngster when the January transfer window opens. The club is currently positioning itself to finalize the deal despite the lack of direct communication between the two organizations involved in the player's development.
