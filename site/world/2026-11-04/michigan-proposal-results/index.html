Mid-Michigan voters head to polls for crucial statewide races and local proposals

By Karim Fouad · Published 04 Nov 2026, 11:01 UTC

Voters in mid-Michigan cast their ballots on Tuesday, Nov. 3, to decide on various statewide races and local bond and millage proposals.

Mid-Michigan voters converged on polling locations Tuesday, Nov. 3, marking the conclusion of a significant election cycle for the state. Beyond the high-profile statewide races capturing national attention, residents are casting critical votes on local bond and millage proposals that will dictate the future of community funding and public infrastructure.

These local decisions matter because they directly impact the tax burden and public service levels for residents across the region. Bond and millage proposals determine how local governments finance essential projects, ranging from school improvements to public utility maintenance. Voters today are not just choosing representatives but are actively shaping the immediate financial landscape of their neighborhoods.

According to WILX, live election results are being compiled as votes are cast throughout the day. The network is facilitating continuous coverage to ensure that the public remains informed as ballot totals shift. PBS is also providing comprehensive, live tracking for the 2026 Michigan elections, offering maps that allow citizens to monitor the electoral landscape across the entire state in real time.

As the day progresses and polls close, News 10 Lansing plans to provide ongoing analysis and updates. Viewers can access the latest developments through the free News 10+ app, which will host victory speeches, candidate check-ins, and detailed breakdowns of how these races and proposals are tilting as the night continues.
