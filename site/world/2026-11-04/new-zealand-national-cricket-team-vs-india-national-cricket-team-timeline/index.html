Virat Kohli Smashes Cricket History With 13,000 ODI Runs at No. 3

By Karim Fouad · Published 04 Nov 2026, 09:01 UTC

Virat Kohli became the first batter to reach 13,000 ODI runs at the number three position during the opening match of India's tour of New Zealand.

Quick facts

Venue
Eden Park, Auckland
India Bowling Lead
Arshdeep Patel 4-70
New Zealand Total
185 all out
Series Format
5-match ODI series

Virat Kohli reached the historic 13,000-run milestone at the number three position by scoring 12 runs off his 13th ball in Auckland. This achievement came during the seventh over of India's chase against New Zealand in the first of five one-day cricket internationals at Eden Park. Kohli reached the mark in 251 innings, holding an average well above 60 at the position while securing 48 centuries and nearly 70 half-centuries in his career at that spot.

This performance solidifies Kohli’s standing as an all-time great, as he maintains a significant lead over former Australia captain Ricky Ponting, the only other player to exceed 10,000 runs batting at number three. Kohli’s consistency remains a focal point for fans and analysts alike, with his ability to command the crease at Eden Park serving as a testament to his elite longevity in international cricket.

The milestone occurred as India looked to chase down New Zealand’s target of 185 runs. India had dominated the first innings, bowling New Zealand out in 40.2 overs. Arshdeep Patel led the bowling attack with 4-70, while Gurnoor Brar and Anshul Kamboj added critical wickets to keep the scoring pressure high despite the short boundaries at the venue. India’s fielding also saw key contributions, including a notable one-handed catch by Kohli to dismiss Nathan Smith.

India continues this series following a successful 3-2 victory in the recent T20 series. The team sees the return of several stars, including captain Shubman Gil, Rohit Sharma, and K.L. Rahul, as they prepare for the remaining four matches of the tour.
