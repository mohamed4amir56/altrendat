Manchester City looks to snap three-match winless streak against perfect RB Leipzig

By Karim Fouad · Published 04 Nov 2026, 19:34 UTC

Manchester City travels to face RB Leipzig, who remains unbeaten at home this season without conceding a single goal.

Quick facts

Leipzig home record
5 wins, 0 goals conceded
Man City current form

Competition
UEFA Champions League

RB Leipzig enters this Champions League fixture with a flawless record on their home turf, having won all five of their matches in all competitions this season without conceding a goal. This defensive streak includes a 1-0 victory over PSV earlier in the tournament, setting up a daunting challenge for the visiting English side. Manchester City arrives at the stadium under unusual pressure, as they head into this match without a win in their last three outings in all competitions. The team is currently struggling to secure a result within the regulation 90 minutes, marking a rare period of difficulty for the squad. Analysts expect Manchester City to place immense pressure on Leipzig’s defensive record as they attempt to break the German side's undefeated home form. This clash serves as a test for both clubs, with Leipzig aiming to maintain their defensive stability and City fighting to snap their winless run and regain momentum in the Champions League campaign. Team news is expected to be released shortly, which will reveal the starting lineups tasked with either continuing the clean sheet streak or finally breaking the Leipzig defense. The match represents a pivotal moment for both teams to prove their current form on the European stage.
