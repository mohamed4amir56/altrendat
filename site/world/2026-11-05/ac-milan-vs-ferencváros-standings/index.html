Lenny Joseph brace stuns San Siro as Ferencvaros downs AC Milan 2-0

By Karim Fouad · Published 05 Nov 2026, 22:31 UTC

AC Milan suffered a damaging 2-0 defeat to Ferencvaros on Thursday, leaving the Italian side with three losses in their opening four Europa League matches.

Lenny Joseph secured a second-half brace at the San Siro to condemn AC Milan to a 2-0 loss, a result manager Ruben Amorim described as a performance he could not explain. The defeat marks a significant low point for the hosts, who have now lost three of their first four group stage fixtures in the current European campaign.

This loss leaves AC Milan in a precarious position within the Europa League standings, threatening their progression as they struggle to maintain competitiveness on two fronts. For the club, the failure to secure points at home against Ferencvaros highlights deep-seated issues with concentration and intensity that Amorim explicitly criticized after the final whistle.

According to Goal.com, Amorim made six changes to his starting lineup following a recent victory in the Derby della Madonnina, but the gamble failed to spark the squad. Despite second-half efforts from substitute stars Luka Modric and Christian Pulisic, the home side could not bypass Ferencvaros goalkeeper Dénes Dibusz. The BBC reported that Milan players attempted several shots throughout the match, including efforts from Diego Moreira and Omari Hutchinson, but they were consistently blocked or saved.

Amorim admitted his players were not prepared to play football, stating that they surrendered the match through lack of ambition and individual mistakes. He noted that while he usually defends his team, he had no excuses for this performance. The manager identified defender Strahinja Pavlovic as the only individual whose work ethic currently meets the standards required for the team moving forward.
