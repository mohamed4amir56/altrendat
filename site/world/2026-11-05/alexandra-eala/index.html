World Number 19 Alexandra Eala Storms Into Prudential Hong Kong Open Draw

By Karim Fouad · Published 05 Nov 2026, 12:01 UTC

Filipino tennis star Alexandra Eala faces American Alycia Parks in the first round of the WTA 250 tournament at Victoria Park Tennis Stadium this Tuesday.

Alexandra Eala enters the Prudential Hong Kong Open ranked world number 19, carrying the second seed into a tournament that marks the final tour-level event of the 2026 season for non-WTA Finals qualifiers. Her return to Hong Kong follows an active stretch of competition that included a quarterfinal appearance last week at the WTA 500 Toray Pan Pacific Open in Tokyo, where she narrowly fell to tournament winner Diana Shnaider in three sets. Eala expressed her excitement at a pre-event press conference on Monday, noting that the crowd and hospitality she experienced during her previous appearance made the return to the event a priority. The Hong Kong tournament serves as a key opportunity for Eala to regain momentum following early exits in Wuhan and Singapore during the recent Asian swing. While Eala successfully secured a singles bronze medal at the Nagoya Asian Games on October 1, her subsequent results in China and Singapore included a difficult loss to world number 180 Tatiana Prozorova at the Singapore Open. According to the Philippine News Agency, Eala is looking to build on the foundation of her 2025 performance at this venue, where she previously advanced past the Round of 32. After her opening match against Parks, Eala is set to navigate a path that could include a second-round meeting with Czech player Tereza Valentova. The outcome of this event will close out the competitive schedule for Eala before the season concludes, as the professional tour shifts focus toward the upcoming WTA Finals held in Indian Wells from November 8 to 15.
