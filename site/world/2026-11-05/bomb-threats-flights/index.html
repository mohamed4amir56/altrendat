18 Commercial Flights Across Europe Diverted Following String of Malicious Bomb Threats

By Karim Fouad · Published 05 Nov 2026, 15:01 UTC

At least 18 flights across Europe faced security disruptions on Thursday due to a series of unconfirmed bomb threats reported to multiple airlines.

Quick facts

Affected flights
18
Affected airlines
Wizz Air and Ryanair
Date of latest incidents
Thursday

Passengers faced urgent security protocols on Thursday as military jets escorted several aircraft after authorities received a wave of malicious bomb threats targeting commercial flights across Europe. The disruptions follow a similar pattern of warnings experienced on Tuesday, forcing unplanned diversions and emergency inspections for carriers including Wizz Air and Ryanair. This surge of threats causes significant operational challenges for major airlines and airports, requiring immediate diversions and localized police inspections to ensure passenger safety. While no actual threats were identified during these security checks, the incidents force thousands of travelers into unexpected delays and logistical arrangements for land transportation between destinations. According to BBC reports, a Wizz Air flight traveling from Milan Malpensa to Warsaw diverted to Vienna after receiving a bomb threat in mid-air. Simultaneously, the Bulgarian transport ministry confirmed that three Ryanair aircraft operating flights to Sofia from Madrid, Copenhagen, and London were subjected to safety procedures. While the flights from Madrid and Copenhagen landed as scheduled in Sofia, the flight from London was diverted to Plovdiv Airport. Officials subsequently cleared all three planes after thorough inspections determined the reports were hoaxes. European transport authorities are currently monitoring the situation to determine if the 18 identified incidents are linked. As of now, airport operations in affected hubs have returned to normal, but airlines continue to follow established security protocols to prioritize the safety of crew and passengers during this ongoing wave of malicious activity.
