Celtic clinches first Europa League victory after hard-fought comeback against Besiktas

By Karim Fouad · Published 05 Nov 2026, 22:31 UTC

Celtic secured their first win of the Europa League campaign after rallying to defeat Besiktas.

Quick facts

Europa League
Opponent

Result
Win

Callum Osmand described the victory as a battle that required the team to dig in and stay strong against a high-quality opponent. The forward highlighted teammate Camilo Duran for his performance, labeling him a poacher who played an amazing role in the outcome. Osmand noted that the match atmosphere and the need to recover from an opponent's goal felt similar to their previous experience against Celta Vigo. After falling behind, the squad managed to regain their composure and find the necessary goals to secure the result.

This victory marks a significant turning point for Celtic, as it stands as the club's first win in the Europa League competition this season. For the players and coaching staff, gaining these points is essential for building momentum in the group stage standings. A successful run in European competition often defines a club's season, providing both financial benefits and a boost in team confidence that can translate into domestic league performance.

According to reports from BBC, the match featured a late push where Celtic capitalized on their opportunities to overcome Besiktas. Callum Osmand acknowledged that coming off the bench can be challenging, but he expressed satisfaction at being in the right place to contribute to the winning effort. The team’s ability to remain disciplined while trailing served as the deciding factor in the fixture.

Looking ahead, the club will aim to carry this performance into their remaining Europa League fixtures. Having finally broken their winless streak in the tournament, the players are focused on maintaining this level of consistency to improve their standing in the competition table.
