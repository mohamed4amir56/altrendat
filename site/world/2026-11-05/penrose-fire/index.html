Penrose inferno hospitalizes three and triggers widespread power outages across Auckland

By Karim Fouad · Published 05 Nov 2026, 05:01 UTC

A massive building fire in Penrose has sent three people to the hospital while causing significant power outages and traffic disruptions.

Quick facts

Casualties
Three hospitalised
Location
Penrose, Auckland

Witnesses described the event as sounding like a bomb as the blaze broke out in a Penrose building, sending thick plumes of smoke billowing across the Auckland skyline. Three people were hospitalized following the fire, which caused an immediate impact on local infrastructure and commuters. The intensity of the blaze resulted in smoke spreading across the surrounding area, reaching into nearby Onehunga. The power outages have disrupted daily life for residents and businesses in the vicinity, creating substantial traffic chaos throughout the suburb. Civil Defence has issued official advice for all Aucklanders affected by the ongoing smoke and utility failures, urging caution as emergency crews manage the situation. The fire serves as a stark disruption to the suburb, forcing a shift from routine activities to emergency safety protocols for those in the path of the smoke and the resulting infrastructure gridlock. Authorities continue to monitor the scene while the electrical grid remains compromised in the wake of the incident. Further updates regarding the stability of the building and the timeline for power restoration are expected as the response from emergency services continues.
