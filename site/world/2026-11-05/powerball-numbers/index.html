The winning sequence drawn for the $847 million prize includes the numbers 15, 34, 41, 53, and 56, with a Powerball of 21. This substantial total ranks among the top 10 largest jackpots in the history of the game. For players choosing the lump-sum payment, the cash value of the prize stands at $357.9 million.

This jackpot opportunity represents a significant event for participants across the country. Powerball tickets are available for $2 per play, providing a chance at one of the largest payouts currently offered by any state-run lottery. In regions like Michigan, ticket sales for the night of a drawing close at 9:45 p.m. local time.

According to Yahoo and The Detroit News, the drawings for this game are conducted regularly on Monday, Wednesday, and Saturday nights at 10:59 p.m. Participation requires matching these specific numbers to secure the top tier of the game's prize structure.

Following the Wednesday, Nov. 4, drawing, the lottery process continues with the next scheduled event. Players will have their next opportunity to participate when the drawing occurs this coming Saturday.
