Sparta Prague topples Bournemouth as 10-man side holds onto narrow lead

By Karim Fouad · Published 05 Nov 2026, 17:31 UTC · Updated 05 Nov 2026, 19:01 UTC

Sparta Prague secured a 1-0 UEFA Europa League victory over Bournemouth after Ebrima Singhateh scored following a fast break.

Quick facts

Match result
Sparta Prague 1, Bournemouth 0
Goalscorer
Ebrima Singhateh
Key event
Tobias Guddal received a second yellow card
Competition
UEFA Europa League

Ebrima Singhateh delivered the decisive moment for Sparta Prague, burying a left-footed shot into the center of the goal after a swift transition during the second half. This strike broke the deadlock after Bournemouth had struggled to capitalize on earlier opportunities, including a missed penalty by Lewis Cook that left the visitors empty-handed. Despite Sparta Prague being reduced to 10 men following a second yellow card issued to Tobias Guddal for a bad foul, the home side managed to successfully defend their narrow advantage until the final whistle.

For Bournemouth, the defeat represents a missed chance to secure points in the competition. The Cherries were unable to find the net despite several attempts throughout the match, including headers from Álvaro Rodríguez and Bafodé Diakité that were saved by Sparta goalkeeper Jakub Surovcík. The inability to convert their possession and attacking pressure into a goal proved costly for the team as they chased the game in the final minutes.

According to reports from the match, Bournemouth struggled to find their best form despite a second-half period where they dominated ball possession. The game remained scoreless for a long duration, characterized by a series of missed attempts, including a long-range effort from James Hill and blocked shots from Ben Gannon-Doak. The fourth official announced 3 minutes of added time, but Bournemouth could not force an equalizer. Ki-Jana Hoever was also shown a yellow card for Sparta Prague during the contest, reflecting the competitive nature of the physical battle on the pitch. The result leaves Bournemouth reflecting on a match they had the opportunity to control but ultimately failed to influence where it mattered most.
