Alexandra Eala battles Antonia Ruzic for a Hong Kong Tennis Open semifinal spot

By Karim Fouad · Published 06 Nov 2026, 15:31 UTC

Alexandra Eala competes against Antonia Ruzic in the Prudential Hong Kong Tennis Open quarter-finals on Friday evening.

Quick facts

Tournament
Prudential Hong Kong Tennis Open
Stage
Quarter-finals
Date
Friday evening
Opponent
Antonia Ruzic

After more than an hour and 20 minutes of a hard-fought battle, Alexandra Eala secured the first set against Antonia Ruzic. The momentum shifted throughout the match, including a moment where rain interrupted play, forcing the umpire to inspect the court before the athletes resumed hitting balls. Eala clawed back from a late-set deficit to force a tiebreak scenario, echoing the intensity of her previous victory against Tereza Valentova.

This quarter-final clash at the Prudential Hong Kong Tennis Open marks a significant stage for Eala, who has been in red-hot form during the tournament. Advancing to the semifinals would solidify her trajectory in the competition after a successful run that saw her eliminate Emerson Jones in the opening round with scores of 6-4, 6-3, followed by a tight win over Valentova in the round of 16.

According to the Daily Tribune, the match featured rapid exchanges, with Eala utilizing precise forehands to find corners and secure leads, such as her 3-2 cushion. Despite a challenging 3-0 run from Ruzic at one point and the pressure of a tiebreak, Eala maintained her composure to claim the opening set. The two players are meeting for the first time on the professional circuit.

As the tournament progresses, the winner of this match moves closer to the title, with all eyes on whether Eala can sustain her performance level against Ruzic. Fans continue to track the live updates as the competition intensifies under the stadium lights.
