Alexandra Eala storms into WTA 250 Hong Kong Open quarterfinals after victory

By Karim Fouad · Published 06 Nov 2026, 10:31 UTC

Filipino second seed Alexandra Eala defeated Tereza Valentova in two sets on Thursday to secure her spot in the tournament quarterfinals.

Alexandra Eala secured a 6-4, 7-6 (7-0) victory against Czech opponent Tereza Valentova on the outdoor hardcourt at Victoria Park Tennis Stadium. This performance pushes the 19-year-old into the quarterfinals during the final WTA Tour event of 2026. Prior to this match, Eala navigated her first round by defeating Australian Emerson Jones in straight sets, 6-4, 6-3.

Advancing through the bracket is critical for Eala as the WTA Tour concludes its season. The Hong Kong Open acts as a final tune-up for participants, occurring simultaneously with the Chennai Open in India. Players are currently finalizing their form before the WTA Finals begin on November 8 at Indian Wells.

According to the Philippine News Agency, Eala will now face Croatian Antonia Ruzic, who reached this stage by defeating sixth seed Xinyu Wang. Other notable results from the event include fourth seed Maria Sakkari, who advanced by overcoming Maya Joint, and American Claire Liu, who bested British eighth seed Katie Boulter. Turkish competitor Zeynep Sonmez and Russian Alexandra Shubladze also booked their places in the next round after respective wins against Mika Stojsavljevic and Elena Pridankina.

The upcoming quarterfinal match against Ruzic will determine if Eala progresses to the final four. The tournament field remains competitive as top-ranked players vie for success in these last remaining matches before the season-ending competition in Indian Wells begins.
