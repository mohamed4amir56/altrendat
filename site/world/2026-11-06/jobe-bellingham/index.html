Jobe Bellingham earns first senior England call-up under head coach Thomas Tuchel

By Karim Fouad · Published 06 Nov 2026, 11:31 UTC

Thomas Tuchel has selected Jobe Bellingham for the senior England squad to face Croatia and Spain in the Nations League.

Quick facts

Squad size
25-man
Upcoming opponents
Croatia and Spain

Borussia Dortmund midfielder Jobe Bellingham joins the senior setup after U21 head coach Lee Carsley spoke highly of his performance for weeks and months. His inclusion marks a significant elevation to the senior squad as England prepares for upcoming Nations League fixtures. This selection highlights the competitive nature of the current squad, as Tuchel opts to bring in fresh faces while leaving out established players such as Phil Foden, Kobbie Mainoo, and Cole Palmer. The decision to include Bellingham alongside Josh King follows a period where the England management team prioritized players who demonstrated consistency during previous camps. For fans and observers, the call-up signals an opportunity for Bellingham to translate his youth international experience into the senior level under Tuchel's leadership. The head coach emphasized that spots in the squad are earned through performance and presence in camp, noting that the competition for places remains high. According to Sky Sports, the 25-man roster features several changes including the return of Ben White and Djed Spence, while the team continues to manage various injuries within the group. The squad is now finalizing its preparations ahead of the critical Nations League matches against Croatia and Spain. As the international break approaches, focus shifts to how the new additions will integrate into the starting rotation. Tuchel's approach suggests that the door remains open for all players who show the required form and fitness, with the current camp acting as the primary evaluation point for future selection cycles.
