Severe Thunderstorms Dump Up To 40mm Of Rain Per Hour In Northland

By Karim Fouad · Published 06 Nov 2026, 10:31 UTC

MetService issued a severe thunderstorm watch for Northland, Auckland, Waikato, Bay of Plenty and Taranaki as a tropical airmass brings unstable conditions today.

Quick facts

Affected Regions
Northland, Auckland, Waikato, Bay of Plenty, Taranaki
Rainfall Rate
25 to 40mm per hour
Start Time
11am

A nest of lightning tracks toward the coast as a warm and humid tropical airmass fuels dangerous conditions across the top half of the North Island. The weather system arrived with high instability, creating a landscape primed for heavy localized downpours.

This weather pattern poses a direct threat to commuters and residents as rainfall rates reaching 40mm per hour are expected to hit throughout Friday afternoon and evening. The rapid accumulation of water carries the significant risk of flash flooding and potential slips, particularly in low-lying areas such as rivers, streams, and narrow valleys. Authorities, including Auckland Emergency Management, have cautioned the public to remain vigilant as these conditions evolve.

According to MetService, the severe thunderstorm watch covers Northland, Auckland, Waikato, Bay of Plenty, and Taranaki. Meteorologist Alanna Burrows noted that the moderate risk of severe thunderstorms is specifically focused on southwestern parts of Northland and Auckland. The forecast indicates that rainfall rates between 25 and 40mm per hour are possible starting from 11am today.

The volatile weather is not expected to clear immediately. MetService warns that the risk of thunderstorms will persist into Saturday, with the potential for the instability to reach parts of the South Island. Officials are advising those heading out to polling stations to monitor live rain radar and the latest official forecasts to stay updated on changing conditions during the voting period.
