Patriots Running Back TreVeyon Henderson Hospitalized Following Mysterious Unexplained Neurological Emergency Symptoms

By Karim Fouad · Published 06 Nov 2026, 16:01 UTC · Updated 06 Nov 2026, 18:32 UTC

New England running back TreVeyon Henderson announced he was hospitalized for several days due to unexplained neurological symptoms that impacted his physical coordination.

Neurological symptoms requiring multiple days of hospitalization have sidelined running back TreVeyon Henderson, who confirmed that medical tests have yet to identify a specific cause for his condition. Henderson, currently in his second season with the New England Patriots, noted that these unexplained issues have directly impacted his physical strength, coordination, and overall bodily functions.

The health of the running back remains the primary concern for the organization as he works with specialists and physical therapists toward recovery. While test results have been described as positive and reassuring, the lack of a definitive diagnosis leaves his return to the field uncertain for the immediate future. Henderson, who has recorded 248 yards from scrimmage this season, is focusing entirely on his long-term well-being and has requested privacy during his rehabilitation process.

According to NFL.com, the Patriots initially reported Henderson’s absence as an illness, keeping him out of practice since October 28. He missed the Week 8 victory against the Miami Dolphins, leaving the team to rely on Rhamondre Stevenson, Corey Kiner, and Lan Larison in the backfield. Prior to this medical emergency, Henderson had been managing a high ankle sprain suffered earlier in the season during a Week 5 win over Las Vegas.

As Henderson continues his recovery, the Patriots move forward with their season schedule, preparing for a matchup against the Green Bay Packers. The team confirmed he has not participated in practices since the onset of his symptoms in late October.
