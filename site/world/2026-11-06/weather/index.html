South Africa braces for level 4 storm alerts as severe weather strikes

By Karim Fouad · Published 06 Nov 2026, 05:31 UTC

The South African Weather Service issued level 4 and level 2 thunderstorm warnings for multiple provinces, bringing threats of heavy rain and flooding.

Quick facts

Warning Level
Level 4 (Gauteng and KZN), Level 2 (Central and Western regions)
Weather Hazards
Heavy rain, hailstones, damaging winds, lightning, localized flooding
Affected Provinces
Gauteng, KwaZulu-Natal, Free State, Eastern Cape, Mpumalanga, North West, Northern Cape

Residents across central and eastern South Africa face a significant weather threat as the South African Weather Service issues a yellow level 4 warning for severe thunderstorms. Developing from early evening, these systems are poised to bring heavy downpours, hailstones, and damaging winds to vulnerable areas.

This weather pattern poses a direct risk to infrastructure and public safety, necessitating extreme caution from those on the roads. Motorists are specifically advised to steer clear of bridges and routes known for localized flooding, as the combination of lightning and intense rainfall threatens to disrupt daily travel across the affected provinces.

According to SA Weather Service spokesperson Lehlohonolo Thobela, the severe conditions are expected to impact Gauteng, KwaZulu-Natal, the Free State, the Eastern Cape, Mpumalanga, and eastern parts of the North West. Additionally, a level 2 warning covers parts of the central interior and western regions, while the Northern Cape faces warnings for potentially damaging weather.

Specific alerts for Friday cover parts of KwaZulu-Natal, including municipalities such as uMlalazi, uMngeni, uMshwathi, and KwaDukuza. Infrastructure damage remains a primary concern for authorities in these areas due to the predicted combination of high wind speeds and concentrated rainfall. Pedestrians and drivers should remain vigilant as these storms move through the interior, prioritizing safety over travel plans until the warnings expire.
