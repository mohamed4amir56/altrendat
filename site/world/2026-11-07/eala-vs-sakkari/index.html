Rain halts Hong Kong semifinal clash between Alexandra Eala and Maria Sakkari

By Karim Fouad · Published 07 Nov 2026, 10:31 UTC

The Hong Kong Tennis Open semifinal between Alexandra Eala and Maria Sakkari was suspended due to rain with Sakkari leading 5-2 in the second set.

Alexandra Eala took the opening set 7-5 against Maria Sakkari before rain forced the match to be paused with Eala trailing 2-5 in the second set. The interruption occurred as Eala sought a spot in her third career final and second of 2026. This showdown serves as the first career meeting between Eala and the No. 4 seed Sakkari on the WTA Tour. The outcome of this match carries significant weight for Eala as she concludes her tournament schedule for the year. A victory would not only advance her to the finals but also bring a Top 15 ranking debut within her reach. The pressure remains high after Eala reached this stage by surviving a grueling quarterfinal against Antonia Ruzic that featured 29 total break points. According to WTA Tennis, Eala secured her semifinal berth by winning 7-6 (7), 7-5 against Ruzic in a match that lasted two hours and 30 minutes. She demonstrated resilience during that quarterfinal by winning the final three games of the second set after a previous rain delay. Live updates from the Hong Kong Tennis Open confirm the current suspension of the semifinal match as both players wait for conditions to improve to resume play. The tournament is the final event of the year for Eala, who continues to look for her second final appearance of the 2026 season. Players and fans await word on when the match will restart to determine who will face the next opponent for the title.
