Harry Kane assists propel Bayern Munich to the top of Bundesliga table

By Karim Fouad · Published 07 Nov 2026, 18:01 UTC

Bayern Munich secured a 2-1 victory over Mainz on Saturday to remain at the top of the Bundesliga standings on goal difference.

Harry Kane reached a league-high eight assists this season by setting up both Bayern Munich goals in their 2-1 win against Mainz. The England captain has provided more assists in the opening nine matchdays than any player since data collection began in 2004-05, further establishing himself as a vital playmaker for the league leaders.

This victory keeps Bayern locked in a tight race at the summit of the table, level on 21 points with Borussia Dortmund after nine rounds. With the clubs separated only by goal difference, every point remains crucial as both teams push for the title in the early stages of the campaign.

Joshua Kimmich opened the scoring in the 14th minute after Kane intercepted a stray ball from defender Danny da Costa. Bayern doubled their lead before the hour mark when Lennart Karl swept home a pass from Kane. Mainz responded in stoppage time with a header from Phillip Tietz, but it was not enough to mount a comeback. The match also saw Manuel Neuer set a record as Bayern’s oldest-ever Bundesliga player at 40 years and 225 days, though he was substituted shortly after the break due to a foot injury.

Bayern managed the win despite missing coach Vincent Kompany and key players including Jamal Musiala, Luis Diaz, Ismael Saibari, and Serge Gnabry. The team will look to maintain their position as the season progresses through this competitive period.
