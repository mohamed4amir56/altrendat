Luka Doncic Storms Back To Power Lakers Past Portland Trail Blazers

By Karim Fouad · Published 07 Nov 2026, 06:31 UTC

The Los Angeles Lakers overcame a 16-point deficit to defeat the Portland Trail Blazers 124-118 in their NBA Cup opener.

Luka Doncic delivered a Herculean performance to lead the Los Angeles Lakers to a 124-118 comeback victory against the Portland Trail Blazers. The Lakers, who previously dropped an early-season meeting to Portland on Oct. 27, erased a 16-point first-half deficit to secure their fourth win in a row. Doncic continues a historic scoring surge, as he joins Wilt Chamberlain as the only players since the 1960-61 season to record five 40-point performances within their first eight games of a campaign.

This victory is significant as it marks the Lakers' first NBA Cup game of the season. For the Trail Blazers, the loss disrupts their momentum despite a strong showing from Deni Avdija, who nearly recorded a triple-double with 22 points, 11 assists, and nine rebounds. Damian Lillard also contributed as the team's leading scorer, providing veteran leadership that has bolstered Portland’s competitive standing this year compared to the previous season.

According to Sports Illustrated, the Blazers struggled with execution, notably suffering from Scoot Henderson's five turnovers and Donovan Clingan's foul trouble, which limited him to 28 minutes of play. While Portland remained within striking distance late, the team ultimately could not contain the Lakers' offensive pressure. Portland’s next challenge arrives quickly as they prepare to host the San Antonio Spurs in a playoff rematch. The Trail Blazers look to bounce back at the Moda Center, with tip-off scheduled for 7 p.m. PT.
