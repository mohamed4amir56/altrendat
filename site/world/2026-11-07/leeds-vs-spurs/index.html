Joe Rodon returns to Leeds United squad for Tottenham Hotspur home clash

By Karim Fouad · Published 07 Nov 2026, 12:01 UTC

Joe Rodon is back in contention for Leeds United as they prepare to host Tottenham Hotspur at Elland Road.

Joe Rodon is back in full team training this week and ready to compete for a spot in the matchday squad. His return follows a lengthy spell on the sidelines, providing a significant boost as Leeds United prepares to face Roberto De Zerbi’s Tottenham Hotspur side at Elland Road. While Rodon’s comeback marks a positive shift for the defense, the team faces uncertainty in other areas of the pitch ahead of the weekend fixture. Leeds United manager Daniel Farke confirmed that offensive options remain limited as several key players struggle with ongoing physical setbacks. According to Farke, Noah Okafor and Anton Stach both face major doubts for the match. Okafor has been restricted to individual rehab work due to adductor problems and has yet to participate in full team sessions this week. Similarly, Stach continues to undergo individual recovery for foot problems. The club is also managing a situation with Dominic Calvert-Lewin, who experienced a recent setback regarding his foot. Although he missed the latest training session, Farke expressed careful optimism that Calvert-Lewin could return to training sessions tomorrow and potentially feature in the upcoming match. Fans can follow the progress of the game through live text commentary and score updates, while BBC Radio 5 Live will provide live coverage of the action. The club will continue to monitor the status of the injured players over the final two days leading up to kickoff to determine the final team selection.
