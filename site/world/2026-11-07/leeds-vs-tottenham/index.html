In-Form Tottenham Pursues Fourth Successive Premier League Win Against Leeds United

By Karim Fouad · Published 07 Nov 2026, 13:01 UTC

Tottenham Hotspur aims for their fourth consecutive league victory while visiting Leeds United at Elland Road today.

Quick facts

Venue
Elland Road
League
Premier League

Roberto De Zerbi’s Tottenham squad arrives at Elland Road searching for a fourth straight Premier League victory, riding the momentum of recent wins against Coventry, Chelsea, and Crystal Palace. The team’s current form marks a significant turnaround from earlier in the season when the side struggled with a three-goal deficit against Aston Villa, a period that raised doubts about the manager’s future. According to The Guardian, the mood within the club, referred to as N17, has shifted to high expectations as the side looks to maintain its winning streak.

For the home side, the stakes are equally clear. Despite holding a comfortable 12th place in the standings with only two defeats so far, Leeds United has faced a recent slump. Daniel Farke’s team has secured only two points from their last four league matches, dampening the optimism that defined their unexpectedly strong early-season progress. A victory at this lunchtime fixture is considered essential for Leeds to recover the momentum lost during this recent dry spell.

The Guardian reports that both teams approach the match with contrasting narratives. While Tottenham enters the clash with heightened pressure to sustain their resurgence, Leeds United requires a decisive result to stabilize their position in the table. The outcome of this encounter will determine whether Tottenham can cement their place among the league's top-performing clubs or if Leeds can snap their winless run at home.
