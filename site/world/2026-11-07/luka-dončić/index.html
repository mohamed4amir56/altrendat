Luka Doncic Routs Portland With 53 Points, Ties Michael Jordan Milestone

By Karim Fouad · Published 07 Nov 2026, 07:31 UTC

Luka Doncic scored 53 points to lead the Los Angeles Lakers to a 124-118 victory over the Portland Trail Blazers on Friday.

Luka Doncic produced a 53-point masterclass to power the Los Angeles Lakers to a 124-118 win, marking his 11th career 50-point game. This performance ties the Slovenian superstar with Michael Jordan for the most games in NBA history with at least 40 points, five rebounds, and five assists, a total of 62 such outings. Beyond the milestone, Doncic became the first player in league history to record seven consecutive games with at least 35 points, five rebounds, five assists, and five three-pointers made.

This offensive surge secures the Lakers' position as they improve to an 8-2 record to start the 2026-27 season. For the franchise, the consistency is historic; Doncic is only the fourth player to score 40 points in four consecutive games, joining Elgin Baylor, George Mikan, and Kobe Bryant, who was the last to achieve the feat in the 2011-12 season. The Lakers relied on this production as a 12-point lead narrowed to four in the final moments, requiring a clutch jumper from Doncic with 28 seconds left.

According to BasketNews, Doncic has scored 361 points through his first nine games, the highest total in the modern NBA and the most since Wilt Chamberlain in 1962. The Guardian notes that the Lakers also saw 25 points from Austin Reaves and 16 points from Walker Kessler, helping avenge an earlier loss to the Blazers. This win marks the Lakers' fourth consecutive victory as they move forward in the NBA Cup competition.
