Manchester City targets bounce back victory against struggling Nottingham Forest this Saturday

By Karim Fouad · Published 07 Nov 2026, 17:01 UTC

Manchester City aims to end a two-match Premier League losing streak when they face Nottingham Forest at the City Ground.

Quick facts

Match Day
Saturday
Venue
City Ground
Forest League Points
6

Manchester City enters the match after successive Premier League defeats against Aston Villa and Brighton & Hove Albion, which allowed Arsenal to take first place in the standings. While City secured a 3-2 victory over RB Leipzig in the Champions League, they have failed to keep a clean sheet in their previous seven matches across all competitions, conceding two or more goals four times during that stretch. Their attack remains potent, headlined by league top scorer Erling Haaland, who continues to lead their efforts on the field.

Victory is essential for Manchester City to remain competitive in the title race, as dropping more points would cause them to fall further behind their rivals. For Nottingham Forest, the stakes are equally high as the club sits in the relegation zone with only six points from nine matches. They have yet to score a goal during their four league matches at the City Ground this season, collecting only a single point at home.

Nottingham Forest boss Oliver Glasner is shaking up his side to address recent form, bringing in Ryan Yates as captain for his first start of the season and replacing Ola Aina with Daniel Munoz. The team receives a potential boost with the return of Igor Jesus to training following a calf injury, though Nicolò Savona remains sidelined after 10 months of recovery. Forest enters this fixture needing to overcome a 17-place gap in the table against the visiting Manchester behemoths.

This match marks the final outing for both clubs before the upcoming November international break. A win for City would snap their league losing run, while Forest looks for only their second victory of the current campaign.
