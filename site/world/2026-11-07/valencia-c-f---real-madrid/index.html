Real Madrid Storms Mestalla Seeking Eighth Straight Win Against Struggling Valencia Side

By Karim Fouad · Published 07 Nov 2026, 19:31 UTC

Real Madrid faces Valencia at Mestalla this Saturday in the 12th round of LaLiga.

Quick facts

Date
Saturday, November 7
Stadium
Mestalla
Referee
Francisco Hernández Maeso
Time (Spain)
21:00

Real Madrid enters Mestalla riding a massive wave of momentum, carrying seven consecutive victories across all competitions into Saturday’s clash. Their latest escape came in Athens, where they snatched a narrow 3-2 Champions League win against AEK, fueling confidence as they return to domestic league action. In stark contrast, Valencia remains mired in a delicate position, currently sitting in 19th place with only nine points accumulated from two wins, three draws, and six defeats. While Valencia managed to find some much-needed rhythm by beating Elche in their last outing, they face a daunting test against a Madrid squad that has consistently found success on this ground recently, including a 2-0 victory last February.

This match carries significant weight for both clubs as the 12th round of LaLiga concludes. For Jose Mourinho’s Real Madrid, currently third with 27 points, maintaining their winning streak is essential to keep pace at the top of the table. Conversely, Valencia is desperate to climb out of the relegation zone and stabilize their season during what is being described as a farewell campaign for the historic Mestalla stadium.

Historical data from Diario AS highlights the difficulty Madrid often faces at this venue. While the visitors secured three points in two of their last four visits, they also recorded a defeat and a draw in their most recent trips during the 2022-23 and 2023-24 seasons. Sports Illustrated confirms that referee Francisco Hernández Maeso will officiate the encounter as the teams prepare for this final Saturday fixture.
