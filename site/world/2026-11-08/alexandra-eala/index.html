Alexandra Eala Clinches Hong Kong Final Berth After Toppling Maria Sakkari

By Karim Fouad · Published 08 Nov 2026, 09:01 UTC

Alexandra Eala defeated Maria Sakkari 7-5, 3-6, 6-4 on Saturday to advance to the Hong Kong Open final against Claire Liu.

The match required 2 hours and 43 minutes of intense effort as Alexandra Eala navigated repeated rain delays to overcome Maria Sakkari. The weather conditions forced frequent stoppages throughout the contest, leaving the court towel crew as central figures in keeping the surface playable while the arena speakers played music during the forced interruptions. Eala credited her ability to maintain focus through these disjointed conditions as the key to her performance.

This victory marks a significant milestone for Eala, placing her in her third career WTA Tour final and her second of 2026. For tennis followers, the result confirms Eala’s form following her earlier success against Antonia Ruzic. Securing a spot in the championship match sets the stage for a rematch against Claire Liu, who previously defeated Eala in Guangzhou last year. Success in this final would represent a high point in Eala's season as she continues her progression on the tour.

According to the WTA, the semifinal saw Sakkari mount multiple comebacks, including erasing a break deficit in the first set and leveling the third set at 4-4 after Eala had surged to a 4-1 lead. Eala ultimately proved more resilient in the decider. Her opponent, Claire Liu, earned her spot in the final after eliminating Alexandra Shubladze in straight sets, 7-5, 6-4, in a match lasting 1 hour and 58 minutes. This championship match represents the first final appearance for Liu since Rabat 2022.

The final showdown between Eala and Liu is scheduled to determine the Hong Kong Open champion following the conclusion of their respective semifinal victories.
