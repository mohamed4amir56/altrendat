ESPN College GameDay Returns to South Bend for Massive Notre Dame-Miami Showdown

By Karim Fouad · Published 08 Nov 2026, 09:31 UTC · Updated 08 Nov 2026, 10:01 UTC

ESPN’s College GameDay broadcasts live from the Notre Dame campus this Saturday at 9 a.m. for the matchup between the No. 11 Fighting Irish and the top-ranked Hurricanes.

Quick facts

Broadcast Time
9 a.m. Saturday
Location
Library Lawn, Notre Dame Stadium
Matchup
No. 11 Notre Dame vs. No. 1 Miami
Point Spread
Notre Dame -7.5

The broadcast crew arrives at Library Lawn on the north side of Notre Dame Stadium as the campus prepares to host a high-stakes rematch of one of last season's most consequential games. While Notre Dame looks to avenge a three-point loss that hindered their previous playoff aspirations, the No. 1 ranked Miami Hurricanes face a challenge against the nation's 122nd-ranked pass defense. According to Bleacher Report, the Fighting Irish enter the contest as 7.5-point favorites, aided by an ankle injury to Miami wide receiver Malachi Toney. The game features a significant clash between top-10 rush defenses, with the Hurricanes boasting the best run defense in the country. Analysts Rece Davis, Kirk Herbstreit, Desmond Howard, Pat McAfee, Nick Saban, 'Stanford' Steve Coughlin, Jen Lada, Jess Sims, and Pete Thamel are scheduled to provide analysis and game predictions. The outcome will likely determine Notre Dame's path toward a playoff berth and potentially displace Miami from the top seed in the rankings. Notre Dame quarterback CJ Carr is expected to test a Miami secondary that allows 251.9 yards per game, while Hurricanes quarterback Darian Mensah attempts to maintain the momentum of an offense averaging 540 total yards per game. The broadcast will join a variety of football-related events on campus, including appearances by Pat McAfee and representatives from Barstool Sports. Fans can tune in to the live show starting at 9 a.m. Saturday to catch the crew’s final insights before the two teams face off.
