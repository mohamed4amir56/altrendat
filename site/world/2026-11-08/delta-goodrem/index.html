Delta Goodrem Returns to Dancing After Recovering From Painful Rib Fracture

By Karim Fouad · Published 08 Nov 2026, 10:31 UTC

Delta Goodrem is back on the Strictly Come Dancing stage following a recovery from a fractured rib that forced her to miss competition time.

Delta Goodrem returned to competition in full form this week, performing the cha-cha to Jennifer Lopez’s 1999 hit Let’s Get Loud. This comeback follows a challenging month for the 41-year-old singer, who was sidelined after suffering a fractured rib while rehearsing for the BBC series in October. At the time of her injury, the star shared footage from her hospital stay, expressing her distress through tears as doctors confirmed the diagnosis and she faced the uncertainty of her future in the show.

Her return to the floor marks a significant recovery for the performer, who initially feared she had a long road ahead before she could dance again. During her weeks of healing, Goodrem participated in remote dance lessons via FaceTime while her husband, Matthew Copley, stepped in to practice with her partner, Kai Widdrington. The singer recently celebrated her progress at home with a steak dinner, confirming she is back on track alongside her husband.

The effort has not gone unnoticed, as her professional dance partner, Kai Widdrington, praised her for her persistence, noting that she puts her heart into every performance. Prior to her latest routine, Goodrem maintained strong momentum in the competition, securing a high score of 33 for her Viennese waltz during Halloween week. That performance, set to The Righteous Brothers’ Unchained Melody, earned her second place on the leaderboard and glowing reviews from judges, including an “incredible” and “sensational” rating from Anton Du Beke.
