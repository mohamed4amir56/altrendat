George Russell Fumes Over Costly Team Miscommunication During Chaotic Brazil Wet Qualifying

By Karim Fouad · Published 08 Nov 2026, 07:21 UTC

George Russell qualified second behind Lando Norris in treacherous rain at Interlagos but faces a five-place grid penalty for Sunday's race.

George Russell reached speeds over 200km/h while aquaplaning in fifth gear, describing visibility as zero during a qualifying session that faced a suspension of over an hour due to torrential rain. While he secured the second-fastest time, Russell fumed on the team radio after being advised by his engineer to abort his final representative lap behind Alex Albon, only for weather conditions to worsen immediately afterward. Russell declared he wanted pole position rather than P2, feeling the missed opportunity left him robbed of the top spot.

The resulting grid reflects a weekend of upheaval at the Interlagos track, where unpredictable weather combined with engine-related grid penalties has scrambled the starting order. With front-runners such as Lewis Hamilton and Max Verstappen relegated to the back, several drivers have secured career-best positions for the upcoming race. This volatility makes the upcoming Grand Prix a critical test for teams managing both the shifting weather and the complex grid penalties that have displaced many of the sport's regulars.

According to Sky Sports, Russell finished 0.204 seconds slower than pole-sitter Lando Norris. Because of a pre-existing five-place grid penalty for an engine change, Russell is set to drop from second to seventh on the grid for Sunday's start. Meanwhile, the BBC reports that Lando Norris achieved his third consecutive pole position in Brazil, expressing hopes for continued rain during the race. Teams remain open-minded as the Sunday forecast currently calls for light rain, which historically defines the challenges at this venue.
