Oilers and Canucks clash again following high-scoring 9-7 and 6-5 games

By Karim Fouad · Published 08 Nov 2026, 05:01 UTC · Updated 08 Nov 2026, 06:01 UTC

The Vancouver Canucks face the Edmonton Oilers at Rogers Place tonight following two previous high-scoring meetings this season.

The previous two meetings between these teams produced a combined 23 goals, starting with a 6-5 overtime win for the Canucks and followed by a 9-7 Oilers victory. As the teams meet for the third time this season, the goaltending matchup and roster adjustments remain the central focus for both sides at Rogers Place. This third matchup is critical for both rosters as they manage recent injuries and shifts in their defensive pairings. For fans, these games have proven to be unpredictable offensive displays that heavily influence the flow of the early 2026-27 season. According to NHL.com and The Hockey News, the Oilers see the return of Zach Hyman to the lineup after he missed five games with a neck injury. Edmonton has also made personnel moves by assigning goalie Matt Tomkins to Bakersfield of the American Hockey League and placing defenseman Atro Leppanen on waivers. On the Vancouver side, Arshdeep Bains returns to the top line alongside Elias Pettersson and Brock Boeser. Tom Willander will miss his third consecutive game for the Canucks, while Leevi Meriläinen is slated to start in net, marking his first start of the season against Edmonton. The teams are scheduled to face off at 8:00 pm PCT.
