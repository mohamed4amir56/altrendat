Liverpool Makes Five Changes Seeking Win Away At Struggling Crystal Palace

By Karim Fouad · Published 08 Nov 2026, 13:31 UTC

Liverpool visits Selhurst Park today after making five lineup changes following consecutive defeats.

Quick facts

Venue
Selhurst Park
Palace missing player
Jean-Philippe Mateta

Liverpool manager Andoni Iraola has recalled Ronald Araujo, Milos Kerkez, Cody Gakpo, Alexander Isak, and Florian Wirtz to the starting side for today's match. The Reds are looking to bounce back after failing to register a single shot on target in their previous Premier League outing against Arsenal and suffering a midweek Champions League defeat to Fenerbahçe. The current transition phase under Iraola has been marked by the team's first two consecutive losses of the season. Liverpool holds a strong historical record at Selhurst Park, having won 10 of the previous 11 Premier League meetings at this venue despite recent difficulties against the London side. Crystal Palace enters the fixture in poor form, currently sitting near the relegation zone with only one win in their last seven matches. The Eagles have failed to score in their three most recent Premier League games and must navigate this match without suspended striker Jean-Philippe Mateta. Manager Pierre Sage is relying on Jørgen Strand Larsen to lead the attack after Daichi Kamada’s late equalizer against Hoffenheim provided a brief reprieve during the week. While Palace has won four of the last seven meetings across all competitions, their current lack of offensive production leaves them vulnerable against a desperate Liverpool squad. The Reds view this away fixture as a vital opportunity to reclaim three points and stabilize their season before the upcoming international break, where injured players like Takehiro Tomiyasu and Ismaïla Sarr are expected to remain sidelined.
