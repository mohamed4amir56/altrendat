Kevin Durant returns to Phoenix as Rockets hunt another Suns victory

By Karim Fouad · Published 08 Nov 2026, 05:01 UTC

The Houston Rockets face the Phoenix Suns tonight at 8:00 PM CST, aiming to extend an eight-game winning streak against the franchise.

The Houston Rockets hold a commanding edge in recent history against the Phoenix Suns, winning nine of their last 10 matchups and eight consecutive meetings. This dominance includes sweeping the season series in each of the last two years, marked by double-digit victory margins in their most recent encounters. Last season, the Rockets secured a 119-105 comeback victory on April 7, featuring a 24-point performance from Kevin Durant against his former team.

This matchup carries significant weight for the visitors as they look to recover from a 117-107 home loss to the Timberwolves. A win tonight would allow Houston to match the L.A. Lakers and Philadelphia 76ers for the best record in the NBA through the first 10 games of the season. For the Suns, the game is a critical part of a seven-game homestand, following a 117-104 win against the Orlando Magic that snapped a four-game slide.

According to Yahoo Sports, the Rockets' starting five all reached double-figures in their previous contest, while Alperen Sengun contributed a triple-double consisting of 17 points, 12 rebounds, and 10 assists. The Suns counter with Devin Booker, who has led Phoenix in scoring for three consecutive games in November. The Rockets currently trail the all-time regular-season series 118-115, a rivalry spanning back to the 1968-69 season.

Team injury reports indicate Rasheer Fleming remains questionable with a left ankle sprain, while Jordan Goodwin is listed as doubtful due to right calf injury management. The teams will take the floor at the Mortgage Matchup Center in Phoenix to continue their competitive West division campaign.
