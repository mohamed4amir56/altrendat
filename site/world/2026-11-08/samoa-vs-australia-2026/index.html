Kangaroos Crush Samoa 44-12 To Clinch Rugby League World Cup Final Spot

By Karim Fouad · Published 08 Nov 2026, 11:31 UTC

Australia defeated Samoa in the semi-final in Sydney to set up a Rugby League World Cup final showdown against the Kiwis.

Quick facts

Score
44-12
Venue
Allianz Stadium, Sydney
Final Opponent
New Zealand Kiwis
Final Date
Next Sunday
Final Location
Suncorp Stadium, Brisbane

Fullback Reece Walsh led the charge with three of Australia’s eight tries, dismantling the Samoan defense in a statement performance at Allianz Stadium. The 44-12 result showcased the Kangaroos' dominance as they forced repeated errors on Samoa’s right-edge defense throughout the match. While Samoa arrived at the semi-final with high momentum following a successful tournament run, their senior players struggled to manage the intensity of the occasion, often leaving gaps that Australia exploited with a relentless attacking strategy. The victory cements Australia's continued dominance on the international stage, maintaining their unblemished record in Rugby League World Cup semi-finals. While Australia controlled the pace, the match was not without controversy, as the Kangaroos benefited from several marginal refereeing decisions during critical stretches of the game. For Australia, the win serves as a major momentum builder heading into the tournament decider. They now move forward to face the Kiwis in what promises to be a highly anticipated rematch of their opening-round encounter, which saw Australia narrowly escape with a 24-22 win last month. Fans are now looking toward the upcoming final in Brisbane, where New Zealand will seek revenge for their previous defeat. The championship match is scheduled for next Sunday at Suncorp Stadium, marking the conclusion of the 2026 World Cup campaign. The Kangaroos will carry the confidence of their high-scoring semi-final performance into the final, as they aim to retain their status as the sport's premier international side.
