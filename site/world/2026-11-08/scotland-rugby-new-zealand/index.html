Beauden Barrett masterclass crushes Scotland as New Zealand extends 121-year dominance

By Karim Fouad · Published 08 Nov 2026, 09:31 UTC

New Zealand secured a 43-21 victory over Scotland at Murrayfield after scoring 19 unanswered second-half points.

Beauden Barrett cemented his status as an all-time great by scoring his 50th Test try during a ruthless display that ultimately trampled Scotland's hopes of ending a 121-year winless streak against the All Blacks. While the first half was a thrill-fest that saw Scotland's Ewan Ashman secure a hat-trick of tries, the momentum shifted decisively in the second half as New Zealand systematically dismantled the host nation's defensive efforts. By the 51st minute, Barrett had already registered his second try of the match, effectively taking Scotland out of their misery long before the final whistle. The All Blacks displayed their characteristic composure, maintaining relentless accuracy and power to finish the game with a 19-0 advantage in the second period alone. For Scotland, the defeat proved to be a dispiritingly routine affair rather than one of their recent agonising near-misses. Head coach Gregor Townsend admitted his frustration, noting that the team failed to deliver the performance required to compete at the level of the visitors. The match underscored a significant gap between the sides, with New Zealand securing a convincing 43-21 win in the Nations Championship. The result marks another chapter in the northern hemisphere’s struggle to topple the southern-hemisphere titans, who currently remain a class apart at the top of their respective table. With two rounds of the round-robin still to play, the performance leaves Scotland searching for answers to their familiar second-half frailties while the All Blacks continue their unbeaten streak in this tournament.
