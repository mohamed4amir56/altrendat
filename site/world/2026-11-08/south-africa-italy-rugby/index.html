Springboks Crush Italy 66-7 in Record Nine-Try Nations Championship Blitz

By Karim Fouad · Published 08 Nov 2026, 09:01 UTC

South Africa secured a record away victory over Italy in Turin on Saturday to reach 20 points in the Nations Championship.

The Springboks dismantled Italy with relentless intensity, scoring five tries within the first 25 minutes of the match and leading 45-0 by halftime. This dominant performance in Turin resulted in nine total tries and a final 66-7 scoreline, marking a record away win for the world champions against the Azzurri. Flyhalf Sacha Feinberg-Mngomezulu delivered a flawless kicking performance, securing 26 individual points by landing all 10 of his attempts at goal.

This victory is vital for the Springboks' campaign, moving them to a maximum of 20 points in the Nations Championship standings with a significant points difference of +140. According to the Daily Maverick, this margin could prove decisive in determining the final tournament rankings. The win also serves as a strong response to the team's September defeat in Australia, marking their fourth consecutive bonus-point victory.

Despite the lopsided result, head coach Rassie Erasmus emphasized that the performance was not perfect, noting that the second half became disjointed as the team integrated changes. Erasmus stated that the squad must elevate their play for upcoming matches against France and Ireland. The preparation for the match saw a last-minute adjustment, as lock Salmaan Moerat was withdrawn during the warm-up due to back spasms, leading to Lood de Jager joining the starting lineup.

Looking ahead, the Springboks face a significant challenge in their next fixture. The team is scheduled to play a high-profile match against Six Nations champions France at the Stade de France this coming Friday.
