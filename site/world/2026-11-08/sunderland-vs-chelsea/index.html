Chelsea looks to storm the table as Sunderland remains winless since August

By Karim Fouad · Published 08 Nov 2026, 13:31 UTC

Chelsea faces Sunderland at the Stadium of Light today, aiming to climb within two points of second place.

Quick facts

Venue
Stadium of Light
League
Premier League

Winless in the Premier League since August, Sunderland enters today's match under significant pressure to reverse their form. The basement-side hosts are tasked with stopping an upwardly-mobile Chelsea team that enters the fixture with momentum following a victory against Manchester United last weekend. For manager Regis Le Bris, the challenge is clear: securing a result at the Stadium of Light to alleviate growing pressure on his position. For Chelsea, the stakes are equally high as the club looks to solidify its position in the league standings.

This match marks a pivotal moment for both clubs during Xabi Alonso's first season in charge of the Blues. With a full week of training preparation, Chelsea enters the game with clear tactical objectives and the opportunity to move within two points of second place. The return to the starting XI of Jordan Henderson provides a boost to the midfield, alongside Romeo Lavia and Moises Caicedo, while Mike Penders maintains his role in goal.

Chelsea has made two tactical changes to the squad for this trip. Pep Chavarria steps into the defensive line replacing Josh Acheampong, and Marco Palestra is expected to move to the right flank. The attacking trio features Cole Palmer, Morgan Rogers, and Pedro Neto. Notably, Aaron Anselmino earns a spot on the bench for the first time this season. The Blues' starting line-up is completed by Fofana and Colwill, while Emi Martinez is absent from the match-day squad. Fans await the opening whistle to see if Sunderland can finally break their winless streak or if Chelsea will continue their rise toward the top of the table.
