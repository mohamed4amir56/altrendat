South Africa Weather Service Issues Level 4 Storm Warnings Across Multiple Provinces

By Karim Fouad · Published 08 Nov 2026, 05:31 UTC

The South African Weather Service has issued level 4 warnings for thunderstorms and rainfall in Mpumalanga and KwaZulu-Natal for the weekend.

Disruptive Rainfall Warning
Level 5 for parts of KwaZulu-Natal

Disaster response teams in KwaZulu-Natal are on high alert as the province prepares for heavy rains and potential flooding. The activation of the disaster joint operations cluster by COGTA MEC Thulasizwe Buthelezi underscores the gravity of the situation for residents in affected areas.

Understanding these weather patterns is essential for safety, particularly for those living in regions prone to incidents. The South African Weather Service cautions that these severe conditions include potential hail and damaging winds, which pose significant risks to both property and human lives. Residents in the eThekwini Metro have been specifically urged by spokesperson Senzelwe Mzila to remain indoors whenever possible to avoid unnecessary danger during the storm events.

Beyond KwaZulu-Natal and Mpumalanga, the weather service has extended level 2 warnings to Gauteng, Limpopo, North West, and the Free State. The Eastern Cape is also anticipating disruptive rainfall, specifically along the Wild Coast. Furthermore, the northern and western sections of the country face a different set of challenges, with parts of the Northern Cape, North West, and Limpopo under alerts for extremely high fire-danger conditions. According to the South African Weather Service, these diverse threats are expected to persist throughout the weekend. Residents are advised to monitor official updates and report any weather-related emergencies to local disaster management centres immediately as the systems develop.
