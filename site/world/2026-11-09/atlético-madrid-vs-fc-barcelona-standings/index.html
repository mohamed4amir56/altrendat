Fermin Lopez brace powers Barcelona past Atletico Madrid to defend top spot

By Karim Fouad · Published 09 Nov 2026, 06:32 UTC

Barcelona secured a 2-1 comeback victory over Atletico Madrid on Sunday to maintain their lead in the La Liga table.

Quick facts

Match result
2-1
League round
12th
Match day
Sunday

Fermin Lopez fired two shots into Diego Simeone’s side to seal the win, successfully overcoming an early goal from Giuliano Simeone. The victory ensures Barcelona holds a three-point lead over the Royal Club and a five-point advantage over Atletico Madrid as the league heads into a two-week international break.

This result is a significant marker for Hansi Flick’s side, as it follows previous losses to Paris Saint-Germain and Real Madrid. By winning this high-stakes fixture, Barcelona has proven its ability to compete against heavyweights after doubts emerged regarding the team’s consistency in big matches. The win solidifies their position as favorites to finish at the top of the standings by the end of May.

According to Hansi Flick, the team struggled with a lack of intensity during the first half, admitting they were not effectively pressing their opponent. Flick adjusted his tactics at halftime, introducing Frenkie de Jong at right-back to bolster the defense. Following these changes, the coach noted a marked improvement in the second half, describing the performance as a mature display that yielded the vital three points.

Historically, this result continues a strong run for Barcelona at the Metropolitano, marking their fourth consecutive away win against Atletico Madrid in La Liga. The club is now positioned to potentially match historical records for successive away victories against the Madrid side should they continue this streak in future encounters.
