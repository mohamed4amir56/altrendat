Fermin Lopez brace powers Barcelona to critical 2-1 win over Atletico Madrid

By Karim Fouad · Published 09 Nov 2026, 06:29 UTC

Barcelona secured a 2-1 victory against Atletico Madrid on Sunday to maintain their position at the top of the La Liga table.

Quick facts

Match Result
2-1
Venue
Metropolitano
La Liga Round
12

Fermin Lopez emerged as the decisive force at the Metropolitano, netting two goals to overturn an early deficit and silence those questioning Barcelona's ability to overcome heavyweight opponents. While Giuliano Simeone provided an explosive start for the hosts, the visitors displayed the maturity they had previously lacked in high-stakes matches against Paris Saint-Germain and Real Madrid. The victory secures three points for Hansi Flick’s side, ensuring they enter the two-week international break firmly at the top of the league standings.

This result is a significant statement of intent from the reigning champions. With a three-point lead over Real Madrid and a five-point advantage over Atletico, the win reinforces Barcelona’s status as favorites to claim the title by May. For manager Hansi Flick, the match was a test of adaptability, requiring him to address a clear lack of intensity in the opening half that saw his team struggling to press effectively.

Flick acknowledged that the first half performance was far from perfect, admitting the team was not properly settled on the pitch. Tactically, he shifted the structure at the break, deploying Frenkie de Jong at right-back after consulting with the player. This adjustment allowed Barcelona to control the second half with greater composure. The win marks four consecutive away league victories for Barcelona against Atletico Madrid, a streak that keeps them in reach of historic territory held by Athletic and Real Madrid.

Looking ahead, Barcelona will focus on building upon this momentum after the international break. Flick emphasized that his squad is committed to winning titles and that the current working process is designed to sustain this performance level through the remainder of the season.
