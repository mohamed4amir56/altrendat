Bookmakers install actress Bethany Antonia as 2/1 favorite for Strictly elimination

With the Halloween special of Strictly Come Dancing rapidly approaching, actress Bethany Antonia sits at 2/1 odds to be the next celebrity to leave the ballroom floor. She heads the market for this weekend's potential departure, narrowly edging out fellow contestants Jaime Winstone, Cach Mercer, and Lawrence Robb, who all remain at risk of facing the dreaded dance-off.

For viewers and fans of the long-running BBC series, these odds serve as a stark reminder of the volatile nature of the competition. As the series progresses toward the final, the margin for error for the remaining celebrities continues to shrink. Betting experts at Betway report that the landscape of the competition is in constant flux, with the favorites for the overall Glitterball Trophy shifting significantly over the last several weeks.

According to Wales Online, the betting market currently identifies the race for the title as a potential two-horse contest between Olympian Tabby Stoecker, who stands at 11/8, and actress Lacey Turner at 7/4. While other contenders like Delta Goodrem remain in the mix, the data suggests their path to the final has become increasingly steep. The previous week saw Shaun Wright-Phillips and partner Alexis Warr depart the show, following the earlier exits of Chris Appleton, Melanie Walters, and Sarah Storey.

The pressure on all participants intensifies this weekend as they prepare for the Halloween-themed performances. Each pair must deliver high-scoring routines to impress both the judges and the public, as a poor performance could see even the strongest dancers suddenly find themselves vulnerable to elimination. All eyes are now on the upcoming broadcast to see if the bookmakers' predictions hold true or if another contestant will face the end of their journey on the show.
