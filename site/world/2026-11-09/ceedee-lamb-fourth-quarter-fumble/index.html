CeeDee Lamb powers Dallas past Colts despite late-game fourth quarter fumble

By Karim Fouad · Published 09 Nov 2026, 05:01 UTC

CeeDee Lamb recorded 195 receiving yards and a touchdown in the Dallas Cowboys' 27-23 victory over the Indianapolis Colts.

CeeDee Lamb recorded 10 receptions and 195 receiving yards, yet his focus remained on the errors that nearly cost his team the game. Alongside three second-half penalties, Lamb committed a fourth-quarter fumble that was initially ruled down by contact before being overturned upon review. The All-Pro receiver expressed dissatisfaction with his own performance despite the statistical success, noting he left significant opportunities on the field. The Cowboys, who struggled in close games against the Ravens and Cardinals previously, successfully finished this drive to secure their fourth win of the season.

For the Cowboys, securing a close win provides a much-needed morale boost after recent late-game losses. The outcome shows a shift in the team's ability to execute in critical moments, particularly on their final offensive drive. For followers of the team, the performance highlights the reliance on Lamb and the emergence of other players when individual mistakes occur.

According to the official Dallas Cowboys site, the team played without George Pickens, who was ruled out due to a hamstring injury. This absence forced other receivers to step up, including Ryan Flournoy, who secured a 46-yard reception during the final offensive drive. Lamb noted that the Colts' defensive coverage shifted toward him throughout the game, which opened lanes for his teammates to make plays. Lamb credited the defense for finishing the game effectively after the offense managed to punch the ball into the end zone for a touchdown rather than settling for a field goal.

Lamb remains focused on self-improvement as the team prepares for Week 10. He emphasized that as an offensive leader, he must eliminate penalties and ball security issues to better help the team finish games.
