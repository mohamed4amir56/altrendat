Fermin Lopez Brace Fires Barcelona To Comeback Win At Atletico Madrid

By Karim Fouad · Published 09 Nov 2026, 12:31 UTC

Barcelona secured a vital comeback victory against Atletico Madrid with two goals from Fermin Lopez, ensuring they head into the international break at the top of LaLiga.

Fermin Lopez proved the difference at the Riyadh Air Metropolitano, scoring twice to lead Barcelona’s turnaround against one of the league’s most formidable defenses. The 23-year-old midfielder registered six shots and maintained an 86.8% passing accuracy during his 77 minutes on the pitch, effectively dismantling an Atletico side that had conceded only 10 league goals prior to this encounter.

For Hansi Flick’s side, this win serves as a critical statement of intent. Coming off recent high-profile setbacks against PSG and Real Madrid, the squad faced significant pressure to prove their capability against Europe’s elite. By overcoming the deficit, Barcelona confirmed their physical and tactical recovery, silencing doubts about their championship credentials while extending their lead in the domestic table.

According to Marca, the victory cements Barcelona’s position at the summit of LaLiga, where they currently hold a balance of 11 wins from 12 matches. Atletico Madrid now drops to third place, while Real Madrid trails the leaders by three points in second. Fermin Lopez’s performance was statistically dominant, with Mundo Deportivo noting he not only scored both goals but also regained three balls and created two key chances. This brings his individual tally for the season to eight goals and seven assists across all competitions.

The result allows the Catalan club to enter the international break firmly in control of their title pursuit. Barcelona will look to maintain their momentum upon the resumption of league play, with their offensive unit having already accumulated 48 goals in the current campaign.
