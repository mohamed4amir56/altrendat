DC Studios Ousts James Gunn and Peter Safran, Names Jeremy Latcham President

By Karim Fouad · Published 09 Nov 2026, 17:32 UTC

James Gunn and Peter Safran are stepping down as co-CEOs of DC Studios on December 1st, replaced by Jeremy Latcham.

Quick facts

Departure Date
December 1st
Incoming President
Jeremy Latcham
Tenure Length
4 years

James Gunn described his four-year tenure at the helm of DC Studios as trying, highlighting his dislike for the corporate nature of the executive role. The departure arrives after Gunn and Safran oversaw the formation of a new shared universe that produced titles including Superman, Supergirl, and Clayface. During their leadership, the studio also launched television projects such as Creature Commandos, Peacemaker, and Lanterns. The exit of the pair is described as unexpected, as they were recently included as part of David Ellison’s business leadership team following a major studio merger. Fans who followed the development of the new DCU now look to a significant shift in creative direction as Jeremy Latcham assumes the role of president and chief creative officer. Latcham, a former Marvel executive, takes the reins at a time when the studio has already begun releasing initial projects under the Gods and Monsters slate. For Gunn, the transition marks the end of a period he called a dream come true, citing his childhood experience drawing comics in Manchester, Missouri, as the root of his passion for the characters. Gunn remains attached to the upcoming film Man of Tomorrow, which is scheduled to hit theaters next summer. His final day in the office alongside Safran is confirmed for December 1st.
