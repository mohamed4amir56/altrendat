Jaguars Acquire Pro Bowl Safety Jessie Bates in Massive Trade Deadline Deal

By Karim Fouad · Published 09 Nov 2026, 13:31 UTC

The Jacksonville Jaguars have traded a 2027 second-round pick and a 2028 fifth-round pick to the Atlanta Falcons for safety Jessie Bates.

Jessie Bates arrives in Jacksonville with a career resume featuring 28 interceptions, four pick-sixes, and 871 total tackles across nine NFL seasons. This season alone, the 29-year-old has recorded 60 tackles, three forced fumbles, and an interception, providing the immediate defensive reinforcement the Jaguars need as they look toward a potential Super Bowl run.

The trade addresses critical injury concerns within the Jacksonville secondary. The team recently lost safety Eric Murray to a season-ending elbow injury, while fellow safety Antonio Johnson has also been hampered by health issues. By securing one of the league's most capable defensive backs, the Jaguars aim to maintain their position atop the AFC South after improving to a 6-2 record following a recent win against the Baltimore Ravens.

According to reports from ESPN's Adam Schefter, the Falcons are facilitating the deal by paying a portion of Bates' remaining salary for the final year of his current contract. The cost for Jacksonville is a 2027 second-round pick and a 2028 fifth-round pick. This move represents an aggressive strategy by the franchise, as they push to upgrade their roster before the official NFL trade deadline on Tuesday.

Bates, a three-time second-team All-Pro selection, brings high-end stability to a defense currently focused on finishing the season strong. While the organization continues to evaluate other roster needs, including the defensive tackle market, the acquisition of Bates marks their primary move to solidify the back end of their defense for the remainder of the campaign.
