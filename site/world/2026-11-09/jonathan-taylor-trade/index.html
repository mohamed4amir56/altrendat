Jonathan Taylor Bids Emotional Farewell To Colts As November Trade Deadline Looms

By Karim Fouad · Published 09 Nov 2026, 10:01 UTC

Pro Bowl running back Jonathan Taylor addressed his potential departure from Indianapolis following the team's fifth consecutive loss.

Quick facts

Colts record
2-7
Trade deadline
November 10
Recent result
27-23 loss to Dallas

After a 27-23 defeat to Dallas dropped the Indianapolis Colts to a 2-7 record, Jonathan Taylor delivered remarks that appeared to serve as a public goodbye to the city. The three-time Pro Bowler, who has emerged as the primary weapon on a struggling offense, expressed deep gratitude for his time in Indianapolis. Taylor noted that while he plays every game as if it is his last, this specific environment helped shape him into the man he is today. He emphasized that it was a pleasure to display his hard work for the city and the team's supporters.

The timing of his comments coincides with the upcoming November 10 trade deadline. With the Colts in a rebuilding phase marked by the firing of general manager Chris Ballard and the departure of key leaders like Quenton Nelson and DeForest Buckner, the franchise is positioned for a fire-sale approach to acquire draft capital. Taylor, who previously secured an NFL rushing crown with 1,800 yards and 18 touchdowns in 2021, remains a focal point for teams seeking a premier running back for a potential postseason push.

According to Fox News, the New York Giants previously proposed a trade package involving a second- and fifth-round pick with additional compensation, but withdrew interest when the Colts demanded a first-round selection. While the high asking price remains a hurdle, other potential landing spots such as Denver and Cincinnati are reportedly monitoring the situation. NFL analysis highlights that clubs like Green Bay and Minnesota also struggle with rushing inefficiency, creating clear tactical fits for a player of Taylor's caliber who has forced 35 missed tackles this season.
