Chiefs Rout Reeling Jets 35-12 To Deepen Season Tailspin For New York

By Karim Fouad · Published 09 Nov 2026, 05:31 UTC

The Kansas City Chiefs secured a dominant 35-12 victory over the New York Jets, handing the Jets their fourth consecutive loss.

Quick facts

Final Score
35-12
Jets Season Record
2-7
Venue
Arrowhead Stadium

The New York Jets fell to a 2-7 record after suffering a 35-12 blowout loss to the Kansas City Chiefs at Arrowhead Stadium. The defeat marks the fourth straight loss for the team, as head coach Aaron Glenn admitted the group has a lot of thinking to do while struggling with recurring issues like defensive breakdowns, red-zone failures, and third-down penalties. The game highlighted the mounting frustration within the locker room, with players Geno Smith and Armand Membou seen throwing helmets and damaging equipment on the sidelines following key fourth-down failures. While the Jets held a 14-12 deficit at halftime after dominating early possessions, they ultimately settled for field goals instead of touchdowns, a trend that has defined their season struggles. The team currently holds the longest streak in American major sports without a playoff appearance, and the owner has recorded an 8-30 record since firing Robert Saleh five games into the 2024 season. With the current trajectory, the team faces an increasingly bleak outlook as they continue a season that has spiraled since their early 2-6 start. The Jets are set to face the Buffalo Bills next Sunday at MetLife Stadium, continuing a difficult stretch of the schedule against opponents currently playing at a higher level of performance than the New York squad.
