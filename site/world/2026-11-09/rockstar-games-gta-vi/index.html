Grand Theft Auto VI Pre-Downloads Start November 10 Ahead of Launch

By Karim Fouad · Published 09 Nov 2026, 17:01 UTC

Rockstar Games confirmed that Grand Theft Auto VI will launch on November 19, with pre-downloads beginning on November 10 for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S.

Players must clear at least 212GB of storage space on their PlayStation 5 console to accommodate the upcoming release. This massive storage requirement reflects the scale of the title as users prepare to dive into the latest installment of the series.

Ensuring the game is downloaded before the official release date allows players to begin their experience immediately when it becomes available. By pre-ordering through the PlayStation Store or participating retailers, users secure their access and can take advantage of the pre-download window starting at local midnight on November 10. The game features a dual-protagonist story involving Jason and Lucia, with additional content included in the Ultimate Edition, such as exclusive vehicles, weapons, and apparel.

According to Rockstar Games, those new to the PlayStation platform should verify their account information in advance to prevent any delays during the launch window. Existing players are encouraged to check their sign-in credentials now. Preparation steps also include exploring storage expansion options if the 212GB requirement exceeds current console capacity. For those looking to upgrade their setup, accessories such as the DualSense Edge wireless controller and Pulse wireless audio products are available for use with the new game.

Rockstar Games maintains that the full launch for both PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S remains set for November 19. Additional details regarding the game and its features are expected to be shared by the developer as the release date approaches.
