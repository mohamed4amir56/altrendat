Georgia Bulldogs Surge to No. 1 After 42-7 Rout of Ole Miss

By Karim Fouad · Published 09 Nov 2026, 08:31 UTC

Georgia takes the top spot in the AP Poll following a dominant win and Miami's loss to Notre Dame.

Georgia officially claims the number one ranking in the Associated Press Top 25 poll this week after a commanding performance on the field. The Bulldogs dismantled Ole Miss 42-7 on the road, showcasing a clear disparity in physical dominance. While both teams threw for 186 yards, Georgia overwhelmed the Rebels on the ground by amassing 295 rushing yards compared to just 66 yards from their opponent. This statement victory provided enough momentum to vault the Bulldogs into the top position following Miami's recent loss to Notre Dame.

This shift in the rankings serves as a critical indicator for the College Football Playoff race, which saw several traditional powerhouses falter during Week 10. For teams like Ohio State, LSU, Ole Miss, and Missouri, the weekend results proved particularly damaging, as each now holds three losses. This tally complicates their postseason aspirations, creating a narrow and potentially impossible path to secure a spot in the playoff bracket.

The volatility of the current season is underscored by the struggles of teams previously considered contenders. Kentucky, for instance, tumbled from sixth to 13th in the rankings after suffering a 34-6 defeat to Tennessee. Meanwhile, LSU faces mounting pressure as their season reaches a critical point, with significant upcoming tests against Texas and Tennessee. Similarly, the loss to Oregon places Ohio State in a difficult position, adding pressure to coach Ryan Day as the program faces a rare scenario where a 10-win season may no longer be a guarantee.

As the regular season draws toward a close, the focus now shifts to the remaining schedule. Programs like LSU must navigate difficult matchups to salvage their standing, while other teams look to capitalize on the chaos caused by the recent upsets.
