Sunny skies and breezy conditions forecast for Kauai this coming Monday

Kauai residents can expect mostly sunny weather on Monday, November 9, 2026, with coastal highs reaching up to 90 degrees.

Clear skies will define the start of the week as Kauai moves into a mostly sunny Monday, November 9, 2026. While shorelines and lower elevations prepare for warm highs between 83 and 90 degrees, those in higher terrains can anticipate milder temperatures peaking around 75 degrees above 3000 feet. Winds will hold steady from the east at 10 to 15 mph throughout the day. Residents across various regions of the island should plan for some light morning activity, with specific reports indicating isolated morning showers in certain areas before the sun takes over for the remainder of the afternoon. As night falls on Monday, the weather transitions toward mostly cloudy conditions in the evening, followed by a shift to partly cloudy skies. Coastal lows are expected to settle near 71 degrees, with temperatures dropping to roughly 63 degrees at elevations above 3000 feet. Wind patterns will remain consistent, maintaining an easterly flow of 10 to 15 mph. For those traveling through more exposed valleys or mountainous sectors, localized conditions may vary slightly. According to data from Kauai Now, the overall chance of rain remains low at 20 percent throughout the day and into the evening. This outlook provides a stable weather pattern for the beginning of the week following the recent regional trends. Residents should keep an eye on shifting wind patterns that could influence temperature fluctuations near shorelines versus the higher inland elevations.
