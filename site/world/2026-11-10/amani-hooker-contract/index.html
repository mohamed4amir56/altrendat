Bills Shore Up Secondary By Acquiring League-Leading Interceptor Amani Hooker From Titans

By Karim Fouad · Published 10 Nov 2026, 20:01 UTC

The Buffalo Bills acquired veteran safety Amani Hooker from the Tennessee Titans at the NFL trade deadline in exchange for a second-round draft pick.

Amani Hooker enters the Bills' defensive lineup as the NFL's current leader in interceptions for the 2026 season, having already recorded five picks this year. A former 2019 fourth-round draft selection, the 28-year-old safety brings significant durability to Buffalo, boasting 103 career appearances and 74 starts, along with over 5,200 snaps of professional experience. Throughout his career, he has accumulated 17 interceptions, 45 passes defensed, and five forced fumbles.

For the Buffalo Bills, this move addresses a glaring vacancy in the secondary as the team looks toward the remainder of the season and beyond. General manager Brandon Beane executed the trade at the deadline to stabilize a defensive unit that was actively scanning the safety pool for reinforcements. Because Hooker remains under contract through the 2028 season, the acquisition provides Buffalo with long-term security, offering a contrast to other recent league trades involving shorter-term contracts.

According to reports from NBC Sports, the transaction involves an exchange of draft capital where the Bills send a second-round selection to Tennessee in return for Hooker and a fifth-round pick. Buffalo Rumblings noted that Hooker’s base salary for the remainder of the current season sits at approximately $2 million, allowing the move to fit within the team's salary cap constraints. While Hooker will not turn 30 until June 2028, his experience as a starter since 2021 makes him a central piece for the current Bills defense.
