Singaporeans Secure Final Assurance Package Cash Payouts of Up To $250

By Karim Fouad · Published 10 Nov 2026, 05:01 UTC · Updated 10 Nov 2026, 07:01 UTC

Approximately 3 million Singaporean adults will receive final Assurance Package cash payouts ranging from $100 to $250 starting December 8.

Over a five-year span ending in 2026, eligible Singaporeans will have collected a total of up to $2,250 in cash benefits through the government’s comprehensive support scheme. This December’s distribution marks the final installment of the enhanced Assurance Package, an initiative originally introduced to help households navigate rising living costs and the impact of the goods and services tax rate increases.

The Ministry of Finance confirmed that this disbursement serves as the final round of support under the program, which was bolstered by a $3 billion top-up during the 2023 budget. With total funding reaching $9.6 billion, the package has provided sustained assistance to lower- and middle-income families across the nation since 2022.

Individual payouts are determined by assessable income and property ownership status. Citizens owning no more than one property with an annual income of $39,000 or below qualify for the maximum $250 payment. Those with incomes between $39,001 and $100,000 will receive $150, while individuals earning above $100,000 or owning multiple properties are set to receive $100. Eligibility applies to all Singaporeans aged 21 and older as of 2027.

Recipients can verify their status via the official govbenefits.gov.sg portal using Singpass credentials. Notifications will be dispatched via SMS from the official gov.sg Sender ID, while those without registered mobile numbers will receive physical letters. To ensure an earlier arrival on December 8, eligible recipients must link their NRIC to PayNow by November 24. Those opting for GIRO payments must submit their banking details by November 30 to receive funds by December 17.
