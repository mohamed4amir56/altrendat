Knicks enter Kith Night as massive 15.5-point favorites against struggling Bucks

By Karim Fouad · Published 11 Nov 2026, 00:01 UTC

The surging New York Knicks host the 1-9 Milwaukee Bucks at 7:30 p.m. ET on Tuesday at Madison Square Garden.

The Milwaukee Bucks arrive at Madison Square Garden carrying a six-game losing streak and a 1-9 record, the worst in the NBA. They face a Knicks squad that has rattled off three consecutive double-digit wins. The matchup is set for Tuesday night as part of the annual Kith Night event at the arena.

The game carries significant implications for both franchises as they trend in opposite directions. For the Knicks, a win would continue their momentum following a 109-97 victory over Cleveland. For Milwaukee, the game is a desperate attempt to snap a skid that has left them tied for the league's worst record.

Milwaukee faces uncertainty as leading scorer Tyler Herro, averaging 21.4 points per game, is questionable with a right contusion. Reserve center Kel'el Ware is also questionable with a left ankle sprain, a status that could force the Bucks to lean heavily on starting center Myles Turner. Meanwhile, the Knicks are managing their own health concerns as Josh Hart is listed as doubtful due to an illness. The Knicks have historically dominated this matchup, winning seven of the last eight meetings against the Bucks.

Following Tuesday's contest, the Knicks remain at home to host the Cleveland Cavaliers on Wednesday to complete their back-to-back set. The SportsLine Projection Model, which has simulated the game 10,000 times, suggests the total score will go over 222.5 points.
