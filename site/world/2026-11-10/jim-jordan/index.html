Jim Jordan Clinches House GOP Leader Spot Defeating Scalise By 33 Votes

By Karim Fouad · Published 10 Nov 2026, 20:01 UTC

Jim Jordan secured the position of House GOP minority leader after defeating Steve Scalise in a closed-door secret ballot vote on Tuesday.

Loud, sustained applause erupted in the hallway outside the meeting room as the official results confirmed Jordan's victory. The Ohio Republican claimed the leadership role by securing 106 votes against the 73 votes cast for Majority Leader Steve Scalise.

This leadership shift follows a period of major losses for Republicans in both state and federal races. With the party hungry for change, the election marks a new chapter for the House GOP as they transition into their minority status in Congress. Jordan is expected to serve as a primary defender for President Donald Trump.

According to Fox News Digital, the election took place on Tuesday during a closed-door session involving incumbent House Republicans and members-elect. The process featured a morning candidate forum where lawmakers questioned the contenders on topics including conference unity and internal leaks. Both candidates during this session emphasized their commitment to allowing individual representatives to vote according to the needs of their specific districts.

In a concurrent leadership decision, House Majority Whip Tom Emmer won the position of minority whip, defeating August Pfluger with a final tally of 103 to 76 votes.
