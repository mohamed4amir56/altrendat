Chiefs acquire Josh Downs from Colts in late deadline day trade

By Karim Fouad · Published 10 Nov 2026, 21:01 UTC

The Kansas City Chiefs secured wide receiver Josh Downs and a 2027 seventh-round pick from the Indianapolis Colts in exchange for two future draft selections.

The Kansas City Chiefs have finalized a deal to add wide receiver Josh Downs to their roster before the NFL trade deadline officially passes. This transaction brings a new offensive target to Kansas City as the team navigates the final window for roster adjustments before the league-wide trade restriction begins at 3:00 p.m. Arrowhead time.

For the Chiefs, the arrival of Downs addresses a need for additional pass-catching depth as the season progresses. The move comes as part of a flurry of activity across the league, with the Indianapolis Colts restructuring their roster by parting ways with multiple veteran players, including defensive tackle DeForest Buckner and guard Quenton Nelson.

According to reports from NBC Sports, the terms of the deal involve a draft pick exchange scheduled for 2027. The Chiefs sent a 2027 fourth-round pick and a 2027 fifth-round pick to Indianapolis to complete the trade. In return, the Colts surrendered both Downs and a 2027 seventh-round pick to Kansas City.

The trade deadline marks the final opportunity for front offices to negotiate player acquisitions via trade. Following the 3:00 p.m. cutoff, teams are limited to free-agent signings for the remainder of the season. With the deal finalized, Downs joins a Chiefs roster that has been the subject of persistent trade rumors involving several high-profile targets throughout the lead-up to the deadline.
