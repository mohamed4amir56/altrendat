49ers bolster receiving corps by acquiring Keenan Allen from the Colts

By Karim Fouad · Published 10 Nov 2026, 19:01 UTC

The San Francisco 49ers acquired wide receiver Keenan Allen from the Indianapolis Colts in exchange for a 2027 sixth-round draft pick.

Quick facts

Acquiring team
49ers
Trading team
Colts
Return for trade
2027 sixth-round pick
Reported by
Tom Pelissero

The trade marks a significant shift for the San Francisco roster as they add veteran depth to their wide receiver room. By sending only a late-round draft selection to Indianapolis, the 49ers secure the experienced pass-catcher without sacrificing premium capital. The deal comes as part of a series of roster adjustments for San Francisco as they push to improve their offensive production for the remainder of the season. For the Colts, the move clears a veteran contract off their books while securing additional draft assets for the 2027 cycle. The move was reported by Tom Pelissero of The Ringer, confirming the 49ers will bring Allen into their offensive rotation immediately. While the Colts remain committed to their core roster, including running back Jonathan Taylor, the departure of Allen highlights the team's willingness to rebalance their assets through trade. Fans and fantasy managers alike are now watching to see how quickly Allen can integrate into the 49ers' scheme during upcoming games. The receiver is expected to begin participating in team activities and practice sessions as he prepares to make his debut for San Francisco in the coming week.
