GTA 6 Clings to 30 FPS on PS5 Pro as Upgrades Revealed

By Karim Fouad · Published 10 Nov 2026, 16:01 UTC

Rockstar Games confirmed GTA 6 will maintain 30 frames per second on all consoles when it launches on November 19.

Rockstar Games has confirmed that Grand Theft Auto VI will run at 30 frames per second on the PlayStation 5 Pro, silencing rumors that the enhanced console would push the frame rate higher to 40 or 60 frames per second. Instead of higher frame rates, the additional power of the PS5 Pro will be dedicated to enhanced visual fidelity, including PlayStation Spectral Super Resolution (PSSR) upscaling for improved clarity and exclusive ray-traced shadows.

For players, this news clarifies the performance expectations for the game's launch. By focusing the console's resources on image quality and environmental density rather than performance speed, Rockstar aims to maintain the visual fidelity of the world of Leonida across the current generation of hardware. The developer noted that the game is designed to push all consoles to their absolute limits to bring the expansive setting to life.

According to Digital Foundry, the PS5 family remains the lead platform for the release, featuring high-quality haptic feedback via the DualSense controller and improved spatial audio rendering. Meanwhile, The Verge reports that Microsoft has secured rights to stream the game via Xbox Cloud Gaming, exclusively for Xbox One and Xbox Series consoles. This move allows those on older Xbox One hardware to experience the game through cloud streaming, which will utilize Xbox Series S graphics settings.

Game pre-loads are currently underway for those ready to play as soon as the title officially launches. While cloud streaming is currently limited to Xbox consoles, Rockstar has confirmed that the game will also support Remote Play on both PlayStation and Xbox devices.
