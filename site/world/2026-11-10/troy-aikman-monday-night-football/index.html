Troy Aikman jokes about needing loan during ESPN contract standoff with network

By Karim Fouad · Published 10 Nov 2026, 12:33 UTC

Analyst Troy Aikman quipped about his finances on air while calling the Bills' 23-6 victory over the Vikings amid stalled contract extension talks.

During Monday's broadcast of the Buffalo Bills' dominant 23-6 win over the Minnesota Vikings, lead analyst Troy Aikman used an observation of Justin Jefferson’s diamond chains to highlight his own precarious contract status. Observing the jewelry, Aikman remarked, "I'm guessing that's insured. I cannot imagine wearing that on a football field. Can't imagine wearing it, period," before adding, "I might buy something similar if somebody would loan me some money." The comment caught the attention of viewers and former quarterback Kurt Benkert, who described the remark as a dig at his booth partner, Joe Buck, who recently secured a six-year, $108 million extension with ESPN.

This tension arrives as ESPN has paused negotiations with Aikman, whose camp reportedly sought a market-resetting extension near $30 million per year. While the duo has worked together since 2002, the current stalemate regarding Aikman’s future has created friction, according to reports. Aikman’s current contract is set to expire in March, leaving the long-running broadcast partnership with an uncertain future beyond the current season.

On the field, the broadcast focused on Minnesota's struggling offense, which Aikman described as difficult to fix. The Vikings benched quarterback Carson Wentz late in the game after he produced only 81 passing yards and three turnovers. The win moved Buffalo to 7-1 on the season as they prepare for a matchup against the New York Jets, while the 6-2 Vikings look to address their quarterback situation ahead of a Week 10 game against the Green Bay Packers.
