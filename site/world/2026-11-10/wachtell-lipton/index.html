Nvidia Stock Plunges 10% Over CEO Jensen Huang Chinese Intelligence Affair Probe

By Karim Fouad · Published 10 Nov 2026, 20:01 UTC

Nvidia’s board hired law firm Wachtell Lipton to investigate CEO Jensen Huang following his admission of an affair with a suspected Chinese intelligence operative.

Quick facts

Law firm
Wachtell, Lipton, Rosen & Katz
Lead investigator
Joon H. Kim
Stock drop
10% cumulative

Nvidia’s market value is reeling as shares dropped 6% on Tuesday, compounding a 4% decline from Monday, after the board of directors launched an urgent probe into CEO Jensen Huang. The investigation follows Huang’s Sunday admission in an email to staff regarding his affair with Alice Huang, a woman identified in reports by The New York Times and the Financial Times as having ties to Chinese intelligence. The company’s board has retained former acting U.S. Attorney for the Southern District of New York Joon H. Kim and the law firm Wachtell, Lipton, Rosen & Katz to determine if the executive's actions compromised sensitive company secrets or broader national security interests.

This crisis presents significant risks for Nvidia, an essential supplier that powers nearly all major artificial intelligence models globally. Investors are increasingly wary that the scandal could force the departure of Huang, who has served as the central public face of the AI industry. Beyond internal leadership instability, the firm faces mounting pressure from politicians and critics regarding the sale of restricted AI chips to Chinese companies.

Legal experts, including former deputy assistant attorney general John Yoo, have raised alarms regarding the national security implications of the relationship. Concerns center on whether the affair facilitated unauthorized technology transfers to Beijing, potentially bypassing existing semiconductor export controls. While Huang stated in his email to staff that he remains committed to his role at the company and emphasized his support for U.S. national security, the firm’s board is conducting what they described as a thorough and independent investigation. The outcome remains uncertain as the company navigates this period of public scrutiny and corporate turmoil.
