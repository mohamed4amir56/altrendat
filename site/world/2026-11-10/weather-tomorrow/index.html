Hamilton rain washout forces Black Caps into desperate fight for series draw

By Karim Fouad · Published 10 Nov 2026, 08:02 UTC

Heavy rain at Seddon Park abandoned the third ODI between New Zealand and India, leaving the hosts trailing 2-0 in the series.

Quick facts

Venue
Seddon Park, Hamilton
Series Status
India leads 2-0
Historical Context
New Zealand has won 11 consecutive home ODI series since 2019

Not a single ball was bowled in Hamilton as persistent weather forced officials to abandon the third ODI of the series. The washout effectively ended New Zealand’s hopes of an ODI series victory against India, leaving the Black Caps unable to win the remaining two matches after already falling 2-0 behind following losses in Auckland and Wellington. The team now faces the prospect of their first home ODI series defeat since 2019.

This result is significant for the Black Caps, who have successfully defended their home turf by winning 11 consecutive ODI series over the last five years. For local fans who turned out for the sellout event, the abandonment denied any chance of a 40-overs-per-side contest. The loss of points and momentum comes at a difficult time as the squad prepares for upcoming test matches.

The challenge for New Zealand deepens as they enter games four and five without several key starters. Players including Tom Latham, Daryl Mitchell, Glenn Phillips, Henry Nicholls, and Nathan Smith are set to depart the ODI group to begin preparations for the test series in Wellington. Additionally, the side faces setbacks with players like Lockie Ferguson withdrawn due to injury, requiring a roster shuffle that brings in reinforcements like Finn Allen and Nick Kelly.

With only two games remaining, New Zealand must secure wins at Bay Oval to avoid a series loss. Both upcoming fixtures will test the depth of the squad as they look to salvage a 2-2 draw.
