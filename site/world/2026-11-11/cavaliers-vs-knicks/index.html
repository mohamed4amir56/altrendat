Knicks host Cavaliers as Cleveland seeks revenge for recent 109-97 home defeat

By Karim Fouad · Published 11 Nov 2026, 23:31 UTC

The Cleveland Cavaliers face the New York Knicks at Madison Square Garden on Wednesday night in an Eastern Conference rematch.

Quick facts

Venue
Madison Square Garden
Day
Wednesday
Last head-to-head score
109-97

The New York Knicks enter this matchup with high momentum following a dominant 117-85 victory over the Milwaukee Bucks on Tuesday. Conversely, the Cleveland Cavaliers arrive in New York aiming to recover from a 124-112 defeat against the Charlotte Hornets on Monday. This game marks the second meeting between the two teams in just five days, as Cleveland looks to settle the score after losing 109-97 on their own court this past Saturday.

For the Cavaliers, the game represents an urgent opportunity to find consistency early in the season. With stars Donovan Mitchell and Evan Mobley leading the roster, Cleveland faces the challenge of overcoming a New York squad that has demonstrated significant offensive firepower. For the Knicks, this game serves as the second half of a back-to-back set, testing their endurance following their Tuesday night win.

According to Bleacher Report, the Knicks receive a significant boost with the return of Josh Hart to the rotation after he missed time due to an ankle injury and illness. Furthermore, Karl-Anthony Towns has been cleared to play, allowing New York to deploy its primary starting five, which also includes Jalen Brunson, Mikal Bridges, and OG Anunoby. The Yahoo Sports report highlights that fans can access the game via live streaming services including Fubo.

This matchup functions as an Eastern Conference showdown at Madison Square Garden. As both teams compete for standing within the conference, the rapid turnaround from Saturday’s result adds competitive pressure to this mid-week fixture.
