Grand Theft Auto VI Smashes Microsoft Store Records Ahead of November Launch

By Karim Fouad · Published 11 Nov 2026, 07:31 UTC

Grand Theft Auto VI launches on consoles and via XBOX Cloud Gaming on November 19, following record-breaking pre-order numbers.

Quick facts

Launch Date
November 19
Pre-load Start Date
November 10
Cloud Streaming Start Time
1am ET, November 19
Platform
PlayStation 5, XBOX Series X|S, XBOX One

Grand Theft Auto VI has secured its place as the most pre-ordered title of all time on the Microsoft Store. This unprecedented demand arrives just before the game’s official release, with Rockstar Games confirming that pre-loading for the highly anticipated title is already underway as of November 10. Players are encouraged to initiate their downloads early to ensure they are fully prepared for the launch day experience.

For many, this news means the game is more accessible than previously anticipated. The expansion into XBOX Cloud Gaming ensures that owners of older hardware, such as the XBOX One, can still participate in the launch on day one. By leveraging cloud technology, the experience will utilize XBOX Series S graphics settings, allowing a broader range of players to access the title on consoles they currently own without needing to upgrade to the latest generation of hardware.

According to official reports from XBOX Wire, the cloud streaming service for the game will officially go live at 1am ET on November 19. Furthermore, starting November 11, users in supported markets will have the option to purchase individual cloud playtime for specific titles without the requirement of a monthly Game Pass subscription. This change aims to accommodate the massive interest surrounding the latest release from Rockstar Games.

While the focus remains on the November 19 launch, XBOX has confirmed plans to expand its Rockstar Games library on the cloud platform in the coming months. Players can expect titles like Grand Theft Auto V and Red Dead Redemption 2 to be added to the streaming service as part of a broader push to increase accessibility across the XBOX ecosystem.
