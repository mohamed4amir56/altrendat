Ultra Lotto jackpot hits P512 million as no ticket matches winning numbers

By Karim Fouad · Published 11 Nov 2026, 12:01 UTC

The Philippine Charity Sweepstakes Office announced that no bettors matched the winning combination of 56-41-37-23-18-14 for the November 10, 2026 draw.

A staggering P512,211,894.61 remains unclaimed after the latest Ultra Lotto 6/58 draw failed to produce a single winner. This massive jackpot has continued to grow, marking a significant period without a top-prize winner since May 5, 2026, when a group of 10 bettors split a P76 million prize. For those regularly participating, these results represent a major opportunity that carries over to the next scheduled drawing cycle. Every draw offers a fresh chance to secure the ballooning pot, which reached its current height after months of unsuccessful attempts by hopeful players. According to the Philippine Charity Sweepstakes Office, the Tuesday, November 10, 2026 draw also left other major jackpots untouched. Superlotto 6/49 bettors missed out on the P97,402,821.03 prize after failing to pick the sequence 44-27-28-16-34-33. Similarly, the Lotto 6/42 jackpot of P12,287,796.09 remains up for grabs following the appearance of the winning combination 42-26-21-25-27-05. These failures follow a year of mixed fortunes for players, including a March 17 win where a long-time bettor claimed a P100.331 million prize after two and a half decades of play. The agency continues to host nightly draws, providing ongoing opportunities for players to match the combinations for the various lottery tiers offered across the country. Players are encouraged to verify their tickets against official results to determine if they hold any secondary winning combinations from the evening sessions.
