Severe storms trap vehicles and trigger widespread road closures across New Zealand

By Karim Fouad · Published 11 Nov 2026, 05:01 UTC

MetService warns of heavy rain and hazardous flooding as emergency crews respond to stranded vehicles near Tokoroa.

Affected Locations
South Waikato, Rotorua, Auckland, Coromandel, Bay of Plenty, Taupō

Up to 130mm of rain is forecast for parts of the South Island, as intense downpours and thunderstorms continue to threaten the region. In the South Waikato area, emergency services were called to assist multiple vehicles trapped in rising floodwaters near Tokoroa starting at 2.25pm. These weather conditions have led to significant road closures, including segments of Old Taupō Rd and Matanuku Rd, according to the NZ Herald. Fire and Emergency New Zealand crews from Putāruru and Tokoroa were deployed to support police operations in the affected areas. Drivers and residents are urged to avoid the vicinity entirely to stay clear of hazardous conditions. MetService warns that the ongoing weather pattern, which includes large hail and torrential rain, could result in further flash flooding. The National Emergency Management Agency specifically advised the public to avoid streams and drainage systems, which present a high risk for rapid water accumulation. The potential for damage to crops, vehicles, and structures remains high throughout the affected regions. A severe thunderstorm warning was in effect until 5.37pm for South Waikato and Rotorua. A broader severe thunderstorm watch remains active for several regions, including Auckland, the Coromandel Peninsula, Waikato, Bay of Plenty, and Taupō. Authorities advise staying alert for further updates as the storms continue to move across the country.
