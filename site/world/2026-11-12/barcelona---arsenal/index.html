Barcelona targets Champions League dominance as Arsenal seeks another historic European shock

By Karim Fouad · Published 12 Nov 2026, 17:31 UTC

Reigning champions Barcelona host Arsenal at the Nou Camp this Thursday with a 100% record in the Women's Champions League group phase.

Barcelona enters Thursday’s clash at the Nou Camp having scored 19 goals in three European matches, maintaining a perfect record this season despite significant roster turnover. While the departure of stars like Alexia Putellas, Mapi Leon, Ona Batlle, and Salma Paralluelo marked the end of a core era, the Spanish giants have lost only once in their last 53 matches across all competitions. Esmee Brugts noted that while the team looks different, the club views the current campaign as an opportunity to craft a new story for the reigning titleholders.

For Arsenal, the fixture represents a formidable test as they attempt to replicate their success from the 2025 Women's Champions League final. Manager Renee Slegers, who oversaw that triumph, currently leads a side sitting sixth in the Women's Super League after a challenging domestic stretch earlier this autumn. The Gunners have recorded seven points from their first three European matches and will need to contain an offense currently averaging over six goals per game in the competition.

According to The Guardian, the challenge for the visitors is immense given Barcelona's historical European consistency, having reached nearly every final since 2018. However, Arsenal holds a competitive historical edge against the hosts, winning three of their five previous Champions League encounters. The match highlights the contrast between the European group leaders and an Arsenal side looking to regain the momentum that defined their 2025 title run.

Thursday’s match serves as a pivotal moment for both clubs as the group stage intensifies. While Barcelona aims to extend their dominant run, Arsenal must secure points to improve their standing as the tournament progresses toward the knockout stages.
