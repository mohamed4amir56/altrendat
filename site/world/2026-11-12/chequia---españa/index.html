World champion Spain visits winless Czech Republic in Nations League clash

By Karim Fouad · Published 12 Nov 2026, 19:31 UTC

Spain travels to the epet ARENA in Prague this Thursday, November 12, to face the Czech Republic in the Nations League Group A3.

Quick facts

Date
November 12
Venue
epet ARENA, Prague
Tournament
Nations League Group A3
Referee
Nikola Dabanović
Kickoff Time (Spain)
20:45

The Czech Republic enters this match having failed to secure a single point across their four previous group stage appearances. Facing a potential relegation to League B, they must contend with a Spanish squad that has maintained a perfect record throughout the tournament thus far. Spain has already secured their spot in the quarterfinals and now looks to seal the group lead before their final match against England. The previous meeting between these two sides in October resulted in a 3-1 victory for the visitors in Oviedo.

For Spanish fans, this match is a critical step in maintaining their momentum. Managing the roster is a key focus for coach Luis de la Fuente, who will be without Lamine Yamal for this fixture following his recent injury. The midfield lineup for this game features Zubimendi, Fabián, and Fermín. Meanwhile, the Czech team, managed by Santi Denia, aims to produce a competitive performance in front of their home crowd to close out their campaign on a positive note.

According to Sports Illustrated, the match kicks off at 14:45 Eastern Time, 13:45 in Mexico, and 20:45 in Spain. Coverage is available through various channels, including TVE La 1 and RTVE Play in Spain, while viewers in the United States can watch via Fox Soccer Plus, FOX One, fuboTV, and ViX. Mexican audiences can access the broadcast through Sky Sports Mexico, Sky+, or izzi. The match will be officiated by referee Nikola Dabanović from Montenegro.
