Barcelona Duo Pau Cubarsi and Fermin Lopez Start for Spain in Prague

By Karim Fouad · Published 12 Nov 2026, 19:31 UTC

Manager Luis de la Fuente has confirmed his starting lineup for Spain’s UEFA Nations League match against Czechia.

Quick facts

Venue
Prague
Competition
UEFA Nations League
Manager
Luis de la Fuente

Pau Cubarsi and Fermin Lopez anchor the Spanish lineup as the World Champions take the pitch in Prague. The pair, both products of the La Masia academy, join the starting XI to challenge Czechia in this UEFA Nations League fixture. Manager Luis de la Fuente confirmed the selection as his team adjusts its formation to account for key absences across the squad. The match serves as a significant test for the national side while they manage a roster affected by ongoing injuries. For fans and analysts, this selection signals a shift in the midfield and defensive hierarchy. Fermin Lopez steps into the No. 10 role, tasked with orchestrating the attack behind former Barcelona striker Ferran Torres. This opportunity arises following the absence of Dani Olmo due to an injury. On the defensive front, Pau Cubarsi continues his streak as a permanent fixture for La Roja, though he will operate without his typical partner Aymeric Laporte, who remains sidelined. The roster changes extend beyond the starting lineup, with Pedri and Joan Garcia named as substitutes for the match. Star player Lamine Yamal did not make the trip to Prague, staying back to treat an inflammation in his knee. The team will look to capitalize on the form of the Barcelona duo to secure a result against their hosts.
