Harry Kane nets winner as he becomes England's most capped player

By Karim Fouad · Published 12 Nov 2026, 22:31 UTC

Harry Kane secured a victory over Croatia with a penalty goal while officially breaking the record for the most appearances for England.

Harry Kane marked his record-breaking night by netting his 92nd goal for England, successfully converting a penalty kick to clinch a narrow victory against Croatia. The historic match saw the England captain surpass the long-standing milestone of 125 international caps previously held by goalkeeper Peter Shilton for nearly four decades. While Kane remained the centerpiece of the evening, teammate Morgan Rogers added to the tally with his third senior international goal.

The outcome is significant for England's current Nations League campaign, following a challenging match that saw defensive tension throughout. Despite a costly error by Jarell Quansah that allowed Croatia to briefly equalize, England maintained control through a composed midfield performance led by Declan Rice and reliable support from players like Lewis Hall. The win reinforces the squad's standing as they navigate the tournament stages, showcasing the depth of their current lineup.

Player contributions varied in intensity, with Newcastle defender Lewis Hall earning praise for his effective left-back performance, while Jordan Pickford remained dependable in goal outside of the conceded mistake. Jude Bellingham provided quality in possession, ensuring England held the necessary pressure to secure the points against a resilient Croatian side. This result serves as a landmark moment in English football, transitioning the record from Shilton’s 1990 finale to Kane’s ongoing captaincy.
