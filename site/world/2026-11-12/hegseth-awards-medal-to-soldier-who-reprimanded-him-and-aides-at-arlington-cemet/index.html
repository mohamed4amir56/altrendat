Defense Secretary Hegseth Awards Medal to Guard Who Silenced His Group

By Karim Fouad · Published 12 Nov 2026, 21:01 UTC

Pete Hegseth presented the Joint Service Commendation Medal to Specialist Thomas Iglesias after the soldier corrected the secretary’s behavior at Arlington National Cemetery.

Quick facts

Recipient
Specialist Thomas Iglesias
Award
Joint Service Commendation Medal
Location
Tomb of the Unknown Soldier, Arlington National Cemetery

The encounter began during a Veterans Day observance at the Tomb of the Unknown Soldier when Specialist Thomas Iglesias stepped forward to address noise coming from a group that included the Secretary of Defense, his wife, and senior advisers. As the group laughed and posed for photographs, the sentry interrupted the commotion, firmly ordering: "All visitors will maintain an atmosphere of silence and respect at all times." This act of military discipline reflects the long-standing protocol at the site, where guards are tasked with maintaining a solemn environment for one of the nation's most sacred war memorials.

Following the incident, the Pentagon announced that Specialist Iglesias received the Joint Service Commendation Medal for his professional execution of duties. The award serves as formal recognition of the soldier’s commitment to standards at the tomb, which has been under 24-hour guard by the 3rd US Infantry Regiment since 1948. Pentagon spokesman Sean Parnell stated that the soldier correctly and respectfully ensured the decorum expected at the site was upheld.

On his official X account, Hegseth shared images of the medal presentation alongside members of the Old Guard. His post emphasized a commitment to military standards, stating, "We love soldiers. We love standards. Well done, Specialist. Carry on Patriots." While such interventions are common at the cemetery—with guards reportedly admonishing visitors several times a day—the public recognition of this specific interaction highlights the rigid expectations for conduct at the tomb. This event occurred during a day that also featured formal wreath-laying ceremonies attended by President Donald Trump and other top government officials.
