Optimum restores internet service after massive third-party infrastructure damage across West Virginia

By Karim Fouad · Published 12 Nov 2026, 18:56 UTC

Optimum restored internet services on November 11, 2026, after a third-party line cut caused widespread outages throughout West Virginia and parts of Ohio.

Quick facts

Outage date
November 11, 2026
Outage start time
5:30 p.m.
Primary location
West Virginia
Secondary locations
Belpre and Marietta, Ohio
Cause
Third-party infrastructure damage

The disruption began at approximately 5:30 p.m. when connectivity suddenly dropped for thousands of users across the state, leaving residents and businesses in areas including the Mid-Ohio Valley without reliable service. Optimum confirmed that the failure stemmed from damage to a third-party’s network that supports their own infrastructure, forcing an immediate reliance on external repair crews to restore the connection. Reliable internet connectivity serves as a critical utility for the community, and the sudden loss of service underscored the fragility of shared network infrastructure. The outage highlights the vulnerability of regional connectivity when third-party providers experience physical line cuts. Optimum noted that service interruptions can occur whenever shared or leased lines are damaged by outside parties. While the company stated that the circumstances remained outside of their direct control, they issued a formal apology to affected customers. According to reports from WTAP, the outage map also showed disruption in select areas of Belpre and Marietta, Ohio, alongside the statewide West Virginia impact. With restoration now confirmed, Optimum has moved from emergency repair efforts to ongoing monitoring of the affected lines.
