Stephen Curry Exits Early With Abdominal Soreness as Warriors Streak Snaps

By Karim Fouad · Published 12 Nov 2026, 18:56 UTC

Stephen Curry left Wednesday's loss to the Houston Rockets after five minutes due to right abdominal soreness.

Quick facts

Next Game
Friday vs. San Antonio Spurs

Stephen Curry exited Wednesday’s game against the Houston Rockets in the first quarter, ending his night after just five minutes of play. The star guard was officially ruled out for the remainder of the contest with right abdominal soreness. Before his early departure, Curry had already extended his streak of consecutive games with a made three-pointer to 102. Head coach Steve Kerr confirmed that Curry is set to undergo an MRI to determine the severity of the injury, and the team remains hopeful that the issue is not long-term. This setback comes as the Warriors are already managing a string of absences, including Jimmy Butler, Moses Moody, Gary Payton II, and Brandon Williams. The injury forced a major rotation shift during the 115-108 loss at the Toyota Center. With Curry off the floor, rookie Yaxel Lendeborg stepped into a leading role, finishing with a team-high 25 points and 10 rebounds. Brandin Podziemski also saw increased usage, contributing 22 points and four three-pointers. Kerr praised the team's competitive spirit despite the defeat, noting that the group stayed connected while attempting to overcome the significant personnel loss. The loss marked the end of an eight-game winning streak for Golden State, their first such run since the 2022 championship season. The Warriors now prepare for their next challenge, scheduled for Friday against the San Antonio Spurs at the Frost Bank Center as part of the 2026 NBA Cup. Players like De'Anthony Melton and L.J. Cryer are expected to manage the point guard duties while the team awaits medical clearance for its sidelined stars.
