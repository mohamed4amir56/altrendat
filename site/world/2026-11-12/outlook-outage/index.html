Outlook outage hits 59,000 users across the Americas on Thursday morning

By Karim Fouad · Published 12 Nov 2026, 18:31 UTC

Microsoft is investigating reports of users unable to access Exchange Online mailboxes through multiple connection methods.

Quick facts

Reported incident ID
EX1550543
User reports
Over 59,000
Affected region
Americas
Day
Thursday

More than 59,000 users reported issues with Outlook on Thursday, according to the online status tracker DownDetector. The disruption left many people unable to access their emails as they attempted to connect through various methods during the morning hours. Microsoft confirmed it is actively investigating the situation to restore full functionality.

This outage matters because it impacts the ability of customers to access Exchange Online mailboxes, which are core components of business and personal communication for millions. When these services fail, workflow and daily professional activities can come to an immediate halt across the affected region, which Microsoft identified as the Americas.

Microsoft officially acknowledged the technical problem through its service incidents social media channel. The company is currently working on resolving the connection issues that prevented users from reaching their mailboxes. Further technical specifics regarding the investigation are being tracked internally through the Microsoft 365 admin center under the identifier EX1550543.

While the company works on a fix, users have been advised to monitor the status through official channels. No timeline for a full restoration was provided, but updates are expected to be shared through the company's designated status platforms as the engineering teams continue their diagnostic work to resolve the mail delivery problems.
