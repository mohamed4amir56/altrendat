Choco Mucho Breaks Seven-Game Losing Streak Against Creamline in Five-Set Thriller

By Karim Fouad · Published 12 Nov 2026, 19:01 UTC

The Choco Mucho Flying Titans defeated the Creamline Cool Smashers 23-25, 25-13, 25-23, 22-25, 15-13 on Thursday night at the Smart Araneta Coliseum.

The Flying Titans entered the match against the league’s winningest franchise having lost 19 of their 21 meetings since 2019. This victory serves as only the second time Choco Mucho has overcome the Cool Smashers, effectively snapping a seven-game losing skid that spanned back to their previous win in the 2024 All-Filipino Conference semifinals.

This result carries significant weight for the Premier Volleyball League (PVL) standings, as Choco Mucho improves to a 4-3 record to climb to fourth place. For Creamline, the narrow loss brings their record to 4-2, shaking up the current hierarchy of the All-Filipino Conference-Siklab Series. The match performance highlights the depth of the Flying Titans roster, particularly their ability to recover from a two-point deficit late in the final set.

According to The Manila Times and Tiebreaker Times, Sisi Rondina led the charge for Choco Mucho with 20 points, supported by Caitlin Viray and Jewel Encarnacion who added 11 and 10 points respectively. Deanna Wong contributed 15 excellent sets. Despite the defeat, Creamline saw strong efforts from Jema Galanza with 16 points and playmaker Jia De Guzman, who recorded 22 excellent sets. Eya Laure made a pivotal return during the fifth set after sitting out earlier frames due to a back injury.

Head coach Dante Alinsunurin credited a total team effort for the outcome, noting that the players successfully compensated for each other’s gaps throughout the grueling contest. The win provides a vital boost for the Flying Titans after suffering back-to-back losses against Nxled and Akari in previous matches.
