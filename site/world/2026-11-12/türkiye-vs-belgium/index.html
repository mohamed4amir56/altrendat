Winless Turkey faces relegation as Belgium arrives for must-win Nations League clash

By Karim Fouad · Published 12 Nov 2026, 16:31 UTC · Updated 12 Nov 2026, 17:01 UTC

Turkey hosts Belgium at Gursel Aksel Stadium on Thursday in a critical UEFA Nations League match as the hosts fight to avoid relegation.

Quick facts

Date
Thursday, Nov. 12
Kickoff Time
12 p.m. ET
Location
Gursel Aksel Stadium, Izmir
TV Channel
FS1

Turkey enters Thursday’s match against Belgium still seeking its first point of the Nations League campaign after failing to secure a result in its opening four fixtures. The home side faces a desperate scenario at Gursel Aksel Stadium, where anything less than a victory will guarantee a last-place finish in Group A1 and likely automatic relegation from the competition’s top tier. In contrast, Belgium travels to Izmir holding a six-point advantage over the hosts and remains in contention for a spot in the quarterfinals despite a recent 4-1 loss to France. This fixture serves as a rematch following Belgium’s 3-0 victory over Turkey on October 2. For Turkish supporters, the game marks the managerial debut of Sergen Yalcin, who takes over following the departure of Vincenzo Montella after his three-year tenure ended last month. The challenge is significant for the new manager, who must navigate the match without the suspended Abdulkerim Bardakci and injured players Kenan Yildiz, Adil Demirbag, Mert Muldur, and Aral Simsir. Mark van Bommel’s Belgian squad faces its own roster adjustments for the trip to Izmir. Romeo Lavia is suspended after picking up a caution during the loss to France, though Kevin De Bruyne and Youri Tielemans are both eligible to return to the lineup after serving their respective suspensions. The outcome of this clash is vital for both nations as they balance the immediate threat of relegation against hopes for tournament advancement.
