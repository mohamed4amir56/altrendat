Spain extends winning streak to 12 matches after routing 10-man Czech Republic

By Karim Fouad · Published 13 Nov 2026, 10:32 UTC

Spain defeated the 10-man Czech Republic 2-0 in the UEFA Nations League, maintaining their unbeaten run in 43 matches.

Alex Baena opened the scoring in the 39th minute with a penalty kick after a handball by Lukas Ambros. The lead remained narrow throughout much of the contest, as the Czechs narrowly missed an equalizer when Adam Hlozek sent a header crashing into the crossbar. The visitors eventually sealed the result in injury time when Marc Pubill intercepted the ball on the edge of the box and slotted it into the net.

This result maintains Spain's momentum as they head toward the final stage of the competition. For the Spanish side, the victory serves as their 12th consecutive win in all competitions, further solidifying a dominant form that has seen them remain unbeaten for 43 matches. The loss leaves the Czech Republic in a difficult position after playing much of the final third of the game with a depleted squad.

Discipline proved to be a critical factor in the match, specifically following a VAR intervention in the 69th minute. Czech captain Ladislav Krejci was initially cautioned with a yellow card for a professional foul on Roberto Fernandez, but the official upgraded the punishment to a red card after reviewing the footage. This forced the home team to finish the match with only 10 players, providing Spain with the tactical advantage needed to secure the late second goal.

Spain has already clinched a position in the UEFA Nations League quarter-finals. They now look ahead to Sunday, where they will host England in the final group stage fixture. The winner of that upcoming match will secure the top spot in the group standings.
