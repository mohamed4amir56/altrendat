Antoine Dupont switches to flyhalf for France in showdown against Springboks

By Karim Fouad · Published 13 Nov 2026, 13:31 UTC

France fields star scrumhalf Antoine Dupont at flyhalf for Friday's Nations Championship match against South Africa at the Stade de France.

Quick facts

Match Date
Friday
Venue
Stade de France
Competition
Nations Championship

Antoine Dupont shifting to the No 10 jersey marks a tactical pivot as France searches for an "unimaginable and unexpected" approach to challenge the world champions in Paris. The selection follows a period where France coach Fabien Galthié faced a shortage of traditional flyhalf options due to the injuries of Romain Ntamack, Matthieu Jalibert, and Antoine Hastoy.

This position adjustment is significant because the Springboks have historically dominated this fixture, winning nine of their past 10 Tests against France. For the visitors, controlling the set-piece remains the primary objective. According to former Springbok prop BJ Botha, South Africa possesses a massive depth advantage in the front row that will likely allow them to dictate terms in the scrum and maul battles at the Stade de France.

Springbok coach Rassie Erasmus noted earlier this week that he anticipated France would prioritize physicality in the flyhalf channel to avoid defensive gaps. While Galthié previously utilized Maxime Lucu at flyhalf during their recent 54-17 victory over Fiji, the decision to start Dupont indicates a shift in strategy. Erasmus remains confident that the match will evolve into a physical struggle for dominance between the two packs.

As the world champions prepare for the second Test of their tour, the pressure is on the French to disrupt a South African team that has won their past five encounters at this venue. Fans will see if Dupont’s creative presence can bridge the gap against a pack that features options like Wilco Louw, Thomas du Toit, and Ox Nché.
