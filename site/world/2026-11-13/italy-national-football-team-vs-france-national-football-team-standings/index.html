Pio Esposito brace inspires Italy to storm past France at San Siro

By Karim Fouad · Published 13 Nov 2026, 12:01 UTC

Italy climbed to the top of Nations League Group A1 after a 3-1 comeback victory over France in Milan.

Quick facts

Final Score
Italy 3-1 France
Venue
San Siro, Milan
Group A1 Leaders
Italy (10 points)

Pio Esposito’s two goals handed Zinedine Zidane his first defeat as France head coach during a rain-soaked encounter at the San Siro. Italy recovered from a disastrous start to secure three points, despite seeing Sebastiano Esposito miss a crucial penalty kick on the hour mark. The win proved decisive for the standings, as Italy now leapfrogs France to take the lead in Group A1 based on their head-to-head record. The result is a significant shift in the group dynamic, as Italy sits on 10 points, moving ahead of France despite being level on points heading into the next rounds of the competition. The visitors initially took the lead after only 10 minutes when Michael Olise capitalized on a Gianluigi Donnarumma error, marking his fifth goal in five Nations League appearances. France dominated the early exchanges, with Bradley Barcola and Rayan Cherki both hitting the woodwork before Italy regained control. Davide Frattesi added to the Italian tally, while France remained without injured stars Kylian Mbappé and Ousmane Dembélé throughout the match. Both teams must now look ahead to their remaining group fixtures as they battle for the top spot. Belgium remains in the mix, sitting just one point behind the leaders following their victory over bottom-placed Turkey.
