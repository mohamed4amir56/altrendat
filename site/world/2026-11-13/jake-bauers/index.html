World Series Champion Jake Bauers Snaps Up Four-Year Deal With Red Sox

By Karim Fouad · Published 13 Nov 2026, 18:01 UTC · Updated 13 Nov 2026, 18:32 UTC

Slugger Jake Bauers signed a four-year, $58 million contract with the Boston Red Sox after declining a qualifying offer from the Milwaukee Brewers.

Following his breakout 2026 campaign that saw the Brewers win the World Series, 31-year-old Jake Bauers is leaving Milwaukee for a $58 million deal in Boston. The decision comes after the first baseman turned down a one-year, $23 million qualifying offer from the Brewers, who were unwilling to match the four-year term offered by the Red Sox.

For the Red Sox, securing Bauers adds a proven performer who reached career-best metrics across the board in 2026. The lefty slugger hit 26 home runs with a .266 average and an .874 OPS, establishing himself as a centerpiece of a championship roster. His performance was marked by a significant increase in hitting power, including a 14% barrel rate and a hard-hit rate of 51.9%, ranking him in the 90th percentile among qualifying hitters.

According to MLB.com, Bauers earned NLCS MVP honors after a dominant postseason run that included six RBIs in a sweep of the Dodgers. His iconic 11-pitch home run against Blake Snell during Game 3 of the NLCS served as a defining moment for Milwaukee’s championship season. Bauers had previously played for the Rays, Guardians, Mariners, and Yankees before finding stability in Milwaukee after signing a Minor League deal in early 2025.

As part of the roster transition, the Brewers will receive a Competitive Balance A Round pick in the upcoming draft due to Bauers signing elsewhere. While he now heads to Boston, Bauers is scheduled to return to American Family Field on September 14 to collect his championship ring with his former teammates.
