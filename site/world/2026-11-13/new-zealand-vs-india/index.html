Matt Henry’s triple-wicket maiden sparks New Zealand’s massive 244-run rout of India

By Karim Fouad · Published 13 Nov 2026, 09:31 UTC

Matt Henry took 6-27 to bowl India out for 89 after Will Young’s 125 propelled New Zealand to a 333-run total at Bay Oval.

Matt Henry dismantled India’s top order with a triple-wicket maiden in the opening over, setting the tone for a decisive 244-run victory at Bay Oval. India’s star batters Rohit Sharma and Virat Kohli departed for zero as Henry claimed three wickets before a single run was scored, ultimately finishing with a devastating 6-27. The performance effectively halted India’s momentum in the best-of-five series and gave the home crowd of thousands a spectacle to witness in the final match of the tour at this venue.

This result keeps New Zealand’s hopes alive for a series draw after previously trailing 2-0. Protecting a six-year home ODI unbeaten run, the Black Caps delivered their most complete performance of the tour, capitalizing on batting conditions that allowed Will Young to hammer a career-best 125. The win provides a critical boost for the hosts as they look to secure a series-saving result on Sunday.

According to Cricbuzz and the NZ Herald, New Zealand posted 333, supported by Tim Seifert’s 58 off 48 balls and Finn Allen’s 41. India struggled from the start, with Shreyas Iyer trapped in front and the side dismissed inside 27 overs. While Kyle Jamieson and Matthew Fisher added to the pressure with steep bounce, Henry remained the chief destroyer, returning to clean up the tail and secure his fourth ODI five-wicket haul.

New Zealand must now prepare for the series finale on Sunday without Matt Henry, who departs to join the Test squad in Wellington. The team aims to replicate this momentum at the Basin Reserve in the upcoming test series.
