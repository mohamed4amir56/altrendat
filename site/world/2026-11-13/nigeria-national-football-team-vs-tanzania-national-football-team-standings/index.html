Super Eagles Seek Crucial AFCON Redemption Against Tanzania in Uyo Home Clash

By Karim Fouad · Published 13 Nov 2026, 18:32 UTC

Nigeria targets a critical Group L victory against Tanzania this Friday following a recent 3-0 defeat to Guinea-Bissau.

Quick facts

5 p.m.

Tanzania Tournament Status
Co-host of 2027 AFCON

Nigeria enters the Godswill Akpabio International Stadium in Uyo today desperate to revive their 2027 Africa Cup of Nations qualifying campaign. The team currently holds three points from two matches, placing them three points behind the group leaders, Guinea-Bissau. Head coach Éric Chelle is under intense pressure to secure a win after the side suffered a disappointing 3-0 loss to Guinea-Bissau just four days after an opening 2-1 victory over Madagascar.

This match represents a pivotal moment for Nigeria to strengthen their standing in Group L and restore team confidence. Because the qualifying race is intensifying, any further setback would leave the three-time African champions trailing significantly behind the group leaders. Securing points at home is vital before the squad heads to Zanzibar for the return fixture against the Taifa Stars.

Nigeria will compete without star striker Victor Osimhen, who is currently recovering from knee surgery following a Champions League injury. Coach Chelle has turned to a 24-man roster, with Wilfred Ndidi serving as captain. The starting XI features Stanley Nwabali in goal, with Calvin Bassey and Benjamin Frederick anchoring the defense. Tanzania, despite their struggles with only one point from two matches, enters the game with the security of having already qualified for the 2027 AFCON as tournament co-hosts alongside Kenya and Uganda.

Looking ahead, the teams will soon prepare for the second leg of this double-header in Zanzibar. Nigeria aims to use this encounter to build momentum for the remainder of their qualification schedule.
