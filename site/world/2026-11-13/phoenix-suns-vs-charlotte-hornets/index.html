Devin Booker Snatches Win Against Hornets While Climbing All-Time Scoring List

By Karim Fouad · Published 13 Nov 2026, 18:01 UTC

Devin Booker scored 30 points to lead the Phoenix Suns to a 101-96 victory over the Charlotte Hornets on Thursday.

Devin Booker surpassed NBA legends Rick Barry and Tracy McGrady on the all-time scoring list during Thursday's contest. With his latest performance, the five-time All-Star reached 18,401 career points, moving into 77th place all-time. The scoring milestone highlights a stretch where the guard has reached at least 30 points in four of his last six outings. This victory carries significant momentum for the Suns as Booker maintains an average of 27.7 points per game over his recent six-game span. For fantasy managers and fans tracking individual production, his current efficiency includes hitting 38.6 percent of his three-point attempts alongside 4.8 assists and 1.7 steals per game. According to FantasyPros, Booker was clinical in his execution against the Hornets, finishing the night shooting 12-of-20 from the field and hitting all four of his free-throw attempts. Beyond his scoring, he contributed seven assists and three steals to secure the five-point margin of victory. Yahoo Sports confirms that he officially eclipsed Rick Barry's total of 18,395 points and Tracy McGrady's 18,381 points to reach his current position in the record books. The Suns continue their season campaign looking to build on this narrow win against Charlotte.
