Commanders Quarterback Sam Hartman Swaps The Football Field For The Bachelor Mansion

By Karim Fouad · Published 13 Nov 2026, 14:01 UTC

Washington Commanders practice squad quarterback Sam Hartman will star in the 30th season of ABC's The Bachelor, premiering on March 21, 2027.

Sam Hartman is preparing for a transition that requires balancing football playbooks with romantic expectations. As he steps into his role as the lead for the milestone 30th season of The Bachelor, the 27-year-old quarterback faces the task of managing conversations with 30 contestants simultaneously. In his first interview about the casting, Hartman admitted he feels excited but very nervous about the upcoming process.

For viewers, this marks a crossover between professional sports and reality television, a format previously explored by former NFL quarterback Jesse Palmer in 2004. While participants often seek long-term relationships, the history of the franchise shows varied results, with over 20 couples from the series currently remaining together. Hartman becomes the second NFL signal-caller to headline the dating show in its two-decade history.

Hartman signed with the Washington Commanders as an undrafted free agent in 2024 following a collegiate career that saw him set several records. He played four seasons at Wake Forest University, where he received the Brian Piccolo Award after overcoming a rare blood clot condition, before transferring to the University of Notre Dame for his final season in 2023. During his time at Wake Forest, he finished with 134 touchdown passes, setting an Atlantic Coast Conference record.

Fans can watch the season premiere of The Bachelor on Sunday, March 21, at 8 p.m. ET on ABC. The episodes will be available to stream on Hulu the day after the broadcast.
