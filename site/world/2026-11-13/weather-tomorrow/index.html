Tornadoes strike Delaware and New Jersey as snow chances arrive Friday morning

By Karim Fouad · Published 13 Nov 2026, 05:01 UTC

Tornadoes touched down in New Castle County and Burlington County on Thursday, Nov. 12, following severe storms across the Philadelphia region.

Quick facts

Date of storms
Thursday, Nov. 12
Affected states
Delaware, New Jersey, Pennsylvania
Survey locations
Chesterfield and Bordentown, NJ

Hail accumulated in piles across the Pennsylvania suburbs on Thursday as severe storms brought heavy wind and an isolated tornado threat to the area. Meteorologist Brittney Shipp of NBC10 reported that the storms moved through the Philadelphia region bringing intense weather conditions before shifting toward the possibility of snow. Residents across the area faced fallen trees and funnel clouds throughout the day as the weather front progressed.

This weather pattern carries significant impacts for regional travel and property safety as cleanup efforts begin. The combination of storm damage and impending winter weather conditions on Friday requires residents to exercise caution during cleanup operations and morning commutes.

The National Weather Service confirmed that tornadoes struck both New Castle County, Delaware, and Burlington County, New Jersey. According to 6abc, officials have planned survey missions for Friday to assess the extent of the damage in the towns of Chesterfield and Bordentown. A preliminary survey attempted on Thursday evening was halted due to limited visibility from darkness. Crews are expected to coordinate further inspections to categorize the strength and duration of the storm activity that impacted these neighborhoods.

Survey crews will head to impacted areas on Friday to document the full scope of the tornado touchdown sites. These findings will help officials determine the specific wind speeds and structural impact caused by the severe weather system as the region prepares for the potential arrival of snow.
