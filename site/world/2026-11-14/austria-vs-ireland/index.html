Nathan Collins returns to lead Ireland against Austria in Nations League clash

By Karim Fouad · Published 14 Nov 2026, 19:31 UTC

Captain Nathan Collins and two other defenders return to the starting line-up for Ireland's UEFA Nations League match against Austria on November 14th.

Quick facts

Date
November 14th
Venue
Raiffeisen Arena, Linz
Kick-off Time
7.45pm
TV Channel
RTÉ2 / RTÉ Player
Referee
Irfan Peljto

Captain Nathan Collins makes his return to the pitch at the Raiffeisen Arena after missing four consecutive fixtures due to a calf injury. Alongside him, defenders Dara O'Shea and James Abankwah are back in the starting side, bolstering a team that aims to secure a win in Linz to push for promotion to League A. Ireland enters the match with the knowledge that two victories in their final group games—against Austria and Kosovo—will guarantee them the top spot in the group.

Securing this win is critical for Ireland's ambitions. Head coach Heimir Hallgrímsson notes that while finishing in the top two is a primary goal, the team is focused on winning the group outright. A victory would serve as a major step toward promotion, a feat that would mark a significant achievement for the squad. The team is also mindful of disciplinary status, as five players, including Jayson Molumby and Liam Scales, are currently carrying yellow cards and risk suspension for the upcoming decider against Kosovo.

According to RTE.ie, Hallgrímsson has confirmed that Finn Azaz will operate in a deeper midfield role alongside Jayson Molumby. Jack Moylan and Adam Idah are tasked with supporting centre-forward Troy Parrott. On the other side, Austria has made four changes to their team, with Bayern Munich's Konrad Laimer set to captain the side. Austria's squad includes new additions such as Christoph Baumgartner and Philipp Lienhart, as they aim to build on their previous 2-2 draw at the Aviva Stadium.

Following the match in Linz, Ireland will return to Dublin to face Kosovo on Tuesday. Hallgrímsson remains focused on maintaining defensive solidity to avoid repeating the errors seen in the second half of their previous meeting with Austria.
