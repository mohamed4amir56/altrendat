Ayyoub Bouaddi dominates midfield as Morocco routs Niger in Casablanca qualifier

By Karim Fouad · Published 14 Nov 2026, 19:02 UTC

Morocco maintained its perfect record in Africa Cup of Nations qualifying by defeating Niger 5-1 in Casablanca.

Ayyoub Bouaddi showcased his precision during the international break, recording a 96% successful pass rate while winning eight duels throughout the match. His composure in possession provided the foundation for Morocco's commanding performance, marking a significant outing in what is only his 11th cap for the national side.

The teenager's influence was felt early as he provided the assist for Achraf Hakimi to double Morocco's lead to 2-0 in the 27th minute. This victory secures Morocco's continued dominance in Group A, where they have now maintained a 100% win rate across their first three matches of the qualification campaign.

Morocco took control of the fixture within four minutes through an opening goal from Yassir Zabiri. Although Mohamed Abdourahmane pulled one back for Niger before halftime, Morocco responded immediately through a second goal from Achraf Hakimi. The hosts pulled away decisively in the second half, with Youssef En Nesyri adding a fourth goal in the 77th minute. Ayoub Oufkir finalized the 5-1 scoreline by finding the net in the 90th minute, capping off a dominant display for the hosts in Casablanca.

Following this win, Morocco remains at the top of Group A with a perfect record. The team will look to carry this momentum into their remaining qualification fixtures as they seek to solidify their path through the tournament stages.
