Groin injury benches Kansas City star Kenneth Walker III for Week 10

By Karim Fouad · Published 14 Nov 2026, 17:01 UTC

Kansas City Chiefs running back Kenneth Walker III will miss Sunday’s game against the Atlanta Falcons due to a groin injury.

Kenneth Walker III enters the weekend sidelined after sustaining a groin injury that forced him to miss practice on Friday. The Kansas City Chiefs confirmed the running back will not participate in Sunday's matchup against the Atlanta Falcons, marking a sudden absence for the team's offensive leader.

This development is significant given that Walker currently holds the second-most rushing yards in the NFL this season with 872. Chief's head coach Andy Reid stated on Friday that the team is prioritizing caution regarding the injury, noting that they do not want to put the player at risk by forcing him to play through the issue.

Reports from ESPN indicate that the organization aims to give Walker one week of recovery time to address the injury. According to NFL Network, the setback is considered minor, prompting the team's decision to hold him out rather than risk further damage.

In the wake of this change, attention shifts to Emmett Johnson, who is expected to make his first career start for the Chiefs. Johnson previously demonstrated offensive utility during a Week 8 contest against the Denver Broncos, where he recorded 128 receiving yards and one touchdown across 13 offensive snaps. Fantasy analysts view Johnson as a potential impact player for Week 10 given his recent performance, despite the Falcons’ defense holding a top-three ranking in EPA per rush.
