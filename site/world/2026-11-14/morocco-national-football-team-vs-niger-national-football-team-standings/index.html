Achraf Hakimi stars as Morocco routs Niger with emphatic 5-1 victory

By Karim Fouad · Published 14 Nov 2026, 05:31 UTC

Morocco extended its home winning streak to 16 matches by defeating Niger 5-1 in the Africa Cup of Nations qualifiers on Friday.

Quick facts

Score
5-1
Date
Friday
Venue
Mohammed V Stadium, Casablanca
Home winning streak
16 matches

Achraf Hakimi cemented his status as a playmaker for the Atlas Lions, becoming the first player to record 20 assists for the national team this century. The right-back found the net twice and provided an assist during the 5-1 win, marking a dominant individual performance at the Mohammed V Stadium. His two goals and his delivery to Youssef En-Nesyri helped Morocco overwhelm their opponents, with the team scoring five times in total to maintain their momentum in the 2027 Africa Cup of Nations qualifying campaign.

This result is significant as it extends Morocco's formidable home record to 16 consecutive qualifying victories. According to reports, this winning streak across Africa Cup of Nations and World Cup qualifiers represents the best home performance for the team in such competitions. These statistics highlight the team's evolution into a consistent force, further cementing their reputation as a top-tier side on the international stage.

The scoring began early when Yassine Zabiri found the net in the fourth minute from a Brahim Diaz assist. While Niger's Mohamed Abdul Rahman reduced the deficit in the 41st minute, the visitors missed a crucial opportunity to equalize when Youssef Ali Omar sent a penalty wide just before the interval. Hakimi responded by curling in a free kick during stoppage time to restore the two-goal cushion. Following En-Nesyri's goal, Ayoub Ouafkir added the final tally in the 90th minute to complete the rout.

Morocco now looks ahead to continue their qualifying path as they aim to solidify their position at the top of their group. The team's reliance on their home-field advantage remains a central component of their current strategic success under manager Mohamed Ouahbi.
