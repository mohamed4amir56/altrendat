Grand Theft Auto VI Surges Past $1 Billion In Preorder Sales Revenue

By Karim Fouad · Published 14 Nov 2026, 16:01 UTC

Rockstar Games will launch Grand Theft Auto VI on Nov. 19 following an estimated development cost exceeding $1 billion.

Quick facts

Official launch date
Nov. 19
Preorder volume
Over 12 million
Development cost
Over $1 billion
Sales forecast
40 million copies by March 2027

With over 12 million preorders logged by Nov. 13, the gaming world is bracing for a title that analysts suggest may become the biggest video game launch of all time. After two separate delays, the release arrives 13 years after the previous installment, marking a significant milestone for parent company Take-Two Interactive. The financial stakes are immense, as the game maker reportedly invested over $1 billion into the development of this specific project.

This release represents more than a singular gaming event; it serves as a massive commercial opportunity for the broader entertainment ecosystem. Industry experts anticipate that the game will sustain long-term engagement for creators on platforms like Twitch and YouTube, potentially rivaling or exceeding the cultural footprint of previous industry giants like Fortnite. Twitch Chief Product Officer Mike Minton noted that the title provides a lasting opportunity for streamers to build and retain audiences well into the future.

Investors have already responded to the anticipation, with Take-Two's stock price more than doubling since the start of 2023. CEO Strauss Zelnick stated in a recent interview that consumer demand for the game is currently vastly exceeding the company's high internal expectations. While initial excitement centers on the launch week, forecasts from Morgan Stanley suggest that the game could reach 40 million copies sold by March 2027.

Fans now have the ability to download the software ahead of the official launch. The sustained performance of the previous title, which remains a top-five most viewed game on Twitch 13 years after its debut, highlights the potential for this new release to remain a dominant force in the gaming market for years to come.
