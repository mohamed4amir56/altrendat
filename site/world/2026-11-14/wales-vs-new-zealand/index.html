Beauden Barrett Captains New Zealand Side Boasting 783 Total Test Caps

By Karim Fouad · Published 14 Nov 2026, 14:01 UTC · Updated 14 Nov 2026, 15:01 UTC

New Zealand faces Wales at Principality Stadium this Saturday in the Nations Championship with Beauden Barrett leading the team for the first time.

Quick facts

Kick-off time
2:10 PM local time
Venue
Principality Stadium, Cardiff
Broadcast
Sky Sport
Captain
Beauden Barrett

Beauden Barrett will captain the All Blacks for the first time in his 149th Test match when the team squares off against Wales in Cardiff. The veteran side features a combined experience of 783 caps, with head coach Dave Rennie making ten changes to the match-day 23 that faced Scotland last weekend. Anton Segner and Fehi Fineanganofo are set to earn their first Test starts in the round five encounter at Principality Stadium. The selection follows a week of internal review where the coaching staff identified Wales flanker Jac Morgan as a key breakdown threat that must be neutralized to avoid defensive stress. With New Zealand currently holding a 73-year winning streak against their Welsh opponents, the team remains focused on maintaining its dominant record in the Nations Championship. Rennie noted that his familiarity with the Welsh coaching staff, including former colleagues Steve Tandy and Peter Murchie, provides further insight into the tactical adjustments Wales has implemented over the past year. Rennie specifically highlighted Wales' line-out strength and the kicking capabilities of scrum-half Tomos Williams as major factors his team must contain throughout the match. By shuffling the lineup, the coaching staff aims to balance squad depth with the intensity required to respect the opposition's challenge. The match is scheduled for 2:10 PM local time on Saturday. Fans can follow the action live on Sky Sport or access highlights through the official All Blacks digital platforms.
