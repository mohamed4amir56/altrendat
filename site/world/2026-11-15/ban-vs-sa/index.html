Proteas Target World Test Championship Points in Two-Match Series Against Bangladesh

By Karim Fouad · Published 15 Nov 2026, 08:31 UTC

South Africa travels to face Bangladesh in a two-match Test series while aiming to secure crucial World Test Championship points.

Quick facts

Series Format
Two-match Test series
Recent Series Result
2-1 win over Australia
WTC Status
2025 Champions
Coach
Shukri Conrad

The South African squad enters this tour following a hard-fought 2-1 home series victory against Australia. According to captain Temba Bavuma, the team is focused on maintaining the high standards established during their recent successful run against Pakistan, India, and Australia. The side views these upcoming matches as a vital opportunity to continue their progression while staying firmly within the race for the 2027 World Test Championship final. Coach Shukri Conrad’s men have been looking to silence critics who previously questioned the team's credentials following their 2025 WTC title win.

Securing a series victory against an unpredictable Bangladesh side is essential for the Proteas to cement their status as a premier cricketing nation. Despite lacking the long list of legendary "superstars" that defined past South African teams, the current squad has proven its competitiveness by winning eight out of 12 matches during the previous WTC cycle. Bavuma noted that the team has taken stock of their journey thus far and remains committed to their internal processes, regardless of external skepticism from the global cricketing community.

The Proteas remain determined to prove that their past success was no fluke by focusing strictly on the task at hand. By playing effectively against the opposition in front of them, the players look to build on the momentum gained from their recent win over the Aussies. The team will prioritize consistency as they tackle the challenge posed by the home side in Bangladesh to remain in strong contention for the next championship final.
