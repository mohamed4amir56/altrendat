Chiefs Rule Out Star Running Back Kenneth Walker III for Week 10

By Karim Fouad · Published 15 Nov 2026, 09:01 UTC

Kansas City Chiefs running back Kenneth Walker III is officially ruled out for Sunday following a groin injury.

Reason

Season stats
872 rushing yards

Kenneth Walker III currently ranks second in the NFL with 872 rushing yards this season, serving as a primary offensive force for the Kansas City Chiefs. His absence creates a significant hole in the roster as the team moves into the second half of the 2026 season. Head coach Andy Reid confirmed that the decision to bench the fifth-year pro was made to avoid putting the player at risk after he tweaked his groin during the week. Walker did not participate in practice on the final day of the week before being officially ruled out on Saturday. The star running back currently leads the league with an average of 109.0 rushing yards per game, having recorded seven rushing touchdowns and four receiving touchdowns through eight games this year. According to NBC Sports, rookie Emmett Johnson is slated to start in his place. The injury news extends beyond the backfield as the Chiefs manage a series of health concerns. The team also ruled out offensive lineman Jaylon Moore due to an ankle injury, defensive lineman Peter Woods with a neck issue, and defensive tackle Omarr Norman-Lott, who is dealing with a knee injury. Additionally, rookie cornerback Mansoor Delane remains questionable with a shoulder injury despite participating in a full practice on Friday. Delane has not appeared in a game since the October 25 loss to the Broncos and was inactive for the two previous weeks. The organization is also managing the status of running back Jaydn Ott, who is listed as questionable while recovering from a hamstring injury. The team continues to assess these roster limitations as they prepare for their upcoming Sunday matchup.
