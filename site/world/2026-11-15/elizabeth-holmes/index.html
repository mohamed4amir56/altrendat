Billy Evans invites cameras home for 34 days before Elizabeth Holmes imprisonment

By Karim Fouad · Published 15 Nov 2026, 15:01 UTC

Partner Billy Evans discusses his controversial portrayal in the new documentary You Can See Everything during his first television interview.

Quick facts

Documentary title
You Can See Everything
Filmmakers
Lance Oppenheim and Nathan Fielder
Filming duration
34 days
Evans profession
MIT graduate and high-tech worker
Holmes prison start date
2023

Billy Evans lived with documentary filmmakers Lance Oppenheim and Nathan Fielder for 34 days in the weeks leading up to the moment his partner, Theranos founder Elizabeth Holmes, reported to prison in 2023. Reflecting on the experience, Evans acknowledged the risk of trusting someone to portray him as they saw fit, admitting he invited the cameras into his home despite the personal fallout. The documentary, titled You Can See Everything, has since gained widespread attention for its unique style and the specific way it casts Evans as a controlling and manipulative figure. While he admits the portrayal is painful, Evans maintains he is not the character depicted in the film. He stated he remains focused on living with integrity rather than reacting with anger toward the filmmakers or the project's edit. Despite being cast as a villain, Evans claims he loves the final product because it highlights the Elizabeth he knows and loves. He stated he chose to speak out now because he and Holmes learned the lesson that they cannot afford to lose their voices amidst intense public scrutiny. Evans expressed concern over threats he says have been made against his family's safety following the film's release. He continues to manage the daily realities of his family life while his partner serves her sentence. By participating in this interview, Evans hopes to counter the public perception that he is a controlling monster, instead positioning himself as a devoted partner focused on his family and his work in high-tech. His primary motivation, he said, was ensuring viewers caught a glimpse of the person he sees when he looks at Holmes, who once held the title of the world's youngest self-made female billionaire according to Forbes.
