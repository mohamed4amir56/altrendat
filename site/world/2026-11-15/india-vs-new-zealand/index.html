India collapses to 209 as Sharma and Kohli struggle in New Zealand

By Karim Fouad · Published 15 Nov 2026, 10:54 UTC

New Zealand bowled out India for 209 in the fifth ODI at Bay Oval on Sunday, as key batters Rohit Sharma and Virat Kohli fell early.

India reached 209 all out during the fifth one-day international against New Zealand at Bay Oval after New Zealand captain Mitch Santner won the toss. The pitch, which held up and turned throughout the day, proved difficult for the touring side as they posted a total significantly lower than required to secure a series sweep. This batting performance followed a previous match on the same surface where India was dismissed for just 89 runs.

The early loss of Rohit Sharma and Virat Kohli creates mounting pressure for the Indian lineup. Sharma managed only 7 runs before being dismissed by pacer Matt Fisher, while Kohli fell for 5 runs after attempting an ambitious slash that was caught by wicketkeeper Tim Seifert. Kohli’s performance in this series marked his third consecutive single-digit score, further extending his seven-year wait for an ODI century in SENA countries.

Despite the top-order collapse, Ishan Kishan and Axar Patel provided some stability by each scoring 46 runs. Patel's innings included five fours and a single six before he was caught by Will Young. Late resistance from Arshdeep Singh and Mohammad Siraj helped bolster the total as they combined for 26 runs in the final wicket partnership. For New Zealand, Mitch Santner claimed 3-47 while Jacob Duffy added 3-60 to dismantle the Indian innings.

India currently leads the five-match series 2-1. New Zealand requires a victory in this final encounter to prevent their first home ODI series loss in seven years. The result of this match remains pivotal for both sides as they look toward future international commitments.
