India Stuns Pakistan With Last-Ball Victory In U-19 Asia Cup Final

By Karim Fouad · Published 15 Nov 2026, 07:01 UTC

India clinched the U-19 Asia Cup title by chasing down a 338-run target with one wicket remaining on the final delivery in Muscat.

Quick facts

Winner
India
Score
339 for 9
Venue
Al Amerat Cricket Ground, Muscat
Date
Saturday

Needing eight runs from the final over, Mohit Ulva struck a boundary off the last ball to seal a dramatic win against Pakistan at the Al Amerat Cricket Ground. Ulva finished unbeaten on 49 off 26 deliveries, securing the championship for India after a high-stakes 50-over battle.

The victory marks a significant turnaround for the Indian side, which appeared headed for defeat at 252 for eight in the 42nd over. This result carries weight as it denies Pakistan a successful title defense, ending the tournament for the previously unbeaten defending champions.

According to Dawn, Pakistan set a daunting target of 337 for nine, fueled by a century from Usman Khan and solid half-centuries from Abdul Qadir and Farhan Yousaf. India’s pursuit began with a 95-run opening partnership between captain Lakshya Raichandani, who scored 80, and Aaryavir Sehwag, who added 58. Despite regular wickets by Pakistani bowlers Rizwanullah and Niqab Shafiq, Manal Chauhan’s quick 47 kept the pressure on. The final shift came when Ulva and Chigurupati Venkata combined for an unbroken 30-run stand to reach the winning total of 339 for nine.

Following the final, pundits Dinesh Karthik and Murali Kartik highlighted the performance on Cricbuzz Live, noting that the victory underscores the current depth of young talent emerging within the Indian cricket system.
