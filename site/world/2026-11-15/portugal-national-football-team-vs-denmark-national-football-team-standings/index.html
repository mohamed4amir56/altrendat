Rasmus Hojlund scores twice as Denmark routs Portugal in Nations League clash

By Karim Fouad · Published 15 Nov 2026, 05:01 UTC

Denmark secured a 4-1 victory over Portugal in Braga as the hosts played their first match following Cristiano Ronaldo's international retirement.

Quick facts

Final Score
Portugal 1-4 Denmark
Location
Braga
Next Match Date
Tuesday, 17 November

The atmosphere in Braga was set for a tribute to the retired legend, with fans holding up 'GOAT' signs and players wearing 'Ronaldo 7' shirts, but the reality on the pitch was a total dismantling of the home side. The scoreboard flashed a tribute in the seventh minute to honor the captain, yet by the 75th minute, the visitors had established a dominant 4-0 lead that silenced the home crowd.

This loss marks a difficult transition for Portugal, who are adjusting to life without their record-breaking striker. Despite the defeat, Portugal remains in a position to secure the top spot in their group, provided they can secure a result in their final upcoming fixture. For Denmark, the victory keeps their momentum alive as they trail behind the Portuguese in the group standings.

According to the BBC, the scoring started with Pierre-Emile Hojbjerg, who converted a penalty just before the interval following a handball by Ruben Dias. Rasmus Hojlund then took control of the match, netting two goals in an eight-minute flurry shortly after the restart. Joachim Andersen extended the lead to four before Bernardo Silva provided a late consolation goal for the hosts in the third minute of added time.

Portugal will travel to Cardiff on Tuesday, 17 November, to face Wales, needing to avoid a loss to finish as group winners. Denmark, currently three points behind the group leaders, will host Norway on the same day in their final group stage test.
