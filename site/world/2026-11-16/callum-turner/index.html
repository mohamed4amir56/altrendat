Amazon MGM Studios officially names Robert Pattinson as the new James Bond

By Karim Fouad · Published 16 Nov 2026, 16:31 UTC

Robert Pattinson has been confirmed by Amazon MGM Studios as the successor to Daniel Craig for the legendary secret agent role.

Quick facts

Robert Pattinson

Expected Release Window
2028

Following years of speculation and reports concerning various British actors, the search for the next 007 has concluded with the official announcement that Robert Pattinson will take on the mantle. The 40-year-old actor expressed his gratitude to the team at Amazon MGM Studios, calling the opportunity to play the iconic spy the honor of a lifetime. Pattinson succeeds Daniel Craig, who departed the franchise following the 2021 release of No Time To Die. While other names, including Callum Turner and Jack Lowden, had been circulating as potential candidates, the studio has solidified its choice with Pattinson. This transition marks the next chapter for a series that represents a significant investment for Amazon, which previously acquired the rights to the franchise from the Broccoli family for over $1 billion. For audiences, the casting news clarifies the direction of the film series, which has been dormant since the release of the final Craig-led installment five years ago. Despite the excitement surrounding the announcement, fans will likely need to exercise patience regarding the next theatrical release. Current reports indicate that a new film is not expected to arrive in cinemas until at least 2028, partly due to the production schedule of director Denis Villeneuve. Villeneuve, who is also working on the upcoming film Dune: Part Three, is expected to focus on scripts and development throughout 2027. While no official release date has been confirmed, industry tracking suggests that two slots in the Amazon release calendar, specifically April 21 and August 4, 2028, have been reserved for an untitled event film, providing potential targets for the return of the world's most famous spy.
