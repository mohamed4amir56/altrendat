Canada’s nationwide emergency alert system blares during second test of 2026

By Karim Fouad · Published 16 Nov 2026, 19:01 UTC

Canada will conduct a nationwide test of the Alert Ready emergency notification system across all provinces and territories on Wednesday, Nov. 18.

Quick facts

Date of test
Wednesday, Nov. 18
Scope
Nationwide across all provinces and territories
2026 alert count
More than 1,215
Device coverage
TV, radio, and LTE/5G wireless devices

More than 1,215 emergency alert messages have already been broadcast across Canada so far in 2026. This Wednesday, residents will hear the familiar signal again as the country performs its second system-wide test of the year.

The Alert Ready system is a critical tool for public safety, designed to warn Canadians about immediate threats such as tornadoes, floods, wildfires, and Amber Alerts. By running these exercises, authorities aim to ensure that television, radio, and mobile infrastructures function correctly so that citizens are familiar with the alarm tone when a real emergency occurs.

According to Global News and CTV News, the test will occur in every province and territory throughout the day. The alert will transmit to all mobile devices connected to LTE or 5G networks, as well as via traditional TV and radio broadcasts. Officials emphasize that the incoming messages will clearly identify themselves as tests, requiring no action from the public.

Participation in the Alert Ready system is mandatory, and individuals cannot opt out of receiving these test notifications. These drills are a routine procedure for the national network, which typically schedules check-ups twice annually during the months of May and November to maintain operational readiness.
