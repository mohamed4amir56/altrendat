Ja'Marr Chase dominates Steelers with 231 receiving yards in Bengals 38-18 victory

By Karim Fouad · Published 16 Nov 2026, 18:31 UTC

Cincinnati wide receiver Ja'Marr Chase hauled in 231 yards during a dominant 38-18 win over the Pittsburgh Steelers in Week 10 of the 2026 season.

Ja'Marr Chase proved unstoppable against the Pittsburgh Steelers, racking up 231 yards on 11 receptions to power the Cincinnati Bengals offense. His massive performance headlined a night where the Bengals offense surged to a 38-18 win, securing the team a firm grip on first place in the AFC North. Quarterback Joe Burrow orchestrated the attack with precision, throwing for 409 yards and four touchdowns throughout the contest.

This explosive offensive display resonated deeply with Bengals safety Bryan Cook, who previously faced this receiving duo while playing for the Kansas City Chiefs. Cook, a two-time Super Bowl champion who joined Cincinnati this season, noted after the game that he finds the current Bengals offensive unit even more intimidating than when he competed against them in 2022. He specifically highlighted Burrow's poise in the pocket and the automatic chemistry developing between the quarterback and his primary targets.

The victory also served as a milestone for the Bengals' signal-caller. By recording at least four passing touchdowns, Joe Burrow matched Peyton Manning as only the second quarterback in NFL history to accomplish that feat in five consecutive games. While teammate Tee Higgins contributed 66 yards on five catches, it was the explosive connection with Chase that consistently dismantled the Steelers' defensive efforts.

For the Bengals, the win marks a significant turning point in their season trajectory. The team now sits at the top of the AFC North standings as they move forward into the remainder of the 2026 schedule with an offense that veteran defenders are calling the most dangerous they have encountered.
