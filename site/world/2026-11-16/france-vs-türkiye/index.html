France clinches Nations League quarter-final spot after 2-1 victory over Turkiye

By Karim Fouad · Published 16 Nov 2026, 05:01 UTC

France secured the top spot in Group A1 of the UEFA Nations League by defeating Turkiye 2-1 at the Matmut Atlantique.

Quick facts

Match result
France 2-1 Turkiye
Venue
Matmut Atlantique, Bordeaux
France goal scorers
Emirhan Topcu (own goal), Eli Junior Kroupi
Turkey goal scorer
Abdulkerim Bardakci
Group A1 standing
France first, Turkey bottom

Turkey finished their Nations League campaign with a record of six defeats from six matches, concluding Group A1 at the bottom of the table. Despite the late effort from Abdulkerim Bardakci, who headed in a corner from Arda Guler during injury time, the visitors were unable to recover from their earlier deficits.

This result is significant as it confirms France's qualification for the Nations League quarter-finals, positioning them at the top of their group ahead of Belgium. For France, the match demonstrated their tactical endurance as they successfully broke down a disciplined low-block defensive structure that had frustrated the hosts throughout the opening half.

According to the BBC, France took the lead through an own goal by Emirhan Topcu following a French corner. Bournemouth forward Eli Junior Kroupi, making his second appearance for the national side, extended the lead to 2-0 after coming off the bench to convert a through ball from Rayan Cherki. Goal.com reports that manager Zinedine Zidane utilized a flexible 4-2-3-1 formation, which transitioned into a 2-3-5 during attacking phases to stretch the Turkish defense. The French side maintained consistent territorial dominance throughout the match in Bordeaux, pinning their opponents deep into their own defensive third while utilizing wide rotations to create scoring opportunities.

France now advances to the quarter-final stage of the competition, while Turkey exits the tournament following the conclusion of the group phase.
