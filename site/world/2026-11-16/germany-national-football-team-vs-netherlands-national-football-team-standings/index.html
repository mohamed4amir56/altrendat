Germany Routs Netherlands 5-0 to Snatch Top Spot in Nations League Group

By Karim Fouad · Published 16 Nov 2026, 22:32 UTC

Germany clinched first place in Group A2 after defeating the Netherlands 5-0 in their final Nations League group stage match.

Quick facts

Final Score
Germany 5-0 Netherlands
Group A2 Points
11
Venue
Berlin

The 5-0 victory marked Germany's most decisive win against their neighbours since a 7-0 result back in 1959. By securing this margin, Jurgen Klopp's side overcame a three-way tie at 11 points to leapfrog both the Netherlands and Greece in the group standings, as the Dutch would have remained in first place had the scoreline been only 4-0. The match in Berlin saw four different goalscorers, with Florian Wirtz netting twice to lead the offensive effort.

This result carries significant weight for Germany's momentum, as both teams have successfully qualified for the upcoming quarter-finals. For the players, the performance represents a tactical success in their goal to dominate opponents and play attacking football, which Wirtz described as a great step in the team's ongoing development. Conversely, it serves as the first defeat for the Netherlands under the management of Xavi Hernandez.

The scoring began when Derry Scherhant struck in the 41st minute, followed by Wirtz, who struck from outside the box for the second. Nico Schlotterbeck added a header from a Brajan Gruda free-kick to extend the lead to 3-0. Wirtz then secured his second goal of the evening before Gruda rounded out the scoring at 5-0, capping off a display where he also contributed two assists.

Following the conclusion of this group stage fixture, both nations move forward to the quarter-final round scheduled for next year.
