Bowen Yang returns to SNL as Jensen Huang in tech mogul spoof

By Karim Fouad · Published 16 Nov 2026, 11:31 UTC

Former SNL cast member Bowen Yang played former Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang in a cold open sketch featuring tech billionaires at CIA headquarters.

Quick facts

Host
John Mulaney
Actor playing Jensen Huang
Bowen Yang

Former cast member Bowen Yang returned to the Saturday Night Live stage to play Jensen Huang, labeling himself as Jensen "Hide Your Wives" Huang in a sketch that took aim at tech industry moguls. The performance arrived during an episode hosted by John Mulaney, who appeared as a CIA officer briefing billionaire executives on national security threats. The sketch focused on the recent news of Huang's personal life, specifically claims regarding an affair with an alleged Chinese spy.

This comedic depiction highlights the ongoing public attention toward the personal conduct of high-profile technology executives. By framing the tech leaders as a group requiring instruction on how to avoid "honey traps," the show used satire to underscore the intersection of private relationships and state intelligence security.

The sketch featured an ensemble of actors portraying major figures, including James Austin Johnson as Peter Thiel, Ben Marshall as Sam Altman, Jane Wickline as Dario Amodei, Jeremy Culhane as Elon Musk, and Andrew Dismukes as Mark Zuckerberg. According to The New York Times and Mashable, the writers used the premise to address the broader risks of intelligence leaks, with characters offering their own cynical takeaways on dating while under the scrutiny of national security officials.

Following his appearance as Huang, Bowen Yang continued the episode by participating in an elaborate musical sketch tribute to Central Park, performing in his costume from the Broadway production Oh, Mary! Viewers can access the full cold open on the show's official platforms to see the sketches covering the tech mogul satire as well as additional segments regarding recent national infrastructure outages.
