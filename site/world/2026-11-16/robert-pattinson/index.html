Robert Pattinson Officially Clinches Role of Legendary Secret Agent James Bond

By Karim Fouad · Published 16 Nov 2026, 15:31 UTC

Amazon MGM Studios confirmed Robert Pattinson as the next 007 actor, succeeding Daniel Craig's five-movie run.

Robert Pattinson described the role as the honour of a lifetime, officially confirming his transition into the iconic suit of the world's most famous spy. The announcement ends months of intense industry speculation regarding who would secure the keys to the Aston Martin following the conclusion of Daniel Craig's tenure. Pattinson expressed deep gratitude to leadership at Amazon MGM Studios, citing the casting as a dream come true. The actor, known for his work in Batman and Odyssey, enters the franchise as it undergoes a new chapter under Amazon MGM Studios' control. This development marks a significant shift for the series, as fans and critics have spent months debating the identity of the next secret agent. For the production team, the casting solidifies the direction of the brand as they look toward future projects featuring the character. Pattinson stated he is excited about the work ahead and the partnership with the studio, signaling he is prepared to take on the mantle previously held by Craig for five films. The actor’s appointment brings a conclusion to the search for a successor, effectively closing a period of uncertainty for the long-running cinematic series. While the casting is now official, further details regarding upcoming production timelines or specific film titles remain to be disclosed by the studio team.
