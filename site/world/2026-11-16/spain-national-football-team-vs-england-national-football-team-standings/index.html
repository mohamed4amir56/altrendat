England Stuns World Champions Spain In 3-2 Nations League Thriller Victory

By Karim Fouad · Published 16 Nov 2026, 05:01 UTC · Updated 16 Nov 2026, 05:31 UTC

England ended Spain's 43-game unbeaten run with a 3-2 victory at the Metropolitano in Sunday's Nations League clash.

Quick facts

Final Score
Spain 2-3 England
Venue
Metropolitano Stadium, Madrid
England Scorers
Harry Kane, Ezri Konsa, Ben White
Spain Scorers
Mikel Oyarzabal, Nico Williams

James Trafford’s dramatic double save in stoppage time secured the result, as he denied Fermin Lopez and Mikel Oyarzabal to preserve a narrow one-goal lead. The victory marks the finest night of head coach Thomas Tuchel’s tenure, snapping Spain’s impressive 43-game unbeaten streak and proving the squad could thrive under high-pressure conditions in Madrid. England took a commanding position early with a Harry Kane penalty followed by goals from Ezri Konsa and Ben White within the first 12 minutes of the second half.

For England, the match served as a critical test of character and tactical flexibility under Tuchel. The decision to start Leeds goalkeeper James Trafford ahead of long-term incumbent Jordan Pickford paid off significantly, with Trafford’s composure under fire helping the side withstand a furious Spanish comeback. The win provides a vital confidence boost for the squad as they look ahead to the Nations League last eight.

According to BBC, the game turned chaotic after England’s Elliot Anderson was sent off in the 65th minute for a foul on Lamine Yamal. This error, compounded by a defensive lapse from Jarell Quansah, allowed Spain to rally through goals from Mikel Oyarzabal and Nico Williams. Despite being forced to defend with ten men for the final half-hour, England held firm, with Sky Sports reporting that Trafford’s reflexes were the decisive factor in denying Spain’s late surge. Tuchel remains committed to competition for the goalkeeper position, stating there is no official No 1 at this stage.
