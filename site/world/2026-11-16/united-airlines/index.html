United Airlines ground system outage prevents aircraft from receiving critical takeoff data

By Karim Fouad · Published 16 Nov 2026, 22:32 UTC

United Airlines flights faced delays Monday as a widespread system outage stopped dispatch data from reaching departing aircraft.

Quick facts

Incident Day
Monday
Issue
Systemwide outage affecting takeoff data transmission
Company
United Airlines

A pilot in Houston requested permission to remain at the gate until the airline's systems were restored, as aircraft were unable to receive essential weight, balance, and performance data required for departure. The disruption prevented dispatch from transmitting the necessary figures for safe takeoff, leading to aircraft remaining at gates or sitting on the tarmac while teams worked to troubleshoot the technical issue. While flights currently in the air were unaffected, the outage halted the departure process for grounded planes. The airline acknowledged the widespread technical failure on social media and stated that teams were working to resolve the issue as soon as possible. This technology failure directly impacts travelers' schedules, forcing passengers to wait for the transmission of data that serves as a mandatory safety requirement for flight operations. According to NBC 5 Chicago, United Airlines confirmed they are actively troubleshooting the technology issue. Air traffic control audio reported that the outage was affecting systems across the network, with no estimated time provided for when the transmission of takeoff data would be fully restored. Passengers are advised to monitor their flight status through the United mobile app or the official airline website. This incident follows a separate, rare nationwide ground stop caused by a router issue during maintenance that disrupted air traffic control communications just days earlier. While carriers were already working to restore normal schedules from that previous disruption, this new internal system failure has created further delays for United passengers on Monday.
