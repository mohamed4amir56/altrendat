TV host Will Best and Dianne Buswell exit Strictly ahead of Blackpool

By Karim Fouad · Published 16 Nov 2026, 06:02 UTC

Will Best and his professional partner Dianne Buswell were eliminated from Strictly Come Dancing in the seventh dance-off of the series.

Quick facts

Eliminated couple
Will Best and Dianne Buswell
Elimination status
Seventh couple to leave
Next appearance
Monday at 6.30pm on BBC Two
Next episode
Saturday at 6.35pm on BBC One

After securing 27 points for their paso doble during Saturday's live broadcast, Will Best and Dianne Buswell were sent home by three of the four judges. They faced actress Jaime Winstone and Carlos Gu in the dance-off, who had previously placed third on the leaderboard with a score of 33 for their Charleston. While judge Anton Du Beke voted to save the pair, Motsi Mabuse, Craig Revel Horwood, and head judge Shirley Ballas elected to keep Winstone in the competition. This decision marks the end of Best's time on the program just one week before the show's trip to the Blackpool Tower Ballroom. The elimination follows a performance that judge Revel Horwood described as 'plonky' during the Saturday show. Best, who has recently become a father, had been praised earlier in the series as one of the show's most consistent dancers. For those following the journey of the remaining contestants, the competition continues next week at the iconic Tower Ballroom venue, where the remaining couples will compete for a spot further in the series. Best and Buswell are scheduled to appear on the spin-off show Strictly – It Takes Two to discuss their departure. The couple's exit leaves Winstone to advance, having survived her fourth dance-off in seven weeks despite judges noting her latest routine was below par. The results show also featured a group number inspired by fantasy themes and a performance by musician Sebastian 'Bash' Croft.
