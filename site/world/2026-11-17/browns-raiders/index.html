NFL Flexes Raiders and Browns Into Sunday Night Football Prime Time Slot

By Karim Fouad · Published 17 Nov 2026, 19:31 UTC

The NFL has officially moved the Raiders-Browns game to the Sunday Night Football broadcast for Week 12, replacing the Patriots-Chargers matchup.

Quick facts

Announcement Date
Tuesday, Nov. 17
New Prime Time Matchup
Las Vegas Raiders at Cleveland Browns
Moved Game Time
4:05 p.m. ET

The Raiders and Browns have officially secured the Sunday Night Football spotlight for Week 12, marking the first major prime-time schedule shift for the NFL this season. The league announced the move on Tuesday, Nov. 17, as it sought a more compelling replacement for the initially scheduled Patriots-Chargers game. The Patriots-Chargers contest will now shift to a 4:05 p.m. ET kickoff, while the Titans-Jaguars game moves earlier in the day to 1 p.m. ET. This change highlights the league's proactive approach to optimizing its prime-time audience, shifting away from a game involving the struggling Patriots and the Chargers. The Patriots, currently navigating a challenging season, face significant roster concerns, including the likely absence of starting quarterback Drake Maye due to a suspected broken ankle suffered in Munich. Meanwhile, the Browns look to capitalize on this rare prime-time opportunity as they continue to mount a potential playoff push. For the Raiders, the move places the spotlight squarely on No. 1 overall pick Fernando Mendoza, who recently made his starting debut during a loss to the Seattle Seahawks. As both teams prepare for the Sunday night stage, the adjustment represents the most significant scheduling modification made by the NFL during the current campaign. The league's decision to flex these specific teams underscores their commitment to prioritizing games with greater competitive intrigue as the playoff race intensifies during the final stretch of the season.
