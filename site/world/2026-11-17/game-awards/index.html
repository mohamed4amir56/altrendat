Grand Theft Auto 6 Eligible for Game of the Year Awards 2026

By Karim Fouad · Published 17 Nov 2026, 17:31 UTC

The Game Awards 2026 will announce nominees on November 17, just two days before the official release of Grand Theft Auto 6.

Gamers will have exactly one day between the arrival of Grand Theft Auto 6 on November 19 and the eligibility cutoff for The Game Awards 2026, set for November 20 at 9 p.m. PT. Because the highly anticipated title launches on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X just before the deadline, it officially qualifies for consideration in the major categories, including the prestigious Game of the Year award.

For the industry, the inclusion of such a major release in the awards cycle highlights the competitive nature of the year-end honors. The nomination reveal provides a vital look at which studios and talent will contend for the top prizes, with voting for fan-based awards opening immediately following the announcement special.

According to Polygon, host Geoff Keighley will lead the 10-minute nomination stream on Tuesday, November 17, at 9 a.m. PST. Viewers can tune in via YouTube and Twitch to see the full list of nominees, with Game of the Year candidates expected to be revealed in the final moments of the broadcast.

The full awards ceremony follows on December 10 at 4:30 p.m. PST. Organizers are expanding the reach of the event significantly this year, adding platforms such as Disney Plus, Roblox, and Live TV on PS5 to the existing list of global streaming partners. Fans in China, South Korea, Japan, and across Southeast Asia will also have localized access to the show through various regional platforms.
