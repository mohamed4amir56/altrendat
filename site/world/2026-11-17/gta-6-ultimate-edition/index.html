Grand Theft Auto VI Smashes Records With Over $1 Billion Pre-Sale Total

By Karim Fouad · Published 17 Nov 2026, 08:02 UTC

Grand Theft Auto VI launches this Thursday, November 19, following over 11 million unit pre-sales on Sony and Microsoft digital stores.

Quick facts

Release date
November 19
Pre-sale revenue
Over $1 billion
Pre-sold units
More than 11 million
Pre-order status
Most pre-ordered game in PlayStation and Xbox digital store history

More than 11 million units of Grand Theft Auto VI have already been sold, cementing the title as the most pre-ordered video game in the history of both PlayStation and Xbox digital stores. This massive pre-sale figure has already eclipsed the $1 billion mark before the official global launch.

The intense demand surrounding this release represents a significant test for global digital infrastructure. Cybersecurity expert Arun Vishwanath noted that while many users have already pre-loaded the game, the primary concern for service providers is managing the massive surge in traffic when millions of players attempt to go live simultaneously on Thursday. Local, regional, and international network backbones have been preparing for this load, though experts anticipate possible pockets of connectivity issues as users flood the servers.

According to Audacy, the excitement stems from years of development at Rockstar Games, marking a major jump in technology since the 2013 release of GTA V, which went on to sell 230 million copies. The scale of the launch has led many to categorize the game as the biggest entertainment event of the year, outstripping major film releases.

As the industry waits for the game to go live on November 19, infrastructure providers remain on alert for potential backend shortages. While experts believe current capacity is largely prepared for the influx of players, the actual performance of the internet during the peak download window remains the final, critical variable in this historic rollout.
