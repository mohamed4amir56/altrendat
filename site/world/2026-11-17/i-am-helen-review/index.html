Nicola Coughlan stars in harrowing look at toxic manosphere and domestic control

By Karim Fouad · Published 17 Nov 2026, 07:02 UTC

Nicola Coughlan plays a woman trapped in an increasingly abusive relationship in the new two-part Channel 4 drama I Am Helen.

Quick facts

Nicola Coughlan

Two-part drama
Runtime

Nearly 40 per cent of women aged 18 to 39 have been choked during sex, a reality that takes center stage in the latest installment of Dominic Savage’s anthology series. The drama opens with Helen, played by Nicola Coughlan, and her partner Steve in their bedroom. What begins as a routine intimacy shifts into a moment of violence when Steve begins to choke her, ignoring her struggle and vocal refusal. When Helen objects, Steve dismisses the incident as an attempt to have fun and never offers an apology, despite Helen eventually apologizing herself for the conflict. This central moment serves as the catalyst for a 90-minute examination of how consent and communication fracture in a modern relationship. The film explores why Helen remains in the relationship with Steve, who is revealed to be grappling with a porn addiction and the influence of colleagues who promote extreme "manosphere" ideologies. These influences push Steve toward increasingly aggressive behaviors, including smashing household objects and asserting a need for control over his partner. According to The Guardian, the film is designed to provoke reflection on the widening gulf between reality, fantasy, and the expectations placed on women in domestic life. The Telegraph notes that the production, created through improvised performances, functions as a warning about the dangers of staying in toxic environments. By following the couple's five-year history, the show illustrates the transition from a seemingly happy partnership to one defined by menace and coercion. The two-part series is now available to viewers on Channel 4.
