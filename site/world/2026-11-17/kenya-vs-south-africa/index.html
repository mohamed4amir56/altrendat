Harambee Stars Battle Bafana Bafana in Pivotal 2027 AFCON Qualifying Match

By Karim Fouad · Published 17 Nov 2026, 16:01 UTC

Kenya hosts South Africa at Nyayo National Stadium on Tuesday, 17 November, in the second of their back-to-back AFCON qualifying fixtures.

Quick facts

Date
Tuesday, 17 November
Venue
Nyayo National Stadium, Nairobi
Group D Status
South Africa 2nd (7 points), Kenya 3rd (2 points)

South Africa enters the contest holding second place in Group D with seven points from three matches, while Kenya sits third with only two points from draws against South Africa and Eritrea. Guinea currently leads the group standings with nine points, making every point critical for the sides looking to improve their position in the qualifying race. Harambee Stars head coach Benni McCarthy faces his home country for the second time in five days, aiming to secure a vital win for Kenya as he navigates the tactical challenge set by South Africa’s coach, Pitso Mosimane. The two coaches have a history of competitive meetings, having each recorded three victories and three draws against one another across their careers. This match at Nyayo National Stadium offers a direct opportunity for either side to gain an advantage in both the qualifying group and their ongoing coaching rivalry. A focal point of the match will be the rematch between Kenyan striker Michael Olunga and South African defender Ime Okon. Olunga successfully converted a penalty during the first leg in Johannesburg, marking his 35th goal of the season, and he remains the key target for the Kenyan attack. Conversely, Okon is tasked with neutralizing the Kenyan captain to preserve South Africa's defensive record. Both teams remain focused on the potential implications of the result for their standing in the 2027 Africa Cup of Nations qualification process.
