Nvidia Shares Plunge 4.1% Ahead Of High-Stakes Earnings Report Following Leadership Shakeup

By Karim Fouad · Published 17 Nov 2026, 11:01 UTC

US stock futures fell by 0.4% on Tuesday as traders braced for Nvidia's quarterly earnings report.

Nvidia stock pulled back 4.1% as investors offloaded shares before the company's fiscal third-quarter results arrive Tuesday after the bell. This earnings event marks a significant milestone as interim CEO Colette Kress prepares to address the market following the abrupt resignation of former leader Jensen Huang last week. The drop in value occurred amid a wider slump that saw Dow Jones Industrial Average, S&P 500, and Nasdaq-100 futures all slip 0.4% during early trading sessions.

This week carries high stakes for the broader AI trade as Wall Street closely monitors the chipmaker's performance. The market remains sensitive to these updates, especially as investors analyze 13F filings to track recent institutional moves into semiconductor and chip materials firms like Lattice Semiconductor and Qnity Electronics. The outcome of Nvidia's report is expected to set the tone for tech sector sentiment throughout the week.

Beyond the chip industry, other major companies are set to influence market direction. Disney will report results before the bell with growth expected in its parks and streaming segments, while The Home Depot provides insight into ongoing consumer spending trends for home projects. Additionally, investors are anticipating a series of economic updates including retail sales figures, export data, and a housing market index. Markets are also looking ahead to Thursday, when Anthropic is scheduled to hold its IPO, an event described as one of the week's most significant developments alongside the ongoing Nvidia earnings focus.
