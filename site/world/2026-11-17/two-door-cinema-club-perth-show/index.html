Two Door Cinema Club abruptly cancels Perth show mid-set after illness strikes

By Karim Fouad · Published 17 Nov 2026, 09:31 UTC

The Northern Irish rock band stopped their Sunday night performance at Perth HPC after lead singer Alex Trimble became too ill to continue.

Quick facts

Venue
Perth HPC
Day
Sunday
Tour
15th Anniversary Tour

Fans stood in confusion for 30 to 45 minutes at the Perth HPC venue on Sunday night after the band abruptly exited the stage. The silence finally broke when band members Sam Halliday and Kevin Baird returned to confirm the remainder of the concert would be cancelled. Baird apologized to the crowd, stating that they had exhausted all efforts to get the lead singer back on stage but that Trimble remained unable to perform. The show was intended to kick off the Australian leg of the band's 15th-anniversary tour for their debut album, Tourist History. Many attendees have since turned to social media to express disappointment and demand information regarding potential ticket refunds. Some concertgoers suggested on Reddit that the singer may have been suffering from vocal strain or heat exhaustion, though the band has yet to provide further updates on his condition. While some fans noted that about 40 minutes of the set remained when the band left, the group has not officially addressed compensation requests as of Monday afternoon. The band is scheduled to continue the remaining dates of their Australian tour despite the mid-show cancellation. Attendees remain waiting for official communication from the organizers regarding a possible reschedule or partial refund for the incomplete event.
