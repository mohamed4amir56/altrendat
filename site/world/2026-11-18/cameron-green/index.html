Cam Green retires hurt after ball strikes helmet in Hobart ODI clash

By Karim Fouad · Published 18 Nov 2026, 10:31 UTC

Cameron Green left the field during Australia's chase against England at Bellerive Oval after suffering a mouth injury from a bouncer.

Quick facts

Venue
Bellerive Oval
Match Type
One-day international
Target score
329
Substitute player
Ollie Peake

The ball hammered directly into the helmet's grille as Cameron Green attempted to pull a delivery from England seamer Gus Atkinson. The impact left the West Australian bleeding from his mouth, forcing him to retire hurt after scoring three runs from 10 deliveries while batting at No. 6. He gave a thumbs up to the crowd upon leaving the field at Bellerive Oval, but was later subbed out of the match by Ollie Peake and took no further part in the contest.

This injury is a significant setback for Australia, as the team faced an imposing chase of 329 to avoid a 2-1 series defeat to England. For Green, the incident marks another physical challenge in a recent string of setbacks. The all-rounder had previously struggled with shoulder soreness during tours of South Africa and Zimbabwe, which prevented him from bowling, and also endured an adductor injury while batting during an earlier series against the Proteas.

Following his return as a specialist batsman for the third Test against South Africa, Green had continued in that capacity for the current ODI series. His premature exit in Hobart hampered Australia's momentum during a critical run chase, further testing their depth against a disciplined English bowling attack. As of the conclusion of the match, the team has not issued a detailed medical update regarding his condition or expected recovery timeline, leaving fans and selectors awaiting further news on his availability for upcoming fixtures.
