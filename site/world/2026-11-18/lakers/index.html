Lakers Trail Hornets After Rough Opening Quarter With Luka Dončić In Foul Trouble

By Karim Fouad · Published 18 Nov 2026, 05:31 UTC

The Los Angeles Lakers trail the Charlotte Hornets 34-30 after the first quarter on November 17 as Luka Dončić struggles with early fouls.

Luka Dončić finished the opening quarter with only nine points, shooting 33.3% from the field and missing all six of his 3-point attempts as the Hornets built a 34-30 lead. The Lakers' superstar faced suffocating defense from Sion James throughout the first 10 minutes and found himself in immediate foul trouble after being called for an off-arm shove during a failed challenge by head coach JJ Redick. With Austin Reaves and Walker Kessler both sidelined for this contest, the pressure is mounting for the Lakers to adjust their rotation at the five-spot, utilizing Kevon Looney, Sandro Mamukelashvili, and Jarred Vanderbilt to compensate for the missing depth.

Managing this deficit is critical for a Lakers squad aiming to extend their eight-game winning streak. While Los Angeles holds an impressive 12-2 record on the season, they have recently struggled with maintaining leads, including a notable instance where they allowed a 31-point lead to slip away against the Phoenix Suns. Resolving these defensive lapses and rebounding concerns is essential if they hope to continue their current momentum in the West.

For the Lakers, this game serves as a test of their depth and resilience without Kessler, who has been a key contributor. The Hornets enter the matchup with a 4-9 record but pose a legitimate threat from the perimeter, as four of their five starters are capable of stretching the floor. Los Angeles must now pivot to contain Charlotte’s high-volume shooting and backcourt duo of Brandon Miller and Coby White to avoid a home loss that would break their current streak.
