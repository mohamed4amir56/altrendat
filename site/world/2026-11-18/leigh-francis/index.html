Comedian Leigh Francis has joined the cast of the 24th series of I’m a Celebrity…Get Me Out of Here! which premiered on November 16.

Leigh Francis hopes to amuse viewers by steering his puke onto presenters Ant and Dec during the show's infamous eating challenges. The comedian revealed that his wife, Jill, encouraged him to join the reality show after he turned 50 to help him become more open to new experiences. He claims he has no fear of the dietary tasks and previously prepared for the outdoors by camping in his garden and spending childhood holidays in a static caravan in Bridlington. While Francis is eager to have a laugh in the jungle, critics suggest that his status as a late addition and a divisive figure with viewers could hinder his success, with predictions placing him in 12th position. The show remains a staple of the festive television season, featuring a diverse cast of celebrities competing to be crowned the king or queen of the jungle. As the 24th series progresses, contestants face grueling trials involving snakes, spiders, and marsupial extremities. The participation of the former Celebrity Juice host adds a new dynamic to the competition, with Francis embracing the opportunity to step outside his comfort zone despite his history of declining such offers. His entry follows reported speculation about his involvement, confirming his place among the group of contestants currently enduring the Australian wilderness under the watchful eyes of Ant and Dec. The series, which kicked off on November 16, continues to draw attention as cast members attempt to balance career ambitions with the realities of jungle living. Viewers can track the progress of Francis and his fellow contestants as they navigate the physical and mental challenges designed to test their endurance throughout the coming weeks.
