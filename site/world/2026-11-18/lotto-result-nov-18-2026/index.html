Ultra Lotto jackpot hits 549 million as no tickets match numbers

By Karim Fouad · Published 18 Nov 2026, 17:05 UTC

The Philippine Charity Sweepstakes Office announced no winners for the P549,576,298.64 Ultra Lotto 6/58 jackpot drawn on November 17, 2026.

The winning combination of 51-56-30-20-29-34 failed to produce a single ticket holder who could claim the massive P549,576,298.64 prize. This result extends the duration of the current jackpot rollover period for the Ultra Lotto 6/58, which has seen no successful top-prize winners since May 5, 2026. On that previous occasion, a group of 10 bettors collectively claimed a jackpot worth P76,305,973.15. For frequent participants, these growing jackpot figures represent the primary draw of the game, as the prize continues to accumulate with every draw that goes unclaimed. According to the Philippine Charity Sweepstakes Office, the scarcity of winners highlights the mathematical difficulty of correctly matching all six numbers in the current format, as evidenced by the high number of previous draws resulting in no jackpot payouts. Beyond the Ultra Lotto, other games also saw no winners during the recent cycle. The Superlotto 6/49 draw, featuring the combination 07-49-42-38-10-31, went unclaimed, leaving a jackpot of P114,215,480.54 on the table. Similarly, the Lotto 6/42 game resulted in no winners for its P23,013,429.70 prize, after the winning numbers were revealed as 16-28-35-29-31-36. These cumulative jackpots remain available for future draws as the Philippine Charity Sweepstakes Office continues its regular schedule of operations. Players often look to historical data for patterns, noting that earlier in the year, specifically on March 17, a long-time bettor of 25 years successfully secured a P100.331 million jackpot as the sole winner of that specific round.
