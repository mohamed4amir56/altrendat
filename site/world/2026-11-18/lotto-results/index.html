13 New Millionaires Crowned As Two Players Snatch Lotto Jackpot Millions

By Karim Fouad · Published 18 Nov 2026, 21:31 UTC

Two ticket-holders shared a £5.1 million jackpot while 11 others won £1 million each in Wednesday's Lotto draw.

Thirteen players became millionaires in a single night after the draw results confirmed the jackpot split and secondary prize wins. Among the winning numbers, 13 itself was drawn, marking what Andy Carter, senior winners' adviser at Allwyn, described as an incredible outcome for participants.

For lottery players, the results determine the distribution of a total £5.1 million jackpot prize. Two ticket-holders successfully matched all six main numbers to claim £2,590,876 each, while 11 additional players matched five main numbers and the bonus ball to secure £1 million each. The significant number of top-tier winners highlights a shift from the previous Wednesday, where only one player secured a £2.8 million jackpot and two players won £1 million.

According to Newswav, the winning Lotto numbers for the first round were 04, 05, 13, 18, 25 and 29, with 16 as the bonus ball. The second round numbers were 06, 26, 38, 53, 57 and 59, with 21 as the bonus. The Thunderball numbers were 02, 15, 22, 31, 34 and the Thunderball 13. While nobody claimed the £500,000 Thunderball top prize, two players matched five numbers for £5,000 each. Lotto HotPicks also saw 22 players match four numbers for £6,500 each, though the £175,000 top prize went unclaimed.

Following this draw, the jackpot prize for the next scheduled Saturday draw is estimated to reach £2 million.
