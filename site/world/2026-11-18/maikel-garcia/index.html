Mariners land Gold Glove infielder Maikel Garcia in massive six-player blockbuster trade

By Karim Fouad · Published 18 Nov 2026, 16:31 UTC

The Seattle Mariners acquired third baseman Maikel Garcia from the Kansas City Royals in a trade involving six players.

Maikel Garcia is owed $52.5 million through 2030, a figure seen as a very reasonable cost for a player under long-term team control. The 26-year-old infielder arrives in Seattle after a 2026 season where he maintained a 1.8 WAR despite missing time due to hand and hamstring injuries. Just one year prior, Garcia recorded a 5.6 WAR season and received MVP votes for his performance at third base.

This deal provides the Mariners with a proven, defensively sound option at a position currently experiencing a league-wide shortage of talent. While Garcia experienced physical setbacks during the 2026 campaign, his defensive profile and established status as a starter offer Seattle stability at third base. The move is viewed by industry observers as a strategic acquisition of a rare, young infielder in an environment where reliable third-base production is increasingly difficult to secure via trade or free agency.

According to reports from ESPN and the Seattle Times, the Royals are set to receive right-handed pitchers Logan Evans and Bryce Miller along with left-handed pitcher Jose Ferrer. The trade effectively moves three arms to Kansas City in exchange for the infield reinforcement headed to the Pacific Northwest. Analysts note that while the park environment for Garcia will change with his move to Seattle, the acquisition remains a calculated bet on his potential to return to his previous peak form. Neither the Mariners nor the Royals have made an official announcement regarding the trade, which stands as the first blockbuster move of the current offseason.
