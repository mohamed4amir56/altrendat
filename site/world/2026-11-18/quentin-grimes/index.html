Quentin Grimes scores career-high 47 points in Lakers overtime comeback win

By Karim Fouad · Published 18 Nov 2026, 05:31 UTC · Updated 18 Nov 2026, 07:32 UTC

Quentin Grimes led the Los Angeles Lakers back from an 18-point deficit to defeat the Charlotte Hornets 118-114 in overtime on Tuesday.

Quentin Grimes erupted for a career-high 47 points, including 23 points in the fourth quarter and overtime, to spark the Los Angeles Lakers' improbable 118-114 comeback victory against the Charlotte Hornets. The Lakers trailed by as many as 18 points during the contest, struggling against a Hornets offense led by Brandon Miller and Naz Reid, who scored 23 points. Despite missing key starters Austin Reaves and Walker Kessler, the Lakers secured their ninth straight win by surging in the final frame.

The comeback was essential for the Lakers, who navigated significant foul trouble for Luka Dončić, who picked up four fouls in the first half alone. With Dončić limited, the Lakers relied on a massive effort on the boards, securing 19 offensive rebounds to generate 19 second-chance points. The victory maintains the team's momentum following a successful four-game road trip, proving the team's resilience despite shooting 41.4% from the field and trailing by seven at halftime.

According to Silver Screen and Roll, Grimes kept the team within reach early by hitting two triples before his massive fourth-quarter performance forced the game into the extra period. Lakers Nation reported that the Hornets, despite being the lowest-ranked offensive team in the NBA, held a 66-59 lead at the half, but could not contain Grimes as he recorded his first 40-point game in a Lakers uniform. The Lakers managed to tighten their defense down the stretch, forcing turnovers that allowed them to tie the game at 104 in the closing minutes.
