Hurricanes eye fifth straight win as league-leading defense hosts Montreal Canadiens

By Karim Fouad · Published 19 Nov 2026, 23:31 UTC

The Carolina Hurricanes carry a four-game win streak into Thursday night's matchup against the visiting Montreal Canadiens at Lenovo Center.

Quick facts

Thursday

Venue

TV Channel
Hurricanes Hockey Network

Pyotr Kochetkov is set to start for the Hurricanes, entering the game having allowed two goals or fewer in 11 of his 13 appearances this season. The team currently boasts a league-low 47 goals allowed, providing a formidable challenge for a Montreal offense that has managed only 11 goals across its previous five contests. Carolina head coach Rod Brind'Amour hopes to build on the positive momentum established during a successful two-game trip to Finland, where the Hurricanes swept the Seattle Kraken. Forward Jackson Blake enters the game looking to extend his career-best point streak to 12 games, having recorded five goals and eight assists since October 20. Forward Eric Robinson also returns to the active lineup after sitting out the team’s last game on Saturday. This matchup serves as the second meeting of the season between the two teams, with Carolina having secured a 6-4 victory in Montreal on October 6. For the visitors, the game marks a return to Raleigh for the first time since the Eastern Conference finals last spring. Montreal arrives in North Carolina following a 3-2 win against the New Jersey Devils on Wednesday night, a victory that snapped a two-game losing skid. Both teams currently sit among the five Eastern Conference clubs that have already reached the 30-point threshold this season. The Hurricanes are expected to dress their full complement of skaters for the second consecutive game as they aim to maintain their current winning pace.
