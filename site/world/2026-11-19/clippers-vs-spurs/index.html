Spurs Target Bounce-Back Victory As Clippers Remain Winless In Road Games

By Karim Fouad · Published 20 Nov 2026, 00:31 UTC · Updated 20 Nov 2026, 01:01 UTC

The 12-3 San Antonio Spurs host the 4-9 Los Angeles Clippers on Thursday at 7PM CT.

The Los Angeles Clippers enter this matchup struggling as one of only two NBA teams yet to secure a road victory this season, maintaining a 0-5 record away from home. In sharp contrast, the San Antonio Spurs return to their home court looking to rebound immediately from a recent loss to Detroit while sporting an impressive 12-3 record.

This game carries significant importance for both franchises as they sit at opposite ends of the defensive spectrum. The Spurs currently boast the NBA's top net rating and the second-ranked defensive efficiency, holding opponents to 107.7 points per 100 possessions. For the Clippers, the challenge is mounting; they rank 23rd in net rating and surrender 118.1 points per 100 possessions, presenting a defensive struggle that analysts suggest provides a distinct edge for the home team.

According to Pounding The Rock, the Clippers have faced persistent issues with predictable passing and an inefficient reliance on screen rolls, averaging only 0.83 points per possession. The Spurs' third-ranked defense specializes in neutralizing lane action, allowing just 44.9 points in the paint. A focal point will be the individual matchup between Victor Wembanyama, who averages 4.1 blocks per game, and Brook Lopez, who remains a threat by converting 80.8 percent of his attempts at the rim.

Looking ahead, both rosters are dealing with notable absences. The Clippers will be without Bradley Beal and Brandon Ingram due to injury, while the Spurs will play without Dylan Harper and Tobias Harris. The game is scheduled for November 19, 2026, and will be broadcast on DAZN with radio coverage available on WOAI 1200 AM.
