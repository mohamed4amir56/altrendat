Taylor Swift Returns to CMA Stage With Surprise Keith Urban Banjo Performance

By Karim Fouad · Published 19 Nov 2026, 05:31 UTC

Taylor Swift marked her first CMA Awards performance in 13 years on November 18, 2026, by singing her Toy Story 5 track and a 2007 classic.

Wearing a white-and-orange frock and cowgirl boots, Taylor Swift channeled the character Jessie from Toy Story for her live performance of "I Knew It, I Knew You" at the 60th Annual CMA Awards in Nashville. The set transformed into a nostalgic mash-up when Keith Urban joined her on the banjo for a rendition of her 2007 track "Our Song." This appearance marked Swift’s first time performing at the ceremony since 2013, when she performed "Red" alongside Vince Gill and Alison Krauss after receiving the Pinnacle Award.

For fans of the country music star, the performance highlights a conscious return to her musical origins. Swift, who last attended the event in 2016 to present the Entertainer of the Year honor to Garth Brooks, has recently spoken about feeling an increased loyalty to the people and the genre that shaped her career. The performance served as both a tribute to her country roots and a creative contribution to the Toy Story movie franchise, which she described as feeling like "coming home."

While her current single "I Knew It, I Knew You" earned a nomination for Single of the Year, the award ultimately went to Ella Langley for "Choosin' Texas." The 60th anniversary event was hosted by Lainey Wilson. Fans watching the ceremony had several digital options to follow the broadcast, including ABC and streaming platforms like Disney+, Hulu + Live TV, Fubo, and DirecTV.
