No. 8 Houston Upset Topples No. 1 Florida in Players Era 8

By Karim Fouad · Published 19 Nov 2026, 21:35 UTC

Eighth-ranked Houston handed top-ranked Florida its first loss of the season with an 86-76 victory on Wednesday at Michelob Ultra Arena.

The Cougars staged an 11-0 second-half run to snap Florida's undefeated streak, forcing 12 turnovers and keeping the Gators without a field goal for nearly five full minutes during a critical stretch. Houston’s surge, capped by a Chase McCarty tip-in, established a 10-point lead that the Gators could not overcome.

This result carries major implications for the national rankings. As Florida drops its first contest after a 5-0 start, the loss likely clears the path for a new program to claim the No. 1 spot in the upcoming Monday poll. For Houston, the win marks a second consecutive victory over a top-ranked team, following their 2025 defeat of Duke in the Final Four.

According to Yahoo Sports, Dedan Thomas Jr. paced Houston with a career-high 30 points, while Joseph Tugler recorded a double-double with 14 points and 10 rebounds. Despite Denzel Aberdeen’s 25-point performance for the Gators and 18 points from Thomas Haugh, Florida struggled to contain Houston’s interior dominance throughout the second half. The Cougars outscored Florida by 13 points after halftime to secure the double-digit margin.

Following this second-round result at the Players Era 8 tournament, Houston advances to face No. 11 Kansas in the championship game. The title matchup is scheduled for Thursday night at the Michelob Ultra Arena.
