Henry Nicholls hits century as Black Caps recover to 243 against India

By Karim Fouad · Published 19 Nov 2026, 18:01 UTC

New Zealand finished day one of the first test against India at 243 for 5 after a top-order collapse.

Quick facts

113 not out
Jasprit Bumrah bowling figures

Venue
Basin Reserve, Wellington
First Test dates
November 19th-23rd

Henry Nicholls reached his 12th test century at the Basin Reserve, steering New Zealand to 243 for 5 by the end of play. This performance arrived after the Black Caps found themselves in a dire position early in the day, losing five wickets for just 70 runs. The team was sent in to bat on a green pitch, where Indian bowler Jasprit Bumrah dismantled the top order. Bumrah dismissed captain Tom Latham for a duck as part of his three-wicket haul, while other key batters including Devon Conway, Daryl Mitchell, Rachin Ravindra, and Tom Blundell fell for low scores.

This recovery is significant for the series momentum, as New Zealand seeks to stabilize their batting performance against a sharp Indian bowling attack. The partnership between Nicholls and Glenn Phillips effectively neutralized the pressure created by the early wickets, shifting the game from a potential collapse to a competitive total.

According to RNZ, the unbeaten 173-run partnership between Nicholls and Phillips defined the day's play. Nicholls finished the session on 113 not out, while Phillips remained at the crease, just six runs short of a century. Jasprit Bumrah ended the day as the primary threat for India, recording figures of three for 52.

The first test continues at the Basin Reserve in Wellington, with play scheduled through November 23rd. Following this match, the two teams will head to Christchurch for the second test, which begins on November 27th at Hagley Oval.
