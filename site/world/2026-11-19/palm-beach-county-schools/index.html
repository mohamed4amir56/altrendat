Palm Beach County Schools cancel all after-school activities due to weather forecast

By Karim Fouad · Published 19 Nov 2026, 11:02 UTC

Palm Beach County schools remain open Wednesday, November 18, but all after-school activities are canceled because of incoming weather.

Quick facts

Date
Wednesday, November 18
Aftercare pickup time
4 p.m.
Status
Schools and offices open

Students are required to be picked up from aftercare by 4 p.m. as the district clears all athletic events, clubs, meetings, and community education classes from the schedule. Families can track the real-time progress of bus routes using the Here Comes the Bus app throughout the day. The School District of Palm Beach County confirmed that while district offices and classrooms remain operational today, these precautions were enacted to prioritize the safety of students and staff members. The decision to clear the afternoon calendar follows weather reports impacting the broader South Florida region, where flooding has already affected transportation and facilities in other nearby areas. The district is actively monitoring the progression of weather conditions to determine if operations will be impacted on Thursday, November 19. An update regarding school status for the following day will be provided to families later this afternoon. Officials emphasized that safety remains the primary factor in these ongoing operational decisions for the district. Parents and staff are encouraged to keep a close watch on official district communications as the weather situation develops.
