NFL Suspends Rams Star Puka Nacua for Three Games Over Personal Conduct

By Karim Fouad · Published 19 Nov 2026, 21:01 UTC

The NFL suspended wide receiver Puka Nacua for three games after he violated the league's personal-conduct policy.

Quick facts

Suspension Length
3 games
Reinstatement Date
Dec. 14
Earliest Return Date
Dec. 20
Season Statistics
70 receptions, 715 yards, 4 touchdowns

Puka Nacua, 25, faces the NFL suspension while already on injured reserve following a right knee sprain sustained during the final offensive play against the Washington Commanders on Nov. 8. The wide receiver will be officially reinstated from the suspension on Dec. 14, though his eligibility to return from injured reserve does not begin until the Dec. 20 game against the Dallas Cowboys. According to the Los Angeles Times, Nacua is the defendant in a civil lawsuit involving allegations of an antisemitic remark and physical assault. The league announced the three-game suspension without pay on Thursday. Nacua has recorded 70 receptions for 715 yards and four touchdowns throughout the current season. This suspension coincides with his recovery period, meaning the Rams' immediate roster plans remain unchanged. The team will shift its passing focus toward Konata Mumpfield while Nacua continues to serve his time away from the field. Before these off-the-field incidents, Nacua was widely considered a candidate for a significant contract extension in the final year of his rookie deal. He previously checked himself into a rehab facility during the spring and participated in offseason workouts. During training camp, Nacua stated he was attempting to take accountability for his actions to facilitate personal growth. The suspension effectively keeps the star receiver sidelined beyond his initial injury timeline as he prepares for a potential return late in December.
