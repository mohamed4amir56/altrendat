South Africa Petrol Prices Set for Major December Cuts Up to R3.10

By Karim Fouad · Published 19 Nov 2026, 13:07 UTC

South African motorists can expect significant petrol price drops in December 2026, driven by lower international oil costs despite changes to fuel tax relief.

Closer to 2 December 2026

Motorists are poised for substantial relief at the pumps as petrol prices are projected to fall by as much as R3.10 per litre for 95 Unleaded, according to Central Energy Fund data. While the specific reductions vary, early projections indicate a major turnaround for household transport budgets heading into the final month of the year.

This shift in fuel costs arrives after a year of intense volatility, where petrol prices previously climbed by nearly R10 per litre and diesel by approximately R13. For consumers, the upcoming price adjustment provides a necessary reprieve, though the overall landscape remains subject to external pressures. Analysts caution that rising freight rates for oil shipping and insurance costs continue to impact the broader transport economy.

According to Carmag South Africa, the projected decrease is attributed to falling international petroleum prices and fluctuations in the Rand. Current data from the Central Energy Fund suggests 93 Unleaded may drop by R2.86 while 50ppm diesel could see a 37-cent reduction. However, Carmag notes a potential 28-cent increase for 500ppm diesel, contrasting with broader relief. The Department of Mineral and Petroleum Resources is expected to confirm the final official prices closer to 2 December 2026.

The volatility in fuel costs is further influenced by tax structure adjustments. Treasury’s temporary fuel tax reprieve is scheduled to scale back on 1 December 2026, which will add a surcharge to the final prices. Additionally, government-mandated slate levies implemented in November 2026 continue to factor into the pricing structure. Market observers warn that any escalation in Middle East tensions remains a critical risk factor that could quickly reverse current downward trends in crude oil pricing.
