Grizzlies Hunt Fourth Straight Win as Warriors Visit for Thursday Night Showdown

By Karim Fouad · Published 20 Nov 2026, 00:31 UTC · Updated 20 Nov 2026, 01:01 UTC

The Golden State Warriors travel to face the Memphis Grizzlies at FedExForum this Thursday night.

Cameron Boozer enters this matchup fresh off a career-high 29-point performance featuring seven 3-pointers, helping the Grizzlies claim their third consecutive win and become the fifth team in the Western Conference to secure double-digit victories. Memphis currently boasts a 10-4 record and holds one of the league’s highest-scoring offenses, having already defeated Golden State 111-108 earlier this season on October 23. That previous loss saw the Warriors surrender a double-digit lead in the final minutes at Chase Center. For Warriors fans, this meeting carries extra weight as the team attempts to improve its 8-6 record while missing key veteran leaders. With Stephen Curry sidelined by a right oblique tear and several other core players out for recovery, the Warriors rely on rookie sensation Yaxel Lendeborg, who is currently averaging 20.8 points per game. Lendeborg’s 291 total points rank second among all rookies this season. Both teams enter the contest on the second night of a back-to-back schedule. Memphis faces the Cleveland Cavaliers on Wednesday before returning home to host Golden State. The Warriors’ depth will be tested, with Brandin Podziemski leading the supporting cast against a Memphis squad that has won four of its last five games. Injury reports confirm both sides face availability challenges. The Warriors remain without Curry, Jimmy Butler III, Moses Moody, Draymond Green, and Kristaps Porzingis. The Grizzlies are managing the absences of Zach Edey, GG Jackson, Scotty Pippen Jr., Richie Saunders, and Ty Jerome, while Isaiah Stewart remains questionable due to illness. Tip-off at FedExForum is set for Thursday.
