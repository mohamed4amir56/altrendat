Ronaldo Leads Al-Nassr Attack Against Al-Ahli In Saudi Roshn League Clasico

By Karim Fouad · Published 20 Nov 2026, 17:31 UTC

Cristiano Ronaldo and Al-Nassr visit Jeddah to face Al-Ahli in a postponed ninth-round Saudi Roshn League match today.

Quick facts

Match
Al-Ahli vs Al-Nassr
League
Saudi Roshn League
Venue
Alinma Stadium, Jeddah
Date
Friday

Firas Al-Buraikan starts on the bench for Al-Ahli after returning to Jeddah today, Friday, following his attendance at the 2026 Asian awards ceremony where he missed out on the Player of the Year prize to Iranian goalkeeper Alireza Beiranvand. Al-Ahli head coach Marino Pusic has opted for a four-man foreign attacking contingent featuring Ivan Toney, Francisco Trincao, Wenderson Galeno, and Eduard Spertsyan to challenge the visitors. The defensive line remains consistent for the hosts with Edouard Mendy in goal behind centre-backs Roger Ibanez and Merih Demiral.

This clash carries significant weight as the sides meet for their postponed ninth-round fixture at the Alinma Stadium. For supporters, the inclusion of marquee names such as Ronaldo and Joao Felix highlights the competitive intensity of the Saudi Roshn League. The match serves as a crucial point-scoring opportunity for both clubs as they navigate their respective campaigns in the standings.

Al-Nassr head coach Ange Postecoglou has deployed a front two of Cristiano Ronaldo and Abdullah Al-Hamdan, supported by Joao Felix. Midfielder Joao Felix arrived in Jeddah on Thursday after international duty with Portugal. Al-Nassr’s defensive formation sees Mohamed Simakan and Inigo Martinez in the center, shielding Brazilian goalkeeper Bento, who starts in place of the injured Nawaf Al-Aqidi. Valentin Atangana returns to the Al-Ahli midfield after recovering from injury, joining Ziyad Al-Johani to anchor the center of the pitch.

Following today’s encounter in Jeddah, both teams will continue their respective schedules in the Saudi Roshn League as they look to secure points in the second half of the season.
