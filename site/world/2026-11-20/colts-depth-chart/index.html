Cornerback Sauce Gardner is not expected to play in Thursday's matchup against the Houston Texans due to a calf injury. This development leaves the Colts secondary significantly depleted as they prepare to face their divisional rivals on the road.

This personnel crisis creates a major challenge for the Indianapolis defense, which must now adapt without two of its primary defensive backs. Gardner has been a central figure for the team this season, recording 31 total tackles and seven passes defensed across nine games. His absence, combined with Charvarius Ward’s verified status as ruled out due to a knee injury, forces the coaching staff to rely on deeper roster options to contain the Texans' passing attack.

According to NBC Sports, the team listed Gardner as a non-participant on all three injury report estimates throughout the week. Ward followed a similar pattern, remaining sidelined for all practice sessions leading up to the game. NFL Network’s Jane Slater reported the status of the secondary ahead of the primetime contest, highlighting the specific injury concerns that have defined the week for Indianapolis. The official list of inactives for both teams will be confirmed 90 minutes before kickoff, providing the final view of the active roster available to the coaching staff.

While the defense manages these personnel setbacks, the Texans are navigating their own roster adjustments for this Week 11 encounter. The final in-game strategy will depend on how the remaining members of the Indianapolis secondary perform under the pressure of a divisional game, with the team needing immediate production to offset the losses of two key starters.
