Heavy Rain Forces UCF Stadium Changes for Friday Clash Against Iowa State

By Karim Fouad · Published 20 Nov 2026, 22:31 UTC · Updated 20 Nov 2026, 23:01 UTC

UCF hosts Iowa State on Friday as both teams enter the matchup with matching 5-5 overall records.

Heavy rainfall in Orlando has triggered significant logistical shifts for Friday night’s matchup, forcing UCF to close multiple stadium gates and relocate pregame activities. While the football game remains on as scheduled, school officials have closed gates 5, 8, 9, 11, and 16B, while canceling the Knights on the Mall festivities and the Corona Concert Series. The Knight Walk has been relocated to Gate 14, and pregame activations previously set for IOA Plaza have been moved to Nicholson Plaza near the stadium’s southwest corner.

These adjustments arrive as both programs fight for position in the bowl picture, with UCF and Iowa State entering the game with identical 3-4 Big 12 conference records. For fans and the teams involved, the contest marks only the second meeting between the two schools. UCF looks to avenge a 2024 loss in Ames, where a late touchdown from Rocco Becht with 30 seconds remaining secured a 38-25 victory for Iowa State.

Iowa State arrives in Orlando following a 41-13 win over Cincinnati and features one of the nation's premier running threats in Aiden Flora. Flora currently leads the country with 1,335 rushing yards and 12 touchdowns. Conversely, UCF faces uncertainty at the quarterback position, as Alonza Barnett has started recent games in place of the injury-hampered Keyone Jenkins. UCF’s defense enters the game allowing 136.9 rushing yards per contest, ranking 58th in the FBS.

UCF officials advise that parking operations have also been impacted, with the Baseball Lot, Baseball OP6, and Arboretum closed due to the weather. Fans are encouraged to monitor UCF Athletics communication channels for further updates as the university coordinates with the National Weather Service.
