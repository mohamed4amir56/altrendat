Kitchen Friction Erupts Between Mariella Frostrup and AJ Odudu in Jungle Camp

By Karim Fouad · Published 20 Nov 2026, 22:31 UTC

Journalist Mariella Frostrup and presenter AJ Odudu clashed over cooking duties in the latest episode of I’m A Celebrity... Get Me Out Of Here!

The jungle camp environment has turned volatile as journalist Mariella Frostrup faces consecutive disagreements with her fellow contestants. Following a tension-filled cooking session, AJ Odudu, 38, expressed frustration after being shut down when offering assistance in the kitchen. Odudu, who recently entered the camp alongside comedian Leigh Francis, described a sense of friction with Frostrup and noted that communication remains a challenge during the group's early days living together.

These clashes over kitchen management are significant as the contestants attempt to navigate the grueling conditions and basic rations of the reality series. For the campmates, the inability to establish a collaborative routine around meal preparation is exacerbating the already stressful environment, leading to emotional strain for those involved in the friction.

According to The Independent, Odudu reported feeling confused by the dynamic after her attempts to help were met with skepticism from Frostrup. This recent dispute follows an earlier confrontation reported by the London Evening Standard, in which Welsh actress Joanna Page stormed out of the camp in tears. During that incident, Page stated that she struggled to listen to Frostrup’s voice while the presenter was giving instructions during dinner preparation. Page, who had previously discussed experiencing inappropriate behavior from a male actor on set, told campmates that the overall situation in the jungle has become very stressful.

As the show continues, the contestants remain under high pressure while living on limited rations. The group is currently adjusting to the arrival of new members and the ongoing demands of the competition, with viewers watching to see if the culinary tensions will subside or continue to disrupt camp life.
