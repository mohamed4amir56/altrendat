No. 13 Oregon Targets Fourth Straight Win Against Injury-Plagued Michigan State Spartans

By Karim Fouad · Published 21 Nov 2026, 00:31 UTC

The No. 13 Oregon Ducks travel to face Michigan State this Friday, Nov. 20, at 8 p.m. ET on FOX.

The No. 13 Oregon Ducks arrive at Spartan Stadium riding a three-game winning streak and averaging 35 points per game over that span. This offensive surge marks a significant turnaround from October, when the Ducks averaged only 14 points across three games. According to Yahoo Sports, Oregon aims to continue this momentum to secure a top-12 spot in the final College Football Playoff rankings with two games remaining in the regular season.

For Michigan State, the stakes are different as the 3-7 Spartans have been ruled out of bowl eligibility. The team faces significant roster challenges, including the absence of starting quarterback Alessio Milivojevic due to an arm injury. Yahoo Sports also reports that senior backup Cam Fancher is listed as questionable, and the Spartans will be without defensive lineman Ben Roberts.

Oregon quarterback Dylan Raiola enters the contest coming off a four-touchdown performance against Michigan, having not thrown an interception in his last five games. FOX Sports notes that Raiola will rely on running back Dierre Hill Jr., who leads the Ducks with 572 rushing yards this season, to anchor the attack against a Michigan State defense allowing 132.8 rushing yards per game.

Michigan State’s offense continues to center on receiver Chrishon McCray, who recorded 92 yards on six catches against Michigan in Week 11. On the ground, the Spartans look to Brandon Tullis, who has shouldered a heavier workload with 57 total carries in recent games. Oregon enters the game following a 31-7 victory over Michigan, while Michigan State seeks to rebound after a 21-14 home loss to Washington.
