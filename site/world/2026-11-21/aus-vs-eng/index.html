England storms to series win as Ben Duckett routs Australia in Hobart

By Karim Fouad · Published 21 Nov 2026, 08:01 UTC

Ben Duckett and Sonny Baker led England to a 97-run victory over Australia on Wednesday to secure a 2-1 series win.

Ben Duckett hammered 133 runs to anchor England's total, setting the stage for a dominant bowling performance that silenced the home crowd in Hobart. While Australia sought to defend its territory, the visitors surged to a massive 328 all out, proving too formidable for the Australian lineup during the final showdown of the series.

This victory marks a definitive turnaround for the English side, which began the contest trailing 1-0 after initial struggles. By overcoming that early deficit, the team demonstrated remarkable resilience, successfully flipping the script after their emphatic 135-run win in Adelaide last Sunday. For fans and observers, the result solidifies England's current momentum in the international cricket format.

The final match saw Australia crumble to 231 all out as England’s pace attack dictated the pace of play. Pace bowler Sonny Baker proved instrumental, dismantling the batting order with four crucial wickets, while teammate Liam Dawson chipped in with another three wickets. Ben Duckett’s century remained the defining offensive effort of the day, ensuring England’s total remained out of reach for the hosts throughout the final innings.

The series concludes with England walking away as winners, having successfully weathered a challenging start to turn the tide across the three-match series. The clinical performance in Hobart confirms the visitors' dominance in the deciding fixture, leaving Australia unable to respond to the relentless pressure applied by the English bowlers and Duckett’s high-scoring display at the crease.
