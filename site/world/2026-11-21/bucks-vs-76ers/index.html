Struggling Bucks face 76ers as Philadelphia chases fifth consecutive NBA regular-season win

By Karim Fouad · Published 21 Nov 2026, 17:34 UTC

The Philadelphia 76ers host the Milwaukee Bucks at Xfinity Mobile Arena this Saturday afternoon in an NBA regular-season matchup.

The Philadelphia 76ers enter this Saturday afternoon matinee riding a four-game winning streak, coming off a 121-116 comeback victory against Orlando where they overcame a 17-point first-quarter deficit. In stark contrast, the Milwaukee Bucks arrive in Philadelphia mired in a 12-game losing streak following a 124-114 defeat to the Portland Trail Blazers on Thursday. The Bucks hold a 1-15 record this season, while the 76ers currently sit atop the Eastern Conference standings with a 13-3 start.

This matchup carries significant implications for both ends of the Eastern Conference table. Philadelphia continues its campaign despite health concerns, as Jaylen Brown remains sidelined with a hamstring injury and Tyrese Maxey is listed as questionable with a foot issue. For Milwaukee, the game represents an attempt to find offensive rhythm; the team currently ranks third from the bottom of the league in scoring, averaging 108.1 points per game. The 76ers previously defeated the Bucks 127-116 in their first meeting of the season on October 24.

According to Yahoo Sports, the broadcast for the game will be carried on the Bucks TV Network and NBC Sports Philadelphia. The SportsLine Projection Model, which has simulated the game 10,000 times, favors Philadelphia by 10.5 points with an over/under set at 228.5. The model suggests a lower-scoring affair, noting that the absence of key Philadelphia players may limit the team's offensive output despite their dominance this season.

Tipoff for the contest is scheduled for 1:00 p.m. ET at the Xfinity Mobile Arena.
