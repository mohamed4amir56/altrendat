Colts dump Daniel Jones as Anthony Richardson takes over starting role

By Karim Fouad · Published 21 Nov 2026, 14:01 UTC

The Indianapolis Colts waived quarterback Daniel Jones on Friday and named Anthony Richardson their new starter following a 26-3 loss to the Houston Texans.

The Indianapolis Colts terminated Daniel Jones' contract Friday, just hours after a lackluster performance in a 26-3 loss to the Houston Texans. Jones, who completed only 12 of 27 passes for 144 yards and an interception during the game, ended his 11-game stint with the team while leading the league with 13 interceptions. Coach Shane Steichen, who had defended the quarterback earlier in the week, ultimately benched him in the fourth quarter before confirming the release was in the best interest of both sides.

This decision forces an immediate shift for opposing teams, including the New York Giants. Giants coach John Harbaugh admitted that the news blindsided his staff, who were already preparing scouting reports for a matchup against an Indianapolis team led by Jones. Because Anthony Richardson brings a vastly different style of play to the position, New York's coaches must now scrap their existing film study and prepare for Richardson’s first start in two years, scheduled for November 29.

According to NFL Network, Jones remains on waivers until Monday at 4 p.m. ET. Any team that claims him inherits the remainder of his contract, which includes just over $2 million for the current season and $38 million in non-guaranteed money for 2027. Despite his struggles, coach Steichen praised Jones' competitiveness, noting the quarterback’s efforts to play through injuries including a broken leg and a torn Achilles during his time with the organization.

The Colts, currently sitting at 3-8, are now fully pivoting to Richardson for the remainder of the season. Richardson made a brief appearance in the final minutes of the loss to Houston, and he is expected to be the focal point of the offense moving forward as the team attempts to reset following the departure of their high-priced veteran.
