Kate Spade Wristlet Plummets 80% As Early Black Friday Sales Go Live

By Karim Fouad · Published 21 Nov 2026, 21:01 UTC

Major retailers have launched early Black Friday 2026 discount events featuring deep price cuts on tech, home appliances, and fashion items.

The Kate Spade Lucy Wristlet is currently available for $29, a sharp drop from its original $139 price tag, marking an 80% discount for early shoppers. According to Yahoo, this is part of a wave of pre-holiday markdowns already active across multiple major brands including Dyson, Hoka, Ugg, and Michael Kors. Other notable savings include the Ring Battery Doorbell 2K, which is currently discounted by 60%, bringing its price down to $40. Apple AirPods Pro 3 are also seeing significant interest at $179, matching their lowest price of 2026. Experts suggest that securing these items now prevents the risk of stock sell-outs often seen closer to the official holiday date.

For consumers, these early sales offer a strategic window to purchase gifts, wardrobe staples, and home technology before the peak of Black Friday chaos. Shopping now allows for price comparison and product availability that may diminish as the holiday shopping season accelerates. By tracking price histories, retail experts indicate that many current markdowns, such as the $100 discount on the Ninja Flip Toaster Oven & Air Fryer, represent some of the most worthwhile bargains available this year.

Retailers have been aggressively competing for holiday spending, with analysts at CNN Underscored reporting they have reviewed 147 separate early sale events to curate a list of the top 98 deals currently live. While current stock remains available, experts anticipate that the list of discounted goods will continue to fluctuate as the season progresses. Shoppers are advised to monitor these retail platforms regularly, as lists of curated deals are being updated throughout the duration of the 2026 holiday event.
