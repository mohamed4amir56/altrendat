England steamrolls All Blacks as 37-12 victory sets record-breaking misery for visitors

By Karim Fouad · Published 21 Nov 2026, 13:31 UTC · Updated 21 Nov 2026, 17:02 UTC

England secured a decisive 37-12 Nations Championship victory over New Zealand at Allianz Stadium, marking a record loss for the All Blacks.

Quick facts

The 80,000 spectators at Twickenham witnessed a one-way fight as England recorded its largest-ever win against the All Blacks, eclipsing the 2012 result of 38-21. This defeat marks the fifth loss for New Zealand this season, matching the worst record in their professional era, a tally not seen since the 1998 season. England, now riding a five-match unbeaten run, physically dominated the breakdowns and forced 16 turnovers from the visitors throughout the contest.

For the All Blacks, the result represents a significant setback in their recent campaign, coming immediately after a historic loss to Wales. The team struggled to maintain momentum, suffering from a failing lineout and a defense that conceded 12 clean breaks. As reported by the NZ Herald, the visitors appeared flat and lethargic against a hungry England side, unable to execute a cohesive attacking platform.

England captain Maro Itoje praised his squad’s rapid start and the impact provided by the bench players in his post-match comments to ITV. The victory cements England’s current form in the Nations Championship and further exposes the ongoing struggles of the All Blacks under Dave Rennie. The result marks the first time New Zealand has lost to England twice in consecutive visits to Twickenham since the 2002-2003 era.

While the Nations Championship rankings continue, the team now looks toward next week's fixtures to maintain their momentum. England remains focused on sustaining their current level of performance, with Itoje emphasizing that the team is only as good as their next game.
