League leaders Galatasaray host struggling Samsunspor to extend dominant Süper Lig lead

By Karim Fouad · Published 21 Nov 2026, 13:31 UTC

Galatasaray faces Samsunspor at NEF Stadyumu as the league leaders aim to build on their 28-point total.

Quick facts

Venue
NEF Stadyumu, Istanbul
League
Süper Lig
Galatasaray Points
28

Galatasaray enters the clash with a formidable record, boasting six consecutive wins across all competitions. This run includes high-profile victories such as a 2-1 derby win against Fenerbahçe and Champions League triumphs over VfB Stuttgart and Lille. Okan Buruk’s squad has been clinical, scoring 13 goals while conceding only four in their last five competitive appearances, including a recent 4-3 Süper Lig victory over Amed Sportif. For Samsunspor, the match represents a critical battle for survival as the team sits second from the bottom of the table. Coach Nenad Bjelica’s side has struggled significantly, losing four of their last five league matches. Their recent defensive woes were highlighted by a 5-2 defeat to Istanbul Basaksehir, and they have conceded 14 goals over their last four winless games. The contrast between the two clubs is stark, with Galatasaray maintaining their position as clear league leaders with 28 points. While the club manages its roster, midfielder Ilkay Gündogan has publicly confirmed he will remain with Galatasaray through the winter despite limited playing time, expressing his commitment to winning titles with the team following his arrival in September 2025. The Süper Lig match takes place at NEF Stadyumu in Istanbul. Fans will be watching to see if the league leaders can further widen the gap at the top of the table or if Samsunspor can secure a much-needed result to improve their standing in the league.
