Manchester City eyes turnaround to break Fulham's 20-game losing streak today

By Karim Fouad · Published 21 Nov 2026, 12:01 UTC · Updated 21 Nov 2026, 12:31 UTC

Manchester City hosts Fulham at the Etihad Stadium today at 12:30pm GMT looking to end a run of three consecutive league defeats.

Fulham has failed to beat Manchester City since 2009, suffering 20 consecutive defeats against today's hosts in a run spanning back to 2012. While City seeks to halt three straight top-flight losses, including a recent defeat to Nottingham Forest, their opponents arrive with an unbeaten record in their last four matches. The Cottagers, currently sitting 15th in the Premier League, recently secured a draw against Newcastle at Craven Cottage after coming from behind.

For Manchester City, the match at the Etihad Stadium represents a vital opportunity to recover momentum after their recent dip in form. Fulham supporters remain optimistic, with fan John Charles citing the steadying influence of head coach Alvaro Arbeloa and pointing to a tightly packed league table where a European charge remains a potential goal as the season progresses.

The Guardian reports that Manchester City enters this clash aiming to correct their downward trajectory in the league following an international break. Fulham, meanwhile, arrives with 10 points from their opening 10 games. The visitors are hopeful that the squad's improved atmosphere under Arbeloa will translate into an exciting performance against the perennial challenge posed by City.

Team news and final lineups are expected ahead of the 12:30pm GMT kick-off. Analysts expect the tight nature of the current league standings to play a significant role in the approach both managers take to the fixture as they look to secure points in a congested mid-table and top-four environment.
