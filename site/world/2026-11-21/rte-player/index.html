Happy Days Star Henry Winkler Rushed to Ennis Hospital After Gout Scare

By Karim Fouad · Published 21 Nov 2026, 17:01 UTC

Actor Henry Winkler was treated at an Ennis emergency department on Thursday following a painful gout flare-up during his Irish tour.

Henry Winkler, globally recognized for his iconic role as Arthur 'The Fonz' Fonzarelli, experienced an unexpected health setback while vacationing in Ireland. The 81-year-old actor revealed that a dietary decision to consume excess cheese led to an acute bout of gout, necessitating an urgent visit to an A+E department in Ennis on Thursday. Despite the pain, Winkler expressed sincere gratitude to the medical staff who provided care during his time of need.

Following his hospital visit, the Emmy-winning performer highlighted the hospitality of local emergency responders. During an appearance on the Late Late Show, Winkler shared that he was later lifted into the air by the Dublin Fire Brigade using a basket, a gesture he described as fantastic. This visit is part of a broader tour of Ireland that has taken the actor through locations including Kinsale, the Rock of Cashel, and the English Market in Cork.

Reporting on his experiences, RTE noted that Winkler is currently visiting the country ahead of his scheduled appearance at Comic Con Ireland 2026. This trip marks the latest in a series of memorable Irish visits for the actor, who previously gained notice during a hotel fire drill evacuation in a prior year. Beyond his television legacy, Winkler continues his active career, with his latest film project, Hexed, set to arrive in cinemas next week.

Looking ahead, the star is set to appear before fans at the RDS in Dublin this weekend for Comic Con. His account of his recent health emergency provided a lighthearted moment during his scheduled promotional tour stops, balancing his professional commitments with his personal travels across the region.
