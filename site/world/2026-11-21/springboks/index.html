Defiant Springboks face Ireland showdown while banned Rassie Erasmus watches from hotel

By Karim Fouad · Published 21 Nov 2026, 17:31 UTC

South Africa enters a crucial Nations Championship Test in Dublin without head coach Rassie Erasmus, who received a six-match ban following a loss to France.

Quick facts

Venue
Aviva Stadium

Rassie Erasmus will host a hotel watchalong for Saturday’s match against Ireland, isolated from the team by a six-match ban that prohibits him from any matchday contact with players or management. The suspension follows the Springboks' recent defeat to France, during which Erasmus publicly questioned the integrity of match officials Andrea Piardi and television match official Matteo Liperini. Adding to the team's disciplinary challenges, back row Jasper Wiese is also sidelined, serving a four-game ban for a dangerous ruck clearout during the same match against the French side.

The absence of their head coach marks a significant hurdle in a fixture already circled as a must-see event. Assistant coach Mzwandile Stick emphasized the team's unity ahead of the encounter, stating that despite the external turmoil, the squad remains in a good space. Stick noted that the players feel the weight of their supporters, particularly given how deep the connection is between the national team's performance and the public mood back in South Africa.

Captain Siya Kolisi pushed back against any suggestion that the team’s emotional response to losses is dramatic. He stressed that rugby remains a significant pillar for their country, and every player understands the responsibility of wearing the jersey. The team now turns its attention to the Aviva Stadium, where they must perform under immense pressure without the presence of their primary tactician. While Erasmus remains locked away in his hotel room, the Springboks must overcome both their Irish opponents and the distraction of a week defined by regulatory discipline to secure their standing in the Nations Championship group.
