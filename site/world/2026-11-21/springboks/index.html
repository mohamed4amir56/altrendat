Defiant Springboks Face Ireland After Rassie Erasmus Receives Six-Match Coaching Ban

By Karim Fouad · Published 21 Nov 2026, 17:34 UTC

The Springboks prepare for their Nations Championship Test against Ireland in Dublin without head coach Rassie Erasmus following his six-match suspension for criticizing officials.

Quick facts

Rassie Erasmus will watch Saturday's match from his team's Dublin hotel, barred from all contact with his players and management due to a suspension for questioning the integrity of referee Andrea Piardi and television match official Matteo Liperini. The ban followed the Springboks' recent defeat in Paris, a game that also saw back row Jasper Wiese suspended for four matches following a dangerous ruck clearout.

This high-stakes fixture at the Aviva Stadium has become a focal point of the November rugby schedule, testing the team's resilience after a turbulent week. Assistant coach Mzwandile Stick remains defiant, stating that the squad feels in a good space and that no external pressure will break the team. The importance of the match extends beyond the pitch, as captain Siya Kolisi emphasized that the players carry the weight of national expectation every time they represent South Africa.

According to the BBC, Erasmus remains a two-time World Cup winner despite the controversy surrounding his recent conduct. The team looks to move past the disciplinary fallout by focusing on their performance on the field against Ireland, aiming to channel the expectations of their supporters back home into a victory. This match continues a series of must-see events in the current Nations Championship, with the focus now shifting entirely to the players and the remaining coaching staff who must navigate the game without their head coach present.
