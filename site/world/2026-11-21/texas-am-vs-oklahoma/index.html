Oklahoma hosts Texas A&M as Aggies fight for critical bowl game eligibility

By Karim Fouad · Published 21 Nov 2026, 20:31 UTC

Oklahoma faces Texas A&M this Saturday at 2:45 p.m. CT with the Aggies desperate to end a four-game road losing streak.

Quick facts

Saturday, Nov. 21
Time
2:45 p.m. CT
Venue
Gaylord Family — Oklahoma Memorial Stadium
TV Channel

Oklahoma enters Saturday's matchup at Owen Field having dominated the series with a 19-12 lead, including seven consecutive home wins against the Aggies by an average margin of 49-14. While the 6-4 Sooners have already secured their 28th consecutive season of bowl eligibility, the 4-6 Aggies must win to keep their own postseason hopes alive following three straight losses. This meeting marks the first time the former Big 12 rivals have played since 2012.

The stakes are distinct for both programs as they enter the final stretch of the regular season. For Texas A&M, a loss would leave the team on track to match its worst performance since 2008. Oklahoma, meanwhile, remains mathematically alive for a potential College Football Playoff spot, though the team requires significant help from conference rivals to close the gap.

Texas A&M head coach Mike Elko faces a difficult test against an Oklahoma defense that ranks 10th nationally in passing defense, allowing just 166.3 yards per game. The Aggies' offense has struggled significantly on the road, totaling only 41 points across four winless away games this year. Backup quarterback Brady Hart is reportedly in contention to start for the Aggies, replacing Marcel Reed, who has failed to record a touchdown pass in five of his last seven appearances.

Oklahoma's defense is bolstered by linebacker Owen Heinecke, a Butkus Award semifinalist who leads the team with 83 tackles. The Sooners look to build on their recent victory over Ole Miss when they host Texas A&M before concluding their campaign. Following this weekend's contest, Texas A&M will return home to face rival Texas in their final game of the season.
