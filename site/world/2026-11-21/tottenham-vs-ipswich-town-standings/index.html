Teenager Luca Williams-Barnett inspires Tottenham to 2-0 victory over Ipswich Town

By Karim Fouad · Published 21 Nov 2026, 17:31 UTC

Luca Williams-Barnett scored and assisted in his first Premier League start to secure a 2-0 win for Tottenham against Ipswich.

Quick facts

Final Score
Tottenham 2-0 Ipswich
Match Kick-off
3pm
Debut Player
Luca Williams-Barnett

Eighteen-year-old Luca Williams-Barnett became Tottenham’s youngest scorer on his first Premier League start as he dominated the fixture against Ipswich Town. The teenager found the net in the 15th minute and later delivered a cross for Dominic Solanke to double the advantage in the second half.

This result provides a significant boost for Tottenham following their narrow defeat to Leeds United before the international break. Manager Roberto De Zerbi signaled a tactical reset by making six changes to his starting eleven, handing opportunities to players including Williams-Barnett, Solanke, and James Maddison to revitalize the side's attacking output.

Sky Sports reports that Williams-Barnett provided the decisive impact by opening the scoring early and setting up the second goal after the break. The victory earns Tottenham three points in the Premier League table. The match, which kicked off at 3pm, saw both sides rotate their squads, with Ipswich Town deploying a lineup captained by O'Shea, while Tottenham captained by Pedro Porro showcased a new-look midfield and attacking rotation.

Tottenham will look to carry this momentum into their upcoming fixtures as the Premier League season progresses through November. The squad depth tested by De Zerbi provided the necessary energy to overcome Ipswich Town in front of the home crowd.
