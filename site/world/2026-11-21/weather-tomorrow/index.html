Breezy Conditions and Scattered Showers Sweep Across Hawaii Islands This Saturday

By Karim Fouad · Published 21 Nov 2026, 08:31 UTC

Maui and Hawaii County face varying weather patterns this weekend with high chances of rain and persistent winds expected across multiple regions.

Residents across Hawaii can expect a weekend dominated by breezy conditions and varying chances of precipitation, as isolated to numerous showers are forecasted to move through the islands. In Maui, the weather outlook for Saturday calls for widespread breezy conditions, with some areas seeing mostly sunny skies while others remain mostly cloudy with up to a 70 percent chance of rain in certain districts. Summit areas, such as the visitor center, will see temperatures ranging from roughly 42 to 65 degrees, accompanied by consistent east winds blowing between 15 and 25 mph.

Understanding these shifts is vital for planning outdoor weekend activities, particularly for those living near the shore or in higher elevations where temperatures and rainfall probabilities differ significantly. The impact of these winds and localized rain showers will dictate daily routines, especially in areas where the chance of precipitation climbs as high as 70 percent throughout the day.

According to reports from Maui Now, the island will experience breezy conditions with intermittent sun and clouds. Meanwhile, Big Island Now indicates that Hawaii County is bracing for a mix of sunny periods and likely showers, particularly in the morning. Regions in Hawaii County should anticipate northeast winds of up to 15 mph, with rain chances fluctuating between 20 and 90 percent depending on the specific location and elevation.

As the weekend progresses, travelers and residents are encouraged to monitor local updates for any rapid changes in wind speeds or precipitation levels. These weather patterns are typical for this time of year, with breezy winds continuing to influence both temperatures and shower frequency across the archipelago through Saturday night.
