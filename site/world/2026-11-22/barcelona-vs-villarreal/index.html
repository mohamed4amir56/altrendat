Barcelona Chases Fourth Straight Win as Villarreal Faces Defending Champions Sunday

By Karim Fouad · Published 22 Nov 2026, 13:01 UTC · Updated 22 Nov 2026, 15:01 UTC

Barcelona aims for a fourth consecutive victory in Sunday's La Liga match against Villarreal at Camp Nou.

Pau Navarro’s second yellow card remains a recent focal point in this fixture, highlighting the disciplinary challenges Villarreal faces as they travel to take on the league leaders. Barcelona enters the match in dominant form, looking to secure their 12th victory in 13 La Liga outings while maintaining a title defense that has already seen the team register an astonishing 48 goals this season.

This match holds significant weight for both sides as Barcelona looks to solidify their position at the summit of La Liga. For the defending champions, the return to league action follows a period of recovery from mid-season setbacks against Paris Saint-Germain and Real Madrid. Meanwhile, Villarreal looks to overcome their inconsistent campaign, hoping their away record—where they have collected 8 points compared to 7 at home—will provide a necessary boost against the league's most prolific attack.

Barcelona enters the clash with momentum, having won their last three competitive matches, including a comeback victory at Atlético Madrid. Key contributors Lamine Yamal, Raphinha, and Fermín López remain central to the team's offensive output, providing consistent goal involvements. The side has managed to re-establish their form despite defensive injury concerns involving players like Marc Bernal. Villarreal, under manager Íñigo Pérez, faces an uphill task coming off a difficult 6-0 loss to PSG, though they enter the match having secured three wins in their last five outings across all competitions.

The historical precedent favors the hosts, with Barcelona having won 11 of the last 14 head-to-head clashes. However, the visitors will look to capitalize on any remaining defensive instability as they attempt a rare upset at Camp Nou, a venue where Barcelona has historically suffered their few losses in this specific fixture.
