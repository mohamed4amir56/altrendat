Jeremiah Smith surges as clear -360 favorite for 2026 Heisman Trophy

By Karim Fouad · Published 22 Nov 2026, 06:31 UTC

Ohio State wide receiver Jeremiah Smith leads the Heisman race with implied odds of 78.26 percent after reaching 1,482 season receiving yards.

Jeremiah Smith remains the dominant front-runner for the 2026 Heisman Trophy, boasting 1,482 yards and 17 touchdowns on 86 receptions this season. Even with starting quarterback Julian Sayin sidelined since October 17, Smith continues to produce, recording at least four catches and 68 yards in every one of his 11 appearances. According to FanDuel Sportsbook, Smith is currently a -360 favorite to secure the award.

Smith's individual performance keeps him at the forefront of the race despite Ohio State finishing the season with a 7-4 record. While quarterbacks have historically dominated the voting, winning 20 of the last 25 trophies, Smith's consistent dominance across all 11 games has established him as the statistical leader. His recent outing against Nebraska included six catches for 165 yards and two touchdowns, further cementing his standing among voters.

Competition for the trophy remains intense, with Miami quarterback Darian Mensah emerging as a top challenger. Mensah leads the FBS in completion percentage and has guided his team into College Football Playoff and ACC title contention. Other notable contenders include Utah's Devon Dampier, who has recorded nine rushing and 22 passing touchdowns, and Notre Dame's CJ Carr, who has led his team to a 9-1 record. Running back Nate Sheppard also enters the conversation as the national leader in rushing touchdowns by a running back.

Smith is scheduled to play his final regular season game against Michigan next Saturday at noon ET. The Heisman ceremony is expected to take place in three weeks, where finalists will be recognized for their contributions throughout the 2026 season.
