Second-placed Brighton travels to MKM Stadium to clash with Hull City

By Karim Fouad · Published 22 Nov 2026, 13:31 UTC · Updated 22 Nov 2026, 14:31 UTC

Brighton looks to extend a strong run against 14th-placed Hull City in their Premier League meeting at 09:00 on November 22, 2026.

Konstantinos Tzolakis denied Diego Gomez an early opening goal, marking the first major test for the Hull goalkeeper as the match got underway. Brighton arrives at the MKM Stadium in second place, having secured back-to-back victories, including a dominant 2-0 performance against Brentford where they maintained 75% possession.

This fixture carries significant weight for both clubs as they navigate the Premier League season. For Brighton, maintaining their high standing is vital to keep pace with the league leaders, while Hull sits in 14th place and hopes to leverage home advantage to climb the table after a recent 1-0 defeat to Arsenal.

Brighton enters the contest with 22 points from 10 games, while Hull holds 11 points from the same number of matches. According to Sportsgambler, Hull must manage their squad carefully as they remain without five injured players: Elliot Matazo, Hidemasa Morita, Ilyas Ansah, Matt Targett, and Charlie Hughes. However, they welcome back Mohamed Belloumi and Nobel Mendy, with Matt Crooks also returning from suspension. Brighton faces its own challenges, missing Evan Ferguson, Stefanos Tzimas, Kaoru Mitoma, and Zadok Yohanna due to injuries, though Jaouen Hadjam is fit to play.

History suggests a closely fought encounter at the MKM Stadium, where the two teams last played to a goalless draw in 2016. Across their last 10 meetings, the rivalry remains balanced, with four draws, three wins for Hull, and three wins for Brighton. The outcome of this match will prove pivotal for Brighton’s momentum and Hull’s efforts to improve their current 11-point tally.
