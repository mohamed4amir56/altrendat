France Clashes With South Africa In Inaugural Nations Championship Twickenham Final

By Karim Fouad · Published 22 Nov 2026, 05:31 UTC

France and South Africa face off in the inaugural Nations Championship final at Allianz Stadium on November 29 following the conclusion of the pool stage.

Quick facts

Final Date
November 29
Venue
Allianz Stadium, Twickenham
Final Matchup
France vs South Africa
Final Kick-off
4.40pm
Tournament Dates
November 27-29

South Africa secured a spot in the final despite suffering consecutive defeats, including a 17-20 loss to Ireland in Dublin. The Springboks finished at the top of the southern hemisphere table after New Zealand failed to earn bonus points during a loss to England at Twickenham. France claimed their place in the final by topping the northern hemisphere table following a 45-17 victory over Argentina in Paris.

The Nations Championship serves as a new joint venture between Six Nations and SANZAAR, aiming to provide more structure and consistent international matchups between northern and southern hemisphere teams. This alignment ensures high-profile fixtures outside of the World Cup window, with the inaugural tournament featuring twelve teams including Fiji and Japan.

According to Sky Sports and Rugby365, the finals weekend at Allianz Stadium will determine final tournament standings based on corresponding finishes from both hemispheres. Following the headline event between France and South Africa, England is scheduled to face New Zealand in a rematch, while Ireland takes on Australia. Other fixtures include Scotland versus Argentina, Wales against Japan, and Italy facing Fiji.

The tournament conclusion takes place across three days at the end of November. Following these matchups, international teams will conclude their involvement in this first edition of the competition.
