40,000 runners storm Philadelphia streets as weekend marathon meets afternoon rain showers

By Karim Fouad · Published 22 Nov 2026, 11:01 UTC

Philadelphia hosts 40,000 runners for the half and full marathon this weekend as road closures and Sunday afternoon rain arrive.

Quick facts

Participants
40,000
Sunday race conditions
Mainly dry morning with afternoon rain
Sunday temperatures
Lower 60s

Runners are arriving in Philadelphia to conquer a course through the city streets, with 40,000 participants registered for the half and full marathon events. While Sunday morning remains mainly dry, the forecast turns as rain arrives by the afternoon, according to NBC10 Philadelphia.

The city faces a logistical challenge this weekend as road closures on the Benjamin Franklin Parkway and Eakins Oval coincide with a high volume of visitors. Beyond the race, the Philadelphia Eagles play at home on Sunday afternoon, further complicating travel for those navigating the city. SEPTA is adding express trips on the Broad Street Line to accommodate fans heading to Lincoln Financial Field, while local residents prepare for significant traffic impacts across the region.

Local businesses are looking toward the influx of runners to bolster holiday market sales. Vendors at Dilworth Park and the Christmas Village in LOVE Park expect a boost from the increased foot traffic as shoppers and spectators congregate downtown. While some residents view the traffic as nightmarish due to ongoing I-95 construction and race-related detours, many runners expressed excitement about the atmosphere.

Despite the impending stormy weather, participants remain undeterred, with many traveling from out of town to experience the course for the first time. The heaviest rainfall is expected to develop during the evening hours on Sunday. Looking ahead to the work week, conditions shift back to dry, breezy, and milder weather on Monday, with another storm system expected to bring wind and rain by Thanksgiving.
