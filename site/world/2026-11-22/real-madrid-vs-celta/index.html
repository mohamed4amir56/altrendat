Carlos Espí clinches Real Madrid comeback win over Celta at Bernabéu

By Karim Fouad · Published 22 Nov 2026, 05:01 UTC

Carlos Espí scored the winning goal in a 2-1 victory against Celta de Vigo, extending Real Madrid's winning streak to nine consecutive games.

Quick facts

Result

Date

Goalscorer

Streak

Carlos Espí described having goosebumps after netting his first home goal for Real Madrid, a strike that secured a dramatic 2-1 comeback victory against Celta de Vigo. The 21-year-old forward emphasized the significance of the moment, noting that scoring his first goal at the Santiago Bernabéu felt like a dream after previously scoring three times on the road. The win solidified the home ground as a fortress, with Real Madrid continuing its perfect streak of victories at the stadium this season.

This victory is vital for Real Madrid as it marks their ninth consecutive win across all competitions, maintaining their momentum in both La Liga and the Champions League. For the players and the club, keeping the home record perfect is a priority, as Espí noted that the team cannot afford to drop points in front of their supporters. The win serves as a high-stakes confidence booster before the team transitions immediately to their next challenge.

According to reports from HolaNews, the match was a difficult test that required the squad to overcome adversity and stay focused throughout the encounter. Marca notes that the team returned to the Bernabéu following a demanding series of four consecutive away matches, including victories against Barcelona and Valencia. The squad remains unified, with players emphasizing that every minute on the pitch must be maximized to keep the current winning form alive.

The team has little time to celebrate the result. Espí confirmed that preparation for the upcoming Champions League fixture begins immediately, with only two days available to ready the squad for the next battle.
