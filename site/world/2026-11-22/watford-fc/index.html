Ben Brereton Diaz double helps Sheffield United rout struggling Watford 4-0

By Karim Fouad · Published 22 Nov 2026, 12:31 UTC

Sheffield United surged to a comfortable home victory over Watford at Bramall Lane as Ben Brereton Diaz scored twice in the opening 10 minutes.

Ben Brereton Diaz struck twice within the first ten minutes at Bramall Lane to put Sheffield United in early control, leaving Watford without a road win for 14 consecutive games. The Chile international opened the scoring after five minutes before converting a penalty three minutes later following a foul by Martin Payero, effectively silencing any hope of a comeback from the visitors.

This lopsided result holds significant implications for Watford, as the Hornets have plummeted to 21st in the Championship table, hovering dangerously close to the relegation zone. Conversely, the victory boosts Sheffield United to ninth place, leaving Chris Wilder's side just two points outside the play-off positions as they prepare for their upcoming meeting with Southampton.

According to the BBC, Jordan Ayew added a header from a Tahith Chong cross before Sydie Peck tapped in a fourth goal following a pass from Patrick Bamford to finalize the 4-0 scoreline. The match proved a frustrating experience for the away side, with Watford manager Alessio Dionisi admitting his team lacked the necessary aggression and courage during their travels. Sky Sports reported that the encounter was a feisty affair, with referee Tim Robinson issuing 13 yellow cards throughout the afternoon as home goalkeeper Michael Cooper faced minimal pressure from the Watford attack.

Watford now faces immediate pressure to reverse their form, with questions mounting regarding the future of head coach Alessio Dionisi. The Hornets will look to improve their standing when they return to action for their next league fixture against Blackburn Rovers at Vicarage Road this Wednesday.
