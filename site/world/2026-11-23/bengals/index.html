Bengals Surge as 6-Point Favorites Against Commanders in Monday Night Football

By Karim Fouad · Published 24 Nov 2026, 02:01 UTC

The Cincinnati Bengals travel to face the Washington Commanders on Monday night in a matchup featuring a 54.5-point total.

Eighty-two percent of the spread handle is currently backing Cincinnati, as the public favors the Bengals by a significant margin ahead of their road contest. Betting markets indicate the team is favored by 5.5 to 6 points, with team totals projected at 30.25 for Cincinnati and 24.25 for Washington, according to Fantasy Footballers and FanDuel reports.

This game matters for both fan bases and bettors, as both teams are structured around high-volume passing and red-zone production. For those analyzing team rosters, Tee Higgins remains a primary target for quarterback Joe Burrow, holding six touchdowns on the season. Meanwhile, Chase Brown serves as the lead back and the primary option for Cincinnati in goal-line situations. On the opposing side, Stefon Diggs serves as a key receiving threat for Washington, holding six touchdowns of his own.

Cincinnati ranks third in passing EPA per dropback, providing a distinct challenge for a Washington pass defense currently ranked 23rd in the league. While the betting public largely supports a Cincinnati victory, the prediction markets show a difference of opinion regarding the point total. Fantasy Footballers notes that market makers lean toward the under, even though the broader betting handle currently favors the over at 62%.
