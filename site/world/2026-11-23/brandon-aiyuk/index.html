San Francisco 49ers release wide receiver Brandon Aiyuk following contract saga

By Karim Fouad · Published 23 Nov 2026, 19:33 UTC

The San Francisco 49ers are releasing wide receiver Brandon Aiyuk on Monday after over a year of negotiations.

Quick facts

Action

Date of announcement

Reported destination
Washington Commanders

The relationship between the San Francisco 49ers and wide receiver Brandon Aiyuk has reached its conclusion as the team parts ways with the former first-round pick. This decision brings an end to a standoff that persisted for more than a year, officially shifting the status of the receiver to free agency. ESPN's Adam Schefter reported the move on Monday, noting that the team is finalizing the release immediately following their Sunday Night Football victory. The release grants Aiyuk the freedom to sign with any other team in the league. For the 49ers, the move follows other front office adjustments made just before their Week 11 win. While the team moves on from their former standout, the professional future of the wide receiver remains a subject of intense speculation across the NFL. According to NFL Network's Ian Rapoport, the Washington Commanders are currently considered the most likely landing spot for the wide receiver. The logistics of the release are now being processed, allowing Aiyuk to explore his options in the open market. As a former first-round draft choice, Aiyuk's sudden availability changes the roster dynamics for multiple franchises looking to bolster their offensive production mid-season. Teams now have the opportunity to negotiate directly with Aiyuk to incorporate him into their offensive schemes for the remainder of the current campaign. The organization has not provided further statements regarding the specific terms of the departure, focusing instead on the team's ongoing progress after their latest result. Further updates regarding his signing will depend on official negotiations with potential new clubs.
