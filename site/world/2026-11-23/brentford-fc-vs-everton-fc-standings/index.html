Brentford routs Everton 4-0 as James Tarkowski sees a late red card

By Karim Fouad · Published 24 Nov 2026, 04:36 UTC

Brentford climbed into sixth place in the Premier League table after securing a dominant 4-0 victory over Everton.

Quick facts

Score
Brentford 4-0 Everton
League Position
Brentford moved to sixth
Red Card
James Tarkowski
Referee
Craig Pawson

Fabio Carvalho broke the deadlock by instinctively kneeing the ball over the line from a crowded penalty box, setting the tone for a one-sided contest. It was a brilliant night for Brentford, who capitalized on their momentum to dismantle the opposition throughout the evening.

This result moves Brentford into sixth place in the Premier League standings, marking a significant step up for the club. For Everton, the defeat represents a difficult outing that leaves the team searching for answers after conceding four times without a reply.

The match took a turn for the worse for the visitors when referee Craig Pawson upgraded a yellow card to a red for James Tarkowski following a VAR review. The officiating team determined that the Everton defender's challenge on Yarmoliuk endangered the safety of his opponent, citing high studs and direct contact with the shin. Sky Sports analyst Alan Smith noted the intensity of the challenge but questioned the severity of the decision given the context of the follow-through, while VAR officials emphasized the dangerous point of contact.

The decisive win leaves Brentford firmly established in the upper portion of the league table, while Everton must look toward their upcoming fixtures to address both their defensive vulnerabilities and disciplinary challenges.
