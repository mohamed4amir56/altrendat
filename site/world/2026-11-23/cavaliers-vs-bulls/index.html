Rested Cavaliers Storm Chicago Facing Exhausted Bulls After 151-145 Overtime Heartbreak

By Karim Fouad · Published 24 Nov 2026, 00:33 UTC

The Cleveland Cavaliers face the struggling Chicago Bulls on Monday, Nov. 23, at the United Center following Chicago's grueling overtime loss.

The Chicago Bulls enter Monday's matchup at the United Center under immense physical strain after playing an overtime game against the Denver Nuggets just last night. Every starter for Chicago logged over 40 minutes in that 151-145 loss, leaving them with less than 24 hours of recovery time before tipping off against a Cleveland Cavaliers squad that enjoyed an entire weekend off. This disparity in rest is a significant focal point as Cleveland looks to capitalize on their opponent's potential fatigue and slower pace.

For the Cavaliers, exploiting the Bulls' defensive vulnerabilities is essential to maintaining momentum. Chicago currently ranks among the league's worst defensively, allowing an average of 124.6 points per game. While Cleveland holds a high-scoring offense, their path to victory relies on tightening their own defensive execution. The Cavs have historically struggled to defend the perimeter, but they face a Chicago team that sits 28th in three-point shooting percentage, hitting less than 33% of their attempts.

Cleveland enters the contest with confidence, having secured a 128-119 victory over the Indiana Pacers in their previous outing. James Harden spearheaded that win with 51 points, complemented by Donovan Mitchell and Peyton Watson. In contrast, the Bulls arrive following two consecutive losses, despite a historic triple-double performance from Josh Giddey, who recorded 23 points, 22 assists, and 14 rebounds against Denver. Cleveland has historically dominated this rivalry, winning seven of the previous ten meetings between the two franchises. The Cavaliers are currently seeking to extend their recent success against a Chicago lineup featuring Giddey, Buddy Hield, and Matas Buzelis.
