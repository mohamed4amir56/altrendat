Pep Biel seals historic 2-1 Charlotte playoff win over Chicago Fire

By Karim Fouad · Published 23 Nov 2026, 06:31 UTC

Charlotte FC secured its first regulation postseason victory in club history by defeating the Chicago Fire 2-1 on Sunday.

Quick facts

Match Date
Sunday, November 22, 2026
Final Score
Charlotte FC 2, Chicago Fire 1
Venue
SeatGeek Stadium
Series
Audi 2026 MLS Cup Playoffs Round One Best-of-3

Pep Biel hammered home a penalty kick in the 11th minute of stoppage time, turning a tense standoff into a landmark result for Charlotte FC. The strike broke a deadlock at SeatGeek Stadium, providing Charlotte with its first regulation-time win in the club's four-year postseason history. The dramatic finish came just moments after goalkeeper Josh Cohen entered the match specifically to defend a potential penalty shootout, only to concede the deciding goal.

For the fifth-seeded Charlotte side, this result carries significant momentum into the best-of-three series. While the fourth-seeded Fire entered the playoffs with a higher conference standing, the loss hands Charlotte the advantage in the opening round. The victory also highlights Charlotte’s persistent offensive threat, having finished the regular season with 66 total goals.

Liel Abada opened the scoring for Charlotte three minutes into the second half, heading in an assist from Henry Kessler. The Fire responded in the 68th minute when Robert Lewandowski leveled the score with a header of his own. Despite the efforts of goalkeeper Chris Brady, who recorded two saves, the Fire defense could not withstand the late pressure. Charlotte goalkeeper Kristijan Kahlina anchored the defensive effort by stopping four shots throughout the match.

The two clubs will face each other again this Saturday for the second match of the series. The Fire are set to travel to Charlotte as they attempt to stay alive in the best-of-three matchup.
