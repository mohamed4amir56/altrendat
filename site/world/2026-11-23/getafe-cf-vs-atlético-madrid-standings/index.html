Diego Simeone Makes History as Atletico Madrid Routs Getafe 3-0 Away

By Karim Fouad · Published 23 Nov 2026, 05:31 UTC

Atlético Madrid secured their fifth consecutive away victory on Sunday by defeating Getafe with goals from Baena, Cardoso, and Grimaldo.

Atlético Madrid reached a historic milestone this Sunday, as manager Diego Simeone surpassed Luis Aragonés to become the coach with the most all-time LaLiga wins. The 3-0 result against Getafe CF marked Simeone's 345th victory in the competition’s 97-year history, cementing a dominant road performance that saw the team claim its sixth away win in seven attempts this season. By November, the team has already matched the total number of away wins achieved during the entirety of the previous campaign.

This trend signals a major shift in mentality for the squad, which had struggled with cagey, uninspiring displays on the road last year. The victory provides a clear reaction to earlier setbacks, proving that the current roster is capable of playing on the front foot with 20 total shots throughout the match. For supporters, the change from the tentative build-up play observed last season to this season's aggressive style marks a significant improvement in the team's ability to secure points away from home.

Álex Baena opened the scoring in the 9th minute with a strike from outside the box. Johnny Cardoso extended the lead in the 30th minute following a goalkeeping error by David Soria. Late in the match, a VAR review confirmed a penalty after Djené Dakonam fouled Alexander Sørloth, resulting in a red card for the Getafe defender. Alejandro Grimaldo converted the spot-kick to finish the scoring, marking his fifth goal of the season and securing the win for Los Rojiblancos.
