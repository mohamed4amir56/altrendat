Cam Skattebo Breaks Backflip Promise During Giants Win Over The Jaguars

By Karim Fouad · Published 23 Nov 2026, 11:31 UTC

Running back Cam Skattebo performed a backflip to seal the New York Giants' victory over the Jacksonville Jaguars just days after pledging to stop the celebration.

Quick facts

Game Result
New York Giants win over Jacksonville Jaguars
Celebration timing
Fourth quarter, over two minutes remaining
Game impact
Five-yard run for first down
Previous commitment
Retiring backflips after Aidan Hutchinson injury

Cam Skattebo chose to ignore his own promise, opting for a signature backflip with just over two minutes remaining in the fourth quarter against the Jacksonville Jaguars. The move followed a crucial five-yard run that secured a first down and cemented the Giants' win, leaving head coach John Harbaugh visibly surprised on the sidelines. Harbaugh later joked with reporters that his running back simply could not help himself, noting that the player's excitement took over during a high-stakes moment in the game.

This decision marks a sharp reversal from earlier in the week when Skattebo publicly stated he was retiring the celebratory maneuver. His initial decision came after witnessing Detroit Lions defensive end Aidan Hutchinson suffer an ACL tear during a post-sack leap. Skattebo had expressed concern about the potential for injury, telling reporters he would avoid the celebration mentally to ensure such an accident would not happen to him, though he had reserved the right to bring it back for potential playoff appearances.

The performance provided a boost for the Giants as they look to maintain their position atop the NFC East following a difficult loss to the Commanders. While Skattebo had a relatively quiet game overall, managing 34 yards on 14 carries and 14 yards on three receptions, his late-game play proved essential to topping the Super Bowl contenders.

Harbaugh indicated he held no significant grudge over the broken promise, largely because Skattebo remained healthy throughout the feat. The team now moves forward with the win, relying on Skattebo's contributions in their ongoing divisional campaign.
