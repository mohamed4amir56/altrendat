Jensen Huang Returns to Nvidia CEO Role as US Stocks Surge Higher

By Karim Fouad · Published 23 Nov 2026, 17:06 UTC · Updated 23 Nov 2026, 19:34 UTC

Nvidia stock leads a Wall Street rally Monday as the board unanimously reinstates Jensen Huang as President and CEO.

Following the surprise return of Jensen Huang, U.S. markets are recovering more than half of their brutal losses from last week. Nvidia, which acts as a primary backer for the artificial intelligence industry, is currently leading the massive rebound on Wall Street. The return of the company's leader has provided a significant boost to investor confidence as markets attempt to claw back from the recent downturn. This leadership transition signals a pivot point for the tech giant as it navigates a new industrial revolution. The board of directors voted unanimously to reinstate Mr. Huang, citing his ability to lead and a confirmed lack of national security risk following government input. The decision was made after discussions regarding Mr. Huang, who had previously been the subject of a foreign intelligence investigation. Harvey C. Jones, the Chair of the Board, expressed that the reinstatement serves the best interests of shareholders, employees, and partners. The board noted that Colette Kress will step down from her role as interim CEO while continuing her duties as Chief Financial Officer. Despite the leadership change, the committee of independent directors tasked with investigating the foreign intelligence targeting of Mr. Huang will continue its work to conclusion. Mr. Huang has publicly stated his commitment to cooperate fully with that ongoing investigation. As he resumes his position, he expressed gratitude to the board and staff for their support during his absence. The market reaction underscores Nvidia's critical role in the broader technology sector and its influence on current stock performance. Investors now look toward the next chapter of the company under this returning leadership as the investigation concludes.
