Kagiso Rabada storms Centurion as Proteas host Bangladesh in second Test match

By Karim Fouad · Published 23 Nov 2026, 09:01 UTC

South Africa and Bangladesh clash in the second Test at SuperSport Park in Centurion, with the Proteas looking to secure a 2-0 series victory.

Quick facts

Venue
SuperSport Park, Centurion
Start Date
Monday
South Africa Captain
Aiden Markram

Kagiso Rabada enters this match as a dominant force at his most successful home ground, boasting 59 wickets at an impressive average of 17.96 and a strike rate of 31.5. His performance at this venue remains a primary threat to the visiting side as the series moves to Centurion following the first Test in Johannesburg.

This match holds significant weight for the series outcome, as South Africa looks to close out a 2-0 victory against a competitive Bangladesh squad. Although Bangladesh posted a respectable 400-run first innings total in the previous Test, highlighted by centuries from Towhid Hridoy and Litton Das, South Africa remains the statistical favorite. The hosts maintain a commanding record at SuperSport Park, having secured 24 wins across 30 previous Test matches played at the venue.

According to Cricbuzz, both teams face roster adjustments due to injury. South Africa will be without captain Temba Bavuma, with Aiden Markram stepping in to lead the side. Bangladesh is similarly depleted, missing high-capped veteran Mushfiqur Rahim and primary strike bowler Hasan Mahmud. Despite these absences, South Africa bolsters its bowling attack with the return of Anrich Nortjé, adding further pressure on the Bangladeshi batting lineup.

Markram brings prior leadership experience to the role, having captained South Africa to two Test victories over Bangladesh during their October 2024 series in Dhaka and Chattogram. The match is scheduled to begin on Monday at SuperSport Park, where the Proteas aim to maintain their historical dominance.
