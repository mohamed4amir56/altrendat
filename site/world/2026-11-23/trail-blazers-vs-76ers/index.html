Sixers face Western Conference opposition for first time in 18th game

By Karim Fouad · Published 23 Nov 2026, 23:31 UTC

The 14-3 Philadelphia 76ers host the 10-6 Portland Trail Blazers on Monday night in their first matchup against a Western Conference team this season.

Quick facts

Matchup
Portland Trail Blazers at Philadelphia 76ers
Date
Monday, November 23th
Time
4:00 PM Pacific
Location
Xfinity Mobile Arena

The 18-game wait to face a Western Conference opponent ties the 1979-80 Atlanta Hawks for the second-longest delay by an Eastern Conference team in history. Only the 1980-81 Milwaukee Bucks went longer, waiting until their 21st game to meet a team from the West. While Philadelphia has built a league-leading 14-3 record, every one of their contests to date has been against Eastern Conference rivals.

This game signals a shift in the schedule for the 14-3 76ers, who carry a five-game winning streak into the contest. Following Monday night, Philadelphia faces a demanding stretch including consecutive games against Memphis and Denver next week, followed by a series of road games against the Lakers, Clippers, Kings, and Suns. The test provides an early look at how the East-leading 76ers compare to a Western Conference that held 11 teams above .500 through Sunday.

Portland enters the arena after a 97-96 loss to the Knicks, where they overcame a 26-point deficit only to fall on a buzzer-beater. Under first-year coach Micah Nori, the Blazers average 116.1 points per game and rank second in the NBA with 15.4 made three-pointers per game. Deni Avdija leads the squad with 23.9 points per game, while 7-foot-2 center Donovan Clingan provides a significant presence in the paint.

Monday night's game at the Xfinity Mobile Arena concludes a six-game road trip for Portland. The Blazers will look to overcome a recent trend of slow starts to remain competitive against the 76ers, who themselves are coming off a 132-129 win over the Milwaukee Bucks.
