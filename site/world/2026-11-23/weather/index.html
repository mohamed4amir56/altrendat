Seven South African provinces under thunderstorm warning as heavy downpours hit KwaZulu-Natal

By Karim Fouad · Published 23 Nov 2026, 05:01 UTC

Disaster management teams in KwaZulu-Natal are on high alert Sunday following a yellow level 4 weather warning for damaging winds and heavy rain.

Quick facts

Warning Level
Yellow level 4
Affected Province
KwaZulu-Natal
Weather Hazards
Heavy downpours, damaging winds, hail, severe lightning
Warning Date
Sunday, 22 November 2026

Residents across seven provinces face a thunderstorm warning this Sunday, while the KwaZulu-Natal Department of Cooperative Governance and Traditional Affairs has placed disaster management teams on high alert. The region is bracing for the continuation of heavy downpours, damaging winds, hail, and severe lightning throughout the day.

This level 4 warning specifically targets the northern and central parts of KwaZulu-Natal. The intensity of the forecasted weather conditions poses a significant risk to the safety of residents, particularly those located in low-lying areas and informal settlements. Officials are highlighting the potential for widespread damage as these weather patterns persist.

According to Cooperative Governance spokesperson Siboniso Mngadi, community members must adhere to safety warnings and prioritize finding shelter. MEC for COGTA, Thulasizwe Buthelezi, issued a direct call for vigilance among motorists and residents alike, citing that recent rains have already caused significant devastation in various parts of the province. The South African Weather Service reports that these conditions are expected to remain a threat for the duration of Sunday.

The alert remains active as authorities monitor the impact of the storm systems across the province. With disaster management teams deployed and on standby, officials continue to urge the public to monitor official updates and avoid high-risk environments until the weather stabilizes.
