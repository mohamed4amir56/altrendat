Cristiano Ronaldo becomes Al-Nassr all-time top scorer in AFC Champions League Elite

By Karim Fouad · Published 24 Nov 2026, 23:01 UTC

Cristiano Ronaldo broke his penalty curse with a 13th-minute strike against Al-Sadd to become Al-Nassr's record scorer in the AFC Champions League Elite.

Quick facts

Ronaldo record
17 goals for Al-Nassr in AFC Champions League Elite
Venue
Al-Awwal Park, Riyadh
Goal time
13th minute
Career goal count
987

Cristiano Ronaldo reached a historic milestone at Al-Awwal Park by netting his 17th goal in the AFC Champions League Elite. This crucial penalty in the 13th minute officially moved him past Abderrazak Hamdallah, who held the previous club record with 16 goals in the competition. The successful strike also ended a recent spot-kick drought for the Portuguese star, who had missed consecutive penalties during his recent Saudi Pro League appearances.

The victory carries significant weight for Al-Nassr’s standing in the regional tournament as they push to improve their league stage position. With Al-Sadd struggling to secure a win in their four matches, the match served as a vital opportunity for the Saudi side to capitalize on their home-field advantage and solidify their campaign against a team currently sitting 15th in the standings with only two points.

According to Goal.com, the goal represents the 987th of Ronaldo's professional career, placing him only 13 goals away from the 1,000 mark. The match occurred during matchday five of the AFC Champions League Elite. Ronaldo’s contribution was essential, as he remains a central figure for the club following his arrival in January 2023. Sports Mole notes that while Al-Nassr sits seventh in the standings with six points from three matches, their domestic form also remains strong, keeping them within striking distance of the Saudi Pro League leaders.

As the tournament progresses, all eyes are on whether Al-Nassr can maintain this momentum toward the knockout stages. Following this fixture, the club continues its dual challenge of balancing domestic league duties with the rigorous demands of the Asian campaign.
