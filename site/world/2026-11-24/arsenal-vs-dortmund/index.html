Viktor Gyokeres strike leads Arsenal to frustrating Champions League draw against Dortmund

By Karim Fouad · Published 24 Nov 2026, 23:31 UTC

Arsenal failed to secure a home win as Maximilian Beier’s 55th-minute equalizer forced a 1-1 draw against Borussia Dortmund.

Viktor Gyokeres gave the Gunners an early edge by firing a powerful looping effort over goalkeeper Gregor Kobel from the edge of the box. Despite controlling the first half with close calls from Christos Tzolis, Noni Madueke, and Declan Rice, Arsenal could not extend their single-goal lead before the break. The missed opportunities proved costly as the visitors shifted momentum in the second half, eventually finding their footing to share the points at Emirates Stadium.

This result directly impacts the standings in the Champions League league phase, leaving Arsenal in fifth place with 11 points. For Borussia Dortmund, the hard-fought point keeps them within striking distance as they sit seventh, one point behind their London opponents. The draw highlights a tactical shift in the second half where Dortmund’s defense tightened and their transition play improved, ultimately preventing the Gunners from pulling further ahead in the table.

According to the BBC, the equalizer came when Dortmund centre-back Joane Gadou carried the ball from deep within his own half to set up Maximilian Beier for a clinical finish into the corner in the 55th minute. Arsenal manager Mikel Arteta attempted to regain the lead with a triple substitution, but a late chance went begging when Gyokeres mistimed a header from inside the six-yard box. Dortmund briefly thought they had snatched the win late in the match when Jobe Bellingham tapped in a cross, but the effort was ruled out due to an offside flag.

Arsenal must now regroup as they continue their campaign, looking to maintain their standing in the league phase. The club remains focused on capitalizing on upcoming matches to secure their position for the later stages of the competition.
