Jack Edwards clinches thriller as Australia overcomes England in T20 series opener

By Karim Fouad · Published 24 Nov 2026, 08:01 UTC

Australia secured a tense final-over victory against England at the Melbourne Cricket Ground to take a 1-0 lead in the five-match series.

Nikhil Chaudhary delivered a standout performance, hitting 66 runs and taking 2-21 to help Australia chase down a target of 191 with three wickets and just one ball remaining. This result marks the highest successful T20 chase for Australia on home soil, proving vital as the team fields a host of new faces to move past recent off-field controversies.

For cricket fans, this opening match signals a shift in momentum toward the series newcomers. The game was marked by high tension and on-field exchanges, particularly involving Chaudhary during his match-defining knock. The atmosphere turned electric at the Melbourne Cricket Ground as the crowd reacted to England's attempts to slow the pace in the closing moments of the game.

England set a competitive total of 187-9, fueled by Harry Brook’s aggressive 65 off 28 balls. Despite England’s efforts to defend 11 runs in the final over, Jack Edwards remained composed to finish 25 not out and seal the win. The performance of Chaudhary, who moved from Delhi to Brisbane, provided the necessary spark to counter the early loss of opener Jake Fraser-McGurk.

With Australia holding a 1-0 series lead, the pressure now shifts to the second match of the series. England will look to level the contest when the two teams meet again on the Gold Coast this Tuesday.
