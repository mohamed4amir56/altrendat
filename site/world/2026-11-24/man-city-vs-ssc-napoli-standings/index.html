Rayan Cherki snatches late point as Manchester City’s winless streak stretches further

By Karim Fouad · Published 25 Nov 2026, 00:31 UTC · Updated 25 Nov 2026, 01:01 UTC

Manchester City were held to a 2-2 Champions League draw at home against Napoli after a late strike from Rayan Cherki.

Quick facts

Final Score
2-2
City Goalscorers
Ayyoub Bouaddi, Rayan Cherki
Napoli Goalscorers
Rasmus Hojlund, Noa Lang
Next Match

Manchester City have now managed just one victory in their past seven matches across all competitions, as the club’s recent run of poor form continued with a 2-2 stalemate against Napoli. City manager Enzo Maresca admitted the team is struggling to maintain momentum, while forward Erling Haaland noted that the squad has begun questioning their approach during a difficult autumn period. Haaland, who has failed to find the net in his last three club appearances, conceded that the team is currently letting opponents in far too easily. This result comes as the side prepares for a challenging upcoming fixture against Arsenal this Sunday, placing additional pressure on the manager to address defensive vulnerabilities. Napoli took the lead through a Rasmus Hojlund finish in the second half, before Moroccan teenager Ayyoub Bouaddi netted his first goal for City to level the score. Napoli regained the advantage in the 76th minute when Noa Lang converted a volley from a cross. City eventually salvaged a point through an angled strike from Rayan Cherki, who capitalized on his persistence during the closing stages. The match at the Etihad Stadium saw City struggle to build rhythm for long stretches, with both sides failing to create significant opportunities during a quiet first half. The visitors currently sit eighth in the Serie A standings, having remained comfortable against a laboured City performance for much of the evening. Supporters voiced frustration late in the match, particularly when referee Istvan Kovacs blew the whistle 20 seconds before the official added time concluded, though it was later clarified that the stadium clock was at fault.
