Maura Higgins chases Len Goodman Mirrorball trophy in Dancing with Stars finale

By Karim Fouad · Published 25 Nov 2026, 02:01 UTC

Irish reality star Maura Higgins will compete in the Dancing with the Stars US finale tonight alongside partner Mark Ballas.

Maura Higgins secured her spot in the finale by earning a perfect 30 out of 30 for her samba and a 29 for her Viennese waltz during the semi-finals. The County Longford native will bring a flavor of Ireland to her performances, which include a judges' choice dance, an instant dance challenge, and a freestyle section. She faces competition from actress Tatyana Ali, Paralympian Ezra Frech, comedian Connor Wood, and Olympic figure skater Amber Glenn for the Len Goodman Mirrorball trophy.

The final represents another milestone for the 35-year-old, whose career has expanded from a hairdressing job in Ballymahon to high-profile appearances in the UK and across the Atlantic. Supporters in her hometown, including local business owner Pat Byrne and former teacher Anne Tully, emphasize that her success is a result of resilience and hard work. For fans, the event highlights her transition from Love Island contestant to a versatile performer who has also competed on The Traitors US and fronted major television projects.

Hosted by Alfonso Ribeiro and Julianne Hough, the show features a judging panel of Derek Hough, Carrie Ann Inaba, and Bruno Tonioli. While Higgins has been ranked as an outside bet for the title, her recent performances have consistently earned high marks from the judges. Voting remains restricted to residents of the US and Canada.

The final airs tonight, with the winner determined by the combination of judge scores and public votes. Following this appearance, Higgins is set to return to television next year as the presenter for a reboot of Blind Date.
