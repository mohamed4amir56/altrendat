Creamline dispatches Choco Mucho in four sets to book Finals berth

By Karim Fouad · Published 24 Nov 2026, 15:31 UTC · Updated 24 Nov 2026, 19:31 UTC

Creamline clinched the first spot in the 2026 PVL All-Filipino Conference Siklab Series Finals after defeating Choco Mucho on Tuesday.

Quick facts

Match Date
Tuesday
Venue
Philsports Arena
Final Score
21-25, 25-17, 25-17, 25-23

The Cool Smashers surged past the Flying Titans with a 21-25, 25-17, 25-17, 25-23 victory at the Philsports Arena. This win serves as a comeback for the team, which recently endured a rare three-game losing skid before reclaiming their championship form. Following a difficult elimination round loss to Choco Mucho on November 12, the team utilized their experience to secure the semifinal triumph.

This victory is vital for the team's pursuit of another league title. By overcoming the Flying Titans, the squad demonstrated the composure needed to navigate pressure, successfully rebounding from the first-set loss to dominate the middle frames. The ability to recover quickly from their previous three-game slump highlights the team's reliance on their system and collective performance.

According to Tiebreaker Times, Jia De Guzman led the team with 19 excellent sets, while six different players contributed at least eight points each. Tots Carlos spearheaded the offense with 13 points, supported by Michele Gumabao with 11 markers and Bernadeth Pons, who added 11 points alongside 10 excellent digs and 14 excellent receptions. In the fourth set, the Cool Smashers trailed 21-19 before rallying to force a deadlock. A late service error by Caitlin Viray and an attack error from Eya Laure ultimately handed Creamline the match point.

Head coach Sherwin Meneses noted that while the three-game losing streak caused frustration, the team's focus remained on recovering game by game. With this result, Creamline now advances to the championship round of the Siklab Series.
