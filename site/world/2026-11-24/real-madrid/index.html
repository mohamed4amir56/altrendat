Mourinho benching Bellingham as Vinícius returns for Champions League against PSV

By Karim Fouad · Published 24 Nov 2026, 20:31 UTC · Updated 24 Nov 2026, 21:12 UTC

Real Madrid coach Jose Mourinho makes six lineup changes for the Champions League clash against PSV, including resting Jude Bellingham.

Quick facts

Match
Real Madrid vs PSV Eindhoven
Competition
Champions League, 5th round
Venue
Santiago Bernabéu
Date
Tuesday
Time
21:00

Real Madrid head coach Jose Mourinho is rotating his squad heavily for tonight’s Champions League match, as Jude Bellingham moves to the bench to accommodate six changes from the team's last appearance. The decision follows Mourinho’s prior warning regarding the heavy schedule, which sees the squad playing three matches within an eight-day window.

This shift in strategy highlights the internal squad management required as Madrid navigates a packed European and domestic calendar. With Vinícius Jr. returning to the starting eleven, the tactical setup shifts significantly, placing Arda Güler in the '10' role, with Diomande on the right and Vinícius operating on the left to support Kylian Mbappé.

The starting defensive line features Valverde and Cucurella as full-backs, with Militao and Rüdiger anchoring the center, while Bernardo and Tchouaméni form the double pivot. Courtois remains the confirmed starter in goal for the encounter at the Santiago Bernabéu.

Opponents PSV Eindhoven arrive with their own tactical adjustment, as coach Peter Bosz brings in 20-year-old Paul Wanner to replace Mauro Júnior, who faces a long-term injury layoff until the end of the year. While questions previously surrounded the fitness of Sergiño Dest following his recent international break, the defender confirmed his availability for tonight’s match.
