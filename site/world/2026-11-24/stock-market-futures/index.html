Nasdaq futures dip after Magnificent Seven adds $1.65 trillion in value

By Karim Fouad · Published 24 Nov 2026, 11:01 UTC

U.S. stock futures remain mixed on Tuesday following Monday's massive AI-driven market rally.

Nvidia alone accounted for $950 billion of the $1.65 trillion in market capitalization added by the Magnificent Seven group during Monday's trading session. This surge marked the best performance for the S&P 500 and Nasdaq Composite since April 2025.

Investors are now calibrating their expectations as Nvidia shares declined 3% on Tuesday following a blistering 31% rally the previous day. While Nasdaq-100 contracts dipped 0.3%, the momentum persisted in Asian markets, with Japan's Nikkei index rising nearly 4% and South Korea's Kospi surging 7%.

The shift in market sentiment follows renewed inflation concerns linked to the AI-driven rally, prompting traders to adjust their interest rate forecasts. According to Yahoo Finance, there is now a 95% probability that the Federal Reserve will implement a 25 basis point rate hike in December, with a 5% chance of a 50 basis point increase. Meanwhile, oil prices have retreated by approximately 1%.

Looking ahead to the upcoming Thanksgiving and Black Friday period, market participants are monitoring a wave of corporate earnings reports. Scheduled releases include Dell Technologies, Dollar Tree, HP, Best Buy, and Dick's Sporting Goods. Additionally, the economic calendar features upcoming data releases regarding consumer confidence and ADP's weekly employment metrics.
