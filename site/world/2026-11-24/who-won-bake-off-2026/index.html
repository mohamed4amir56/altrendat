Mo, Moyin, and Tom Clash in All-Male Great British Bake Off Final

By Karim Fouad · Published 24 Nov 2026, 21:31 UTC

The 17th series of The Great British Bake Off concludes on Tuesday night with three young finalists competing for the crown.

Law student Mo Mahir, political economy graduate Moyin Odeniran, and hairdresser Tom Whittaker are set to battle for the 17th Bake Off title. The grand final features an entirely male lineup, with each contestant aged 25 or under. Tom Whittaker enters the final as the bookies' favourite, having already secured the Star Baker title three times throughout the competition. The finalists have endured nine weeks of challenges in the tent to reach this concluding episode. The series has been marked by the arrival of new judge Nigella Lawson, who joined Paul Hollywood for this edition, sparking a resurgence in popularity. With viewing figures reaching 7.1 million for the series opener, the show has outperformed other major television programmes, cementing its status as a ratings success for Channel 4. Lawson’s presence has been widely credited with providing a fresh dynamic alongside the veteran judge, helping the franchise overcome previous signs of stagnation. Fans and critics have noted the contrast in styles between the judges, with Lawson’s articulate and passionate approach to tasting becoming a signature element of the season. The competition concludes on Tuesday night, when the winner will be crowned following the final set of challenges.
