Sixers Star Joel Embiid Ruled Out For NBA Cup Against Miami Heat

By Karim Fouad · Published 25 Nov 2026, 23:01 UTC

Joel Embiid will miss his second consecutive game due to illness as Philadelphia hosts Miami for Wednesday's NBA Cup matchup.

The Philadelphia 76ers will be without center Joel Embiid for Wednesday's NBA Cup game against the Miami Heat, as he remains sidelined by illness. Tony Jones of The Athletic confirmed the official downgrade from doubtful to out shortly before tip-off, marking the fifth game Embiid has missed during the 18-game season. Embiid also sat out Monday's 128-111 loss to the Portland Trail Blazers.

His absence creates a significant hole in the 76ers lineup, as Embiid currently serves as the team's third-leading scorer with 20.4 points per game and leads the squad with 8.9 rebounds. Despite his recent strong performance, which included a near triple-double of 26 points, 17 rebounds, and 9 assists in a 132-129 win over the Milwaukee Bucks, the team must now adjust its rotation to face the visiting Heat without their primary interior presence.

Looking ahead, the 76ers face a challenging schedule with back-to-back road games looming. Philadelphia is set to visit the Indiana Pacers on Friday before traveling to play the Houston Rockets on Saturday. Head coach Nick Nurse stated on Wednesday that the organization has not yet determined whether Embiid will join the team for the upcoming road trip, citing both his ongoing illness and the need to manage his right foot plantar fascia.
