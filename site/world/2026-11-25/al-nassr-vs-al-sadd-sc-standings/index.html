João Félix bicycle kick and Ronaldo penalty clinch win over Al-Sadd

By Karim Fouad · Published 25 Nov 2026, 05:31 UTC

Al-Nassr defeated Al-Sadd 2-1 on Tuesday at Al-Awwal Park during matchday five of the AFC Champions League Elite.

João Félix secured the victory with a bicycle kick in the 52nd minute, collecting a high pass on the edge of the penalty area and firing past goalkeeper Meshaal Barsham. Félix described the spectacular strike as potentially the most beautiful goal of his career, noting that his role this season involves linking build-up play before moving into positions to combine with captain Cristiano Ronaldo.

The win helps Al-Nassr maintain momentum in the AFC Champions League Elite league stage. For supporters, the result marks a significant shift as the club continues to integrate its high-quality group, with players like Sadio Mane, Angelo, and Félix showing deep familiarity with each other's movements on the pitch.

Cristiano Ronaldo opened the scoring in the 13th minute by converting a penalty after he was brought down inside the box. This goal ended a personal streak of spot-kick difficulties for the Portuguese forward and moved him past Abderrazak Hamdallah to become Al-Nassr's all-time top scorer in the AFC Champions League Elite. Ronaldo now holds 17 goals for the club in the competition. The strike also marked the 987th goal of his professional career, bringing him within 13 goals of the 1,000 mark.

Al-Nassr played at home in the Saudi capital of Riyadh, with Félix highlighting that the team remains focused on maintaining its high level of play throughout the tournament.
