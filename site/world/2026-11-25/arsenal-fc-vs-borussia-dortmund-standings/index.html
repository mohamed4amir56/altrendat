Viktor Gyokeres strike leads as Arsenal held by Borussia Dortmund in Europe

By Karim Fouad · Published 25 Nov 2026, 05:31 UTC

Arsenal remain fifth in the Champions League standings after a 1-1 draw with Borussia Dortmund at the Emirates Stadium.

Arsenal squandered 30 shots during their Champions League clash against Borussia Dortmund, marking their highest total in the competition across this season and the last. Despite this offensive volume, only four of those attempts hit the target, leaving the Gunners to share the points after a frustrating second-half lapse.

This result keeps Arsenal fifth in the league phase table with 11 points, while Dortmund sits two places behind them in seventh. For Mikel Arteta's side, the failure to secure a win follows a recurring pattern of missed opportunities, mirroring a recent league draw against Newcastle where early dominance also failed to translate into a decisive lead.

Viktor Gyokeres opened the scoring with a powerful looping strike from the edge of the box, but Dortmund improved significantly after the interval. The equalizer arrived in the 55th minute when Joane Gadou carried the ball from deep before setting up Maximilian Beier for a clinical finish past Kepa Arrizabalaga. Dortmund nearly secured a turnaround late in the game, though an effort from Jobe Bellingham was disallowed by the offside flag.

Arsenal now prepare to return to domestic action, with a crucial Premier League fixture against Manchester City scheduled for Sunday. The team looks to improve its clinical edge after failing to capitalize on multiple chances, including a header from Gyokeres inside the six-yard box that narrowly missed the mark.
