Former Pinoy Big Brother Housemate Hasna Cabral Dies At Age 37

By Karim Fouad · Published 25 Nov 2026, 12:01 UTC · Updated 25 Nov 2026, 12:31 UTC

Former television host and reality star Hasna Cabral died following a stroke attack on November 25.

Cause of death

Hasna Cabral was 37 years old when she passed away after experiencing a stroke that severely affected her brain activity. Her family announced the news of her death on Wednesday, November 25, requesting that supporters keep her memory alive by prioritizing their own health and well-being. According to the family, she had been unable to afford the rest she truly deserved during her life and they encouraged others to cherish their own time and express love to their families at every opportunity. The loss of the former Pinoy Big Brother Otso contestant has prompted an outpouring of public interest in her final moments. However, her relatives clarified that she has already been buried in accordance with Islamic rites, honoring her religious faith immediately following her passing. The family expressed deep appreciation for the public support and condolences they have received since the announcement. They explicitly asked fans and followers to refrain from organizing or contributing to any donation drives initiated in her name. They emphasized that thoughts, prayers, and du'a are already more than enough during this time of mourning. As she was laid to rest promptly to comply with her religious obligations, the family remains focused on their private grieving process while honoring her legacy through the simple, heartfelt messages shared with the public. They continue to urge fans to maintain their own health as a way of keeping her memory active, reflecting on her life and the sudden nature of her departure.
