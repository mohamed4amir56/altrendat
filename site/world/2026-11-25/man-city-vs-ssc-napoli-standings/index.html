Rayan Cherki late strike rescues draw for struggling Manchester City against Napoli

By Karim Fouad · Published 25 Nov 2026, 05:01 UTC

Manchester City's winless streak continued as they were held to a 2-2 draw by Napoli in the Champions League.

Competition

Manchester City has now won only one of its past seven games in all competitions following a frustrating stalemate. The home side struggled to find momentum throughout the match, leaving supporters visibly impatient as the team failed to secure a victory at home. Manager Enzo Maresca noted the team has dropped off dramatically since a perfect start to the season. The result highlights a continuing decline for a squad that was once considered a slick machine but is now grinding to a halt. While City dominated early stages, they were unable to convert chances, with Elliot Anderson seeing a strike batted away by Vanja Milinkovic-Savic in the opening minute. The defensive unit showed vulnerabilities, particularly when Rasmus Hojlund turned Marc Guehi inside out to open the scoring for Napoli. Although teenager Ayyoub Bouaddi equalized with his first goal for the club, Napoli quickly regained the lead through a Noa Lang volley. Rayan Cherki finally secured the point with a late, low drive into the bottom corner after Josko Gvardiol had previously hit the post. Following this result, the club will look to address their ongoing defensive gaps and lack of attacking consistency before their next competitive challenge. The performance underlined concerns regarding tactical positioning, as highlighted by Nico O'Reilly leaving a hole in the midfield that allowed Napoli to exploit the space for their second goal.
