Cameron Pring equalizes as Bristol City under Skubala stops losing streak

By Karim Fouad · Published 25 Nov 2026, 12:01 UTC

Bristol City salvaged a 1-1 Championship draw against Wrexham at Ashton Gate after Cameron Pring scored a second-half goal to cancel out Joe Worrall's opener.

Bristol City head coach Michael Skubala faced explicit demands for his resignation from the home crowd as fans chanted about his employment prospects following Joe Worrall's seventh-minute header. The early deficit left the Robins trailing as boos echoed around the stadium at halftime, marking a difficult start for a team that had lost its previous four home fixtures. The atmosphere remained tense as Bristol City struggled to register a single shot on target during the opening 45 minutes, managing only five touches inside the opposing box.

Securing this point provides essential relief for Bristol City, allowing the side to climb two spots in the Sky Bet Championship table to 16th. For the club, ending a streak of three consecutive defeats offers a stabilizing outcome after a disheartening period of form. Conversely, Wrexham will likely view the result as a missed opportunity, as the team failed to capitalize on the hosts' early lack of confidence and extended their own unbeaten run to four matches without claiming all three points.

The game shifted significantly after the interval when Cameron Pring found the net in the 51st minute, marking his first goal for the club since December 2023. According to Sky Sports, Bristol City improved intensity late in the game, culminating in a controversial moment during stoppage time when fans unsuccessfully appealed for a penalty following a collision between Danny Imray and Scott Twine. Despite the pressure, Wrexham remained dangerous, with Ollie Rathbone striking the post eight minutes from time.

Bristol City now looks to build on this second-half display to improve their standing further. Wrexham remains 14th in the league, continuing their unbeaten streak as they look toward future matches to maintain momentum.
