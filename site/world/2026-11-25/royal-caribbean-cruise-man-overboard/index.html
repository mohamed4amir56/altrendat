Search Underway as Passenger Plunges From Royal Caribbean Ship Near Bahamas

By Karim Fouad · Published 25 Nov 2026, 07:31 UTC

The Royal Bahamas Defence Force and U.S. Coast Guard are searching for a man who went overboard Monday night near San Salvador Island.

Quick facts

Ship name
Allure of the Seas
Date of incident
Monday, Nov. 23
Location
San Salvador Island, Bahamas
Coast Guard notification time
11:30 p.m. Monday

The cruise ship’s public address system broadcast a passenger’s name repeatedly for several hours before the crew confirmed that the individual had gone overboard. Passengers aboard the Allure of the Seas described a somber atmosphere, noting that the ship turned around from its original course toward St. Maarten to begin searching the waters off San Salvador Island, Bahamas.

This incident highlights the serious security and safety procedures implemented during emergency operations at sea. As the ship's itinerary faces disruption, Royal Caribbean’s care team is currently providing support to the missing guest's loved ones while the vessel remains involved in the search-and-rescue efforts coordinated with regional authorities.

According to CBS News and reports from passengers, the ship’s captain informed those on board that the crew had received a report of a missing guest earlier on Monday before confirming the man had gone overboard. The U.S. Coast Guard confirmed they were alerted to the situation at approximately 11:30 p.m. Monday. The Royal Bahamas Defence Force is leading the search, with the U.S. Coast Guard providing assistance to the ongoing operation.

The Allure of the Seas had departed from PortMiami on Sunday for an eight-day Thanksgiving cruise. While the vessel was originally scheduled to visit Philipsburg, St. Maarten, St. Thomas, and Puerto Plata, the captain announced that the emergency situation would require changes to the planned voyage. As of Tuesday, Nov. 24, officials had not confirmed that the passenger had been located, and the identity of the individual has not been publicly released by authorities.
