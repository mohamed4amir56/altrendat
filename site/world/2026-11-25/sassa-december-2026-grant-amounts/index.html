SASSA Confirms December 2026 Grant Payment Dates and Official Monthly Payout Amounts

By Karim Fouad · Published 25 Nov 2026, 12:01 UTC · Updated 25 Nov 2026, 12:32 UTC

The South African Social Security Agency will begin staggering social grant payments on December 2, 2026.

Quick facts

Older Persons Grant Date
December 2
Disability Grant Date
December 3
Children's Grants Date
December 4
Older Persons Grant Amount
R2 400 (R2 420 for 75+)
Child Support Grant Amount
R580

Older persons are first in line to receive their funds on December 2, followed by disability grant beneficiaries on December 3, and children’s grant recipients on December 4. This staggered schedule, approved by the National Treasury, aims to reduce crowding at payment points across the country during the busy festive season. The agency emphasizes that there are no bonus payments or double grants issued this month, as all beneficiaries will receive their standard monthly allocations.

For households managing festive season expenses, these set dates provide a clear timeline for budget planning. SASSA maintains that ensuring timely access to these funds is a priority, and the agency remains committed to a smooth distribution process for all eligible recipients.

Regarding individual payment amounts, the agency confirmed that beneficiaries will receive the rates established during the 2026 financial year. The Older Persons Grant and Disability Grant are set at R2 400, while those 75 and older receive R2 420. The Care Dependency Grant is R2 400, the Foster Child Grant is R1 300, and the Child Support Grant and Grant-in-Aid are both R580, according to The Witness. The War Veterans Grant is set at R2 420.

Beneficiaries who do not receive their expected funds on the designated days are advised by SASSA to visit their local office for assistance. Additionally, the agency reminds recipients that they are legally obligated to disclose any changes in their financial status, such as securing employment, which may impact their eligibility for future assistance.
