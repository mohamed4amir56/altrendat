Paralympic Athlete Ezra Frech Snatches Dancing With The Stars Mirrorball Trophy Win

By Karim Fouad · Published 25 Nov 2026, 08:01 UTC

Ezra Frech and partner Daniella Karagach were crowned winners of the 35th season of Dancing with the Stars after a live final on Tuesday.

Ezra Frech and professional partner Daniella Karagach claimed the top prize in the US version of Dancing with the Stars, securing the Len Goodman Mirrorball Trophy during Tuesday night’s live final. The victory concluded a three-hour broadcast that saw finalists achieve perfect scores from the judges across multiple high-stakes rounds. While other contestants like Maura Higgins earned 90 out of 90 points for their performances, the final outcome relied on a combination of these judges' scores and the crucial public vote.

The public vote solidified the standing for Frech and Karagach, who competed alongside four other pairs in the season finale. This competitive field included second-place finishers Amber Glenn and Pasha Pashkov, third-place duo Connor Wood and Rylee Arnold, fourth-place competitors Tatyana Ali and Jan Ravnik, and fifth-place Maura Higgins and Mark Ballas. The result reflects the final consensus of both the expert panel and the audience after the season's intense live performances.

Following the announcement, the 35th season of the long-running entertainment show officially reached its conclusion. Frech, a Paralympic athlete, reached the top of the leaderboard alongside Higgins and Glenn, as all three earned perfect scores across their three individual routines. The event served as the final chapter for the current season, which featured contestants from diverse backgrounds in sports, comedy, and television.
