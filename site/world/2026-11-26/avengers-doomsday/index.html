Avengers, X-Men and Fantastic Four Assemble Against Robert Downey Jr.'s Doctor Doom

By Karim Fouad · Published 26 Nov 2026, 20:31 UTC

Marvel Studios will release Avengers: Doomsday in theaters on December 18, featuring a massive crossover that marks the return of Robert Downey Jr. as the film's primary villain.

Quick facts

Release date
December 18
Primary villain
Robert Downey Jr. as Doctor Doom
Directors
Joe and Anthony Russo
Franchise status
MCU fifth Avengers movie

For the first time in Marvel cinematic history, the Avengers, X-Men, and Fantastic Four will unite on screen to confront a massive existential threat. This unprecedented crossover film forces heroes from three different Earths to band together to face the looming danger of total extinction, according to Hypebeast.

This release holds significant weight for fans as it brings back legacy stars alongside the current Marvel Cinematic Universe cast. By reintroducing Chris Evans as Steve Rogers and Hayley Atwell as Peggy Carter, the film interrupts the conclusion previously established in 2019’s Avengers: Endgame. The project represents a monumental scale for the franchise, aiming to deliver an experience unlike anything previously seen in the MCU.

According to MovieWeb, Robert Downey Jr. leads the film as the villainous Victor Von Doom. A recent 31-second international teaser highlights a reunion between Evans' Steve Rogers and Chris Hemsworth’s Thor. The footage shows the pair approaching a massive staircase leading toward Doom’s throne, marking the first time these characters have faced the actor since his original turn as Iron Man in the 2019 film. Reports from MovieWeb note that this trio last shared the screen seven years ago during the final confrontation with Thanos.

As the studio enters the final stages of marketing for its fifth Avengers movie, the film is set for a wide release on December 18. While specific plot details remain under wraps, the narrative will integrate legacy actors who have portrayed these roles for over two decades. The anticipation centers on how these various cinematic timelines will collide during the battle against the fearsome new antagonist.
