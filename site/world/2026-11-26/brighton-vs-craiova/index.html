Brighton Hosts Universitatea Craiova Seeking Rebound After Recent Premier League Defeat

By Karim Fouad · Published 26 Nov 2026, 19:04 UTC

Brighton and Hove Albion take on Universitatea Craiova in the UEFA Conference League at the American Express Stadium on November 26.

Universitatea Craiova enters this UEFA Conference League fixture riding the momentum of a dominant 6-2 away victory against UTA Arad, a game where Stefan Baiaram scored twice. In stark contrast, Brighton and Hove Albion look to recover from a 2-0 defeat to Hull City, despite recording 70% possession and eight shots on target in that match. The Lions from Bănie arrive in England boasting an impressive form of eight wins and two draws in their last ten outings, averaging 2.7 goals per game while maintaining 53.6% possession. Meanwhile, Fabian Hurzeler’s side has maintained a solid record this season with seven wins, two losses, and one draw, bolstered by goalkeeper Bart Verbruggen’s five clean sheets across competitions. The outcome of this match carries significant weight for group standings as both clubs continue their European campaigns. Brighton’s tactical setup relies heavily on the distribution of playmakers like Charalampos Kostoulas, Ibrahim Osman, and Georginio Rutter, who each lead the team with two assists. On the other side, Alexandru Cicaldau has provided six assists in his last ten matches, fueling a potent attack led by Assad Al Hamlawi and Simon Elisor, who have each netted seven goals. Sportsgambler analysts have noted an Asian Handicap of +2.25 for the Romanian visitors, reflecting the defensive challenge expected for the underdogs at the American Express Stadium. The fixture provides a chance for Brighton to leverage their home advantage after their recent league disappointment. Kick-off is scheduled for 11:45 on Thursday, 26 November, with live commentary and match statistics provided by BBC Sport.
