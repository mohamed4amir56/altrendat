While the thermometer displays 17°C in Capital Federal, the actual thermal sensation drops to 13°C due to persistent wind gusts reaching 36 km/h. This four-degree difference highlights the impact of the air currents, even as the sky remains entirely clear with zero cloud cover reported by El Destape. The dry conditions are further evidenced by a 49% humidity level and a dew point of 6°C, ensuring the morning remains free of mist or fog. Such meteorological stability is reflected in the atmospheric pressure, which holds steady at 1023 hPa, providing excellent visibility of 55 km across the city. Residents should prepare for the day by wearing extra layers, as the wind continues to intensify the feeling of cold despite the lack of precipitation. The current snow level in the cordillera is registered at 3400 meters, serving as a regional reference point. With no rain forecasted and visibility remaining unobstructed, the city maintains a stable but brisk atmosphere for the coming hours.
