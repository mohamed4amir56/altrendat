David Corenswet Shines as Underdog Football Star in Upcoming Mr. Irrelevant Biopic

By Karim Fouad · Published 26 Nov 2026, 19:01 UTC

David Corenswet portrays John Tuggle, the final pick of the 1983 NFL draft, in the new sports drama Mr. Irrelevant arriving in theaters December 25, 2026.

Coach Bill Parcells tells his player, "I have never learned as much from a player as I learned from you," setting the stage for an emotional underdog story. David Corenswet stars as John Tuggle, a man who defied expectations both on and off the field after being selected as the final pick in the 1983 NFL draft by the New York Giants. His performance in the upcoming film is already generating Oscar-worthy buzz as he transitions from his recent role as Superman. The movie explores how Tuggle's perseverance and hope challenged his team and his head coach, Bill Parcells, played by Michael Shannon.

This sports drama is a significant project for fans of true-life athletic stories, highlighting the challenges faced by an athlete initially branded as the Mr. Irrelevant of the year. For those wanting an early look, select radio listeners have the opportunity to secure tickets for an advanced screening event held in December before the film's wide release.

Directed by Jonathan Levine and written by Nick Santora, the film features a large cast including Isabel May as Katie, Tuggle's love interest. Production was supported by a wide team of producers and executive producers, including David Ellison and Dana Goldberg. The film depicts the real-life struggle of a player fighting to prove his worth on the professional football field.

Following the advanced screening on December 16 at the Cinemark Egyptian Theater in Arundel Mills Mall, the full theatrical release for Mr. Irrelevant is scheduled for December 25, 2026.
