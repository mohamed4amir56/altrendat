Yaté and Thio face orange weather alert as heavy rains hit territory

By Karim Fouad · Published 27 Nov 2026, 02:01 UTC

Météo-France has placed Yaté and Thio under orange alert for heavy rain and storms until Friday afternoon.

Quick facts

Orange alert zones
Yaté and Thio
Peak rain forecast
150 mm in 12 hours
General wind speed
15 to 20 knots
Minimum temperatures
20 to 23 degrees

Up to 150 mm of rain may fall locally in just twelve hours between Thursday night and Friday in Yaté and Thio, according to Météo-France. While these two communes face an orange alert, the rest of New Caledonia remains under a yellow weather watch. In these yellow-alert areas, meteorologists forecast accumulation totals of 50 to 100 mm over a twelve-hour period.

Following heavy rainfall, authorities urge the public to limit travel to essential needs only. Residents are advised not to cross flooded roads or bridges, even on foot, and to keep children away from streams and rivers. The municipality of Ouégoa warned that rising waters could potentially submerge the Mission bridge at Bondé, noting that the radier at the Paimboa tribe has already been inundated.

According to NoumeaPost, a slow meteorological improvement is expected starting Friday, particularly in the South, where showers are becoming less frequent. However, the Northeast remains exposed to stormy downpours capable of generating significant local accumulation. Winds are generally blowing from the East between 15 and 20 knots, with the possibility of gusts during thunderstorms.

Looking ahead to the weekend, conditions will remain very cloudy across the country. Saturday will bring frequent and locally sustained showers, especially on the East Coast, the mountain range, Belep, the Isle of Pines, Lifou, and Maré. By Sunday, although the weather will stay cloudy, precipitation is expected to become more infrequent. Temperatures are forecast to rise, climbing above seasonal averages by the end of the weekend as the wind shifts and eases across the region.
