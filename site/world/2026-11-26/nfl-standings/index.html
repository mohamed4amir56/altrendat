Bills and Chiefs Clash for AFC Top Seed in Thanksgiving Week Showdown

By Karim Fouad · Published 26 Nov 2026, 05:01 UTC

The Bills and Chiefs prepare for a pivotal Thanksgiving game as all 32 NFL teams remain in the playoff hunt heading into Week 12.

With Thanksgiving approaching, every single one of the league's 32 teams remains mathematically eligible for a postseason berth. While the Arizona Cardinals face a remote possibility of elimination this week, they would require five specific outcomes, including a tie in the Packers-Rams game, to be knocked out. The upcoming schedule features 109 regular-season games remaining, with analysts simulating the final field to determine the eight division winners and the 14 total playoff participants.

Week 12 is defined by the high-stakes matchup between the 9-1 Buffalo Bills and 8-2 Kansas City Chiefs. According to NBC Sports, the victor of this Thanksgiving night game secures the inside track for the AFC's top seed and the accompanying first-round bye. The race tightened following recent losses by the Bengals and Jaguars, creating a crowded field at the top of the conference hierarchy.

In the AFC, the Denver Broncos hold an 8-2 record, maintaining a tiebreaker advantage over Kansas City based on common game results, while the Bengals and Jaguars both sit at 7-3. CBS Sports notes that Pittsburgh's decision to bench quarterback Aaron Rodgers following an ugly loss in Philadelphia marks the most significant personnel shift of the week. Despite the team's internal volatility, projections continue to keep the 6-4 Steelers in the postseason hunt.

The NFC playoff picture is similarly dense, with the 9-1 49ers leading the conference. The Thanksgiving schedule further intensifies the race, highlighted by a divisional battle between the 8-2 Lions and 8-2 Bears. Meanwhile, the 6-3-1 Giants have emerged as division leaders, and the 6-4 Packers and Rams are set to face off in a critical midweek encounter that will shape the conference standings.
