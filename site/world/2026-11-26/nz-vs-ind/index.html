India snatches toss and debuts Gurnoor Brar in second test showdown

By Karim Fouad · Published 26 Nov 2026, 23:01 UTC

India captain Shubman Gill chose to bowl at Hagley Oval on Friday as his team looks to break the deadlock in the second test against New Zealand.

Current series score
0-0

Gurnoor Brar makes his debut for India in Christchurch, stepping in for Prasidh Krishna to bolster the pace attack. The decision follows a heavy workload for the bowlers in the first test at the Basin Reserve, a match where India controlled long periods of play before it ended in a draw. Both teams enter the contest with the series locked at 0-0, desperate for a result to bolster their standings in the World Test Championship.

This match carries significant stakes for both squads as they fight to climb the World Test Championship table. New Zealand currently holds third place, while India sits in fourth. Securing a victory at Hagley Oval is essential for either side to improve their chances of reaching the final. India’s captain Shubman Gill noted that his pace bowlers are fresh and ready for the challenge, while New Zealand captain Tom Latham opted for an unchanged lineup despite his personal struggles in the series opener.

Conditions at Hagley Oval may favor the bowlers early on. According to reports from the venue, the pitch features a green covering with grass, though it is expected to be moist and slower on the first day. Latham noted that while he also intended to bowl, he expects the surface to quicken as the match progresses into the second and third days. Following the persistent rain in Christchurch that lasted until early Thursday afternoon, the state of the surface remains a primary focus for both team strategies.

India will field a side including Jaiswal, Rahul, Padikkal, Gill, Pant, Jurel, Sundar, Kamboj, Akash Deep, Bumrah, and the debutant Brar. New Zealand remains unchanged with Latham, Conway, Nicholls, Ravindra, Mitchell, Blundell, Phillips, Smith, Jamieson, Henry, and Duffy.
