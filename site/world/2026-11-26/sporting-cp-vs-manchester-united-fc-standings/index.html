Goalkeeper howler denies Mauricio Pochettino first Manchester United victory at Sporting

By Karim Fouad · Published 26 Nov 2026, 05:01 UTC

Manchester United settled for a 2-2 Champions League draw against Sporting after goalkeeper Senne Lammens committed a costly error leading to a late equalizer.

Manchester United’s hopes for a victory crumbled in the final ten minutes when goalkeeper Senne Lammens dropped a high ball inside the box, allowing Luis Suárez to level the score with a bicycle kick. This late collapse ruined a night that saw Bruno Fernandes net two goals for United, including one strike that bizarrely bounced into the net after hitting a divot in the pitch. The draw leaves United languishing in 19th place in the Champions League table as the team continues to struggle under new management.

The result highlights the ongoing defensive instability plaguing the squad, which has now conceded two penalties and seen five players sent off in just six weeks. For Mauricio Pochettino, the frustration was compounded by controversial officiating that saw two potential United goals ruled out, one of which followed an on-field video assistant referee review for an alleged foul by Patrick Dorgu.

Following the 4-0 defeat against Liverpool, United remains in a precarious position with little room for error. The match was further marred by a stoppage-time red card for Youri Tielemans, who was dismissed after receiving a second yellow card. Refereeing decisions throughout the game remained a significant point of contention, with Pochettino describing the initial booking of Tielemans as difficult to understand.

Manchester United will now look to regroup as they prepare for their upcoming fixture against Brentford. This weekend's match marks the first time Pochettino will lead the team at Old Trafford, where he faces the challenge of turning around a campaign that has seen only four victories thus far.
