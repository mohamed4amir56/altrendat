Undefeated Spurs Target NBA Cup Group Lead Against Portland Trail Blazers Tonight

By Karim Fouad · Published 26 Nov 2026, 05:31 UTC

The San Antonio Spurs face the Portland Trail Blazers in a pivotal NBA Cup group stage contest at 7:00pm Pacific.

The San Antonio Spurs enter tonight’s matchup against the Portland Trail Blazers riding a blistering wave of momentum, having claimed victories in 15 of their last 16 games. While the Spurs managed a recent 123-107 road win over the Sacramento Kings despite missing key players, they now look to extend their perfect record in West Group C of the NBA Cup tournament. For the 11-6 Trail Blazers, this home game marks their first appearance back after completing a 3-3 road trip that featured a victory over the Philadelphia 76ers.

This game carries significant weight for tournament progression. As the only undefeated team in their group, a victory for San Antonio puts them in a strong position to secure the group title ahead of a final showdown against the Lakers on Friday. For the Trail Blazers, the game represents a critical opportunity to regain footing in the cup standings while facing a team that previously defeated them 125-103 on November 7.

Injury reports add a layer of intrigue to the rotation, with San Antonio’s Victor Wembanyama and De’Aaron Fox both listed as questionable for the contest. The Trail Blazers will proceed without Vit Krejci, Ja Morant, Shaedon Sharpe, and Chris Youngblood. On the San Antonio side, Dylan Harper, Tobias Harris, and Jayden Quaintance remain sidelined.

Fans can tune in via the Rip City Television Network or KATU 2 for local broadcast coverage. Those looking to stream the action can access BlazerVision in Oregon and Washington, or use League Pass to watch from anywhere else.
