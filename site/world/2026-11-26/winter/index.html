Winter storm advisory snarls central Illinois Thanksgiving travel with icy road conditions

By Karim Fouad · Published 26 Nov 2026, 07:01 UTC

The National Weather Service issued a winter weather advisory for Springfield starting Wednesday afternoon through Thursday evening as snow threatens local commutes.

Quick facts

Advisory Period
Wednesday noon through Thursday 6 p.m.
Springfield Snowfall
1 to 3 inches
Jacksonville Area Snowfall
3 to 6 inches or more
Wednesday High
39 degrees
Thursday High
41 degrees

Accumulations of 1 to 3 inches are expected from Canton to Petersburg and Springfield as a quick-hitting system moves into central Illinois. While higher snowfall amounts reaching 3 to 6 inches are possible in the Jacksonville area and counties to the west, most snow will accumulate on grassy and elevated surfaces, according to the National Weather Service.

Travelers should prepare for slippery conditions that could impact Wednesday evening and Thursday morning commutes. Heavier snow bands remain a concern, as they may overcome warmer pavement and disrupt travel plans for those navigating the region during the Thanksgiving holiday.

Drivers are advised to slow down and maintain extra distance between vehicles. Forecast models have recently downgraded earlier snowfall projections for the Springfield area, though uncertainties persist regarding the potential for a rain and snow mixture.

Conditions are expected to transition as the system moves through, with the chance of precipitation remaining high at 90% to 100% through Thanksgiving Day. The forecast calls for a high near 39 degrees on Wednesday with north-northeast gusts reaching up to 24 mph by Thursday, before partly sunny skies return on Friday with highs near 43 degrees.
