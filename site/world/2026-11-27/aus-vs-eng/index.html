Australia Pips England By Three Wickets In Gripping T20 Series Opener

By Karim Fouad · Published 27 Nov 2026, 08:31 UTC

Australia clinched a three-wicket win over England with one ball to spare in the first T20 match at the MCG.

Jack Edwards drilled a boundary from Sam Curran’s penultimate delivery to seal a dramatic victory for Australia in front of a 39,000-strong crowd. The home side, fielding an unfamiliar lineup, successfully chased down England's record total of 187 for nine to claim the win with only one ball remaining in the contest. The result provides a lift for the home side following a difficult week for Australian cricket.

At the heart of the result was Nikhil Chaudhary, an India-born all-rounder who previously arrived in the country on a travel visa during the 2020 pandemic. Chaudhary claimed two wickets to stifle England’s batting momentum, including the crucial dismissal of Harry Brook, who had scored 65 from 28 deliveries. The all-rounder then anchored the response with a 38-ball 66 at number four, fundamentally breaking the back of the English bowling attack.

Despite the victory, the night brought injury concerns for the tourists. England vice-captain Will Jacks was forced to leave the field with a bleeding bowling hand after being struck by a powerful drive from Ben Dwarshuis. England, who recently recovered from a 1-0 deficit during their one-day series, now look toward the remaining fixtures in the five-match T20 tour.

Meanwhile, the second T20 match between the two sides has moved to Carrara, Queensland. During this second encounter, Australia’s Ben Dwarshuis showcased his athleticism by plucking a spectacular caught-and-bowled dismissal from a Phil Salt pull shot.
