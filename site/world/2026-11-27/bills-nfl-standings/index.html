Buffalo Bills Surge to AFC Number One Seed After Toppling Kansas City

By Karim Fouad · Published 27 Nov 2026, 05:01 UTC · Updated 27 Nov 2026, 05:32 UTC

The Buffalo Bills secured the top spot in the AFC playoff standings after a hard-fought 13-10 victory over the Kansas City Chiefs on Thanksgiving night.

Buffalo is currently sitting atop the AFC with a 10-1 record, holding a one-and-a-half-game lead over the second-place Denver Broncos. This status was cemented by a grueling win during the holiday weekend where the team overcame both adverse weather conditions and the Kansas City Chiefs in a contest that finalized their position at the head of the conference. Josh Allen described the team's perspective on the narrow 13-10 victory during the postgame response to the Thanksgiving night result. The Chiefs currently hold an 8-3 record following the outcome of this specific matchup. This positioning is significant for the postseason race, as Buffalo controls its own destiny to secure the single top seed in the AFC if they can successfully win their remaining conference games. The path for Buffalo is clearly defined, with observers highlighting their upcoming schedule as a critical stretch for maintaining this lead. Beyond the immediate celebrations, the team faces a high-stakes challenge on Christmas, when they are scheduled to play the Denver Broncos in a game that will likely dictate the landscape of the conference playoff picture. The Bengals and Jaguars, both previously lurking in the standings, saw their own momentum falter after recent losses earlier in the week, allowing Buffalo to create this current gap. The team now looks ahead to maintaining this momentum through the remainder of the regular season as they strive to secure the number one seed for the playoffs.
