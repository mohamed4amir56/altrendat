No. 15 USC and No. 9 UCLA clash in biggest rivalry matchup

The College Football Playoff committee has left teams in the dreaded spots just outside the top 11, specifically ranked Nos. 12 through 15, as the regular season enters its final week. This pressure has turned standard rivalry games into high-stakes battles that could either secure a postseason berth or send a top program into a late-season tailspin. While some coaches face intense scrutiny regarding their job security after difficult campaigns, others are playing for win-and-in scenarios that dictate their conference championship futures. CBS Sports reports that the rivalry series between No. 15 USC and No. 9 UCLA is seeing its most significant matchup in more than 20 years, with both programs fighting for a conference championship berth. According to Yahoo Sports, the betting landscape reflects this volatility, with UCLA currently listed as a touchdown underdog against USC following a quarterback injury to Ty Dieffenbach. Elsewhere in the country, No. 6 Alabama faces a major challenge from Auburn in the Iron Bowl, while No. 24 Washington attempts to block No. 12 Oregon from the playoff field. In the Big 12, Texas Tech has positioned itself with favorable odds to secure a spot in the conference title game, while Tennessee’s recent victory over LSU has altered the SEC rankings, pushing the Volunteers ahead of the Crimson Tide. The results of these final games will directly determine which teams earn coveted spots in the expanded College Football Playoff bracket and which programs are forced to reconcile with missed opportunities and coaching questions.
