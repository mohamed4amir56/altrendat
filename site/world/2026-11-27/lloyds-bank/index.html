Lloyds Banking Group app and online services crash across the UK

By Karim Fouad · Published 27 Nov 2026, 11:31 UTC · Updated 27 Nov 2026, 12:02 UTC

Millions of Lloyds Bank and Bank of Scotland customers lost access to their accounts following a major digital outage on Friday morning.

Quick facts

Affected banks
Lloyds Bank, Bank of Scotland
Estimated customer base
28 million
Start time
Around 10am
Date
Friday

Digital access for 28 million customers across the Lloyds Banking Group evaporated on Friday morning as the company’s mobile app and internet banking systems suffered a widespread failure. Beginning around 10am, users across the UK encountered persistent error messages, finding themselves unable to complete transactions, check account balances, or navigate basic banking functions. According to The Independent, the outage also crippled services for Bank of Scotland, which operates under the same banking group umbrella. While the bank’s official status page claimed no scheduled work was underway, it did acknowledge that telephone banking wait times had grown longer than usual. For customers, the sudden loss of digital connectivity created immediate friction, as the systems designed to provide constant access to funds became unreachable. Despite advice from the bank to simply log out and sign back in, many users reported that such attempts failed to restore service. The bank acknowledged the widespread difficulty in an official error message, stating they were working to return systems to normal as quickly as possible. As of the latest reports, the bank has provided no official timeline for a full restoration of its digital infrastructure. Customers currently face the uncertainty of limited account management until the technical issues are fully resolved by the group's internal teams.
