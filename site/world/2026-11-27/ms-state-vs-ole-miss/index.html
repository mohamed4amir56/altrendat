No. 22 Ole Miss Favored to Extend Egg Bowl Dominance Over Bulldogs

Mississippi State enters Vaught-Hemingway Stadium needing a win to secure bowl eligibility, while Ole Miss aims to extend a three-game winning streak in the rivalry. The Bulldogs, currently 5-6, face a steep challenge as they take on the 7-4 Rebels in a game that annually anchors college football's Rivalry Week. The Rebels are listed as 17.5-point favorites for this matchup in Oxford.

This game holds significant weight for the visitors, as a victory would mark a critical turnaround for a team that has struggled offensively since losing starting quarterback Kamario Taylor to injury on October 24. For the hosts, the focus remains on finishing the regular season with momentum as they look to capitalize on the performance of star quarterback Trinidad Chambliss. Having already recorded 24 touchdowns and 2,853 passing yards this season, Chambliss represents the centerpiece of an offense looking to rebound from recent losses against Oklahoma and Georgia.

The Bulldogs arrive in Oxford following a 34-16 victory over Tennessee Tech, a result that snapped a six-game losing streak. Despite this win, the SportsLine projection model notes a sharp decline in Mississippi State's scoring, with the team averaging only 10 points per game over the last five weeks. The model, which simulated the game 10,000 times, suggests the total score will fall under 56.5 points due to recent offensive struggles for both squads.

Historically, the 94-mile journey to Oxford has not been entirely daunting for the Bulldogs, who have claimed three of their last four victories in this series at the Rebels' home stadium. However, recent trends heavily favor Ole Miss, which has lost to Mississippi State only once during the six-season tenure of coach Lane Kiffin. The outcome this Friday will conclude the regular-season schedules for both programs.
