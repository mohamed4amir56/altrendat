Jaguars receiver Parker Washington ruled out for Sunday with shoulder injury

By Karim Fouad · Published 27 Nov 2026, 16:01 UTC

Jacksonville wide receiver Parker Washington will miss Sunday's game against the Tennessee Titans due to a shoulder injury sustained in practice.

Parker Washington sustained his shoulder injury during Thursday's practice session in Jacksonville, moving from off the injury report to being ruled out in less than 48 hours. The wide receiver was coming off a high-profile performance last Sunday against the New York Giants, where he recorded eight catches for 103 yards despite a controversial fourth-quarter ruling that denied him a touchdown pass. Head coach Liam Coen officially confirmed the injury status on Friday.

For the Jaguars, losing their primary receiving option poses a significant challenge for the upcoming game against the Titans. Washington has emerged as the team's top pass-catcher this season, leading the roster in every major receiving category with 44 total catches, 748 yards, and six touchdowns. His absence disrupts an offensive core already managing injuries to other key players, including running back Bhayshul Tuten.

According to reports from Sports Illustrated and NBC Sports, Washington's effectiveness has been critical to the Jaguars' passing attack, particularly on downfield targets where he has accounted for 483 receiving yards. While the team maintains depth with pass-catchers such as Jakobi Meyers, Brenton Strange, Brian Thomas Jr., and Josh Cameron, none function as a direct replacement for Washington's specific role in the slot.

Looking ahead, the coaching staff has designated Washington as a week-to-week player. The Jaguars continue to prepare for their Week 12 matchup against Tennessee without his availability, while the team balances the need for offensive continuity amidst a critical stretch of the season.
