Stellenbosch targets 150th club victory against struggling Rockets this Friday evening

By Karim Fouad · Published 27 Nov 2026, 17:31 UTC

Stellenbosch FC hosts TS Galaxy in a Betway Premiership clash on November 27 at 19:30.

Quick facts

Date
27 November
Time
19:30
League
Betway Premiership
Venue
Danie Craven Stadium

Stellenbosch FC enters Friday’s match against TS Galaxy eyeing a significant milestone, as a victory would mark the club’s 150th win across all competitions. The Maroons have historically dominated this fixture with 11 wins and 28 goals recorded over 17 previous meetings, representing club records against any single opponent. By contrast, the visiting Rockets have managed only a single victory against Stellenbosch, which occurred in November last year.

The outcome carries weight for the league standings, with only three points separating 8th-placed Stellenbosch from 13th-placed TS Galaxy. While the hosts start as betting favorites, they are looking to improve their form, having secured only one victory in their last six league matches. For the visitors, the points are equally vital as they sit just two points clear of the relegation zone.

Familiar faces will be on the pitch, as the Stellenbosch roster features former TS Galaxy players Given Msimango, Qobolwakhe Sibande, and Terrence Mashego. Conversely, former Stellenbosch development players Azola Jakalashe and Simthandile Sishi now feature for the opposition. Iqraam Rayners and Devin Titus remain the joint-leading goalscorers for this specific fixture with four goals each, though both previously played for the home side.

The match will be held at Danie Craven Stadium. Last season, the sides played out a 1-1 draw at this venue, a game highlighted by Wonderboy Makhubu scoring his only goal for Stellenbosch FC to date.
