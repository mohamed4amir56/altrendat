Camilo Duran 85th-minute header snatches crucial 2-1 Europa League win for Celtic

By Karim Fouad · Published 27 Nov 2026, 07:01 UTC

Substitute Camilo Duran secured a late victory for Celtic against Torreense in Portugal with his 15th goal of the season.

Quick facts

Match Result
Torreense 1-2 Celtic
Winner Goal Time
85th minute
Celtic Points
6

Camilo Duran secured his 15th goal of the season in the 85th minute, peeling off to the back post to meet a cross from James Forrest and directing a downward header into the net. The victory against the Portuguese second-tier side brings Celtic to six points in the Europa League table.

This result provides a vital boost for Celtic following their previous comeback win against Besiktas. The win spares the team from further European struggles after a match characterized by periods of inefficient passing and sloppy defensive play. Securing these three points is necessary for the squad's standing as they navigate a challenging European schedule.

The match began with an early advantage for the visitors when Javi Vazquez redirected a cross from Jota into his own net in the ninth minute. Torreense responded 10 minutes later after a sequence involving Alex Alfaro hitting the crossbar, eventually leading to a volley from Dany Jean that leveled the score. While Celtic dominated possession in the second half, clear scoring opportunities remained limited until the late intervention by Duran. According to BBC reporting, the team now prepares for a difficult fixture scheduled for Sunday.

Celtic will look to carry this momentum into their next outing as they continue their Europa League campaign.
