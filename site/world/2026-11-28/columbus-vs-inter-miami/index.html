Columbus Crew routs Inter Miami 3-0 in dominant South Beach soccer display

By Karim Fouad · Published 28 Nov 2026, 05:01 UTC

Columbus Crew defeated Inter Miami 3-0 on Saturday evening after exploiting defensive weaknesses and benefiting from a red card.

Quick facts

Final Score

Red Card Minute

Taha Habroune stood near the box with time to consider his options, reflecting the ease with which Columbus cut through a shoddy Miami defense to net the team's second goal. The Crew dismantled the reigning MLS Cup champions throughout the match, ultimately securing a comfortable 3-0 victory away from home. While Miami was reduced to 10 men following a second yellow card issued to Gonzalo Lujan in the 36th minute, the visiting side had already claimed the lead while playing against a full squad. The win serves as a tactical blueprint for other teams, demonstrating how an ambitious passing game can expose the defensive frailties inherent in the current Miami lineup. By consistently finding space and executing intricate passing sequences, Columbus maintained control even when facing individual threats from Lionel Messi, who was repeatedly denied by excellent saves from goalkeeper Patrick Schulte. According to Goal.com, the result was far too simple for the Crew, who passed and moved their way to victory without breaking a sweat against a team struggling to cope with organized opponents. The game highlights significant gaps in the Miami defensive structure that persist despite the presence of star players. Both sides will look to adjust their strategies following this performance, as Miami attempts to address their defensive vulnerabilities while the Crew continues to demonstrate the effectiveness of their brave, possession-based approach in the Major League Soccer season.
