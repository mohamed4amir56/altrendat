YEIDEP Grant Payments Begin January 5 Across 13 Nigerian Financial Institutions

By Karim Fouad · Published 28 Nov 2026, 07:01 UTC · Updated 28 Nov 2026, 07:31 UTC

The Federal Government will begin disbursing Youth Economic Intervention and De-Radicalisation Programme funds on January 5, 2027.

Quick facts

Payment Start Date
January 5, 2027
Registration Deadline
February 15, 2027
Target Age Range
18 to 50
Number of Participating Banks
13

Beneficiaries are urged to visit their respective financial institutions immediately as the nationwide distribution of Automated Teller Machine cards begins ahead of the scheduled payment start date. The Youth Economic Intervention and De-Radicalisation Programme (YEIDEP) mandate requires that all accounts be opened and domiciled with one of the 13 participating banks to ensure seamless access to the upcoming funds.

This initiative serves as a crucial financial support mechanism for Nigerian citizens aged 18 to 50. By coordinating with 13 major banks, the programme aims to streamline the disbursement process, ensuring that registered participants can collect their cards and prepare their accounts before the disbursement kicks off in early January.

According to Comrade Kennedy Iyere, the Coordinator-General of YEIDEP, the participating banks include Keystone Bank, Fidelity Bank, United Bank for Africa, Union Bank, Guaranty Trust Bank, Wema Bank, Zenith Bank, Access Bank, Signature Bank, Ecobank, Lotus Bank, Alpha Morgan Bank, and TAJ Bank. These institutions have been instructed to conduct large-scale issuance of cards to all registered members whose accounts are currently domiciled within their systems.

Despite the upcoming January deadline for payments, the enrolment phase for the programme remains active. Prospective beneficiaries can continue to register and open their accounts until February 15, 2027, as the recruitment drive stays valid throughout the initial stages of the fund distribution.
