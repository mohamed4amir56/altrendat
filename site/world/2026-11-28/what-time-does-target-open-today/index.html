Target Stores Prepare For Early Morning Rush During 2026 Black Friday Event

By Karim Fouad · Published 28 Nov 2026, 11:31 UTC

Target locations will be open for in-store shopping on Black Friday, November 27, 2026, after remaining closed on Thanksgiving Day.

Target shoppers looking to browse store aisles in person should prepare for early morning hours this Black Friday, November 27, 2026. While many major big-box and department stores keep their physical doors locked throughout the entirety of Thanksgiving Day, November 26, the arrival of Black Friday marks the transition back to standard retail operations for most locations.

Knowing when your specific Target branch opens is essential for planning a shopping route, as regional schedules and local mall rules often dictate the exact start time for doors to open. While the company shifts away from the tradition of opening on the holiday itself, the Black Friday shopping window remains one of the busiest times of the year. Because local operations can vary significantly depending on the site or shopping center, shoppers should confirm hours for their nearest location before heading out to ensure they do not arrive before the store is ready.

According to NBC 5 Chicago, many stores across the region are planning to open their doors well before the sun comes up to accommodate the holiday crowds. Mashable notes that while physical locations remain closed on Thanksgiving, digital platforms, apps, and websites will continue to host Black Friday deals throughout the holiday. Retailers expect a high volume of traffic as customers move from online browsing on Thanksgiving to the traditional, in-store shopping experience the following morning.

Shoppers are encouraged to verify specific store hours through the Target website or app, as localized adjustments are common. With the shift in holiday retail trends, the in-store experience now centers entirely on the Friday morning opening.
